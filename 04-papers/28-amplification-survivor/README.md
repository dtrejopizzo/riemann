# Paper 28 — Two closures and a survivor: the amplification route to the Riemann Hypothesis through almost-prime sieve bounds

Paper 27 left two forms surviving the one-dimensional no-go (two-dimensional propagation, Form A; index amplification, Form B) and an auxiliary Form C, an effective version of Bagchi's self-approximation criterion. This paper reports the outcomes. Form C closes: the amplifier is self-blocking, since the anchored density and the zero-density ceiling carry the same exponent. Form A closes: the only joint heat-time/arithmetic trace with unconditional edges vanishes identically in the interior. Form B survives: the negative index is superadditive under the Kreĭn tensor product, giving exponential amplification on the off-critical subspace, and the two-variable Weil functional on a thickened multiplicative diagonal has an autonomous arithmetic side supported on almost-primes. The remaining difficulty is the sieve-to-index bridge, stated precisely. No proof of RH is claimed.
