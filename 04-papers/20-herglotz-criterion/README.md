# Paper 20 — The Riemann Hypothesis as a Herglotz positivity: Re ξ′/ξ > 0 in the right half-plane, an unconditional boundary identity, and the Hilbert–Pólya reformulation

The paper gives a self-contained account of the de Branges chain criterion in its sharpest analytic form: RH is equivalent to Re ξ′/ξ(σ+iτ) > 0 for all σ > ½, i.e. to ξ′/ξ being Herglotz on the right half-plane. It isolates one unconditional ingredient, the boundary identity Re ξ′/ξ(½+iτ) = 0, and recasts the criterion as a harmonic-function problem in which an off-line zero is exactly an interior pole forcing a sign change. The content is classical (Hermite–Biehler, Laguerre–Pólya); the contribution is the unified statement, the explicit boundary identity, the harmonic-measure formulation, and numerical verification. It is a reformulation, not a proof.

## Main results
- `prop:crit` (The criterion is RH): positivity of Re ξ′/ξ on σ > ½ ⟺ no zero with β > ½. PROVED (classical content, CITED).
- `thm:boundary` (Boundary identity): Re ξ′/ξ(½+iτ) = 0 for all real τ, from the functional equation alone. PROVED.
- `cor:min`: the critical line is a σ-critical line of |ξ|; local minimum under RH. PROVED (minimum statement CONDITIONAL on RH).
- `prop:minprinciple` (Minimum principle vs interior poles): positivity ⟺ no interior poles. PROVED.
- Chain RH ⟺ Herglotz ⟺ Hilbert–Pólya (Weyl function of a self-adjoint operator). CITED.
- Exclusion of interior poles from boundary data: OPEN.
- Numerical checks of Re ξ′/ξ. NUMERICAL.
