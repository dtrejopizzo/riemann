# Paper 40 — Branch Selection and Global Polarization in the Connes–CCM Route to RH: Finite Prime–Gamma Inertia, Tate Boundary Geometry, and the Divisor-Sensitivity Obstruction

The paper organizes the Connes–Consani–Moscovici and Suzuki operator programmes as one route from finite semilocal models to the global Weil criterion. The first part builds a joint prime–Gamma finite-source refinement keeping the von Mangoldt channels, the archimedean channel, the scalar threshold and the exact radical in one Hermitian system; Loewner monotonicity, Haynsworth additivity, Schur innovations, adaptive Krylov recovery and heat-core exhaustion remove the selection, tail and form-core losses, and the remaining sign is a strict source surplus over the anti-shorted deficit. The second part constructs local geometric data: a cyclic root Laplacian whose determinant increments and root-stratum incidences multiply to Λ(p^k)/√(p^k), and at the infinite place a Gamma spin operator and polar plane giving the complete factor ½s(s−1)π^{−s/2}Γ(s/2) by zeta determinants. The ordinary Hilbert completion of these data has absolutely continuous scale spectrum and cannot be the missing polarization. Six terminal formulations are shown to each contain the complete Weil sign, and two open programmes are isolated.
