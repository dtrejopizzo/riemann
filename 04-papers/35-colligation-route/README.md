# Paper 35 — The Colligation Route to the Riemann Hypothesis: de Branges Realization, a Curvature Theorem, and the Pole-Pinned Spectral Radius at the Wall

The paper assembles a single route built on the de Branges / Lax–Phillips colligation attached to the completed zeta function ξ and locates the remaining obstruction. It shows that finite-window boundedness of the oscillatory part of the Weil form implies RH, identifies the index of the route as the Kreĭn–Langer negative-square count, proves an unconditional Bakry–Émery curvature bound for the prime part of the Weil generator after Doob conjugation, and realizes Weil positivity as the absence of a Schrödinger bound state for an explicit Birman–Schwinger operator, in which the pole of ζ at s = 1 pins the spectral radius from below at 1. The synthesis: every geometric and functional-analytic structure on the route is Doob-invariant and proves the unconditional shape, while the sign is the upper pinning of the spectral radius at 1. In the function-field case this pinning is supplied by the Weil pairing; over Spec ℤ the corresponding arithmetic pairing is missing and is stated precisely.

## Main results

- `prop:model` (de Branges–Rovnyak model; safe region) — PROVED.
- `thm:angle` (angle criterion) — PROVED, recorded as an equivalence, not an independent route.
- `thm:L1` (L1 ⇒ RH, the Laplace–pole theorem) — PROVED, including the Paley–Wiener nonvanishing lemma; the converse RH ⇒ L1 is CONDITIONAL on a regularized zero-sum estimate.
- `thm:kl` (sharp index: negative-square count equals the number of off-line zeros) — PROVED.
- `prop:hodge` (Hodge-index reformulation) — PROVED as a reformulation.
- `thm:gamma2kernel`, `thm:CD0` (exact Γ₂ kernel; CD(0,∞) for the prime kernel; the Davenport–Heilbronn falsifier fails it) — PROVED, unconditional.
- `prop:bs` (Birman–Schwinger form of the wall) — PROVED; the bound ‖K_λ(0)‖ ≤ 1 itself is OPEN.
