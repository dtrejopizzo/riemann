# Paper 39 — Cofinal Weil Quotients: Exact Transport Defects, Fixed-Cutoff Summability, and the Global Curvature Gate

Starting from the finite self-adjoint operators of Connes–Consani–Moscovici (principal Fourier restrictions of the semilocal Weil form, shifted by the least Ritz value and quotiented by the radical) and Suzuki's localized operator, the paper develops the analytic chain from finite radical quotients to a cutoff-free spectral-gap formulation of Weil positivity. At fixed cutoff L it builds canonical isometries between successive positive quotients, computes the generator defect exactly (two rank-one channels of opposite parity plus a double-operator-integral correction), and proves a Cauchy-transform formula for the second-resolvent trace, which gives cofinal summability at every fixed L. The global limit is reduced, after an explicit mesh error, to convergence of the logarithmic curvature ∂²_z log(φ̂_L/k̂_L) to zero, where k_L is the prolate model. The paper then analyzes this gate: it carries the full zero-divisor content, and the branch choice is isolated as a signed source estimate that concentration determines up to sign only.
