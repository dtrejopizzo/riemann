# Paper 41 — Obstructions to Weil Positivity: A Catalogue of No-Go Theorems, Falsifiers and Countermodels in the Prime–Gamma Doob Coordinate

The companion Paper 40 reduces the route to one strict inequality on a cofinal family of finite rows, the source surplus g > δ. This paper does not attempt that inequality; it records the mechanisms that provably cannot prove it. In the full-kernel Doob coordinate Weil positivity is a single sharp Poincaré inequality E_K(r) ≥ ½Var_{μ_K}(r). The catalogue has thirty-one entries in eight families (order and total positivity; coupling, transport and martingales; local squares, sums of squares and Hodge–Korn completions; determinants and minor charging; finite heads and adaptive filtering; spectral detectors; realization and factorization rigidity; the semilocal branch-selection coordinate), each classified as a no-go theorem, a conditional falsifier under the off-line counterfactual, a countermodel, or a scale obstruction. Three principles account for every entry: radical saturation, measure capacity and counterfactual invariance. The surviving unconditional structure is also recorded.

## Main results

- `thm:doob` (full-kernel Doob–variance identity) — PROVED.
- `thm:ess`, `thm:heat`, `thm:bounded`, `thm:margin` (essential threshold; heat-core Galerkin exhaustion; bounded prime generator; pointwise theta–Gamma margin) — PROVED.
- `thm:absorption` (exact Gamma–connection factorization; Gamma absorption 2K(u) < (1501/2000) r_Γ(u)) — PROVED.
- `prop:pnt` (common primitive) — PROVED.
- `thm:antisurplus`, `cor:invariance` (off-line anti-surplus; counterfactual invariance) — CONDITIONAL on the existence of an off-line orbit, used as falsifiers.
- Principles P1 (radical saturation), P2 (measure capacity), P3 (counterfactual invariance) — PROVED as stated.
- Thirty-one catalogue entries — NO-GO / conditional falsifiers / countermodels, as classified in each entry.
- The source surplus inequality itself — OPEN.
