# Paper 34 — The Architecture of Obstruction: A Complete Mathematical Map of the Riemann Hypothesis Program

The paper is a survey of the analytic, functional-analytic and geometric approaches developed in Papers 01–33, organized not as a catalogue of results but as a map of the obstruction that terminates each route. Six families are covered: the ω-class programme, the localized Weil quadratic form, the capstone wrong-sign paradigm, the geometric programmes (Spec ℤ cohomology, Lefschetz dichotomy), the Kreĭn/Pontryagin and de Branges arc, and the information barrier for ω-class objects. Three surviving walls are identified: W1 (Weil positivity), W2 (de Branges canonical systems requiring a global geometry of Spec ℤ) and W3 (pair correlation and gap universality). The paper ends with a synthesis statement: the zero-location constraint is global, one-sided and orthogonal to the available symmetry data.
