# Paper 34 — Obstructions in a series of approaches to the Riemann Hypothesis: a heuristic survey

The paper surveys the approaches to the Riemann Hypothesis studied in Papers 02–21 of the series (logarithmic sums of q^ω(n), localized Weil forms, Kreĭn and Pontryagin formulations, de Branges spaces, Hodge-index and Lefschetz analogies). For each it states what the corresponding paper proves, with its hypotheses, and describes heuristically where the approach stops.

What the paper recalls (all proved in the cited papers unless marked):

- Paper 21: Selberg–Delange asymptotics of M_q(N) = Σ_{n≤N} q^ω(n)/n, and C(h,N;q)/M_q(N)² ≪ 1/log N for q ≥ 1; positivity of the local densities.
- Paper 18: κ_V ≤ m for the negative index of the Weil form on even tests when there are m < ∞ off-line quartets (equality open); structure of de Branges realizations.
- Paper 16: the second-moment identity for V(d,T); under RH, V_w ≪ dN(T) for d = AL/2 with fixed A ≥ 1.
- Paper 13: monotonicity of the off-line mass for real polynomials with simple zeros under the backward heat flow (not proved for H_t).
- Paper 14: b(k) = 2^{2k+3} m_{2k}/(2k)! and an upper bound for λ_min of a sine-kernel Gram matrix.
- Paper 17: results on the archimedean term and on sl₂ triples.
- Paper 07: the change of the localized zero-side Gram matrix when a double point is split, of order δ² with a rank ≤ 3 leading matrix having a negative eigenvalue.
- Paper 11: a conditional reduction for the finiteness of the negative index; κ_d < ∞ on each band H_d (unconditional); uniformity in d open.
- Paper 12: a largest generalized eigenvalue equal to 1 to five digits at T₀ = 1000, and sine-kernel structure at T₀ = 80; numerical observations only.

The organisation into three "walls" and the sign-versus-symmetry principle are heuristic. The paper proves no new theorem, and nothing in it bears on the truth of RH. Several of the recalled statements are classical (von Koch, Weil, Selberg–Delange, Goldston–Montgomery, Pontryagin, de Branges, Kaltenbäck–Woracek).
