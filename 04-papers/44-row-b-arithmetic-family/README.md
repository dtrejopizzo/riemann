# Paper 44: An arithmetic Frobenius family and its integral shadow

**What it proves (row B).** Inside the algebra of Paper 43 it builds the Frobenius measures Frob_n
(strong monoidal realization of (N,×), inverse-semigroup closure), proves their contact with the
diagonal is Λ(n)n^{-1/2} at every smoothing level, constructs the integral shadow [L_S →n→ L_S] with
Möbius cofiber [Z →Φ_n(1)→ Z] (identities of degrees), computes the two Gram forms (Frobenius family:
finite kernel only; unit-averaged family: whole Weil kernel), the self-pairing −log(1/ε)+O(1), positivity
of the finite-place form on one primitive class, and uniqueness of the normalization vol(Z_p^×) = log p.
