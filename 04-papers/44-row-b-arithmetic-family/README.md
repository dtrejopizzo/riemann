# An arithmetic Frobenius family on the idele class group

For each positive integer `n` with prime divisors in `S`, the paper defines a probability measure
`Frob_n` on the semilocal idele class group, the Haar measure of a coset of a compact subgroup.
These measures multiply according to the product of their indices and, with their adjoints,
generate a commutative inverse semigroup. On smooth approximations of `Frob_n` the finite local
terms of the trace sum to `Λ(n)/√n` times the value of the archimedean profile at `1/n`. The paper
also treats the family obtained by averaging over the maximal compact subgroup, computes the
Hermitian form of the trace on both families, and describes an integral complex whose cohomology
is `Z/Φ_n(1)`.

It uses the algebra, involution and trace of
[Paper 43](../43-row-a-torsion-theory/).
