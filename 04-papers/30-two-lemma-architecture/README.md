# Paper 30 — Finiteness and rigidity: a two-lemma architecture for the Riemann Hypothesis

Let m be the number of quadruples of non-trivial zeros of ζ off the critical line. The paper proves a conditional dichotomy: if ζ admits pointwise self-approximation along a single unbounded sequence of shifts on compact disks of the open strip (Lemma LP-112, formally weaker than Bagchi's strong recurrence), then m ∈ {0, ∞}. The hypothesis m < ∞ freezes all constants in the Bohr–Rouché replication argument so that one replication suffices, and the Euler product enters at three identified points. RH therefore follows from two lemmas: (L8) finiteness, m < ∞, and (LP-112) sequential rigidity. A byproduct is an unconditional theorem: ζ is not Stepanov almost periodic, in the band sense, on any sub-strip of the critical strip.

## Main results

- `thm:dichotomy` (conditional dichotomy): LP-112 implies m ∈ {0, ∞} — CONDITIONAL on LP-112; the implication is PROVED.
- `thm:stepanov` (no Stepanov almost-periodicity, band sense) — PROVED, unconditional.
- `thm:architecture` (two-lemma reduction): RH follows from L8 together with LP-112 — PROVED as an implication; L8 and LP-112 are OPEN.
- L8 as the countable form of cohomological finite-dimensionality over Spec ℤ — ARGUED.
- Absence of a literature precedent for LP-112 (every known self-approximation theorem is a density statement) — ARGUED, with the Montel-plus-Besicovitch chain shown to fail at the identification of the limit.
