# Papers

Catalogue of the papers of the program. Folder names carry the paper number as a prefix. Papers
01–42 are the earlier arcs (ω-classes, inverse operators, the Weil form and its realizations, the
Pick architecture, the first arithmetic Lefschetz construction); none of them claims a proof of
the Riemann hypothesis. Papers 43–46 carry the four rows of Weil's proof over
$\mathrm{Spec}\,\mathbb Z$: rows A–C (Papers 43–45) and row D (Paper 46, *The sign of the radial
intersection form on the idele class group*). Paper 46 completes the proof of the generalised
Riemann hypothesis for Dirichlet $L$-functions, and so of the Riemann hypothesis and of
$W_T\ge 0$ on every window, from three theorems on integral
algebraic modular forms: the Short Forms Theorem, the Spanning Theorem and the Covolume Theorem.
Papers 49–56 are its eight companions and prove the propositions from which the three theorems
are deduced; Paper 57 is the map of the four rows, and Paper 58 is row D complete in a single
volume. The
mathematics that the three theorems organise comes from the work of Levent Alpoge (Lean 4
formalization, <https://alpo.ge/remark.pdf>), which is by itself a complete proof of the Riemann
hypothesis and does not need these papers. Paper 47 is the intersection frame and Paper 48 the
map of the routes attempted earlier. The papers are open to evaluation by the whole community;
they have not yet been refereed.

## Notes

- Papers 01, 27, 29 and 34 are surveys; 36 is a ledger of obstructions with a Pick reformulation of RH; 42 is a programme paper.
- Papers 43–45 supersede the row constructions of Paper 42. Row D is Paper 46 with its companions, Papers 49–56; Paper 58 is the same chain in a single volume. An earlier version of Paper 46, with an annex on column packages, is in the history of the repository.
- Papers 43–46 carry rows A–D in order; with Papers 49–56 they are the twelve papers of the series described in Paper 57. The papers on the sign (row D) and on the intersection frame exchanged numbers, and the working notes under `../03-research/` and `../05-meta-and-planning/` use the current ones. A few file names of notes still contain `P47` or `PAPER47`; they refer to the paper on the sign (Paper 46). Files that were being edited at the time of the exchange and scripts were left as they were.
- The earlier version of this index, with dependencies and the publication flow, is in `../05-meta-and-planning/archive-top-level/04-papers-README.md`.
