# Paper 23 — A Jacobi-operator spectral-triple reformulation of the Riemann Hypothesis and the incidence equivalence

The Connes–Consani–Moscovici zeta spectral triple yields self-adjoint Jacobi operators D_λ^N whose real eigenvalues approximate the zeros of Ξ. The paper extracts a limit operator J_∞ on ℓ²(ℕ₀), identifies its spectrum with the real zero set of an explicit potential C_∞ = w − Ψ, shows every real zero of C_∞ is a zero of Ξ, and proves a set of unconditional structural facts on the truncated potential. The central result is an equivalence of RH with the incidence inclusion (every real zero of Ξ is a real zero of C_∞); the remaining direction reduces to a moment-determinacy question.

## Main results
- `thm:ccm`: the CCM construction. CITED.
- `prop:kpos`, `prop:count`, `cor:weak`: positivity of the Weil kernel, zero count to O(log T), weak convergence of spectral measures. PROVED.
- `thm:C` (Spectral identification) and `thm:B` (Direct inclusion): PROVED; `cor:few` (few off-critical zeros) PROVED.
- `thm:zero-free`: zero-free region Im z > ½ for the truncated potential. PROVED.
- `prop:geom`, `prop:approach`: birth of complex zeros at subthreshold minima; approach rate ≥ λ^{−1/2}. PROVED.
- `prop:three`, `thm:had`, `prop:reg`: equivalent forms and Hadamard–Mittag-Leffler regular part. PROVED.
- `thm:rh-implies-ii` (RH ⇒ Inc.Inv.) and `thm:D` (Inc.Inv. ⇒ RH); `thm:equiv` (Complete equivalence). PROVED (equivalence).
- `thm:E` (Conditional full hypothesis): CONDITIONAL on LI of {log|ρ|} and simplicity of zeros.
- `prop:moment` (Reduction to determinacy): the open direction reduces to moment determinacy. OPEN.
