# Paper 14 — Two Modern Routes to the Riemann Hypothesis and the Robustness of the Wrong-Sign Obstruction: the Hodge-index and hyperbolicity reformulations

The paper studies two reformulations whose mechanism is not, on its face, a quadratic-form
positivity. The first is cohomological. The band-limited Weil–Carleson form is read as the
primitive part of a candidate Hodge-index pairing. Its regularized gap is positive-definite and
equals the squared minimum normalized gap. The second is hyperbolicity: the Laguerre–Pólya class
and hyperbolicity of the Jensen polynomials. The Hermite-form criterion shows that real-rootedness
is itself a positivity. Both routes therefore reduce to the same one-sided target, which confirms
the wrong-sign obstruction across eight paradigms.

## Main results

- `thm:reggap`: the regularized Hodge gap is positive-definite and
  $\lambda_{\min}(G)\approx\frac{\pi^2}{6}b_{\min}^2$ — the identity is NUMERICAL (approximate),
  with a sine-kernel level-repulsion explanation ARGUED.
- `cor:price`: reformulations as a uniform Riesz sequence, a uniform Muckenhoupt $A_2$ condition,
  and extremal regularity of $S(T)$ — CONDITIONAL on the preceding identity.
- Moment identity $b(k)=(-1)^ka(k)=m_{2k}/(2k)!$ for the Jensen coefficients — PROVED.
- `prop:blind`: the shift operator is an arithmetic-blind stability preserver — PROVED.
- `thm:hermite`: real-rootedness is a positivity (Hermite-form criterion) — PROVED (classical
  input CITED).
- `thm:capstone`: in eight reformulations the unconditional handles are lower bounds while the
  target is a one-sided upper constraint — ARGUED (structural synthesis).
