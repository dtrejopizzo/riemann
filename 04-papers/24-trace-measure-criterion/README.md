# Paper 24 — A trace and measure criterion for the Riemann Hypothesis from the zeta spectral triple

The zeta spectral triple attaches two spectral measures to ζ: one built from |ζ(½+is)|² and an on-critical one built from the Hadamard product with every zero moved to the line. The paper studies their difference dν. The central unconditional result is a dominance theorem: R(s) = |ζ/ζ_on(½+is)|² ≥ 1, with equality iff all zeros lie on the line, so dν ≥ 0 and dν = 0 ⟺ RH. Eight a priori different conditions are shown pairwise equivalent to dν = 0, an off-line zero is shown to force a second-order lower bound on the trace T_λ, and recovery of dν from the trace family is stated under an explicit completeness hypothesis (C). The function-field analogue is used to isolate the missing ingredient as the Hadamard barrier.

## Main results
- `thm:dominance` (Unconditional dominance of ζ over ζ_on): PROVED.
- `cor:dnu`: dν ≥ 0 is a non-negative invariant vanishing iff RH. PROVED.
- `thm:eight` (Eight equivalent criteria): PROVED.
- `prop:lower` (Lower bound under ¬RH): second order in the off-line displacement. PROVED.
- `thm:trace-crit` (Trace criterion under (C)): CONDITIONAL on completeness of the Abel kernels in L²(dν).
- `thm:ff` (Function-field trace vanishes): PROVED (via Weil's theorem, CITED).
- `prop:barrier` (The barrier is genuine): the Hadamard product gives dν ≥ 0 but not completeness or vanishing. PROVED.
