# Paper 36 — The Riemann Hypothesis: An Obstruction Ledger and the Arithmetic Pick Architecture It Shaped

The paper has two parts. The first is a ledger of the concrete no-gos that closed the spectral, arithmetic, adelic and positivity-based routes of the programme, distilled into structural walls (arithmetic propagation, wrong-sign positivity, infinite-dimensional Hasse–Minkowski failure, per-place indefiniteness, the cohomological-Frobenius wall, sign versus magnitude, and the uniform-norm wall), each with an explicit logical classification. Two earlier results of the programme are recorded as withdrawn or downgraded to conditional. The second part constructs the arithmetic Pick positivity (ARP-P) chain, fifteen steps establishing ARP-P ⟺ RH; fourteen steps are closed, and the open Step 5 reduces to the terminal-defect inequality δ_N ≥ 0 for all N, which is the classical Li criterion. A falsification harness accompanies the paper.

- `thm:mirror-criterion`, `thm:live-H5` (window positivity is equivalent to ARP-P) — PROVED.
- `cor:live-H6`, `prop:H6-witness`, `thm:H6-closed` (finite negative witness, single-cluster reduction) — PROVED.
- `thm:det-serialization`, `cor:schur-coords` (determinant serialization) — PROVED.
- `thm:cluster` (H8 for scalar fixed cluster patterns) — PROVED.
- Step 5 / Ω₇ (Li positivity) — OPEN; the paper states it carries the full difficulty of the hypothesis.
