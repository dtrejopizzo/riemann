# The Weil--Toeplitz algebra of window forms and a logistic tensor-tower identity

The paper arranges window forms attached to the Weil explicit formula as a bounded field of finite-dimensional Hilbert spaces over the natural numbers. The bounded sections form a C\*-algebra, and the image of a section in the corona (bounded sections modulo sections that vanish at infinity) is its symbol. Inside an axiomatized model of the windows it studies what the symbol can and cannot detect about off-critical zeros. Separately it studies how the negative index behaves under tensor powers, and applies this to a localized quantity built from a doubled form over pairs of zeros.

## Results

Proved unconditionally:
- Operator lemmas. The defect equals the dimension of the negative spectral subspace. Strict essential positivity is exactly the positivity that survives compact perturbations (Theorem 2.7). This is standard, and a proof is included. The norm and the spectrum of the symbol are described by the limits of the windows (Lemmas 3.3, 3.4).
- A trichotomy inside the axiomatized model (Theorems 4.5–4.13). Fat quadruples make the symbol negative. Thin visible quadruples accumulate at 0 in the spectrum of the symbol. Quadruples with zero visibility leave the symbol unchanged. Part (a) of the calendar no-go theorem (Theorem 4.11) uses an extra axiom (V3).
- The exact logistic identity for normalized negative indices of tensor powers (Theorem 5.1) and its localized form (Proposition 5.6).
- An appendix on finite Euler products. It shows that a finite product satisfies the positivity property exactly when every local parameter is pure. This material is classical.
- The localized dichotomy (Theorem 5.19) for the doubled form Q₂, which the paper defines by an absolutely convergent spectral sum over pairs of zeros: under RH the localized quantity is 0, and under ¬RH it is positive (Theorem 5.16). The ¬RH branch uses an explicit family of B-spline-type bumps, sinc^M times a fixed smooth factor, with constants C_M = 3^M and log-support M/2 + 1 (Lemma 5.13), and a localization radius R = 4000. Remark 5.14 shows that the tail bound of Lemma 5.12 cannot be made to work at radius 2 with any family of bumps.
- Since Q₂ is defined through the zeros, the equivalence of RH with the vanishing of the localized quantity is close to a restatement of the existence of an off-critical zero. Its content is that such a zero is detected by a finite cell specified without zero positions. It is not an arithmetic criterion.

Not established:
- A prime-side expression for Q₂.
- The model axioms (S1)–(S3) and Hypotheses 3.7–3.9 for the zeta function.

The paper does not prove RH and its proofs use no numerical computation (one numerically evaluated constant appears in a remark and is not used in any proof).

## Known and new

The window C\*-algebra is an ℓ^∞-product and its corona is a standard construction. Theorem 2.7 and the Weyl and interlacing lemmas are standard, and the appendix is the easy direction of Weil's criterion for finite Euler products. What is new is the model trichotomy, the logistic tensor-tower identity, and the localized dichotomy for Q₂. One Lorentzian block per off-critical quartet is quoted from Paper 18 as motivation.
