# Paper 26 — An operatorial geometry for ζ: the Weil–Toeplitz algebra and a logistic criterion for the Riemann Hypothesis

The renormalized window forms of the explicit formula are organized into a bounded field of finite-dimensional Hilbert spaces over ℕ. Bounded sections form a C*-algebra W, sections vanishing at infinity a closed ideal J ≅ c₀, and the corona W/J carries a symbol σ(Q) whose spectrum is the limit set of the window spectra; "mean over windows" versus "uniformly over windows" becomes Cesàro states versus norm. Within an axiomatized spectral model the paper proves a trichotomy of off-critical zeros (fat, thin, sub-resolution) and a sharp characterization of robust finiteness of the defect. Separately it proves an exact logistic law for the tensor tower of window indices and a localized criterion whose ¬RH branch is conditional on one named analytic gap (Paley–Wiener uniformity). It is a reformulation and a framework, not a proof.
