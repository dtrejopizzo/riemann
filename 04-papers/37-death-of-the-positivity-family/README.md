# Paper 37 — The Limits of the Positivity Family in the Li–Keiper Route to the Riemann Hypothesis

The direct route from the Li–Keiper criterion terminates in a compact–tail inequality (★), bounding the prime counting error weighted by a Laguerre kernel. A cutoff audit corrects an earlier overstatement of the required margin: the intrinsic cofinal statement is 4λ_n − A_n > 0, and no quantitative claim that semidefiniteness falls short by a divergent amount survives. The surviving thesis is structural: every instrument considered certifies nonnegativity of an auxiliary object (a measure, a square, a Gram form, a totally positive kernel, a completely monotone function), while the object at stake, the completed cocycle, is not of that kind. Its Euler and Gamma factors are positive by infinite divisibility, but its polar factor is signed with an exactly computable negative density. The target is therefore a coercivity estimate, not a semidefiniteness one. Sixteen attacks on (★) are closed, each with an exact witness.
