\section{Corrected A--B--C--D construction and current obligations}
\label{sec:abcd-current}

This section records the construction through P123.062 (5 September 2026).
It supersedes earlier claims in this manuscript that complete rows
(a)--(c) have been established. The earlier categorical proposals are
retained as conditional constructions, not as a completed proof chain.
No complete row (a), (b), (c), or (d), and no proof of RH, is asserted.
The full arguments, counterexamples, scripts and endpoint evidence are

Numbered P123 references below refer to those archived notes. Their

referee certification of all its contents.

\subsection{Row (a): repaired source, tensor law and charts}
The minimal unitization of a crossed product does not automatically
contain its gauge multipliers. P123.018--019 replace that construction by
the rapid crossed product of the coefficient unitization. With
$B=C_0(X_S)$, $B^+=B+\C1$ and positive $S$-units $\Gamma_S$, it is
\[
 A_S^u=\{(b_\gamma):b_\gamma\in B^+,\quad
 p_k(b)=\sum_\gamma\|b_\gamma\|H(\gamma)^k<\infty\ (k\ge0)\}.
\]
Here $H$ is the maximum of numerator and denominator. Submultiplicativity
of $H$ proves completeness and continuous crossed multiplication and
involution. The elements $U_\gamma$ now belong to the algebra. Evaluation
at coefficient infinity gives a split map to scalar rapid labels, whose
kernel retains the original $C_0$ coefficient ideal. Ambient nuclearity
is not asserted.

For an external automorphism $\beta$, let $E_\beta={}_1A_\beta$.
P123.019 proves the topological isomorphism
\[
 E_\beta\widehat\otimes_A E_\eta\simeq E_{\beta\eta},
 \qquad x\otimes y\longmapsto x\beta(y),
 \qquad z\longmapsto[z\otimes1].
\]
Associativity, the dagger $x\mapsto\beta^{-1}(x^*)$, and lift change
$x\mapsto xU_\gamma$ are checked in this actual algebra.
P123.020--023 construct normalized semilocal charts: adding the new
integral lattice characteristic functions preserves the coefficient
product, while normalized external projections forget probability-Haar
unit factors. Raw append-one lifts and unrestricted full-Hom chart
claims are not valid substitutes. The established chart category is
the specified gauge-generated one. Integrated external action is not
by itself a faithful nuclear realization.

P123.027--030 sharpen the descent issue. Substituting $T\mapsto U_q$
gives a one-sided algebraic perfect-module realization; the resulting
differentials need not be bimodule maps. Two-sided induction has unit
$A\otimes_R A$, not automatically $A$. Faithful flatness is proved in
the stated one-prime complex algebraic setting. Completed Amitsur
contraction is instead proved over the split rapid scalar algebra, with
continuous homotopy and closed complemented images. These results do
not remove integral torsion obstructions or prove a common shadow.

\subsection{Row (b): integral contacts and the operations they require}
P123.022,025--026 retain the integral complex
\[
 K_n=[\Z\xrightarrow{\Phi_n(1)}\Z],\qquad
 H^0(K_2),H^0(K_4),H^0(K_3),H^0(K_6)
 =\Z/2,\Z/2,\Z/3,0.
\]
The cyclotomic filtration and its Tor connecting map are integral;
rational splitting loses gluing information. Derived tensor products
retain Tor and are not the reduced greatest-common-divisor contact.
An additive complex-linear source cannot realize nonzero integral
$p$-torsion by an additive functor: multiplication by $p$ is invertible
in the former. Integral source data must therefore be retained.

P123.037 constructs the power maps $z\mapsto z^n$ on $\mathbb G_m$,
their unit cotangent complexes $[\Z\xrightarrow n\Z]$, and the primitive
divisor total-cofiber cube. It gives the same four contact objects.
P123.038--040 place the arithmetic action on actual finite lattices and
retain the unit coordinates of the local-uniformizer family. Uniform
finite scaling and local-uniformizer ideles have the same lattice index
but need not give the same nonradial external character. Their chart and
divisor maps require the stated unit corrections.

On the integral orbit lattice $V_n=\Z[T]/\Phi_n(T)$, P123.039 proves
\[
 \log\#H^0[V_n\xrightarrow{1-T}V_n]
 =\log\det(1-T)
 =\lim_{r\uparrow1}-\sum_{j\ge1}\frac{r^j\Tr(T^j)}j.
\]
Finite sums recover compact radial prime packets. This logarithmic
trace assignment is not itself a convolution representation.

P123.060 gives a necessary distinction: the integral graph of the normal
dilation $z\mapsto nz$ meets the diagonal in
\[
 [\Z[z]\xrightarrow{(n-1)z}\Z[z]],
 \qquad C_{\rm fix}(n)=[\Z\xrightarrow{n-1}\Z]
\]
for the transverse cotangent factor at zero. Ramification has differential
$n$. At $n=p^k$, the fixed factor is contractible over $\Z_p$, whereas
the primitive ramification contact has $\Z/p$. Direct identification of
these operations is refuted; the existing ramification contacts remain valid.

P123.061 constructs a common integral deformation
\[
 F_n(t,z)=\sum_{j=1}^n\binom nj t^{j-1}z^j,
 \quad F_m(t,F_n(t,z))=F_{mn}(t,z),\quad F_n(0,z)=nz.
\]
The primitive relative-fiber factor is $\Phi_n(1+tz)$, and its derived
unit contact is the constant perfect complex
$[\Z[t]\xrightarrow{\Phi_n(1)}\Z[t]]$. Prime-power information survives
as vertical torsion at $t=0$. This is a relative fiber, not the fixed
equation $F_n-z=0$; trace specialization is a further requirement.

\subsection{Row (c): normalized local distributions and actual operators}
P123.031 distinguishes internal Jacobian traces, which fail the proposed
chart compatibility, from external local-idele distributions. For additive
self-dual Haar on $\Q_p$, multiplicative unit volume one, and
$\chi_p=1_{\Z_p}$, P123.032--034,054,058 give
\[
 R_p(\phi)=\int_{\Q_p^*}(\phi(y)-\chi_p(y)\phi(0))\,d^*y,
 \quad
 \langle-\mathcal F\log|\cdot|_p,\phi\rangle
 =\log p\,R_p(\phi)+\frac{\log p}{p-1}\phi(0).
\]
For $a(x)=f(j_p(x))$, put
$L_p(a)(y)=|1-y|_p^{-1/2}a(1-y)$, extended by zero near $y=1$.
Then
\[
 W_p(f)=\log p\,R_p(L_p(a))+\frac{\log p}{p-1}f(1).
\]
This includes the unit counterterm; off the units it gives
$\log p\sum_{k\ne0}p^{-|k|/2}\int_{\Z_p^*}f(j_p(p^ku))\,du$.
Compact real-log support and finite unit level make the global finite-place
sum testwise finite. No infinite operator sum is silently interchanged.

P123.035 fixes the real Fourier normalization independently. For
$\mathcal F\phi(y)=\int\phi(x)e^{2\pi ixy}\,dx$, the off-zero density
of $\mathcal F\log|x|$ is $-1/(2|y|)$, not $-1/|y|$.
The resulting real multiplier is
$\log\pi-\operatorname{Re}\psi(1/4+i\tau/2)$.
An unhalved imported local coefficient cannot be combined with this
convention. This correction does not refute a complete nuclear theorem;
it requires matching its exact normalization before importing it.

P123.041--043 construct operators. The single-zero boundary orbit is
$Y_{S,p}=H_S/K_{S,p}$, a compact mapping torus of volume $\log p$.
Induce the normal unitary dilation
$D_xv(z)=|x|_p^{-1/2}v(z/x)$ from $K_{S,p}\simeq\Q_p^*$.
The induced action preserves external convolution. With position and
Fourier projections $E_{MN}=P_MQ_N$, $L=M+N\ge0$, the exact normal trace is
\[
 \Tr(E_{MN}D_x)=|x|_p^{1/2}\min(p^L,|1-x|_p^{-1}).
\]
The induced fiberwise compression $B_{p,MN}(f)$ is trace class: its
finite-unit-level, finite-normal-rank kernel is smooth on a compact real
rectangle and has an absolutely summable finite-rank expansion. For
sufficient $L$ depending on the test,
\[
 \Tr B_{p,MN}(f)-(L+1)(\log p)f(1)=W_p(f).
\]
P123.046 identifies all these uncut induced representations with the same
regular representation on $L^2(H_S)$. The prime-dependent geometric
cutoffs remain different and cannot be discarded under unitary equivalence.

P123.059 makes the local displacement operation composable. In
$\Q_p\setminus\{1\}$ its group law is $y\odot z=y+z-yz$ and its
transported convolution uses $\psi((y-z)/(1-z))$. The transported
involution is
$\phi^\sharp(y)=|1-y|_p^{-1}\overline{\phi(-y/(1-y))}$.
The normal graph representation preserves this product and involution.
This is not pointwise coefficient multiplication or a homomorphic
restriction of the full external test algebra.

\subsection{All finite strata, attaching maps and the trace domain}
P123.044 identifies the induced normal model with the actual open chart
where all coordinates other than $p$ are nonzero. Distinct such charts
overlap on the same generic orbit. Their union can have a non-Hausdorff
orbit quotient, so the group action must be retained in descent.
P123.045,048 construct strict rapid source sequences for the full filtration
by the number of zero finite coordinates. Every stratum has an explicit
compact transversal with its residual $\Z^{|J|-1}$ action. The first
double-zero example is an irrational rotation groupoid (P123.047).
Its nonzero transversal trace is not automatically its Weil contribution.

P123.049 computes the attaching defect of the explicit linear section:
$\omega(b,c)=s(b)s(c)-s(bc)$. For two primes and
$\gamma=p^mq^n$, its coefficient is
\[
 (\chi\alpha_\gamma(\chi)-\chi)g(t)h(t-\log\gamma),
 \qquad \chi=1_{\Z_p}1_{\Z_q}.
\]
The two shell decompositions into single-zero charts differ precisely by
the generic-overlap relation. P123.050 evaluates this defect by convergent
source boundary traces. If $\delta_p$ and $\delta_q$ are the label
valuation derivations and $\tau_B(b)=\int b_1(t)\,dt$, the induced cyclic
functionals satisfy
\[
 c_q(x,y)=-\tau_B(\delta_p(x)y),\quad
 c_p(x,y)=-\tau_B(\delta_q(x)y),\quad
 (\log p)c_q+(\log q)c_p=-\eta([x,y]),
\]
where $\eta(b)=\int t b_1(t)\,dt$. Ordinary trace positivity here is
not positivity of Weil's quadratic form.

P123.051--053 construct the stronger integrable completion on which these
identities are continuous, prove external and gauge stability, and adjoin
actual rapid gauge unitaries to obtain the completed bimodule tensor law.
Adding a new lattice coordinate does not preserve naive multiplicative
boundary $L^1$: its mass is infinite. P123.054 instead proves
\[
 R_p(\alpha_a\phi)=R_p(\phi)-v_p(a)\phi(0),
 \quad R_p(\chi_p)=0,\quad\operatorname{ev}_0(\chi_p)=1.
\]
The evaluation defect is retained. P123.055--056 assemble the relative
local Banach factors and real moment space into projective semilocal
coefficient algebras and rapid crossed products, with coherent injective
charts. Explicit bounded finite-rank approximations prove faithful
coefficient realization. P123.057 includes the two-prime relative algebra
in the earlier integrable source, preserving its quotient, section,
attaching defect and boundary traces. These are source constructions;
their global categorical shadow is still a required comparison.

\subsection{Local deformation of operators and remaining global scope}
P123.062 proves a local specialization result for the family $F_n$.
For $t\in p\Z_p$ when $p$ is odd, or $t\in4\Z_2$, the map
$H_t(z)=\log(1+tz)/t$ on $\Z_p$ is a bijective Haar-preserving isometry,
with $H_0(z)=z$ and $H_t(F_n(t,z))=nH_t(z)$.
It conjugates the compact-ball half-density semigroup operators and
commutes with projection onto functions constant modulo $p^N$. Their
finite-rank traces are therefore exactly
\[
 |n|_p^{1/2}\min(p^N,|1-n|_p^{-1}),
\]
throughout that parameter neighborhood. This excludes $t=1$ and is not
a global unitary power-map/dilation equivalence. Together with P123.061
it puts integral contacts and local operator traces on one explicit
family, but does not identify the two operations by mixed RR.

\subsection{Row (d): retained endpoint evidence and open sign law}
P123.036 matches the corrected quarter-Gamma normalization to the existing
paper-42 endpoint certificate and records a completed replay of its eight
jobs. The source hashes, logs, Arb enclosure check and report are archived
under \path{updates/2026-09-05/phase-123/endpoint_replay_20260905/}.
The recombined even margin exceeds $8.63386022095\cdot10^{-8}$ and the
odd margin exceeds $8.13856175478\cdot10^{-6}$ in the stated endpoint
certificate. This update does not rerun those computations or extend
their finite scope. Finite numerical experiments are not global sign proofs.

The first-contact/zero-flux implication or an independent global Hodge
inequality remains open. Harmonicity alone does not imply zero flux,
and a positive Gram decomposition cannot be assumed as the missing sign
input. The classical control also distinguishes effective correspondences
from positive-semidefinite convolution squares; Frobenius transpose gives
only the appropriately rationalized cycle inverse, not an integral kernel
inverse (P123.021). Earlier claims conflating these cones or inverses are
not used in the current argument.

\subsection{Acceptance gates after this update}
Row (a) still requires the common correspondence realization with the full
declared compatibility and descent contracts. Row (b) still requires its
integral primitive contact to be the contact of that same source operation.
Row (c) still requires global graph/diagonal localization, compatibility
with all attaching maps, the archimedean and nuclear comparison, and mixed
RR; the local scalar and operator equalities above do not imply these
categorical claims. Row (d) still requires the global sign argument.
The current results are substantive local constructions and corrected
comparisons, not a replacement of these four acceptance gates.
