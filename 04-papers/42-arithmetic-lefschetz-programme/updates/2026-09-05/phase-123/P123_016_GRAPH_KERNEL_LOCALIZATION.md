idele-class bimodules as graph kernels on the product of the semilocal

The result is for the graph-generated correspondence category and its

## 1. The quotient carrier and its square

For a semilocal chart `S`, let

```text
X_S=[A_S/Gamma_S]                                     (1.1)
```

be the topological groupoid stack of the rational `S`-unit action, and put

```text
Y_S=X_S x X_S.                                        (1.2)
```

The convolution structure algebra of the presentation groupoid is the
algebraic core

```text
S(A_S) rtimes Gamma_S,
```

and P123.006 is its complete rapid `C_0` kernel enlargement.  Thus
`Acal_S` is a chosen complete coefficient model of `X_S`, not a free label
algebra detached from a carrier.

## 2. External graph on the quotient stack

An idele lift `a in A_S^times` defines multiplication

```text
m_a:A_S->A_S, x |->a x.                               (2.1)
```

It commutes with the `Gamma_S` action and hence defines an automorphism
`m_[a]` of `X_S`.  If `a'=gamma a`, multiplication by `gamma` is a natural
2-isomorphism between the two quotient-stack maps.  Therefore the graph

```text
Graph_[a]:X_S->Y_S,
x |->(x,m_[a](x))                                     (2.2)
```

depends only on the idele class, up to a canonical coherent 2-isomorphism.

Let `K_[a]` be its structure kernel.  In the crossed-product presentation,
the left action on the graph is the usual one and the right action is
transported through the automorphism `beta_a`.  Hence

```text
K_[a] corresponds to {}_1 Acal_S_(beta_a)=E_[a].      (2.3)
```

This is obtained by writing the two anchor actions on functions; it is not
an appeal to an unspecified Morita equivalence.

## 3. Pull--tensor--push equals bimodule tensor

On `X_S x X_S x X_S`, the fiber product of two graph supports is

```text
{(x,y,z):y=m_u(x), z=m_v(y)} ~=X_S.                   (3.1)
```

The projection to `(x,z)` identifies it with `Graph_(vu)`.  The graph maps
are isomorphisms onto their images, so there is no higher excess term in
this composition.  Consequently

```text
Rp_13!(Lp_12^*K_u tensor^L Lp_23^*K_v)
 ~=K_(vu).                                             (3.2)
```

Under (2.3), the map in (3.2) is exactly

```text
x tensor y |->x beta_u(y),                            (3.3)
```

the completed balanced isomorphism of P123.014.  Thus geometric
pull--tensor--push and algebraic bimodule composition are the same
operation on the declared category.

## 4. Diagonal and transpose

For the identity class, (2.2) is the diagonal, and

```text
K_1=K_Delta corresponds to Acal_S=E_1.                (4.1)
```

It is the two-sided unit in both descriptions.  Swapping the two factors
of `Y_S` sends `Graph_u` to the transposed graph, which is the graph
correspondence of `u^-1`.  Hence

```text
K_u^dagger ~=K_(u^-1)                                 (4.2)
```

and (2.3) carries this to `E_u^dagger=E_(u^-1)`.

## 5. Compact superpositions

The Picard/Fell bundle of P123.015 is therefore the bundle of graph kernels

```text
u |->K_u on Y_S.                                      (5.1)
```

Its complete compact-section LF algebra is the completion of compactly
supported smooth/locally constant superpositions of actual graph kernels.
Convolution in that LF algebra is the continuous extension of (3.2), not a
new product declared on labels.

This proves the required faithful geometric localization on the
graph-generated sector: a section retains its coefficient kernel and its
external graph support.  Only after applying the quotient representation
do gauge-equivalent coefficient choices become invisible.

## 6. Restriction over `Spec Z`

For `S subset S'`, the map

```text
A_S ->A_(S'), x |->(x, compact-unit coordinates)      (6.1)
```

intertwines the presentation groupoids and the extended idele action.
It carries (2.2) to the graph of the extended class.  Therefore restriction
of graph kernels agrees with restriction of bimodules and of Fell sections
proved in P123.014--015.

The strict structure-kernel descent of P123.006 supplies the coefficient
equalizers.  The lift-change cocycle of P123.015 supplies the 2-descent of
external graphs.  Together they give a stack of graph-generated
correspondence categories over the finite-prime Zariski site.

## 7. Noncollapse and rulings

The square (1.2) is not collapsed to `X_S`: its two projections are
different anchor functors, and a graph kernel records both source and
target actions.  The external Tate realization of P123.014 compactifies
their two degree directions into the hyperbolic basis `e_-,e_+`, exchanged
by transpose.  Thus the ruling plane and the mixed graph sector arise from
the same source/target square.

This does not assert an isomorphism with the earlier spherical
`Y_S` notation of `Perf_IDN`; it supplies the concrete groupoid-stack
carrier which the stronger A contract required.  Comparison of determinant
cohomology, rather than existence of the square or localization of mixed
kernels, is the remaining relation with that earlier model.
