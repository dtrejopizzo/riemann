## 1. Internal algebra and external action

Let

```text
Acal_S=unitization(K_S^rapid)                          (1.1)
```

for the internal structure-kernel algebra of P123.006.  If
`a in A_S^times` is a semilocal idele lift, define

```text
beta_a(f_gamma U_gamma)=alpha_a(f_gamma)U_gamma,
alpha_a(f)(x)=f(a^-1 x).                              (1.2)
```

Multiplication by `a` commutes with multiplication by every
`gamma in Gamma_S`.  Consequently (1.2) is a continuous unital dagger
automorphism and

```text
beta_a beta_b=beta_(ab),
beta_a(F^dagger)=beta_a(F)^dagger.                    (1.3)
```

For `gamma_0 in Gamma_S`, the crossed-product relation gives

```text
beta_(gamma_0 a)=Ad(U_(gamma_0)) beta_a.              (1.4)
```

## 2. Twisted bimodules

For an automorphism `beta` of `Acal_S`, let

```text
E_beta={}_1 Acal_S_beta                                (2.1)
```

be `Acal_S` as a complete left module, with right action

```text
x dot c=x beta(c).                                    (2.2)
```

Multiplication defines a continuous balanced isomorphism

```text
mu_(beta,gamma):E_beta completed_tensor_Acal E_gamma
                 ->E_(beta gamma),
x tensor y |->x beta(y).                              (2.3)
```

The inverse is `z -> z tensor 1`.  Direct substitution in (2.3) proves
the pentagon strictly: both triple products send
`x tensor y tensor z` to `x beta(y) beta gamma(z)`.

The unit is `E_id=Acal_S`.  The conjugate-dual bimodule satisfies

```text
E_beta^dagger ~=E_(beta^-1).                          (2.4)
```

## 3. Independence of the idele lift

Let `u=[a] in C_(Q,S)=Gamma_S backslash A_S^times` and put

```text
E_u=E_(beta_a).                                       (3.1)
```

If `a'=gamma_0 a`, (1.4) and

```text
Phi_(gamma_0):E_(Ad(U_gamma0) beta_a)->E_(beta_a),
x |->x U_(gamma_0)                                   (3.2)
```

give a bimodule isomorphism.  Right compatibility follows from

```text
x Ad(U)(beta_a(c)) U=x U beta_a(c).
```

The relations `U_gamma U_delta=U_(gamma delta)` make (3.2) coherent under
two changes of lift.  Hence (3.1) is canonical up to a specified coherent
isomorphism, exactly the correct notion for a correspondence object.

### Theorem 3.1 — external correspondence law

For idele classes `u,v`,

```text
E_u completed_tensor_Acal E_v ~=E_(uv),
E_u^dagger ~=E_(u^-1),
E_1 ~=Acal_S.                                         (3.3)
```

### Proof

Choose lifts and use (2.3)--(2.4).  Equations (1.4) and (3.2) show that the
result is independent of both choices.  Their multiplicative coherence
also proves associativity on classes.  QED.

This is the external analogue of graph composition.  Unlike the retracted
identification `U_gamma=E_[gamma]`, it does not collapse: `E_[gamma]=E_1`
because `[gamma]=1`, while a nontrivial idele class gives a genuinely
external automorphism bimodule.

## 4. Restriction between semilocal charts

For `S subset S'`, extend an idele lift by unit components at the new
places.  Coefficient restriction from P123.006 intertwines the two
automorphisms:

```text
res beta_a=beta_(a,1) res.                            (4.1)
```

It therefore sends `E_[a]` to `E_[(a,1)]` and commutes with (2.3), dagger
and the lift-change maps (3.2).  If the enlarged unit group makes a class
trivial, (3.2) supplies the required trivialization rather than falsely
claiming injectivity of the class map.

Thus `S |-> Corr_ext,S`, the additive idempotent completion generated by
the `E_u`, is a Zariski-compatible prestack of complete correspondence
categories.  Effective descent for arbitrary completed objects is not yet
claimed; it will be imposed on the compact-section completion below.

## 5. Compactly supported superpositions

The fibers `E_u` with multiplication (3.3) form a saturated Fell/Picard
bundle over the locally compact abelian group `C_(Q,S)`.  Let

```text
L_S^c=C_c^infinity(C_(Q,S),E)                         (5.1)
```

mean smooth in the real module direction, locally constant in the totally
disconnected directions, compactly supported, complete-fiber-valued
sections.  Define

```text
(F star G)(w)=integral_(uv=w) mu_(u,v)(F(u),G(v))d^*u,
F^dagger(u)=F(u^-1)^dagger.                           (5.2)
```

Fubini and the pentagon for (2.3) give associativity; Haar inversion and
(2.4) give the dagger law.  The delta section at the identity is a
multiplier and represents the diagonal bimodule `E_1`.

There is generally no canonical vector `1_(E_u)`: changing an idele lift
sends the vector `1` to the gauge multiplier `U_gamma`.  Thus a scalar test
`h` does not define a canonical element of the faithful Fell algebra
without a splitting or the corresponding line twist.

After passing to the quotient representation, however, every gauge
multiplier acts as the identity by P123.013.  Choosing arbitrary lifts and
integrating their represented bimodules therefore gives a choice-independent
operator

```text
theta(h)=integral h(u)theta(u)d^*u,                   (5.3)
```

the exact source operator used in P123.004, P123.007 and P123.012.  Thus
the previously separate external test algebra is a canonical **represented
ray** of the bimodule carrier.  A faithful internal lift of that ray is an
additional descent datum and is not silently asserted.

The lift/splitting issue and the LF/bornological completion of (5.1) must
still be matched to the trace-class seminorms of the semilocal theorem.
Until that is proved, only the compact Fell core and its canonical quotient
representation, not a faithful scalar embedding or maximal completed Fell
algebra, are asserted.

## 6. The true external Tate realization

The semilocal module satisfies

```text
|gamma|_S=1  for gamma in Gamma_S                     (6.1)
```

by the product formula, because an `S`-unit has unit absolute value outside
`S`.  Therefore

```text
rho_T^ext(u)=(|u|_S^(-1/2),|u|_S^(1/2))               (6.2)
```

is well defined on the idele **class**, independent of its lift.  It is
multiplicative and

```text
rho_T^ext(u^-1)=rho_T^ext(u)^sharp                    (6.3)
```

for the swap involution of P123.008.

With `e_-=(1,0),e_+=(0,1)`, the form

```text
beta_T(z,w)=z_- conjugate(w_+)+z_+ conjugate(w_-)
```

still has matrix

```text
[[0,1],[1,0]].                                        (6.4)
```

Unlike the internal map of P123.008, (6.2) is trivial on gauge arrows and
therefore descends to the physical quotient.  It supplies the actual
external two-ruling degree realization required by A.
