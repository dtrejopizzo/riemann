## Status

`PROVED-IN-REPOSITORY` for the graph-generated perfect-kernel comparison on
a smooth projective curve over a finite field: composition, unit,
transpose, derived diagonal contact, numerical equivalence, effectivity and
the hyperbolic ruling plane recover their classical meanings.

`IMPORTED` only for the standard existence of derived pullback, proper
pushforward, projection formula and derived base change for perfect kernels
on smooth proper schemes.  The load-bearing Frobenius graph calculations
are given directly below.

This is a control theorem for the architecture of row A.  It is not a
specialization of `Spec Z` to characteristic `p`, and it does not import the
finite-field Hodge sign into the number-field problem.

## 1. Classical kernel carrier

Let `C` be a smooth projective geometrically connected curve over
`k=F_Q`, and put `Y=C x_k C`.  For perfect kernels `K,L in Perf(Y)`, define

```text
K star L=Rp_13*(Lp_12^*K tensor^L Lp_23^*L)           (1.1)
```

on `C x C x C`.  Smoothness and properness make the three projections
perfect/proper; hence (1.1) is again perfect.  Derived base change and the
projection formula give associativity.  The diagonal kernel `O_Delta` is a
two-sided unit.

Let `sigma(x,y)=(y,x)` and define the correspondence transpose

```text
K^dagger=sigma^*K.                                    (1.2)
```

Permuting the first and third factors in the triple product gives

```text
(K star L)^dagger ~=L^dagger star K^dagger,
O_Delta^dagger ~=O_Delta.                             (1.3)
```

The stable idempotent-complete subcategory generated by finite
correspondence graphs, their transposes and `O_Delta` is therefore closed
under all the operations used below.

## 2. Graph composition

For a finite endomorphism `f:C->C`, write `Gamma_f subset C x C` for the
graph with the convention `(x,f(x))`.  The fiber product appearing in
(1.1) is

```text
{(x,y,z):y=f(x), z=g(y)} ~=C,                         (2.1)
```

and `p_13` identifies it with `Gamma_(g o f)`.  Therefore

```text
O_(Gamma_f) star O_(Gamma_g) ~=O_(Gamma_(g o f)).     (2.2)
```

For the geometric Frobenius `F`, this gives

```text
O_(Gamma_(F^m)) star O_(Gamma_(F^n))
 ~=O_(Gamma_(F^(m+n))).                               (2.3)
```

If multiplicative labels are written `n=Q^m`, (2.3) is exactly
`Gamma_n star Gamma_r=Gamma_(nr)`.  Formula (1.2) sends a graph to its
transposed correspondence, exactly as the rational-dilation dagger sends
`q` to `q^-1`.

## 3. Derived diagonal contact

Define the categorical diagonal trace object

```text
Tr_C(K)=R Gamma(C,LDelta^*K).                          (3.1)
```

For a graph kernel, `LDelta^*O_(Gamma_f)` is the derived intersection of
the graph and diagonal.  At a fixed point `x`, its normal/excess complex is

```text
[T_x C --(1-df_x)--> T_x C].                          (3.2)
```

This is the exact finite-field counterpart of P123.009
`[N --(1-q^-1)--> N]`.

For `f=F^n`, `dF^n=0`.  Hence (3.2) is acyclic off degree zero with unit
normal determinant, the intersection is transverse and reduced, and

```text
chi Tr_C(O_(Gamma_(F^n)))
 =deg(Gamma_(F^n).Delta)
 =#C(F_(Q^n)).                                          (3.3)
```

Thus the trace is not appended to the Frobenius label: it is derived from
graph contact with the diagonal, exactly as demanded in the new A/B split.

## 4. Numerical equivalence and effectivity

On the Grothendieck group of the graph-generated category define

```text
I_C([K],[L])=chi Tr_C(K star L^dagger).                (4.1)
```

Its radical is stable under composition and dagger by cyclicity of the
kernel trace, so quotienting gives the usual numerical correspondence
space on this subcategory.  Under the cycle map from perfect graph kernels,
(4.1) is ordinary intersection multiplicity.  Therefore its radical is
ordinary numerical equivalence restricted to the graph-generated cycles.

Integral graph cycles and their finite nonnegative sums give the classical
effective cone.  A graph section and its adjoint produce the kernel square
analogue used in P123.010.  Hence the source-square cone specializes to
effective graph correspondences on the control subcategory; no assertion
is made that every analytic `C*`-positive element is an algebraic cycle.

## 5. The two rulings

For a rational point after a harmless finite base extension, let

```text
F_v=C x {x_0},
F_h={x_0} x C.
```

Their intersection numbers are

```text
F_v^2=F_h^2=0,
F_v.F_h=F_h.F_v=1.                                   (5.1)
```

Thus their matrix is

```text
[[0,1],[1,0]],                                        (5.2)
```

identical to the Tate/Rosati plane of P123.008.  Transpose exchanges the
two rulings.

## 7. What this control does not prove

The theorem does not prove:

1. that the semilocal identity trace is a perfect determinant;
2. convergence of infinite arithmetic correspondence sums;
3. a global comparison with Meyer's quotient;
4. an arithmetic Hodge-index theorem.

Those are genuinely number-field/topological issues absent from the finite
field model.  Passing this control test closes A.9; it does not close A.6 or
A.8 by analogy.
