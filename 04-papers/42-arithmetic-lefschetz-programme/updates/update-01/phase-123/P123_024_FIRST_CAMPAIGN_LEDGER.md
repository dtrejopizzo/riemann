## 1. What has actually changed

The two ill-defined maps of P123.018 now have rigorously typed replacements
on a declared, restricted category. The old minimal unitization is replaced
by a larger rapid coefficient algebra; raw idele extension is replaced by
valuation-normalized transition. Neither repair proves full row A.

The minimal B test has a decisive result: the existing cyclotomic integral
contact survives, but a purely complex source cannot supply its nonzero
integral torsion through an additive contact functor. A genuine integral
source and analytic realization are required by the strong contract.

The classical cone test also fails: source squares and effective graph
cycles differ even on two points. This reopens the former A.9 cone claim.

## 4. Next concrete construction

The next target is **not** another completion of A_S^u alone. It is an
integral exact correspondence category with a complex analytic realization:

1. retain the integral Witt/endomorphism source and its distinguished
   cyclic/lambda-characteristic construction;

3. construct a geometric contact/shadow and prove how it removes the
   known horizontal excess, retaining derived Tor data;

   exactly which information complexification loses and a metrized
   determinant retains;

ends of a missing functor, not its existence proof.
