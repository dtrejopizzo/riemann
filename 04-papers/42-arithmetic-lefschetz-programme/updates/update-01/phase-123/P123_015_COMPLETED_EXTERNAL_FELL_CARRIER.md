## 1. The lift cocycle

Write

```text
1 ->Gamma_S ->A_S^times ->C_(Q,S) ->1.                (1.1)
```

The subgroup `Gamma_S` is discrete.  The quotient map therefore has local
continuous sections.  Choose such a section `s` on a chart of `C_(Q,S)`.
For composable points define

```text
c_s(u,v)=s(u)s(v)s(uv)^(-1) in Gamma_S.               (1.2)
```

Associativity in `A_S^times` gives the cocycle identity

```text
c_s(u,v)c_s(uv,w)=c_s(v,w)c_s(u,vw).                 (1.3)
```

Inside the multiplier algebra use `omega_s(u,v)=U_(c_s(u,v))`.  Formula
(1.3) is exactly the associativity constraint of the external bimodules in
P123.014.

If `s'(u)=b(u)s(u)` with `b(u) in Gamma_S`, then

```text
c_(s')(u,v)=b(u)b(v)c_s(u,v)b(uv)^(-1),               (1.4)
```

and multiplication by `U_(b(u))` is the change-of-trivialization map.
Thus different splittings give isomorphic twisted section algebras, with
the isomorphisms satisfying the evident cocycle on triple overlaps.

## 2. Intrinsic compact sections

Let `E ->C_(Q,S)` be the Picard/Fell bundle with fiber `E_u` from
P123.014.  Define

```text
L_S^c=D_c(C_(Q,S),E)                                  (2.1)
```

intrinsically as compactly supported sections which are smooth along the
real module coordinate and locally constant along totally disconnected
coordinates.  A local splitting identifies (2.1) with compactly supported
`Acal_S`-valued tests and turns convolution into the cocycle formula

```text
(F star_s G)(w)
 =integral_(uv=w) F(u) beta_(s(u))(G(v))
                   omega_s(u,v)d^*u.                 (2.2)
```

Changing the splitting by (1.4) conjugates (2.2) with the continuous
fiberwise gauge map, so (2.1) and its product are independent of `s`.
The dagger is the corresponding inverse-fiber map from P123.014.

## 3. Complete LF topology

For a compact set `K` contained in one finite trivializing cover and a
fixed finite level `N` of local constancy in the totally disconnected
directions, give the sections supported in `K` the completed projective
topology generated by:

1. the usual smooth derivative seminorms in the real directions;
2. the fixed level-`N` locally constant norm in the nonarchimedean
   directions;
3. the Frechet seminorms of `Acal_S` on the fiber coefficients.

Denote this complete Frechet space by `L_(S,K,N)`.  Exhaust the
second-countable base by compact sets and the locally constant functions by
finite levels, and put

```text
L_S=indlim_(K,N) L_(S,K,N).                           (3.1)
```

The inclusions are closed support/finite-level inclusions, so (3.1) is a
complete strict LF space.  On fixed support and level, (2.2) is a continuous
bilinear map by
the fiber estimates of P123.006, continuity of the action and the compact
Haar integral.  Support satisfies

```text
supp(F star G) subset supp(F)supp(G).                 (3.2)
```

The universal property of the strict LF limit therefore makes convolution
and dagger continuous on `L_S`.  The identity bimodule is a multiplier
delta section, as expected for a nondiscrete external group.

No nuclearity is asserted for `L_S`, because its `C_0(A_S)` coefficient
fiber is not nuclear as a Frechet algebra.

## 4. Canonical scalar trace ray after gauge quotient

In the quotient representation of P123.013,

```text
U_gamma |->1  (gamma in Gamma_S).                     (4.1)
```

Hence every cocycle multiplier `omega_s(u,v)` maps to one and every
change-of-splitting multiplier `U_(b(u))` maps to one.  The represented
scalar subquotient of (2.1) is consequently canonical and equals

```text
D_S=D_c(C_(Q,S)),                                     (4.2)
```

with ordinary group convolution and modular dagger.  A test `h` acts by

```text
theta(h)=integral h(u)theta(u)d^*u.                   (4.3)
```

This resolves the apparent missing scalar section of P123.014: it is not a
faithful section before quotienting, but it is a canonical descent object
in precisely the representation where the semilocal trace is defined.

The space `D_S` is a complete nuclear LF algebra.  Thus A contains a
nuclear trace-class admissibility sector even though its ambient kernel
algebra is larger and nonnuclear.

## 5. Trace and determinant continuity

For every fixed compact support, the local fixed-point distributions in
the semilocal theorem have finite distribution order.  Therefore

```text
T_S:D_S->C                                             (5.1)
```

is continuous on every Frechet step and hence on the LF limit.  Convolution
is jointly continuous, so

```text
I_S(f,g)=T_S(f star g^dagger)                         (5.2)
```

is a continuous Hermitian form.

P123.012 gives

```text
d_S^rel(h)=exp(T_S(h)).                               (5.3)
```

The exponential is continuous, so the relative determinant character and
its biextension are continuous on `D_S`.  This proves determinant
continuity on the **complete nuclear trace ray**; it does not assert a trace
on every coefficient-valued element of `L_S`.

## 6. Restriction and descent

The coefficient restrictions of P123.006 and the idele extension maps of
P123.014 carry lift cocycles to lift cocycles and changes of lift to inner
gauge changes.  Therefore they induce continuous functors between the Fell
section categories and commute with convolution/dagger.

After (4.1), the restriction is the usual semilocal restriction of test
functions.  The source theorem proves support stabilization of (5.1), so
the trace and determinant glue after the canonical finite set of
contributing places has entered.  This is trace descent, not a claim that
the raw local numerical summands are individually invariant.


This closes the topology/completion part of A.2 and the finite-test

third would be a stronger optional theorem because A.5 permits a declared
trace-class domain.
