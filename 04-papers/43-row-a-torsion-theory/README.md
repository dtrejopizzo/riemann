# A correspondence algebra with a canonical trace on the idele class group

For a finite set of places `S` of `Q` containing the real place and at least one prime, the paper
studies the graph bimodules of idele classes on the semilocal adelic space, with their composition
and involution, and computes their Hochschild complexes up to homotopy equivalence. On the
convolution algebra of the semilocal idele class group it defines a trace as a sum of local
fixed-point distributions and computes its Fourier symbol. The Hermitian form attached to the
trace is nondegenerate and, in the normalization with zero constant term, has infinite index of
both signs. The two Tate characters give the bidegree and define the primitive subspace; the two
ruling classes are limits of test functions in a completion.
