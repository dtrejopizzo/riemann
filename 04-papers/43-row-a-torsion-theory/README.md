# A correspondence algebra on the idele class group

For a finite set of places `S` of `Q`, the paper studies the graph bimodules of idele classes
on the semilocal adelic space, their composition and involution, and their Hochschild complexes.
On the convolution algebra of the semilocal idele class group it defines a trace as a sum of
local fixed-point distributions, computes its Fourier symbol, and proves that the associated
Hermitian form is nondegenerate with infinite index of both signs. The two Tate characters give
the bidegree and define the primitive subspace; the two ruling classes are constructed in a
completion.
