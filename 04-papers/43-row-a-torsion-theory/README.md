# A correspondence algebra and the Weil distribution on semilocal idele class groups

For a finite set of places `S` of `Q` containing the real place and a prime, `C_S` is the quotient of
the semilocal idele group by the positive `S`-units, a twofold cover of the `S`-idele class group of
Connes and Meyer. The paper has two independent parts.

- **Graph bimodules.** The graph bimodules of idele classes over a crossed product, with their
  composition and involution; each lattice part of their Hochschild complex is computed up to
  homotopy equivalence, using a twisted excision theorem. An appendix treats the analogous
  composition and duality for graphs of automorphisms of a curve.
- **The functional `T`.** On `C_c^∞(C_S)`, `T` is the sum over `S` of the local terms of Weil's
  explicit formula in Connes's form, plus a free constant at the identity. The paper computes its
  Fourier transform, proves that the Hermitian form `T(F ⋆ G†)` is nondegenerate, determines its
  signature, describes a completion containing the two ruling classes, and treats the cone of
  non-negative functions, a determinant relative to `T`, the Riemann–Roch pairing, and the limit
  over `S`.
