# Paper 18 — The localized Weil form and its Kreĭn–Pontryagin index: localization, obstruction, and rigidity

The paper gives a functional-analytic anatomy of the localized Weil form Q, whose negative index κ counts nontrivial zeros in one open half of the critical strip, and of the problem of carrying κ by an operator. Q is defined autonomously on even real test functions through an entire transform. Each off-line zero acts through an off-axis evaluation functional contributing a Lorentzian block, while each on-line zero contributes a nonnegative square. From this localization the paper derives obstruction theorems for realizations of κ, the mechanism (bilinear vs sesquilinear) and the count κ = 2m, and a rigidity analysis of the de Branges axioms. Nothing depends on or proves RH.

## Main results
- `thm:loc` (Localization of the negative directions): Q = Q_on + Q_off with on-line squares and off-line Lorentzian blocks. PROVED.
- `prop:weights`, `thm:modular`: modular-surface resonance pairings are half-plane definite for every zero configuration. PROVED (weights NUMERICAL for the first fifteen zeros).
- `thm:A`, `thm:B`, `thm:C`: positive kernels, one-sided Cauchy kernels, and smooth non-degenerate families in β cannot carry κ. PROVED.
- `cor:class`: the excluded class and the two escape routes (indefinite kernel or degeneration at the line). PROVED.
- `prop:vs`, `thm:E`: sesquilinear evaluation forms are positive; bilinear ones have signature (1,1) off-axis. PROVED.
- κ = 2m: PROVED in the finite-index regime via an independence lemma and the de Branges Pontryagin-index theorem (CITED).
- Identification E = E_ξ for finite-defect realizations: OPEN.
- `prop:sym`: the symmetry data of any realization is unconditional. PROVED.
