# Sent material

This folder keeps material that has been sent outside the repository, such as notes prepared for
correspondence with other researchers. Each subfolder holds one sent item together with its
drafts and the review and proofreading reports produced before sending. The folder is an archive;
the mathematics it summarises lives in the research phases and papers.

## Contents

- `connes-semilocal-sonin/` — a short note, "A semilocal Sonin space and a finite reduction of
  window positivity: notes on a construction of Connes and Consani", with its LaTeX sources,
  compiled PDF and review reports.
