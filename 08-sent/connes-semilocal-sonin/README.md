# Note on a semilocal Sonin space

This folder holds a short note (about 10 pages) prepared for correspondence with A. Connes:
"A semilocal Sonin space and a finite reduction of window positivity: notes on a construction of
Connes and Consani". The note presents the semilocal Sonin chain and the reduction of window
positivity to a finite matrix condition, both taken from the row D research record, with honest
status labels. The folder keeps the sent version and the earlier stages that led to it.

## Contents

- `main.tex` — final LaTeX source of the note.
- `main.pdf` — compiled version of `main.tex`.
- `main_pre_proofread.tex` — version before the line-by-line proofreading pass.
- `main_first_draft.tex` — first draft.
- `REVIEW.md` — critical review of the draft against its proved sources (status of each theorem,
  open checks).
- `PROOFREAD.md` — line-by-line proofreading list, by severity (mathematics, symbols and
  citations, LaTeX, English), with exact replacements.
