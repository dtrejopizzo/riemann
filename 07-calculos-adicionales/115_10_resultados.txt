======================================================================
1. THE NUMBER THAT GOVERNS THE WHOLE DRAWING
======================================================================
  113 * 2pi = 709.999939711
  710       = 710
  delta     = 710 - 113*2pi = +6.028871e-05 rad
  Advancing p by 710 rotates the point by exactly delta radians.
  delta does NOT depend on the ray: hence the rays are congruent.

======================================================================
2. THE 280 RAYS ARE THE SAME CURVE (prediction with nothing fitted)
======================================================================
  prediction:  dtheta(p) = delta*(p - p mod 710)/710
  maximal deviation over the 100000 primes: 3.901e-11 rad
  (the width of a ray is 2pi/710 = 8.850e-03 rad, 2.3e+08 times larger)
  They do not merely resemble each other: they agree to machine precision.

======================================================================
3. HOW MUCH THEY BEND
======================================================================
  at the edge of the drawing (p = 1299709):
     accumulated turn = 0.110363 rad = 6.323 degrees
     width of a ray = 0.008850 rad
     => 12.47 ray widths, in the same direction for all 280
  This is exactly 'they all have the same wave and bend at the end'.

======================================================================
4. HOW MANY RAYS, AND THE ONLY PART THAT IS ABOUT THE PRIMES
======================================================================
  classes mod 710 occupied by primes: 283
  phi(710) = 280   (710 = 2*5*71)
  occupied classes NOT coprime to 710: [2, 5, 71]  (the primes 2, 5, 71 themselves)
  With ALL integers there would be 710 rays.  The primes use 280:
  the 430 missing ones are the classes sharing a factor with 710.
  That -- and only that -- is what the drawing says about the primes.
  That the 280 shine evenly is Dirichlet's theorem.

======================================================================
5. WHY THE DRAWING CHANGES FAMILY WITH THE RADIUS
======================================================================
     6/1    delta=-2.8319e-01  breaks up at r ~           22
    19/3    delta=+1.5044e-01  breaks up at r ~           42
    25/4    delta=-1.3274e-01  breaks up at r ~           47
    44/7    delta=+1.7703e-02  breaks up at r ~          355
   333/53   delta=-8.8213e-03  breaks up at r ~          712
   710/113  delta=+6.0289e-05  breaks up at r ~      104,218
  the drawing reaches r = 1,299,709
  Hence: near the centre the families 6 and 44 dominate (they break up
  quickly), further out the family 710 dominates, and only beyond r ~ 10^5
  does 710 start to bend visibly.  None of this is about the primes:
  these are the convergents of 2pi.
