## Known mechanisms

The following classical and modern mechanisms are background, not closure:

- Li criterion and the Bombieri--Lagarias extension identify positivity of
  all Li coefficients with the zero-location statement.
- Voros gives sharp asymptotic and sensitivity information for the
  Keiper--Li sequence.
- Coffey-type explicit formulae express Li coefficients through prime-power
  and Gamma terms, often using Laguerre polynomials.
- Explicit formula and PNT literature provide admissible tail inputs for A0.
- Pair-correlation improvements of PNT errors are conditional inputs and are
  not a proof of A1.
