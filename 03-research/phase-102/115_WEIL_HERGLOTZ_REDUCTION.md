# Weil-Herglotz reduction of the A1 sign

## Purpose

This document tests whether the A1 core can be closed by a standard positive
kernel principle. The answer is precise: the relevant positivity is exactly
the Li/Weil positivity restricted to the A1 test family. Thus this route is
allowed, but it still needs a new proof of that restricted positivity from
Euler--Gamma data.

## Completed logarithmic derivative

Let
\[
  \Xi_\log(s)={\xi'\over\xi}(s).
\]

The functional equation gives
\[
  \Xi_\log(s)=-\Xi_\log(1-s).
\]

If all zeros lie on the critical line, then after shifting to the line
coordinate \(s=1/2+z\), the logarithmic derivative is a Herglotz-type
boundary transform of a symmetric real divisor. Pairing conjugate zeros gives
positive Weil forms for the standard admissible test class.

## The A1 test family

The Li coefficient can be recovered from the finite-order kernel
\[
  \mathcal K_n(\rho)=1-\left(1-{1\over\rho}\right)^n.
\]

The paired arithmetic expression
\[
  \lambda_n^{\rm prime}
  =
  \lim_{\varepsilon\downarrow0}
  \left[-n+\int_1^\infty(\psi(y)-y)f'_{n,\varepsilon}(y)\,dy\right]
\]
is the Euler--Gamma side of this same test.

After A0, the tail has been removed. The remaining compact test is
\[
  C_n=
  -n+\int_1^{e^{T_n}}(\psi(y)-y)f'_{n,0}(y)\,dy
  +{3\over4}\lambda_n^{\rm arch}.
\]

Thus a Herglotz/Weil proof of A1 must prove positivity on precisely the
restricted family generated by the Li kernels \(\mathcal K_n\), with the A0
tail budget included.

## Equivalence warning

If the proof assumes the divisor is real, the positivity is immediate:
\[
  \lambda_n
  =
  4\sum_{\gamma>0}\sin^2\left({n\theta_\gamma\over2}\right)\ge0.
\]

But that is the conclusion. Therefore a Herglotz proof may not start from
real divisor support or from positivity of the Weil form.

The live theorem must be:

For the zeta Euler--Gamma data, the Weil/Herglotz form is nonnegative on the
Li test family \(\mathcal K_n\) for every \(n\ge8\), proved without assuming
the divisor is real.

## What would close A1

A sufficient theorem is:

There exists a zeta-specific Herglotz representation
\[
  \Xi_\log(1/2+z)=A(z)+\int_{\mathbb R}{1+tz\over t-z}\,d\mu(t),
\]
with \(\mu\ge0\), whose construction uses only the Euler product, Gamma factor,
functional equation and paired boundary prescription.

Such a theorem would force the divisor to be real and hence close Omega7.
It is therefore a valid force-RH target.

## Obstruction found

The Euler product gives direct positivity only in a right half-plane of
absolute convergence. A1 needs a boundary statement after continuation. The
missing step is exactly the positivity-preserving continuation from the
Euler-product half-plane to the Li boundary.

This is not supplied by the standard Herglotz theorem unless the required
positive measure is already constructed.

## Status

The Herglotz/Weil route is reduced to construction of a positive
Euler--Gamma representing measure at the Li boundary. No such construction is
present in the phase. The route remains open and equivalent to the A1 sign
theorem.
