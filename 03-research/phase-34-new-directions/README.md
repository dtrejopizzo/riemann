# Phase 34 — New Directions (A–D)

Starting from the unresolved "Hadamard Barrier" of Phase 33 (no known property of $\zeta$, independent of the zero positions, forces the central object $d\nu=0$), this phase opens four independent directions in search of such a property: (A) external arithmetic information via the explicit formula, (B) a Pontryagin-space / indefinite-metric reformulation of the problem, (C) the Jacobi spectral inverse problem, and (D) second-variation / variational-minimizer analysis of the distance to the critical manifold $\mathcal M$.

## Results
- Direction A (explicit formula as a source of external arithmetic information): the Weil–Guinand formula is symmetric and bijective between primes and zeros, so it cannot supply genuinely new information about the zeros; no arithmetic analogue of Weil's geometric intersection-positivity (used in the function-field case) is found. (NO-GO, structural obstruction)
- Direction B (Pontryagin space, negative index of $H_C$; deepened across three layers: condition C5/finiteness of off-critical zeros, dissipativity, characteristic function and Krein's theorem): structural reformulations built, no closure reached. (OPEN)
- Direction C (Jacobi spectral inverse problem): formulated as an alternative route; not carried to a definite result in this phase. (OPEN)
- Direction D (second variation of the variational distance to $\mathcal M$; deepened via gap conditions ND1–ND4, global uniqueness of the minimizer, and the "shadow minimizer" $g^*$ with its Euler–Lagrange equations): under ¬RH the on-critical candidate $f_0^{\mathrm{on}}$ does not satisfy the Euler–Lagrange condition and is shown to be only a local, not global, minimum; a canonical uniqueness conjecture for the shadow minimizer is formulated but not proved. (CONDITIONAL / OPEN)
