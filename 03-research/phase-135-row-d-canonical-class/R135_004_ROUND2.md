**Diagnosis of the round-1 `P2` failure (two causes, both fixed).** (1) The
archimedean integral was a tanh–sinh rule over `[−80,80]` whose integrand
carried the oscillatory factor `n^{−iτ}` (period `2π/\log n ≈ 4.5` at `n = 4`)
and was itself a nested tanh–sinh quadrature; it is now a fixed composite
Gauss–Legendre panel rule of width `1`, and the `ζ`-dependent node data
`Ξ(τ)` and `\mathrm{Re}\,ψ(¼+iτ/2)−\log π` are computed once in `mpmath` at 25
digits. (2) **The dominant cause**: the prime sum was truncated at `n = 12`,
which is right for `a = 0` but not for a translate — a translate by `\log n`
shifts the effective cut-off by a factor `n`, and the omitted terms at `n = 4`
were of size `10^{−6}`. The cut-off is now `n = 160`. `P3` additionally needed
`τ_{\max} = 400` rather than `70`, because `\hat h = |Φ_F|²` for `P3` carries
no `Ξ(τ)` damping.

## 4. Remaining weak points (its own list, nothing concealed)

1. **(23) is the real hole.** §13's route closure now holds only on finite
   truncations of `Λ_W`. Remark 13.6b argues the round-1 mechanism *cannot* be
   repaired — adjacent prime powers give nearly parallel generators, so
   `B(m) = O(m)` should not be expected — but it offers no substitute. The
   missing quantity is a bound on `ρ_Q` of Lemma 13.5a uniform in the rank.
2. **(20) satisfiability.** Theorem 12.3 remains a one-directional reduction
   with no example, under RH or in any model, showing (a)+(b′)+(c) is
   consistent. This is the single most important thing Part II does not know.
3. **(22)** is now open again: nothing here excludes a functional of `H⁰(E)`.
4. **(21)** the kernel representation of `ω_∞` on `D^W` is unproved. Nothing
   depends on it after the M9 repair, but Remark 11.14a′ is dead weight until
   it is settled.
5. **Lemma 13.5b is weaker than it looks.** The functionals `Φ_E(σ) ≥ 0` give
   one-sided bounds in complementary `σ`-ranges and do not enclose a bounded
   set; the report tried and failed to extract a two-sided coefficient bound
   from them. That failure is why (23) is open.
6. **Proposition 12.4B leans on `106_11`.** The degree bump `D ↦ D+4` is
   argued at the level of which estimate absorbs the factor; it was not
   re-derived from `106_11` (27)–(30) line by line. If an auditor wants that
   checked, it is the weakest line in §12.3.
7. **Numerics are `float64`.** The floor is `10^{−14}`–`10^{−15}`; round 1's
   `mpmath` figures at `10^{−18}` and `10^{−26}` survive only where
   `polya_class.py` (still `mpmath`) produces them.
8. **Restatement 12.4a's hypothesis class is informal.** "A derivation that
   uses only (12.3)" is not a formal proof-theoretic notion, and the schema is
   therefore a methodological principle, not a metatheorem. It is labelled
   `RESTATEMENT (schema)` for exactly that reason, and it should not be leaned
   on harder than that label permits.
9. **§1.13's amendment changes a compliance claim.** It is now a claim about
   what is *proved*, not about what is *mentioned*. A reader who wants the
   original, stronger-sounding, and false claim will not find it.
