# Selection: powers and falling factorials

Status: no candidate ready for construction; independent cross-check completed (PASS).

## Prior cross-check

The change between powers and falling factorials, given by
Stirling matrices, was considered to couple divisibility and the gamma part of the complete form.
No sign identity beyond that change of basis has been written.

Antecedents consulted:

- Master index, Part V, «Selección de cuadrados mixtos divisoriales y cocientes
  factoriales colectivos»: the divisorial change preserves the sign obligation;
  explicit quadratic exponents, positive valuations and the complete
  identification with the form, including regularization and limits, are missing.
- `../phase-101-covariant-bordered-current/E101_090_ENDPOINT_TOEPLITZ_HANKEL_SEPARATION.md`,
  §§23–25: binomial carries as postcomposition of the divisorial incidence.
  Closure requires a signed identity different from that postcomposition.
- `../phase-103-direct-a1-closure/103_16_EXPONENTIAL_COORDINATE_PRINGSHEIM_NO_GO.md`,
  §1: Stirling transformations on another object. Its exclusion does not transfer to
  the present proposal merely because the matrix shares the name.
- `../phase-106-global-modular-star-audit/106_117_POSTSHORT_MOBIUS_BAR_HOMOTOPY_AND_ZERO_FIBER_GATE.md`,
  §4: for N a product of r distinct primes, the number of ordered factorizations
  into k factors is k!S(r,k). The alternating cancellation recovers μ(N); this use of
  Stirling numbers does not identify the complete form nor sign its gamma part either.

## Decision

No operation has been identified that avoids the difficulty of the first two
antecedents, nor a new inequality on the complete form. The mere change of
basis does not satisfy the novelty filter. This selection is stopped before
derivations, computations or certificates.

This does not prove the impossibility of every application of falling factorials
nor attribute novelty to the proposal. The search was targeted, not exhaustive.
Reopening requires first writing a concrete identity or comparison, with control
of the complete crossing, that is not just the already recorded postcomposition.

## Result

No new mechanism. No sign proof nor new mathematical NO-GO was obtained.
The selection is recorded so as not to relaunch the same change-of-basis suggestion.
Global positivity remains OPEN; papers untouched.
