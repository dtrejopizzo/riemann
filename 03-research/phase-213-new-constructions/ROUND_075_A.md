# What is lost by cutting the prime sum of 074 before centering it

Fix 0<a<1. Under the global sign, the prefix J_a(x)=Σ_{p≤x^a}(log p)U(x/p) has oscillations of both signs at scale x^((1+a)/2), even at fourth powers. Hence the attempt to prove 074 by demanding the ceiling O(√x log x) for both pieces separately asks for something that fails under the desired conclusion itself. The continuous density that must be compensated when making cuts is also identified. The quantitative results of this round are conditional on the sign.

**Profile of the cut.** As in 074, under the sign shift the Mellin integral of U to Re s=0.45. If b_γ is the residue of Û at 1/2+iγ (one entry per distinct ordinate), then
     
    U(u)=√u Σ_γ b_γ exp(iγ log u)+O(u^0.45)     (u≥1).

For the series and each fixed logarithmic derivative there is absolute convergence by the decay of A from 074 and the zero count with multiplicity. There is no constant term and b_γ≠0 at the first ordinate by 074. In particular U and its fixed logarithmic derivatives are O(√u) on the whole half-line by lower support.

For P=x^a, the PNT and partial summation replace the sum over primes up to P by ∫₀ᴾ U(x/t)dt at cost o(√(xP)). In detail, on a block t≈Q the sup norm plus variation of U(x/t) is O(√(x/Q)); the error of the weighted count is o(Q), uniformly above a large fixed threshold. Summing over blocks costs o(√(xP)); the interval below the threshold contributes O_threshold(√x), even when integrating from zero. Substituting the series in the integral yields
    
    J_a(x)/x^((1+a)/2) = F((1−a)log x)+o(1),
    F(v)=Σ_γ b_γ exp(iγv)/(1/2−iγ).

The error of the O(u^0.45) term is O((x/P)^(−0.05)) after normalizing. F is real, continuous and almost periodic by uniform convergence of the trigonometric polynomials. Its mean is zero and it is not identically zero, since the coefficient of the first ordinate does not vanish (recovery by Fourier mean). By uniform recurrence, such a function takes values bounded away from zero in both directions at arbitrarily large times: a recurrent positive zone of fixed width would have positive mean if it never took negative values. At x=j⁴ the steps of (1−a)log x tend to zero, so both oscillations persist. The complement of J_a has the opposite profile, since 074 gives J=O(√x log x) under the sign.

**Which centering would work locally.** Let h be smooth with compact support in (1,2), P≥2, x>0. Under the sign one has uniformly
    
    |Σ_p (log p) h(p/P)U(x/p) − ∫ h(t/P)U(x/t)dt| ≪_h √x.

To see this, write the test on the scale t=Pz as g(z)=h(z)U(x/(Pz)). Its derivative norms up to any fixed order are O_h(√(x/P)) on the compact set. By integration by parts its ordinary Mellin transform on fixed vertical strips is bounded by C_N√(x/P)(1+|Im s|)^(−N). For the sum with Λ, invert against −ζ'/ζ(s)P^s: the pole at 1 gives the written integral; the zeros give O(√P√(x/P)) by their multiplicities and the decay, shifting to Re s=0.45 as in 074. The new line contributes O(P^0.45√(x/P)). To return to primes, the sum of log p for p^k in [P,2P], k≥2, is O(√P) by Chebyshev (squares dominate and exponents ≥3 cost O(P^(1/3)log P)). Thus O(√x) is preserved. Smoothing the edges matters in this estimate.

**Cross-check.** 072 isolated the largest prime of the numerator, without log p, and obtained two unconditional masses of order x/log³x. Here the prime index belongs to the sum J of 074, is weighted by log p and is cut at scale x^a. The expansion over zeros and the replacement by density are classical. The conclusion for this route is that centering must precede local control: imposing a root bound on the raw prefix would lose a larger and necessary oscillation. The bound for the centered block has been deduced from the sign; proving it arithmetically without that hypothesis and assembling the blocks are separate questions.

**Assembly without estimating the density separately.** There is an exact local condition for the sign. Choose a smooth real transition τ, zero for t≤1 and equal to 1 for t≥2; for this paragraph use h(z)=τ(z)−τ(z/2), supported in [1,4]. The local proof above applies literally to that compact set and also to P=1. Denote by D_j the difference sum minus integral for P=2^j, j≥0. The sign is equivalent to the existence of a single constant with D_j(x)≤C√x for all j≥0 and every sufficiently large fourth power x. The necessity above is even two-sided.

Indeed Σ_j h(t/2^j)=τ(t) and τ(p)=1. There are O(log x) active blocks by the lower support of U. Hence the local bound gives on the samples an upper bound O(√x log x) for the centered sum B(x)=J(x)−∫τ(t)U(x/t)dt. Interpolate as in 074; the integral and its first two logarithmic derivatives are O(x log x) by 069 and the support. In Mellin its multiplier relative to Û is I_τ(s)=∫₁^∞τ(t)t^(−s)dt, which continues as 2^(1−s)/(s−1) plus an entire function. Consequently B̂=Û(−L−T−I_τ) has the same double poles off the critical line as Ĵ and is regular on the real axis s>1/2. Landau as in 074 yields the sign directly from B. This settles the assembly for this partition; the only pending estimate in this criterion is the uniform ceiling of the centered blocks.

**How many interior blocks must reach root scale.** For the partition of the previous paragraph, under RH there exist ε,c>0 such that for each sign σ=±1 there are arbitrarily large fourth powers x where at least c log x indices simultaneously satisfy

    x^ε ≤ 2^j ≤ x^(1−ε),       σD_j(x) ≥ c√x.

Indeed, under RH the integral ∫₀^∞U(x/t)dt=xÛ(1) is absolutely convergent by |U(v)|≪√v and the lower support, and equals zero by the double zero of 072. Replacing 1 by τ in this integral costs O(√x) by integration over 0<t<2. Hence B(x)=J(x)+O(√x). The two-sided excursions proved in 074 provide, for each σ, a sequence of samples with σB≥b√x log x, with common b>0 by taking the smaller of the two margins. There are at most K log x active blocks and |D_j|≤M√x by 075. By the lower support of U the last active scale is O(x); the two excluded extreme bands contain at most 2ε log x/log 2+O(1) indices. Choose ε small so that the total cost of removing them is ≤b√x log x/3. Interior indices with σD_j<b√x/(3K) contribute in total at most b√x log x/3. Thus at least b log x/(3M) indices remain with the stated sign and size. The argument holds for each sign on its own sequence.

Hence a uniform improvement o(√x) on all interior blocks for each fixed ε would be incompatible with RH. Nor would a model suffice in which only o(log x) interior blocks can share a root excursion of the same sign and the rest are negligible. The pending global estimate must admit this accumulation.

**Mean profile on any interior band of continuous scales (conditional on RH).** For fixed 0<a<b<1 and h smooth with compact support in (0,∞), let H(z)=∫h(u)u^z du/u. With the residues b_γ of this note and multiplicities m_γ, as x tends to infinity,

    ∫_(x^a)^(x^b) D_P(x) dP/P / [(b−a)√x log x]
        = −H(0) Σ_γ m_γ b_γ exp(iγ log x) + o(1).

To check this put Y=x/P. Insert U(Y/u)=√(Y/u)Σ b_γ (Y/u)^(iγ) plus remainder into the centered discrepancy. On the compact set of the test, the remainder and any fixed number of logarithmic derivatives in u cost O(Y^0.45), by the same contour shift of 075 with powers of s. The explicit-formula estimate for smooth tests of 075 gives for that remainder O(√P Y^0.45), uniformly.

For each fixed mode γ apply the explicit formula to the test h(u)u^(−1/2−iγ). After dividing the discrepancy by √P one obtains the series −Σ_η m_η P^(iη) H(iη−iγ), and the term −H(−iγ)/2 from the prime squares, plus o_γ(1). Indeed the main term of Λ cancels the density, the 0.45 contour leaves O_γ(P^(−0.05)), powers of order ≥3 cost o_γ(1), and the sum over squares divided by √P tends by PNT to half the integral of the test against u^(−1/2)du. The outer multiplier of the mode after normalizing by √x is b_γ Y^(iγ). To truncate first in γ uniformly, the bound of 075 suffices, whose cost for this test is polynomial in |γ| (a fixed number of derivatives suffices); the b_γ decay faster than any power. For each γ the series in η converges absolutely by smoothness of h and the zero count.

Each pair is multiplied by exp(iγ log x) and by the average of exp(i(η−γ)log P) over an interval of length (b−a)log x. For η different from γ this average tends to zero, uniformly in the origin of the interval; for η=γ it equals one. The squares term also vanishes since γ≠0. Truncating the series first avoids any separation hypothesis between ordinates. This proves the formula even at fourth powers. For H(0)>0 the profile is not identically zero by the first residue of 074. The coincidence of the profile on disjoint bands refers to the continuous average in log P; extending the argument to a fixed mesh would require treating congruent frequencies.

**Average over the original dyadic partition.** Under the same hypotheses as the mean profile, take specifically h(u)=τ(u)−τ(u/2) from the assembly. For any integers J≤K depending on x, if J→∞, x/2^K→∞ and N=K−J+1→∞, then

    (1/N) Σ_(j=J)^K D_j(x)/√x = −(log 2) Σ_γ m_γ b_γ x^(iγ) + o(1).

In particular the bands may move with x and their logarithmic width may grow arbitrarily slowly. To verify the exact cancellation, h(u)=∫_(u/2)^u τ'(v)dv gives H(z)=[(2^z−1)/z]∫τ'(v)v^z dv. Thus H(0)=log 2 and H(iω)=0 for every nonzero frequency with 2^(iω)=1. For a fixed pair of the expansion above the finite mean of 2^(ij(η−γ)) has modulus ≤2/(N|1−2^(i(η−γ))|) if the denominator is nonzero. If it vanishes with η≠γ, the coefficient H(i(η−γ)) is exactly zero. The same dichotomy eliminates the squares term with frequency −γ, since γ≠0. Only the terms η=γ survive. The uniform absolute truncations of the continuous profile allow taking a finite list of frequencies first; the estimate of the remainder in Y is made uniform by Y≥x/2^K and the prime error for each fixed mode by P≥2^J. No lower bound on differences of ordinates is required. For another weight without the zeros of H this cancellation is not justified.

Normalization consequence: B(x)/(√x log x)=−Σ_γ m_γ b_γ x^(iγ)+o(1) under RH. Take J and the distance from K to log₂x of order √(log x); the O(√(log x)) excluded blocks cost O(√(x log x)) by the two-sided ceiling and the lower support of U. Since J(x)−B(x)=O(√x) by the computation of the integral above, the same formula holds for the prime sum J(x) of 074.

**Independent review of the three final extensions: PROVED under RH.** For the density of blocks, the excursions of 074 and the identity ∫₀∞U(x/t)dt=xÛ(1)=0 give B=J+O(√x). The integral converges absolutely: near t=0 the majorant is C√(x/t) and for large t it vanishes by support. The extreme bands contain at most 2εlogx/log2+O(1) indices. Their absolute cost is controlled by the common ceiling M√x, and the threshold argument leaves at least b logx/(3M) blocks of the desired sign; it is not assumed that the blocks are independent nor that the two orientations occur in the same sample.

For the mode γ, the explicit formula with φ_γ(u)=h(u)u^(−1/2−iγ) has, after dividing by √P, zero term −Σ_ηm_ηP^(iη)H(iη−iγ) and squares term −H(−iγ)/2. The factor 1/2 comes from the change u=q² in the density of primes p≈√P and from the weight log p, not log(p²). Exponents ≥3 are O_h(P^(−1/6)logP) for each fixed mode. A fixed number of derivatives of the test costs only a power of 1+|γ|; the arbitrary decay of b_γ allows truncating in γ uniformly in P and x. After that truncation, the series in η is absolutely convergent by smoothness of h and the count with multiplicities. The remainder of U contributes O(Y^(−0.05)) normalized, uniformly as Y=x/P→∞. This justifies the means without imposing separation between ordinates.

The diagonal η=γ contributes −m_γb_γ: b_γ is already the full residue of Û and the second multiplicity comes from the zero of ζ in the prime explicit formula. In the continuous mean, every nonzero frequency vanishes by the exponential average; in the dyadic one it may resonate, but the factor (2^z−1)/z of H annihilates it exactly. This includes the squares if −γ is resonant; if not, the geometric mean eliminates them. Continuity of the limits requires only J→∞, x/2^K→∞ and N→∞, because frequencies are truncated first and the bounds are uniform in the origins of the bands. Finally N/logx→1/log2 on the chosen almost complete band cancels H(0)=log2. The O(√logx) omitted blocks give O(1/√logx) after normalizing, and the difference J−B=O(√x) does not alter the profile either. No unconditional bound for J or for the blocks is obtained by this derivation.
