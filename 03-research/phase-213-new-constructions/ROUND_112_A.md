# Unconditional bound at all scales between 10¹⁰ and 10¹² for 109

For the profile w of 109, on the whole real interval 10¹⁰≤x≤10¹² one has E(x)≤−0.008 V(1/2)√x. The verification of Platt and Trudgian, collected in paper 01 §Computational verification, is used as a prior theorem only up to H=10¹². RH is not assumed above H.

**Zeros.** In the explicit formula for Λ with this profile, the error relative to xV(1) is −Σρ x^ρ V(ρ)−Σ_{k≥1}x^(−2k)V(−2k). This is obtained by integrating the formula for ψ against the piecewise continuous weight, as in 109. The constant term of ψ vanishes by support in (1,∞) after scaling. The trivial series is convergent and favorable by positivity of w. For the low zeros, on the line by the cited theorem, the certificate of 109 gives a sum of moduli <0.487 V(1/2) even without global RH: Hadamard's identity D=Σ Re[1/(ρ(1−ρ))] has positive summands for every zero in the strip, and therefore dominates the low sum of 1/(1/4+γ²).

For |γ|>H one has |V(ρ)|≤2C/γ² by the formula of 109. At σ=H+1, Hadamard gives ξ'/ξ(σ)=Σ(σ−β)/[(σ−β)²+γ²]. Each high summand dominates H/(3γ²). Moreover ξ'/ξ(σ)<log H by the formula for the gamma factor (ψ_digamma(t)≤log t by Jensen on the gamma distribution), ζ'/ζ(σ)<0 and the rational terms 1/σ+1/(σ−1). Thus the high contribution divided by V(1/2)√x is ≤(2C/V(1/2))√x·3log H/H<0.002.

**Squares.** At x=Y² the contribution of p² divided by √x is Σ (log p)/p sinh((h−|2log(p/Y)|)/2) on its support. In each cell y≤Y≤y+5, y≥10⁵, replace Y by y and h by h'=h−2log(1+5/10⁵), taking the positive part of the profile. The triangle inequality guarantees a common minorant for the whole cell. Expanding sinh gives exactly the differences of the prefixes of log p and (log p)/p² in verify_112.py. The certificate with 100-bit balls gave >0.497 V(1/2) on the 180000 cells without exception; the sieve only goes up to 1300000. All higher powers can be removed with favorable sign. Subtracting 0.497 from the ceilings 0.487+0.002 proves the claim. The interval is disjoint from that of 111 and does not by itself imply any eventual ceiling.
