# Head on two cells and arbitrary tail in a growing band

**Cross-check.** BUILD084(1) signs each profile F_j and some planes with an anchored index. 016-A extracts the signed mass for translations of a single profile, and 017-A gives a growing floor on short support. Here the tail varies freely across a whole band and the head stays on the first two cells. The two moments replace the relation between translations of a single profile.

**Statement.** There exist θ,c>0 such that for K sufficiently large and L=θ K^(2/3)/(log K)^(1/3), one has a(U(f+b))≥c(||f||₂²+(log K)||b||₂²) if f belongs to the span of the indicators of [1,2) and [2,3), b is supported on [K,K+L], Ub belongs to the form domain and the integrals of f+b against 1 and 1/x vanish. Complex coefficients are allowed. The notation Ug(t)=exp(t/2)g(exp t) preserves the L² norm.

**Head and tail.** Write a₀=log 2, b₀=log(3/2), p=b₀1_[1,2)−a₀1_[2,3), t=1_[1,2)/a₀. Thus ∫p(x)dx/x=0 and ∫t(x)dx/x=1. For f=zp+et the second moment gives |e|≤√L ||b||₂/K. The head matrix is fixed, and a(Up)>0.15 by BUILD084. Hence a(Uf)≥0.14|z|²−C|e|² by Young. The support of Ub in the logarithmic variable has length ℓ=log(1+L/K). As in 017-A, on a fixed translated window the Fourier mass below frequency ℓ^(−1/2) is at most √ℓ/π times the total mass. The growth of m and the boundedness of the comb on that window imply a(Ub)≥κ(log K)||b||₂² for K sufficiently large, with some absolute κ>0 for 0<θ≤1.

**Physical crossing.** For y in the band let S_f(y)=Σ Λ(n)f(y/n)/n. The comb crossing in absolute value is the modulus of 2 Re ∫conjugate(b(y)) S_f(y)dy. Write S_f(y)=S_f(K)+(S_f(y)−S_f(K)). The constant contribution keeps the ordinary moment ∫b=−∫f. For the vector p, PNT and partial summation on the two bands [K/(i+1),K/i] (with the lower endpoint excluded) give S_p(K)=o(1) since its inverse moment is zero. For t one has S_t(K)=O(1). Thus the constant contribution is bounded in modulus by C(o(1)|z|+|e|)(|z|+|e|).

In the variable part the endpoints move by O(L+1) integers of size comparable to K. Each summand costs O(log K/K), so the modulus of the variable crossing is ≤C (L+1)√L log K/K (|z|+|e|)||b||₂. For the chosen L and large K this coefficient is ≤2C θ^(3/2)√(log K). The archimedean crossing costs O(√L/K)(|z|+|e|)||b||₂ by the off-diagonal physical kernel of 014-A. The formula holds for b in the domain: Ub can be smoothed in the logarithmic variable in the Fourier norm with weight 1+|m| and slightly widened support, and one passes to the limit in the separated kernel and in the comb of the fixed window for each K.

**Absorption.** Take θ fixed and sufficiently small. By Young, the mixed cost with |z| in the variable part fits in 0.04|z|²+(κ/4)(log K)||b||₂². All terms with |e| are absorbed for large K by |e|≤√L||b||₂/K and the previous floors. The archimedean crossing and o(1)|z|² are also absorbed. The bound of the statement follows because ||f||₂≤C(|z|+|e|).

In particular the full form is positive definite on the whole space spanned jointly by the F_j with [j,j+1) contained in [K,K+L]. Their tails on distinct cells make those profiles linearly independent. The dimension of these spaces grows without bound. The continuous statement moreover admits any tail of the domain in that band with head adjusted by the moments. Control over multiple separated bands and global positivity remain open.
