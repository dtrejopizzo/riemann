# The prime ceiling of 109 holds on the whole real interval from 2 to 10¹²

In the gap 999983≤x≤10¹⁰ the joint contribution of squares and cubes, divided by V(1/2)√x, exceeds 0.497. By 112 the zeros contribute at most 0.487+0.002, uniformly also in this gap: the bound for the high ones improves as x decreases and that for the low ones does not depend on x. The trivial terms and higher powers are removed with favorable sign. Thus E(x)≤−0.008 V(1/2)√x on [999983,10¹²] and E(x)<0 on [2,10¹²] by 111. No eventual threshold is obtained.

The [certificate](verify_113.py) uses 9216 contiguous cells with integer endpoints x₀ and min(10¹⁰,x₀+floor(x₀/1000)), starting exactly at 999983. In each cell h is reduced by log(1001/1000), since the logarithmic distance from the center to the initial endpoint does not exceed that amount. For n=p² or p³ the normalized summand before dividing by V(1/2) is (log p)/√n times sinh((h−|log(n/x)|)/2) on the support. By the triangle inequality the reduced weight at x₀ minorizes the original weight on the whole cell. Expanding sinh gives two differences of prefixes of log p and (log p)/n over the nodes n, exactly the four contributions of the script. The sieve includes all necessary nodes since exp(h)<2. Signs are compared with 100-bit balls, without using the midpoint to decide. It was not necessary to sum over primes of size x.
