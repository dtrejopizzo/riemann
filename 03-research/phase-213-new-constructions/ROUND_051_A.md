# Signed cancellation in rows whose prime repeats in the second factor

Use the weights and the compensation on primes from 050, with L=√x at x=j⁴, y=2x. The terms of a+c are expanded by rows of prime powers p^e>L/2 dividing k, with k=p^e v. The weight of each row, apart from U(k), μ(m) and V(km/y), has absolute value ≤C log p; the double rows only admit even v and the compensation only e=1. There is multiplicity bounded by three in this description. On the support k≤CLT, m≥cL/T, v≤CT, with T=(log x)².

**Threshold of the absolute method of 050.** For squarefree m, sf(km)≥m/sf(k), by the formula sf(km)=m sf(k)/gcd(m,sf(k))². If e is even, sf(k)≤v. If e≥3 is odd, sf(k)≤pv and pv=k/(p^e)^((e−1)/e)≤C L^(1/3)T. Thus all rows with e≥2 satisfy sf(km)≥c L^(2/3)/T². For e=1, p>L/2 exceeds v. If p does not divide m, it also divides sf(km); if it divides m, write m=pw. From p²vw≤By one obtains p≤CL and vw≤C. The absolute sum of these rows is O(L) by Chebyshev. In particular the absolute elimination of 050 reaches sf(km)≤x^(1/3−ε) for any fixed positive ε less than 1/3.

For each fixed η>0, the extreme sf(km)≤ηL^(2/3) already contains positive terms whose sum is ≫η' L log log x, with η'>0 depending on η. Choose p prime between D L^(1/3) and 2D L^(1/3), D fixed and sufficiently large, v odd up to log x and w in an interval [αy/(p⁴v),βy/(p⁴v)] with [α,β] interior to the support and V bounded below there. Take μ(w)=−1 and p not dividing w. There are ≫y/(p⁴v) choices: the density of μ=−1 is 1/(2ζ(2)) by PNT and the density of squarefree integers, valid uniformly for intervals of fixed ratio with lower endpoint tending to infinity; excluding multiples of p costs o(y/(p⁴v)). At k=p³v, m=pw, the only row weight that survives is +log p: k is odd, p²<L/2, v<p and all factors of v have powers ≤v. U(k)=1 for large x; μ(m)=+1 and sf(km)≤vw≤ηL^(2/3). They are distinct pairs. Summing first over w, then over v and p, the weighted sum exceeds a constant times L log log x. This proof refers to the coefficient collected for each pair (k,m), and excludes an absolute bound O(L) at the extreme.

**What can be eliminated while preserving the signs.** The signed total of all rows with e≥2 and p|m, without restriction on sf(km), is O_A(L/(log x)^A) for any fixed A. To see this, in the row k=p^e v put m=pw. Only w with p not dividing w count, and μ(m)=−μ(w). The natural size of w is W=y/(p^(e+1)v); since p≤(CLT)^(1/2), one has W≫L^(1/2)/T^(3/2). The classical quantitative bound for M(u)=Σ_{n≤u}μ(n) used in 045 gives, uniformly in p,

    |Σ_{w≤u, p∤w} μ(w)| ≤ C_N u/(log u)^N     (u large).

Indeed, the sum is Σ_{a≥0, p^a≤u} M(u/p^a), by recursion. Up to p^a≤√u apply the bound for M with log(u/p^a)≥(log u)/2; the rest costs O(√u) by geometric series. By partial summation with the fixed weight V(w/W), the signed cost per row and v, including its weight, is O_N((log p)W/(log x)^N). The weights U(k) are independent of w. To sum, one uses

    Σ_{p^e≤CLT, p^e>L/2, e≥2} (log p)/p^(e+1) ≤ (C/L) Σ_{p≤√(CLT)} (log p)/p = O((log x)/L).

The first inequality sums the geometric progression from the first allowed power. Summing also 1/v for v≤CT costs O(log x), and N is arbitrary. For e=1 and p|m the absolute bound O(L) was already proved. One can therefore eliminate all incidences p|m, understanding the elimination per row if k admits more than one.

**Remaining criterion.** In each kept row p does not divide m. All primes of m not dividing v appear with exponent one in km; hence sf(km)≥m/gcd(m,v)≥m/v≥cL/(log x)^4. To decide the sign the same upper bound O(L) on the rows with p not dividing m suffices. The precision is preserved by the signed control above. The original sum cut arbitrarily by sf has not been bounded by this means: restricting w according to its squarefree part would invalidate the partial summation used here.

**Cross-check and scope.** The Möbius tool in coprimality progressions and the PNT are classical; 045 already uses the quantitative PNT. Here they are applied after detecting a repetition between the prime of a row and the factor carrying μ. That repetition provides the factor 1/p required to sum the errors at square-root precision. 044 removed power indices in another expression and 050 had only estimated absolute values. The sign of the complement remains pending.
