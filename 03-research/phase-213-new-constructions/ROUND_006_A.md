# A negative upper tail in the physical cell matrix

The simultaneous decomposition of the logarithms of BUILD_084(3) into real cuts was cross-checked. Against the antecedents on carries and factorial quotients, here a concrete sufficient condition is checked in the original physical coordinates. The claim concerns the upper tails of the logarithmic terms, with the diagonal constant reserved at the origin of the integral. The independent gate approved this direct diagnosis. The antecedent search was targeted.

For a matrix of N cells write each log k with k a positive integer as the integral of the indicator of 1<x≤k with respect to dx/x. Omit the summands 0 log 0. For each nonzero Λ(n) use log p where n is a power of p, keeping the divisor n in the denominator of the weight. Let L_N(x) be the matrix of these integrands. Then M_N=C I+∫L_N(x) dx/x, with C=−γ−log(2π). The moments of the test are imposed on its original coefficients.

For 2N+1<x<2N+2, the only active logarithm is log(2N+2), which enters with coefficient −(N+1) at position (N,N) through −h(2N)/2. In a crossing i<j the largest argument of h(i+j) is i+j+2≤2N+1. The comb terms have p≤n≤j≤N. The earlier diagonals and the first diagonal differences also lie below the cut. Consequently, for 2N+1<a<2N+2 the tail evaluated at α is exactly

    ∫_[a,∞) α*L_N(x)α dx/x = −(N+1)|α_N|² log((2N+2)/a).

For every N≥3 the test f_N of BUILD_084(1) satisfies both moments and has α_N=log(4/3). Its tail is strictly negative, while its full energy is strictly positive by BUILD_084(2). This refutes the requirement that all these tails be positive on the admissible tests. The diagonal constant contributes C||α||² to the full energy and lies outside this tail. A comparison between intervals or a redistribution of terms would require an additional identity. The global sign remains open.
