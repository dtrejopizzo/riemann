# Which correlation of simple primes the band needs

Write S(x)=Σ_p log p K(x/p) with K of 160. For X=2^j integrate over 1≤v≤2 the square of S(Xv). Call D_X the sum of the terms p=q and R_X the ordered sum p≠q, both already integrated. The global sign is equivalent to R_X≤0 for all sufficiently large j. Moreover a macroscopic part of R_X already has negative sign unconditionally, with the following precision and limitation.

**Diagonal and criterion.** Let f_v(t)=K(v/t) for t>0, extended by zero for t≤0. For 1≤v≤2 these functions are continuous, supported in a common compact set and uniformly bounded, by the two ends of 160. Let c=∫₁²∫ f_v(t)²dt dv>0, since B is not identically zero. By the prime number theorem, D_X∼c X log X: the measure Σ_p(log p)²δ_(p/X)/(X log X) converges to Lebesgue on the positive compact set, with the zero end controlled by Chebyshev. Under RH, 160 (by its two-sided proof) and 161 give S(x)=O(√x). Hence R_X=−D_X+O(X) and it is eventually negative.

Conversely if R_X≤0 eventually, ∫₁² S(Xv)²dv≤D_X=O(X log X). By Cauchy–Schwarz on each block, ∫₁^∞ |S(x)|x^(−σ−1)dx converges for all σ>1/2 (geometric series over blocks). This gives Mellin holomorphy in that half-plane. The part on x<1 has support bounded away from zero. On Re s>1 the complete transform is B(s)Σ_p(log p)p^(−s); on Re s>1/2 the second factor is −ζ'/ζ minus a holomorphic series of higher powers. If there is a zero to the right, the extreme one at its ordinate keeps the pole by 160. This contradicts holomorphy and proves the converse. No rate of negativity needs to be established.

**A part with unconditional sign.** Fix small η>0. Let N_X(η) be the part of R_X with 0<|p−q|≤ηX. By PNT and convergence of the product measures,
    N_X(η)/X² → n(η)=∫₁²∫∫_{|t−u|≤η} f_v(t)f_v(u)dt du dv.
The band boundaries have measure zero; removing the diagonal costs O(log X/X) after normalizing. By continuity of translation in L² one has n(η)=2η c+o(η) as η tends to zero. In particular n(η)>0 for fixed sufficiently small η. The mass of the product over all pairs divided by X² tends to zero: ∫f_v(t)dt=v B(1)=0 for each v. Consequently the distant block |p−q|>ηX equals −n(η)X²+o_η(X²), eventually strictly negative, without RH. The known positive and negative parts are of the same order X²; their main terms cancel exactly. Their signs taken separately do not fix that of R_X.

**Decision.** 056–058 already treat off-diagonal terms for Möbius, obtained by decomposing Λ. Here the diagonal is that of the simple primes when squaring and integrating S, of size X log X, and with a mere sign criterion on dyadic scales. PNT in both indices proves the sign of the distant block but leaves an o(X²) error, too large for the sign of the total. Repeating PNT separately on the two blocks does not resolve the remaining cancellation.
