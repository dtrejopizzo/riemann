# Required resolution of a factorial majorant of the prime mean

Certifying the inequality of 127 via a quotient of factorials of arguments ≤16X, X=M⁴, was examined. Its kernel on prime powers v is K(v)=Σ_i c_i floor(n_i/v), by log(n!)=Σ_{v≤n}Λ(v)floor(n/v). The staircase to be majorized for the primes is W(v)=M⁻¹ #{M<m≤2M : v≤m⁴}. The margin available in the mean of 127 is of order √X. Here a restriction is proved for the method that pays the excess of the majorant by its area, not for all arithmetic certificates.

**Necessary condition.** If K≥W on [2X,8X] and ∫(K−W)≤C√X there, ≫M distinct factorial arguments are needed for M sufficiently large depending on C. In the interior, for example on [3X,7X], there are ≍M jumps of W separated by ≍X/M; each lowers the weight by 1/M. Choose δ fixed positive small enough that the neighbourhoods of radius δX/M are disjoint. If a neighbourhood lacks a jump of K, its constant value is at least the left value of W; it therefore pays at least δX/M²=δ√X on the right. At most C/δ neighbourhoods can remain without a jump. Each floor(n_i/v) has at most eight jumps in the band, independently of c_i. This proves the required order even with signed real coefficients.

**Constructive test on the upper band.** That cost linear in M suffices to reproduce the band exactly at the integers: use the arguments floor(m⁴/d) with coefficients μ(d)/M for 1≤d≤8 and M<m≤2M. For every integer v≥2X, floor(floor(m⁴/d)/v)=floor(m⁴/(dv)); summing first over d one gets Σ_{a≤m⁴/v}Σ_{d|a}μ(d)=1_{v≤m⁴}. Thus the value at every prime and prime power of the band is exact. For real v one can use m⁴/d instead of the integer argument for the continuous identity. The use of Möbius inversion and factorial quotients has antecedents, among others 045 and the factorial selection of Part V. The resolution computation corresponds to the M specific jumps of the new target.

**Limit of this construction.** For 8≤y<10, the truncated sum Σ_{d≤8} μ(d)floor(y/d) still coincides with 1 (μ(9)=0; on passing 10 the defect changes). More precisely on 10≤y<11 the term μ(10)=1 is missing and the value is 0. Hence not even each individual summand remains a majorant below the band. This local test does not determine the sign of the average of the arithmetic errors at the smaller scales. A bounded number of factorials with excess area of square-root size is discarded under the declared hypotheses; arguments larger than 16X, estimates only over primes and growing families are not discarded.

The failure persists after averaging over m and at the actual primes. For 3X/2≤v≤151X/100 one has m⁴/v≤32/3<11. The deficit W−K of the eight-dilation candidate at an integer v equals exactly M⁻¹ #{m: m⁴≥10v, M<m≤2M}. It is at least 2−(15.1)^(1/4)−O(1/M)>0 uniformly for large M. By the PNT on the interval of fixed ratio [1.50X,1.51X], the loss weighted by log p on that single band is ≫X. The margin √X does not allow absorbing it in absolute value, although it could be compensated in a signed global comparison.
