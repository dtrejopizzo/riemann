# Short arbitrary window followed by three tails of arbitrary rates

There exist L₀>0 and c>0 such that W(F)≥c||F||₂² for every arbitrary datum of the form domain on [−L₀,0] followed on y>0 by an element of the rational block of up to three real poles −v_i, v_i>1/2. Multiplicities are allowed with their complete confluent space and complex coefficients. Both constants are existential. The result is common to all such listings. The arithmetic ingredient is the four-node positivity of POWER_FOUR_GENERATORS_085; 197 treated a single tail.

**Why collision keeps a margin.** Denote M(v)=ξ'(v+1/2)/ξ(v+1/2). On a compact set of rates [1/2,V] parametrize an ordered list by the successive divided differences of exp(−vy), including their derivative limits. They form a basis even with collisions: their Laplace transforms are (−1)^(j−1)/∏_{i≤j}(w+v_i), independent by their distinct relative degrees. The L² and W Gram matrices are continuous up to all collisions by integrals of derivatives for the divided differences, and for W by the kernel K(v,w)=(M(v)+M(w))/(v+w) of 001. At the endpoint 1/2 its continuation is taken. Semidefiniteness of the Gram of any block of up to three of these data PLUS a free node w>1/2 follows by congruence and limit from four-node positivity at distinct nodes. If a nonzero vector of the three-block were null for the form, its crossing with each w would be zero by the augmented matrix. But that crossing is M(w)R(w)+Q(w), with R,Q rational in w: R is the Laplace transform of the vector (or of its conjugate depending on convention). R is not identically zero. This would force M(w) to be rational, impossible by M(w)=(1/2)log w+O(1) for large real w. By compactness there is uniform coercivity on each box of up to three bounded rates, also in the confluent basis. Norm-one combinations in that box have bounded coefficients in that basis. The divergent terms of W at the boundary are not evaluated separately.

**Escape of some rates.** In any sequence pass to a subsequence in which each rate converges in [1/2,∞]. Group the tail into a slow component s, bounded rates, and a fast one h, with minimum u→∞, by partial fractions. Each component has at most three poles. For ||h||₂=1 the reflection of 213-085 gives ||exp(uy/2)h||₂≤27, uniformly including multiplicities. In particular ||h||₁≤27/√u and ||exp(by)h||₂=O(1) for each fixed b. On every fixed frequency interval the mass of ĥ tends to zero by the L¹ bound. Since the archimedean symbol tends to infinity and has an absolute floor, A(h)→∞ uniformly. The other terms are bounded by the norm with weight exp(y). Thus the floor of W on the fast part tends to infinity.

**Control of the crossings.** The following bounds are bilinear in L² norms. For normalized s with rates in a fixed box, ||m(D)s||₂ is bounded: in the divided basis their transforms are the rational products above, uniformly O((1+|τ|)⁻¹), and m grows logarithmically. For the non-archimedean crossing with h on the SAME half-line, act on exp(−vy) and evaluate at t≥0. The pointwise coefficient is
    
    exp(−vt) ζ'/ζ(v+1/2) + exp(−t/2)/(v−1/2)
    −exp(−vt) Σ_{n≤exp t}Λ(n)n^(v−1/2) + exp(t/2)/(v+1/2).

The singularity of the first two summands combines as [exp(−t/2)−exp(−vt)]/(v−1/2) plus a regular term. In a fixed box, the expression and its first two derivatives in v are O(exp(Ct)) uniformly, by the integral formula for the quotient and the trivial bound on the finite sum. The bound persists in the divided basis. Integrating against h gives O(1) by Cauchy–Schwarz with weight exp((C+1)t). This controls the slow-fast crossing even as slow rates approach 1/2.

For a datum g on [−L,0], L≤1, the crossing with h is bounded by Carleman between half-lines for the archimedean term and the weights exp(y) for comb and polar (translate the origin to −1). For the crossing with s one can use 197: the function k_v(x) and its first two derivatives with respect to v are O(1+|log x|) on 0<x≤1, uniformly in the box. Indeed the polar singularity is already removed, the prime prefix is finite, and differentiating the archimedean integral only adds powers of r under the exponential. These bounds are again transferred via divided differences. The three crossings are uniformly bounded in the chosen subsequence.

**Uniform conclusion.** If the statement failed, by inclusion there would exist norm-one data F_j with L_j↓0 and W(F_j)≤1/j. For compactly supported data, the floor of W(g)/||g||² tends to infinity as L↓0, by the Lévy computation of 197. In the extracted subsequence write F_j=g_j+s_j+h_j, omitting empty components. The slow component has a fixed floor δ>0 by compactness; the compact and fast floors grow without bound. The bounded crossings are absorbed by Young, leaving for large j at least min(1,δ/2) times the sum of the three squared norms (if s is missing use 1). Since ||F_j||² is at most three times that sum, contradiction. No orthogonality between s and h is required. Left OPEN: a common numerical length, the same statement for an arbitrary number of tails, and the global sign.
