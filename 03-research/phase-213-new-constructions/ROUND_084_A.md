# Thirteen fast rates with the physical cutoff of any window

Let n≤13, v_j≥63/2 arbitrary (repetitions allowed), F(s)=P(s)/∏(s+v_j) real strictly proper, f its causal inverse Laplace transform, and f_L=f 1_[0,L]. In the normalization of P46, the translate of f_L to [−L/2,L/2] has strictly positive full energy for all L>0 if P≠0. For L≥log 11 one also has W(f_L)>||f||²/8000. This includes all real combinations of up to thirteen exponentials with rates ≥31.5 toward a common endpoint, or their reflections. For repeated poles the corresponding powers of time enter. The norm in the margin of the second statement is that of the uncut function.

**Antecedents and normalization.** POWER_FULL_THIRTEEN_053 gives W(f)>||f||²/4000 without cutoff; POWER_COMMON_TRANSFER_010 uses rational bases and weighted estimates. The physical translation preserves autocorrelation and polar product. On [0,∞), the three parts of W are m(D), −2Σ Λ(n)n^(−1/2)c_f(log n), and 2F(1/2)F(−1/2). For L<log 11 the window positivity of the reproduced external certificate holds (index, entry log11 COMPLETE). Even its non-negativity at the endpoint suffices, by the strict decrease of the full floor in P46. For all L≥log 11 we verify here a single error, uniform without minimal separation or upper bound on the poles.

**Uniform tail.** Normalize ||f||=1. The Malmquist orthonormal basis has elements √(2v_j)/(s+v_j) times the product of (s−v_i)/(s+v_i) for i<j, also with repetitions; the coefficients have Euclidean norm 1. At s₀=−20+iy set D=√(11.5²+y²). On the circle |s−s₀|=D/20 each denominator has modulus ≥0.95|s₀+v_i|. Since |s₀−v_i|/|s₀+v_i|≤51.5/11.5, each factor of the product has modulus <4.77. Also v_i/|s₀+v_i|≤31.5/11.5. Thus |F(s)|≤M D^(−1/2) with M=2·10⁹, since √13·4.77¹²√(63/11.5)/0.95<M. Cauchy gives |F'(s₀)|≤20M D^(−3/2) and |(sF)''(s₀)|≤2000M D^(−3/2), using |s₀|≤2D. The integral of D^(−3/2)dy/(2π) is ≤3/(π√11.5)<0.29 by cutting at |y|=11.5. Invert along the line −20: −F' transforms tf and (sF)'' transforms t² f' for t>0 (the atom at zero of the causal derivative vanishes on multiplying by t²). With t≥1 one gets
    
    |f(t)|≤2·10¹⁰ exp(−20t),    |f'(t)|≤2·10¹² exp(−20t).

**Budget after the cutoff.** Let g=f 1_(L,∞). In Fourier, using first its L¹ norm and then an integration by parts that includes the jump at L, one has |ĝ(ξ)|≤2·10¹¹ exp(−20L) min(1,1/|ξ|). By the digamma bounds of P46 lem:mbounds, |m(ξ)|≤10+log(1+|ξ|) (for |ξ|≤1 use monotonicity). The L²(dξ/(2π)) norm of that logarithmic majorant times min(1,1/|ξ|) is <20: integrate over [0,1] and use log(1+t)≤log 2+log t in the tail. Thus the difference of archimedean energies is ≤8·10¹² exp(−20L) by ||f||+||f_L||≤2 and symmetry of the multiplier.

For u≥0 the difference of autocorrelations is just the integral from max(L,u) of f(t)f(t−u); the other endpoint is fixed by causality. Cauchy in the second copy gives modulus ≤(2·10¹⁰/√40) exp(−20 max(L,u)). Since 20 max(L,log n)≥19L+log n, the loss from the comb is ≤(4·10¹⁰/√40)exp(−19L) Σ_{n≥2}(log n)n^(−3/2), and the series is <6 by integral comparison. For the two polar moments of f and f_L, the modulus is <5: use Cauchy on [0,2] with e^(t/2) and the tail bound after 2. The difference of each moment is ≤(2·10¹⁰/19.5)exp(−19.5L). The polar cost is at most 20 times that difference. At L=log11 the sum of the three majorants is <2·10^(−8), below 1/8000, and each summand decreases afterwards. Subtracting from the margin of 053 concludes. For n<13 one can also complete the denominator with factors cancelled in the numerator.

**Other exponents.** For up to four fixed distinct rates v_j>1/2, strict positivity without cutoff comes from 085 (its exponents are v_j+1/2). At fixed rates the convergence of the truncated matrices follows from exponential decay with rate >1/2, which makes the errors of both moments tend to zero, those of m(D) by the same integration by parts, and those of the comb by Cauchy and ΣΛ(n)n^(−1/2−b)<∞ for 1/2<b<min v_j. Compactness of the coefficient sphere in finite dimension transfers positivity to L large enough for those rates. This observation does not uniformly cover intermediate lengths for slow rates.

The main statement does control all cross terms of the fast subspace and all windows. It uses 053 and the existing local certificate; it does not increase the dimension, does not treat mixtures of both orientations and does not decide the sign for general data.
