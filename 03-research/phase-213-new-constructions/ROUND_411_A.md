# Counterexample: removing all critical pairs in order with positive kernels

**Status: PROVED, with independent review of the count, the quotients, the support and the residual limit.**

For any prescribed height H there exists an even entire bilateral transform of order one, of a positive density, with all zeros simple in |Re w|<1/2, all those of height ≤H on the imaginary axis, and

    N_+(T)=(T/(2π)) log T + O(T).

Each quotient after removing an arbitrary prefix of the pairs of imaginary zeros in increasing order is the transform of an even, non-negative and unimodal density. Even so there are infinitely many zeros off the axis. The leading coefficient of the Riemann count is preserved, not the arithmetic explicit formula. The convolution of uniforms already appears in F159/G2_B6_R2 and 213-047; here spectra of boxes are combined with removal in order and a residual factor without imaginary zeros.

**Zeros on the axis.** Fix τ=√2 and take the sum of independent variables uniform on [−r_j,r_j], r_j=1/[2(j+τ)], j≥1. The sum converges in L² and almost surely by the summable variances; its transform is B(w)=∏sinh(r_j w)/(r_j w), by normal convergence and integrable uniformity of the exponentials (use the product at 2Re w). Its zeros are ±2π i n(j+τ), n≥1. They are simple because τ is irrational and the tail of the product converges locally to a zero-free factor. Their upper number up to T is Σ_j floor(T/[2π(j+τ)])=(T/(2π))log T+O(T) truncating at j≤T/(2π). The bound log max_{|w|≤R}|B(w)|≤(R/2)log R+O(R) results from summing Rr_j for j≤R and O((Rr_j)²) afterwards; the count prevents order less than one. The same moment bound gives tail probability O(exp(−c exp(2v))) by Chernoff with R proportional to exp(2v).

**Positive quotients at every order.** In a box of radius r, removing its first k pairs is equivalent to changing the uniform density into the normalized density proportional to cos^(2k)(πv/(2r)) on |v|≤r. Indeed, applying ∂²+(kπ/r)² to that power gives a positive multiple of the power 2k−2, without boundary terms (value and first derivative vanish for k≥1). The normalization at zero determines the factor of the transform 1+w²/(kπ/r)². A prefix of zeros of B removes in each box precisely a prefix, changing only a finite number of boxes. Parity, unimodality and non-negativity are preserved by convolution and weak limit. The laws have full support: the finite sums of radii diverge and the independent tail has variance tending to zero. Convolution with a density positive near zero gives them a strictly positive density on the whole line.

**Off-axis factor.** Choose 0<a<1/2 and 0<r<π/(H+1). Convolve a density proportional to exp(av) on [−r,r] with its reflection. Its normalized transform is

    D(w)= [sinh(r(w+a))/(r(w+a))] [sinh(r(w−a))/(r(w−a))] / [sinh(ra)/(ra)]².

On the imaginary axis it is strictly positive. Its simple zeros are ±a+iπn/r for non-zero integers n. The density is proportional to sinh(a(2r−|v|)) on |v|≤2r, even and decreasing on the positive half-line of its support. Multiplying B by D preserves all the previous positive quotients, the order and the leading count, and adds only O(T) zeros, all above H in absolute height. The support argument ensures strictly positive density also at each finite stage. Removing all imaginary pairs leaves exactly D in the local limit of transforms: the canonical product of the pairs of B converges absolutely on compact sets and B(0)=1. Its variance is strictly positive.

**Review record.** The equality n(j+sqrt2)=m(k+sqrt2) forces n=m and j=k; all zeros ofB are therefore simple. The count is the stated sum of floors, and the error of replacing each floor by its argument is O(T), giving the exact coefficient1/(2π). The summable variances give almost sure convergence and convergence inL²; the products of moments at2Re w are bounded and justify the integrable uniformity needed to identify the entire transform. The bound RlogR/2+O(R) proves order≤1 and Chernoff gives the tail exp(−c exp(2v)); the zero count excludes order<1.

For f_k(v)=cos^(2k)(πv/(2r)) on|v|≤r, one has f_k''+(kπ/r)²f_k=2k(2k−1)(π/(2r))²f_(k−1). Vanishing value and first derivative at the edges prevent extra distributional terms. After normalizing masses, each step divides exactly by1+w²/(kπ/r)². A finite global prefix modifies only finitely many boxes, and in each box the first zeros, so that this identity realizes the required quotient in order. Parity and unimodality are preserved under convolution and weak limit. Full support is checked by choosing a prefix whose sum of radii exceeds any target and then a tail of variance so small that it has positive probability near0. Separating a box with positive density in the interior and applying the same argument to the rest gives strictly positive density at each point.

The factorD has simple zeros on the two lines±a, without coincidences among themselves or withB; on the imaginary axis it is a strictly positive squared modulus. Its density is a positive constant times sinh(a(2r−|v|)), and it adds only O(T) zeros to the count. Finally Σ_{j,n}(n(j+sqrt2))^(−2)<∞ allows freely reordering the paired canonical factors; the product of the non-removed factors tends to1 locally. The limit is exactlyD, not a constant nor a point mass, and its positive variance is compatible with all the positive finite stages.

**Implication for 407–410.** Even a general proof of positivity and unimodality at all steps, accompanied by the correct leading density and any finite verification, would be insufficient as an abstract closing principle. For the kernel of ξ one needs to exploit an additional datum that excludes a residual factor of this type. No zero of ζ nor a counterexample to the sign of paper 47 is constructed.
