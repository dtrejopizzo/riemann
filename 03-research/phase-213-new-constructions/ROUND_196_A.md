# A pole that stays fixed leaves a tail in the limit problem

Fix v>1/2. To each fast listing of 195 (minimum tending to infinity, sum of reciprocals tending to S>0) add a pole at −v. The bottom of the complete form on the untruncated blocks converges to the bottom of W on the space of arbitrary functions on [0,L], L=2S, followed on (L,∞) by a multiple of exp(−v(y−L)). On the compact part the form domain is understood. In particular, for S=553/200 and every fixed rate v large enough, that bottom is strictly positive and the sign is eventual on the whole block. No numerical minimal rate is fixed.

**Persistence of the slow mode.** Discarding the first indices, the partial fractions give E_j=E_j^fast+span{k}, k(y)=exp(−vy) on the half-line. In the representation of 195, the distance from k to the fast space is |B_j(v)|/√(2v): the projection onto B_j H² of the evaluation kernel k_v is conj(B_j(v))B_j k_v. The product of the factors (v_l−v)/(v_l+v) tends to exp(−2Sv) by logarithmic expansion with error O_v(Σv_l⁻³). Hence, in a unit f_j=g_j+c_j k, both c_j and ||g_j||₂ are uniformly bounded for this fixed v.

**Limit of the energies.** Take 1/2<η<η'<v. The Hardy weight of 085 with η' gives ||exp(η'y)f_j||₂=O(1). The arithmetic and polar parts are bilinearly continuous with the weight η, since ΣΛ(n)n^(−1/2−η)<∞. For the minimizers the archimedean floor is absolute and the total value is bounded above by the fixed test k. By the positive logarithmic growth of m, this gives uniformly small L² energy tails in Fourier outside large frequencies. The spatial tails are controlled with the weight η'. By the Fréchet–Kolmogorov compactness criterion there is a subsequence converging in L², with limit of norm one. Interpolating weights there is convergence in L²(exp(2ηy)dy). Pass also to c_j→c. The fast tail of 195 beyond any L+ε tends to zero; the limit function is ck there. Lower semicontinuity of the archimedean term (multiplier bounded below) and continuity of the other terms give the liminf.

For the limsup write the target datum as a compact function on [0,L] plus ck. Subtracting k and the boundary jump are legitimate in the domain: the exponential truncated to an interval or a half-line has finite logarithmic energy. Smooth interior functions are a core for the compact part (approximation by contraction of the interval and mollification in the Fourier norm with weight 1+log(2+|τ|)). Apply the recovery projection of 195 to those tests and add ck. Convergence in H^s is preserved for each smooth test (0<s<1/2) and in the weight η by 085. Density and normalization prove the equality of bottoms.

**Sign of the extension by a tail.** Suppose the compact bottom λ_W(S)=δ is positive. Write the test as g+z h, with g supported in [0,L] and h(y)=√(2v) exp(−v(y−L)) for y>L, zero before. It has squared norm ||g||²+|z|². There is a constant C_L uniform in v≥2 such that |W(g,h)|≤C_L||g||₂: the archimedean crossing between half-lines is bounded by Carleman (077); for comb and polar use the weight η=1 and ||e^y h||₂=e^L√(v/(v−1))≤√2 e^L. By translation invariance and the same weighted control, the non-archimedean terms of W(h) are O(1) when h is translated to the origin. Its normalized spectral density is v/[π(v²+τ²)]; the symbol m(τ)=log(2+|τ|)+O(1) gives A(h)=log v+O(1) by τ=vx and integrability of |log|x||/(1+x²) near zero and infinity. Thus W(h) grows and

    W(g+zh) ≥ (δ/2)||g||² + [W(h)−2C_L²/δ]|z|².

For v large enough one obtains floor δ/2 on the limit space. This holds in particular at the endpoint certified by 193. For general fixed v, if A_S is the window operator and k_{S,v} represents in L² the functional g↦W(g,h), completing squares gives the exact criterion W(h)≥⟨k_{S,v},A_S⁻¹k_{S,v}⟩ for semidefiniteness. The uniform argument above only covers large v; the sufficient rate may be huge.

**Scope.** Rounds 086–087 and 195 required all poles to escape. The model-space identity and the Schur complement are classical. Here the surviving tail is identified, the limit is taken in the unbounded form with both poles, and its positivity is proved for large enough rate. It does not represent arbitrary data beyond L.

**Audit.** The exact distance, the compactness, the choice of two weights and the Schur criterion were cross-checked. For 1/2<v≤2 a bounded crossing functional with v-dependent constant is obtained using any η∈(1/2,v). The Schur inequality is not inferred in that regime.
