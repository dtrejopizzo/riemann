# The exact rate on explicit matrices without penalty

For T≥1 let N=ceil(T³). Restrict the full form to the N even Dirichlet modes cos((k+1/2)πt/T), 0≤k<N, extended by zero outside [−T,T]. In the primitive version also impose ∫F(t)cosh(t/2)dt=0 (the odd condition is automatic). In both cases the negative part M of the smallest Rayleigh quotient satisfies

    lim log(1+M(T))/(2T)=Δ.

No derivative term is added and no special local density is assumed. In particular it suffices for the global sign that along a sequence T→∞ the smallest generalized eigenvalue of these matrices be ≥−exp(o(T)). The quantitative statement is the rate in each window, stronger than eventual detection by density of a union of spaces.

**Ceiling.** In these spaces the extension belongs to H¹ and ∥F'∥₂≤πN∥F∥₂/T by orthogonality and zero trace. By Jensen the logarithmic Plancherel moment of a unit vector is O(log(2+N/T)). The strip comparison of 117, with κ=1 trivial, gives M(T)≪(1+T)^6 exp(2ΔT)log(2+N/T). It extends from smooth tests by H¹₀ approximation and continuity of the form. Under RH both negative parts are zero.

**Preparation of the witnesses.** For η<η'<Δ, η≥0, use the primitive construction of 115 with a real even Gevrey 3/2 bump, of integral one and support in (−1/8,1/8). One obtains it for example by convolving symmetric uniforms of lengths c/n^(3/2) (as in 047), with c small. The first m Fourier factors give modulus ≤(2/(c|ξ|))^m(m!)^(3/2), whence exp(−c'|ξ|^(2/3)) by choosing m. The sum of lengths is finite and the decay gives smoothness. Dilate the bump inside the support so that its transform does not vanish at the chosen zero, possible by its value at zero. Applying P=−∂²+1/4 preserves the decay with a smaller constant. The symmetric pair of translates of Pg of 115 gives even, primitive witnesses with fixed L² norms and bounded H¹ norms, of energy ≤−exp(2η'T) after normalizing for all large T.

**Approximation and correction.** Each coefficient in the unnormalized cosine basis is the integral of the test against the mode divided by T; by the real translations and the above decay it has modulus ≤C/T exp(−c(k/T)^(2/3)). The basis is complete for the even H¹₀ functions (Neumann at 0, Dirichlet at T). The tail in squared H¹ norm is the sum of T(1+((k+1/2)π/T)²) times the squared coefficients; for k≥N its norm is ≤exp(−c' T^(4/3)) eventually, absorbing powers. If the truncation loses the primitive moment, the moment error is O(exp(T/2) times the norm of the tail). The moment of the mode k=0 is 2a cosh(T/2)/(1/4+a²) with a=π/(2T), comparable to exp(T/2)/T. Subtracting a multiple of that mode corrects the moment exactly at an H¹ cost at most polynomial in T times the norm of the tail.

**Passage to the energy.** The archimedean part is two-sidedly continuous on H¹ by |m(ξ)|≪log(2+|ξ|). The comb and the polar term cost O(exp T) in L² by 081. Thus correcting and renormalizing the test costs o(1) in energy. The depth ≥exp(2ηT) is retained in the primitive subspace and in the full one. The approximation constants may depend on the chosen zero and on η', without changing the prescribed spaces.

**Scope.** 081 warns about the dimension needed to certify separately the whole complement of a discretization. Here the complement is not certified: the universal negative test fits, to the required precision, in the explicit space. 118 kept the space and changed the form. Here the form is kept and the rate of the minimum of its restriction is computed. A subexponential bound for the negative part of these matrices along an unbounded sequence of windows is still missing.
