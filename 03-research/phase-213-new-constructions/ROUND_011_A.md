# Two distant jump tubes prevent a uniform floor for free remainders

Antecedents 213-10-A, BUILD084 and P46 lem:transl. The local proof requires a floor for any remainder of the domain supported in the tubes. That condition is checked for the full family of profiles, admitting only two of the physical tubes. The prior cross-check distinguished this test from the problem of the remainders actually produced by translations of the same profile.

Fix any δ>0 and take φ real, smooth, nonnegative and nonzero, with compact support in (0,δ). For large integer k, g_k=φ+V_(log k)φ is supported in two tubes of length δ starting at 0 and log k, jump positions of F_k for k≥4. Its squared norm is 2||φ||². The archimedean energy is uniformly bounded above: the transform is the product of that of φ by a factor bounded by 2, and ∫|m||φ̂|² is finite.

The autocorrelation c_φ is nonnegative and exceeds b>0 on [−η,η] for some η>0. The autocorrelation of g_k contains c_φ(d−log k) with positive sign and the remaining contributions are also nonnegative. The comb at d=log n subtracts 2Λ(n)c_(g_k)(log n)/√n. It suffices to make the prime mass in the band |log(p/k)|≤η grow.

For h=η/2, split the primes into bands jh≤log p<(j+1)h. If all sums of (log p)/√p in those bands were bounded by C, the sum of 1/p in band j≥1 would be at most C exp(−jh/2)/(jh). This contradicts the divergence of the sum of reciprocals of primes (elementary proof in 212-95-B). There is a sequence j→∞ with masses tending to infinity. Take k the integer closest to exp((j+1/2)h). The whole band falls in |log(p/k)|≤η for large j. Hence a(g_k)/||g_k||²→−∞ along that sequence.

Thus for each fixed tube length even a finite lower floor uniform in k fails on the free remainders. These tests, positive as functions, have nonzero moments. In the primitive application of 213-10-A the effective remainder g=Y−cF_k has both moments zero, because Y and F_k have them. The question of a uniform radius for primitive combinations of translations of F_k keeps the algebraic constraint linking all its pieces. The argument does not decide the global sign either. PROVED for the floor on free remainders after independent mathematical review.
