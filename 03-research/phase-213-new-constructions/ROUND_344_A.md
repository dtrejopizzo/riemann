# Which terms compensate the isolated rectangle

In the observable A_h of 338 use D=2√X and P,L of 342. For each first index d≤D call complete row the sum over all e≤D and all k, keeping the two pulses W_x(kde)−2W_x(2kde) and the coefficients μ(d)μ(e). Then for large X:

* Before removing the small products, **each row with √X≤d≤2√X is exactly zero**, uniformly for X≤x≤2X.
* After imposing de>P, the sum of the absolute values of the balances of all those rows is O_B(X^(−B)) for each fixed B. Thus the hard rectangle of 340 has an explicit complement within its own rows that compensates it, even before integrating.
* In C_h of 342 one can impose at the same time the new row restriction and the pruning by quotients of 342. It only remains to study rows L<d<√X with e≤D, de>P and reduced quotient of height >X^ρ, in addition to the copy with scaled limits. Here ρ<1/6−7δ/6 and δ=1/j, j≥16.

**Complete rows.** For either of the two pulses and a fixed d, group by m=ek. The inner coefficient is Σ_{e|m,e≤D}μ(e). If d≥2X/D, every m that reaches the support satisfies m≤2X/d≤D. Also m>1 for large X since d≤2√X and the support of both pulses requires dm≥Xe^(−h)/2. By Möbius inversion the coefficient vanishes for each individual m. This includes the interval I_Y of rectangle 340; there the terms with e∈I_Y can be large, and the other e of the same row give exactly their negative. The cancellation uses the arithmetic signs and holds separately in the two pulses before the truncation.

**Restoring the truncation.** For de≤P, the sum of the two pulses in k of 338 costs O_N(X^(−(N−1)δ/4)) per pair, by Poisson with cancellation of the integrals. Since there are O(X) pairs, the errors can be summed in modulus even by rows. After requiring de>P the bounds d,e>L are automatic. In the copy x/4 all index limits are divided by two, including 2X/D, and that of the product by four. The two families of rows are removed with the same arbitrary saving.

**Compatibility with the quotients.** After removing these rows, at fixed quotient r/s the interval J of 342 is intersected with gr<√X. The second copy has exactly the interval J/2. The cancellation of densities and the bound for the variation of each truncated pulse remain valid. This justifies the joint pruning without assuming that two small sets have small intersection.

**What remains of the identity.** For the remaining rows the quotient m can exceed D. The exact balance becomes −Σ_{e|m,e>D}μ(e); if D<m≤2D only −μ(m) remains. Hence the vanishing proved for the upper rows does not formally extend to the whole block. It is elementary divisor inversion; the contribution relative to 340 and 343 is identifying the compensators in the full observable and verifying that they survive the two truncations of 342. The global sign remains open.
