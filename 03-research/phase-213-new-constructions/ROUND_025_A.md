# Coercivity on a fraction of dimensions tending to one

Extension of the mechanism of 024. Antecedents of the independent cross-check: R170_809/810 (compression and means), index at 32420–32422 (triples and scope of a subfamily), rational carrier of 212 (sublinear codimension and bridge separated by refinement). The dimension count here only measures how many directions can have negative sign.

Let 0<α<1/2 be fixed, N_k=3·2^k, t_k=log N_k. In each band [N_k,2N_k) form consecutive groups of L_k=max(1,⌊t_k^α⌋) cells, with a shorter final group if needed. Let w have finite support in the bands k≥m, constant per cell, with zero sum in each group and a single vanishing inverse moment over the whole support. For m sufficiently large,

    a(Uw) ≥ (1/2) Σ_k t_k ||w_k||².

Indeed, for a fixed w, each nonzero restriction to a group is written as its norm times a unit vector of zero sum; the columns so chosen are disjoint. All the following bounds are uniform in these columns. In the comb from k to l≥k+2, write R=2^(l−k), L=L_l. As in 024 the row sum is O(√L), by the two partial sums of length ≤L and Chebyshev. For a column there are at most L−1 possible interior endpoints m', each with at most two rows per divisor n of m', R/2≤n≤2R. Each partial sum costs ≤√L, so the column sum is O(L^(3/2)t_l/R) by the divisor identity. The norm divided by √(t_k t_l) is O(L_l 2^(−(l−k)/2)/√t_k). For d=l−k, t_l=t_k+d log2, so this expression is O(t_m^(α−1/2)(1+d)^α 2^(−d/2)). The symmetric majorant has both sums O(t_m^(α−1/2)). Equal and neighbouring bands cost O(1/t_m) in the same normalization by the contacts n≤4 of 024.

For the off-diagonal archimedean remainder, on two distant bands the unnormalized norm is O(2^(−d/2)) by Hilbert–Schmidt as in 024. On two equal or neighbouring bands apply on the columns the orthogonal projection that subtracts the mean of each group. Write L for the maximal group length in the column band. In a row i separate the groups at minimal distance ≤2L from i. The matrix restricted to those row/group pairs before projecting has both sums O(log(2L)), by the bound O(1/(1+|i−j|)) on the entries and |i−j|≤3L. The projection does not increase the norm and respects this cut by complete groups in each row. For the other groups, subtracting from each entry its mean over the group gives O(L/(1+|i−j|)²), by the bound on Δh of 024. At distances >2L this majorant has both sums O(1). Thus each nearby block costs O(log(2L)); normalized it costs O(log(2t_m)/t_m) uniformly for large m. There are at most three nearby blocks per row or column of bands. Summing by Schur, both normalized remainders tend to zero and the archimedean diagonal log j+O(1) gives the claimed floor.

In a complete band of N_k cells there are g_k=⌈N_k/L_k⌉ groups. In the complex space of cells with both global moments the codimension of the restriction of sums per group is g_k−1 for large k: the group indicators are independent, sum to the constant vector, and the vector of inverse moments varies within a group. Hence the negative index of the primitive form on that band is ≤g_k−1=o(N_k). For several consecutive complete bands k=m,...,n, the same argument bounds the index by Σg_k−1, whose proportion relative to the total number of cells is O(1/L_m), uniformly in n. A single negative direction would suffice to violate the global sign. PROVED after independent mathematical audit.

**Why replicating a test does not transfer this floor.** For fixed primitive f, cellwise constant and with compact support in (0,∞), its normalized integer replica z_j=q^(−1/2)f(j/q) preserves the norm and the energy by logarithmic translation (212-75 and 213-007). When subtracting the group means only groups crossing jumps survive. Their number is bounded by the number of jumps of f, their length is O_f((log q)^α) and the initial amplitude is O_f(1/√q). The projection is contractive on each group. The squared norm of the projected part is therefore O_f((log q)^α/q). Intersecting with the kernel of the inverse moment only reduces the norm of the orthogonal projection. Thus the distance of the replica to the signed space tends to ||f||, even in Euclidean norm. Corollary independently reviewed.

**Necessary comparison with P46.** For [N,2N) and [N,4N), centring the logarithmic support gives respectively T=(log 2)/2 and T=log 2. P46, thm:log5A and thm:log5W, already gives index zero on the whole corresponding carrier. For d consecutive bands with d fixed, lem:compact gives index O_d(1), by comparison with the window T=d(log 2)/2. The multiscale content of 025 is the weighted floor on an explicit subspace with cut independent of d. Cutting at N_m of order √X and extending the last band by zero, the same floor leaves a positive subspace of codimension O_α(X/(log X)^α) in the full cell matrices up to X: N_m initial coordinates, one inverse moment and at most O(X/(log X)^α+log X) groups are paid. Corollary and comparison independently cross-checked.
