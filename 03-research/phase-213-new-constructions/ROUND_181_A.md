# Sign of the true form at almost all modulations at the height of 180

Fix κ>0. For T→∞ put G=exp(κ exp T). There is an exceptional set of frequencies in [2G,3G] of measure ≤G^(1/2+o(1)) such that, at each remaining Γ, one has simultaneously
    W_T(exp(−iΓ·)h) ≥ (1−o(1))log G
for every unit h in L², supported in [−T,T], whose extension by zero has total variation ≤exp T. The constants may depend on κ; the relative loss tends to zero uniformly over the indicated profiles. This uses the true primes without a verification hypothesis at height G. The conclusion refers to modulations of controlled profiles, not to the whole unit ball of the form.

**Moments over primes.** Let X=exp(2T) and D(t)=Σ_{p≤X}a_p exp(it log p), a_p=(log p)/√p. By Chebyshev and partial summation Σa_p²=O(T²), Σa_p=O(exp T). The part of the comb with powers of exponent ≥2 has sup norm O(T) by Mertens for the squares and absolute convergence for exponents ≥3. The factor two of the complete comb is kept when estimating 2 Re D.

For j=floor((log G−3 log log G)/(2T)), D^j is a Dirichlet polynomial of length Y≤G/(log G)^3. Unique factorization gives Σ|b_n|²≤j!(Σa_p²)^j for its coefficients: the square of the multinomial coefficient of each multiset is ≤j! times the multinomial coefficient itself. On [G,4G] the integral of |D|^(2j) is ≤C G Σ|b_n|². Indeed each off-diagonal integral costs ≤2/|log(n/l)|≤2Y/|n−l|; use 2|b_nb_l|≤|b_n|²+|b_l|² and sum the harmonic series. For λ=log G/T, Chebyshev gives
    meas{|D|>λ/2} ≤ C G [Cj T²/λ²]^j = G^(1/2+o(1))
as an upper bound. Here log j=T+O(log T), log(λ)=T+O(log T), and j=(1+o(1))log G/(2T). The prime phases are not treated as independent.

**A common band for all profiles.** Let L=exp(3T). Since ||D'||∞=O(T exp T), each point of [2G−L,3G+L] with |D|>λ has around it an interval of radius c_κ/T² with |D|>λ/2. Divide that region into cells of sufficiently small length c_κ/T². Every cell containing such a point lies in the lower superlevel set, and the partition can be extended one step at both ends inside [G,4G]. The number of affected cells is ≤O_κ(T²)G^(1/2+o(1)). Enlarging them by L on each side keeps the measure bound G^(1/2+o(1)) because log L=3T=o(log G). Outside that union one has |D(t)|≤λ on the whole band |t−Γ|≤L; conjugation gives the same statement near −Γ.

**Estimate on the real datum.** By total variation, |ĥ(v)|≤exp(T)/|v|, so the normalized Fourier mass outside |v|≤L is O(exp(−T)), independently of h. On the band centred at Γ the archimedean multiplier is ≥log G−O(1) and the comb has modulus ≤2λ+O(T). On the complement the global floor C₀−A_T suffices, with A_T=O(exp T). On integrating one loses O(1) there in addition to the fraction O(exp(−T)) of the main term. The polar moments of the modulated test cost O(exp(3T)/Γ²) in the form by total variation of h(t)exp(±t/2), which is O(exp(3T/2)). These estimates prove the statement for the full form.

**Comparison with 180.** For the specific odd profile of 180, the archimedean ceiling log G+O(1), together with the same control of the comb, also gives W_T≤(1+o(1))log G at all these good frequencies. Hence, if 0<κ<1, the artificial quartet of 180 can be placed where the true form has a positive margin of size log G for the whole class of profiles. The twist still changes the arithmetic side. The measure bound leaves open the existence of exceptional frequencies and does not control profiles of arbitrary variation either. The antecedent in P46 is the mean square of the comb in prop:shape-crit; here the interest is in high moments on the finite band with G tied to T and the same exceptional set for all the indicated tests.
