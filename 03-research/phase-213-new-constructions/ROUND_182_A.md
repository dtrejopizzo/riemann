# Margin at two neighbouring modulations over the whole band of 181

Let δ=π/(2T), M_v f(t)=exp(ivt)f(t) and A the archimedean form. For T≥1 and every f in the domain of 47 supported in [−T,T],

    |(W−A)(f)+(W−A)(M_δ f)| ≤ C exp(T)/T ||f||₂².

Indeed the comb and the polar term act on the autocorrelation of f, of modulus at most ||f||₂². Its support is |s|≤2T. On adding the two evaluations the factor 1+exp(±iδs) appears, whose modulus is ≤δ(2T−|s|). The polar density has modulus ≤2 exp(|s|/2), and the integral weighted by 2T−|s| is O(exp T), by the change u=2T−|s|. For the comb, in each strip r≤2T−log n<r+1 with r a nonnegative integer, the mass of Λ(n)/√n is O(exp(T−r/2)) by the Chebyshev bound and the lower end of the strip. Summing (r+1)exp(−r/2) and multiplying by δ proves the claim, including the prime powers.

For fixed positive κ, G=exp(κ exp T), every Γ in [2G,3G] and each unit profile h of 181 (variation of its extension by zero ≤exp T), it follows that

    max{W(M_Γ h),W(M_(Γ+δ) h)} ≥ log G−O_κ(exp(T)/T).

In each of the two modulations, the band |v|≤exp(3T) of the profile contains relative mass 1−O(exp(−T)) by the BV estimate of 181. There the archimedean symbol is ≥log G−O(1), and on the rest it has an absolute floor. Thus each archimedean term contributes ≥log G−O_κ(1). Apply the first inequality and take the mean. The member of the pair attaining the margin may depend on h. One pair is controlled at each frequency, even inside the exceptional set of 181. The inequality allows one of the two values to be negative and does not change the window of 179.
