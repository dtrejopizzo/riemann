# A single fixed block can contain the whole criterion; there is also detection in the interior

For the h of the partition of 075 write D_P(x)=Σ_p(log p)h(p/P)U(x/p)−∫h(t/P)U(x/t)dt, allowing real positive P. There are two different conclusions.

1. With an admissible choice of the dyadic transition, the one-sided ceiling O(√x) at fourth powers for the single block P=1 is already equivalent to the sign.
2. For any real, smooth, nonnegative h, not identically zero and with compact support in (0,∞), the sign is equivalent to the nonexistence of a sequence of real scales P→∞ and fourth powers x with x/P→∞ and D_P(x)/√x→+∞. If the sign fails, the witnesses can moreover satisfy x≥G(P) for any prescribed finite function G. Neither a law P=x^a nor this interior statement for P restricted to powers of two is obtained.

**Detection at a fixed scale.** In Mellin the transform in x for fixed P is Û(s) M_P(s), where the entire multiplier is
     
    M_P(s)=Σ_p(log p)h(p/P)p^(−s) − ∫ h(t/P)t^(−s)dt.

If M_P(ρ)≠0 for some zero off the line with Re ρ>1/2, an eventual upper bound at fourth powers is impossible. The two logarithmic derivatives of the fixed block are O_P(x); interpolating costs O_P(√x). Landau forces holomorphy in Re s>1/2 as in 069, contradicting the simple pole at ρ. This remains true when D_P is replaced by −D_P.

Take τ smooth nondecreasing, zero up to 1, one from 5/4 on, and h(t)=τ(t)−τ(t/2). At P=1 only the prime 2 enters, with h(2)=1. The multiplier is
    
    (log 2)2^(−s) − [(2^(1−s)−1)/(1−s)]∫ τ'(u)u^(1−s)du.

In 1/2<Re s<1, |Im s|≥10 the second term has modulus ≤(√2+1)√(5/4)/10 < (log 2)/2, a lower bound for the modulus of the first. The verified data cited in paper 47 already exclude zeros off the line below that height. Hence every possible relevant pole survives. Under the sign the necessity of the ceiling for the fixed block is immediate from |U(x)|≪√x. This explains why the partition equivalence of 075 by itself did not constitute an arithmetic simplification.

**Detection for arbitrarily large P.** Suppose such a ρ exists. Let H(z)=∫h(u)u^z du/u. In Re z>1−Re ρ, by absolute convergence and integration in P,
     
    ∫₁^∞ M_P(ρ)P^(−z)dP/P
      = H(z)Σ_p(log p)p^(−ρ−z) − [∫h(u)u^(−ρ)du]/(ρ+z−1) + E(z).

E is entire: for each prime term the change u=p/P replaces H by the integral up to p, and only finitely many primes cut the support of h. The continuous density was integrated directly from P=1. The prime series continues as −ζ'/ζ(ρ+z)−T(ρ+z), with T holomorphic for Re(ρ+z)>1/2 by 074. At z=0 the right side has residue −mult(ρ)H(0)≠0. If M_P(ρ) vanished for all large P, the integral on the left would be entire by compact support in P. The contradiction gives arbitrarily large scales P with M_P(ρ)≠0. A continuous transform in P is used; no sum of poles is interchanged over a mesh of frequencies.

Choose P_n→∞ among those scales. For each fixed n, by the detection argument above there exist arbitrarily large fourth powers with D_{P_n}(x)/√x>n. One may impose at the same time x≥max(nP_n,G(P_n)). Necessity under the sign in the opposite direction is the uniform bound of the smooth block of 075 (the argument holds on any fixed positive compact set). The reasoning also gives sequences of opposite sign if the sign fails. There is no effective control of how large the chosen fourth powers must be.

**Cross-check.** The nonzero multiplier and Landau are inherited from 047 and 060. In 075 all blocks were summed and their constants had to be common. Here the auxiliary transform in P prevents a single zero from staying hidden throughout the tail of continuous scales. The uniform estimate in that interior region remains open. The observation on P=1 avoids again calling sign progress a reformulation that already contains the problem in its first block.

**Control without prime atoms.** If the partition ratio may be changed to R=5/4, the same degeneration can be checked without numerical zero data. Let τ be the cumulative distribution of exp((log R)V), with V of law ν from 047, and h(t)=τ(t)−τ(t/R). At P=1 the support [1,R²] contains no primes and the block is just the continuous integral with a minus sign. Its multiplier is −[(R^(1−s)−1)/(1−s)]P_ν((1−s)log R), by integration by parts. Both factors are nonzero for 1/2<Re s<1 by the convergent product of 047. Thus even this prime-free block is equivalent to the sign. It is a direct application of the compact averaging mechanism of 047; by itself it does not provide an estimate.

**Dyadic scales with a profile chosen once.** For any admissible nonzero h as in item 2, write h_λ(u)=u^λ h(u), with 0<λ<1. Except for an at most countable set of λ, the multiplier for h_λ is nonzero simultaneously at each exterior zero ρ with Re ρ>1/2 and at each P=2^k, k≥0. Indeed, for fixed ρ and P it is an entire function of λ, the exponential transform of a compact complex measure in v=log(t/P). Its continuous part, coming from −h(t/P)t^(−ρ)dt, is nonzero; the prime part has only finitely many atoms and cannot cancel it. If the transform were identically zero, all integrals of polynomials in v would be zero, and by uniform approximation the measure would be zero. Thus the zeros in λ are isolated. The union over the pairs (ρ,k) is countable.

Consequently, if RH fails, for almost every λ and for EACH prescribed dyadic scale there are excursions of both signs with D_P(j⁴)/j² unbounded, using the fixed-P argument of this note. For any prescribed increasing sequence of dyadic scales one can choose witnesses with j⁴≥G(P) for any given finite G. The witnesses may depend on the profile and the scale. The bound under RH of 075 is preserved for that fixed profile. This argument settles the mesh restriction for generic profiles within the family, without effective constants or a verdict on λ=0. A dyadic mesh by itself does not eliminate the excursions in these blocks; for the sign the arithmetic compensation is still missing.

**Cost of choosing the profile after seeing the primes.** In a suitable regime x/P→y fixed there is an unconditional obstacle to optimizing the profile within each block. There exist c>0, y>0 such that for every large P=2^k and L≥1 there is a fourth power x with x/P→y and a smooth profile 0≤h≤1 supported in (1,2), with ||h'||∞≤L, for which

    |D_P(x)| ≥ c min(P,L log P).

Indeed, U is continuous and not identically zero (074 exhibits a nonzero residue of its transform). Choose y and a closed interval I interior to (1,2) where U(y/u) has fixed sign and modulus bounded away from zero. Take j the integer nearest to (yP)^(1/4), x=j⁴; by uniform continuity the margin on I is preserved. Divide I into M equal intervals with M an integer comparable to K P/log P, where K is a large fixed constant. By Chebyshev there are O(P/log P) primes in [P,2P], so at least M/2 intervals have interior free of p/P. On each of these translate and rescale a single smooth positive bump with compact support interior to (0,1), of height proportional to min(1,L|I|/M), choosing the constant to satisfy both norm bounds. Sum the bumps. The prime sum is zero, while the integral has modulus ≥cP min(1,L/M) by the fixed sign. The argument also allows choosing the sign of the integral if both signs of U are available at different scales.

Thus, when L log P/√P tends to infinity, a root bound with a common constant for this class of profiles is false already at fourth powers, independently of RH. The profile-dependent constant in 075 is essential. The obstruction concerns the end with x/P bounded and weights varying with P; it does not decide compensation for fixed profiles in the interior.

**Exchange of scales for the original profile.** Now fix Y=x/P, let P be real and keep any smooth nonnegative nonzero h from item 2, without tilting it. Let g_Y(u)=h(u)U(Y/u). Then D_P(YP) is exactly the prime discrepancy with test g_Y(t/P). The residue of its Mellin transform in P at a hypothetical right zero ρ is −mult(ρ) C_Y(ρ), with C_Y(ρ)=∫h(u)U(Y/u)u^ρ du/u. Indeed, integrating from a small fixed positive P (below which no primes enter) multiplies the prime series by the positive Mellin transform of g_Y; the continuous density only cancels the pole at 1 and adds regular terms near ρ. The higher powers are holomorphic in Re s>1/2, as in 074.

For Re z>1, integrating C_Y(ρ) in Y with weight Y^(−z)dY/Y gives exactly Û(z)H(ρ−z). At z=ρ the pole survives because H(0)>0. C_Y is zero for Y small enough by the lower support of U. Hence it cannot be zero for all large Y: its transform would be entire. In particular the original profile detects the zero for arbitrarily large fixed ratios Y. Moreover, at those ratios the integral determining the residue already encodes the same transform of U as in 074.

For each such Y, Landau applied to the block at fixed Y gives unbounded excursions of both signs after dividing by √P: the transform is regular on the real axis s>1/2, including 1 after centering. They also hold at P=j⁴/Y: two logarithmic derivatives are O_Y(P) by smoothness, compact support and Chebyshev, and the interpolation error between samples is O_Y(√P). Under RH the bound of 075 applies. Thus the ceiling at each sufficiently large fixed ratio Y, even with a Y-dependent constant, implies RH. This is the residue argument of 076 in the opposite variable; splitting the proof into fixed-ratio problems and letting their constants worsen with the ratio is discarded as a shortcut. It does not select an individual Y in advance nor simultaneously impose dyadic P.

**Independent review of the extensions: PROVED.** The complex measure of the generic argument, in v=log(t/P), has density −P^(1−ρ)h(exp v)exp((1−ρ)v) and a finite atomic part. The nonzero density cannot be annihilated by the atoms. Uniqueness of compact moments proves that its transform in λ is not identically zero; only then are discreteness of its zeros and the countable union over (ρ,k) used. It is the same generic analytic selection mechanism of 292 and 298, applied here to profiles and scales, not a new source of compensation. It does not certify an explicit λ, nor λ=0, nor that a prescribed irrational avoids the exceptional set. The perturbation u^λh need not preserve the telescoping partition: detection uses the multiplier of the fixed block and the bound of 075 for a positive compact set.

In the adaptive profile, choosing M≥2 times the Chebyshev bound leaves M/2 empty cells; the bumps have support strictly inside them, so no primes enter at their edges either. Their height is ≍min(1,L/M), their total integral is of the same order and their derivative does not exceed L after fixing the constant. The choice of the fourth power has error x/P−y=O(P^(−1/4)), independent of the budget L. This confirms the floor c min(P,LlogP), without claiming an interior witness with x/P→∞ for those moving profiles.

For the exchange of scales, the one-sided Mellin transform in P has the form C_Y(s)Σ_p(logp)p^(−s)−C_Y(1)P₀^(1−s)/(s−1), up to an entire function; the pole at 1 cancels exactly and the residue at ρ is −mult(ρ)C_Y(ρ). The second Mellin transform is exactly Û(z)H(ρ−z). By continuity of C_Y in Y, its arbitrarily large nonzero values also exist at rational ratios. Hence the arithmetic criterion suffices, without exterior ordinates as data: for each sufficiently large rational Y, the block at P=j⁴/Y satisfies a ceiling C_Yj² eventually. Under RH it holds by 075 and, if RH fails, some rational Y violates the ceiling in both directions. It is not a finite proof and does not decide an individual ray chosen in advance.

There is moreover a sufficient non-cancellation certificate for a given original profile at fixed P, without evaluating M at any exterior ordinate. Write M_P=A_P−I_P. If some atom q satisfies, uniformly in 1/2≤σ≤1,

    (log q)h(q/P)q^(−σ)−Σ_{p≠q}(logp)h(p/P)p^(−σ) ≥ d>0,

and |I_P(σ+it)|<d is certified for |t|≥T, then every possible exterior zero above T survives. The second condition is obtained from integration by parts in log t with derivative norms of the profile; it suffices to verify the critical line up to T. The concrete transition profile of this note satisfies it at P=1,T=10 with d=log2/2 and the bound already written. The atom-free example with ratio 5/4 is another direct application of the zero-free multiplier of 047. For an arbitrary h without those certificates, the generic argument does not decide the original case λ=0. The targeted cross-check of the notes and index found no exact earlier application to all dyadic blocks; the averaging and generic selection mechanisms were already available.
