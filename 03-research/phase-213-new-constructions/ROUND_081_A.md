# Unavoidable cost of separating the comb from the archimedean symbol on the complement

In the normalization of paper 46 let C_T be the comb with positive sign, so that W=m(D)−C_T+polar in the window. Then ||C_T||=(1+o(1))exp(T) as T→∞. For each T, the essential supremum of C_T coincides exactly with ||C_T||, even after imposing any finite number of continuous linear constraints in L². Therefore separating the archimedean floor and the non-archimedean floor on the complement of cells, even using their true infima, requires log(1/h)≥(1−o(1))exp(T) to obtain a non-negative floor, for h≤1. This is a limitation of the separated certificate, not of the full form.

**Leading constant of the compressed comb.** C_T sums the compressed translations ±log n, each with coefficient Λ(n)/√n. It is self-adjoint and preserves non-negative functions; |⟨f,C_T f⟩|≤⟨|f|,C_T|f|⟩. Use w(t)=exp(t/2)+exp(−t/2) on [−T,T]. For almost every t in the interval, with X_±=exp(T±t), A(x)=Σ_{n≤x}Λ(n)/n and ψ(x)=Σ_{n≤x}Λ(n), one has
    
    C_T w(t)=e^(−t/2) ψ(X_+) + e^(t/2) ψ(X_−)
             + e^(t/2) A(X_+) + e^(−t/2) A(X_−).

The choice of ≤ only changes the values on a null set. The A terms add up to O((1+T)w) by Chebyshev. Replacing ψ(X) by X in both leading terms gives exactly exp(T)w(t). The error is o(exp(T)w) uniformly: for X_±≥exp(T/2) the uniform PNT applies in the tail. If for example X_+<exp(T/2), then t<−T/2 and the contribution majorized by Chebyshev is O(exp(T)e^(t/2)), at most O(exp(−T/2)) in proportion to exp(T)w. The other case is symmetric. The Rayleigh quotient of w gives the lower bound and the weighted Schur test for the positive symmetric kernel gives the upper one. This argument uses a genuine row of the compressed operator, not the supremum of the symbol on the whole line.

**Complements and polar operator.** With T fixed, choose frequencies ν_j→∞ such that exp(iν_j log n)→1 simultaneously for all contacts, by recurrence on a finite torus. For any unit vector f the modulations f_j=exp(iν_j t)f tend weakly to zero and their quotients for C_T converge to that of f. Projecting them off a finite-dimensional subspace changes their norms and their quotients by o(1). The variational argument with f arbitrarily close to the supremum also shows that every subspace of finite codimension preserves that supremum; this is the variational characterization of the essential edge. Any bounded compact form has value o(1) on the f_j. Hence the infimum of −C_T+polar on the complement is ≤−||C_T||. The sign of the comb here comes from its positive entries; C_T is not assumed positive semidefinite.

**What this imposes on the cell size.** The floor of the archimedean block on the complement of the means is ≤log(1/h)+O(1), with an absolute constant for h≤1. It suffices to place inside a cell the unitary dilation of a fixed smooth function, of norm one, support in (0,1) and zero integral. By m(ξ)≤C+log(2+|ξ|), the additional cost relative to log(1/h) is bounded by the logarithmic moment of that fixed profile. The lower bound of 078 has the same order with bounded additive error. Adding the two best separated floors gives at most log(1/h)+O(1)−||C_T||. Since N=2T/h, a strategy that needs the positivity of that sum forces log N≥(1−o(1))exp(T). Refining the cross correction of 080 does not change this necessity. The modulation proving the obstruction does not have bounded archimedean cost: it is precisely the coupling of frequency and comb that remains available in a joint estimate.

**Cross-check.** P46 lem:mbounds already uses Kronecker for the uncompressed symbol, and its proof of non-compactness of the comb uses a weakly null sequence. 026 and 079 already warned that a constant floor could be costly. Here the essential edge of the compressed comb in growing windows is quantified and a doubly exponential necessity for cell separation is deduced. It is not a limit for all Schur methods nor a statement about λ_W(T).

**Earlier problem that is closed.** The closest antecedent is phase-211-sign-sandbox/ROUND_006_A.md, Theorems 1–3. There the supremum was already transported to the Tate restriction by modulation and the liminf with cosh(t/2) was proved; the recorded interval for the quotient by A_T=Σv_n was [1/4,1/2]. What the computation of weighted rows adds is the matching limsup: by partial summation and PNT, A_T∼4exp(T), so the quotient converges to 1/4, also in the primitive space of finite codimension. The deduction of cost per cell additionally uses 078. The modulation and the lower test are not counted as new.
