# Size of the excesses at the original crossings if there is an attained extreme

If b=max(Re ρ−1/2)>0 exists, there are c>0 and K>1 such that for all X large enough, [X,KX] contains a crossing p_n<H_n<p_(n+1) with −B_n≥c H_n^(1/2+b). It also contains a prime p_m with H_(m−1)<p_m<H_m where F(p_m)≤−c p_m^(1/2+b), with F=S−x. The two statements admit the same constants after reducing c and increasing K. Thus the maximal order x^(1/2+b) is attained with both signs at local extrema of F in each multiplicative band of that width.

Indeed by 092 and the dominated convergence of 091, for x=exp(2T) one has F(x)/x^(1/2+b)=−P(T)+o(1), where P is the absolutely convergent series of the extreme zeros with coefficients 1/ρ² and phases exp(2iγT). It is real, almost periodic and of mean zero, by term-by-term integration with no zero frequency. P(0)<0 by 091 and it takes some positive value: otherwise, its uniform returns near the strictly negative initial value would give intervals of fixed length with bounded gaps where P stays below a negative constant, contradicting the mean. The uniform returns to each value supply constants η,L>0 such that every interval of length L contains values above η and below −η. This uses the continuous recurrence already used in phase 159 G2_B5_R5.

Split [log(X)/2,log(X)/2+3L] into three pieces. Choose in order signs −,+,− of F, with moduli ≥(η/2)x^(1/2+b). The maximum between the extremes gives the strict crossing of 094. Since x varies at most by the factor K=exp(6L), the order is preserved with c=(η/2)K^(−1/2−b). Repeat with signs +,−,+ for the minimum. In a gap F is strictly concave, so the interior minimum can only be at a prime. If the left derivative there were zero, F would increase immediately before by strict concavity; if the right one were zero, it would decrease immediately after. Hence the jump crosses zero strictly at that prime. The upper bound of order x^(1/2+b) for |F| follows from the same bounded series.

**Scope.** The unshifted crossings of 094 are used. The hypothesis of an attained extreme gives control of the ratio between the scales of the witness and of the maximum; the mere two-sided oscillation of 092 does not give that control.
