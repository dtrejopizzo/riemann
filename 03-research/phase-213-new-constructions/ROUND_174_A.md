# A finite verification threshold that suffices for strict positivity in each fixed window

For each T>0 there are numbers G_T and c_T>0, determined by archimedean data, the primes of an enlarged window and unconditional zero-counting bounds, with the following implication: if all zeros up to G_T are on the critical line, then W_T(f)≥c_T||f||₂² for every admissible test. The conclusion is indeed strict positivity. The hypothesis has NOT been verified for a new window; in particular G_T is not identified with the height of Z2. The construction does not use the individual ordinates of the verified zeros.

**Margin that was previously discarded.** Keep the extensions b(τ)dτ and μ of 173 on |u|<2T+1. Choose R≥1, M≥1 so that b≥1 on |τ|≥R and b≥−M on the whole axis; they exist by the arithmetic formula of 173. For tests supported in [−T,T] there is η>0, depending on T and R, with
    ∫_{|τ|≥2R}|f̂|² ≥ η∫|f̂|².
Indeed, the compression to [−T,T] of the Fourier projection onto [−2R,2R] is a compact positive operator K with continuous sinc kernel. If its norm were one, an eigenfunction of norm one would have both supports compact simultaneously, which excludes the entire continuation of its transform. η=1−||K|| works. If a rational lower bound is required, K is trace class and Tr(K^n)→0. Search n until Tr(K^n)<1 is certified by integration with controlled error of the product of continuous kernels on the compact cube; 1−Tr(K^n)^(1/n) then gives a positive lower bound. This termination argument does not estimate the cost.

Choose an integer m with 2^m η>2M and AFTERWARDS fix H>max(10R,2m+1). Write a=R²/(3H²), u(τ)=1−sinc²(τ/H) and φ=1−u^m. On |τ|≤R one has u≤a (by sinc v≥1−v²/6). On |τ|≥2R one has u≥2a: for |v|≤1 use 1−sinc² v≥v²/4 by the alternating Taylor series; for |v|≥1, |sinc v|≤max(sin(1),1/2) using monotonicity up to 2 and the majorant 1/|v| afterwards. Hence, for Δ_T=a^m(2^mη−M)>0,
    ∫ b(1−φ)|f̂|² ≥ Δ_T ∫|f̂|².
The band between R and 2R only adds positively. The margin depends only on the parameters already fixed, without involvement of G.

**Absorbing the correction.** Since 2m/H<1, λ=φμ+(1−φ)b dτ reproduces exactly the datum of W_T by 173. The atoms of φμ are positive. Under verification up to G, for large G the continuous remainder satisfies
    ||φ C||∞ ≤ C_T [G^(−3)log(2G)+mH²G^(−2)log(2G)].
Use exponent four of the correction of 172 when |τ|≤G/2 and φ≤m sinc²(τ/H) for |τ|>G/2. The constant is obtained from four derivative norms of a fixed cutoff on compact support and the unit count with multiplicities, and is independent of G. Choose G_T so large that the right-hand side is ≤Δ_T/2 for G≥G_T. Plancherel gives the statement, with the factor 2π of the corresponding convention. The smooth core extends to the domain of the form as in 173.

**Scope relative to the corpus.** finZ of the paper prevents certifying a flat-top profile by a truncated spectral sum alone. Here one additionally uses the exact arithmetic density b of the enlarged window and an estimate of ALL the remaining zeros. Uncertainty is used only for a fixed positive margin; the threshold may be immense. The almost-positive bound of 173 by itself did not give this implication, because its mask depended on G and its positive margin was not controlled. To obtain a new effective window one must exhibit data R,M,η,m,H and correction bounds that actually pass at the verified height, or improve the margin; there is no estimate uniform in T nor a proof of the global sign.
