# A single channel of possible negativity in a band of length proportional to K

**Cross-check.** In 020-A the whole carrier is controlled for a band of length o(K). Here a fixed endpoint ratio C>1 is kept and one coordinate of the head is split off. The codimension-one arguments of P47 correspond to other operators. The specific step is a uniform comparison with W of the tail that keeps its internal contacts.

With the notation of 020-A, let f=zp+et and b a tail of the domain supported on [K,CK]. We impose the two moments of f+b. On the subspace z=0 one has, uniformly in b,

    a(U(et+b)) = W(Ub) + o_C(1)||b||₂²,     K→∞.

Indeed, ∫b/x=−e implies |e|≤[(1−1/C)/K]^(1/2)||b||₂. Moreover ∫b=−e∫t. The pole of Ub is therefore O_C(|e|²), and the head energy is O(|e|²). For the archimedean crossing the separated kernel is O_C(1/K), so at most O_C(|e| ||b||₂/√K) is paid. In the mixed comb S_t(y)=ΣΛ(n)t(y/n)/n appears with t=1_[1,2)/log 2. By PNT and partial summation on (y/2,y], S_t(y)=1+o(1) uniformly for K≤y≤CK. The constant one pays only ∫b=O(|e|). The error costs o(1)|e| ||b||₁=o_C(1)||b||₂², since the band has length (C−1)K. This proves the comparison including the factor two of the polarization. The off-diagonal identities on the domain are justified as in 020-A.

If W has floor λ>0 on the window of length log C, by translation invariance W(Ub)≥λ||b||₂². For K sufficiently large the subspace z=0 has floor λ/2 in the tail norm. Every two-dimensional complex subspace contains a nonzero vector with z=0 and it has nonzero b. Hence the negative index of the primitive form restricted to head and band is at most one. The same argument excludes even a plane on which the form is nonpositive. Arbitrary gaps inside the band preserve the claim by restriction.

The hypothesis on W holds unconditionally for C>1 sufficiently close to 1: relocate the window around zero and apply the short-support floor of 010-A to the pole-free part, while the polar term has modulus ≤2 exp(ℓ/2)ℓ||Ub||², ℓ=log C, by Cauchy–Schwarz. For other ratios C a verified floor of the full local form suffices. A floor of the primitive restriction alone on that window does not suffice to invoke the argument.
