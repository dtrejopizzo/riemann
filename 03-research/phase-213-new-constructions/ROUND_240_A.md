# Transition law for the polynomial fit of a compact prime count

A, D and the degree m of 239 are kept, changing the fixed kernel once. Let a_j=1/[20j(1+log j)²], W the convolution of the uniforms on [0,a_j], and H its transform ∫W(v)exp(sv)dv. For t=log X, 0<h≤1 and log(1/h)/(log t log log t)→λ finite, λ≥0, the result is

    lim log(1+D(t,h))/t = 2 max(B−λ,0),
    B=sup_ρ(Re ρ−1/2).

**Kernel.** The sum of lengths is ≤1/10 by integration. The proof by differentiating boxes of 239 gives smoothness, and the product H does not vanish for Re s>0. For large |τ| the first J=floor(c|τ|/log²|τ|) factors have modulus ≤1/2 uniformly in 0≤Re s≤1: take c small so that a_J|τ|≥6. The remaining factors in product cost ≤exp(1/10). Thus |H(s)|≤C exp(−c'|τ|/log²|τ|).

**Upper bound.** The smoothed explicit formula of 239 gives A(u)=−Σ_ρ H(ρ)exp(ρu)+O(exp(u/2)poly(u)) for large u, counting multiplicity. The error includes the prime powers. Let r=m+1. By the unit count of zeros and the decay above, Σ |H(ρ)| |ρ|^r≤exp(O(r log(r+2))) (the majorant exp(−c√|τ|) suffices). Use the real Taylor polynomial of degree m of the spectral sum with its sign at u=t. Conjugation guarantees real coefficients. The remainder on [t,t+h] costs at most exp((1/2+B)t+O(r log(r+2))) h^r. Here r log r=o(t) and r log(1/h)=λt+o(t). Integrating the square and adding the power error gives the announced limsup.

**Lower bound.** λ<B suffices. Fix a zero ρ₀ with β₀>1/2+λ and choose 1/2<σ<β₀−λ. Up to height G=t(log t)³, Ingham as in 230 gives M≤t^(q+o(1)) other distinct zeros with β≥σ, q=3(1−σ)/(2−σ)<1. For each w=ρ−ρ₀ of this list take the measure of mass one (δ_d−exp(wd)δ₀)/(1−exp(wd)), d=h/[10(G+1)(M+1)]. Its exponential transform vanishes at w. The distances |w| are uniformly bounded away from zero by discreteness around the fixed zero; |wd|≤1/2 for large t. The logarithm of the variation of the convolution product is O(M(log(G+2)+log(M+2)+log(1/h)+1))=o(t).

Add r factors of the same formula with w=−ρ₀ and d=h/(4r). They impose a zero of multiplicity r and cost in variation at most exp(λt+o(t)). Convolve with the uniform on [0,h/4]. The resulting complex density f has support in [0,h], mass one and L² norm ≤exp(λt+o(t)) since log(1/h)=o(t). On integrating (A(t+v)−P(v)) exp(−ρ₀(t+v))f(v)dv, every polynomial of degree ≤m disappears by multiplicity. The chosen zero contributes exactly −mult(ρ₀)H(ρ₀). The other zeros up to G with β≥σ disappear. Those with β<σ and the prime powers cost together ≤exp((σ−β₀+λ)t+o(t)); for |Im ρ|>G the absolute sum of the weights is ≤exp(−c G/log²G), which absorbs any exp(Ct). Thus the modulus of the integral stays bounded away from zero. Cauchy–Schwarz implies D≥exp((2β₀−1−2λ)t−o(t)). Taking the supremum over β₀ concludes. For λ≥B the lower bound for log(1+D) is immediate.

**Arithmetic obligation.** For this kernel, a subsequence with D=exp(o(t)) or smaller at a fixed λ implies B≤λ. It suffices to achieve such control for arbitrarily small positive λ, even with different subsequences. The spectral proof computes the exact cost of the fit but does not produce those subsequences. Compared with 239, the multiplier and the finite elimination of zeros change, while locality in primes is preserved.
