# An explicit unconditional ceiling for the terminal prime

**Status: PROVED, with independent review of the free-region ceiling, the growing band and the saddles.** With h=log log x and Q=h^(2/3)(log h)^(1/3), there is an absolute constant D such that, eventually,

    p_*(x) ≤ 2 exp(D h Q).

In 392 only x^o(1) was obtained by diagonalization. The goal of 393 is log p_*≤(2+o(1))h, outside this bound.

**Size of the target to cover.** In the contour of 377 use the strip of 371 up to 2√L, with shift c/Q. For z=L² one has log E_z(1−c/Q)≪exp(2ch/Q)log h=o(L/Q), and the reciprocal of ζ costs exp(O(h²)) in the inner strip. The Gaussian tails remain negligible. Hence |S|≤exp(L−c₁L/Q) unconditionally with c₁>0.

**Saddle of a growing band.** For exp(DhQ)≤Y≤2exp(DhQ) set B=log Y, β the positive saddle, d=1−β, ξ=dB. One has exp(ξ)/d∼L, ξ∼h, d∼1/(DQ), M=Σa_p∼L/B, where a_p=h_pp^(−β) and h_p=1 except for the allowed terminal weight. Indeed, for ξ=h−log(B/h)+c with bounded c, Σ(log p)a_p∼exp(ξ)/d by partial summation, and the denominators 1+a_p change the sum by O(1); try positive and negative c and use the saddle equation. The law of r_p=d(B−log p) with weights a_p/M converges to exp(−r)dr with all fixed moments. For log p≥B/2, the change r in the PNT integral gives that limit on compact sets and the bands j≤r<j+1 have mass O(M exp(−j)) by Chebyshev and partial summation. On log p<B/2 the mass is O(exp(ξ/2)log B), negligible even multiplied by ξ to any fixed power. This also holds for the weights of the mean of 398 that contain log p. One obtains G'' of size L B near the two main phases and G'''=O(LB²).

**Separation of the two phases.** The cost in the logarithm of the signed modulus is M times the mean of 1+cos(t log p), with error O(Σa_p²)=O(1). For |t|≤K/B study tB=ℓπ+q/ξ with odd ℓ and bounded q, obtaining a cost normalized by M/ξ² equal to [(q−ℓπ)²+(ℓπ)²]/2+o(1), by the moments up to order four. If ξ dist(tB,π+2πℤ)→∞ the subblock 0≤r≤1 suffices. For K/B≤|t|≤2√L the band 0≤r≤1 gives loss ≫M min((t/d)²,1), by the phase-length argument of 370 with free offset: keep a fixed proportion of logarithmic length in subintervals of length ≫min(1/d,1/|t|) at angular distance ≫min(|t|/d,1) from the nearest odd multiple. To apply the short PNT subdivide the long pieces into logarithmic pieces of length ≍min(1,1/|t|), discarding at most a fixed fraction; their lengths in primes, around P≥exp(B−1/d), are ≫P/(1+2√L)≫P^(3/4), since B/h→∞. The weighted mass is ≫M by summing those pieces. Choose K fixed and large. As in 397 a fixed margin times M/ξ² remains outside |t∓π/(B+m)|≤ε/(Bξ), with m the mean of log p−B used in 398, dm→−1.

**Inversion and terminal phase.** At β+iπ/(B+m) the first derivative is O(L/ξ²), thanks to the cancellation of the linear term and the second moment of r. The second is (1+O(1/ξ)) times the positive curvature ≍LB, by the first moment. Rouché as in 398 gives a simple saddle at distance O(1/(Bξ²)). When moving the vertical to its real part the weights vary by a factor 1+O(1/ξ²); their normalized moments keep the limits. The exterior loss exceeds that of the saddle by c_ε M/ξ²≫h, and the same Gaussian integration in the two bands gives the cosine of 398 with additive error o(1). In particular, at extremes of the cosine the logarithm of the amplitude is βL+O(M+L/(Bξ²)+h)=L−(1+o(1))L/(DQ). Here M=o(L/Q), so this coarse equivalent suffices.

The phase when adding the terminal prime decreases by ≍Y^(−β)/ξ per unit weight, since Im(s_*)B−π=(π+o(1))/ξ. On [exp(DhQ),2exp(DhQ)] the sum of those variations is ≫exp(ξ)/(Bξ)≍L/B²→∞, and the saddles are continuous between steps as in 398. Choose fixed D>2/c₁. The two signs reach magnitudes larger than |S| at each large x. Interpolating between them proves the announced ceiling without RH. The terminal weight remains adaptive and the ceiling uses the classical Vinogradov–Korobov free region; it does not reach the range that would exclude exterior zeros.

**Review record.** For the targetS, the width c/Q is interior to the region of371 over the whole height2sqrt L; the costs exp(O(h²)) and exp(O(exp(2ch/Q)log h)) are absorbed by the saving L/Q. For the law of r, the range log p<B/2 has relative mass O(ξlog B exp(−ξ/2)), which still tends to0 when multiplied by any fixed power ofξ. On the upper range, 1/(1−r/ξ)≤2 allows exponential domination and all moments used. The normalized loss uses M/ξ², not M/B²; the width of the bands is therefore1/(Bξ). The shortest prime intervals have length P/(2sqrt L) with log P∼B≫h, above P^(3/4), and the short PNT applies uniformly after subdividing the long ones.

In Rouché, the constant costs L/ξ², the linear term on radius C/(Bξ²) costs CL/ξ² and the remainder O(LB²·C²/(B²ξ⁴))=O(C²L/ξ⁴). This justifies the unique simple root and the real displacement used. The parameter M=L/B is o(L/Q) and also L/(Bξ²)=o(L/Q), so the logarithm of the amplitude has an error small enough to be compared withS, not just an o(L) without rate. The phase joins by the invariant identity B+m for the mean of log p, and exp(ξ)/(Bξ)∼L/B² diverges. D is fixed after c₁ and before x→∞; no diagonalization of results with fixed parameters is used to obtain this explicit ceiling.
