**Theorem 3 (no self-dual multiplicative lattice; PROVED).** The window form lives on the multiplicative side (u = log x). Viazovska-type
eigenfunctions need a locally compact abelian group G with a Pontryagin self-duality G ≅ Ĝ carrying a discrete cocompact subgroup to its
annihilator (ℝ⁸ ⊃ E8; 𝔸 ⊃ ℚ). For the multiplicative data of ℚ no such pair exists: the finite-idele group 𝔸_f^× ≅ ℚ^×_{>0} × Ẑ^× is totally disconnected, while its dual contains the dual of the free abelian group ℚ^×_{>0}, the
infinite-dimensional torus 𝕋^{primes} (connected). So 𝔸_f^× ≇ its dual. On the u-line itself, the subgroup generated by log ℕ is
log ℚ_{>0}, dense and not discrete (ℚ-independence of log p): no lattice. The multiplicative Pontryagin pair is (ℤ^{(primes)} discrete,
solenoid/torus compact) — exactly CA_L_002's (exponent vectors, 𝕋^X) — between which a "Fourier eigenfunction" is meaningless
(different spaces).
*Proof.* Pontryagin duality exchanges discrete and compact and preserves connectedness of the dual of torsion-free groups (dual of a
torsion-free discrete group is connected, CITED Hewitt–Ross 24.25); a totally disconnected group with a nontrivial connected dual subgroup
quotient is not self-dual. ∎

## 3. Modular objects cannot generate the comb (PROVED for moderate growth, ARGUED for weakly holomorphic)
**Theorem 4.** There is no automorphic form f of moderate growth on Γ₀(N) (holomorphic or Maass, any weight, character) whose L-series
Σa_n n^{−s} differs from −ζ′/ζ(s) = ΣΛ(n)n^{−s} by a function meromorphic on ℂ with finitely many poles.
*Proof.* By Hecke (CITED; Iwaniec, Topics in classical automorphic forms, ch. 7), the L-series of a moderate-growth automorphic form has a
meromorphic continuation with finitely many poles. −ζ′/ζ has a pole at every zero of ζ, and ζ has infinitely many zeros (ξ entire of
order 1 and not of the form e^{a+bs}P(s): Hadamard, CITED). ∎ For weakly holomorphic forms (Viazovska's inputs) the regularized
L-series (Bringmann–Fricke–Kent) also has finitely many poles: ARGUED (citation not re-verified).
**Corollary 4.1 (amplitude gap).** Constructions from modular/Tate data are linear in θ-type objects; their Mellin images lie in the algebra
generated by ζ, its twists and products (amplitude |ζ|^k, k ≥ 1: Rankin–Selberg, Eisenstein, CA_A front). The window symbol is σ_T =
m − Σ_{n<e^{2T}}v_n cos(τlog n) with m = −2Re ζ′/ζ(½+i·) (CA_M_001 identity): amplitude 0 (logarithmic). The bridge k ≥ 1 → k = 0 is
CA_D_002 (closure NO-GO, PROVED) and CA_D_001 §4 (non-integer k needs log Z_X holomorphic: zero side).
