# Phase 193 — creation campaign

A systematic campaign that screens a very large number of candidate
mathematical mechanisms (automorphic/spectral, local-global/adelic,
phase/Hardy, topological/geometric, algebraic/representation-theoretic,
quantum/operator-algebraic, semiring-theoretic, PDE/variational,
correlation-inequality, frustrated-quadratic-form, sphere-packing/magic-
function, and prime-sited) against the single target: a signed inequality
that would decide the positivity of the window quadratic form 𝔞_T / λ_A(T)
for ζ. Each candidate is screened, then developed into one or more memos,
reviewed, and checked against a fixed list of model-class invariance
operations (O1–O15) and recurring exclusion patterns ((B″) comb-generic,
(R) zero-side, REF-D exact restatement of the target).

## Results
Organized by front (A through N, plus a re-examination Round R); within
each front, essentially every candidate is excluded by one of: comb-generic
positivity (survives all O1–O15, so carries no ζ-specific information),
a functional-equation-generic / Epstein-class survival (O10/O12), a
zero-side reading, or because it is an exact restatement of row D (REF-D).
- **Front A** (automorphic/Eisenstein): Petersson/Maass–Selberg/Rankin–
  Selberg norm positivity, Lewis–Zagier/Mayer transfer operators, Hecke
  multiplicativity, weight-½ metaplectic Eisenstein series, abscissa
  transport 1→½, homotopy/boundary-triple/reflection-positivity seeds,
  integer (spectral-flow) invariants — all **NO-GO** or **REF-D**; one
  exact positive side-result, no invariant positive form at Re s = ¼
  (line doubling withdrawn as a *general* barrier, holds only for the norm
  class). **PROVED** (class-restricted).
- **Front B** (matching/σ-derivative identities): matching identity,
  Riesz/Jensen decomposition, self-matching ⟺ functional equation (**PROVED**
  Theorem), concavity-gap and open-ball (Lemma B) analyses — all excluded
  as sign steps (comb-generic or REF-D).
- **Front C** (local-global/adelic): product-formula couplings, harmonic-
  measure classes, η-family numerics — **NO-GO** for all declared classes;
  one open necessary-only sub-statement (Fam_η) for η > 2/3.
- **Front D** (phase/Hardy): Hardy-amplitude transfer theorem (**PROVED**,
  reusable tool) and its closure — **NO-GO** as a route to row D.
- **Fronts E–G** (quantum/operator-algebra, topology/geometry, algebra/
  representation theory): condensed mathematics, trace formulas, polarized
  objects over ℤ, monoid/Elias–Williamson constructions, separability and
  Schur-factorization arguments — all **NO-GO** or **REF-D**; one open gap
  (MW-5, an unbuilt object).
- **Fronts H–M** (semiring +/·, PDE of the minimizer, correlation
  inequalities, frustrated quadratic forms, sphere-packing/magic functions,
  prime-sited reformulations): Buium δ-structures, Erdős successor
  invariant, Markov/Krein–Rutman comparison, lattice reflection positivity,
  GKS/FKG, diamagnetic and Guerra-interpolation bounds, Viazovska-style
  magic-function and LP constructions — all **NO-GO** or **REF-D**.
- **Front N** (Viazovska V1–V3 program): V1 impossible exact+attained on
  the window LP; V2's unimodular analytic-signal transfer impossible for
  all T beyond ½log3 (**PROVED**); V3's Tate eigenstructure already spent,
  no self-dual multiplicative lattice and no moderate-growth automorphic
  generator of Λ exist (**PROVED**); the built toolbox is sign-blind on the
  Epstein class.
- **Round R** (re-examination): the separation set 𝖯⋆ is a genuine
  identification-only separation, not an inequality (REF-D as a route,
  **PROVED**); the amplitude-homotopy and θ-line (SPEC_A) continuations
  are **NO-GO**/REF-D; one new open residue, **D_∃c**, a weaker-or-equal,
  O4-invariant statement.
- Campaign-wide: roughly 130 memos/reviews across ~14 fronts; **zero**
  admissible candidates found; no negative value for ζ ever certified.
  Recurring wall, confirmed on every front: a ζ-specific comb property
  survives the quartet operation O7, and a property of the form survives
  the functional-equation class (O10/O12) or is comb-generic; what remains
  is an exact restatement of row D.
