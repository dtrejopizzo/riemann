# Phase 45 — Fredholm

This phase drills at the wall from the Fredholm-operator side, developing idea B.4 of Phase 44's defect theory: does the Weil quadratic form decompose as an autonomous (prime-computable) symbol plus a compact perturbation, so that strict essential positivity of the symbol alone would bound the number of off-critical zeros without touching the master-quantifier circularity? It also proves the first two-prime Weil-positivity theorem in the new $\mathcal Arith$ category and studies the realizability of an "infinitesimal signature" invariant under ¬RH.

## Results
- Weil–Toeplitz algebra and the symbol/compact decomposition: proved that only *strict* essential positivity (spectral gap $[c,\infty)$, $c>0$) — not merely weak essential positivity ($[0,\infty)$) — implies a finite defect $\delta<\infty$ robustly under compact perturbations; in fixed-weight CCM-type realizations the gap is destroyed by the normalization itself, independent of the zeros, so the certificate is empty. A trichotomy of off-critical-zero visibility is proved (fat/thin-visible/sub-resolution), showing that for every window-resolution "calendar" there exist invisible $m=\infty$ configurations — the master quantifier reappears as a resolution quantifier. (PROVED exact theorems within the framework; overall NO-GO for the Fredholm route as originally hoped)
- Two-prime theorem: Dirichlet convolution of Euler objects in $\mathcal Arith$ adds Weil data exactly, with the mixed support carrying zero Weil mass; the two-prime object with incommensurable periods satisfies Weil positivity (Axiom H) iff both constituent primes satisfy the classical purity condition $|\alpha|=\sqrt p$ — the first Weil-positivity theorem proved with two primes and incommensurable periods, without reference to $\zeta$, together with its exact converse. An exact/uniform positivity gap is computed for small windows, with a genuine open gap ("GAP-135.A") for the uniform gap at intermediate window lengths. (PROVED, two-prime positivity theorem and its converse; OPEN for GAP-135.A)
- Problem 133.A (realizability of the infinitesimal signature $\sigma^0$ under ¬RH): proved that for the *global* canonical scheme, $\sigma^0=0$ under ¬RH with finite defect for every ultrafilter — i.e. the global version of the 0-vs-½ dichotomy does NOT distinguish RH, refuting the previously claimed bridge-theorem; a corrected, *localized* infinitesimal signature is then defined, and shown to close positively: under ¬RH a minimal off-critical quadruple forces $\sigma^0_{\mathrm{loc}}\ge 1/(2D_0)>0$ for a standard constant $D_0$, for every ultrafilter. (NO-GO for the global scheme — refutes a prior claim; PROVED for the repaired localized scheme)
