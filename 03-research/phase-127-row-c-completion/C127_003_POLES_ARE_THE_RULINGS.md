# C127.003 — the poles are the rulings

Date: 2026-09-12.  Status: `PROVED`.  Full statement: Paper 45 §8.

The polar packet of the corpus's form, on `h = F⋆F^†`, is

    M_+(F)·conj(M_−(F)) + M_−(F)·conj(M_+(F))          (P123.036)

— explicitly a product, not `|M_+|² + |M_−|²`.  The Hermitian form of the two
Tate characters is

    β_T(z,w) = z_−·conj(w_+) + z_+·conj(w_−)           (P123.008 (2.2))

— the hyperbolic plane.  Under `z(F) = (M_−(F), M_+(F)) = (Φ_F(0), Φ_F(1))`
these are the same expression, and `2Φ_F(0)Φ_F(1) = ĥ(0) + ĥ(1)`.

So the two polar terms of the explicit formula are the intersection with the
ruling plane of `N_A` (Paper 43 Thm. 9.1), with no adjustment, and:

- **primitivity = zero ruling component**;
- row D's target `q ≥ 0` on `P_T` is, verbatim, the **Hodge index statement**
  for the intersection form on the complement of the hyperbolic plane.

That is the decomposition Weil's proof uses on a surface.
