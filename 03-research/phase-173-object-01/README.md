# Phase 173 — Construction attempt for object (0.1): a Hermitian form satisfying (Q1)–(Q4)

Following phase 172's identification of a candidate minimal coupling (Euler structure over ℕ plus
compatible Poisson/functional-equation structure), this phase attempts to construct a concrete
Hermitian form realizing that coupling and satisfying four required properties (Q1)–(Q4), across
three families of standard models: (a) adelic Schwartz–Bruhat constructions, (b) Jacobi theta with
Hecke operator T_{p²}, and (c) Weil–Connes realizations.

## Results

- (a) Adelic Schwartz–Bruhat route — **NO-GO**: the natural Euler operator built from Λ(n)/√n is unbounded with trivial strong-convergence domain; any bounded dense-range Euler insertion leaves positivity formally inert (a density argument shows it cannot create a sign), excluding the raw Euler sums and GNS/norm constructions examined (not every renormalized adelic crossed form).
- (b) Jacobi theta / T_{p²} route — **NO-GO**: the sign obtainable is only the scalar positivity of a character, producing no zero bound; fails (Q4).
- (c) Weil–Connes route — **NO-GO**, trichotomy of standard realizations: L²_δ is positive prior to the spectrum but does not use the Euler–Poisson coupling and only realizes critical zeros; the nuclear H¹ of Meyer–CCM couples Euler and Poisson but its positivity is Weil's criterion itself; Connes's compressions are positive but use forbidden cutoffs and transferring the sign globally is again equivalent to Weil's criterion.
- None of the three NO-GOs is claimed as a universal impossibility theorem; a classification theorem for all admissible prespectral Hermitian forms is explicitly identified as the still-missing requirement.
