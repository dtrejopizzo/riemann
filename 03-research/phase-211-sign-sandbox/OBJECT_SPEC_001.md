# Specification of the object to build (round 5 onward)

Target (★): λ_A(T) ≥ 0 for every T (STATEMENT_001.md). What the record proves about any sign-carrying
object (STATE_001.md §5–6, ROUND_004_A Prop. B, Paper 47 thm:shape / prop:spec / arena theorem):

R1. A family of subspaces E_T ⊂ P_T (or of finite matrices) with dim E_T → ∞ as T grows; no fixed
    finite Λ-free subspace can carry the sign (ROUND_004_A Prop. B: a condition with margin ε > 0 on
    such a subspace survives all weight changes below ε and all cΛ with |c − 1| small).
R2. E_T is built from the exact values Λ(n), n < e^{2T} (not from a class closed under modification),
    so that the matrix of a_T on E_T has entries that are NON-LINEAR in (v_n) at the step that decides
    the sign (products v_m v_n, iterated comb correlations, Dirichlet convolutions evaluated at
    archimedean kernels), and so that the condition fails for cΛ (c ≠ 1), for a_P, for finite
    modifications of the weights, and for Beurling systems reaching the line.
R3. The reduction of (★) to E_T must be exact or carry the margin: either (i) E_T captures the
    minimiser of a_T on P_T to precision λ_A(T) ≤ 4·10⁹ T e^{−15.449T}, with a PROVED bound on the
    Schur coupling to E_T^⊥ and PROVED coercivity of a_T on E_T^⊥; or (ii) a_T ⪰ 0 on E_T is itself
    equivalent to (★) at T (an exact reduction like (L2), (L2′)) with a provable finite inequality.
R4. The archimedean data (C_0, K, Γ(s/2) at conductor 1, the two Tate conditions) and the Euler
    product over ℕ must enter jointly; the arena theorem (N*(T) ≥ exp(4e^T(1+o(1))) competing even
    modes) means any family that is archimedean-first needs dimension doubly exponential in T; a
    family that is comb-first (generated by the prime operator Π) is not subject to that count — this
    is the gap to exploit.
R5. Falsifier test mandatory (BRIEF_CONSTRUCTOR.md rule 4). Labels on every claim.

Candidate directions (not prescriptions): the Krylov family generated by the comb operator Π from
the archimedean negative cone (entries = iterated comb correlations ⟨e, Π^i e′⟩, polynomial in v_n;
note the index entry L5 of R164_502c concerns the Krylov space of the symbol operator, a different
generator); Λ-twisted Dirichlet polynomials on the Nyquist mesh; the span of the comb's own
"responses" Π e_k for the negative Nyquist block of (L2′).
