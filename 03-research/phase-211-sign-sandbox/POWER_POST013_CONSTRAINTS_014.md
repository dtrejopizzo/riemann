# POWER_POST013_CONSTRAINTS_014 — Conditions for a signed continuation

Status: audit of scope and concrete requirements; it closes no route for being a reformulation. Papers untouched.

## 1. What the registered operator calculus really says

Read Paper46 prop:opcalc (lines8419 onwards), lem:shape-lin, phase188/N_BUILD_018_LINEAR_CENTRE_REDUCES_TO_OMEGA_MU.md, the index and the filter memos004–013. The operator proposition shows that convolution identities are linear identities in the autocorrelation; by themselves they do not supply a new sign. It expressly covers convolution operators. It does not classify or exclude every inequality that preserves a spectral measure restricted to rational numerators, the positive frequency terms or an exact coupling with the polar.

The rational filters present are causal convolutions of delta and integrable exponential kernels. Their transport identity does belong to the language of opcalc. What is new must lie in the inequality proved AFTERWARDS: asserting the identity again, applying Cauchy--Schwarz without a demonstrated gain, or rewriting the comb with Euler does not provide that step. The finite closures from four to seven already show by construction why that scope observation must not be turned into an absolute NO-GO.

## 2. What round013 discarded exactly

POWER_DEFICIT_LIMIT_013 excludes the estimate that replaces the loss by its worst pointwise envelope, independently of the rational probability, and discards the positive gain. Its numbers only prevent optimising that estimate at eight. Keeping gains or the numerator constraints changes an essential hypothesis of the class. That continuation is not discarded for being a representation of the same form.

A valid development must identify precisely:

* the subspace of numerators and the conditions preserved by the pole map;
* the spectral measure or the signed block being estimated;
* the uniform inequality over ALL its coefficients, with its proved margin;
* the full polar and comb, with justified interchanges of sums or limits.

The finite certificates can be used as real inputs of that inequality; a pattern of eigenvalues or a single vector does not replace its positivity on the whole subspace.

## 3. Arithmetic needed when the dimension grows

A mechanism that only seeks positive AP for ALL degrees fails in the concrete test of WITNESS006: that vector of dimension twelve has negative AP. This does not prevent using AP to advance in a declared degree, nor building a more arithmetic AP inequality. The certificate BLOCK007 does cross that region by keeping contact2 with its cross terms and controlling the tail.

Moreover WITNESS006 and its upper tail bound prove a negative sign when EXCLUSIVELY contact2 is deleted. Therefore any assumption of a theorem for all blocks that also holds after that deletion cannot produce the desired conclusion. This is a proof by a specific falsifier, not a classification of all arguments. An exact use of weights or arithmetic identities that distinguishes the deletion remains a legitimate target.

In a filter that includes the comb, the cos(tau logq) are not monotone in frequency. Their covariance does not acquire a sign from Lambda>=0; a signed compensation must be proved. Working first with a finite comb and then passing to the full one requires controlling the sum of differences of correlations; for the rational spaces of rates>=31.5 that convergence can be justified by exponential times polynomial, but it does not turn the identity into an inequality.

## 4. Smaller sufficient target in parameters

POWER_CONFLUENT_BRIDGE_014 proves that semidefiniteness of ALL Laguerre blocks at ONE single fixed rate u>1/2 suffices. The bridge is in weighted H¹ controlling the full form, not only in L². This removes an unnecessary obligation for a closure: there is no need to prove all arbitrary sets of rates if the full confluent sequence with all its cross terms is achieved directly.

A single scalar chain f_n and a union of small spaces do not inherit that sufficiency. The inequalities of the chain remain legitimate advances and necessary targets; they only need to keep their scope. The scalar identity CHAIN009 requires a new estimate for its signed arithmetic combination and is not closed by the Euler formulas.
