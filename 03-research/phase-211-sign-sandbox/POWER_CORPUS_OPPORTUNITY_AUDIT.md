## Reading corrections that avoid retries

1. O-151.4 is not a never-developed route: R168_202 already analyzes the declared families. The old entry of06 was outdated. That classification does not prove that any new object is impossible.
2. The old recommendation of06R4 to seek a nonperturbative global barrier must be read together with phase197 and AUDIT_197_2. The audit distinguishes reductions from true impossibilities and corrects constants and a sharpness argument.
3. R186_092 corrects TB3-neg(ii): it is not valid to infer «the conclusion is not stable under deletion, hence its derivation uses completeness». The valid argument uses premises stable under deletion: the truncated datum satisfies them and therefore satisfies their consequences. Do not extend the NO-GO beyond that class.
4. REF-D means reformulation; it is not by itself an exclusion criterion. Nor does OPEN or «no candidate» mean that a concrete opportunity exists.

## Result of the audit and next filter

Among the mechanisms examined no new construction already justified for uniform closure was identified. This does not claim that none exists: it identifies what must not be repeated and where an object is still missing.

The check of E equal to the set of squares was settled in `SQUARE_COMPLETENESS_OBSTRUCTION.md`: the extreme of replacing2 by4 already appeared inR163_102 and admits transfer of the negative witness007_C. The condition on squares, even with integer counts and nonnegative logarithmic coefficients, does not suffice. The same source proves a non-integer falsifier for simultaneous completeness at ALL perfect powers; on adding integrality and logarithmic positivity, those conditions identify the original datum, without proving its sign. No construction based only on this condition is opened. Section11 adds a deformation with residue exactly1 and cumulative count x+O(1), keeping ALL-POWERS and nonnegative logarithmic coefficients: factors at primes outside the window normalize the residue without changing the negative test. This class only requires N nonnegative and bounded, not N<=1 nor integrality. There was no new quadrature nor campaign; no deformation is presented as satisfying the original functional equation and the NO-GO remains limited to the declared hypotheses.

The global target remains OPEN. WM and112 remain valid as recorded auxiliary results; they contribute no new window nor authorize extrapolation to all orders.


## Additional cross-check: theta heat, Poisson and joint inequalities

Review with additional reading of E101_070 and106_110. No construction is opened from the following identities.

- `phase-101-covariant-bordered-current/E101_070_PEARSON_THETA_CLOSURE_NO_GO.md`, §§2–5: already contains the two-variable theta representation and the projection to an infinite hierarchy with boundary flux. Its Pearson NO-GO only excludes fixed scalar polynomial coefficients, because of the interior atoms; it does not exclude any nonlocal equation. The heat identity does not deliver the signed closure.
- `phase-106-global-modular-star-audit/106_110_HEAT_PROFILE_COVARIANCE_FLOW_AND_DISSIPATION_BUDGET.md`, §7: complete Poisson acts via an isometry and keeps both traces of the budget. Re-expressing the sources does not modify the pending inequality. A new comparison between blocks would be necessary, not just the modular identity.
- `phase-192-exact-identity/EI_002_LATTICE_MELLIN_IDENTITY.md`: the joint use of Poisson and Euler in an exact identity was already studied; it is not by itself a lower bound.
- `phase-196-global-object/INV_V_001_HEISENBERG_HECKE_WINDOW_AND_SHA_FORM.md`, V1.3–V1.4: positivity that factors over primes survives eliminations; the scope of the obstruction is that class, not every use of Poisson.
- `phase-195-modern-tools/INV_N_001_BAKRY_EMERY_GAMMA2_GAP_CEILING_NO_GO.md`, §3: adding separate infima of the jump and the potential does not deliver the required bound. It does not exclude a joint inequality; the auxiliary numbers are not promoted here to interval certificates.

No new first lemma paying the sign for all sizes was identified in these readings. Nor is that absence turned into a general NO-GO. The condition for reopening remains to exhibit a concrete joint inequality, with defined object and coefficients, and to check why it is not just the identity or the budget already recorded. Returning to heat or to variation diminishing by changing their name or variable is not proposed.
