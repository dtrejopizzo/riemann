## 2. Blueprint claims REFUTED (with counterexamples)

**B1. "The `†`-closed family is `{Frob_r : r ∈ Q_{>0}^×}`, with
`Frob_{a/b} := Frob_a⋆Frob_b^†`, a commutative inverse semigroup."**  The
semigroup statement is right; the **indexing is false** — the assignment is not
well defined.  *Counterexample* (Thm 3ter.7(iv)): `2/1 = 6/3`, but

    Frob_2⋆Frob_1^† = μ_{j_2(2)·K_{{2}}},
    Frob_6⋆Frob_3^† = μ_{j_2(2)·K_{{2,3}}},

which have **different supports** — the second projects onto all of `Z_3^×` at
the place 3, the first onto the single point `1/2 ∈ Z_3^×`.  *Correct
statement, proved*: the index set is pairs `(r,F)` with `r ∈ Q_{>0}^×` and
`P(r) ⊆ F ⊂ S_f` finite — `Q_{>0}^×` **graded by the semilattice of finite
prime sets**.  This is not a defect; it is what makes `𝔉_S` an inverse
semigroup rather than a group, the idempotents `Δ^{(F)}` being the extra data.
Recorded as §9bis W13.

**B2. "The smeared finite contact is exactly `Λ(n)n^{−1/2}·∫ρ_ε` for every `N`
and small `ε`."**  **False.**  The shell integral evaluates the archimedean
profile at the *single point* `x_∞ = p^{−k}`, because `a_p = F∘j_p` is
**constant** on the shell; what appears is the **value** `h(1/n)`, not the
integral.  With an `L¹`-normalized bump `h(1/n) ≍ 1/ε → ∞`, so the claimed
quantity is not the contact of `Frob_n^{[ρ_ε,N]}`.  *Correct statement, proved
(Thm 3ter.10, Rem 3ter.10a)*: the contact is `Λ(n)n^{−1/2}h(1/n)` **exactly,
for every `N` and every admissible `h`** — strictly stronger than what was
claimed, since it is an identity for a genuine test function with no limit.
Recorded as §9bis W14.

**B3. "The self-pairing diverges like `−2log(1/ε)`."**  The coefficient `2` is
not obtained in either natural normalization, and the **rate** differs between
them: `−ε^{−1}‖φ‖_2²log(1/ε)` for the mass-normalized approximate identity (one
power of `ε^{−1}` faster than claimed), `−log(1/ε)+O(1)` for the
`L²`-normalization (coefficient `1`).  Both are proved (Thm 8bis.2,
Rem 8bis.2b).  The qualitative claim `Δ·Δ = −∞` is correct.  Recorded as
§9bis W15.

**A fourth correction, not in the blueprint.**  The blueprint's `W_∞` for the
smeared Frobenius is not the archimedean density: with the indicators centred
at `n^{−1}` at the places `r ∤ n`, `W_∞(Frob_n^{[h,N]})` carries the factor
`∏_{r∉P(n)}\mathbf 1[r^N | n−1]`, which **vanishes for all large `N`**.  That
is the right answer and it is a theorem, not a defect: `supp Frob_n` is
disjoint from `Im j_∞` (Cor 3ter.10b).  The archimedean density belongs to the
**archimedean** Frobenius `Frob_∞(t)`, i.e. to Paper 44's element class
(Def–Prop 3ter.13).  The two families sit on complementary slices, which is the
cleanest statement of the element/Frobenius dichotomy this paper has reached.
