## 1. Audit R131.002

### F1 `FATAL (framing)` — "`Γ_n^rad` is not the ideal class". **REPAIRED, and accepted as mathematics.**

The audit is right, and the argument is now a theorem of the paper.

* **Lemma 3bis.3b (new, proved).** On a chart with `P(n) ⊆ S_f` — the standing
  hypothesis of §3bis — `n ∈ Γ_S`, so `nZ_S = Z_S` is the **unit** ideal; and
  the realization factors as `(N_{≥1},×) ↪ (R_{>0},×) ≅ {level measures}`
  (3bis.2a), an isomorphism of topological monoids in the second slot. So the
  object forgets factorization: `Γ_6^rad` is structurally indistinguishable
  from `Γ^rad_{(t)}` for generic real `t`.
* **Renamed throughout**: "ideal class" → **norm class**; `Γ^rad_{(t)}`,
  `t ∈ R`, is primary and `Γ_n^rad := Γ^rad_{(log n)}` is its value at an
  integer label (Definition 3bis.4 rewritten, (3bis.3)/(3bis.3′)). Banner, §0,
  §3bis heading, §6.2a′, §8, §9, §10 all updated.
* **Remark 3bis.4a corrected, not deleted.** The audit offered deletion or a
  non-`S`-unit setting; I kept it in the corrected weak form it actually needs:
  the lifts of `a_S(n)` of modulus `1/n` are exactly `δ_S(1/n)K^♮`, and **the
  coset depends only on the modulus**. The "all generators of the ideal"
  reading is explicitly retracted as vacuous.
* **Theorem 3bis.7′ (new, proved) — the positive half the the round record asked
  for.** The finite-place representing measure `ω_fin` is **purely atomic**,
  `supp ω_fin = {±k log p : p ∈ S_f, k ≥ 1}` (on the colimit, all primes), with
  atom masses `(log p)p^{−k/2}`; equivalently, for `t > 0`, `ω_fin({t}) ≠ 0`
  **iff** `e^t` is a prime power, and then `ω_fin({t}) = Λ(e^t)e^{−t/2}`. Local
  finiteness is proved (discreteness of `{k log p}` in a compact window). So
  the prime powers, `Λ`, and `Λ(composite) = 0` are **read off the contact of
  the family with the diagonal**, not carried by the objects. Remark 3bis.7′a
  states what this is and is not.
* **B.1/B.2/B.6 wording reassessed** in new Remark 3bis.5b(a)–(d) and in the
  §10 ledger: realized as objects = the monoid inside the modulus group
  (forgetting factorization); realized on the integral model = the divisor
  poset on `Q_{S,·}`; **detected on the carrier** = the contact locus, with the
  divisor structure appearing as `Σ_{d|n}Λ(d) = log n`, the sum of the atoms at
  the divisors' moduli. B.2 and B.6 now carry the emptiness note explicitly;
  B.6's first half reads "`PROVED` but vacuous for composite `n`".
