## 4. The one claim of the round-7 instruction that I refuted

**"Π multiplies both factors by the same real constant … so the pair term
becomes `2|ρ_0(1−ρ_0)|² ĝ(ρ_0)ĝ(1−ρ̄_0)`, still positive" (item p5 of the
instruction).**  **Refuted.**  The instruction's own preceding sentence is
right and its conclusion does not follow from it.

*Counterexample / correct computation.*  `\widehat{Πg}(s) = s(1−s)\hat g(s)`.
Put `A := ρ_0(1−ρ_0)`. At the second resonant point the factor is
`(1−ρ̄_0)ρ̄_0 = \overline{A}` — as the instruction says. But the pair term is
**Hermitian**, i.e. `\widehat{Πg}(ρ_0)\overline{\widehat{Πg}(1−\bar ρ_0)} +
c.c.`, and

    A\,ĝ(ρ_0)\;\overline{\overline{A}\,ĝ(1−\bar ρ_0)}
      = A\cdot A\;ĝ(ρ_0)ĝ(1−\bar ρ_0)   (the ĝ's being positive reals),

so the coefficient is `2\,\mathrm{Re}(A^2)`, **not** `2|A|^2`. The conjugation
in the Hermitian pairing cancels the conjugation in `\overline A`; it does not
add to it. The distinction matters: `|A|^2 > 0` always, whereas
`\mathrm{Re}(A^2)` need **not** be. With `A = \tfrac14 − δ^2 + γ_0^2 −
2iδγ_0`,

    \mathrm{Re}(A^2) = \big(\tfrac14−δ^2+γ_0^2\big)^2 − 4δ^2γ_0^2
      = \big[(|γ_0|−δ)^2+\tfrac14−2δ^2\big]\cdot
        \big[(|γ_0|+δ)^2+\tfrac14−2δ^2\big] ,

which is `> 0` **iff** `|γ_0| > 2δ` for `0 < δ < \tfrac12` — true for every
`|γ_0| ≥ 1`, hence for every nontrivial zero of `ζ` (all have `|γ| > 14`), but
**false** for `|γ_0| < 2δ`. So the conclusion the instruction wanted is correct
*under a condition it did not state*, and the reason it gave is wrong. The
paper states the correct coefficient, the correct condition, and the
factorisation that proves it (Lemma 8bis.6b(v)), and records the correction in
place. This is the audit's `p2` reading, which is the right one.
