## 2. Status of each target

**(7.1) is REF (exact reformulation of a_T ≥ 0 on P_T for each T).** Proof (verified here): for
G ∈ PW_T^0 in the form domain, ∫ r_T|G|² = ∫ r_T^+|G|² − ∫ r_T^−|G|², hence
∫ r_T|G|² ≥ 0 ∀G ⟺ quotient ≤ 1 ∀G. The denominator does not vanish for G ≠ 0: r_T ≥ c log|τ| − O_T(1) → +∞,
so {r_T > 0} contains a neighbourhood of ∞ and a nonzero entire G does not vanish there a.e.; the «range
convention» of the memo is unnecessary. With (2.3) and the dictionary of 1.1, (7.1) ⟺ a_T ≥ 0 on P_T. It is neither stronger
nor another object: it is the multiplier split pointwise into positive and negative parts. The phrase «completes
row D» describes an equivalence, not a closure. «Depth» = generalized Rayleigh quotient of the pair
(r_T^−, r_T^+) compressed to PW_T^0. The threshold quotient (5.5) is the same statement via successive Schur
complements (induction by cells): also REF, cell by cell.

**λ_max(K_T) ≤ 1 (D.135 (6.1)) and ‖𝓦_T𝓡_T^{−1/2}‖ ≤ 1 (D.134 (8.3)): both REF.** Congruence by the
positive operator 𝓡_T^{1/2} (resp. H_{5/4,T}^{1/2}) with compact inverse:
A − B ≥ 0 ⟺ I − A^{−1/2}BA^{−1/2} ≥ 0 for A > 0. It is exact, without loss. All they add is
structural information (compactness, not Schatten, infinite positive index, d_T = number of eigenvalues > 1),
none of which gives a sign. D.134 (5.4) d_T = ind_−(a_T|P_T) and D.135 (0.2) are the standard
Birman–Schwinger principle.
