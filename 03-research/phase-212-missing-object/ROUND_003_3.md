## 1. Three short lemmas

Let 𝒜 be the set of operators Y = Y_0 + JY_1 on L²(ℝ; 𝒦), with 𝒦 an auxiliary Hilbert space and Y_0, Y_1
multipliers (commuting with all translations) of measurable symbols α_0, α_1 : ℝ → 𝒦,
acting on scalar F ∈ L²(ℝ). It contains X, Z, the translations, J, the transform 𝓕 = U_∞J of
R161_034 (1.1) and the atoms d_k; it is closed under composition with scalar multipliers and under
conjugation by J, because JS_aJ = S_{−a} and, in general, JY_1J is the multiplier of symbol
α_1(−τ) ((Jg)^(τ) = ĝ(−τ)).

**Lemma P0 (the form splits by parity). PROVED.** P_T is invariant under J and, with
F_± = (F ± JF)/2, a_T(F) = a_T(F_+) + a_T(F_−) and ‖F‖² = ‖F_+‖² + ‖F_−‖². Hence
λ_A(T) = min(λ_A^+(T), λ_A^−(T)), with λ_A^ε the infimum over P_T^ε = {F ∈ P_T : JF = εF}.

*Proof.* A(JF) = ∫F(−t)e^{t/2}dt = B(F) and B(JF) = A(F), hence JP_T = P_T and I_T is symmetric.
(F_±)^(−τ) = ±(F_±)^(τ). The cross part of a_T is 2Re(1/2π)∫s_T (F_+)^ conj (F_−)^ dτ; s_T is even
(m(τ) = Re ψ(¼ + iτ/2) − log π is even and cos is even), and the integrand is odd, hence it vanishes. The
same with s_T ≡ 1 gives the norm. ∎ (Elementary fact; almost surely used in the corpus; no
novelty is claimed.)

**Lemma P1 (in a sector, the squares of 𝒜 are squares of multipliers). PROVED.** If JF = εF
and Y ∈ 𝒜, then YF = Y^εF with Y^ε the multiplier of symbol α^ε(τ) = α_0(τ) + εα_1(−τ), and

    ‖YF‖² = (1/2π)∫‖α_0(τ) + εα_1(−τ)‖²_𝒦 |F̂(τ)|² dτ.

In particular ‖(A − JY_1)F‖² = ‖(A − εJY_1J)F‖² for A a scalar multiplier; with A = I and
Y_1 = X: ‖(I − JX)F‖² = ‖(I − εX^*)F‖² (since JXJ = X^* on the line), and the expansion contains
‖XF‖², the quotient comb of E70_10 with coefficients ≥ 0.

*Proof.* JY_1F = (JY_1J)(JF) = ε(JY_1J)F, and JY_1J has symbol α_1(−τ). Plancherel. ∎

Consequence (PROVED, with Lemma B-2 of `REVIEW_002_BC.md`): if a_T(F) = Σ_j‖Y_jF‖² + ρ(F) on
P_T^ε with Y_j ∈ 𝒜 not depending on F, then ρ(F) = (1/2π)∫(s_T − σ)|F̂|² with
σ(τ) = Σ_j‖α_j^ε(τ)‖² ≥ 0 (only its even part counts, because |F̂|² is even). Grading by J does not
enlarge class (i). By Lemma C-1 of the same review, within that class there are squares with
cross terms −w_pw_q cos τ(ℓ_p + ℓ_q) without quotients, at the cost of mass; J adds nothing to that.

**Lemma P2 (the J pairing is the convolution and is not a square). PROVED.** If JF = εF and
Y ∈ 𝒜 has scalar symbol α^ε (as in P1), then

    ⟨YF, JYF⟩ = (ε/2π)∫ α^ε(τ) conj(α^ε(−τ)) |F̂(τ)|² dτ.

For Y = X: α(τ)conj α(−τ) = Σ_{m,n}b_mb_n (mn)^{−iτ} (with the convention n^{−iτ} of the translation by
log n), hence

    Q_ε(F) = ⟨XF, JXF⟩ = ε⟨X²F, F⟩ = ε Σ_{k} (Λ∗Λ)(k) k^{−1/2} c_F(−log k),

linear in c_F, with atoms at log k, k a product of two prime powers. The symbol
Re[α(τ)conj α(−τ)] = Re D(½ + iτ)² (D = Σ_{n<N}Λ(n)n^{−s}) has no sign.

*Proof.* ⟨YF, JYF⟩ = ⟨YF, (JYJ)JF⟩ = ε⟨Y^εF, (JY^εJ)F⟩ and JY^εJ has symbol α^ε(−τ);
Plancherel. For X, JXJ = Σ b_nS_{−log n} = X^*, so that ⟨XF, X^*F⟩ = ⟨X²F, F⟩, and
X² = Σ_k(b∗b)_kS_{log k} with (b∗b)_k = (Λ∗Λ)(k)k^{−1/2}. The scalar form comes from
⟨S_aF, S_{−b}F⟩ = c_F(−a − b) (Lemma 2 of `ROUND_003_8.md`). ∎

Reading: in 𝒜, «products» and «square» are mutually exclusive in the following sense. The products mn
with the weights Λ(m)Λ(n) and without quotients appear in the pairing ε⟨·, J·⟩, which is a
multiplier form without sign (P2); in the positive pairing parity reduces everything to a
multiplier with symbol ≥ 0 (P1), where products appear only with positive quotients and mass
(`REVIEW_002_BC.md` C.3 point 1) or with separated channels and mass (C-1). It is R170_854 §6.3 written as a
statement for the class 𝒜 in the parity sectors. **NO-GO with class** (PROVED): no
identity a_T = Σ‖Y_jF‖² + Σ_i ε_i⟨Y_iF, JY_iF⟩ + ρ with Y_j, Y_i ∈ 𝒜 independent of F carries its own
sign: it amounts to a pointwise partition of the even symbol and leaves in ρ a multiplier problem of the
same type as (★). Declared class: 𝒜 on L²(ℝ) (no compression to the window, no M_t, no
finite-rank functionals, no dependence on F).

## 2. The five questions

(i) **Square of an explicit operator?** ‖(A − JV)F‖² in sector ε is ‖(A − εV^*)F‖² (P1): the
cross term reproduces the comb of a_T with correct sign only if the sign of V is chosen according to ε, and the
diagonal ‖VF‖² is the quotient comb of E70_10. A superalgebra with J as grading gives, with
the supertrace, ε⟨V²F,F⟩ (P2): products, but without sign. Neither of the two contains the comb of a_T
with a manifestly ≥ 0 remainder.

(ii) **Selberg and the archimedean diagonal.** With P2, ε⟨(δX + X²)F, F⟩ = ε Σ_k(μ∗log²)(k)k^{−1/2}c_F(−log k),
δ = ad M_t (`ROUND_003_8.md`). It is linear in c_F: the relation Λ·log + Λ∗Λ = μ∗log² only rewrites a
certificate, and by Lemma A of `ROUND_001_D.md` it is a consequence of the linear atoms. In the J form
there is no diagonal to pay; in the positive form the diagonal is ‖XF‖² (quotients) or, in separated
channels, a mass (r/2)Σw_p² (C-1) that the archimedean part would have to pay with symbol, again
Lemma B-2. Moreover a_T contains no atoms at log k with k composed of two distinct primes, so
that the term μ∗log² would have to cancel against another (R170_856 §4: it cancels with the
Poisson branch, CITED in `REVIEW_002_BC.md` C.4 row 11).

(iii) **Support.** For F ∈ L²(I_T), c_F(u) = 0 if |u| ≥ 2T, hence Q_ε runs exactly over the
hyperbola mn < N (PROVED, immediate). With Z in place of one of the factors:

    ⟨XF, JZF⟩ = ε Σ_{k<N}(Λ∗1)(k)k^{−1/2}c_F(−log k) = ε Σ_{k<N}(log k)k^{−1/2}c_F(−log k),

exact for every F ∈ L²(I_T) of parity ε (PROVED: P2 with two distinct symbols and the divisor
identity at each k < N, all of whose divisors are < N). It breaks for cΛ and a_P at the line
indicated in the Cross-check (5). It is the hyperbola identity of P47 thm:PCfinite (H_{M,N} without
coefficient at k ≤ N) in the form of R161_035 (orientations K_N𝓕 and JK_N), and it is linear in c_F; by
R170_869 (`RULES_AVOID.md` I2) it falls into the factorized ideal and does not fix a sign.

(iv) **Relation with thm:PCfinite and R161_030–039.** thm:PCfinite is an operator identity with
J whose two sides, squared, are blind to the sign (remark of P47 after the proof);
R161_034 computes the Gram of the oriented atoms and finds quotients and products at once; R161_035
constructs the parallelogram of the two orientations. Q_ε is the product part of that Gram
restricted to a sector, and P1–P2 explain why the quotients cannot be removed within 𝒜. No
derivation of those memos is repeated; nor is anything contributed that surpasses them. Their living residue is
the compression R161_034 (5.2), ‖(I − P)Σ_k(log k)d_kPF‖², outside 𝒜.

(v) **Parity of R170_870.** The parity of 870 is F ↦ −F: a linear class (odd in F) cannot
have a one-sided sign unless it is zero (Lemma 2.1 there). Q_ε is quadratic in F, hence even in that
sense: the lemma of 870 does not exclude it. The parity that matters here is that of J: the Kreĭn term
ε⟨X²F,F⟩ changes sign with the sector. By P0 (★) must be proved in both sectors; a Kreĭn term
with ⟨X²F,F⟩ = Σ(Λ∗Λ)(k)k^{−1/2}Re c_F(log k) would be useful in one sector only if in the other
it had the opposite sign, which in turn requires sign information on a linear functional
of c_F in each sector: the same type of statement that R161_032 resolves for the rank-two Tate block
(by identity (2.2) there, which is a Gram equality) and that for the comb Λ∗Λ has no
written analogue. ARGUED.

## What was proved

Lemma P0 (λ_A = min of the two parity sectors; P_T invariant under J). Lemma P1 (in a sector, every
Y ∈ 𝒜 acts as a multiplier of symbol α_0(τ) + εα_1(−τ); the graded squares are squares
of class (i)). Lemma P2 (the pairing ⟨YF, JYF⟩ is the multiplier ε α(τ)conj α(−τ); for X it gives
ε⟨X²F,F⟩ = ε Σ(Λ∗Λ)(k)k^{−1/2}c_F(−log k), without sign). NO-GO with class: no identity with
squares and J pairings of operators of 𝒜 carries its own sign. Support: the sum runs over
exactly mn < N, and ⟨XF, JZF⟩ is the Λ-free identity of the hyperbola (linear in c_F).

## What failed and why

J converts quotients into products only in the indefinite pairing; in the positive one, parity
reduces everything to a multiplier and quotients or mass return. The divisor identity on the
hyperbola is exact per window and breaks for cΛ and a_P, but it is linear in c_F (certificate) and is that
of thm:PCfinite. It was not possible to write ℋ_T, V_T, ρ_T with a sign step.
