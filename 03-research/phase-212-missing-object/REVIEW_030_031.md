## A. ROUND_030_A

**A0. Framework: what `D_d` is.** With the box `|d_n| ≤ C(log n + 1)`, the measure `β_d = Σ d_n(δ_{log n} + δ_{−log n})` has
mass `≍ e^u u` on `[u, u+1]`: **it is not tempered**. Its transform `D_d` is not a distribution in `𝒮′` nor in `𝒟′`; it only
exists as a functional on tests with compact transform (or as a hyperfunction). G2_B9_R3 §3 sets as an explicit
hypothesis `b ∈ 𝒯 := {β_b ∈ 𝒮′}` and, inside it, `𝒯_meas`. The memo says that temperedness "enters only so that
`D∗K` makes sense". In fact it enters earlier: without it `D_d` has no points, atoms or order.

**A1. (R′) "`D_d ≤ 0` outside `Z`".** There is a correct path: `T → ∞` with fixed `φ` and the bound `a^v_T(X) ≤ η` of
021_A (1.2). From there `⟨β_d ∗ (M∗M̃), φ∗φ̃⟩ ≤ 0` for every `φ ∈ C_c^∞`. Then the distribution `−β_d ∗ (M∗M̃)` is positive
definite and, by Bochner–Schwartz, it is tempered and the transform of a measure `μ ≥ 0`. Thus `μ = −|Φ_M|² D_d`. That step
is solid. What is missing, and is not in the memo nor in 021_A as cited, is that `c_X − c_{M∗φ}` (effect of `Π_T`) is small
**with the weight `|d_n| ≍ log n`, summed up to `e^{2T}`**. 021_A (1.2) bounds `a_T(Π_Tφ)` for the weight `v`, not
`Σ d_n c_X(log n)`. Dividing by `|Φ_M|²` outside `Z` gives `D_d ≤ 0` on `ℝ∖Z` **only if `D_d` is already a distribution**
(A0). Status: NOT VERIFIED as written; CORRECTABLE under `d ∈ 𝒯` and with the bound on the coupling of `Π_T` proved.

**A2. Prop. 1 — CORRECTABLE (correct deduction, incomplete hypotheses).**
*Corrected statement.* Let `d ∈ 𝒯` (that is, `β_d ∈ 𝒮′`), with `D_d` a locally finite measure and `|D_d|(Z) = 0`. If
(R′) holds for `w = v + d` (A1), then `d = 0`.
*Reconstructed proof.* Since `D_d ≤ 0` on `ℝ∖Z` and does not charge `Z`, one has `D_d ≤ 0`. Take `h ∈ 𝒮` with
`ĥ ∈ C_c^∞(−½log 2, ½log 2)` and `K = |h|²`, so that `K ∈ 𝒮` (not Fejér, which is not Schwartz),
`K ≥ 0` and `supp K̂ ⊂ (−log 2, log 2)`. For every `x`, `∫K(x − τ)dD_d(τ) = ⟨β_d, e^{ix·}K̂⟩ = 0`, because `|log n| ≥ log 2`;
the pairing is legitimate because `D_d` is tempered. Since `D_d ≤ 0`, `D_d` vanishes on `{K(x − ·) > 0}` for every
`x`, and the union of these sets is `ℝ`. Hence `D_d = 0`, `β_d = 0` and `d = 0`.
The memo says "`K > 0` on an open set"; that is not enough for an isolated `x`, and the argument needs all translates.
*What changes with respect to G2_B9_R3 R′.* The final deduction does not use the dichotomy [H]; that is true. But the class
`𝒯_meas` is exactly the one there. The claim "hypothesis strictly weaker than 𝒯_meas" (§3, at the end) is
**false** without temperedness (A0, A4). Correct label: CONDITIONAL on (R′).

**A3. Exclusion of `(1 ± s)v` — CONFIRMED (with a shortcut and one hypothesis fewer).**
One verifies the exact identity `a^{(1−s)v}_T = (1−s)a^v_T + s·Arch` and `a^{(1+s)v}_T = (1+s)a^v_T − s·Arch`.
- With `s ∈ (0, 1]`: `a^{(1−s)v}(X) ≤ (1−s)η + s·Arch(X)`. With `φ̂` concentrated on `|τ| < τ_0` and `ℓ` large,
  `Arch(X) ≈ (2π)^{−1}∫Ω|Φ_M φ̂|² < 0`.
- With `+s`, `s > 0`: concentrate `φ̂` on `|τ| > τ_0`, where `Ω > 0`.
There is no need to go through `D_v` nor the relation `D_v = Ω − G`. Nor is `min Z > τ_0` needed: it suffices that `Φ_M` not
be identically zero on the region (`Z` is discrete). It is also needed that `Π_T` and the tails of `φ̂` alter `Arch(X)`
by `o(1)·‖φ‖²` compared with `|Ω|`. That is standard for `ℓ → ∞` (prolates), but the memo does not write it. Status:
CONFIRMED modulo the citation 021_A (1.2) (upper bound `a^v(X) ≤ η`, internal CITED) and that tail estimate. The
correction to 025_B §1 is correct: `(1−s)v` is not a counterexample.

**A4. "Atoms and dipoles at `Z` contribute exactly 0 in any window" — CORRECTABLE; and (C2) "order ≤ 1" FALSE.**
(i) It is exact only for the ideal pairing `⟨P, |Φ_M φ̂|²⟩` (with `M∗φ` unprojected), and only if the zeros of
`Φ_M` at `Z` are simple or of higher order. In a window, the object is the finite polynomial `D_ℓ` and the test is
`X = Π_T(M∗φ)`, with `X̂ ≠ Φ_M φ̂` (021_A (1.1): terms `A u_+ + B u_−`). An atom of `D` is not an atom of `D_ℓ`.
The sentence "exactly 0 in any window" mixes the two levels.
(ii) Suppose `d ∈ 𝒯`. A term `c δ″_γ` at a simple `γ ∈ Z` satisfies
`|Φ_M|²·c δ″_γ = 2c·h(γ) δ_γ`, with `|Φ_M|² = (τ−γ)² h`, `h(γ) > 0`. That is a point mass of `μ`, **compatible**
with `μ ≥ 0` if `c ≤ 0`. Hence (C2) must admit order ≤ 2 at `Z`, with the coefficient of `δ″` of sign `≤ 0`
(and interaction with `a`, `b` free). "Only `δ″` would be visible, hence it is excluded" is a leap: visible does not mean
forbidden.
(iii) Moreover `N = μ/|Φ_M|²` may fail to be locally integrable near `γ` (arbitrary `μ` near `γ` gives
`N ≍ (τ−γ)^{−2}`). Then `D = −N + P` requires a regularization (finite part) that reintroduces terms at `Z`.
"`N ≥ 0` measure on `ℝ∖Z`" holds only on the open set.
Without temperedness, the whole decomposition is not defined (A0).
Corrected statement of Prop. 2: under `d ∈ 𝒯` and (R′), `μ := −|Φ_M|²D_d ≥ 0` is a tempered measure and
`D_d − (−μ/|Φ_M|²)_{reg}` is supported on `Z` with order ≤ 2 at simple zeros. Without `d ∈ 𝒯`: NOT VERIFIED.

**A5. (I_{T,ℓ}) and its error — NOT VERIFIED (error miswritten).**
The tail `Σ_{e^{ℓ+C_M} < n < e^{2T}} |d_n||c_{M∗φ}(log n)|` starts where the double-exponential bound is `O(1)`. The
correct factor is `exp(−2πe^{2(log n − ℓ − C_M)})`, not `exp(−2πe^{2(T−ℓ−C_M)})`. With the box, the tail is
`≍ Σ_u e^u u·exp(−2πe^{2(u−ℓ−C_M)})`, which is `≍ e^{ℓ+C_M}ℓ` and is not small. Repair: cut the polynomial at
`log n ≤ ℓ + C_M + κ` with `κ ≍ ½log(ℓ+C_M) + O(1)`. Then the tail is `O(e^{−c e^{2κ}})`, subject to `ℓ + C_M + κ ≤ 2T`.
The term `η` of 021_A (1.2) depends on `δ − η` and its identification with `T − ℓ − C_M` is not done in the memo. Moreover
the coupling of `Π_T` with `|d_n|` is missing (A1). The memo itself labels it ARGUED; it does not go up.

## B. ROUND_030_B

**B1. Monotonicity of `m` — CONFIRMED.** From `ψ(z) = −γ + Σ_k [1/(k+1) − 1/(k+z)]` one gets
`Re ψ(a+iy) − ψ(a) = Σ_k y²/((k+a)((k+a)² + y²))`. It is even and increasing in `|y|`, with absolute convergence. Moreover
`C_0 = Ω(0) = m(0)`, so the layer-cake formula `∫m dμ = m(0) + ∫_0^∞ m′(s)μ(|τ| > s)ds` is correct. The
identity `𝔈 = ∫m dμ` is the one in the proof of `thm:A` (P47, Tonelli; cross-checked).

**B2. Bound `𝔈 ≥ M_2 − 5.0434/(J+1)` — CONFIRMED.**
- `J = ⌈2T/log 2⌉ − 1` counts the `j ≥ 1` with `2^j < e^{2T}`, also in the integer case.
- `ν̂(j) = Φ(j log 2) = 0` for `1 ≤ |j| ≤ J`; the condition for `−j` follows from `Φ` being Hermitian.
- Selberg majorant of degree `J` of an arc of normalized length `x`: integral `x + 1/(J+1)`. CITED, correct.
  For `s ≤ L` the arc `2s log 2 ≤ 2π` is proper, and `μ(|τ| ≤ s) ≤ ν(arc)` because the preimage contains the set.
- Using the lower bound `1 − s/L − 1/(J+1)`, even though it is negative near `L`, remains valid because `m′ ≥ 0`.
  The contributions with `s > L` are discarded since they are `≥ 0`.
- Integration by parts gives exactly `M_2 − (m(L) − m(0))/(J+1)`. Recomputed.

**B3. Optimality — CORRECTABLE (scope).** The uniform measure on `[−L, L]` gives `Φ = sinc(πu/log 2)`, which vanishes at every
`j log 2` and gives `𝔈 = M_2`. But `Φ` does not have compact support, does not vanish at the remaining `log n` and does not satisfy
`eq:Kprim`. Correct statement: "`M_2` is the supremum of the bounds that use only `Φ(j log 2) = 0` for every `j ≥ 1`"
(`J = ∞`, without type). For finite `J` it was not proved that the term `−5.0434/(J+1)` is optimal, and for the real problem
(type `2T`, all `n`, primitivity) there is no optimality. The "ceiling" of §2.3 must be read with this restriction.

**B4. "Orthogonality by divisibility adds no information" — CONFIRMED.**
`⟨ξ_{log m}, ξ_{log k}⟩ = Φ(log(k/m))`, and with `m | k`, `k < e^{2T}`, the quotient `k/m ∈ [2, e^{2T})` is already a
constraint `Φ(log n) = 0`. The closure of the chain multipliers is the free-weight class: every Hermitian matrix with
zero diagonal is the off-diagonal part of `H + cI ⪰ 0`. Verified. The claim about the torus (§2.3,
quadrants) remains ARGUED, as the memo says.

## C. ROUND_031_B

**C1. Interlacing — CONFIRMED.** Arch is a closed form, bounded below (`Ω ≥ m(0)`), with domain
`{∫log(2+|τ|)|F̂|² < ∞}`. Its embedding into `L²(−T,T)` is compact: the support is bounded and there is tightness in
frequency (Kolmogorov–Riesz), so the spectrum is discrete. The functionals `F ↦ ∫F e^{±t/2}` are bounded and
linearly independent (Gram `(sinh²T − T²)/π² > 0`, recomputed). Then `P_T ∩ dom` is dense in `P_T` and
Courant–Fischer gives `λ_k ≤ λ_k^P ≤ λ_{k+2}`. Since Ω is even, Arch commutes with `R: t ↦ −t`. `cosh(t/2)` is even and
`sinh(t/2)` odd, so each parity sector loses one dimension and `λ_k^{ev} ≤ λ_k^{ev,P} ≤ λ_{k+1}^{ev}` holds
(likewise odd). The corollaries `n_−(P_T) ≥ n_−(L²) − 2` and `λ_1^P ≤ λ_3` follow.

**C2. Commutation with `t ↦ −t` — CONFIRMED.** P47 (proof of `thm:A`) uses
`c_F(u) = (2π)^{−1}∫cos(τu)|F̂|²`. Since `|(RF)^(τ)|² = |F̂(−τ)|²` and the cosine is even, `c_{RF} = c_F`; by
polarization, each `C_n` commutes with `R`. `P_T` is `R`-invariant. The compressions to the block are
diagonal by parity, and the even–odd part of `ρ` does not enter any trace. Correct.

**C3. "(PBP_T) ⟸ [generation] + [Schur control]" — CONFIRMED, with explicit hypotheses.**
- Schur: if `A ⪰ α`, `B ⪰ β` with `α, β > 0` and `‖X‖² ≤ αβ`, then `[[A, X], [X*, B]] ⪰ 0`; it holds with `B` in
  infinite dimension.
- `|c_F(u)| ≤ ‖F‖²` gives `‖C_n‖ ≤ 1` and the same bound for their compressions.
- `Arch^{CC} ⪰ γ` with `γ` the smallest eigenvalue `≥ 0` of Arch|P_T. If `γ = 0`, the criterion is empty; one must require `γ > 0`.
- The minimum-norm solution exists because `Arch^{BB} − αI` is parity-diagonal and lies in the assumed range. It satisfies
  `‖w‖_1 ≤ √#n·‖w‖_2 ≤ √#n·‖Arch^{BB} − αI‖_F/σ_min`.
- Weak duality: requires `ρ ⪰ 0` of trace 1, with range in the form domain and `Tr(ρ C_n) = 0` for every `n` with
  `w_n ≠ 0`. It suffices that `w` be supported on `2 ≤ n < e^{2T}`, because `c_F(log n) = 0` if `log n ≥ 2T`.
Everything closes. As the memo itself says, it is only a sufficient condition; numerically it fails for `T ≥ 1.2`.
