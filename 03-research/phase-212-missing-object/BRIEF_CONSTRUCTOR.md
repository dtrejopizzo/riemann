# Brief for every memo of phase 212

Read first, in this order: `README.md`, `RULES_AVOID.md`, `OBJECT_SPEC.md` of this folder;
`../phase-211-sign-sandbox/STATEMENT_001.md` (definitions). Then search for the keywords of the
object at hand in: `05-meta-and-planning/ROUTES-MASTER-INDEX.md` (master index, the only record; Parts I–III and the relevant stretch of Part IV);
`03-research/phase-162-row-d-first-contact-closure/grid/R162_2300_MASTER_GRID.md`;
`05-meta-and-planning/ABCD-CLOSURE-PLAN/CROSSWORD-TO-D.md`; `04-papers/48-row-d-route-map/main.tex`;
`../phase-211-sign-sandbox/LEDGER.md` and `POWER_GLOBAL_ROUTE_REVIEW.md`; the annexes `ANNEX_*.md` of
this folder (complete reading of the index and of phase 211); and, because the index summarizes by phase and not
by file, the relevant phase folders of `03-research/` (grep by keyword).

Rules.

1. Sandbox: the target is (★) λ_A(T) ≥ 0 for all T, stated on an explicit quadratic form.
   Do not name, invoke or reason from any equivalence of (★) with another statement; do not reduce to
   zeros; do not argue «this is as hard as…». If a line of reasoning needs it, it is
   abandoned.
2. Routes already tried: nothing in the index is retried, neither under another name nor as a variant, unless the
   memo writes which hypothesis, object or class of methods changes and why that difference avoids the
   recorded reason for the failure. A search without textual matches does not prove novelty.
3. The memo begins with the section «Cross-check»: (1) closest antecedent with file/section;
   (2) genuinely new object or claim; (3) why the change avoids the concrete difficulty of the
   antecedent; (4) which identity or inequality connects the attempt with the global positivity of the
   complete form. If the cross-check identifies repetition, the memo ends there and says so.
4. Changing names, notation, parameters or representation, or building another auxiliary energy without
   a new mechanism for the sign, is not novelty. Neither is reproducing external results or accumulating
   finite orders.
5. Labels on every claim: PROVED / CITED / CONDITIONAL / ARGUED / OPEN / NO-GO / NUMERICAL.
   PROVED carries a complete proof. A computation validates a point and proves nothing.
6. Falsifier test at every sign step (`RULES_AVOID.md` §E): point out the exact line that
   fails for cΛ (c ≠ 1), a_P, finite deletion and amplification, Davenport–Heilbronn, Epstein,
   quartets, f_t and a Beurling system. If the argument survives any of them, it is wrong.
7. Pure mathematics. If a tool does not exist, it is built and proved; if a step fails, it is
   recorded which identity or inequality fails and by how much.
8. If there is no defensible new idea, say so. Novelty is not invented to comply with the rule.
9. Plain mathematical prose; no authors, dates, model names or process roles in the file.
   File: `03-research/phase-212-missing-object/ROUND_nnn_<X>.md`. No paper and
   no other file of the repository is edited.
10. Closing of the memo: «Result» (PROVED piece / NO-GO with class / candidate ready for construction /
    no new mechanism), «What was proved», «What failed and why», «Next concrete step».
