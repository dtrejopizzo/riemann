## Results recorded (all without sign)

R3-a (PROVED, `ROUND_003_3.md` P0–P2). In the algebra generated by multipliers and the reflection J, on
each parity sector of P_T, every identity with squares and pairings by J is a pointwise splitting
of the symbol; the products mn appear only in ε⟨X²F, F⟩, linear in the autocorrelation and of opposite sign
in the two sectors. Does not exclude operators that use the compression to the window, M_t or finite rank.

R3-b (PROVED, `ROUND_003_2.md` Lemma 3; `ROUND_003_7.md` Cor. 3). Paired affine class: V = V_0 + V_1(Λ)
with coefficients independent of the weight and cross term equal to the comb. Then ρ ≤ Arch (≤ Arch_M
in (S)), which is not ⪰ 0 for T ≥ 0.60 (resp. M ≥ M_1). Does not exclude unpaired affine V, V_0 dependent
on the weight, nor V non-affine in Λ.

R3-c (PROVED, `ROUND_003_4.md` Lemma 1, Prop. 2, Lemma 3). In a realization of a_T by restriction or
partial infimum of a semidefinite form, a null vector (in particular the anchor with boundary) does not influence
the identity; the format with boundary coincides with (G). Does not exclude indefinite forms with a non-radical
neutral vector nor the proof-by-contradiction scheme of R170_862.

R3-d (PROVED, `ROUND_003_5.md` Lemma 2). For T > ½ log 2 there is no a_T = κ𝒱_T + ρ_T on P_T ∩ C_c^∞
with κ ≠ 0 and ρ_T ≥ −C‖·‖². Generic in the comb. Does not exclude generators other than pure dilation
nor the pair VIR–LB/VIR–DET on the radical.

R3-e (PROVED relative to P47 thm:PCfinite, `ROUND_003_6.md` Lemma 1, Cor. 2, Lemma 3). Identity on all of
P_T with a truncated Möbius test: a_T = f_0 − f_1 − R_0 + R_1. Under cΛ each exterior block is
proportional to c; every sign statement about a separate exterior block holds for all c > 0 or for
none. The interior cancellation is reabsorbed: g = b_T − f_1 + R_1. Not examined: the sign of the remainder R(F).

R3-f (PROVED relative to P47 thm:oneprofile, not effective, `ROUND_003_7.md` Lemma 1). For M ≥ M_0 there is no
𝔸_M = WᵀDW with W supported on divisibility and D real diagonal. Generic in the comb. Does not exclude W with
support on the pairs m ≈ kn nor identities with remainder.

R3-g (PROVED relative to `ROUND_001_L.md`, `ROUND_003_9.md` Lemma 2). Class of Green identities of resolvent
type M(u) = βu + u⟨g, (H + u²)⁻¹g⟩, H ⪰ 0, on any Hilbert space: the spectral measure is
fixed by M and its positivity is equivalent to that of the ladder. Circularity with class. Does not exclude
dependences on u that are not of resolvent type nor indefinite forms.

R3-h (PROVED, `ROUND_003_1.md` Lemmas E and F). If (G) holds, V_T is bounded in the norm
N_T² = ∫(1 + log(2 + |τ|))|F̂|²: values and pointwise jets are not admissible components. For squares
of grouped truncated translations with indices ≤ I, (G) at T implies
a_{T′}(F) ≥ (1/2π)∫σ_B|F̂|² on P_{T′}, T′ = T − ½ log I: necessary condition.

R3-i (PROVED in two independent memos: `ROUND_003_2.md` Lemma 1; `ROUND_003_4.md` Lemma 4, part on the
leading coefficient). That an identity stops holding for cΛ does not characterize a sign step: every
polynomial identity in the weight valid at Λ differs from its version at another weight by a combination of
the atoms Σ_{d|n}a(d) − log n. Later refinement of O4: an isolated formal breakdown does not prove a sign,
but a proved complete identity with positive right-hand side may fail only in its equality under
a falsifier. It is not necessary for the auxiliary remainder to lose positivity. See the audit of O4/O17
and of Lemma T at the end of Part V of the master index.
