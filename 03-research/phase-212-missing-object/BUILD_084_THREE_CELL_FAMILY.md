# Construction 084 — Primitive family of cells (1,2,k)

## Cross-check and scope

Exact antecedent: cell matrices of 212-59 and carrier equivalence of 212-75. ROUND_018_A of phase 211 treats three cells of a transported tail, with other restrictions and a fixed window. Here three indicators in the physical variable x are studied, with the two exact moments and an arbitrarily large index k. The selection gate and the proof were checked independently.

Status: PROVED for this family, with an analytic proof of the tail and an interval certificate for the finite range. No new representation of the form is claimed, nor positivity for all triples, nor positivity on combinations of the tests. The difference with respect to the local certificates is that the index and the window are unbounded. The result does not by itself satisfy OBJECT_SPEC: it does not separate the falsifiers.

The sign operation is to keep the (1,2) block, discard only the favorable cross term (2,k) and bound the adverse return (1,k) by divisibility of the central binomial. An upper bound of the weights cannot be substituted indiscriminately into the favorable cross terms.

## Family and theorem

Let I_j=[j,j+1), k≥3 an integer and

    a=log 2, b=log(3/2), c=log(1+1/k), d=a−b,
    f_k=(b−c)1_(I_1)+(c−a)1_(I_2)+d 1_(I_k).             (1)

Exactly ∫f_k=0 and ∫f_k(x)/x dx=0 hold: the second equality is
a(b−c)+b(c−a)+c(a−b)=0. Hence F_k(t)=e^(t/2)f_k(e^t) has both primitive moments zero. Its support is in [0,log(k+1)]; translating it by −log(k+1)/2 places it in the window T_k=log(k+1)/2. Translation preserves norm and form. The tests belong to the form domain by 212-75.

For any complex scalar z and any window containing the translated test,

    a_T(zF_k) ≥ (1/1000)||zF_k||².                         (2)

The statement concerns each line generated by F_k. It does not concern the linear span of all of them.

## Exact matrix

Use h(m)=(m+2)log(m+2)−2(m+1)log(m+1)+m log m, with 0 log0=0, and C=−γ−log(2π). The matrix of 212-59/75 is M=A−C_comb, where

    A_ii=C+(i+1)log(i+1)−i log i−h(2i)/2,
    A_ij=−[h(i+j)+h(j−i−1)]/2                 (i<j),
    (C_comb)_ij=Σ_(n i≤j<n(i+1), n≥2) Λ(n)/n (i<j),
    (C_comb)_ii=0.                                        (3)

The energy is α* M α for α=(b−c,c−a,d), at positions (1,2,k). The factor two of each cross term is included. Since h≥0, Λ≥0, c−a<0 and d>0, the cross term (2,k) is non-negative. The (1,2) block equals

    q(c)=M11(b−c)²+M22(a−c)²−2M12(b−c)(a−c),
    M12=A12−(log2)/2.                                    (4)

## Adverse return: a binomial bound

The return is exactly

    R_k=(C_comb)_1k=Σ_(k/2<n≤k)Λ(n)/n.

For k=2m, each prime power q in (m,2m] contributes its prime logarithm to the valuation of the binomial (2m choose m). The remaining contributions to that valuation are non-negative. For k=2m+1 the same happens in (m+1,2m+1]; the possible prime power m+1 has to be added separately, and its contribution does not exceed log k. In both cases,

    Σ_(k/2<n≤k) Λ(n) ≤ log(k choose floor(k/2))+log k
                       ≤ k log2+log k.

Since 1/n≤2/k on that interval,

    R_k ≤ 2log2+2log k/k.                                 (5)

No asymptotic prime estimate was used and the odd case was not omitted.

## Uniform tail k≥256

The identity h(m)=∫_0^1∫_0^1(m+s+t)^(-1) ds dt gives h(m)≤1/m for m>0. Moreover
(k+1)log(k+1)−k log k=∫_k^(k+1)(log x+1)dx≥log k+1. Consequently,

    A_kk≥log k+C,
    |A_1k|≤[1/(k+1)+1/(k−2)]/2.                          (6)

For k≥256, 0<c<1/256, b−c≤b and log k/k is decreasing. Interval arithmetic over ALL c∈[0,1/256] gives q(c)>0.15. Keeping the (1,2) block and omitting only the favorable cross term one obtains

    α*Mα ≥ q(c)+d²(log k+C)
              −2bd(2log2+2log k/k+|A_1k|)
           > 0.08.                                       (7)

The certificate evaluates the right-hand side with k=256 in all the terms that worsen the bound. Its enclosed lower endpoint exceeds 0.08173135. Thus (7) is a bound for infinitely many k, not a numerical extrapolation.

## Finite range and normalization

build_084_data/certify_three_cells.py computes the entries (3) with Arb at 100 digits and generates Λ exactly on the prime powers. It verifies α*Mα>1/1000 for each integer 3≤k≤255. These are 253 verifications of closed formulas; there is no quadrature and no approximate eigenvectors. The file three_cell_family_certificate.json keeps each enclosure and the tail constants. The computation was reproduced independently.

Finally,

    ||F_k||²=||f_k||²=(b−c)²+(a−c)²+d²
                       ≤b²+a²+d²<1.                      (8)

The last inequality is verified with the same interval constants. (7), the finite range and (8) prove (2), also for complex multiples.

## Scope control and next problem

The script variant --scale 11/10 replaces each Λ(n) by (11/10)Λ(n), including M12, and multiplies the bound (5) by 11/10. It also passes the 253 verifications and the tail, whose lower endpoint exceeds 0.06756358. This control confirms that the family does NOT separate that falsifier; the files of both runs are kept separate. It is not a global sign proof for either of the two weights.

To pass from this family to a subspace one needs to control a(F_j,F_k), j≠k. The first part, (1)-(8), does not control those pairings; the next extension treats the cross terms G_3k. For this base claim the F_k are used without individual centering, or all translated by the same amount to a common window. In fact, the F_k form the circuit basis with anchors 1 and 2 of the cell subspace with both moments zero: the coefficient of F_k in cell k is the nonzero constant d. Eliminating the coordinates k≥3 by means of F_k/d leaves a vector on cells 1 and 2; its two moments force it to be zero because a≠b. Independence is read off the coordinates k≥3. Individual positivity only signs the diagonal in that basis; it does not sign its full matrix. The next concrete problem is a rule for its cross terms that preserves the arithmetic cancellations, before enlarging certificates or indices.

## Extension: one full cross term, uniform in the moving index

Status: PROVED for each plane span(F_3,F_k), k≥4, with an analytic tail proof and a finite interval certificate reproduced independently. The F_j use the common coordinates above. It is not a proof for subspaces with several moving indices. The additional operation is to control the full Schur complement, including the cross terms, and not merely to enlarge a table of diagonals.

Write G_jk=a(F_j,F_k). For u=(b−c_3,c_3−a,d), c_3=log(4/3), set

    t_i=Σ_(r=1)^3 u_r M_ri, i=1,2,
    s(c)=(b−c)t_1+(c−a)t_2.

The direct congruence, without oblique projection of the comb, gives

    G_3k=s(c)+d Σ_(r=1)^3 u_r M_rk,
    G_kk=q(c)+d²M_kk+2d[(b−c)M_1k+(c−a)M_2k].           (9)

Here G_33>0.08381066 is certified exactly as in the first family. The necessary and sufficient condition for the plane is

    S_k=G_kk−|G_3k|²/G_33>0.                             (10)

### Bounds for returns 2 and 3

For i=2,3, A=floor(k/(i+1)), B=floor(k/i), k≥12, one has B≤2A. Each prime power q with A<q≤B contributes one unit to the corresponding valuation of binom(B,A): q>A and q>B−A. The other contributions are non-negative. Hence

    C_ik ≤ log binom(B,A)/(A+1)
          ≤ B log2/(A+1) < ((i+1)/i)log2.                (11)

(5) is kept for i=1. Likewise, |A_ik|≤[1/(k+i)+1/(k−i−1)]/2 when k>i+1.

### A majorant of the whole cross term

Fix N=16384 and define

    J_i=[1/(N+i)+1/(N−i−1)]/2,
    P_+=(−u_2)[(3/2)log2+J_2],
    P_−=u_1[2log2+2logN/N+J_1]+u_3[(4/3)log2+J_3].

The signs of u are (+,−,+), while M_rk≤0. For all k≥N,

    s(c)−dP_− ≤ G_3k ≤ s(c)+dP_+,
    0<c≤1/N.                                             (12)

Evaluating s over that whole interval and taking the maximum of the absolute values of both endpoints gives |G_3k|≤B_*=0.21007079018... (with enclosure in the certificate). The anchor block, the three returns and the whole archimedean part are included. A signed sum is not replaced by a sum of absolute values of its six pieces.

For the diagonal the positive unit omitted in (6) is kept:

    A_kk≥log k+C+1−1/(4k).

With q_box the interval enclosing q(c) for c∈[0,1/N],

    S_k ≥ q_box+d²(logN+C+1−1/(4N))
           −2bd[2log2+2logN/N+J_1]−B_*²/G_33
         >0.00270647>0.002                 (k≥N).         (13)

The use of N is valid because log k increases, log k/k decreases, and the J_i and 1/k decrease. All endpoints are rounded outward.

### Bound in the original norm

The certificates also verify G_33>0.08 and |G_3k|<1/4 on the finite range and the tail. By (8), both norms of the generators are less than one. With r=G_3k/G_33 and zeta=z+rw, |r|<25/8 and

    ||zF_3+wF_k||²≤2(|z|²+|w|²)
                      ≤4|zeta|²+(657/16)|w|².

Comparing with (14), G_33>0.08 and S_k>0.001 give

    a(zF_3+wF_k) ≥ (1/50000)||zF_3+wF_k||².              (15)

The use of the norm Gram is replaced here by an explicit norm inequality; coordinate pivots are not confused with L² eigenvalues. The limit is still a family of planes: the cross terms between two moving indices and their joint effect in arbitrary dimensions are missing.

## Joint control: a negative combination despite all planes being positive

Status: PROVED by an explicit test with an interval certificate, reproduced independently without eigenvectors. The limit of (2) and (15) is checked on the falsifier itself, instead of inferring a universal obstruction from an abstract matrix example.

A single block N=256 and the weight (11/10)Λ were taken. A floating-point eigenvector on the kernel of the TWO moments proposed the coordinates; that diagnostic plays no role in the proof. The 254 circuit coefficients were frozen as

    gamma_k=m_k/2^40, m_k integer, 3≤k≤256,
    F=Σ_(k=3)^256 gamma_k F_k.                            (16)

The complete list of integers is in build_084_data/joint_scaled_witness_256.json. The parametrization (16) guarantees the two moments exactly. joint_falsifier_witness.py --verify reconstructs the full form with Arb at 100 digits from those integers, without running the solver. All F_k are translated by the same amount; the window T=log(257)/2 suffices.

For the same F it is certified that

    a_(11Λ/10)(F)/||F||² < −0.09317,
    a_Λ(F)/||F||² > 0.03674.                             (17)

The enclosed squared norm is approximately 1.00000000000040757. The sign does not depend on the rounding of the eigenvector: the interval evaluation is on the frozen test. (17) is only a positive test for Λ, not a bound for the whole block for that weight.

Let E_diag=Σ gamma_k²G_kk and E_cross=a(F)−E_diag. The certificate keeps all terms and obtains:

The values are enclosures from the report, summarized here; the sign does not come from subtracting rounded decimals of this table. No single pair responsible for the difference is identified: E_cross is the joint sum.

Eliminating the generator F_3 exactly gives

    a(F)=|a(F_3,F)|²/G_33 + R(F),
    R(F)=Σ_(j,k≥4) gamma_j gamma_k
                   [G_jk−G_j3 G_3k/G_33].                (18)

The first contribution is positive: approximately 1.03656946e−6 for Λ and 1.54367925e−5 for the scaled weight. The remainder is respectively more than 0.03674416 and less than −0.09319156. Thus, the scaled negativity remains entirely in the coupling of the moving indices after extracting the square already built.

### Decision for the next design

The weight (11/10)Λ satisfies ALL the planes of (15) and violates positivity of a combination of them. Therefore, the global proof cannot be continued simply by accumulating those plane controls. The next step must act on the collective pairings of the remainder of (18), with a law that uses the true arithmetic data. (18) is the already known Schur identity, now accompanied by a control showing where the extension fails; it is not presented as a new equivalence nor as a sign mechanism. No paper was modified and no new positivity window on the full carrier was declared.
