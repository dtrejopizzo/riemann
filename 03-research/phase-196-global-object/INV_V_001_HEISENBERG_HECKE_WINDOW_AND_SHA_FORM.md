## 1. Definitions

**Embedding.** For `F ∈ L²(I_T)` put `f(x) = |x|^{−1/2} F(log|x|)` (`x ∈ ℝ∖0`), even, supported in `e^{−T}<|x|<e^T`.
Then `‖f‖²_{L²(ℝ)} = 2‖F‖²`.

**Operators on L²(ℝ).** `τ_a g(x) = g(x−a)`, `M_b g(x) = e^{2πibx} g(x)`, `δ_n g(x) = n^{1/2} g(nx)` (unitary),
`𝔉 g(ξ) = ∫ g(x) e^{−2πixξ} dx`.

**The algebra.** `𝔅 := C*(τ_1, M_1, δ_n : n ∈ ℕ) ⊂ B(L²(ℝ))`.

**The Ш-form.** For Schwartz `u, w`: `⟨u,w⟩_Ш := Σ_{j∈ℤ} û(j) \overline{ŵ(j)} = Σ_{a∈ℤ} (u * w̃)(a)` (Poisson), `w̃(x)=\overline{w(−x)}`.

**FE-dual of F.** `G` defined by `f̂(ξ) = |ξ|^{−1/2} G(−log|ξ|)`; equivalently `Ĝ(t) = γ(½+it) F̂(t)` with Tate's archimedean
ratio `γ(s) = π^{1/2−s} Γ(s/2)/Γ((1−s)/2)`, `|γ(½+it)| = 1` (CITED: Tate's local functional equation; sign convention of
`t` as in P46 §1, up to `t ↦ −t`). `G` is not window-supported.

## 2. Results

**Thm V1.1 (exact vector-state realisation; PROVED).** `⟨δ_n f, f⟩ = 2𝒞F(log n)`, hence
`𝔞_T(F) = A_∞(F) − ½ Σ_{n∈𝒩_T} v_n Re⟨δ_n f, f⟩`, with `A_∞(F) = (1/2π)∫ m |F̂|²` a function of the dilation generator.
*Proof.* `∫ n^{1/2} f(nx) \bar f(x) dx = 2∫_0^∞ x^{−1}F(log x+log n)\bar F(log x)dx = 2𝒞F(log n)`. ∎

**Thm V1.2 (relations; non-factorisation of 𝔅; PROVED).**
(i) `τ_1 δ_n = δ_n τ_1^n`, `δ_n M_1 = M_1^n δ_n`, `δ_n τ_1 δ_n^* = τ_{1/n}`, `𝔉 τ_1 𝔉* = M_{−1}`, `𝔉 δ_n 𝔉* = δ_n^*`.
So `𝔅` contains `τ_q, M_q` for all `q ∈ ℚ` and is generated by the rational Heisenberg group `H(ℚ)` ⋊ `ℚ^×_{>0}`; the
additive generator `τ_1` is conjugated by every `δ_p` into `τ_{1/p}` — the semiring relation `(+)∘(·)`.
(ii) `𝔅` is not a tensor product `⊗_p 𝔅_p ⊗ 𝔅_∞` of algebras each containing `δ_p` and commuting with `δ_q`, `q ≠ p`,
in which `τ_1` lies in the `∞`-factor: if it were, `δ_p τ_1 δ_p^* = τ_{1/p}` would lie in `𝔅_p ⊗ 𝔅_∞` for every `p`, and
`τ_{1/(pq)} = δ_p τ_{1/q} δ_p^*` would require `τ_{1/q} ∈ 𝔅_p⊗𝔅_∞` and `∈ 𝔅_q⊗𝔅_∞`, i.e. in `𝔅_∞`, so `δ_q` would commute
with `τ_{1/q}`, false. ∎ (The abelian subalgebra `𝔇 = C*(δ_n, functions of the dilation generator)` does factor: Mellin
diagonalises it to functions of `t` and `δ_n ↦ n^{it}`.)

**Lemma V1.2′ (the abelian part carries nothing new; PROVED).** Any positivity statement expressed inside `𝔇` (positive
elements, states, functional calculus) is a pointwise statement on a function of `τ` on the line, i.e. a symbol inequality;
by thm:Z / def:PD its window version is a dual certificate. Only the non-commuting generators `τ_1, M_1` can carry
something that is not a dual certificate.

**Thm V1.3 (Ш-form = Schur product over places at abscissa 1; PROVED, modulo the `t`-sign convention).**
For `F ∈ 𝒫_T ∩ C_c^∞(I_T)`: `f̂(0) = 2A(F) = 0`, and
(a) `⟨δ_n f, f⟩_Ш = 2 Σ_{j≥1} j^{−1} G(log n − log j) \overline{G(−log j)}`, while `⟨δ_n f, f⟩ = 2∫_ℝ G(u+log n)\bar G(u)du`
(= `2𝒞F(log n)`, since `|Ĝ| = |F̂|`). The Ш-form replaces the Lebesgue integral in the FE-dual log variable by the
Riemann sum at the nodes `u = −log j`, weights `1/j`.
(b) In Mellin variables, `‖f‖_Ш² = (2/(2π)²) ∬ Ĝ(s)\overline{Ĝ(s′)} ζ(1+i(s−s′)) ds ds′` (as the limit `ε↓0` of
`ζ(1+ε+i(s−s′))`), and `ζ(1+ε+iu) = ∏_p Σ_{k≥0} p^{−k(1+ε)} p^{−iku}` is a **Schur (Hadamard) product of positive-definite
Toeplitz kernels, one per prime**, multiplied by the rank-one archimedean kernel `γ(½+is)\overline{γ(½+is′)}`.
*Proof.* (a) `(δ_n f)^(ξ) = n^{−1/2} f̂(ξ/n)`, insert `f̂(ξ) = |ξ|^{−1/2}G(−log|ξ|)`; the `j=0` term vanishes because
`f̂(0) = ∫f = 2∫F e^{u/2} = 2A(F) = 0`; `j` and `−j` give equal terms (f even). (b) Write `G(−log j) = (1/2π)∫Ĝ(s) j^{−is}ds`
(convention), sum `j^{−1−ε} j^{−i(s−s′)}`; positivity of each Euler factor is Bochner for the measure `Σ_k p^{−k}δ_{k log p}`. ∎

**Corollary V1.4 (NO-GO for "Poisson on ℤ as a unitary/positive form" on the window; PROVED for the class 𝒦_Ш).**
Let `𝒦_Ш` be the arguments whose positivity input is the positive-definiteness of Ш (equivalently of `⟨·,·⟩_Ш`) applied to
vectors in `span{δ_n f}` and whose arithmetic enters only through the comb weights. Then: (1) the free positivity is the
Schur-product positivity of `ζ(1+iu)` over places (Thm V1.3b) — it factorises place by place (fails requirement
"non-factorizable"); deleting primes `p < P` keeps it (`∏_{p≥P}` is still PSD), so it is O_6-stable on the positivity side;
(2) the comb `v` enters linearly, so the class is comb-generic (B″, clause (1)). Hence 𝒦_Ш proves nothing at `T ≥ 0.60`.
This makes lesson (iii) of phases 194–195 precise: **Poisson self-duality of ℤ, read through Mellin, is the Euler product at
σ = 1.** The ℤ-structure is not lost; it is converted into a product over places at the other abscissa.

**Remark V1.5 (where the ℤ-structure survives; PROVED).** The same form has a *finite additive* description:
for window `f`, `(δ_n f * f̃)(a) = 0` unless `|a| < e^T(1 + 1/n)`, so
`⟨δ_n f, f⟩_Ш = Σ_{|a| < e^T(1+1/n)} (δ_n f * f̃)(a)` — finitely many integer shifts `|a| ≲ √N`. The Mellin side
(all primes, Schur product) equals a finite sum over the additive lattice. The `a = 0` term is the window prime form; the
`a ≠ 0` terms are the **affine near-coincidence form**, the object of INV_V_002. That piece is not diagonal in Mellin
(its kernel is `ζ(1+iu) − 2πδ(u)` up to normalisation) and is the only part of the construction that is neither a dual
certificate (Lemma V1.2′) nor Schur-factorised (Thm V1.3).
