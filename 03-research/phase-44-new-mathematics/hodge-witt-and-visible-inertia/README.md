# Phase 44, second thread — Hodge–Witt and visible inertia

A short, explicitly exploratory restart after the circularity verdict of Phase 43. Rather than seeking another positive quadratic form, this phase sets out to define an object "prior to positivity" — one that survives when RH fails and makes the failure visible — replacing the search for Kähler leaves with a Hodge–Witt/profinite-boundary framework carrying a natural symmetric form over Morishita's profinite transversal.

## Results
- A starting framework with four internal lemmas proved within its own definitions (labeled [THEOREM] but valid only inside the newly defined objects, not yet connected to $\zeta$). (PROVED, internal to the new framework only)
- The bridge from this framework to RH is stated as conditional on constructing a "pre-RH Hodge–Witt polarization with visible failure," which does not yet exist. (OPEN — explicit wish, not a result)
- Three concrete open problems are posed: building a profinite-boundary complex with nontrivial cohomology and a natural symmetric form (replacing "Kähler leaves" with "profinite boundaries with Witt duality"), and verifying that the scalar/trace shadow of the construction recovers the known explicit formula. (OPEN)
