## 1. Correction prior to the premise of the objective: 𝒜, A, B are not gauge invariant

The objective assumes that 𝒜, A and B are gauge invariant and F is not. **That is false, and the existing data refute it.**
prop:ext-gauge (Paper 47, l.9725) only gives the invariance of 𝒲. In `step5_gauge.log` (NUMERICAL), for a pure gauge φ_{k=2}
one gets 𝒜 = −6.843e−5, 2AB = +6.794e−5 and F′(0) = −4.95e−7. Each piece is ≠ 0 and the sum is ≈ 0 (2e−14). The correct
statement is:

**Lemma G.1 (PROVED, given Theorem D of A_MU and prop:ext-gauge).** Let r be admissible and let r′ = r + φ with φ ∈ 𝒢_T (C²). Then

 𝒜[Zr′] + 2A B(r′) − F′_{r′}(0) = 𝒲([r])

is independent of φ, while each summand depends on φ. For every ε > 0 let

 L_{r′}(ε) := 𝒜[Zr′] + 2AB(r′) − (F_{r′}(ε) − F_{r′}(0))/ε,  Δ_ε(r′) := (F_{r′}(ε) − F_{r′}(0))/ε − F′_{r′}(0).

The exact identity **L_{r′}(ε) = 𝒲([r]) − Δ_ε(r′)** holds.

*Proof.* Subtract the two definitions and apply Theorem D to r′, which is admissible. ∎

Δ_ε is a quadratic form in r′. With ν_{r′} = Σ K_{r′}(m,n) δ_{log mn} (LEMMA_F §2) and g_ε(t) := (e^{εt} − 1 − εt)/ε ≥ 0,
one writes **Δ_ε(r′) = ∫ g_ε dν_{r′}**. The measure ν_{r′} has sign, so Δ_ε is indefinite. If F_{r′} is convex on
[0, ε], then Δ_ε(r′) ≥ 0.

All the information in the A_MU bound over the fiber is in G.1. **The bound is 𝒲([r]) minus a non-negative quantity that
depends on the representative.**

## 2. Exact statement

**Lemma F∃ (gauge-existential).** There exists ε₀ = ε₀(μ) > 0 such that for every class [r] ∈ 𝒬_μ = Π_μ(𝒟) there is an
admissible representative r′ ∈ r + 𝒢_T with ε ↦ F_{r′}(ε) convex on [0, ε₀].

**Controlled version (F∃_c).** For ε ∈ (0, ε₀] define

 V_ε([r]) := inf{ Δ_ε(r′) : r′ ∈ r + 𝒢_T admissible, F_{r′} convex on [0, ε] } ∈ [0, ∞].

The best bound given by Lemma 6.1 of LEMMA_F, optimized over the fiber, is

 𝒲([r]) ≥ sup_{r′ convex} L_{r′}(ε) = 𝒲([r]) − V_ε([r]).

## 3. Decision

### 3.1 Lemma F∃: true by scaling

**Lemma G.2 (positive cone inside the gauge; PROVED on 𝒢̄_T).** Let ψ ∈ C_c^∞ be even, with supp ψ ⊂ [−1/(2λ), 1/(2λ)], ∫ψ = 0 and
ψ ≢ 0. Let φ₀ := ψ ∗ ψ̃. Then φ₀ ∈ L²_0 (even, supp ⊂ [−1/λ,1/λ], ∫φ₀ = |𝓕ψ(0)|² = 0). By Π.0, 𝓕φ₀|_{(λ,∞)} ∈ 𝒢̄_T,
and ρ_{φ₀} = |𝓕ψ|² ≥ 0. By LEMMA_F Prop. 5.2, F_{φ₀} is log-convex.

The convexity is strict: |𝓕ψ|² is entire and nonzero, so K_{φ₀}(m,n) > 0 for all m, n. Hence ν_{φ₀} ≥ 0 has
mass at t = log 2 > 0, and F″_{φ₀}(ε) ≥ c₀ > 0 on [0, ε₀]. ∎

There is an obstacle: φ₀ ∉ 𝒢_T, because φ₀(0) = ‖ψ‖² ≠ 0, and def:ext (l.9662) requires φ(0) = 0 for the admissible C²
gauge. The C² case is handled as follows. Π.0 gives the density of {φ ∈ C², φ(0) = 0, ∫φ = 0} in L²_0 for the L² norm of
the tails. The inequality F″ ≥ c₀ on [0, ε₀] is open in the weighted norm ‖ω^{1+δ}ρ‖_{L²}, but its continuity is the same
gap as F⁻.2(b). There is also NUMERICAL support: the two pure C² gauges of step5 give F″_φ(0) = 5.2e−7 and 1.1e−5, both > 0.
**Status: F″_φ > 0 on [0, ε₀] for some φ ∈ 𝒢_T is ARGUED (density + continuity) and NUMERICAL (step5).**

**Theorem G.3 (F∃ true; PROVED on 𝒢̄_T, ARGUED on 𝒢_T).** Let φ be as in G.2. For each r there exists t₀(r) such that, if
t ≥ t₀, F_{r+tφ} is convex on [0, ε₀].

*Proof.* F″_{r+tφ}(ε) = F″_r(ε) + 2t·B″_ε(r,φ) + t²F″_φ(ε). The three coefficients are continuous in ε on the compact
[0, ε₀], and F″_φ ≥ c₀ > 0. It suffices to take t² c₀ > sup|F″_r| + 2t·sup|B″_ε|. ∎

**Answer to 2(a).** The question is whether the class of the negative PW direction of T10.1 (F″/ℓ = −5.4e−3) has a
convex representative. **Yes**, by G.3 (ARGUED in C², as above). Convexity appears through scaling, without any
computation on v_min. v_min is not in a fixed gauge: T10.1 does not project with Π_μ nor impose g(T) = 0. It was not
determined whether its minimal-leak representative Π_μ v_min still has F″ < 0, since that would require a new
computation. It remains OPEN with no consequence (§3.3).

### 3.2 Why it is empty

By G.1, at the convexified representative r_t = r + tφ:

 L_{r_t}(ε) = 𝒲([r]) − Δ_ε(r) − 2tΔ_ε(r,φ) − t²Δ_ε(φ),  Δ_ε(φ) = ∫g_ε dν_φ ≥ (ε/2)·F″_φ(0) > 0.

**The bound produced is 𝒲 − Θ(t²) → −∞.** F∃ is true and **empty**: it imposes no restriction on 𝒲.

### 3.3 The controlled version F∃_c: tautological or stronger than the objective

(i) **Trivial bound (PROVED).** By G.1, sup_{r′ convex} L_{r′}(ε) = 𝒲([r]) − V_ε([r]) ≤ 𝒲([r]). The bound over the fiber
never exceeds 𝒲, which Theorem D already gives exactly with the same data. This is point 2 of LEMMA_F §6, now over the
class.

(ii) **Two regimes, neither useful.**
- With ε free per class, Δ_ε(r′) → 0 as ε → 0 for every r′ with F″ bounded near 0. Then V_ε → 0 on the classes that have
  some convex representative in a neighborhood of 0, and that holds for all of them by G.3. The bound converges to 𝒲 and
  is a **tautology**.
- With ε = ε₀ uniform, positivity of the bound on 𝒬_μ means inf_{[r]} (𝒲 − V_{ε₀})/ℓ > 0. Since V ≥ 0, this statement is
  **strictly stronger** than inf 𝒲/ℓ > 0 (PROVED: it implies the objective and is not implied by it). It is an upward
  TRANSFER, not an input.

(iii) **The variational problem is not tractable.** One must minimize the indefinite form Δ_ε over an affine fiber of
infinite dimension, with the constraint {F″_{r′}(s) ≥ 0, s ∈ [0, ε]}. That constraint is a family of cones of indefinite
forms: the kernel (log m + log n)² has signature (2,1) and F⁻.1 gives infinite negative index. The feasible set **is not
convex**. It is a non-convex QCQP with no available strong duality structure: ARGUED, by the S-lemma only with one
constraint (CITED, Yakubovich), and here there is a continuum of constraints. One obtains neither existence of a
minimizer nor a formula.

(iv) **The hidden h*.** Π_μ does not use h*: it is the archimedean projector onto 𝒦_T (PI_MU Π.0′/Π.1, PROVED, without Λ
or Guo). h* appears in two other places:
- (a) The minimizer of V_ε over the fiber is not Π_μ r. With t ↦ Δ(r + tφ) quadratic, the optimum depends on r and on
  the form Δ_ε, which contains the weights n^ε of the comb. That optimum is not a fixed projector, so A_ε − (correction)
  is neither linear nor independent of the vector. Gate G16.0 asked for a correction **without h*** that would make A_ε
  vanish on 𝒢̄_T. The only linear, h*-free correction is Π_μ, and at Π_μ r convexity is not guaranteed: F⁻.2(a) ARGUED
  gives an r that is its own minimal-leak representative and has F″ < 0.
- (b) The sign is only seen at the extremal. To control inf_{[r]} one must evaluate 𝒲 − V at the minimizing class, that
  of r* (Mori). The only signed data point is L_{r*}(0.186) ≈ 2.5e−70 (F0_TEST, NUMERICAL, at a single representative and
  without proving convexity: r* is in no cone of LEMMA_F §5). That number is ≤ 𝒲(r*) ≈ 4.98e−70, as required by (i), and
  there is no mechanism to extend it beyond r*. **Every version of F∃_c with content is an inequality evaluated at the
  minimizer.** It falls into 𝒦_H (Hellmann–Feynman/minimizer; l.602, l.604).

(v) **Answer to "is the inf computable and does it have a sign at r*?".** On the class of r*, V_ε([r*]) ≤ Δ_ε(r*) = 𝒲 − L
≈ 2.5e−70 (NUMERICAL). So 𝒲 − V ≥ 2.5e−70 > 0 **on that class**, which is a worse bound than the exact value 4.98e−70.
Computing the exact inf on that class would require solving the QCQP of (iii). Even if it were solved, by (i) it would
give a number ≤ 4.98e−70 and nothing about the other classes.

## 4. Verdict of gate G16.0

**G16.0: PROVED-trivial-empty (F∃), with a content NO-GO (F∃_c).**
- F∃ (existence of a convex representative per class): **true**. PROVED on the closure 𝒢̄_T with the cone ρ ≥ 0 of
  ψ ∗ ψ̃ and scaling. ARGUED/NUMERICAL for the admissible C² gauge (missing the weighted continuity of F″, the same gap as
  F⁻.2). It is empty: the resulting bound is 𝒲 − Θ(t²).
- Lemma G.1 (PROVED): L_{r′}(ε) = 𝒲([r]) − Δ_ε(r′). It corrects the premise: 𝒜, A, B and F′(0) depend on the gauge and
  only their combination is invariant (step5, NUMERICAL).
- F∃_c: with ε → 0 it is a tautology; with uniform ε₀ it is strictly stronger than the objective (upward TRANSFER); as a
  variational problem it is a non-convex QCQP whose only signed evaluation is at the minimizer r* (𝒦_H). **NO-GO as
  an input** (PROVED in (i) and (ii); ARGUED in (iii) and (iv)).
- The gate asked for a "gauge correction without h* that makes A_ε vanish on 𝒢̄_T and lowers F(ε) to 𝒬_μ." **It does
  not exist in a useful form.** The exact descent is G.1, which replaces F(ε) with 𝒲. The only linear correction
  without h* is Π_μ, and it does not preserve convexity (F⁻.2(a), ARGUED).

**What remains.** G16 leaves the tractability queue: 0 YES. One OPEN residue remains, with no consequence for D: the
sign of F″ at Π_μ v_min (T10.1 with fixed gauge and g(T) = 0). If it were negative, F⁻.2(a) would move from ARGUED to
NUMERICAL in the admissible class; if it were positive, nothing would change by §3.3(i). The only thing G16 leaves for
Paper 47 is G.1 as a remark, at most a note after thm:ext-half. It is not integrated.

**Do not retry:** Lemma F in any variant over representatives (uniform, existential, minimal-leak,
sup/inf over the gauge), unless an object is presented that is not a lower bound of 𝒲 over the fiber.
