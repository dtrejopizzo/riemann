Question: did we grossly discard some reformulation of D that had its own proof path, even if a stronger one?
**Answer: there was NO gross error in the outcome; there WAS a method error.** ~350 REF-D labels were reviewed (README 103,
04_PHASES 237, 06_ 10, phase 193 9) plus 25 CIRCULAR rows from phases 0–99 and ~20 omissions found via synonyms. Unified, these are **43
distinct reformulations**. Step 1 proposed 4+3+0 "YES" candidates (R6, R10, R13; G4, G10, G14, G16; K10, K11, K12, K26); after
three audits and seven targeted constructions, the final count is **0 YES / 22 TRANSFER / 21 NO**. Each candidate fell through
one of three routes: (i) transfer — the applicable general theory (Szegő, Nevanlinna, Douglas, Kreĭn, Müntz, Hahn–Banach) consumes
the positivity of the window datum; (ii) 𝒦_H / Theorem X / W2 — the continuation-in-T step is a functional of the minimizer h*;
(iii) explicit falsifier (Theorem F⁻, K12's Lemma 2.1, Lemma J as a basis artifact). The method error: the index never
recorded tractability and phases 193 and 200 used "REF-D" as an outcome; 38 of 90 README entries had been discarded by
judgment alone. The judgment turned out correct in every case, but it was not written down. What the phase did produce: **ten
independent PROVED results** (Lemma T3, Theorem P1.A, Lemma 2.7, PC-2, Lemma WT, Theorem F⁻, Lemma P2-S.3, K12's Lemma 2.1, Lemma J, Lemma G.1),
five integrated into Paper 46 (189 pp). The only one with genuinely new content is **prop:WT** (per-sector inertia criterion without
λ_A, in infinite dimension). None bounds the sign. Five named OPEN questions remain, of which three are REF-D at bottom.

## 3. Closed answer to the original question

**PARTIAL: there was no gross error in the outcome; there was a method error, now corrected.**

1. **Method error.** The index never recorded a tractability column; "REF-D" was used as an outcome (phases 193, 200), against
   the index's own header. 42% of README entries were discarded by judgment alone, without writing down why the
   general theory gave no path of its own.
2. **Why the outcome did not change.** The 43 reformulations fall into three boxes: (a) **transfer**: the applicable general theorem
   (Szegő, Nevanlinna/de Branges, Douglas, Kreĭn, Müntz, Hahn–Banach, Fredholm) has as its hypothesis the positivity of the window
   datum, which is exactly what is missing; (b) **𝒦_H / Theorem X / W2**: every continuation-in-T step (contacts, polar
   capacity, Robbin–Salamon, eigenvector rigidity) is a functional of the minimizer h* or requires cancellation at doubly
   exponential precision (R169 Cor 2′), and lem:monoT forbids gaining anything at the crossings; (c) **explicit falsifier**: Theorem F⁻ (Lemma F),
   K12's Lemma 2.1 (Λ + cδ_N), Lemma J (basis jump, intrinsically null).
3. **What would have changed by doing this earlier.** Nothing regarding the sign. But: the six structural lemmas (T3, P1.A, 2.7, PC-2, WT,
   F⁻) would have been obtained several phases earlier, and prop:WT would have been available as a per-sector inertia tool.
