## Summary (≤200 words)

Lemma B.1: **PROVED, but with the definition of a_P mistranscribed.** In Paper 46, a_P = Λ·1{p ≥ P} (the primes
p < P are erased, all their powers k ≥ 1), the archimedean symbol is the same m(τ) as for ζ, and no term appears
from the zeros of ∏(1−p^{−s}) on Re s = 0. Risk (i) is thereby ruled out. With 𝒫_< := {p < P} the identity holds,
𝔠_p = Σ_k v_{p^k} Re c_F(k log p), with no fixed sign. Lemma B.2: **PROVED** for every T > ½log p (η = T − ½log p is
any positive margin). Cor. B.3: correct in its formula, but (a) in the window T ≤ ½log P the comb of a_P is
**empty**, so Σ𝔠_p(g*) is the entire comb of ζ and the corollary is a restatement of "the archimedean minimizer
has a negative comb"; (b) the "reading" has the sign flipped: erasing lowers, restoring raises; (c) the label is
PROVED for T ≤ ½log5 and CONDITIONAL (Mori, CITED) for T ≤ ½log15. Lemma A.1: trivial and with one incorrect
sentence (near-coincidence ≠ exact relation); C-emb survives in reformulated form. F8, as written, excludes the
form 𝔞^ζ itself: it is **ill-posed**. Verdict: NO-GO round accepted; F8 withdrawn or rewritten.

## 1. Lemma B.1 against prop:shape-eulerdel

**Exact definition from the paper.** prop:shape-eulerdel: "a_P := Λ·1{n is a prime power p ≥ P}", with
ζ_{a_P}(s) = ζ(s)∏_{p<P}(1−p^{−s}). Therefore, for p < P **all** powers p^k, k ≥ 1, lose their weight
(not just Λ(p)). SPEC_007 writes "∏_{p∈P}" with P a set; one must fix P := {p < P}. If this is not fixed, Cor. B.3
cites a witness that does not exist (the paper gives no witnesses for arbitrary sets of erased primes).

**Archimedean term.** The paper's proof states "the comb of a_P is empty in 𝒩_T when e^{2T} ≤ P, so
WP_T(a_P) is λ_inf^P(T)". That is, the form of a_P has the same shape as eq:form with the **same** m(τ) = Re ψ(¼+iτ/2) − log π and the
comb v_n replaced. Def:shape-class treats the comb as the only datum that changes. The zeros of ∏_{p<P}(1−p^{−s})
on Re s = 0 do not enter: the paper only uses that they do not break the critical strip and that they break the functional
equation. **Risk (i): ruled out.**

**Signs.** eq:symbol gives s_T = m − Σ v_n cos(τ log n), and (1/2π)∫cos(τu)|F̂|² = Re c_F(u). Then
𝔞^L(F) = 𝔪(F) − Σ_n v_n^L Re c_F(log n), and erasing the comb of p adds +Σ_k v_{p^k} Re c_F(k log p). Therefore

  𝔠_p(F) = Σ_{k≥1, k log p < 2T} (2 log p) p^{−k/2} Re c_F(k log p),

which matches SPEC_007's formula (the "2" from v_n appears there as 2Re(g∗g̃); there is no double counting). On P_T the
2Re(A B̄) term from eq:W vanishes; "C-pol" is not needed. **Lemma B.1: PROVED** (linearity in the comb), with the
correction P := {p < P}.

## 2. Lemma B.2

The construction is correct and only strictly needs T > ½log p. Let η := T − ½log p > 0. Bumps φ with support of
radius ε < η/4 at x₀ = −T + ε and x₀ + log p, both inside (−T, T). For r, two more bumps are used in the interior, at
positions y₁ ≠ y₂ chosen so that no difference between centers falls in (k log p − 2ε, k log p + 2ε) for k ≥ 1,
except that of the pair (a, b). This is possible: every interior center difference is < 2T − 2ε, and only finitely
many short intervals need to be avoided. The coefficients of r come from a 2×2 system with matrix [[e^{y₁/2}, e^{y₂/2}],
[e^{−y₁/2}, e^{−y₂/2}]] (integrated against φ), whose determinant is ∝ 2 sinh((y₂ − y₁)/2)·(∫φe^{±t/2})-factors ≠ 0. Hence
A = B = 0 holds. For k ≥ 2 there are no pairs of supports at distance k log p (and if 2T < 2 log p, it does not even
enter the comb). Result: 𝔠_p(F) = v_p·a b‖φ‖²·(1 + 0), and the sign is that of ab with a, b real. Here c_F(log p) is
the pair's autocorrelation, which equals ab‖φ‖²·1 at u = log p exactly: φ∗φ̃(0) = ‖φ‖². **PROVED.** The "+η" in the
statement is not an extra hypothesis: it is the name of the margin. No larger window is needed.

Scope remark: the lemma proves indefiniteness on P_T, not that the 𝔠_p(g*) have mixed signs in any
concrete witness.

## 3. Cor. B.3

For T ∈ [0.60, ½log P] one has e^{2T} ≤ P, so **the comb of a_P is empty in the window**. Then
𝔞^{a_P} = 𝔪 (archimedean only), g* is the primitive archimedean minimizer (λ_inf^P(T) < 0, cor:Tarch) and

  Σ_{p<P} 𝔠_p(g*) = Σ_{n∈𝒩_T} v_n Re c_{g*}(log n) = 𝔪(g*) − 𝔞^ζ(g*).

The inequality Σ𝔠_p(g*) < −𝔞^ζ(g*) ≤ 0 is correct if 𝔞^ζ(g*) ≥ 0. Labels:
- T ≤ ½log5 (≈0.805): **PROVED** by thm:log5A (λ_A ≥ 2.5·10^{−12}) and lem:monoT.
- ½log5 < T ≤ ½log15: **CONDITIONAL** on the Mori certificate (CITED, verified in phases 194–198).
- T > ½log15: OPEN.
(The interval [0.60, ½log P] is nonempty only if P ≥ 5.)

**Sign error in the "reading".** Since Σ𝔠_p(g*) < 0 and 𝔞^{a_P} = 𝔞^ζ + Σ𝔠_p, it is **erasing** the primes that pushes
the form below zero, and **restoring** them that raises it to ≥ 0. The sentence "Restoring the primes of P pushes the form below
zero" is reversed. The conclusion ("the primes of P are necessary in g*") is correct.

**Over-reading.** That positivity is a "collective balance" does not follow from B.2 + B.3. It may be that all
𝔠_p(g*) are individually negative. B.3 only says that the full comb of ζ in the window is negative at the
archimedean minimizer. That is already the content of cor:Tarch plus thm:log5A, nothing new. Label it a
restatement.

## 4. Lemma A.1

It is **trivial**: any functional of ν_Λ|_{[−2T,2T]} is a function of the finite data {n ≤ e^{2T}, Λ(n)}. There is no need
to speak of factorizations, because Λ(n) already encodes them. One sentence is false: a near-coincidence
|log m − log(kn)| < π/T with m ≠ kn is **not** an exact integer relation. It is the inequality between integers
|m − kn| < kn(e^{π/T} − 1) (approx. kn·π/T), and it is decided by exact finite arithmetic. Reformulation: "near-
coincidences at resolution π/T are decidable predicates on the finite list of integers ≤ e^{2T}; they are data, not
equalities." **Conclusion C-emb (a) does not change**: C-data classifies data with no free variable, whether they are
equalities or inequalities. But the citation of Theorem C1 ("equalities between DATA") must be explicitly broadened to
finite predicates. If it is not broadened, branch (a) has a formal gap.

## 5. C-emb and F8

**C-emb.** With the correction from §4 it is well formulated as a trilemma: (a) finite datum / (b) linear in the comb /
(c) support only. Clause (c) depends on Lemma E (support ⊇ ζ): ok per LEMMA_E_EPSTEIN_001. It lacks a fourth branch: Ψ that
reads the embedding **outside** the window. SPEC mentions it (strategy E, phase 199) but does not include it in the
statement. It must be added as (d) with its reference.

**F8 is ill-posed.** It states: "the object cannot be written as Σ_p Ψ_p(𝔠_p) with Ψ_p monotone." But the form
itself is 𝔞^ζ = 𝔪 − Σ_p 𝔠_p, additive over primes with Ψ_p = −id (monotone). So F8 excludes the target itself, and that each
𝔠_p is indefinite blocks nothing: a sum of indefinite forms can be positive semidefinite with no special mechanism
needed. What remains is the reading "not factorizable as a sum of per-prime certificates with fixed sign," and that is already
stated by W-lin / Theorem X (l.536) and the frustration of l.281. If F8 additionally demands a non-positional free
variable (§D), in the window there are only two variables: the data (C-data) and the test g. First-order functionals in
g* are Theorem X. A genuine free variable would have to be (i) an outer parameter (positions > e^{2T}, strategy E,
already recorded), (ii) a continuous deformation parameter of the family (T itself, or a θ from C-θ, which is affine ⇒
branch 1), or (iii) an auxiliary space of higher-rank tests (Gram/Schur over several g), which is W-det/W-mom. There is
no candidate outside what is recorded. **F8: withdraw as a positive requirement**, or rewrite as "the object must use g
at order ≥ 2 not reducible to W-det/W-mom, or outer data with a reason new relative to E.1/E.1′," and label it OPEN with
no constructive content.
