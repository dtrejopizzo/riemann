## Summary (≤250 words)

Over 23 rounds phase 200 produced about 45 PROVED tools. Four groups stand out. (i) The exact anatomy of the
exterior form: c_N = −μ(N) on [μ,2μ), T.1, T.3, the c_{2M} rule, Lemma R, A.1, and Π.0–Π.3 (the leak quotient is a
prolate projector). (ii) Two new falsifiers certified by interval arithmetic: Epstein x²+5y² (Lemma E, T = 159/100,
−0.05994) and Davenport–Heilbronn (CERT_DH, T = 9/5, −7.05·10⁻⁹ odd and −2.38·10⁻⁷ complete; **still
unaudited**). (iii) The algebra of the Euler defect: the Λ_cosh identity, Theorem S (the form is affine in θ),
Lemma 12.1 (cumulants), Lemma 13.1 (the class defect is an indefinite finite comb) and the additivity Φ.12, which is
already in P47. (iv) Method theorems: M200, Φ200 (five branches; PROVED in every channel, ARGUED in exhaustiveness
and composition), C-data, C-pol, C-MvN, C-RR, HA/HB/HB′, Thm 3′ and N2.

The constraint table now has 33 constraints from phase 200 plus the 28 earlier ones. The v12 living specification
demands an object that lives on P_T, is nonlinear and discontinuous in the comb, feels Λ_cosh, does not depend only
on (ℚ,ℚ^×), is neither data nor a quotient coordinate, is not an analytic-rank count on the test nor an oscillation
count, and whose output is a quadratic RR with a computed χ (SPEC-𝔇, S1–S6), with the exogenous variable outside the
L-functions with FE over ℚ. No known object satisfies S1 ∧ S3. Three yes/no questions remain for phase 201 (§6).

## 4. Living specification of the sign object (v12)

### 4.1 Mandatory properties

Each property comes with the constraint that imposes it. A phase 201 candidate must state in writing how it meets
each one.

- **O1. Live on P_T.** The object is evaluated on tests with A = B = 0. By C-pol, the pole, the residue, the Tate
  mode, the lattice origin and 𝔊_T are null there. By C-RR, a curve's Serre duality is also null.
- **O2. Nonlinear and discontinuous in the comb.** There are three causes: C-θ (affinity in θ), C-τ (S_∞ has empty
  interior) and constraint 1 (TV). No continuous bound on the comb, uniform in T, survives. Also the object cannot be
  linear in Λ (Φ200 branch 1, Φ.14).
- **O3. Feel Λ_cosh.** By C-E′ and by Lemma E, positivity must fail on ½(Λ_K+Λ_ψ)+Λ_cosh. The object must see the
  quadratic term Λ_cosh(n) = log n·Σκ_jb^{∗2j}(n), and more generally the cumulant comb Λ_def, which Lemma
  13.1 shows is indefinite. So it is not enough to "see" Λ_cosh with a fixed sign: it must see it coupled to the test.
- **O4. Not depend only on (ℚ,ℚ^×).** By C-MvN, every isomorphism-class invariant of the additive-multiplicative
  pair dies, because Beurling, a_P and K share L(G) = R. Λ's information lives in the log embedding: ℚ^× → ℝ. By
  C-emb, every functional of that embedding inside the window is either data or linear.
- **O5. Be neither data nor a quotient coordinate.** By C-data, a relation between data does not move the dual
  value. By R-Q2, R-Q6 and R-Π, levels, mollifiers, classes, bands and projectors with kernel ⊇ 𝒢̄_T are coordinates
  inside the radical or are archimedean. Anti-tautology test P10: evaluate modulo 𝔑; if the result reduces to
  𝒲[g], it is a NO-GO.
- **O6. Be neither Hankel nor a product.** By R-K1, a Hankel on the gap has NN′ structure. By COSH_DEFECT and
  Lemma 12.1, the multiplicative Hankel 𝒬_g is indefinite. By R11_2/O2, Selberg's identity Λ∗Λ is data.
- **O7. Not be an analytic-rank count on the test.** By Thm 3′ and δ ≡ 0, a rank-type χ is generic (Vandermonde)
  and does not see the test. If it sees it through the form, it fails S1.
- **O8. Not be an oscillation count.** By Lemma N2, the gap comes from a cutoff unrelated to the comb, and
  negativity lives in ≤ 8 low-frequency prolates (CS-1).
- **O9. Output = quadratic RR with computed χ (SPEC-𝔇).** Construct 𝔇 ⊋ D^W and prove on 𝔇 a quadratic
  Riemann–Roch equality with χ(E) ~ ½I_∞(E,E), obtained by computation:
  - S1: without using the form or its kernel.
  - S2: with an integer dimension that reads (ℕ,+,·) (C-MvN).
  - S3: with a Serre duality that is not Poisson (C-RR).
  - S4: on P_T, without a polar term.
  - S5: discontinuous in the comb, with Epstein's failure localized in 𝔇. AUDIT_DCONS: 𝔇 does not exist over
    positions of Z_Q but does exist over ideals of O_K.
  - S6: it must see Λ_cosh.
- **O10. Exogenous variable.** By Prop 18.4 and the conclusion of SPEC_009, the free variable is introduced by
  the object and lives outside the category of L-functions with FE over ℚ. Weight, level and spectral parameter
  enter only via Γ/conductor and are additive via Φ.12.
- **O11. Declaration rules** (META_200 + C-Guo). Every candidate must state (a) through which channel Λ enters it
  (symbol, coordinate, projector, gap, quantitative Guo, embedding, or exogenous), (b) which property of ζ is
  missing in Epstein and in F_DH that its method uses, and (c) which falsifier from battery 1–12 would kill it if it
  were comb-generic.
