## 3. Lemma 12.1 (unified statement)

**Notation.** ℓ_χ := log L(s,χ) = Σ ℓ_χ(n)n^{−s}, ℓ̄ := h^{−1}Σ_χ ℓ_χ (the "Euler average": log of the degree-2
Euler product with common Γ, ζ_K^{1/h}Π L_χ^{1/h}), y_χ := ℓ_χ − ℓ̄ (Dirichlet series with no constant term, Σ_χ y_χ = 0).
w_g(N) := log N · N^{−½} c_g(log N) (COSH_DEFECT §1.2), ⟨u, w_g⟩ := Σ_N u(N) w_g(N).

**Lemma 12.1 (PROVED).** Let C = C₀ be the principal class. Then
  (h/w) Z_{C₀} = Σ_χ e^{ℓ_χ} = h · e^{ℓ̄} · E_χ[e^{y_χ}]   (E_χ = uniform average over Ĉl),
  Λ_{Q}(n) = Λ̄(n) + Λ_def(n),  Λ̄ := h^{−1}Σ_χ Λ_χ,  Λ_def(n) = log n · [n^{−s}] 𝒦(y),  𝒦(y) := log E_χ[e^{y_χ}]
and 𝒦 is the cumulant generating function (in the Dirichlet algebra, product = multiplicative convolution):
  𝒦 = Σ_{j≥2} (1/j!) κ_j[y],  κ₂ = E_χ[y_χ^{∗2}],  κ₃ = E_χ[y_χ^{∗3}],  κ₄ = E[y^{∗4}] − 3E[y^{∗2}]^{∗2}, …
(κ₁ = E y = 0). In window form (same Γ and conductor ⇒ C₀ and the Lévy term cancel exactly):
  **𝔇^{(h)}(g) := 𝔞^{Q}(g) − h^{−1}Σ_χ 𝔞^{L_χ}(g) = −𝒬_g^{(h)}[y] − R₃(g),**
  **𝒬_g^{(h)}[y] := (1/h)Σ_χ ⟨y_χ ∗ y_χ, w_g⟩ = (1/h)Σ_χ Σ_{m,n} y_χ(m) y_χ(n) w_g(mn),**
  R₃(g) := Σ_{j≥3} (2/j!)⟨κ_j[y], w_g⟩.
𝒬_g^{(h)} is a sum of h multiplicative Hankel forms with the **same** kernel K_g(m,n) = w_g(mn); it is real because
y_χ̄ = ȳ_χ and the sum is invariant under χ ↔ χ̄ ("B" = the h × ∞ matrix of differences y_χ(n), and 𝒬 = h^{−1}tr(B K_g Bᵀ)).

*Proof.* log Z = log(w/h) + log Σ_χ e^{ℓ_χ}; factor out e^{ℓ̄}; −d/ds of Σ u(n) n^{−s} is Σ u(n) log n n^{−s}; the factor 2
and the sign come from the term −Σ 2Λ(n)n^{−½}c(log n) of the template; the cumulant expansion of log E e^{y} is
formal and converges in the truncated Dirichlet algebra (each n has bounded Ω(n)). ∎

(a) **The natural form of the defect is the cumulant one, not the Hankel one.** 𝔇^{(h)} = −⟨𝒦(y), w_g⟩ (with §3's normalization) is the pairing of

of coefficients; the pairing with indefinite w_g does not inherit a sign. There is no "𝒬_g ≥ 0 on log-Euler differences."

(b) **Structural proposal (ARGUED, falsifiable).** What is positive in both calibrated cases is not 𝒬_g but the
**exponential**: 𝔞^{Q} − 𝔞^{base} measures log E_χ e^{y_χ} against w_g, and in the witness the composite term n = m·n′ with m, n′
non-principal split primes is what weighs most (6 in both h's). The useful hypothesis would be "w_g ≥ 0 on class
composites" — false in general (c_g(log 8), c_g(log 16) < 0 give favorable terms), so there is no uniform sign here either.

(c) **The non-principal class is not a perturbation of anything.** Its defect includes −2log m_C·c(0) and frequencies outside ℕ.

principal class is the only regime of Lemma 12.1.
