## 2. Common failure signature (phases 100–162)

Every route in the slice either (i) produced an exact identity whose positive part is a *square that is blind to orientation* (it contains `A_M²`, the truncated Weil square, or a Bochner/Cauchy–Schwarz autocorrelation, so it gives lower bounds while D needs the opposite direction — W4, R161_002, R161_081), or (ii) produced a positivity that is *place-local* (prime-only, archimedean-only, single fibre, single window, fixed finite basis) and therefore is beaten by the arena law P4 and the margin law P12, or (iii) produced a global object whose positivity was *postulated* (h⁰ with (b′), MW-5 carriers, Kreĭn indeterminacy, flat-top certificate) and turned out to be a REF-D form. Underneath all three: the arguments used Λ only linearly or through Λ≥0/support, so a falsifier from the §I-b catalogue (Ξ_a, DH, deletion, M_m, DMV) shares every premise; the only place where ζ was ever seen *as ζ* inside the form was the contact itself (P7: the truncated Pólya radical) and the Gamma-pole/image structure of (PC) (P15) — and both were reached only in the "if a first-contact vector exists" mode, never as a free positive quantity with a direction.

## 4. The most distinctive property of the sign object (from this slice alone)

The sign object must be **a positive quantity attached to the exterior of the window that is faithful on, and only on, the truncated Pólya radical of ζ**. Everything certified in 100–162 points to the same location: inside the window the sign is an exponentially fine cancellation among a growing arena of archimedean modes (P4, P12) that no local or finite argument can resolve, and the only places where ζ appears *as ζ* rather than as one member of a falsifier class are (a) the near-kernel of the form, which is the explicit truncated radical `P(z²)Ξ(z)` (P7), and (b) the reflected exterior image whose Gamma-pole Hankel energy is positive and faithful (P15). The object would therefore not certify positivity on the window directly; it would carry a *one-sided coercivity across the window edge*, transversal to the Pólya radical, whose positivity comes from a free square on the exterior (Gamma poles or the σ=1 Euler/Schur positivity) and whose failure for Ξ_a is algebraic (cross term, P9) rather than numerical. Its signature property: it is *orientation-sensitive* — it changes sign under `A ↦ −A`, unlike every square built in this slice.
