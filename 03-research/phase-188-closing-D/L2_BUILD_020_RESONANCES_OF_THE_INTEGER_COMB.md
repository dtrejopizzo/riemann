## 1. Integer near-triples are exact at resolution Ω ≥ N³ (PROVED)
For n_k, n_l, n_j < N put x_± := log n_k − log n_l ± log n_j. Write x_− = log(n_k/(n_l n_j)), x_+ = log(n_k n_j/n_l). If x is not 0
and |x| ≤ log 2, then the two integers in the ratio are within a factor 2 of each other; in each case one of them is a single support
point < N (n_k in x_−, n_l in x_+), so both are ≤ 2N, and |x| ≥ |A − B|/max(A,B) ≥ 1/(2N). If |x| > log 2, then Ŵ(x) ≤ 4/(Ω log²2).
Hence for every non-exact triple Ŵ_Ω(x) ≤ 4·4N²/Ω = 16N²/Ω, and for exact triples Ŵ_Ω = Ω.
Exact triples with all three in the support of Λ (prime powers): n_k = n_l n_j forces n_l = p^a, n_j = p^b, n_k = p^{a+b} (one prime).

## 2. The natural resonance (a = v) gives nothing beyond O(1) (PROVED)
With a_k = v_k in 019 §3 and Ω = N³:
 numerator Num = ½Σ_{j,k,l}v_jv_kv_l[Ŵ(u_k−u_l+u_j) + Ŵ(u_k−u_l−u_j)] = Ω·C_ex(T) + R, 0 ≤ R ≤ A³·16N²/Ω = 16A³/N = O(e^{T}),
 C_ex(T) := ½·2·Σ_p Σ_{a,b≥1, p^{a+b}<N} v_{p^a}v_{p^b}v_{p^{a+b}} = Σ_p 8(log p)³ Σ_{a,b} p^{−(a+b)} ≤ 8Σ_p (log p)³/(p−1)² =: C_ex < ∞;
 denominator Den = Σ_{k,l}v_kv_lŴ(u_k−u_l) = ΩΣv² + R′, 0 ≤ R′ ≤ A²·16N²/Ω = O(1).
So sup_{|τ|≤N³} Re S ≥ Num/Den ≥ C_ex(T)/(8T²)·(1 + o(1)) — O(T^{−2}). For ℚ-independent ("generic") positions the exact-triple
term is 0. **The integer comb and generic positions differ in the natural resonance only by the prime-power ladder constant
C_ex = O(1)** (finite: (log p)³/p² summable).

## 2b. At the coordinator's scale Ω ≈ N = e^{2T} (ARGUED, density computation)
At Ω ≈ N the non-exact near-triples are NOT suppressed (|x| ≥ 1/(2N) gives Ωx ≥ ½ only). Replace the k-sum by the comb density
(PNT, CITED: Σ_{n∈du}v_n ≈ 2e^{u/2}du) and ∫Ŵ_Ω = 2π: for n_ln_j < N, Σ_k v_kŴ(log n_k − log(n_ln_j)) ≈ 4π√(n_ln_j). Hence
 Num ≈ 2πΣ_{n_ln_j<N} 4Λ(n_l)Λ(n_j) = 8πΣ_{m<N}(Λ*Λ)(m) ≈ 8πN log N = 16πTN,   Den ≈ ΩΣv² + 2πΣ_k v_k·2e^{u_k/2} ≈ N(8T² + 8π),
so the resonance ratio is ≈ 2π/T: small. The near-triple mass at this scale is the DENSITY average (the Λ*Λ mean value), which any
comb with the same density has (generic positions included); it is the part that the subtraction of G in E removes. Near-triples
give no excess beyond density at Ω ≈ N in this mean sense; fluctuations of Σ_{m<x}Λ*Λ(m) − x log x (Selberg-type, CITED order
O(x)) would be the only ℤ-specific term, of relative size O(1/log N) here.

## 3. The integer resonator (multiplicative structure of ℕ used) — PROVED identity, quantitative bound PROVED up to stated errors
Let P be a finite set of primes, r multiplicative, supported on squarefree P-numbers, r(p) = x_p ∈ (0,1], L := Π_{p∈P}p. Use
Φ = W_Ω|R|², R(τ) = Σ_n r(n)n^{iτ}. Exactly as in 019 §3, every term is ≥ 0, and the exact relations m = n·p (p ∈ P ∩ supp v)
give, for Ω ≥ C·L·N (all relevant ratios m/(n p^{±1}) separated by ≥ 1/(2LN)):
 sup_{|τ|≤Ω} Re S(τ) ≥ ½ Σ_{p∈P} v_p · x_p/(1+x_p²) − (errors O(A·LN/Ω) + O(Σ_{p∉P}…)·0) − |G|-type term (019, ≤ 4κ(e^T+1)/|w| — negligible
 at high τ but not at low τ; restrict W to |τ| ≥ Ω/2 by using W_Ω − W_{Ω/2}… omitted).
Proof of the main term: Σ_n r(n)r(np) = x_p Π_{q≠p}(1+x_q²) and Σ_n r(n)² = Π_q(1+x_q²); ratio x_p/(1+x_p²). ∎
Optimizing with x_p = 1 and P = {p < y}: log L = θ(y) ≈ y, and ½Σ_{p<y}v_p·½ = ½Σ_{p<y}log p/√p ≈ √y. Hence
  sup_{|τ|≤Ω} Re S(τ) ≳ √(log Ω)  (Ω ≈ e^{y}N, y ≤ log Ω).
This is the familiar Montgomery-type √log size (CITED as the known order; the lower bound here is self-contained up to the error
terms, which are ARGUED small for Ω ≥ L N²).

## 4. Comparison (PROVED as statements about these bounds)
(i) g_1(Ω) ≈ log Ω ≫ √(log Ω): the resonance lower bounds never reach the archimedean symbol at high frequency. They do not produce
    symbol negativity for ζ at |τ| ≳ e^{2T}, and they are of the same order for generic positions (for ℚ-independent positions with
    the same weights, Kronecker gives the same √log-order lower bounds by an analogous resonator on the free abelian group
    generated by the positions — ARGUED).
(ii) ℤ versus generic: the only additive relations among support points of Λ are the prime-power ladders p^a·p^b = p^{a+b}; their
    total weight C_ex is O(1). In the frequency-space resonance sense **ℤ's comb is additively generic up to O(1)**: no measurable
    structural advantage or disadvantage at the level of sup Re S. (The integer resonator in §3 uses ℕ's multiplicativity, but its
    output depends only on the weights v_p and on ℚ-independence of log p, which generic positions also have.)
(iii) Consequence: multiplicativity of ℤ cannot act through the SIZE of the symbol's excursions (pointwise level); if it acts at all,
    it acts through the P_T-correlation (which positions the excursions relative to the Tate-constrained band-limited test functions)
    — consistent with 015 (no cross terms in the form), 017 (anti-alignment needed), 019 (low-band density deficit).
