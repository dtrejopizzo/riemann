  uniformly in T (N_011 Thm 1; CONFIRMED). Continuous Beurling systems ψ_B = κ(x−x₀)_+ are in Pos (Cor 2, with 4κ(√x₀−1) < η_κ).
- **Atomic positive comb.** A multiscale trapezoid lattice comb with κ > κ* lies in Pos for all T (L2_018 Thm B; CONFIRMED).
  Not Beurling-integral, not about ζ.

  Euler–Lagrange with transversal crossing (N_012).

- **Criticality.** Packet limits of the fluctuation equal m^♯(γ) at every frequency (N_013), the centre case being L2_010; the
