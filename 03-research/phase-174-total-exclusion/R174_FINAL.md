## TC-174 — prespectral Euler–Poisson classification

Let \(\mathcal S(\mathbb A_{\mathbb Q})\) be the Schwartz–Bruhat space and
let \(\mathcal A_{EP}\) be the full involutive algebra generated by the
standard Fourier transform, the idelic dilations, the local operators of
the spherical vectors \(\mathbf1_{\mathbb Z_p}\) assembled via restricted
tensor product, and the Poisson distribution of the diagonal
\(\mathbb Q\subset\mathbb A_{\mathbb Q}\), with all its relations.

Denote by \(\chi_{EP}\) the character distribution obtained in **a single
step** by evaluating those relations: its local side is the Euler product
of \(\mathbb Z\) and its additive side is Poisson over that same
\(\mathbb Z\).

Let \((H,\pi,\Omega)\) be a continuous, cyclic representation of
\(\mathcal A_{EP}\) on a complete nuclear space; its Morita amplifications
and its decompositions into minimal cyclic components are allowed.
Suppose that:

- \(\pi\) preserves all the above relations and its character is
  \(\chi_{EP}\);
- the Tate–Meyer quotient \(H^1_{\mathrm{alg}}\) injects into \(H\) and
  contains, with multiplicity, every integer term of the character;
- **minimality of the character:** for every nonzero closed invariant
  submodule \(M\ne0\),
  \[
  \bigcap_{a\in\mathcal A_{EP}}
  \ker\!\left((\chi_{EP}\!\circ\pi(a))|_M\right)=\{0\},
  \]
  where the composition denotes the induced continuous functional;
- \(Q\) is a closed positive Hermitian form on a common dense domain; its
  positivity comes, prior to the spectral decomposition, from a square, a
  positive trace, or an index, and it is
  \(\mathcal A_{EP}\)-balanced:
  \[
  Q(\pi(a)x,y)=Q(x,\pi(a^*)y).
  \]

**Pending theorem.** Every triple with these properties is Morita
equivalent to the regular Tate–Meyer module; under that equivalence,
\(H^1_{\mathrm{alg}}\) is essential and there exists an operator \(A\),
affiliated to the commutant of \(\pi(\mathcal A_{EP})\), with dense range,
such that
\[
Q(x,y)=W(Ax,Ay),
\]
where \(W\) is the global Weil form. In particular, every compatible,
minimal, prespectral positivity able to read the full character is
Weil's criterion, up to that dense-range amplification.

The hypotheses assign no positive weight to the eigenspaces and contain
no windows, densities, or spectral multisets. A Beurling system,
including \(P_{171}\), lacks an additive diagonal
\(\mathbb Q\subset\mathbb A_{\mathbb Q}\) with Poisson compatible with its
Euler factors; Davenport–Heilbronn lacks the local Tate tensor product
compatible with its functional equation. The filter thus acts before the
spectrum.
