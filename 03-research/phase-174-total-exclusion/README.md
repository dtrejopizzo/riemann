# Phase 174 — Toward a general classification theorem excluding all standard constructions

Phase 173 found three specific Hermitian-form constructions all NO-GO but could not rule out every
possible construction. This phase attempts to state and prove a single general classification
theorem (TC-174) that would show any representation of the full Euler–Poisson algebra compatible
with minimality and balanced positivity must be Morita equivalent to the regular Tate–Meyer module,
reducing row D's closure to one precise, isolated statement.

## Results

- TC-174, the prespectral Euler–Poisson classification theorem, is stated precisely but remains a **pending theorem**, not proved: three rounds did not establish it with the label PROVED.
- The construction of the Tate–Meyer module's H¹_alg and B_Φ within that model — **PROVED**.
- Morita universality (every admissible representation equivalent to the regular module) — **missing/OPEN**, identified as the first unresolved cut.
- The A22 synthesis (essentiality of H¹_alg in every admissible completion, and factorization of every closed balanced form as A*WA) — **missing/OPEN**, the second unresolved cut.
- Beurling systems (including P₁₇₁ of phase 171) are shown to lack the additive diagonal ℚ ⊂ 𝔸_ℚ compatible with Poisson; Davenport–Heilbronn lacks the compatible local Tate tensor product — both excluded as counterexamples to TC-174 before any spectral argument, i.e. the filter acts pre-spectrally — **PROVED** for these two families.
- Overall status: **NO-GO** for obtaining TC-174 (or its negation) within three rounds; the available exhaustivity evidence is labeled `ANALYSIS`, not `PROVED`.
