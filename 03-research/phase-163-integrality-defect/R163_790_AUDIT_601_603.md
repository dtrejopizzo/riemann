## R163_601

**Theorem 1. CORRECT, checked line by line.**
- Step 1: $h=|\widehat\psi|^2=\widehat{\psi*\tilde\psi}$ with $\tilde\psi(t)=\overline{\psi(-t)}$; $\mathrm{supp}\,\psi*\tilde\psi\subset(-2\varepsilon,2\varepsilon)$.
  Poisson for $\sum_kh(\tau-kL)$ gives $\frac{2\pi}L\sum_m(\psi*\tilde\psi)(\pm2\pi m/L)e^{\pm2\pi im\tau/L}$ (the sign of the
  exponent is convention-dependent and irrelevant). The nodes $2\pi m/L$, $m\ne0$, lie outside $(-2\varepsilon,2\varepsilon)$ iff
  $2\pi/L\ge2\varepsilon$, i.e. $\varepsilon L\le\pi$; the memo's strict $\varepsilon L<\pi$ is safe. $m=0$ gives $\frac{2\pi}L\|\psi\|^2=1$. ✓
- Step 2: $\widehat\psi^\#(z)=\overline{\widehat\psi(\bar z)}$ is entire; the PWS bound $C_Ne^{\varepsilon|y|}(1+|x-kL|)^{-N}$ makes the series locally
  uniformly convergent; identity theorem. ✓ (Only (a) on $\mathbb R$ is needed for (b),(c); (a) on $\mathbb C$ is used in Cor 3.)
- Step 4: for $\mathrm{supp}F\subset[-T,T]$, $c_F$ is continuous with support $[-2T,2T]$ and $c_F(\pm2T)=0$, so the extra comb
  terms with $2T\le\log n<2T+2\varepsilon$ vanish; hence $\frac1{2\pi}\int\sigma_{T+\varepsilon}|\widehat F|^2=\mathfrak a_T(F)$. Dominated convergence
  is legitimate: $|\sigma_{T+\varepsilon}|\le\log(2+|\tau|)+C$, $F\in\mathcal D_T$ is exactly the log-weighted domain of `eq:form`, and the
  partial sums of $\sum_kh(\cdot-kL)$ lie in $[0,1]$. ✓ (Primitivity of $F$ is not even used in (b),(c).)
- Primitivity preservation: $\widehat F_k(\pm i/2)=\widehat\psi(\pm i/2-kL)\widehat F(\pm i/2)=0$ because $\widehat\psi$ is entire. ✓
  Support: $\mathrm{supp}F_k\subset[-T-\varepsilon,T+\varepsilon]$. ✓ $F_k$ is complex; Lemma 0 covers this. ✓
- "No cross terms": correct and for the stated reason ($\mathfrak a$ is a Fourier multiplier, the partition is quadratic,
  the polar term is killed by primitivity). Rem 3a is right that a linear partition, or sharp cells, would not do this.

**F1 (Def, minor).** "Closed-range subspace (image of an injective bounded map)": injective + bounded does not give
closed range, and here the range is *not* closed ($\widehat H\mapsto\widehat\psi(\cdot-\gamma)\widehat H$ with $\widehat\psi$ decaying
super-polynomially). Delete "closed-range". Harmless for the statements (the Rayleigh infimum over $\mathrm{Loc}$ equals the
infimum over its form-norm closure).

**F2 (substantive, affects §3(2c), §4.2 bullets, Prop 4 "Reading").** The concentration bullet of §1 bounds the far spectral
mass by $s_\psi(R)^2\|H\|^2$, i.e. relative to $\|H\|$, not $\|F\|$. Since $\|F\|/\|H\|$ is unbounded below on $\mathrm{Loc}$ (take $\widehat H$
small near $\gamma$), the *normalised* elements of $\mathrm{Loc}_{T'}(\gamma)$ are not concentrated near $\gamma$, and the Rayleigh bottom
$\lambda_{\rm loc}(\gamma,T')$ is the bottom on the closure, which contains every $F\in\mathcal P_{T'}$ with $\widehat F$ vanishing on the
zero set $Z(\widehat\psi)+\gamma$ (necessary; whether it is also sufficient is a Beurling–Malliavin-type question, not settled here).
$Z(\widehat\psi)$ has real-part density $\varepsilon/\pi$ spread over all of $\mathbb R$, so the constraint is global, not a cell
condition. Consequences:
- §3(2c) "Loc with $\varepsilon\asymp T'$ is effectively $O(1)$-dimensional (Slepian)" is **wrong** for the Rayleigh problem (and
  contradicts the memo's own "infinite-dimensional"). Slepian counting applies to the $\|H\|$-concentrated cone only.
- §4.2 "every $k$ in the $O(1)$-dimensional local cone": same fix.
- Prop 4 "Reading" ("the doubly exponential smallness must sit where $\log\gamma\lesssim2T'$"): unsupported. The small
  Rayleigh quotients can sit in the non-concentrated part of $\mathrm{Loc}(\gamma)$ at any $\gamma$. It is labelled ARGUED; downgrade to
  HEURISTIC or delete.
- The title word "local" should be qualified: $P_{\rm loc}$ is a positivity statement on a $\gamma$-dependent subspace defined
  by a $\gamma$-shifted zero set; locality holds only on the cone $\{\|F\|\ge c\|H\|\}$.
Thm 1, Cor 2, Prop 4 (the inequality itself) are untouched by F2: they are exact identities.

## R163_603

**L3.1. CORRECT.** $G_s=G+sG/(z-x)$; $\widehat H_x=G/(z-x)\in PW_T$, primitive ($\widehat H_x(\pm i/2)=0$), in $\mathcal D_T$; $W(F,H_x)=0$ since $F\in\ker$
and $W_{T_c}\ge0$ at the contact; so $W(G_s)=s^2W(H_x)$, and $=s^2w_xG'(x)^2$ by definition of $w_x$. $w_x=0\Rightarrow H_x\in E_c$ ✓ (null vectors
of a nonnegative closed form are in the kernel). The "parity normalisation" is broken by sliding one node; irrelevant.

**L3.2. CORRECT WITH FIXES (F10).** A cleaner proof than the memo's: with $K:=G/((z-a)(z-b))$, $\widehat H_a=(z-b)K$, $\widehat H_b=(z-a)K$, the symbol is
real, so $W(H_a,H_b)=\frac1{2\pi}\int\sigma(\tau-a)(\tau-b)|K|^2=W(F,K)=0$ ($K$ primitive, in the domain). Hence:
- the identity holds for **any two distinct nodes**, not only consecutive ones;
- the correct zero-side form is $\sum_{\rm on}\frac{G(\gamma)^2}{(\gamma-a)(\gamma-b)}=-\sum_q\sum_{t\in\{t_q,\bar t_q,-t_q,-\bar t_q\}}\frac{G(t)^2}{(t-a)(t-b)}
  =-2\sum_q\mathrm{Re}\Big[\frac{G(t_q)^2}{(t_q-a)(t_q-b)}+\frac{G(t_q)^2}{(t_q+a)(t_q+b)}\Big]$
  (using $G(-t)^2=G(t)^2$, $G(\bar t)=\overline{G(t)}$). The displayed (3.2) with $4\mathrm{Re}\,G(t_q)^2/((t_q-a)(t_q-b))$ is **false** unless
  $\{a,b\}=\{-b,-a\}$; "symmetrising over $(a,b)$ and $(-b,-a)$" changes the left side too, so it does not rescue the display.
  The memo's proof text half-admits this; the displayed formula must be replaced.
- Reading, first bullet: correct. Second bullet: $\mathrm{Re}\frac{t-a}{t-b}=\frac{(\gamma-a)(\gamma-b)+\delta^2}{|t-b|^2}$ ✓, negative iff $(\gamma-a)(b-\gamma)>\delta^2$ ✓,
  but this is **not** "iff outside both cones": outside both cones ($|\gamma-a|,|b-\gamma|>\delta$) implies it, not conversely
  (e.g. $\gamma-a=2\delta$, $b-\gamma=0.6\delta$). Replace "iff" by "if".
