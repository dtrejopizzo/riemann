Status: **partial construction; Row D and O2601.1 remain open**. This phase gathers the construction developed in chat after reading paper 48. It does not modify the papers.

- [R163_001](R163_001_CANONICAL_EXCHANGE_AND_DEFECT.md): simultaneous exchange of quartets, generalized-prime current, and a faithful test defect.
- [R163_002](R163_002_ARITHMETIC_VANISHING_LAW.md): precise attempt at the arithmetic vanishing property, positive convolution lemma, countermodel, and a surviving target.

Central logical point: for the strict quotient `ζ/E`, the positivity of the generalized-prime measure **does not follow** from ζ having integer coefficients and an Euler product; in the non-trivial finite case, `R162_2901` proves it fails.
