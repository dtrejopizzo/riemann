# R161_051 — Terminal equivalence after the three-block audit

## Setting

Assume the imported first-contact reduction: if row D fails after the certified initial interval, there is a finite T and a nonzero parity vector F in the primitive first-contact radical N_T. Conversely, exclusion of every such nonzero radical, together with the imported nesting and cofinal results, proves D.

The statements below concern every F in every hypothetical N_T.

## Equivalent sufficient closure properties

Consider any one of the following universal equality-state properties:

1. the right compressed scattering equation P_R C_log g=-epsilon g;
2. the extremal correlation X_T(F)=0;
3. removability of W_epsilon^0 at every positive odd Gamma pole;
4. vanishing of the faithful pole energy D_T^pole(F);
5. physical co-Poisson range membership of the corrected product port;
6. active Euler-ideal preservation for all p<exp(2T);
7. coherent PC saturation of R161_037.

Each property implies F=0 by a theorem already proved in the cited phase-161 or phase-121 files. Hence, if any property is established for every first-contact radical vector, no nonzero first contact exists and D follows.

Conversely, if D is already known through the first-contact reduction, there are no nonzero first-contact radical vectors. Each universal equality-state property is then true vacuously (and true at F=0 for the displayed constructions). Therefore, relative to the imported reduction, proving any one of these properties on all hypothetical first-contact states has the full logical strength of closing D.

This does not say the formulations are equally useful analytically. It says that none becomes a weaker theorem merely by being called a range lemma, a residue cancellation, an exterior reciprocity, or a finite saturation law.

## Result of the resumed audit

The resumed construction established three additional facts:

1. the abstract PC countermodel does not prove independence from all physical hypotheses (R161_046);
2. the fixed Gamma residues live in the exterior and finite translations carry exact non-negligible collars (R161_047-R161_048);
3. the completed incoming theorem kills the central block, not the right exterior block, and central cancellation plus unitary scattering parity does not transfer between them (R161_049-R161_050).

Thus no unproved implication can be filled by the proposed algebraic replacement of P_0 with P_R. The remaining possible proof must establish one of the physical arithmetic properties above load-bearingly. The current corpus contains its consequences and several exact coordinate descriptions, but no derivation of the property from the first-contact equation.
