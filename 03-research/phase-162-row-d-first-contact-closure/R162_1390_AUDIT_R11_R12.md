## R162_1202

Notation clash, applies throughout: 1202 writes "P = L²(I_{T_*}) (parity sector)" and "P_T(F*F)" for compression to the
window, while Paper 46's 𝒫_T is the primitive subspace. H ≥ 0 and HF = 0 hold on 𝒫_T only. Every compression in
1202 must be Π∘1_I (restrict, then project onto 𝒫_T). With plain restriction, Thm A(d) and Thm D are wrong as written.

**Thm A. CORRECT WITH FIXES.**
- (a) Correct (HF = 0, real symmetric).
- (b) The Taylor bookkeeping is correct: odd coefficients are sums of B(h_i,h_j) with i + j odd, free of F. The label
  "every odd-order variation of λ at F vanishes identically" is FALSE. Counterexample: F_ε = F + εh_1 + ε²h_2 gives
  Q = ε²Q(h_1) + 2ε³B(h_1,h_2) + ε⁴Q(h_2), and the ε³ term is generally nonzero. Correct label: "odd-order terms contain
  no F", plus "vanishing along great circles/odd curves". "Bounded through the even-order terms Q(h_i)": the Q(h_i) are not
  the even Taylor coefficients. Rephrase as a Cauchy–Schwarz bound by Q(h_i)^{1/2}Q(h_j)^{1/2}.
- (c) Correct: Rayleigh = sin²ε·Q(G), which is even in ε. The nullity on the unit sphere at F is dim N − 1, not dim N;
  the radial direction is not tangent.
- (d) Correct only with Π: F + εΠ(1_I(F*F)).

**Thm B. CORRECT.** (a) −F ∈ N. (b) is a correct reduction; the tensor-lift citation was not re-verified here.

**Thm C. CORRECT WITH FIXES.** Order-ε expansion checked: D_f⟨HG,𝔅(G)⟩|_F = ⟨Hf,𝔅(F)⟩ + ⟨HF,D𝔅·f⟩ =
⟨f,H𝔅(F)⟩, which gives q(F)e_m = H𝔅(F). Fixes:
(1) 𝔅(F) must lie in the form domain, and H is read as the form operator V → V*. State it.
(2) The conclusion is e_m ∈ ran H. That is stronger than the Sonin statement (4.2), e_m ∈ closure(ran H) (equivalent to
Gate 1 on N). ran H need not be closed. So the correct reading is: a polynomial route needs e_m ∈ ran H, which is at
least as hard as (4.2). This strengthens the NO-GO.
(3) The hypothesis dim N = 1 is not used.
(4) ℓ_m must be linear. If the "Q_+ history term" makes it affine, redo the ε⁰ step.

**Thm D. CORRECT WITH FIXES.** φ = c_F*F = F̃*(F*F) is the cross-correlation of F*F and F, so the linear Weil
functional at φ is the bilinear form W(F, F*F). φ̂ = F̂²·conj F̂. Checked. But HF = 0 on 𝒫_T gives A_TF = r =
αe^{t/2} + βe^{−t/2} on I, not 0. So W(F, 1_I(F*F)) = 𝔞(F, 1_I(F*F)) = ⟨r, 1_I(F*F)⟩; the Tate cross term vanishes because
A(F) = B(F) = 0. For odd F, r ∝ sinh(t/2) and F*F is even, so this term is 0 and the memo's identity holds. For even F,
r = α cosh(t/2), and ⟨cosh(t/2), 1_I(F*F)⟩ ≠ 0 in general. Correct identity:
W[φ] = α⟨cosh(t/2), 1_I(F*F)⟩ + E(F,(F*F)_out) (even F). This term is also cubic and odd in F (α is linear in F), so
"odd, no sign" survives.

**Prop E. CORRECT.** K_{F̃}(z) = K_F(−z) = εK_F(z). The residue is ∝ K_F(a_m)^{j+k}, which vanishes iff K_F(a_m) = 0.
The phrase "K_{c_F}(z)^{…}" is garbled; write K_φ(z)ζ(s)^{−1}N_s.

**Prop H. CORRECT WITH FIXES.** Σ_dΛ(d)w(d) = log 2 log 3 − log 3 log 2 = 0. Checked. The inversion
ĝ(k) = ½Σ_jμ(j)u(kj) with u supported on {2,3} gives ĝ(1) = −½(u_2+u_3), ĝ(2) = ½u_2, ĝ(3) = ½u_3, and u_g(1) = 0. Checked.

Direct check: 2[log 2·ĝ(2) + log 3·ĝ(3)] = 0. The correction to R162_1101 §7 is endorsed; it agrees with Fix 1 above.
