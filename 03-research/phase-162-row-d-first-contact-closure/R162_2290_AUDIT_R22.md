## R162_2201

**Lemma N0.** The PW step is correct: entire, type ≤ T′, L² on ℝ ⇒ PW_{T′}; the pairing with |Ĝ|² sees the
autocorrelation on [−2T′,2T′], hence Λ(n) only for log n < 2T′, which is N_{T′} of `eq:symbol`. The Λχ₂ numbers check:
T_c ≤ 0.38 gives X = e^{0.76} = 2.138 < 3, and contact n=2 needs T_c > ½log 2 = 0.3466. The truncated cocycle at p=2,
k=1 reads Φ̂(2)−Φ̂(1) = χ(2)log 2 − 0 = −log 2 ≠ log 2. Correct for χ(2)=−1.
*F1.* The infinite-quotient clause is loose. (i) The product Π_{x∈S}(z−x) needs Hadamard convergence factors
(1−z/x)e^{z/x}. (ii) "Zero density T_c/π − dens S" is not what Cartwright–Levinson gives for arbitrary S. The claim
needed is only that the quotient is Cartwright class with indicator ≤ T_c|sin θ|, hence PW_{T_c} if L². State it
that way. The S=𝒳 case (Ce^{bz}z^m) is correct.

**Thm N1(a).** The Möbius formula is off by a factor 2. With ν̂_d(k)=1_{d|k}, Φ̂_a(k)=(a∗1)(|k|) for k≠0 and
Φ̂_a(0)=0. The two-sided sum gives
⟨Φ_a,g⟩ = Σ_{k≠0}(a∗1)(|k|)·½Σ_jμ(j)u(|k|j) = Σ_{n≥1}a(n)u(n) = 2Σ a(n)n^{−s}Σ_xq_xn^{−ix} = 2𝒜_a.
*F2.* Take u(n) := n^{−s}Σ_xq_xn^{−ix} (drop the 2), or drop the ½. Also say that the pairing representation holds for
Re s > 1 only. At s=½, 𝒜 is the meromorphic continuation, not a pairing.

**Thm N1(b).** a=Λ ⟹ 𝒜_a = Σq_x(−ζ′/ζ)(s+ix) is correct (the x-sum is finite). *F3.* The converse is false for a
fixed G. Uniqueness of Dirichlet coefficients gives only a(n)U(n)=Λ(n)U(n) with U(n)=Σ_xq_x cos(x log n), and U(n)
can vanish. Write ⟹, or quantify over all admissible (S,q).

**Thm N1(c).** Verified. From ζ(½+it)=e^{−iθ(t)}Z(t), differentiation gives iζ′/ζ = −iθ′+Z′/Z, so
−ζ′/ζ(½+ix) = θ′(x)+iZ′(x)/Z(x). Z is even and θ is odd, so Z′/Z is odd and θ′ is even. q_{−x}=q_x holds because:
𝒳=−𝒳; d_{−x}=d_x by ι; |F̂′(−x)|=|F̂′(x)|; and Ĝ(−x)=conj Ĝ(x) for real G. So the Z′/Z part cancels. The
hypothesis "no node is an ordinate of an on-line zero" is needed and is stated. Off-line zeros do not affect Z's
reality. *F4 (wording).* "We may assume S closed under x↦−x" is not an assumption. Reality of G forces it:
c_x≠0 ⇒ Ĝ(−x)=conj Ĝ(x)≠0 ⇒ c_{−x}≠0. Also say plainly that the cancellation is just the statement that 𝒜_Λ(½;G)
is real.

**Thm N1(d).** Correct. Only real nodes and q_{−x}=q_x are used.

**Prop N2.** The quadrature on the span is Thm G(d). The extension to 𝒟_{T_c} under OS-3 is fine, because Σw_x|Ĝ(x)|²
is dominated by W on the span and point evaluation is PW-continuous. *F5.* Two points. (i) Uniqueness of the positive

**Thm N3.** *F6.* As stated ("every element of 𝔖 is one of α, β, γ", with 𝔖 = all consequences) this is not a
theorem. The proof classifies three constructions (band-limited, the canonical separable kernel, μ_F). It does not
classify all consequences. Two gaps: "non-band-limited ⇒ separable" is not proved (N0 says F-built objects of
exponential type are PW; it does not say every non-PW object is separable), and "the canonical separable kernel" is
one choice, not all separable kernels. Restate it as: "the three canonical node-built pairings yield only (α), (β),
(γ); none is contradictory under ¬RH, and each has a Λχ₂ analogue". Downgrade to PROPOSITION (NO-GO for these routes).
The Λχ₂ analogue itself holds. For real χ, L(½−it,χ) = conj L(½+it,χ), so Z_χ is real and even and θ_χ is odd; N1(c)
transfers.

**Λχ₂ twisted cocycle.** With Φ̂^χ(k) := χ(k)log|k| one gets

*F7.* Φ^χ is not Φ_{Λχ₂} of N0. It is the χ-twisted torsion distribution Σ_dΛ(d)χ(d)(χ-twisted ν_d), and

## R162_2202

**Thm NI(a).** Verified. K = F̂/(z−x)² − F̂′(x)/(z−x) is entire: the singularity at x is removable, since F̂(x)=0
and x is simple. K has type ≤ T_c and is O(|F̂|/τ²+1/τ) on ℝ, so K ∈ PW_{T_c} with ∫log(2+|τ|)|K|²<∞. The polar
evaluations are finite, so K ∈ 𝒟. Then (p−x)Ĥ_x^# = F̂^# (x real) gives W(H_x)=F̂′(x)L(x)+W(K,F), and
W(K,F)=conj W(F,K)=0. Absolute convergence of L(x) follows by Cauchy–Schwarz as stated. For real even F, F̂′(x) is real,
so w_x = d_x/F̂′(x)² = L(x)/F̂′(x), consistent with Thm G(d).

**Thm NI(b).** Verified for even F. (z−x)Ĥ=F̂ ⟺ iH′−xH=F. Since F̂(x)=0,
H_x(t) = −ie^{−ixt}∫_{−T_c}^tFe^{ixs} = ie^{−ixt}∫_t^{T_c}Fe^{ixs}. For even F and u ≥ 0,
H_x(u)+H_x(−u) = i∫_u^{T_c}F(s)[e^{ix(s−u)}−e^{−ix(s−u)}]ds = −2k_F(x,u). Also
(1/2π)∫Ĥ_x cos(τu)dτ = ½(H_x(u)+H_x(−u)). So the comb term −v_n·(−k_F) = +v_nk_F. Polar term:
Ĥ^#(−i/2)+Ĥ^#(i/2) = B/(−i/2−x)+A/(i/2−x) = −[A/(x−i/2)+B/(x+i/2)], which is −2Ax/(x²+¼) at A=B; evenness gives A=B.
**"Only n < e^{T_c}" is CORRECT.** supp H_x ⊂ [−T_c,T_c] and L is linear in Ĥ_x, so k_F(x,log n)=0 for log n ≥ T_c.
The contacts in [T_c,2T_c) enter W only quadratically. This is consistent with N0 of 2201 (quadratic, up to e^{2T_c}).
*F8.* "The odd case only changes signs" is not accurate. For real odd F, F̂ is imaginary on ℝ, A=−B, the kernel
becomes ∫_u^{T_c}F(s)cos(x(s−u))ds, and the polar term is −iA/(x²+¼)·(…). Either write the odd case out or restrict NI
to even F.

**Thm NI(c).** The split F̂(p)/(p−y) = F̂(y)/(p−y)+H⁰_y with H⁰_y∈𝒟 and W(H⁰_y,F)=0 is correct, and Ψ(x)=0 is exactly
G(e): the polar part of Ψ(x) is −2ABx/(x²+¼). *F9.* L(y) has poles at y=±i/2 from the point masses. State it for
y ∉ ℝ∪{±i/2}.

**Cor NI.1.** The sign bookkeeping is correct: for adjacent simple zeros F̂′(x)F̂′(x′)<0, so L(x)L(x′)<0 when
w_xw_{x′}>0. The count ≤ 2(dim E_c−1) follows from Thm G(c). *F10.* The intermediate-value step needs L continuous on
[x,x′], but L at real non-nodes is not absolutely convergent (F̂(y)≠0). Define L there as the principal-value Hilbert
transform of σF̂ plus the polar terms, and note that it is continuous because σF̂ is real-analytic and decays. The E1
reading (Hermite–Biehler) is correct.

**Prop T.** Commutation of multipliers is correct, and so is the node-set invariance when D has no real zero. *F11.*
(i) "Isometrically" is correct only for the form (with ν_D), not for L², since |D|≠1; say "form-preserving".
(ii) The support is shifted to [−T, T+log N], so it lies in PW_{T+log N} after recentring. That is fine, but say so.
(iii) "For ζ the choice D = partial sums of Z is allowed" is false in general: 1+2^{iz} vanishes at z = π(2k+1)/log 2
∈ ℝ. Restrict to those N (or shifted sums) with no real zero. Also require D(±i/2)D^#(±i/2)≠0 for the polar rescaling.
The conclusion "ascent is transport of structure" is unaffected.

**Thm ND.** Main step correct: the zeros of Φ are zeros of F̂, and Theorem R(a) gives finitely many non-real zeros.
Bohr/Rouché verified. Exponential polynomials are uniformly almost periodic in Re z on compact horizontal strips. Take
ε < min_{∂B′}|D| with D zero-free on ∂B′ and a zero inside. For τ in a relatively dense set, |D(·+τ)−D|<ε on ∂B′, so
D has a zero in B′+τ, which is off ℝ. That gives infinitely many. The Euler-factor zeros check:
Im z = (log|a|−½log p)/log p, which is ≠0 iff |a|≠√p. The condition b₁≠0 is not needed. *F12.* Theorem R is stated for
**admissible and ι-invariant** data. ND says "any admissible window datum"; add ι-invariance, or cite a version of R
without it. The Reading's claim that the balanced factors 1+e^{iθ}n^{iz} are "compatible with type T_c and density
T_c/π for every datum" is unproved (only non-exclusion is shown). Mark it HEURISTIC. It is recorded as O2202.3 anyway.
