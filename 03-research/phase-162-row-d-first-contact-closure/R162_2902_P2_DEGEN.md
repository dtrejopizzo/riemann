## 1. The reflection lemma (all T ≤ log 2)

**Lemma 1 (PROVED).** Let 0 < T ≤ log 2, so that log n ≥ T for every n ∈ N_T. Put I_n = (log n − T, T) ⊂ [0,T)
and let R_n be the reflection s ↦ log n − s of I_n, extended by 0 to L²(0,T). For even F write f = F|_{(0,T)}
(‖F‖² = 2‖f‖²). For odd F write f the same way. Then
  c_F(log n) = +⟨f, R_n f⟩ (F even), c_F(log n) = −⟨f, R_n f⟩ (F odd),
and the cross terms between parities vanish.

*Proof.* c_F(u) = ∫_{u−T}^{T} F(t)F(t−u)dt. For u ≥ T, t ∈ (u−T,T) ⊂ [0,T) and t−u ∈ (−T, T−u) ⊂ (−T,0]. By parity,
F(t−u) = ±f(u−t), with u − t ∈ I_n. The symmetrised shift ½(τ_u+τ_u^*) commutes with F(t) ↦ F(−t), so the
mixed-parity terms cancel. ∎

**Corollary 1 (PROVED).** For T ≤ log 2, |c_F(log n)| ≤ ½‖F‖² for every F ∈ L²(−T,T) and every n ∈ N_T. The
bound is sharp (take F even with f R_n-symmetric on I_n). Direct proof: the two intervals in c_F are disjoint,
so Cauchy–Schwarz plus AM–GM applies. Consequence: ‖Π_T‖ ≤ ½ A_T (1.1244 at log 2), halving the proved bound
‖Π_T‖ ≤ A_T of R155_026 (F2).

This is the operator form of R162_2401 Prop P2 (H(F^ev) = −H(F^odd)): the prime form is −Σ v_n C_n with
C_n ≅ +½R_n on the even sector and −½R_n on the odd sector (normalising φ = √2 f, ‖φ‖ = ‖F‖).

## 2. P2: the structural identity at log 2 — PROVED

**Theorem 2 (orbit decomposition).** For T ≤ log 2, the operator B = Σ_{n∈N_T} v_n R_n on L²(0,T) is unitarily
equivalent to a direct integral of weighted path-graph adjacency matrices with at most 4 vertices.

*Proof.* The orbit of s under the partial involutions R_2, R_3 (N_T ⊂ {2,3} for T ≤ log 2, since
log 4 = 2log 2 ≥ 2T) is a chain whose edges alternate R_2, R_3. R_3R_2 is translation by +log(3/2) where it is
defined, and R_2R_3 is its inverse. A 5-vertex chain would contain two points at distance 2log(3/2) = 0.811 > T
inside (0,T). That is impossible. Orbits are parametrised measurably by a fundamental domain, which gives the
direct integral. ∎

**At T = log 2 exactly** (I_2 = (0, log 2), I_3 = (log 3/2, log 2), R_2 I_3 = (0, log 4/3)), put
M = (log 4/3, log 3/2) and A = (0, log 4/3). Then:
* on M (R_2-invariant, disjoint from I_3) the blocks are 2-point with B-matrix [[0,v_2],[v_2,0]];
* on A ∪ I_3 the blocks are 4-point chains x —R_2— log2−x —R_3— x+log(3/2) —R_2— log(4/3)−x,
  x ∈ (0, ½log(4/3)), with weights (v_2, v_3, v_2).

The path P_4 with weights (α,β,α) has eigenvalues ±(√(4α²+β²) ± β)/2. Hence, relative to ‖F‖²:

> **Theorem P2 (PROVED).** At T = log 2 the prime form Π_{log2} = −Σ v_n C_n splits as
> Π^even ≅ −½B, Π^odd ≅ +½B, and
>   spec(Π_{log2}) = {±v_2/2} ∪ {±(√(v_2² + v_3²/4) ± v_3/2)/2}
>   = {±0.4901291, ±0.9009276, ±0.2666435},
> pure point, each eigenvalue of infinite multiplicity, in both parity sectors. On P_T (codimension 1 in each
> sector) the extreme values are still attained, because the eigenspaces are infinite-dimensional. So
> sup_{F∈P_T} Σ v_n c_F(log n)/‖F‖² = (√(v_2²+v_3²/4)+v_3/2)/2 = **0.9009276162618…**.

**Falsifier line (passed).** R154_002 row 3d / `chk3.py` measured the joint truncated contact maximum
numerically as 0.9009276162618641 (K = 200, on P_T and unconstrained). The closed form gives

**Consequence for S_even.** S_even = Schur complement, with respect to F*, of
A_∞^even − ½(v_2R_2 + v_3R_3) on the even part of P_{log2}. The prime block is now completely explicit: it is a
finite-matrix field over the orbit space. The 58 % cancellation between n = 2 and n = 3 in R155_032 is the
P_4 interference between the α and β edges on A ∪ I_3. It is absent on M, where only v_2 acts. What remains
non-explicit is A_∞^even, a convolution form with a log-singular symbol. It is not diagonal in the orbit
decomposition, so S_even has no closed spectrum. "P2 = identity for S_even" is therefore settled as: exact
for the prime part; no identity exists for the full S_even beyond the archimedean spectrum itself (§4).

## 3. O3 / lem:degen — PARTIAL

Recall (R157_004 Lemma D): V(v) = inf_{F∈P_T,‖F‖=1} 𝔞^{(v)}_T(F) is concave on ℝ^{N_T}, and V(v) ≤ V(v⁰) − Σ(v_n−v⁰_n)c_{F*}(log n).

**Proposition D1 (PROVED).** V is ½-Lipschitz in each coordinate (Cor. 1) and V(v) → −∞ as |v| → ∞ in every
direction. For every w ≠ 0 there is F ∈ P_T with Σ w_n c_F(log n) > 0. Take F even with f R_n-symmetric on a
subset of I_n where only one R_n acts (M-type or chain-end sets), then correct by a rank-2 perturbation to meet
the constraints. Hence argmax V is a nonempty compact convex set 𝒦_T. The superdifferential of V at v is
conv{−(c_F(log n))_n : F a minimiser}. So
  **degenerate (some minimiser has c_{F*} ≡ 0 on log N_T) ⟹ v_T(Λ) ∈ 𝒦_T,**
and ⟺ holds when the minimiser is unique up to sign.

**Proposition D2 (degenerate case is harmless for positivity; PROVED).** In the degenerate case,
λ^A_T = M_T := max_{v∈ℝ^{N_T}} V_T(v). M_T depends only on the archimedean form and on N_T. It depends on Λ
only through its support. In particular the degenerate case gives λ^A_T ≥ λ^{∞,P}_T, and ≥ V_T(v) for every
comb v. It can only make positivity easier. What it destroys is O3's *margin reading* (the one-weight
perturbation threshold ε_*), not any sign.

**Proposition D3 (reflection form of the condition; PROVED).** For T ≤ log 2, take the minimiser of pure
parity σ (possible because W splits). Degeneracy ⟺ ‖P_n^+φ*‖² = ‖P_n^−φ*‖² on I_n for each n ∈ N_T, where P_n^±
are the R_n-(anti)symmetric projections. This is one real equation per contact (two at log 2, for n = 2, 3).

**Exclusion where available (PROVED).** If λ^A_T < λ^{∞,P}_T, the degenerate case fails, because
degenerate ⟹ V(v⁰) ≥ V(0). This covers every window where "primes cost" is certified. It does **not** cover
log 2: there, primes are necessary (λ^A > λ^{∞,P}), and ruling the case out would need either a certified
V(v) > V(v⁰) for some explicit v, or a certified imbalance in D3. Both need a two-sided enclosure of the
complete-form minimiser, which the record does not have ("inf W at log 2 WITHDRAWN").
**Status: OPEN at log 2; its only content is Λ-blind (M_T).**

Falsifier line: D2 holds verbatim for every comb (Λ, deleted Λ, M_m, Λ̃, DH weights). The degenerate case
therefore cannot separate ζ from any falsifier.

## 4. P153.17 — no closed prime-free law (NO-GO), with the exact replacement

For v = 0, λ_min(M_0 − v·S | P_T) = λ^{∞,P}_T, the bottom of the archimedean form on P_T. It has no known closed
form (prolate-type; the record has only bounds: R157_004 Lemma E, R155_047 Thm P). Any closed law for the whole
v-family specialises to one for λ^{∞,P}_T. **NO-GO in this sense (reduction, not a transcendence proof).**
PROVED replacements for T ≤ ½log 3 (N_T = {2}, S = C_2):

* S ≅ ½(P_+ − P_−) ⊕ (−½)(P_+ − P_−) (even ⊕ odd), where P_± project onto R_2-(anti)symmetric functions on
  I_2 = (log2 − T, T). So spec S = {0, ±½}, and 0 is carried by (0, log2 − T).
* V(v) = min(V_e(v), V_o(v)) with V_e(v) = λ_min(A_e − (v/2)(P_+−P_−)) and V_o(v) = λ_min(A_o + (v/2)(P_+−P_−)).
  V is concave, ½-Lipschitz, and V(v) ≥ λ^{∞,P}_T − |v|/2 (sharp to first order).
* Danskin: V′(v±) = −c_{F*_v}(log 2) ∈ [−½, ½].
* Large coupling: V(v) = −|v|/2 + λ_min(A_∞ | P_T ∩ E_{sgn v}) + o(1), where E_+ = {even, f R_2-symmetric on
  I_2, 0 off I_2} ⊕ {odd, f antisymmetric on I_2}, and E_− is the complementary pair.
* For ½log3 < T ≤ log 2 the same holds with the ≤4-vertex path-block spectrum of Theorem 2 in place of
  {0, ±½}.
