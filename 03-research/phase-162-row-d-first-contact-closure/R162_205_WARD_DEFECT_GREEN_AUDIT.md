# R162_205 — Ward-defect insertion into the four-block Green identity


Status: **EXACT NO-GO FOR LITERAL GREEN SUBSTITUTION / GATE 1 OPEN.** The divisor defect identified
in R162_204 enters the finite Poisson--Chebyshev anomaly at the correct physical location. However,
literal insertion into the already constructed mixed Green or double-commutator pairings produces

is exactly the unresolved exterior correlation of R161_057--R161_060. No critical-jet square is

## 1. Exact interior defect operator

Let `a=Lambda+eta`, with finite-support `eta`, and use the notation of R161_001:

    L_M^a=sum_(d<=M)a(d)d^(-1/2)tau_(log d),
    H_(M,N)^a=L_M^a S_N-K_N.                                   (1.1)

For `M>=N`, put

    Delta_N^a
      =sum_(k<=N)b_a(k)k^(-1/2)tau_(log k),
    b_a(k)=sum_(d|k)a(d)-log k.                                 (1.2)

Then the interior compression of the hyperbola remainder is exactly

    1_(k<=N) H_(M,N)^a=Delta_N^a.                               (1.3)

For `a=Lambda`, `Delta_N^Lambda=0` for every `N`. For a nontrivial finite modification, (1.2)
contains the infinitely propagated divisor defect, truncated at `N`.

## 2. Difference of the two finite anomalies

Write `L_M^eta=L_M^a-L_M^Lambda` and

    D_(M,N)^eta=H_(M,N)^a-H_(M,N)^Lambda=L_M^eta S_N.            (2.1)

The exact `(PC)` identity for `a` minus that for `Lambda` is

    -S_N(L_M^eta+(L_M^eta)^*)Fcal
      =-(L_M^eta)^*E_N-JD_(M,N)^eta-D_(M,N)^eta Fcal.            (2.2)

*Proof.* Subtract R161_001 (3.1) for the two coefficient systems. Equation (2.1) follows directly
from (1.1), because `K_N` is coefficient-independent. This proves (2.2). Equivalently, expand
`E_N=S_NFcal-JS_N`, use `JL_M^eta=(L_M^eta)^*J`, and commute the finite translation sums. ∎

Thus the falsifier is not hidden in a boundary limit: its complete finite contribution is the
explicit vector on either side of (2.2), and its interior portion is (1.2).

## 3. What happens in the four-block Green pairing

Let `g_B^+` and `g_B^-` be the two finite tests generated by the causal and reflected compressed
histories in R161_060. Pairing (2.2) with them adds to the two-orientation Green expression the term

    D_Green^a(F)
      =Re<-(L_M^eta)^*E_N-JD_(M,N)^eta-D_(M,N)^eta Fcal,
             g_B^++g_B^->,                                    (3.1)

with the corresponding split if the two orientations are kept separate. Its interior arithmetic
piece is obtained by replacing `D_(M,N)^eta` by `Delta_N^a`.

Two facts are immediate and exact:

1. `D_Green^Lambda(F)=0` at every finite level;
2. under `eta -> -eta`, the entire expression (3.1) changes sign.

Consequently (3.1) is an oriented Ward term, not a nonnegative defect energy. Squaring it would make
it even in `eta`, discard the terminal orientation and return to the sign-blind construction excluded
in R161_002 and R161_099.

## 4. Evaluation at the zeta system

Set `a=Lambda`. The Ward term (3.1) vanishes, but the remaining finite Green identity is unchanged
from the literal substitution already audited in R161_060:

    Q_B(F)
      =Re<R_PC Fcal F,g_B^+>
       +Re<R_PC Fcal F,g_B^->
       +polar/Tate terms.                                      (4.1)

First-contact radicality removes the primitive interior pairings and the crossed Tate calculation
fixes the scalar endpoints. The right side of (4.1) nevertheless reconstructs the antisymmetric
exterior correlation; it does not vanish and does not equal the negative of the coherent-jet square.

Therefore the schematic balance R162_204 (4.2) is **not** obtained by:

- substituting `(PC-ren)` into the existing four-block Green identity;
- subtracting the same identity for a finite modification;
- polarizing the resulting linear Ward defect;
- or squaring the coefficient defect after orientation has been lost.

## 5. What a new construction must change

The algebra leaves only two ways to obtain new information from the same finite data:

1. introduce a second, genuinely independent parameter before setting `a=Lambda`, so that a mixed
   derivative can pair the odd Ward defect with an independently oriented physical variation; or
2. construct a finite conservation law whose boundary value is already the coherent difference
   `K_N^ren Fcal-JK_N^ren`, instead of deriving that difference by differentiating the complementary

The first alternative cannot use arbitrary coefficient perturbations: positivity is not known and is false for
the modification family. The second must overcome the precise orientation obstruction of R161_041. In either
case, merely reusing the Green pairing of R161_057 is exhausted by (4.1).
