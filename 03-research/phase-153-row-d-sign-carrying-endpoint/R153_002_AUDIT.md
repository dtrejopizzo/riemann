## 2. Details that matter

**2.1 The one number that decides everything (#9).** The construction's whole "`a* → 0`, the endpoint is null" narrative
rests on `λ_min(A_{\log2}|P_T)` appearing to fall to `0` like `h^{1.7}`. In a spectral basis the same quantity is

```
  K = 40 / 80 / 160 / 240   ->   2A_T value 2.330 / 2.227 / 2.135 / 2.088 e-7
  i.e.  λ_min(A_{log2}|P_T) ≈ 1.08e-7   (stable to 2% over a 6-fold basis increase)
```
and the mesh-free `b`-scan independently returns the same `1.084·10⁻⁷` at `b=\log3`. Piecewise constants at 512
cells give `2.08·10⁻⁴`: a factor `2000`. Every conclusion the construction drew from "no positive plateau" is therefore
void, and — the positive by-product — the corpus's certified margin `8.63386·10⁻⁸` is not a loose enclosure but is
within 25% of the true eigenvalue. The certificate is nearly sharp.

**2.2 What survives, and is confirmed twice (#7, #10, #13).** Built from Prop. 12.6's data and nothing else
(symbol `Σ_±\mathrm{Re}ψ(¼±a/2+iτ/2)−2\logπ`, weights `Λ(n)(n^a+n^{−a})/√n`, four moment conditions), the
minimum of the `Ξ_a` form over `P_T^{(a)}` at `T=\log2` is `−5.07·10⁻²` at `a=0.45` — agreeing with the construction's
conjugation assembly to 2%. This simultaneously (i) validates Theorem A numerically and (ii) confirms the sign.
At `T=½\log3` both assemblies give `+0.79`. So **D.12-α is answered affirmatively in substance**; the label `OPEN`
pending one interval certificate is the correct label and the target (`|λ| ≈ 5·10⁻²`) is comfortable.

**2.3 The mechanism is not what the report says (#5, #6, #11).** `a*(\log2) = 0.0800`, converged; below it the form
is positive (`+1.24·10⁻³` for all `a ≤ 0.05`), so the `10⁻⁷` near-null direction is *destroyed* by the two extra
moment conditions and is irrelevant. What produces the loss is the archimedean block at low frequency: (A3) has the
inequality **backwards**, `Σ_±\mathrm{Re}ψ(¼±a/2)` diverges to `−∞` as `a→½` (at `τ=0`, `−8.45` at `a=0`, `−12.99`
at `a=0.3`, `−42.63` at `a=0.45`), i.e. the `Ξ_a` archimedean channel is heavily *adverse* near `τ=0` and the
weight growth `n^a+n^{−a}` only adds to it. Consistently, the negativity has nothing to do with the endpoint:
`T*(a=0.45) = 0.6240`, strictly between `½\log3` and `\log2`.

**2.4 Q3(iii).** See #17. The correct statement the construction could have made — and which I verified — is:
*the tail inequality `Σ_{j≥J}\mathcal E_{b_j} ≥ (B/4)\mathcal E_B` is equivalent to a `T`-free pointwise
inequality between Fourier multipliers, and that inequality is true with ≤0.7% to spare*. It does **not** follow
that the `log 2` capacity step is `T`-uniform, because (2.3)–(2.4) of `114_d_77` evaluate `d_{B,e}` from Robin
eigenvalues of `I_T` under `⟨F,\cosh(t/2)⟩=0`.

## 4. Honest one-paragraph reading of phase 153

Stripped of its over-claims, phase 153 has done one genuinely new and one genuinely useful thing. The new thing is
Theorem A and its corollary (A1): the `Ξ_a` Weil form is not a sum of two `ξ` forms but their cross term
`Σ_ρ\hat G_+(ρ)\hat G_-(ρ)`, which is why no transport of `A_T ≥ 0` can prove `A_T^{(a)} ≥ 0` — elementary, correct,
and absent from the corpus. The useful thing is that, in two independent discretisations, the `Ξ_a` form is
strictly negative on the four-moment primitive space for `a ≳ 0.08` at `T = \log 2` while `ξ`'s form is certified
non-negative there: **D.12-α is answered in substance** (not yet certified), and the corpus does hold an inequality
that a function with off-line zeros violates at finite `T` — something Connes–Consani's archimedean window does not
provide. Everything else in the report is wrong or unsupported: the `a*→0` extrapolation, the claim of negativity
for every `a`, the near-nullity mechanism, the archimedean convexity lemma, the `v₁` diagnostics, and the
`T`-uniformity of the certificate's capacity step. The phase's most solid quantitative finding is one the report
does not contain: the true `λ_min(A_{\log2}|\mathcal P_{\log2}) ≈ 1.1·10⁻⁷`, so the corpus's certified margin
`8.63·10⁻⁸` is near-sharp, the `\log2` endpoint is critical to four significant figures in the position of the
second contact, and the property that carries sign is not the criticality of the endpoint — it already holds at
`T ≈ 0.624`.
