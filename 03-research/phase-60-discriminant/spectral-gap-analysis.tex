\begin{abstract}
When attempting to bound the combined tail $\mathcal W_\lambda$ by exploiting the
spectral gap $[\lambda,\lambda^2]$ (Prop.\ 4.2 of \texttt{weil-remainder-attack}),
I ran into a fact \emph{already proved} by the program: the \textbf{exact
restriction} $A_\lambda=A_\infty|_{\El}$. Its consequence is that the tail of
primes $>\lambda^2$ contributes \emph{exactly zero} to the form on band-limited
functions; hence the target inequality is \textbf{vacuous} on $\El$
(\S\ref{sec:vacuo}). This \RETRACT{}s Prop.\ 4.2 and relocates the front: the floor
of Link 2 is not a symbol tail (invisible to the form) but the \textbf{subspace
exhaustion} error of the Dirac operator $D_{\log}$, governed by approximation in
the de Branges space $H(\El)$ (\S\ref{sec:real}). The circularity obstacle
survives, but in its known classical form: the persistence of Weil positivity
$A_\infty\ge0$, equivalent to $\varepsilon_0(\lambda)$ not sinking. The only
non-circular residue is a PNT lower bound on $\varepsilon_0(\lambda)$.
\end{abstract}

\section{The fact that changes everything: exact restriction}\label{sec:vacuo}
% =====================================================================

Let $\El=\PW_{L/2}$, $L=2\log\lambda$, be the space of band-limited functions of
type $L/2$. The truncated Weil form admits the Fourier reduction \PR
\[
  A_\lambda(g,g)=\frac1{2\pi}\int_\R|\widehat g(t)|^2\,W_\lambda(t)\,dt,
  \qquad
  W_\lambda(t)=2\theta'(t)-2\Real\!\Bigl[\sum_{n\le\lambda^2}\tfrac{\Lam(n)}{n^{1/2+it}}\Bigr].
\]

\begin{lemma}[Exact restriction, \PR\ fact of the program]\label{lem:exact}
For every $g\in\El$ and every prime (power) $n>\lambda^2$, the contribution of
term $n$ to $A_\lambda(g,g)$ is \emph{exactly zero}. Consequently
\[
  A_\lambda(g,g)=A_\infty(g,g)\qquad\text{for all }g\in\El,
\]
i.e.\ $A_\lambda=A_\infty|_{\El}$: truncation to primes $\le\lambda^2$ \emph{is not
an approximation}, it is the exact restriction of the full form to the subspace.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}[Reason]
$\int|\widehat g(t)|^2\cos(t\log n)\,dt$ is the autocorrelation $R_g(\log n)$, and
for $g$ of type $L/2$ the support of $R_g$ is $[-L,L]=[-2\log\lambda,2\log\lambda]$.
Since $\log n>\log\lambda^2=L$ for $n>\lambda^2$, we have $R_g(\log n)=0$. \qed
\end{proof}

\begin{proposition}[The target inequality is vacuous \RETRACT]\label{prop:vacuo}
The quantity from Prop.\ 4.2 of \texttt{weil-remainder-attack},
\[
  \frac1{2\pi}\int|\widehat g_n(t)|^2\,\mathcal W_\lambda(t)\,dt
  \;=\;\frac1{2\pi}\int|\widehat g_n(t)|^2\,\bigl(W_\infty-W_\lambda\bigr)\,dt,
\]
is \textbf{identically zero} for $g_n\in\El$, by Lemma \ref{lem:exact}. Hence it
does not capture the floor, and the ``spectral gap $[\lambda,\lambda^2]$ as the
room to fight the tail'' \textbf{does not go through}: there is no tail to fight ---
the tail is invisible to the form.
\end{proposition}

\begin{remark}[Where the error came from]
\texttt{weil-remainder-attack} conflated two different operators: it bounded the
\emph{shift of zeros} (spectrum of the Dirac $D_{\log}$) by a \emph{tail of the
form} $\mathcal W_\lambda$. But by exact restriction that tail is $0$ on $\El$. The
circularity theorem (remainder $R_\lambda$ RH-equivalent) remains \emph{true} as a
statement about the \emph{symbol truncation}
$-\zeta'/\zeta\mapsto\sum_{n\le\lambda^2}$, but \textbf{that route is not the one
the model executes}: the model restricts the subspace, it does not truncate the
symbol. The wall $R_\lambda$ describes a path CCM does not take.
\end{remark}

\section{The real front: subspace exhaustion}\label{sec:real}
% =====================================================================

There are \emph{two} objects, and they should not be confused:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.5em]
\item the \textbf{form} $A_\lambda=A_\infty|_{\El}$ (positivity $\Rightarrow$
Link 1);
\item the \textbf{Dirac} $D_{\log}^{(\lambda)}$, whose spectrum is the zeros of
$\xih_\lambda$ (Link 2).
\end{itemize}

\begin{proposition}[Correct reformulation of Link 2]\label{prop:real}
Since $A_\lambda=A_\infty|_{\El}$ and $\El\uparrow$ (dense union), the convergence
$\xih_\lambda\to\Xi$ is the \textbf{subspace exhaustion} of the fixed form
$A_\infty$: the spectrum of the restricted Dirac converges to that of $A_\infty$.
The floor $\mathrm{floor}_\lambda(n)$ is the error of approximating the zero
$\gamma_n$ by the best band-limited element of $H(\El)$ (de Branges space),
governed by the rate $e^{-c\lambda^2}$ measured numerically (band layer).
\end{proposition}

\noindent This \emph{explains} the numerical finding that was so hard to
interpret: the entry of primes produced no jump because \textbf{there is no
symbol truncation} --- only the subspace grows. It was all band, as the data
showed.

\subsection{Where circularity reappears (classical form)}

The exhaustion $A_\infty|_{\El}\to A_\infty$ would converge for free (monotone
convergence of forms) \emph{if} $A_\infty$ were a semi-bounded form $\ge0$. But
\[
  A_\infty(g,g)\ge0\ \ \forall g\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad\text{RH}
  \qquad(\text{Weil positivity criterion}).
\]
This is the circularity, now in its genuine form: the limit is positive \emph{iff}
RH. What the program has \PR\ is the \emph{finite} positivity
\[
  A_\lambda(g,g)\;\ge\;\varepsilon_0(\lambda)\,\|g\|^2\;>\;0\qquad\text{on }\El,
\]
with $\varepsilon_0(\lambda)\sim C_0/\lambda^2\to0$. The RH question is whether the
margin \textbf{sinks to zero from above} (RH: $A_\infty\ge0$) or \textbf{crosses
into negative} (an off-line zero, once the band widens enough to resolve it).

\begin{theorem}[Clean, RH-equivalent reformulation]\label{thm:clean}
RH $\Longleftrightarrow$ the proved finite margins $\varepsilon_0(\lambda)>0$
remain $\ge0$ as $\lambda\to\infty$ (no eigenvalue of $A_\infty|_{\El}$ crosses
into negative). Equivalently, RH $\Longleftrightarrow$ no negative eigenvalue of
$A_\infty$ appears in any finite band.
\end{theorem}

\section{The only non-circular residue}\label{sec:residue}
% =====================================================================

Theorem \ref{thm:clean} is RH-equivalent and therefore is not, in itself, a
bridge. The only non-circular opening identified is:

\begin{quote}\itshape
\GAP\ Prove a lower bound $\varepsilon_0(\lambda)\ge c/\lambda^2$ (or any
$\varepsilon_0(\lambda)>0$ that does not vanish prematurely) using \emph{only} the
prime side --- moments $M_0,M_2$, kinetic coefficient $c=-\psi''(\tfrac14)/8$,
prime distribution (PNT/classical zero-free regions) --- without the position of
the zeros.
\end{quote}

\noindent This is Objective B with its already-known \GAP\ (the de Branges
extremal asymptotics of the canonical product truncated at critical density). The
central question:
\[
  \textbf{does the lower bound of }\varepsilon_0\textbf{ follow from DENSITY (PNT)
  or does it need POSITION (RH)?}
\]
If density suffices $\Rightarrow$ non-circular bridge. If it needs position
$\Rightarrow$ circular. The singular terms near the horizon $T^*\sim2\pi\lambda^2$
(spacing $\sim2\pi/L$) are exactly where this is decided --- and where de
Branges/Connes should look.

\section{Verdict of this block}\label{sec:verdict}
% =====================================================================

\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.6em]
\item \RETRACT\ The attack ``spectral gap $[\lambda,\lambda^2]$ against the tail''
\textbf{does not go through}: by exact restriction (Lemma \ref{lem:exact}) the
tail is invisible to the form and the target inequality is vacuous (Prop.\
\ref{prop:vacuo}). Self-correction of \texttt{weil-remainder-attack} Prop.\ 4.2.
\item \PR\ The circularity theorem for the remainder $R_\lambda$ remains valid,
but it describes the \emph{symbol truncation}, which is \emph{not} the model's
mechanism.
\item \PR\ (relocation) Link 2 is subspace exhaustion of $A_\infty$ (Prop.\
\ref{prop:real}); its RH content is the persistence of Weil positivity
$A_\infty\ge0$ (Theorem \ref{thm:clean}), unifying Objectives A (positivity) and B
(scale of $\varepsilon_0$).
\item \OP\ Only non-circular residue: PNT lower bound of $\varepsilon_0(\lambda)$
(\S\ref{sec:residue}). Legitimate front: density or position?
\end{enumerate}

\noindent\emph{Honesty:} this block is mostly \emph{corrective}. It did not
produce a bound; it produced (a)~the refutation of an attack that looked
promising but was vacuous due to an already-proved fact, and (b)~the relocation
of the front to the only real non-circular object: the lower bound of
$\varepsilon_0$ from prime density. It is less than we hoped for, but it prevents
wasting effort against a phantom tail.
