## 4. Autopsy

The exact commutator is real and useful, but the raw R1 inference is not
licensed.

First, RDP-1 is rank two.  Its null action contains every vector orthogonal
to the two generator moments `1` and `s`.  In dimension `2N+1`, that blind
subspace has dimension `2N-1`, so the blind fraction is

```text
(2N-1)/(2N+1) -> 1.
```

At N=18 this is already `35/37 = 0.945945...`.  A positive-commutator
argument needs coercivity on the spectral window being controlled.  A
rank-two skew displacement identity gives no such coercivity on a
growing-dimensional blind subspace.

Second, the identity is falsifier-neutral.  With the correct planted sine
symbol, the planted matrices satisfy the same rank-two displacement law to
`~1e-60`.  Therefore RDP-1 alone is not an arithmetic discriminator.  This
matches the Phase-76 warning: the planted falsifier may satisfy structured
stability; arithmetic identification must occur elsewhere.

Third, the zeta ground vector has rapidly vanishing generator moments in
this audit, while planted ground vectors keep visible generator mass.  That
is a useful diagnostic, but it is not a proof of absence of l2 point
spectrum: it is precisely a statement about how eigenvectors sit relative
to the two generators, and cannot be recovered from rank two alone.

## 5. Reduced Target

E77.2 does not close LP.  It reduces the R1 route to the following smaller
finite object:

```text
E77.LP-ENV / GEN:
prove that any normalized approximate kernel vector of the CCM mesh cannot
remain in the generator-blind subspace while keeping bounded canonical
energy S_N.  Equivalently, use the displacement recursion to force positive
block/parity lower-envelope growth of S_N.
```

This is strictly smaller than raw LP because it names the only missing
mechanism: escape from the codimension-two blind sector under repeated
displacement propagation.
