# 102 RDI to Li minimal bridge

## Required theorem

The alternative route can re-enter only through a literal bridge.  The
minimal acceptable theorem is:

If the RDI core holds with its true Euler--Gamma normalization and with all
limits taken in the declared order, then

[
  \lambda_n\ge0\qquad(n\ge1).
]

A second acceptable theorem is:

[
  \hbox{RDI core}
  \Longrightarrow
  \hbox{all zeros of }\Xi\hbox{ are real}.
]

The second theorem implies the first through the standard Li
sum-of-squares representation on the line.

## Missing normalizations

The current RDI material does not yet provide:

- a true-measure `BTG-DIV` statement;
- a `GAP-Z` proof including the signed ZERO term;
- a core identity that identifies the Euler--Gamma limit without choosing the
  answer in advance;
- a transfer from the resulting object to each Li coefficient.

## Minimal bridge formula

The most economical bridge would be a locally uniform convergence theorem

[
  \log\Theta_{L,N(L)}(-i\sigma)
  \longrightarrow
  2\log{\Xi(1/2+\sigma)\over\Xi(1/2+\sigma_0)}
  \qquad(\sigma>1/2),
]

with the divisor controlled by real-rooted approximants before the limit.
If this convergence is proved in a domain large enough for Hurwitz and the
normal family passage, then real zeros follow.

## Status

The bridge is not proved.  Carril B remains suspended as a closure route until
this theorem, or a direct `RDI => Li` coefficient formula, is supplied.
