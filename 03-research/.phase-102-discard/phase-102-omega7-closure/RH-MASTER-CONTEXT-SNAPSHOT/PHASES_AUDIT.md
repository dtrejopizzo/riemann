# Comprehensive audit of the phases

## Verdict

The phases do not form a sequence of more than a hundred cumulative closures. They form a search with
branches, corrections, and numerous changes of coordinate. There are proved, reusable results, but no
unconditional link from finite arithmetic to \(\Omega_7\).

The full trajectory can be compressed as follows:

\[
\begin{aligned}
&\text{localized Weil and operator}
\to \text{sign no-go}
\to \text{Hodge/index/geometry}\\
&\to \text{Connes/CCM}
\to \text{Feshbach--Cauchy--adjugate}
\to \text{LP+IDENT}\\
&\to \text{RDI-ANCHOR}
\to \text{bordered current/divisor}
\to \boxed{\text{open signed arithmetic identification}}.
\end{aligned}
\]

The final box did not get smaller by changing notation. The recent phases managed to identify which
parts are finite algebra, which are convergence, and which is the identification that must fail in the
presence of an off-line divisor. They also discovered that attributing all the force to a single
`DISCRIMINANT` is still not proved.

## Tree coverage and anomalies

The 101 `phase-*` directories were reviewed. Labels 1 and 2 do not exist; label 44 appears in two
directories. Both branches were preserved. File-by-file reading details are in the fragments linked at
the end.

## Phases 0–25: detector, walls and missing geometry

Phase 0 correctly identifies that the full Weil form is the natural object. Phase 3 builds the
instrumentation and already recognizes that a uniform inequality for the whole test class would be RH.
Phase 4 attempts a Kreĭn realization; the robust result is negative: faithfully representing a form
does not determine its sign. The coercivity supporting part of the realization depends on a uniform
density hypothesis and should not be cited as unconditional.

Phases 5–11 prove or try several ways to obtain sign: residual decomposition, reflection, Carleson,
heat, universality, Riesz frames and hyperbolicity. Each route ends in an equivalent positivity or a
bound with the wrong direction. Phases 12–14 open the probabilistic and \(\omega\) frontier; they
produce independent structure, but the absence of zeros is not deduced from typical statistics. The
attempt \(\omega\to\) zeros becomes self-referential via factors built with \(\zeta\).

Phases 15–18 formulate the SURF/Hodge frontier: a surface, cohomology and polarization could mimic the
proof in function fields. The corpus does not build those objects. Functional models or testbeds are
not equivalent to a geometry with correspondences and a Hodge index.

Phases 21–25 study a finite number of off-line defects. Index parity and several defect formulas are
useful conditional results, but everything depends on a finiteness Hypothesis D. RH's complement need
not satisfy it.

## Phases 26–50: average, Hodge and phase→inertia

Phase 26 does not achieve the necessary Kreĭn–Connes bridge. Phases 27–33 explore CCM, moment
problems, de Bruijn–Newman and deficit measures. The pattern is:

\[
\text{small average or convergent subsequence}
\not\Rightarrow
\text{absent atom or individual zero on the line}.
\]

Phases 39–45 formalize G1/G2, dynamic Hodge, foliated Hodge and Fredholm. The results within toys or
assumed categories are valid in their domain; applying them to \(\zeta\) needs precisely the missing
geometry or positivity. Phase 41 correctly points out that generic positivity does not supply the
particular Hodge index.

Phases 46–50 turn optimistic claims into explicit gaps. The rational transversality of \(\{\log p\}\)
acts on the phase torus, but does not transport that information to inertia. The no-go results are for
local/Tauberian and average classes; they do not rule out every possible non-local Diophantine theorem.

Phase 27 retains metadata inconsistencies. Label 44 has two distinct branches and both must be cited
by name, not only by number.

## Phases 51–75: equivalences and signed residue

Phases 51–59 prove that homotopy, Stone, variable metric and abstract index do not force the
arithmetic arrow. They deliver theorems within hypotheses and two conditional towers, not a closed
chain.

Phase 60 numerically proves that its localized discriminant does not detect multiplicativity: smooth
non-multiplicative controls outperform \(\zeta\). Phase 62 shows that Cesàro preserves growth of an
off-line defect. Phase 63 does not reproduce the Frobenius mechanism of finite fields.

Phases 64–71 make a decisive turn: every useful construction ends up requiring that approximants with
real zeros converge locally to \(\Xi\), or that the signature survive the limit. Hurwitz then does the
rest. That convergence is not a detail; it is an RH-strength condition. In phase 65, the file that
announces QED is superseded by corrections that leave `D8.5` open.

Phases 72–75 provide the corpus's richest algebraic infrastructure:

\[
\text{Feshbach leak}
\to \text{current}
\to \text{Cauchy interpolation}
\to \text{divisibility}
\to \text{bordered adjugate}.
\]

However, the terminal names are not four distinct lemmas:

\[
\texttt{PW-Cauchy}
\sim
\texttt{HPR-DIV}
\sim
\texttt{EG\_LOCK}
\sim
\texttt{ADJ-ARITH-LOCK}.
\]

All of them require the same signed arithmetic cancellation that is not obtained from absolute bounds,
smoothing, a single gap, two jets, or a pseudoinverse.

## Phases 76–79: the point where the spiral appeared

Phase 76 corrects the eigenvector's scale ambiguity via a normalized characteristic, builds calculus on
a safe axis, and separates

\[
\texttt{SAFE-LIMIT-POINT}=\texttt{LP}+\texttt{IDENT}.
\]

The finite identities and the Euler tail at \(\Re s>1\) survive. `LP`, `IDENT`, `RDP-SHELL`, PROLATE
and WEIL-TAIL remain open.

Phase 77 corrects LP's literal formulation. Since \(H_L=D_L+B_L\) has a compact resolvent, the lower
eigenvalue \(\mu_L\) has a nontrivial kernel. The target becomes energy divergence `BTG-DIV` and
contraction of the bordered disk. LP's neutrality against the planted control was proclaimed from
Outcome A, but the evidence uses finite sections and a \(\mu_{\rm ref}\), not the true limit in
\(\mu_L\). For this reason:

\[
\text{LP build-neutral}
\quad\text{is still}\quad
\textbf{CONDITIONAL},
\]

not a theorem.

Phase 78 obtains the exact decomposition

\[
g_{N+2}-g_N=\texttt{ZERO}+\texttt{MESH}+\texttt{BND},
\]

with

\[
\texttt{MESH}=O(N^{-2}),
\qquad
\texttt{BND}=O(N^{-3}).
\]

The `ZERO` term remains open. Bounding it by summing shell magnitudes is forbidden by the K3 audit
itself, because it erases the signed cancellation. This is `GAP-Z`.

Phase 79 surveys a large family of proxies: codimension-one closure, balance, cloud coherence,
rank-one escape, two-scale laws, and crossing number. These are numerical probes. Several correct one
another: E78.154 is retracted; the crossing number stops discriminating; a drift vanishes at higher
precision; and `mean(d)` silently changes definition. E79.6 was not proved as either necessary or
sufficient for IDENT.

The correct conclusion is not "GAP-Z is already neutral and DISCRIMINANT contains exactly all of RH",
but rather:

\[
\begin{cases}
\texttt{GAP-Z}:&\text{open signed convergence, designed to be neutral};\\
\texttt{DISCRIMINANT}:&\text{proposed arithmetic separator, numerical/conjectural};\\
\texttt{LP}:&\text{open; neutrality not certified at the true }\mu_L.
\end{cases}
\]

## Phases 80–89: three loops toward the same anchor

Phase 80 establishes an independent Euler–Gamma product on the safe half-plane and separates

\[
\texttt{RDI-CONV}
\qquad\text{from}\qquad
\texttt{RDI-ANCHOR}.
\]

Convergence and coherence do not identify the arithmetic limit. `RDI-ANCHOR` is the missing bridge.

The following phases form three loops:

\[
81\to82\to(\text{LP+tails}),
\]

because the homogeneous projective limit loses the source;

\[
83\to84\to85\to86\to\texttt{RDI-ANCHOR},
\]

because the distributional coboundary reduces to signed parity sums; and

\[
87\to88\to89\to\texttt{PROFILE-ROTATION-RDI}\to\texttt{RDI-ANCHOR},
\]

because the Feshbach layer cancels scales but does not identify the Euler profile.

Durable results: a secular form without dividing by a scalar that can vanish; Euler–Möbius
representation; the obstruction \(\ker X=\{0\}\) in \(L^2\); repair with \(\delta_0\); Weyl parity
defects; the Abel identity; Euler deformation; layer Feshbach and projective current. None proves the
anchor.

## Phases 90–101: atlas of a single load

Phases 90–93 turn Kato, coboundary and the bordered numerator into the implication

\[
\texttt{DIRECT-BORDERED-ANCHOR}
\Longrightarrow
\Omega_7.
\]

Phases 94–100 express the same object as cofactors, characteristic Jacobian, von Mangoldt response,
Euler commutator, Fourier shell, and chain rule. These identities are algebraic advances, not seven
closures. The adjugate's characteristic term does not vanish; it is the chain rule and must be
recombined.

Phase 101 builds an atlas of Stieltjes transforms, moments, heat, Abel and currents. Its final results
fix four limits:

1. `C_core` is the correct source after extracting the outer factor;
2. identifying its limit on a safe interval has RH-strength by Pick–Nevanlinna propagation;
3. a finite star or feature normality does not automatically transport \(\Xi\)'s divisor;
4. single-level holomorphic moments and trace products lose transversal support or linear
   multiplicity.

The recent names

\[
\texttt{LOCAL-COVARIANT-IDENT},
\texttt{STIELTJES-IDENT},
\texttt{XI-PARITY-CURRENT-NULL},
\texttt{TRUE-DIVISOR-IDENT}
\]

are versions of the same identification problem, the last one stronger than the minimal condition.

## What was actually closed

1. Many finite identities of localized Weil, Feshbach, Cauchy, adjugate, cofactors, Jacobians,
   commutators and Abel.
2. Several specific no-go results: zeros-only Gram, multiplicativity via the tried discriminant,
   Cesàro, clean GLT, singular pseudoinverse, cell-by-cell matching, single-level holomorphic
   inversion, and single-scale parity.
3. Important corrections: withdrawal of QED in phase 65; replacement of the trivial-kernel LP;
   raw/core separation; preservation of the characteristic term; distinction between convergence and
   identification.
4. A cartography of the missing piece far more precise than at the start.

## What was not closed

1. Global arithmetic positivity, \(\Omega_7\) or RH.
2. LP/BTG at the true \(\mu_L\).
3. `GAP-Z`, due to the signed `ZERO` term.
4. `RDI-ANCHOR` or any equivalent Euler–Gamma identification.
5. The directional tails and downstream bridges needed for the LP+IDENT route.
6. An external geometry with independent polarization.
7. A divisor convergence that preserves real support and multiplicity without assuming RH.

## Future accounting rule

A new document only counts as progress if it does at least one of these things:

- proves an open obligation with strictly earlier hypotheses;
- refutes the obligation via a counterexample;
- reduces the number of obligations without substituting one for an equivalent;
- proves that a piece is neutral in a class that contains an off-line control;
- creates an independent signed arithmetic identity that distinguishes that control.

A new formula for the same anchor counts as a useful coordinate, not as a closure.
