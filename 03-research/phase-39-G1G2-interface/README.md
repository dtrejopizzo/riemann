# Phase 39 — G1/G2 Interface

This phase steps back from the specific constructions of Phases 29–38 to ask, by backward derivation, what axioms a cohomology theory would need to satisfy to supply the missing positivity/duality ingredient (the "G1/G2 interface"), then inventories the Connes–Consani(–Moscovici) (CC/CCM) source material and measures how much of the CC machinery already satisfies those forced axioms.

## Results
- Forced axioms: a list of requirements is derived backward from what the sought cohomology theory must provide (by analogy with the classical Mattuck–Tate/Grothendieck/Weil and Guillemin/Connes trace-formula routes). (framework construction, no status label stated as proved/open — structural)
- Source inventory of the CC/CCM machinery: a verified bibliography of the relevant CC/CCM papers (arithmetic site, scaling site, Riemann–Roch strategies, archimedean positivity, zeta spectral triples) with source-verification flags. (inventory, not a mathematical result)
- Gap analysis: the existing CC machinery is measured against the forced axioms of Doc 119; the comparison identifies which pieces are already supplied by the literature and which remain missing. (OPEN — gaps identified, not closed)
