## Verdict

**Corpus:** nothing in the corpus states the dual (Theorem D1), the non-attainment (Theorem O), or
any certificate (Theorem C). The genuine partial anticipations are: the **primal** LP and the
Bochner obstruction (R153_016 §2.3), the Lévy form of the prime generator and the correct
identification of a continuous archimedean part (phase 64, with a wrong diffusion identification),
the complete-Bernstein-in-`τ²` fact (114 D.69) and the full Lévy–Khintchine + killed-on-`I_T`
framing (114 D.182), and the recorded failure of the killed-process/ground-state route (114 D.98).
Theorem A is correctly labelled a **partial restatement**; the Structure theorem is a **correction**
of phase 64 §5; D1, O and C are new to the corpus.

**Literature:** (a) and the non-attainment half of (d) are, on this search, **not in the
literature**. (b) is not in the literature *in our form*, but Suzuki's screw-function programme is
close enough in genus that the primary texts must be read before claiming it. (c) is the one place
where the claim in R154_012 §2 must be **narrowed**: Zhu arXiv:2608.24827 (Aug 2026) certifies
positivity on a strictly larger window (`supp f ⊂ [−0.8,0.8]`) by finite Galerkin + interval
arithmetic, and Groskin arXiv:2607.02828 does the same with a tail budget. The defensible claim is:
*first proof of Weil positivity with a prime coupled by an explicit outside-window dual certificate
— mechanism-visible, no Galerkin compression, no eigenvalue computation* — together with the first
statement of the finite-support dual equivalence itself.

**Actions before publication:** obtain Bombieri 2000 and Yoshida 1992; read Suzuki
arXiv:2206.03682 / 2301.00421 for any Lévy–Khintchine statement of the screw kernel; read Zhu
arXiv:2608.24827 and Groskin arXiv:2607.02828 in full and cite them in Paper 46/47; verify or drop
the unverified "`log 2 ≤ L < log 3` via `−W₂`" attribution to Connes–Consani.
