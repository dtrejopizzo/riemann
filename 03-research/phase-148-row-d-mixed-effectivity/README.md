## The question

Phase 147 closed the Galois-ring thread on the negative and left a test for every successor proposal:
*any further "more correspondences" idea must first show that its `+` does not commute with the radial
projection* (`R147_005` §5). Phase 148's proposal was the full square `X = C_Q × C_Q`, with an effective
cone generated by **mixed classes**

    k(x,y) = Φ(y/x) − φ(x)ψ(y) ≥ 0 ,

i.e. translation kernels `(T)` minus rank-one kernels `(R)`. The claim tested was **not** new arithmetic
data — `R146_003` §D.49 item 4 had already barred the square on that ground, citing `P122.028` — but a new
**effectivity constraint**. Does it raise the support-function constant at the lowest zero above Paper 46's
`s₁ = 0.99846016…`, which is what the frame defect theorem (Thms 19.23, 19.25) lives on?

## The answer

**No.** The effectivity condition *is* tropical: `k ≥ 0` iff

    Φ(w) ≥ (φ ⊛ ψ)(w) := sup_u φ(u) ψ(u+w) ,

a max-plus correlation in `R_max` — the first appearance in row D of the `(max,+)` structure under
Connes–Consani's arithmetic site. But it points the wrong way, and for a reason that needs **no decay,
no Hilbert–Schmidt hypothesis and no sign convention** (the repaired Lemma D.56.3′):

* for **nonnegative** factors, `φ ⊛ ψ ≥ 0` is a supremum of nonnegative numbers — unconditionally. So
  `Φ ≥ φ⊛ψ` **implies** `Φ ≥ 0`. No growth condition can touch this, which closes the `ψ ≍ e^{|w|/2}`
  "Weil-density" escape before it starts;
* for **signed** factors, `(φ⊛ψ)(w) < 0` forces `φ(u)ψ(u+w) ≤ −ε` for *every* `u`, hence
  `χ_{φ,ψ}(w) = −∞`: the escape destroys the **`(T)×(R)`** block of the form — the weaker of the two —
  not merely the Hilbert–Schmidt block;
* since `D^W ⊂ C²`, an enlargement of the shadow needs a **nonempty open** set of such `w`, so a null set
  of bad points cannot help, and the form fails against an open cone of translation tests.

Hence the translation shadow of the mixed cone is *exactly* the frame's effective cone `𝓔`, **on the
entire domain where `B` is defined**, at every finite rank and **for both signs** (Thms D.56.4″, D.56.4′).
The support function is unchanged:

    s^(M)(γ₁, π/2) = s₁ = 0.99846016271880910923… ,   1 − s₁ = 1.53984×10⁻³ ,   d₀ = 324.9598266…

Subtracting (or adding) a rank-one kernel can never repair an ineffective `Φ`. The relaxed clause (b′)⁺_X
is *equivalent* to (b′)⁺ and is refuted by the same witness `tF₀ + dK`.

## Structural findings about the form `B`

`B` is a **single, well-defined, bilinear, symmetric** form on `(T) ⊕ (R)` — the sum is direct (a
translation kernel of finite rank is an exponential polynomial, excluded by (11.1)), and that directness
is the *only* reason it is well defined. Three things follow, none of them a tension inside `B`:

1. **No common radial representation** (Thm D.55.6′). `B(·,k_Φ)` is represented on `(T)` by the Weil
   density `w_Φ` and on `(R)` by `Φ` itself; at `Φ = 2K`, `w_{2K} = 2cosh(w/2)` grows while `2K` decays.
   There is no linear `R : 𝒦 → D^W` with `B(k,k_Φ) = ∫R(k)·w_Φ`.
2. **No radial projection and no degree** (Cor. D.55.7′). The diagonal *mean* kills `(R)`; the diagonal
   *integral* is `+∞` on `(T)`. A blockwise linear map does exist — it is simply not an averaging limit.
   The two candidate degree functionals agree on `(T)` and disagree on `(R)`.
3. **Two inequivalent prescriptions, glued by fiat** (Thm D.55.5′, replacing a withdrawn round-1 title).
   `(T)×(T)` is a *regularised* trace; `(T)×(R)` and `(R)×(R)` are *honest* traces of trace-class
   operators. The gluing along the direct sum is legitimate and is a **choice**, recorded as the
   blueprint asked. The normalisation that makes `(R)` converge to `(T)` pointwise is not the
   normalisation under which `B` converges (Thm D.55.8′; the unrescaled pairing is constant in `Λ`).
   *Any resemblance to Connes–Consani's "delicate principal values" is an **`ANALOGY`**, not a
   restatement — downgraded on audit.*

Decisively for the negative: the `(T)×(R)` block is an ordinary integral `∫X·χ_{φ,ψ}` whose **integrand
contains no zeta data**, and which is **not a function of any class's coset data** — so by Thm D.31.2(iii)
it cannot refute (b′)⁺. The `(R)` sector is zero-blind. The blueprint's
`⟨Frob_n, φ⊗ψ⟩ = ∫φ(u)ψ(u+log n)du` is indeed the first finite, nonnegative, zero-free Frobenius pairing
in row D — and being zero-free is exactly why it is useless.

## What this phase does NOT show

**It does not show that the square admits no intersection theory.** It shows that *the one form written
down here, on the one domain chosen here, is trivial and zero-blind.* Phase-41 doc 125
(`03-research/phase-41-build-G2/125-forma-interseccion-cuadrado.md`) is the **same object one level up**:
its flow correspondences `Δ_μ = {v = u + log μ}` are `(T)` un-smeared, and its fibre classes
`f₁ = C×{pt}`, `f₂ = {pt}×C` lie **outside** `(T) ⊕ (R)` and are paired there with `f₁·f₂ = 1` — a finite
nonzero **integer**, on exactly the classes `B` cannot pair. That is O-148.2 made concrete.
