# §D.55 — The bilinear form `B` on `(T) ⊕ (R)`

## D.55.0 Conventions

Work in the logarithmic coordinate of Paper 47 §11: `C_Q ⊃ R_+^×`, `u := log|x|`, invariant measure
`d^×x = du` (Lebesgue), so that the "square" is `X := R × R` with `du\,dv`, and the multiplicative ratio
`y/x` is the additive difference `v − u`. All of `D^W`, `Φ_E(s) = ∫E(u)e^{(s−½)u}du`, `I_∞`, `𝓝_∞`,
`deg`, `K`, `𝓔 := {E ∈ D^W : E ≥ 0, E ≢ 0}` are Paper 47 §§11.1–11.5 verbatim.

**Definition D.55.1 (the three families).** For `Φ, φ, ψ ∈ D^W` real:

* **(T)** the *translation kernels* `k_Φ(u,v) := Φ(v−u)`;
* **(R)** the *rank-one kernels* `(φ⊗ψ)(u,v) := φ(u)ψ(v)`, and their finite real spans;
* **(M)** the *mixed effective classes* `k = k_Φ − φ⊗ψ` with `k(u,v) ≥ 0` for every `(u,v) ∈ X`.

`(T)` and `(R)` are real vector spaces of continuous kernels on `X`; write `𝒦 := (T) + (R)`.

> **Why the choice `C_Q × C_Q` versus `C_S × C_S` is not a choice** (the blueprint's "decide"). Every
> object below is a function of the modulus alone, and the `S`-chart enters nowhere: `(T)` is `D^W`,
> which by Paper 47 Thm 14.3 is a `D_A^{rad}`-module and by Finding 11.0 is **not** the colimit
> `T_∞`-domain. Taking `C_S × C_S` and projecting to the modulus gives `X` back, identically, for every
> `S`. This is the same collapse as R147's Lemma D.50.2 and Cor. D.12.2/Finding D.12.3 of phase 137, one
> level up. `PROVED` (by inspection of the definitions).

## D.55.1 `(T) ∩ (R) = 0`, so the sum is direct

**Theorem D.55.2 (`PROVED`, unconditional).** If `Φ ∈ D^W` and `k_Φ` is a finite sum of rank-one kernels
with factors vanishing at infinity, then `Φ ≡ 0`. Hence `(T) ∩ (R) = 0` and `𝒦 = (T) ⊕ (R)`.

*Proof.* Suppose `Φ(v−u) = Σ_{i=1}^n φ_i(u)ψ_i(v)` for all `u,v`. For each fixed `v` the function
`u ↦ Φ(v−u) = (τ_{−v}Φ^∨)(u)` lies in `span\{φ_1,…,φ_n\}`, a space of dimension `≤ n`. So the set of all
translates `\{τ_cΦ^∨ : c ∈ R\}` spans a real vector space of dimension `≤ n`. A continuous function whose
translates span a finite-dimensional space satisfies a linear ODE with constant coefficients
(`CLASSICAL`; apply the finite-dimensionality to the derivatives of `c ↦ τ_cΦ^∨`, which exist in that
space), hence is an *exponential polynomial* `Σ_j p_j(t)e^{λ_jt}`. By (11.1) such a function is
`O(e^{−(1+ε)|t|})` **in both directions**, which forces every `p_j ≡ 0`. So `Φ ≡ 0`. ∎ `PROVED`

> This is not a formality: it is what makes `B` *definable at all*. Had the two families overlapped, the
> blueprint's two block formulas would have had to agree on the overlap, and §D.55.3 shows they do not
> agree on anything they can be compared on.

## D.55.2 The form, and that it is forced rather than chosen

**Definition D.55.3.** For `k, k' ∈ 𝒦` set, by bilinear extension from Theorem D.55.2,

    B(k_Φ , k_{Φ'})            := I_∞(Φ, Φ')                                       (T)×(T)
    B(k_Φ , φ⊗ψ)               := ∫∫_X Φ(v−u) φ(u) ψ(v) du dv                      (T)×(R)
    B(φ⊗ψ , φ'⊗ψ')             := ⟨φ,φ'⟩_{L²} ⟨ψ,ψ'⟩_{L²}                          (R)×(R)

**Lemma D.55.4 (`PROVED`).** Write `χ_{φ,ψ} := φ^∨ ⋆ ψ ∈ D^W`, i.e.
`χ_{φ,ψ}(w) = ∫φ(u)ψ(u+w)du` (the *cross-correlation*). Then
`B(k_Φ, φ⊗ψ) = ∫_R Φ(w)\,χ_{φ,ψ}(w)\,dw`, absolutely convergent, with
`|B(k_Φ,φ⊗ψ)| ≤ ‖Φ‖_∞‖φ‖_{L¹}‖ψ‖_{L¹} < ∞`.

*Proof.* Fubini (licensed by the bound displayed, which is (11.1) with `j = 0`) and the substitution
`w = v − u`. `χ_{φ,ψ} ∈ D^W` by Lemma 11.2(i). ∎ `PROVED`

**Theorem D.55.5 (the three blocks are the three restrictions of one composition trace; `PROVED`).**
Read `k ∈ 𝒦` as an integral operator on `L²(R, du)`. Then:

 (i) `k_Φ ∘ k_{Φ'}^† = k_{Φ⋆Φ'^∨}`, and `B` on `(T)×(T)` is Paper 47 Def. 11.4, i.e.
     `⟨(Φ⋆Φ'^∨)_{\mathrm{ev}}, W_{fin}+ω_∞⟩`, the **regularized** trace;
 (ii) `k_Φ ∘ (φ⊗ψ)^†` is the **rank-one** operator `(u,w) ↦ (Φ^∨⋆ψ)(u)·φ(w)`, it is trace class, and
     its honest trace is `∫(Φ^∨⋆ψ)(u)φ(u)du = B(k_Φ,φ⊗ψ)` of Definition D.55.3 — no regularization is
     used or available;
 (iii) `(φ⊗ψ)∘(φ'⊗ψ')^†` has honest trace `⟨φ,φ'⟩⟨ψ,ψ'⟩`;
 (iv) `B` is bilinear and **symmetric** on `(T) ⊕ (R)`.

*Proof.* (i) is the convolution identity plus Definition 11.4 and Lemma 11.4a. (ii): with
`(Aξ)(u) = φ(u)∫ψξ` and `(Mξ)(u) = ∫Φ(v−u)ξ(v)dv`, the kernel of `MA^†` is `g(u)φ(w)` with
`g(u) = ∫Φ(v−u)ψ(v)dv = (Φ^∨⋆ψ)(u)`; a rank-one operator with continuous `L¹∩L²` factors is trace class
with `Tr = ∫gφ`, and unwinding `g` returns the double integral. (iii) is the Hilbert–Schmidt inner
product. (iv): `I_∞` is symmetric (Thm 11.5); the `(T)×(R)` block is symmetric because `Tr(MA^†)` and
`Tr(A^†M)` coincide for a trace-class product (direct check: both equal
`∫∫Φ(v−u)φ(u)ψ(v)dudv`); the `(R)×(R)` block is symmetric by inspection. ∎ `PROVED`

> **W-a.** No positivity of `B` is asserted. The `(R)×(R)` block is positive semidefinite **by
> definition** — it is the Hilbert–Schmidt inner product — and that is not a result. Nothing is claimed
> about the `(T)×(T)` block, which is `I_∞`, or about the sign of `B` on `(M)`.

## D.55.3 **Finding**: the two blocks carry different representing densities, hence no degree and no
## radial projection exists on the square

This is the blueprint's "first thing that can fail". It does fail, in a specific and provable way, and
the failure is what decides D.56.

**Theorem D.55.6 (the density anomaly; `PROVED`, unconditional).** Fix `Φ ∈ D^W` and let
`w_Φ` be the density (11.16) of Theorem 11.14(i). Then, as functionals on `𝒦`,

    B(k_Ψ , k_Φ) = ∫ Ψ(w)\,w_Φ(w)\,dw ,        B(φ⊗ψ , k_Φ) = ∫ χ_{φ,ψ}(w)\,Φ(w)\,dw .   (148.1)

So `B(·,k_Φ)` is represented on the `(T)` sector by the **Weil density `w_Φ`** and on the `(R)` sector by
**`Φ` itself**. These are different functions: at `Φ = 2K` one has `w_{2K}(w) = 2\cosh(w/2)`, which is
**unbounded and increasing in `|w|`**, while `2K` is the Pólya class, positive and decaying faster than
every exponential (Paper 47 Thm 11.7(ii)); they agree nowhere outside a bounded set.

*Proof.* The first display is Theorem 11.14(i). The second is Lemma D.55.4. For `Φ = 2K`, (11.16) with
`Φ_{2K}(0) = Φ_{2K}(1) = 1` and `Φ_{2K}(1−ρ) = 2ξ(1−ρ) = 0` at every zero gives
`w_{2K}(w) = e^{−w/2}+e^{w/2} = 2\cosh(w/2)`. ∎ `PROVED`

**Corollary D.55.7 (the square has no radial projection, and no degree; `PROVED`).**

 (i) *(no radial projection)* The two natural candidates are `R_{mean}(k)(w) := \lim_{Λ→∞}
     \frac1{2Λ}∫_{−Λ}^{Λ}k(u,u+w)du` and `R_{int}(k)(w) := ∫_R k(u,u+w)du`. Then
     `R_{mean}(k_Φ) = Φ` and `R_{mean}(φ⊗ψ) = 0`; `R_{int}(φ⊗ψ) = χ_{φ,ψ}` and
     `R_{int}(k_Φ) ≡ +∞` for `Φ ≢ 0`. **Neither is defined and nonzero on both summands**, and no
     linear map `𝒦 → D^W` restricting to the identity on `(T)` and to `φ⊗ψ ↦ χ_{φ,ψ}` on `(R)` is a
     limit of diagonal averages.
 (ii) *(no degree)* The two natural degree functionals on `𝒦` — `k ↦ B(k, k_{2K})` and
     `k ↦ \deg R_{int}(k)` — agree on `(T)` (both give `\deg Φ`) and **disagree on `(R)`**: the first
     gives `∫2K·χ_{φ,ψ}`, the second `∫2\cosh(w/2)·χ_{φ,ψ}(w)dw`, and their difference
     `∫χ_{φ,ψ}(w)\,[2\cosh(w/2) − 2K(w)]\,dw` is **strictly positive** for every `φ,ψ ≥ 0`, `≢ 0`
     (the bracket is `> 0` outside a compact set, where `2K → 0` and `2\cosh → ∞`, and `χ_{φ,ψ} ≥ 0`).

*Proof.* (i) is the computation of the two limits: the diagonal integral of a translation-invariant
kernel is `Φ(w)·∫_Rdu`; the diagonal mean of `φ⊗ψ` is `(2Λ)^{−1}χ_{φ,ψ}(w)(1+o(1)) → 0`. The last clause
is (ii). (ii) is Theorem D.55.6 with `Φ = 2K`, plus `2\cosh(w/2) − 2K(w) > 0` for `|w|` large and
`χ_{φ,ψ} ≥ 0` continuous. ∎ `PROVED`

## D.55.4 `B` exists but is not continuous between the blocks

**Theorem D.55.8 (the block divergence; `PROVED`, unconditional).** Let `T_a` be the diagonal translation
`(T_ak)(u,v) := k(u−a, v−a)`, under which `B` is invariant and `(T)` is pointwise fixed. Put
`A_Λ(φ⊗ψ) := \frac1{2Λ}∫_{−Λ}^{Λ}T_a(φ⊗ψ)\,da`. Then:

 (i) `2Λ·A_Λ(φ⊗ψ) → k_{χ_{φ,ψ}}` pointwise on `X` as `Λ → ∞`: **the `(T)` sector is the rescaled
     diagonal-average limit of the `(R)` sector**;
 (ii) for every fixed `Φ ∈ D^W`,
     `B\big(2Λ·A_Λ(φ⊗ψ),\,k_Φ\big) = 2Λ·∫Φ·χ_{φ,ψ}`, which **diverges linearly in `Λ`** whenever
     `∫Φχ_{φ,ψ} ≠ 0`, while the limit object has the finite value
     `B(k_{χ_{φ,ψ}}, k_Φ) = I_∞(χ_{φ,ψ}, Φ)`.

*Proof.* (i) `\frac1{2Λ}∫_{−Λ}^{Λ}φ(u−a)ψ(v−a)da → \frac1{2Λ}χ_{φ,ψ}(v−u)` for `Λ` beyond the effective

# §D.56 — The kill test

## D.56.1 The tropical characterization of `(M)`

**Definition D.56.1 (the tropical correlation).** For `φ, ψ : R → R` put

    (φ ⊛ ψ)(w) := \sup_{u∈R} φ(u)\,ψ(u+w) ∈ [−∞, +∞] .

In the logarithms this is the `(\max,+)` — *tropical* — correlation: if `φ = e^{-α}`, `ψ = e^{-β}` with
`α,β : R → R ∪ \{+∞\}`, then `\log(φ ⊛ ψ)(w) = \sup_u\big[(−α)(u) + (−β)(u+w)\big]`, the max-plus
convolution of `−α` and `(−β)^∨` in the semiring `R_{\max} = (R∪\{−∞\}, \max, +)`.

**Theorem D.56.2 (characterization of `(M)`; `PROVED`, unconditional).** For `Φ, φ, ψ ∈ D^W`,

    k_Φ − φ⊗ψ ≥ 0 \text{ on } X \iff Φ(w) ≥ (φ ⊛ ψ)(w) \text{ for every } w ∈ R .

*Proof.* `Φ(v−u) ≥ φ(u)ψ(v)` for all `(u,v)` is, after `v = u+w`, `Φ(w) ≥ φ(u)ψ(u+w)` for all `u` and all
`w`; take the supremum in `u` at fixed `w`. ∎ `PROVED`

> **Corpus placement (`NEW` as a condition; `IMPORTED`+`RESTATEMENT` as structure).** The semiring is
> Connes–Consani's `Z_{\max}`/`R_+^{\max}`, verified in the corpus at
> `phase-39-G1G2-interface/120-source-inventory-of-the-connes-consani-moscovici.md:81–92` against 1502.05580 Def. 3.1. What is new is
> only that an **effectivity condition on a kernel over the square is a tropical inequality**; the
> semiring, the arithmetic site and the scaling site are imported and are not rebuilt here (blueprint
> warning, obeyed). No CC theorem is used below.

## D.56.2 The collapse: the tropical condition forces the translation part to be effective

**Lemma D.56.3 (`PROVED`, unconditional, two lines).** For every `φ, ψ : R → R` continuous with
`φ(u) → 0` and `ψ(u) → 0` as `|u| → ∞` — in particular for every `φ, ψ ∈ D^W`, and more generally for
every pair for which the `(R)×(R)` block of `B` is finite —

    (φ ⊛ ψ)(w) ≥ 0 \qquad\text{for every } w ∈ R .

*Proof.* Fix `w`. The function `p_w(u) := φ(u)ψ(u+w)` is continuous and `p_w(u) → 0` as `u → +∞`.
Therefore `\sup_u p_w(u) ≥ \limsup_{u→+∞}p_w(u) = 0`. ∎ `PROVED`

**Theorem D.56.4 (THE KILL; `PROVED`, unconditional).** Let `k = k_Φ − φ⊗ψ ∈ (M)`. Then `Φ ≥ 0`
pointwise. Consequently, writing `𝓣(M) := \{Φ ∈ D^W : ∃\,φ,ψ ∈ D^W,\ k_Φ − φ⊗ψ ∈ (M)\}` for the
**translation shadow** of the mixed cone,

    𝓣(M) \;=\; \{Φ ∈ D^W : Φ ≥ 0\} \;=\; 𝓔 ∪ \{0\} .

*Proof.* `⊆`: Theorem D.56.2 and Lemma D.56.3 give `Φ ≥ φ⊛ψ ≥ 0`. `⊇`: given `Φ ≥ 0` take `φ = ψ = 0`,
so `k_Φ ∈ (M)`. ∎ `PROVED`

> **The direction of the enlargement.** The blueprint hoped that a mixed class would be *more* general
> than an effective radial class, so that the frame's necessary conditions could be run on a larger
> cone. The tropical condition does the opposite: it is a **lower bound** on `Φ` by a nonnegative
> function, hence a *strengthening* of `Φ ≥ 0`. The rank-one part is a *cost*, never a *credit*.
> Subtracting `φ⊗ψ` can never repair an ineffective `Φ`.
>
> **And the collapse is structural, not accidental.** Lemma D.56.3 uses exactly one property of `(R)`:
> that its factors vanish at infinity. That is precisely the property that makes the `(R)×(R)` block of
> `B` — the Hilbert–Schmidt inner product — finite. **Any enlargement of `(R)` that escapes the collapse
> destroys the form.** (Take `φ = ψ ≡ 1`: then `(φ⊛ψ) ≡ 1` and the condition `Φ ≥ 1` is impossible in
> `D^W` anyway; take `φ ≡ 1`, `ψ = −g` with `g > 0`: then `φ⊛ψ = −\inf g ≤ 0` and the collapse fails,
> but `φ⊗ψ` is not Hilbert–Schmidt and `B` is undefined on it.) `PROVED`

## D.56.3 The support-function comparison at `ρ₁`

**Definition D.56.5.** For a zero `ρ = ½+iγ` of `ξ` on the critical line and `θ ∈ R`, the
**support-function constant of the mixed cone** in the direction `(γ,θ)` is

## D.56.5 The `(R)` sector is zero-blind: why no `B`-route can help either

Theorem D.56.6 settles the shadow. It remains to check the only other route to an inequality: pairing a
mixed class against a `(T)`-test through `B` itself.

**Theorem D.56.8 (the `(R)` block names no zeta; `PROVED`).** For `k = k_Ψ − φ⊗ψ ∈ (M)` and any test

    B(k, k_X) \;=\; I_∞(Ψ, X) \;-\; ∫_R X(w)\,χ_{φ,ψ}(w)\,dw ,                     (148.2)

and the second term is an ordinary, absolutely convergent Lebesgue integral in which **no zero, no pole,
no Euler factor and no `L`-function occurs**: it is defined by the measure `du\,dv` and the functions
`X, φ, ψ` alone. Consequently:

 (i) the second term of (148.2) is **numerically identical** for `ξ`, for `Ξ_a`, for `Ξ_Q` and for every
     `ζ_K`: the entire `ξ`-dependence of `B(k,k_X)` sits in `I_∞(Ψ,X)`, i.e. in the `(T)×(T)` block;
 (ii) the second term is **not a function of the coset data** `(M(·),(Φ_·(ρ))_ρ)` of any class: it does

     `Φ_N(0) = Φ_N(1) = Φ_N(ρ) = 0` says nothing about `∫Nχ_{φ,ψ}`, which is nonzero in general.

*Proof.* (148.2) is Lemma D.55.4 and Definition D.55.3; (i) and (ii) are read off it, the former because
`Ξ_a`, `Ξ_Q`, `ζ_K` are admissible `Z`'s in the sense of Paper 47 §12.5 entering only through `I_∞`. ∎
`PROVED`

> **Two consequences, both negative and both sharp.**

# §D.58 — Synthesis

**The kill test, in one paragraph.** The blueprint proposed to enlarge row D's carrier from the radial
line to the square `C_Q × C_Q`, generating an effective cone by mixed classes `k_Φ − φ⊗ψ ≥ 0`, in the
hope that the resulting cone's support function at the lowest zero would exceed the frame's `s_1` and so
lift Theorem 19.23. The effectivity condition is indeed tropical — `Φ ≥ φ ⊛ ψ` with
`(φ⊛ψ)(w) = \sup_uφ(u)ψ(u+w)`, a max-plus correlation in `R_{\max}`, and this is the first appearance in
row D of the `(\max,+)` structure that Connes–Consani put at the base of the arithmetic site. **But the
tropical condition points the wrong way.** Because the factors of a Hilbert–Schmidt rank-one kernel
vanish at infinity, `φ⊛ψ ≥ 0` automatically, so `Φ ≥ φ⊛ψ` **implies** `Φ ≥ 0`: the translation shadow of
the mixed cone is *exactly* the frame's effective cone `𝓔`, not larger. The support function is therefore
unchanged, `s^{(M)}(γ_1,π/2) = s_1 = 0.99846016271880910923…`, margin `1−s_1 = 1.53984×10^{−3}` and
threshold `d_0 = 324.9598266…` both untouched; the relaxed clause (b′)⁺_X is *equivalent* to (b′)⁺ and is
refuted by the same witness `tF_0 + dK`; and the only other route — pairing through `B` — is worse than
useless, because the `(T)×(R)` block is an ordinary integral `∫Xχ_{φ,ψ}` that names no zeta function, is
not a function of any class's coset data, and therefore yields no member of the testable family (★). The whole new content of
the phase is `Ξ_a`-blind, so no step could have used equimodularity, and none does. **Outcome (i); no
FATAL flag; no label changes.**

**Is the tropical condition a new ingredient or a restatement?** It is a **new condition** (corpus check
§0: no hit for max-plus or sup-convolution as an effectivity condition anywhere in `03-research` or
`04-papers`) built on an **imported structure** (`Z_{\max}`, verified in the corpus since phase 39). And
it is a **new constraint that constrains nothing new**: Theorem D.56.4 shows it is equivalent, on the
shadow, to the frame's own `Φ ≥ 0`. This is the phase's honest contribution — a named, elementary reason
why the max-plus structure of the arithmetic site does not reach the zeros of `ζ` through the effective
cone.

**What P122.028 says about it, and whether a new constraint would have been enough.** `R146_003` §D.49
item 4 issued the order *"Do not pursue a two-variable square on `C_S×C_S` (no source of zero data;
P122.028 bars the only correction term the Euler product offers)"*, in the weakened verb ordered by
audit finding m2: **barred as an independent input**, not refuted. P122.028 (`PROVED-IN-REPOSITORY`) is
the exact two-prime finite Green complex; its verdict is that the complex *"has an exact positive
Dirichlet-to-Neumann form whose kernel is precisely the transverse seam. Nothing in the affine relations
or the first-contact scalar equation forces that energy to be zero. Thus Gate S remains the whole missing
horizontal theorem, not a consequence of the carrier."* Phase 148 set out to answer the objection
sideways — not by producing new arithmetic data, but by producing a new *effectivity constraint*. The
answer is: **a new constraint is not enough, and this particular constraint is not even new in effect.**
D.49 item 4's bar is confirmed, now by a second and independent mechanism (Theorem D.56.8: the square's
new block is zero-free and non-invariant), and phase 148 adds to it the sharper structural statement that
**the square carries no degree and no radial projection at all** (Cor. D.55.7), so there is no
Riemann–Roch to be run on it even if data were supplied.

**Where this sits in the row-D record.** Phases 145–148 have now named the missing ingredient four times.
146: `Spec Z` has no ring of correspondences. 147.1: where a ring exists (Galois), it is invisible on the
radial carrier. 147.3: where a carrier sees it (graded), it acts away from the zeros of `ζ`. **148: where
a genuinely non-radial carrier exists (the square), the additional sector is Hilbert–Schmidt, hence
vanishing at infinity, hence tropically nonnegative, hence unable to enlarge the effective cone — and it
is zero-blind besides.** R147_005's closing test — *"Any further 'more correspondences' proposal (Hecke,
Borger, `F₁`, CC) must first show it does not commute with the radial projection"* — is passed in form
and failed in substance: the square's `+` does not commute with the radial projection because **there is
no radial projection**, and the sector that would have supplied the non-commutation is exactly the
sector that carries no zeros.

**Recommendation.** Close the square. Do not open D.57, D.59, or any successor built on `C×C` kernels of
Hilbert–Schmidt type: Lemma D.56.3 shows the obstruction is a consequence of finiteness of the
`(R)×(R)` block, so every variant within the form's domain of definition inherits it. If this is wanted,
one further probe, the only opening this phase leaves is **O-148.4** below — a `(T)`-sector object with
zero data and an unbounded `(R)`-type sector would have to violate Theorem D.55.8's divergence, i.e. it
would have to be a genuine regularization of the square's trace, which is Connes–Consani's own stated
open problem and not a programme-scale increment. Otherwise: record phases 134–148 in Paper 47 Part IV
and close row D at the map, per R147_005 §5.
