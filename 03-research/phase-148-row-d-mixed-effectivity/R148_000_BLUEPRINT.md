## The object
`X := C_Q × C_Q` (or `C_S × C_S`, decide). Kernels `k(x,y)` on `X`, real, of a decay class to be fixed.
Three families: (T) translation kernels `k_Φ(x,y) = Φ(y/x)`, `Φ ∈ D^W` (the frame's radial classes);
(R) rank-one kernels `φ⊗ψ`; (M) mixed classes `k = k_Φ − φ⊗ψ` with `k ≥ 0` pointwise.
Pairing: on (T)×(T) the frame's `I_∞` (regularized trace of composition = explicit formula, CLASSICAL:
Connes); on (T)×(R): `⟨k_Φ, φ⊗ψ⟩ := ∫∫ Φ(y/x)φ(x)ψ(y) dx dy = ⟨φ, Φ⋆ψ⟩` (finite, ≥ 0 for nonneg data);
on (R)×(R): Hilbert–Schmidt `⟨φ⊗ψ, φ'⊗ψ'⟩ = ⟨φ,φ'⟩⟨ψ,ψ'⟩`. State the bilinear form `B` on
`(T) ⊕ (R)` and prove it is well defined and symmetric (fix the invariant measure and the regularization
so that the (T)×(R) block is consistent with the (T)×(T) regularization — this is the first thing that
can fail: record it).

## D.56 — The kill test (do first; decides the phase)
**Question.** Does mixed effectivity produce an inequality on `I_∞` not derivable from the radial sector?
1. Characterize (M): for which `Φ ≥ 0` and `φ, ψ ≥ 0` is `Φ(y/x) ≥ φ(x)ψ(y)` for all `x,y`? (Necessary:
   `φ(x)ψ(y) ≤ Φ(y/x)`; taking sup over `x` at fixed ratio gives `sup_x φ(x)ψ(rx) ≤ Φ(r)`, i.e. `Φ ≥
   φ ⊛ ψ` where `(φ ⊛ ψ)(r) := sup_x φ(x)ψ(rx)` — the **max-plus (tropical) convolution**. Record that
   the effectivity condition is tropical: this is where the two structures `+` and `×` of the integers
   meet in the frame, and it is the first appearance of the max-plus structure of Connes–Consani's
   arithmetic site inside row D. Corpus check: grep "tropical", "max-plus", "Z_max", "arithmetic site".)
2. Consequences of effectivity: the frame's Theorem 11.14 / Lemma D.31.4 bound `|Re e^{iθ}Φ_k(ρ)| ≤ ½ s
   deg k` for effective radial classes has an analogue for effective classes on `X`: what is the
   "degree" of a mixed class (the rulings `∫∫k`, i.e. `Φ_Φ(0)Φ_Φ(1)`-type data minus `∫φ∫ψ`)? What is
   the "value at a zero" of a mixed class (the (T) part sees `Φ_Φ(ρ)`; the (R) part has no zero data —
   or does the pairing with `K` or with `k_{τ_a K}` give it some)? Derive the inequality that `k ∈ (M)`
   imposes on `Φ_Φ(ρ)`. **Compare with the radial cosh bound**: is the constant improved (i.e., is the
   support function of the cone generated by (M)-classes, restricted to the (T) sector, larger than `s₁`
   at `ρ₁`)? This is the entire test. Outcomes: (i) the constant is unchanged ⟹ the (R) sector is
   invisible at the zeros, the phase closes negative, record the mechanism; (ii) improved but `< 1`;
   (iii) reaches `1` ⟹ FATAL-flag territory (would lift Thm 19.23): stop, run `Ξ_a`.
3. The `Ξ_a` check at the start: everything in (T), (R), (M) is defined verbatim for `Ξ_a` (radial
   classes with the `Ξ_a` explicit formula); the tropical condition is `Ξ_a`-blind. So any improvement
   in item 2 must come from a step that uses A3 (equimodularity). Identify it or conclude (i).

## D.57 — If the test passes (ii)/(iii): the ring
(R) ⊕ (T) with composition `∘` and the pointwise sum is a ring; effective classes (nonneg kernels) are
closed under `+` and `∘`; Frobenius graphs `Frob_n = k_{δ_{log n}}` (as limits). The `+` here does not
commute with the radial projection: `R(φ⊗ψ) = ` the radial average `∫φ(x)ψ(rx)dx`, which is a class in
`D^W` — compute it and show that `R` is not injective on (R) (this is the non-commutation). Degree as a
count: `⟨Frob_n, φ⊗ψ⟩ = ∫φ(x)ψ(nx)dx` is the first finite, nonneg, zero-free pairing of a Frobenius with
a class of the frame. State what "Riemann–Roch" would be here (h⁰ of a nonneg kernel = ?) and what
(b′) means; do not build `h⁰` unless the test passed.
