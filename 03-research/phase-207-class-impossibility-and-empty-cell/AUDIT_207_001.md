## A. CLASS_001

### A.1 Cor. 2–3: is the node an LMI in v? Is the theorem about premises or about functionals?

**Linearity.** By lem:combsine and by eq:Aform in the proof of lem:degen, 𝔞^{(v)}_T(F) = C₀ + L_ev(F) − Σ_n v_n c_F(log n),
where c_F(log n) is quadratic in F and does not depend on v. Compressed to E_N^ev ∩ c^⊥, the node is

  𝔄_N(v) = 𝔪_N + 𝔢_N − Σ_{n<e^{2T}} v_n C_n ⪰ 0 on c^⊥,

with the C_n fixed matrices. It is an affine LMI in v, and its feasible set G_T is a convex spectrahedron. This matches the
concavity of μ_T from lem:degen. **PROVED** (CITED P46).

**Corollaries 2 and 3 as statements.** "For fixed T the node depends on a only through v^a" is correct and trivial. "Weight
n^{−½}" and "FE only as symmetry" do not narrow the set of possible **statements** of the node: the node is a single one, an
LMI. **PROVED, trivial.**

**Substantive objection.** CLASS_001 moves from "the node depends only on v" to "every step depends on a only through v^a"
(l. 11, l. 118). That step is illegitimate. A **proof step** Ψ is not the node. It is a deduction "premise(a) ⇒ node(v^a)", and
the premise may be a theorem only on a subvariety of data that is not a cylinder over v. Examples:

- premises that are theorems only for a = coefficients of −L′/L of an L-function with an Euler product, continuation and
  the declared shape of FE (a lives in an infinite-dimensional variety; v is its projection);
- premises about values of ζ outside the window (in the tail n ≥ e^{2T}, or at Re s > 1) that restrict v only
  indirectly.

For those steps the intermediate step is not "a function of v". The only thing that matters is whether the **premise
variety**, projected to the v coordinate, lies inside G_T. CLASS_001 handles them in practice, because the class 𝒦_inh
(l. 95) is defined by inherited premises. But Corollary 2 declares them "within functionals of v", and that is false as
a statement.

**Required correction.** Cor. 2 should say: "(K3) is empty for the class of **functionals** Ψ(v;F); for **steps** with a
premise, the weight enters as a property of the premise variety (where it is a theorem), not of the functional". CLASS_001's
theorem is then a theorem **about premises**: 𝒦_inh by inheritance and 𝒦_marg by robust radius. It is not a theorem about
functionals. This fits better with thm:geo-tv, which is formulated over premise sets Π (§A.3).

What (K3) does not exclude: Ψ defined only on log-derivatives of Euler products, with a premise **not** inherited by a_P or
cΛ. That is precisely the open cell. CLASS_001 lets it through. That is correct, but it does not say so. It needs to be
named: "Ψ whose premise is a theorem only on {Λ_L : L with Euler product over all primes} and fails for a_P with
P > e^{2T}". Neither Theorem A nor B touch it if its margin exceeds e^{−15.449T}·(…), because Theorem A only requires a
robust radius in total variation over **all** combs (see §A.3).

### A.3 Theorem A: location, (H_ev), (H_N), tautology

**Location.** The statement is cor:margin1, eq:closedmargin (l. 3576): λ_A ≤ 4·10⁹Te^{−15.449T}, T ≥ 1. The bound comes from
the lem:testfam (iii) test: F = ϖ(∂)G with G(t) = ω_k(t/T), ω_k = b_k^{∗k}, and k = ⌈21T/e⌉. The 15.449 is 42/e, from
optimizing (k/21T)^{2k} (see §0). **CITED.**

**(H_ev).** Is the witness even? Yes:
- b_k = (k/2)1_{[−1/k,1/k]} is even, so ω_k = b_k^{∗k} is even;
- G(t) = ω_k(t/T) is even;
- ϖ(∂) = ∂⁴ + (γ₁² − ¼)∂² − γ₁²/4 only has even-order derivatives, which preserve parity. Hence F is even.
- F ∈ P_T (lem:testfam (iii)). In the even sector, P_T ∩ even = c^⊥ ∩ even, because ∫F e^{±t/2} = 0 is equivalent, for
  even F, to ⟨F, cosh(t/2)⟩ = 0 = ℓ(F).

Hence μ_ev(T) := inf_{even, c^⊥} 𝔞_T ≤ 𝔞_T(F)/‖F‖² ≤ 4·10⁹Te^{−15.449T}. **(H_ev) is PROVED** (mechanical check, CITED
P46). Part (a) of Theorem A holds for the even sector and not only for two sectors. CLASS_001 l. 151 left it OPEN; it is
closed here.

**(H_N).** Here CLASS_001 has a gap it does not acknowledge. For V_N = c^⊥ ∩ E_N^ev ⊂ c^⊥ ∩ 𝒟_T^ev, min–max (Rayleigh) gives

  μ_N(T) = inf_{V_N} 𝔞_T ≥ inf_{c^⊥∩𝒟^ev} 𝔞_T = μ_∞(T).

The inequality goes in the **opposite** direction from the one needed. The P46 upper bound gives no upper bound on μ_N. The
lem:testfam witness is not in E_N (it is not a finite combination of e_k), and its projection to E_N has a different Rayleigh
quotient. CLASS_001 (l. 145) writes "CONDITIONAL on (H_N), NUMERICAL evidence" without pointing out that the P46 → μ_N route
is blocked by direction. It is also not obvious that μ_N(T) → 0 with N ≈ 4T:
- if N grows fast enough, μ_N → μ_∞ (by density of ∪E_N in 𝒟^ev and continuity of 𝔞_T in form norm; ARGUED);
- with N ≈ 4T fixed in proportion there is no estimate. The evidence is CALIB/NODE_001: 0.00185 with 2 modes and 10⁻⁶–10⁻⁷
  with 3 modes at ½log5. These are three or four data points at a single T. They do not measure a rate in T.

**(H_N) is OPEN.** There is pointwise NUMERICAL evidence, without a rate. Closing it requires a witness **in E_N**: a
trigonometric polynomial in the e_k basis of degree ~N that kills the first zero, with ratio e^{−cT}. It is an
approximation problem, OPEN. Theorem A(b) should be labeled "CONDITIONAL on (H_N), OPEN", not "NUMERICAL in favor".

**Is the dichotomy tautological?** Yes. 𝒦_marg is defined (CLASS_001 §1) by: Ψ ≥ 0 on a ball of radius η/L around Λ
(in multiplier norm, i.e. of combs) and Ψ ≥ 0 ⇒ node. Then the node holds on the ball, so the inscribed radius of G_T at
v^Λ is ≥ η/L. Since μ is concave in v with |c_F(log n)| ≤ 1, that radius is ≤ μ. The "dichotomy" "𝒦_marg has a valid step
for all T ⟺ inf_T μ(T) > 0" is the definition plus one line. **Tautological.**

**Is Theorem A new?** No. It is **P46's thm:geo-tv** (l. 9236–9237): "If Π is robust of radius r at T and b_T(ν) ≥ 0 for
every ν ∈ Π, then r ≤ λ_A; for T ≥ 1, r ≤ 4·10⁹Te^{−15.449T}". The preceding P46 paragraph already includes the case of
"Lipschitz premises X_j with slacks s_j, r = min s_j/L_j", which is literally the class 𝒦_marg. It is also in dichotomy
(v) of the l. 6986 theorem and in lem:shape-margin. The only thing CLASS_001 adds is the E_N version, and that is
CONDITIONAL on (H_N) OPEN. **Honest label for Theorem A: CITED (= P46 thm:geo-tv + cor:margin1), with (H_ev) PROVED in
this audit; the E_N part is CONDITIONAL/OPEN.** CLASS_001 should cite thm:geo-tv and withdraw "PROVED, new".

### B.8 Lemma G and the a∗a point (the most important one)

**Source.** The computation is the stationary phase of I(r) = ∫ w(t)e^{2iθ(t) − it log r}dt. φ′(t) = log(t/2π) − log r + O(t^{−2}),
so t₀ = 2πr and φ″(t₀) = 1/t₀. φ(t₀) = t₀ log r − t₀ − π/4 − t₀ log r = −2πr − π/4. I verified it. The factor
√(2π/φ″) = √(2πt₀) = 2π√r cancels (mn)^{−½}, and e^{−2πimn} = 1. This is Gonek's lemma (1984, Invent. Math. 75, Lemma 5:
asymptotic value of ∫ (t/2π)^{−it}… e^{it log(t/2πe)…}; CITED), the basis of the averages Σ_{n} a_n χ(…) n^{it} and of
the χ approximation in Conrey–Ghosh–Gonek. **The computation is classical.** The identity
∫ χ(½−it)A(½+it)B(½+it)w ≈ Σ_N w(2πN)(a∗b)(N) is exactly the standard use of Gonek's lemma for "mixed" moments with χ.
**It is not new.** What is new in CELL_001 is the **reading** within the program: "the FE part of every weight-½ form is a
functional of the convolution a∗a at N = mn", i.e. a multiplicative Hankel form with symbol N ↦ w(2πN). That reading is
correct.

**Error.** For w smooth and compactly supported on [T₁, 2T₁] with scale-T₁ derivatives, the Gonek error is
O(r^{−½}·(1 + …)) per term. The control CELL_001 gives (l. 171) is reasonable for truncated a (n ≤ X with X² ≪ T₁) and
**PROVED given CITED** in that regime. For a = Λ without truncation the sum does not converge. It must be truncated, and
the error depends on X. That is fine for Dirichlet polynomials, which is the declared frame.

**Which a does CELL_001 use?** I resolved the initial suspicion by reading the body (l. 186–190, l. 194):
- (iii) "**If a is completely multiplicative**, (a∗a)(N) = a_N·d(N)". Correct: Σ_{mn=N} a(m)a(n) = a(N)·#{mn = N}. Holds
  for a_n = 1 (ζ), λ(n), characters, coefficients of ζ_{a_P} = ∏_{p≥P}(1−p^{−s})^{−1}, etc.
- "For the node, Λ must be put in the coefficients. If D = −ζ′/ζ truncated, a = Λ and (Λ∗Λ)(N) ≥ 0". Correct. Λ∗Λ
  **is not** Λ·d. By Selberg's identity, Λ∗Λ = μ∗log² − Λ·log, and it is quadratic in Λ.

So the body does **not** make the error "Λ∗Λ = Λ·d". The error is in the **summary** (l. 19): "With full Euler,
a∗a = a·d is linear in a". There, "full Euler" refers to coefficients of a series with an Euler product (a_n completely
multiplicative), not to Λ. And "linear in a" is **not** "linear in Λ": a_N = ∏ a_p^{k} depends on Λ exponentially
(a = exp_∗(Λ/log)). Equally misleading is Theorem C (l. 21): "The J part is linear in the coefficients under
Euler". **Correction required in the summary and in Theorem C**: "(a) if a are coefficients of a series with a completely
multiplicative Euler product, the J part is Σ w(2πN)d(N)a_N, linear in a but not in Λ; (b) if a = Λ, the J part is
the quadratic form Σ_N w(2πN)(Λ∗Λ)(N), quadratic in Λ with native FE".

**Does branch (a) say anything about Λ?** With a_n = 1 (D = partial sum of ζ), Euler does not enter: the form is
Σ w(2πN)d(N), a fixed number. It says nothing about Λ. **SERIOUS if presented as a result about Λ.** CELL_001 does not do
this in the body, but Theorem C conflates the two branches. With variable completely multiplicative a (𝒰_mult, §7.1), the
form reads the a_p and hence the Λ(p^k) = a_p^k log p **only at primes and prime powers**, in a nonlinear polynomial way.
That branch is not "linear in Λ". CELL_001 §7.1 treats it as positivity over a variety, and that is correct.

**Branch (b), the one we call "exactly the cell".** Q_w(Λ) := 2π Σ_N w(2πN)(Λ∗Λ)(N) is:
1. native weight ½: it comes from D_Λ = Σ Λ(n)n^{−½−it};
2. native FE: it is the J component;
3. quadratic in Λ: it is a multiplicative Hankel form K(m,n) = w(2πmn), the same family of kernels as Helson (whose
   kernel is 1/(√(mn)log mn)). The parallel is exact: Helson has symbol N ↦ 1/(√N log N), and Lemma G has symbol
   N ↦ w(2πN)·√N·N^{−½} = w(2πN).

Three facts lower expectations:
- **w ≥ 0.** Λ∗Λ ≥ 0 pointwise, so Q_w(Λ) ≥ 0 at main order. It is trivial and is a positivity of non-negative
  coefficients. It is inherited by every a ≥ 0: a_P (Λ_P∗Λ_P ≥ 0), cΛ (c²Λ∗Λ), Beurling, DMV. By Theorem B (inheritable
  premise) **it is not a sign step of the node**. The index already records "Selberg identity is blind"
  (AUDIT_B l. 60, DMV) and [ΛΛ:Λ] (SPEC_SIGN_OBJECT_005). Chaining "Q_w ≥ 0 ⇒ node" is impossible: the
  premise holds at a_P with P > e^{2T}, where the node is false. **NO-GO, PROVED** (Theorem B + trivial positivity).
- **w of indefinite sign (the node's).** Q_w(Λ) is an evaluation: Σ w(2πN)(Λ∗Λ)(N) with w of both signs. There is no
  principle that gives it a sign. A multiplicative Hankel form is ⪰ 0 on every a only if its symbol is a multiplicative
  moment sequence (Helson/Nehari criterion); an indefinite w is not one. Positivity at the concrete vector Λ is a

### B.10 (O1) "non-local in t" and stability under J

The node lives in the variable x, with support in [−T,T]. In t, F̂ is entire of exponential type T: it is Paley–Wiener
PW_T. The compression to E_N is, in t, a sum of sincs of width ~1/T centered at ω_k: **local at scale 1/T** and not
local at finer scales. J: f(t) ↦ χ(½+it)f(−t). The reflection t → −t preserves PW_T. Multiplication by χ(½+it)
**does not** preserve it: χ(½+it) = e^{−2iθ(t)} has phase ~ −t log(t/2πe), which is not of finite exponential type. Its
transform in x is not compactly supported, and JF leaves the window [−T,T]. **The compression to the node is not
J-stable.** PROVED, because e^{−2iθ} is not a multiplier of PW_T (superlinear phase growth). This confirms CELL_001's
reading (O1) and pins down the reason: the FE as operator J and the node's window are incompatible. Every form
⟨·,J·⟩ restricted to the node is ⟨P_{PW_T}·, J·⟩, which is not a "native" form of the window. This is another version of
the band mismatch from §B.8.
