# Phase 46 — Audit and Attacks

This phase adversarially audits the main theorems of Phase 45 (local purity, the two-prime theorem, the Weil–Toeplitz trichotomy) and Phase 44's repaired logistic-signature criterion, then launches new direct attacks on the repulsion lemma LP-134 and on the semilocal gap GAP-135.B (destructive regime beyond the two-prime constructive case) and the quantitative localized positivity problem 136.A.

## Results
- Adversarial audit of the Local Purity Theorem (Doc 131, Thm. 6.7): full destroy-mandate protocol executed. (AUDIT — see document for the piece-by-piece verdict)
- Adversarial audit of the two-prime theorem (Doc 135): closed-form re-derivation of all central identities. (AUDIT)
- Adversarial audit of the logistic equivalence RH $\iff \lim\sigma_{\mathrm{loc}}^{(k)}=0$ (Doc 136): the logistic law itself survives exactly; the RH$\Rightarrow$limit-0 branch survives; the ¬RH$\Rightarrow$limit-½ branch dies as a "theorem with complete proof" (two real gaps — a false uniformity claim and unannihilated resonant pairs), downgraded to CONDITIONAL/OPEN, repairable in principle. (PARTIAL NO-GO — repairable gap identified)
- Adversarial audit of the Weil–Toeplitz algebra, visibility trichotomy, and Fredholm theorem (Doc 134): the C*-algebraic skeleton survives intact; the boundedness needed for the Weil instance of the field is an explicitly declared gap (GAP-134.R); construction B.4 survives as the most solid piece; the trichotomy is literally true but its [THEOREM] label oversells it (largely axiomatic). (AUDIT — mixed verdict, core survives, some claims downgraded)
- Attack on LP-134 (resolution/repulsion, "is there a lower bound forcing off-line zeros away from the critical line uniformly?"): reformulated to its correct, non-trivial form; proved equivalent to a strict hierarchy (RH $\Rightarrow$ finitely many off-line zeros $\Rightarrow$ LP-134); shown to be the weakest statement in the program's hierarchy and nonetheless still unreachable — all known density/statistical technology (Selberg, Jutila, vertical statistics) is proved structurally blind to it, and classical Deuring–Heilbronn-type repulsion is shown not to transpose (the relevant positivity lever vanishes identically on the critical line). (NO-GO — precisely diagnosed obstruction)
- Attack on GAP-135.B (destructive semilocal regime: $n$ primes with $a=\Lambda\ge0$): Axiom H fails for every finite prime set $S$, including $S=\varnothing$; a first semilocal positivity theorem is nonetheless proved on short windows ($2T\le1/150$); an explicit family shows strict negativity on wide low-frequency windows, with each added prime worsening the violation; Conjecture H⁺ survives only in refined/localized form, and deciding whether the semilocal object is ever spectral is left as GAP-142.B. (PROVED, both the failure and the restricted positivity theorem; OPEN for GAP-142.B)
- Attack on Problem 136.A (quantitative localized positivity modulus): the naive question is degenerate under RH (the signature is identically 0); reformulated in terms of the positivity modulus $\lambda_{\min}(p)$, for which a closed Gram–Vandermonde lower bound is proved in terms of zero-gaps, the Lehmer-pair degradation exponent is computed exactly (=2), and uniformity in height $T$ is shown to be exactly equivalent to a new vertical-repulsion statement LP-143, which GUE predicts to be false. (PROVED, conditional on RH for the main bound; the open status of LP-143 is reported, conjecturally false)
