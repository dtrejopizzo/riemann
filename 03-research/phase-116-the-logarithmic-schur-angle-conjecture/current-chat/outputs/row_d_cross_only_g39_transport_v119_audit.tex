\section{The old/new block does not contain the zeroth-order obstruction}

Let $H=H_0\oplus E$ be an orthogonal old/new decomposition and let
$P_0,E_0$ denote the two coordinate projection/inclusion maps.  Let $U$ be
a unitary translation on the ambient zero-extension line and put
\begin{equation}
 J_-=(U-I)/\sqrt2,\qquad J_+=(U+I)/\sqrt2.                    \tag{1}
\end{equation}

\begin{theorem}[Exact cross-only plus/minus identity --- \PROVED]
On the old/new block one has
\begin{align}
 P_0J_-^*J_-E_0&=-\frac12P_0(U+U^*)E_0,\notag\\
 P_0J_+^*J_+E_0&=+\frac12P_0(U+U^*)E_0,                     \tag{2}\\
 P_0(J_-^*J_--J_+^*J_+)E_0&=-P_0(U+U^*)E_0.                \tag{3}
\end{align}
If in addition $E_0^*(U+U^*)E_0=0$, then
\begin{equation}
 E_0^*J_-^*J_-E_0=E_0^*J_+^*J_+E_0=I_E.                   \tag{4}
\end{equation}
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Direct multiplication gives
\[
 J_-^*J_-=I-\tfrac12(U+U^*),\qquad
 J_+^*J_+=I+\tfrac12(U+U^*).
\]
Since $P_0E_0=0$, their old/new blocks are (2), and subtraction gives
(3).  Compression to $E$ gives (4) under the stated no-self-contact
condition.
\end{proof}

Thus the obstruction of v114, $P_0(U+U^*)P_0\ne0$, concerns the old/old
block.  It is absent from the zeroth coefficient of the old/new block
because $P_0E_0=0$.  A threshold proof does not require a unitary
identification of $J_-P_0$ with $J_+P_0$; it requires only (3).

\section{Arithmetic threshold fiberization}

Use
\[
 T_N=\tfrac12\log N,\qquad
 E_N=(-T_{N+1},-T_N)\cup(T_N,T_{N+1}),\qquad I_N=(-T_N,T_N).
\]
For $2\le n\le N$ let $U_n=S_{\log n}$ and
$c_n=\Lambda(n)/\sqrt n$.  Define
\begin{equation}
 \mathcal B_N^{\rm ar}
 :=-P_{I_N}\sum_{2\le n\le N}c_n(U_n+U_n^*)\big|_{L^2(E_N)}.
                                                                    \tag{5}
\end{equation}
Let
\begin{equation}
 \mathscr D_N=\ell^2\{2,\ldots,N\}\otimes L^2(E_N),\qquad
 \mathbf c_N=\sum_{n=2}^Nc_ne_n.                            \tag{6}
\end{equation}
The translated interval isometries of v111 give an isometry
\begin{equation}
 \mathfrak S_N:\mathscr D_N\longrightarrow L^2(I_N)         \tag{7}
\end{equation}
after separating the left and right newborn wings; its $n$th coordinate
is the unique orientation of $U_n+U_n^*$ which enters $I_N$.

\begin{theorem}[Physical realization of the G39 incidence column ---
\PROVED]
For every $e\in L^2(E_N)$,
\begin{equation}
 \boxed{\mathcal B_N^{\rm ar}e
 =-\mathfrak S_N(\mathbf c_N\otimes e).}                    \tag{8}
\end{equation}
Consequently
\begin{equation}
 (\mathcal B_N^{\rm ar})^*\mathcal B_N^{\rm ar}
 =\left(\sum_{n=2}^N\frac{\Lambda(n)^2}{n}\right)I_{E_N}.   \tag{9}
\end{equation}
Moreover the physical signed old/new block of the authoritative prime
factors is exactly (5), not merely Gram-equivalent to it.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
For a vector on the right newborn wing, only $U_n$ enters the old interval;
for a vector on the left wing, only $U_n^*$ enters it.  These are exactly
the $R_n$ and $L_n$ channels of v111, which are pairwise disjoint.  This
proves (8), and isometry gives (9).  Finally apply (3) to every $U_n$ and
sum with the fixed coefficients $c_n$.
\end{proof}

The label vector in (6) is the first logarithmic incidence column
$\mathcal C_N\delta_1$ of G39.  More generally, multiplication of labels by
the finite divisibility shift $S_m$ agrees, on every channel which remains
inside the threshold fiber, with physical translation by $\log m$.
Iteration gives the word columns
\begin{equation}
 (\log N)^{-k}\mathcal C_N^k\delta_1
 =\left\{\frac{\Lambda_k(n)}{\sqrt n(\log N)^k}\right\}_{n\le N}.
                                                                    \tag{10}
\end{equation}
This constructs the source map for the arithmetic cross identity.  It does
not identify the finite G39 old Gram with the complete infinite-rank
physical old Gram, which v94 correctly ruled out.

\section{What remains in the inverse metric}

Let $A_{00}>0$ be the already positive old primitive block.  Equations
(8)--(10) prove the physical realization of the load
$b_e^{\rm ar}=\mathcal B_N^{\rm ar}e$.  Its return energy is
\begin{equation}
 \langle A_{00}^{-1}b_e^{\rm ar},b_e^{\rm ar}\rangle.        \tag{11}
\end{equation}
A coefficient-space word norm bounds (11) only if the threshold
fiberization is contractive for the shorted old metric, namely if
\begin{equation}
 (\mathfrak S_N)^*A_{00}^{-1}\mathfrak S_N
 \le \mathfrak G_N                                          \tag{12}
\end{equation}
for a source Gram $\mathfrak G_N$ whose value on the Witt word vector is
the strict budget of v109--v110.  Equation (12), rather than a full
$J_-\to J_+$ intertwiner, is the correctly typed cross-place estimate.

The Gamma old/new cross of v112 and the conservative Tate terminal of v117
must be adjoined to both sides of (12).  Their source maps are explicit;
what is not yet proved is that their joint Gram dominates the shorted
physical inverse metric with unit constant.
