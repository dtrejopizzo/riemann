\section{Objective}

The requested objective has four clauses:
\begin{enumerate}
\item derive the contractivity
\begin{equation}
 \|\pi(\sqrt nU_n)\|\le1                              \tag{C}
\end{equation}
on every simple Loewy factor from rows (a)--(c);
\item construct the positive Hilbert completion with the Witt adjoint;
\item preserve the odd nuclear character, including multiplicities;
\item conclude row (d), condition $G$, without circularity.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Equivalence of the load-bearing clause}

On the simple factor with Mellin parameter $\rho$, row (c) gives
\begin{equation}
                 \pi(\sqrt nU_n)=n^{1/2-\rho}.        \tag{1}
\end{equation}
Consequently (C) is equivalent to $\Re\rho\ge1/2$.  Applying the same
inequality to the source-defined mirror $1-\bar\rho$ is equivalent to
$\Re\rho\le1/2$.  Therefore
\begin{equation}
 \boxed{\text{(C) on all simple factors}
 \iff \Re\rho=\tfrac12\text{ for all spectral parameters}.}  \tag{2}
\end{equation}
This equivalence is \PROVED.  It shows that (C) is the terminal theorem,
not a formal consequence of choosing the scalar normalization $n^{-1/2}$.

\section{Completion status}

Finite Loewy semisimplification and preservation of finite traces are
\PROVED in v99.  If (C) were proved, the simple factors would be unitary;
their Hilbert direct sum, repeated with Loewy multiplicity, would be trace
class for every test action and would have the same odd character.  This
conditional assembly is also \PROVED in v99.

What is not proved is the premise (C).  No source map in rows (a)--(c)
defines a positive norm on the simple Loewy factors, and every attempted
existing map either fails continuity, loses the quotient, fails the trace
comparison or leaves the explicit non-Tate residual displayed above.

\section{Terminal verdict}

The current files do not prove the requested objective.  Marking (C), row
(d), or $G$ as \PROVED would insert a missing norm-enriched fiber functor
whose one-dimensional assertion is already equation (2).  A valid
continuation requires new mathematical input constructing such a functor
or an independent proof of the one-sided half-plane bound.  Neither is
present in the supplied sources or in v89--v103.
