# 114.a.38 — I7 no-go: ordinary Witt scalar transport collapses to the diagonal

```
+--------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| SOURCE      B=Z_1 tensor_F Z_2 is the non-totally-commutative generic    |
|             algebra of Haran's square.                                   |
| TARGET      Every W_N is an ordinary commutative ring, hence a totally   |
|             commutative generalized ring.                               |
| THEOREM     Any map B -> G(W_N) induced by the two unital Z-maps sends   |
|             delta_1=delta_2 and factors through the fold B -> Z.         |
| GEOMETRY    Spec(W_N) -> Spec(B) lands on the diagonal; it cannot carry  |
|             Witt graphs into off-diagonal surface correspondences.       |
| CONSEQUENCE H7-WBASE must be a genuinely non-total kernel/functor, not   |
|             ordinary scalar base change.                                |
+--------------------------------------------------------------------------+
```

## 1. The only canonical scalar map

Let

\[
 B=\mathbb Z_1\underset{\mathbb F\{\pm1\}}\otimes\mathbb Z_2              \tag{1.1}
\]

be the generic non-totally-commutative algebra of Haran's literal square.
It is generated by the two addition vectors `delta_1,delta_2`.

For every finite Witt stage `W_N`, regard its ordinary commutative-ring
structure as a totally commutative generalized ring `G(W_N)`. There is only
one unital ordinary ring map

\[
 \iota:\mathbb Z\longrightarrow\mathcal W_N,
 \qquad n\longmapsto n\phi_1.                           \tag{1.2}
\]

Consequently the two maps from the coproduct factors are equal:

\[
 \iota_1=\iota_2=\iota.                                \tag{1.3}
\]

## 2. Diagonal factorization theorem

Let

\[
 \nabla:B\longrightarrow\mathbb Z                     \tag{2.1}
\]

be the fold induced by the identity on both factors.

### Theorem 2.1

The canonical map supplied by the coproduct universal property,

\[
 \psi_N:B\longrightarrow\mathcal G(\mathcal W_N),     \tag{2.2}
\]

factors uniquely as

\[
 \boxed{\quad\psi_N=\mathcal G(\iota)\circ\nabla.\quad}                  \tag{2.3}
\]

In particular,

\[
 \psi_N(\delta_1)=\psi_N(\delta_2).                    \tag{2.4}
\]

### Proof

Both sides of (2.3) restrict to the same map (1.2) on each coproduct factor.
The pushout universal property makes the induced map unique. Equation (2.4)
follows immediately. This is also the concrete content of Haran 2017,
Remark (7.17): total commutativity forces the two addition generators to
coincide and collapses `Z tensor_F Z` to `Z`. QED.

## 3. Geometric meaning

Contravariance turns (2.3) into

\[
 \mathrm{Spec}\,\mathcal W_N
 \longrightarrow\mathrm{Spec}\,\mathbb Z
 \xrightarrow{\Delta}\mathrm{Spec}\,B.             \tag{3.1}
\]

Thus the image lies on the diagonal of the generic arithmetic plane. After
compactification or passage to the pro-system, the same factorization holds
stagewise wherever the scalar map is used.

The two independent additions are precisely what produced the off-diagonal
entropy of `a_21`, the bounded mixed code of `a_30`--`a_35`, and the two
ruling divisor lattice of `a_19`. A map satisfying (2.4) cannot preserve any
of those distinctions.

### Corollary 3.1 (scalar H7-WBASE no-go)

Transporting the Witt graphs `G_{n,N}` of `a_37` to Haran's square through
an ordinary-ring/generalized-ring scalar morphism necessarily lands on the
diagonal. It cannot solve H7-WBASE or H7-I7-REAL.

This is independent of whether the graph fixed-point degrees are later
regularized: the loss occurs before intersection is defined.

## 4. The surviving transport type

Any viable H7-WBASE construction must retain `delta_1 != delta_2`. Therefore
it must use at least one genuinely non-totally-commutative object, for
example:

1. a bimodule/kernel correspondence rather than a scalar morphism;
2. a functor from Witt operator modules to completed sheaves on the Haran
   square that is not induced by an ordinary commutative target ring;
3. a new non-total Witt enhancement carrying two distinct integral maps.

Whichever type is chosen must still preserve graph composition, send the
cyclic vector to the diagonal, meet the prime-incidence carriers of `a_17`,
and satisfy H7-WLEF. Merely writing the two canonical copies of `Z` inside
an ordinary Witt ring cannot do so.

## 5. Verification scope

the equality of the two integer maps on finite Witt bases, the resulting
fold factorization and explicit loss of the two labels. It does not rule out
the non-total kernel/functor alternatives in Section 4.
