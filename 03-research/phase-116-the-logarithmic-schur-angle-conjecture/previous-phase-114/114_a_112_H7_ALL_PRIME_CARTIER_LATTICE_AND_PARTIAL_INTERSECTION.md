# 114.a.112 — H7: all-prime Cartier lattice and the exact anti-diagonal gate

```
+-------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| GENERATORS  One regular Cartier-act symbol D_(p,i) per prime and ruling. |
| AXES        Every single-ruling all-prime lattice embeds in Pic_cmp.     |
| KERNEL      Every possible two-ruling relation is prime anti-diagonal.   |
| CONTACT     Delta gives a geometric partial intersection sum m log p.    |
| LIMIT       Anti-diagonal Picard injectivity and all other products open.|
+-------------------------------------------------------------------------+
```

## 1. The full prime-generated presentation lattice

Let `Y^reg` be the repaired pro-square of `a109`--`a110`.  For every rational
prime `p` and ruling `i in {1,2}`, let

\[
 D_{p,i}=V^{\rm reg}_{p,i},\qquad
 \mathcal L_{p,i}=\mathcal L^{\rm reg}_{p,i}.                         \tag{1.1}
\]

The first object is a regular principal generalized Cartier datum and the
second is its completed inverse lattice.  Define the finite-support
presentation lattice

\[
 \mathfrak D_{\rm pr}
 =\bigoplus_p\bigl(\mathbb Z e_{p,1}\oplus\mathbb Z e_{p,2}\bigr)       \tag{1.2}
\]

and its completed Picard realization

\[
 \rho:\mathfrak D_{\rm pr}\longrightarrow
 \mathrm{Pic}_{\rm cmp}(Y^{\rm reg}),
 \qquad
 (m_{p,i})\longmapsto
 \bigotimes_{p,i}\mathcal L_{p,i}^{\otimes m_{p,i}}.                  \tag{1.3}
\]

Calling (1.2) a presentation lattice avoids assuming that all named Cartier
data are independent.  Determining the kernel of (1.3) is part of the
divisor-class problem.

## 2. The diagonal computes the only possible kernel

Diagonal pullback gives

\[
 \Delta^*\rho(m_{p,i})
 =\bigotimes_p L_p^{\otimes(m_{p,1}+m_{p,2})}.                         \tag{2.1}
\]

If `rho(m)=1`, the idelic degree and unique factorization imply

\[
 \sum_p(m_{p,1}+m_{p,2})\log p=0
 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad
 m_{p,1}+m_{p,2}=0\quad\text{for every }p.                             \tag{2.2}
\]

Therefore

\[
 \ker\rho\subseteq
 \mathfrak A:=\bigoplus_p\mathbb Z(e_{p,1}-e_{p,2}).                  \tag{2.3}
\]

### Theorem 2.1 (all-prime axis theorem)

Each restriction

\[
 \rho_i:\bigoplus_p\mathbb Z e_{p,i}longrightarrow
 \mathrm{Pic}_{\rm cmp}(Y^{\rm reg})                            \tag{2.4}
\]

is injective.  More generally, `rho` is injective on any sublattice whose
sets of primes used in the two rulings are disjoint.

### Proof

For one ruling, (2.2) says every coefficient is zero.  For disjoint prime
supports, each equation `m_(p,1)+m_(p,2)=0` has at most one nonzero term and
again forces it to vanish.  QED.

This strictly extends the distinct two-prime result `a111` to arbitrary
finite-support prime sets.

## 3. Exact residual anti-diagonal map

Define

\[
 \delta_{\rm pr}:\bigoplus_p\mathbb Z e_p
 \longrightarrow\mathrm{Pic}_{\rm cmp}(Y^{\rm reg}),
 \qquad
 (a_p)\longmapsto
 \bigotimes_p
 \left(\mathcal L_{p,1}\otimes\mathcal L_{p,2}^{-1}\right)^{\otimes a_p}.
                                                                         \tag{3.1}
\]

### Proposition 3.1

The full map `rho` is injective if and only if `delta_pr` is injective, and

\[
 \ker\rho=
 \left\{\sum_pa_p(e_{p,1}-e_{p,2}):a\in\ker\delta_{\rm pr}\right\}.     \tag{3.2}
\]

### Proof

Inclusion (2.3) says that every kernel element has the displayed unique
anti-diagonal form.  Substitution into (1.3) gives exactly (3.1).  QED.

Thus regular reflection removes the denominator obstruction but does not by
itself prove anti-diagonal Picard independence.  On the prime-generated
sector the exact target is injectivity of (3.1).  The later type correction
`a122` observes that H7-U3/H7-LD on the literal square is not sufficient after
closed restriction to `Y^reg`; one additionally needs H7-REFL-PIC, or directly
the two-coordinate H7-RULING-PF product formula on the repaired square.

## 4. A genuine partial intersection with the diagonal

The scheme-theoretic fiber products already proved after reflection are

\[
 \Delta^{\rm reg}\times_{Y^{\rm reg}}D_{p,i}
 \simeq\mathrm{Spec}\,\mathbb F_p.                                \tag{4.1}
\]

Let `Z Delta` denote the free group generated by the diagonal.  Bilinear
extension of the literal finite masses in (4.1) defines

\[
 I_\Delta:\mathbb Z\Delta\times\mathfrak D_{\rm pr}\longrightarrow
 \mathbb R,
 \qquad
 I_\Delta\!\left(k\Delta,\sum_{p,i}m_{p,i}e_{p,i}\right)
 =k\sum_p(m_{p,1}+m_{p,2})\log p.                                    \tag{4.2}
\]

This is a geometric **partial intersection pairing**: every generator value
comes from the actual finite fiber product (4.1), rather than from a chosen
quadratic form.  It vanishes on `ker rho` by (2.2), so it descends to the
prime-generated completed Picard image.

Its radical on the presentation lattice is exactly the real hyperplane
defined by the sum in (4.2); on integer finite-support vectors, unique
factorization sharpens this to

\[
 \ker I_\Delta(\Delta,-)=\mathfrak A.                                 \tag{4.3}
\]

Hence diagonal contact detects all single-axis divisors and loses exactly
the anti-diagonal sector.

## 5. Exact remaining intersection matrix

The construction determines only the `Delta` row/column:

\[
 I(\Delta,D_{p,i})=\log p.                                            \tag{5.1}
\]

It does not determine

\[
 I(\Delta,\Delta),\qquad
 I(D_{p,i},D_{q,j}),                                                   \tag{5.2}
\]

nor the archimedean Green contribution needed for principal invariance.
Ordinary fiber products for (5.2) exist, but Haran supplies no canonical
length/degree theory for their generally non-ordinary generalized schemes.
Consequently (4.2) must not be extended by declaring the missing entries.

The next exact gate is:

> **H7-REG-MIXDEG.** Construct a functorial degree/Euler characteristic for
> the zero-dimensional proper intersections of regular Cartier data on
> `Y^reg`, prove additivity and the product formula, and compute (5.2).

Until H7-REG-MIXDEG and the Green term are proved, H7-REG-INTER and row A
remain open.  No assertion of RH is made.

## 6. Verification scope

`114_a_112_h7_all_prime_cartier_lattice_verify.py` checks finite-support
valuation laws, the exact anti-diagonal kernel of diagonal pullback,
single-axis/disjoint-support injectivity, bilinearity and descent of the
partial contact pairing.  The geometric generator identity (4.1) is the
categorical theorem of `a109`, not a numerical inference.

**Later boundary reduction (`a128`--`a129`).**  The tempting global fraction
`p_2/p_1` cancels all finite valuations but does not trivialize the completed
object: its correction factors are bounded nonunits on the real chart.
Hence (2.3) is not promoted to equality.  The possible kernel is exactly the
kernel of the residual archimedean boundary classes H7-ARCH-BDRY.
