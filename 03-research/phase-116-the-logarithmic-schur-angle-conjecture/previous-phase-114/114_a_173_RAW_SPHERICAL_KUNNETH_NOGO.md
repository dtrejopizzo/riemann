# The raw spherical Kunneth dimension is exponentially large

## 1. Setup

For an integer `m>=1`, let

`H_m=(H Z)_[-m,m]`

be the bounded absolute section Gamma-set.  Put

`E_(m,n)=H_m smash_S H_n`.

We give `E_(m,n)(1_+)` the discrete tolerance relation and use the
Connes--Consani notion of `S`-dimension: the least cardinality of a subset
which generates every level-one element by an admissible zero--one sum.

Let

`B_s=(1,-2,4,...,(-2)^(s-1))`.

Write `M_s` for the maximum absolute value of a subset sum of `B_s` and
let `s(n)=max{s:M_s<=n}`.  Negabinary uniqueness says that the `2^s`
subset sums of `B_s` are distinct, and `s(n)=log_2(n)+O(1)`.

## 2. An exponentially large free assembly quotient

Take the element `1 in H_m(1_+)` and `B_s in H_n(s_+)`, where
`s=s(n)`.  For each subset `J subset {0,...,s-1}`, let

`v_J:1_+ smash s_+ -> 1_+`

retain precisely the pairs `(1,j)` with `j in J`.  The Day presentation
gives

`z_J=[1_+,s_+,v_J,1 smash B_s] in E_(m,n)(1_+)`.

Apply the canonical assembly map

`alpha:H Z smash_S H Z -> tilde(H Z) o H Z`.

Its explicit formula gives

`alpha(z_J)=[b_J]`,

where

`b_J=sum_(j in J)(-2)^j`.

At level `1_+`, the target contains the reduced free abelian group

`A=Z[Z]/Z[0]`.

For nonempty `J`, negabinary uniqueness makes the integers `b_J` nonzero
and pairwise distinct.  Hence the `2^s-1` elements `[b_J]` are distinct
basis vectors of `A` and are linearly independent.

## 3. Generator lower bound

Let `F subset E_(m,n)(1_+)` generate the level-one Gamma-set.  Every
`z_J` is an admissible zero--one sum of a subset of `F`.  Assembly is a
morphism of Gamma-sets, and its target is special, so the image of an
admissible sum is the ordinary sum of the images.  Therefore every
`[b_J]` belongs to the abelian subgroup generated by `alpha(F)`.

The rank of that subgroup is at most `#F`, whereas it contains the
`2^s-1` independent basis vectors above.  Thus

`dim_S E_(m,n) >= 2^(s(n))-1`.

This argument does not use a cardinality bound on a non-special Gamma-set;
it passes to a special additive quotient first, so multivalued lifts in the
source cannot weaken it.

### Theorem

For all `m>=1,n>=1`,

`dim_S(H_m smash_S H_n) >= 2^(s(n))-1`.

By symmetry the same bound holds with `m` and `n` exchanged.

If `n_t=floor(exp(t b))`, `b>0`, then

`2^(s(n_t))-1 = exp(t b+O(1))`.

Consequently the raw spherical smash cannot have surface growth `O(t^2)`
and its absolute dimension cannot equal the product of the two
one-dimensional absolute dimensions.

## 4. Consequence for strong row A

The Kunneth comparison of derived spherical objects may still exist as a
homotopical statement, but it cannot be combined with the raw
Connes--Consani generator dimension to produce the required quadratic
Riemann--Roch function.  The excess is intrinsic and is already detected
at level one by the canonical assembly map.

Thus the remaining construction must provide an internal separated
cohomology functor which removes this assembly-free exponential sector and
retains the canonical quadratic mixed block.  Calling the full raw smash
that cohomology would contradict the theorem above.
