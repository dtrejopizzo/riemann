## What changed materially

The carrier and section theory are no longer related only by prime labels.
The 2026 arithmetic pullback supplies a canonical map whose fibers are the
periodic curves, while the spherical square supplies actual invertible
divisor modules and a genuine derived global-section functor.  Exact
arithmetic correspondences now live in a finite-type module over a nuclear
coefficient algebra, which is the coherent resolution of the rank
contradiction.

## The remaining critical theorem

Let `C(D)` denote the published one-dimensional adelic spherical
cohomology object.  The unresolved statement is

`C(D) derived-smash_S C(E)
  -> R Gamma_S(Y_S,L(D,E))`

being an equivalence in a class on which an Euler dimension is defined and
multiplicative.  A proof must also account for the nonzero positive homotopy
of `H Z derived-smash_S H Z`; those terms cannot be deleted because their
point-set precursor is exactly what makes the square noncollapsed.

This is a narrower and better-typed problem than the former request for an
unknown valued structure sheaf.  It is nevertheless a genuine theorem
still to be proved.  The word "complete" is reserved until this theorem and
the resulting determinant comparison are established.
