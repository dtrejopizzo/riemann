# D.29 — The logarithmic cyclotomic audit and the Kummer-motive pivot

## 1. Why the logarithmic pair is the correct first repair

Replace the proper genus-zero curve by the logarithmic pair

```text
(P1_Z, {0,infinity}).
```

Its generic generalized Jacobian is `G_m`, so the objection “P1 has no
degree one” is no longer literally true.  Arithmetic Chow rings with
log--log Green forms along a normal-crossing divisor are available in the
theory of Burgos Gil--Kramer--Kuehn.  Thus the compactification and its
singular metrics can be typed without inventing a new intersection ring.

## 2. Torsion test

The cyclotomic divisor `Phi_n=0` is supported on roots of unity.  Every
root of unity is a torsion point of `G_m`.  Any canonical quadratic height
on a polarized group, when defined, vanishes on torsion:

```text
N zeta_n=0  =>  N^2 hhat(zeta_n)=hhat(N zeta_n)=0.
```

Consequently a Hodge sign obtained solely from the generalized-Jacobian
height cannot retain the nonzero local number

```text
log |Res(Phi_m,Phi_n)| / phi(n) = log p
```

when `m/n` is a power of `p`.  On the complete arithmetic pair, these
finite local resultants must be cancelled by the boundary/Green
contribution for the corresponding torsion class.

### Conclusion

The log pair repairs the missing toric degree one but does not give the
desired global contact.  Its canonical torsion height kills exactly the
cyclotomic classes whose finite intersections produce `Lambda`.

This is stronger than the genus-zero objection of 106.210: even after
adding the correct generalized Jacobian, the automatic global height is
too small.

## 3. Kummer 1-motives retain the required period

For every prime `p`, consider the Kummer 1-motive

```text
M_p=[ Z -> G_m ],            1 |-> p.                (3.1)
```

Unlike a root of unity, `p` is not torsion in `G_m`.  Its real regulator
is

```text
reg_infinity(M_p)=log p.                             (3.2)
```

The tensor power recording `p^k` retains the full torsor length `k log p`,
while the reduced derived contact can retain one copy of the Kummer
extension, hence `log p`.  This is precisely the separation already built
in rows A--B:

```text
T_(p^k)  remembers k log p,
C_(p^k)  has determinant mass log p.                 (3.3)
```

Thus the Kummer object is not an unrelated replacement; it is a motivic
lift of the metric torsor already present in the programme.

## 4. Candidate mixed category

Let `Kum_Q` be the exact tensor category generated by

```text
Q(0), Q(1), {M_p}_p,
```

their duals, finite extensions, and the Gamma/polar real object.  For a
finite prime-power support `S`, form the two-ruling object

```text
H_S = direct_sum_(p^k in S)
      (M_p^(k) boxplus M_p^(k), Tate-conjugate copy),
```

but apply the first Eulerian idempotent only to the contact character, not
to the tensor/Rosati product.  This preserves multiplicative composition
and removes disconnected mixed-prime contributions at the trace level.

The desired polarization must be a biextension metric

```text
< , >_Kum : H_S x H_S -> R                           (4.1)
```

with the following independently checkable properties:

1. `M_p` has regulator norm `log p`;
2. distinct primes are orthogonal in the reduced local contact;
3. the two Tate rulings form one hyperbolic plane;
4. the orthogonal primitive complement is negative;
5. Gamma realization supplies the exact real local term;
6. realization to the Witt/nuclear correspondence sends the Eulerian
   contact to `Lambda(n)/sqrt(n)`.

If (4.1)--(4.6) are proved inside the 1-motive category before nuclear
realization, Hodge is independent and the B--C comparison closes D.

## 5. Exact new gate

Mixed Hodge structures and arithmetic Chow groups provide biextension
heights, but they do not automatically provide the required definiteness
on a semiabelian/mixed-Tate extension.  The new force-bearing theorem is:

> **Kummer--Rosati theorem.** The completed biextension pairing on the
> primitive restricted product of the Kummer 1-motives (3.1), after joint
> Gamma--polar renormalization, is negative semidefinite, with zero only on
> principal classes.

This theorem is not asserted here.  Its advantage over the previous gates
is that all local classes are nontorsion, the number `log p` is an actual
regulator before comparison, and the category has duality and
biextensions.  The next audit must determine whether an existing
polarization theorem for 1-motives proves it, or whether the completed
restricted product introduces a new unproved sign.
