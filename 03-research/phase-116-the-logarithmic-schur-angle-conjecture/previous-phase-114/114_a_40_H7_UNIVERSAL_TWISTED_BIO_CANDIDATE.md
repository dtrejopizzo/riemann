## 1. Two ring bios with one multiplicative monoid

Let `R_1` be the ordinary real field and `R_u` the transported field of
`a_39`. Their underlying sets, zero, unit, multiplication and identity
involution coincide; only their additions differ.

Haran 2022, (2.13)--(2.15), associates to each involutive ring its
involutive bio

\[
 \mathcal P_1:=\mathcal P_{\mathbb R_1},
 \qquad \mathcal P_u:=\mathcal P_{\mathbb R_u}.         \tag{1.1}
\]

Both contain the signed field-with-one-element bio `F`, because they share
`0,1,-1` and multiplication.

## 2. The universal quotient

The categories of commutative bios and their involutive versions are
cocomplete (Haran 2022, after (4.8)); in particular pushouts and
coequalizer quotients exist. Form

\[
 \mathcal C_u
 :=\mathcal P_1\boxtimes_{\mathbb F}\mathcal P_u.      \tag{2.1}
\]

Write `j_1,j_u` for its two structural maps. On unary operations impose the
relations

\[
 j_1(x)\sim j_u(x)\qquad(x\in\mathbb R),                \tag{2.2}
\]

where `x` denotes the same element of the common multiplicative real set,
not the integer generated by the two different additions. Let

\[
 \mathcal Q_u:=\mathcal C_u/\!\sim                     \tag{2.3}
\]

be the smallest involutive-bio congruence containing (2.2).

### Proposition 2.1 (formal existence)

`Q_u` is a well-defined commutative involutive bio. It contains a canonical
common unary monoid map

\[
 \eta_u:(\mathbb R,\cdot)\longrightarrow\mathcal Q_u(1),                  \tag{2.4}
\]

and two integral bio maps whose binary addition generators act, on the
image of `eta_u`, by

\[
 \eta_u(x)+_{(1)}\eta_u(y)=\eta_u(x+y),
 \qquad
 \eta_u(x)+_{(2)}\eta_u(y)=\eta_u(x+_{(2,u)}y).         \tag{2.5}
\]

### Proof

Existence and all higher-arity bio axioms follow from the pushout and
coequalizer in the stated category. The unary identifications are
multiplicatively and involutively stable because both fields have the same
multiplication and identity involution. Equations (2.5) are the images of
the defining addition relations in the two ring bios. QED.

This removes the higher-arity construction ambiguity left in `a_39`.
However, a quotient can identify additional unary elements or even collapse
`0` and `1`; formal existence alone does not provide a useful model.

## 3. The exact unary normal-form theorem

The assertion isolated here was:

> **H7-UEMB(u).** The map `eta_u` in (2.4) is injective.

Theorem 4.1 of `a_49` proves H7-UEMB(u) for every `u>0` by mapping `Q_u` to
the involutive double of the homogeneous endomorphism bio and separating
scalar multiplications by evaluation at `1`.

### Theorem 3.1

If H7-UEMB(u) holds, then `Q_u` realizes H7-TBIO at the parameter `u`.
For `u!=1`, its two addition generators are distinct.

### Proof

All required bio structure already exists by Proposition 2.1. If the two
generators were equal, apply them to `(eta_u(1),eta_u(1))`. By (2.5),

\[
 \eta_u(2)=\eta_u(2^u).                                \tag{3.1}
\]

Injectivity and `u!=1` contradict (3.1). Thus the bio is non-total and has
the scalar laws of `a_39`. QED.

### Corollary 3.2

If H7-UEMB(u) holds for every `u>0`, then all power evaluations descend,
H7-LNF holds by `a_26`, and H7-FMD holds by `a_33`.

Thus the former request to invent all operations of a twisted bio is reduced
to one concrete normal-form statement about the unary part of the explicit
quotient (2.3).

## 4. Boundary cases and nontrivial evidence

For `u=1`, the two fields and their ring bios coincide. The fold is a
retraction from (2.3) to `P_R` and is the identity on the common unary real
copy. Therefore `eta_1` has a left inverse and H7-UEMB(1) holds. This does
not require, and does not assert, that the entire higher-arity bio `Q_1` is
isomorphic to `P_R`.

For `u!=1`, neither the ordinary fold nor the scalar transport of `a_38` is
a retraction. `a_49` supplies the second admissible route listed in the
original audit: a representation whose unary action separates real scalars.

## 5. Geometric scope

H7-DYNAMIC-LIFT or H7-WLEF. The quotient `Q_u` is a non-total scalar target;
one still needs a compatible dynamic kernel on the pro-square and an
intersection/RR theory.
