## 1. The prime Cartier-act generators

Let `p != q` be rational primes and let `Y^reg` be the reflected pro-square
constructed in `a110`.  Write

\[
 D_{p,1}=V^{\rm reg}_{p,1},\qquad D_{q,2}=V^{\rm reg}_{q,2}.              \tag{1.1}
\]

Every nonzero integer is regular on every chart and operation arity of
`Y^reg`.  Therefore `a67`, applied after reflection, identifies each object
in (1.1) with a regular principal generalized Cartier datum.  Its principal
ideal is an invertible right act, and its inverse completed lattice is

\[
 \mathcal L^{\rm reg}_{p,1},\qquad \mathcal L^{\rm reg}_{q,2}.           \tag{1.2}
\]

Define the two-prime Cartier-act group to be the formal group

\[
 \operatorname{Div}^{\rm act}_{p,q}(Y^{\rm reg})
 :=\mathbb Z D_{p,1}\oplus\mathbb Z D_{q,2}.                              \tag{1.3}
\]

Its positive monoid consists of the effective formal sums with nonnegative
coefficients.  This is a subgroup generated by actual regular Cartier data;
it is not a definition of all Weil or Cartier divisors on `Y^reg`.

## 2. Faithful realization in completed Picard classes

There is a homomorphism

\[
 \rho_{p,q}:\operatorname{Div}^{\rm act}_{p,q}(Y^{\rm reg})
 \longrightarrow \operatorname{Pic}_{\rm cmp}(Y^{\rm reg}),
 \quad
 mD_{p,1}+nD_{q,2}\longmapsto
 (\mathcal L^{\rm reg}_{p,1})^{\otimes m}\otimes
 (\mathcal L^{\rm reg}_{q,2})^{\otimes n}.                              \tag{2.1}
\]

### Theorem 2.1

The map `rho_(p,q)` is injective.

### Proof

Suppose that the class on the right of (2.1) is trivial.  Pull it back along
the surviving diagonal `Delta^reg`.  By `a109`--`a110` this gives on the
arithmetic curve

\[
 L_p^{\otimes m}\otimes L_q^{\otimes n}\simeq\mathcal O_X.              \tag{2.2}
\]

The idelic degree computed in `a18` gives

\[
 m\log p+n\log q=0.                                                       \tag{2.3}
\]

Exponentiating yields `p^m q^n=1` in `Q^*`.  Unique factorization and
`p != q` imply `m=n=0`.  Hence the kernel is zero.  QED.

Thus the abstract two-prime bigrade of `a19` is now realized by completed
lattices attached to regular Cartier-act data on the repaired square.  The
conditional completion caveat in `a19` is removed for this reflected space.

## 3. Diagonal-contact degree

The reflected quotient retains the exact fiber products

\[
 \Delta^{\rm reg}\times_{Y^{\rm reg}}D_{r,i}
 \simeq\operatorname{Spec}\mathbb F_r,
 \qquad r\in\{p,q\}.                                                     \tag{3.1}
\]

Consequently the literal finite masses of `a17` define an additive
homomorphism on (1.3):

\[
 \deg_\Delta^{\rm ct}
   (mD_{p,1}+nD_{q,2})=m\log p+n\log q.                                  \tag{3.2}
\]

This homomorphism agrees with completed-lattice degree after diagonal
pullback and is injective on the chosen rank-two group.  In particular the
two generators have the geometrically forced values

\[
 \deg_\Delta^{\rm ct}(D_{p,1})=\log p,
 \qquad
 \deg_\Delta^{\rm ct}(D_{q,2})=\log q.                                  \tag{3.3}
\]

The notation `ct` is essential: (3.2) is the linear extension of proved
diagonal contact masses.  It is **not** obtained by applying an already
constructed bilinear intersection product.

## 4. What is closed and what remains

This closes a precise prime-generated part of G-7 on the live repaired route:

1. a genuine free rank-two group generated by regular effective Cartier-act
   data;
2. its faithful realization by completed fraction lattices;
3. an intrinsic diagonal-contact degree agreeing with `log p` incidence.

It does not construct the full divisor group of `Y^reg`, products between
arbitrary divisors, self-intersections, a moving lemma, dynamic cycles, the
selective Riemann--Roch theorem or the Green gauge.  In particular, assigning
multiplicity `k` to `D_p` gives `k log p`; it must not be confused with the
von Mangoldt label `Lambda(p^k)=log p`, whose dynamic realization remains
H7-DYNAMIC-LIFT.

Therefore H7-REG-DIV is closed only on this two-prime subgroup and
H7-REG-INTER remains open.  Row A and RH are not claimed.

**Later extension (`a112`).**  The same argument controls every finite set
of primes on either ruling, and any two disjoint prime supports across the
rulings.  It also identifies the exact residual presentation kernel as
prime-by-prime anti-diagonal and promotes the contact degree to a geometric
