# D.78 — Closure of the complete first prime cell by nesting

Let \(T_3=\frac12\log3\).  D.77 proves

\[
 QW_{T_3}(F,F)>0.00011599\|F\|_2^2                    \tag{1}
\]

on the exact two-Tate-moment primitive subspace.  For \(0<T\le T_3\),
extension by zero

\[
 E_{T,T_3}:L^2([-T,T])\longrightarrow L^2([-T_3,T_3]) \tag{2}
\]

is isometric and preserves both Tate moments exactly.  Every term of the
source form is a global translation correlation or Gamma translation
energy; hence

\[
 QW_T(F,F)=QW_{T_3}(E_{T,T_3}F,E_{T,T_3}F).             \tag{3}
\]

At \(T_3\), shifts \(\log n\ge\log3\) have disjoint supports up to a null
set.  Thus the only nonzero finite contact is \(n=2\), exactly the contact
retained by D.77.  Applying (1) to (3) proves

\[
 \boxed{
 QW_T(F,F)>0.00011599\|F\|_2^2
 \quad(0<T\le\tfrac12\log3)
 }                                                       \tag{4}
\]

for every nonzero primitive \(F\).

This exact restriction argument replaces interval propagation throughout
the first prime cell.  D.59, D.63, D.65 and D.66 remain independent local
cross-checks.  The statement does not cover support windows beyond
\(\frac12\log3\), where the \(n=3\) contact has positive overlap.
