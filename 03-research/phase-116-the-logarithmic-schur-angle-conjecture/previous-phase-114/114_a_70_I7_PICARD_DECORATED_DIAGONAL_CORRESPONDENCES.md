## 1. The decorated span category

For generalized schemes carrying the unit-torsor theory of `a_66`, define a
Picard-decorated span from `X` to `X` to be

\[
 \Gamma=(X\mathop{\longleftarrow}^{\ell}Z
             \mathop{\longrightarrow}^{r}X;\,T),
 \qquad T\in Pic_{tor}(Z).                                             \tag{1.1}
\]

Two are isomorphic when an isomorphism of middle schemes commutes with both
legs and identifies the torsors.  Compose

\[
 (Z,T)\circ(W,U)=
 \left(Z\times_XW,\;\mathrm{pr}_Z^*T\otimes
                         \mathrm{pr}_W^*U\right).                \tag{1.2}
\]

Associativity follows from associativity of fiber products, torsor pullback
and tensor product.  The identity is `(X,id,id;1)`.  Thus (1.1)--(1.2)
define a bicategory, or an ordinary category after passing to isomorphism
classes.  This construction uses only finite limits and `Pic_tor` pullback,
both already available.

## 2. The faithful arithmetic kernels

Let `T_n` be the faithful torsor family of `a_61`/`a_66`. Define

\[
 \Gamma_n:=left(X\mathop{\longleftarrow}^{id}X
                    \mathop{\longrightarrow}^{id}X;T_n\right).        \tag{2.1}
\]

As an object over `X times_S X`, its carrier is the diagonal and its torsor
is the arithmetic decoration.

### Theorem 2.1 (dynamic monoid)

There are canonical coherent isomorphisms

\[
 \Gamma_m\circ\Gamma_n\simeq\Gamma_{mn},\qquad
 \Gamma_1\simeq\mathrm{id}_X,                                  \tag{2.2}
\]

and `n -> [Gamma_n]` is injective.

### Proof

The middle fiber product in (1.2) is `X times_X X=X`.  Its decoration is
`T_m tensor T_n`, canonically `T_mn`.  The associativity and unit diagrams
are those of the Picard tensor product.  If `Gamma_m~=Gamma_n`, the identity
legs force an automorphism over `X` identifying `T_m` and `T_n`; in
particular their Picard-torsor classes agree.  Faithfulness from `a_66`
gives `m=n`.  QED.

Since tensoring unit torsors is symmetric, this family represents the
commutative monoid `N^x`, unlike the undecorated prime-ruling spans ruled out
in `a_48`.

## 3. Contact as a monoidal shadow

On the monoidal subcategory generated by the `Gamma_p`, define

\[
 \mathrm{Cont}(\Gamma_n):=\mathcal P_n,                         \tag{3.1}
\]

using the contact sheaves of `a_45`--`a_46`.  Their exact tensor law gives

\[
 \mathrm{Cont}(\Gamma_m\circ\Gamma_n)
 \simeq\mathrm{Cont}(\Gamma_m)\otimes
       \mathrm{Cont}(\Gamma_n),                                \tag{3.2}
\]

so `Cont` is symmetric monoidal on this arithmetic kernel monoid.  It is
deliberately nonfaithful: all powers of one prime have contact `F_p`, and
mixed-prime labels have zero contact.

Under H7-PRIME-REG, `a_67` identifies `T_p` with the completed lattice of the
principal ruling `V_p`.  Independently, `a_68`--`a_69` construct the global
cotangent conormal and split its `F_p[1]` contact canonically from the excess.
Thus, conditional only for the completed-lattice comparison, (3.1) is the
reduced derived diagonal contact attached to the decoration, and

\[
 \log\#\Gamma\mathrm{Cont}(\Gamma_n)=\Lambda(n)\qquad(n>1).    \tag{3.3}
\]

Here `P_1=Z` is the tensor unit and is distinct from the zero module occurring
for mixed-prime labels. The abstract monoidal functor (3.1) itself is
unconditional.

## 4. Relation to the ruling-span no-go

`a_48` proves that the spans carried by `V_p=x_p times_S X` cannot themselves
give a faithful commutative representation: their left endpoints retain the
ordered prime.  The present construction changes the carrier:

- `Gamma_p` is the diagonal identity span decorated by `T_p`;
- `V_p` is the divisor/contact geometry associated to that decoration once
  the completed comparison is available.

Therefore no assertion of `a_48` is contradicted.  The carrier provides
composition; the decoration provides arithmetic faithfulness; the ruling
provides the finite contact shadow.

## 5. Exact achievement and limitation

This closes H7-PIC-TO-DYNAMIC in the following explicit sense:

> **I7-DYN-TOR.** There is a genuine category of geometric decorated spans
> containing a faithful symmetric monoidal family `Gamma_n` and a symmetric
> monoidal contact functor with mass `Lambda(n)`.

It does not prove that the undecorated supports are distinct algebraic cycles.
Indeed forgetting `T_n` sends every `Gamma_n` to the same diagonal.  Hence a
requirement that `Gamma_n` be an undecorated Chow-type cycle remains open and
is strictly stronger than I7-DYN-TOR.

It also does not supply the H7 global section gauge or Riemann--Roch theorem.

## 6. Verification scope

`114_a_70_i7_decorated_diagonal_verify.py` checks valuation-torsor
faithfulness, associative decorated composition, the exact contact tensor
law, collisions after contact and the `Lambda(n)` mass.  The categorical
construction (1.1)--(1.2) is proved by universal properties above.

**Later no-go (`a133`).**  Replacing the decoration by an integer
multiplicity `k(n)Delta` cannot work: convolution makes `k` completely
multiplicative, while bilinear diagonal intersection can never equal
`Lambda(p)=Lambda(p^2)`.  Any undecorated replacement must therefore use a
genuine thickening/derived structure or moving support.
