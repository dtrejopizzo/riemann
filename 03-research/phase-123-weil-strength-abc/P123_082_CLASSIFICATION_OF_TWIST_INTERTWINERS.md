# P123.082 — Classification of the full twisted-bimodule intertwiners

Date: 2026-09-06. Status: PROVED for the repaired coefficient-unitized
rapid algebra with nonempty S_f. This identifies a limitation of the
stable twist-only category, not of every possible mixed source category.

Let a,b be invertible external ideles acting by coefficient dilations,
and let v=sum_gamma v_gamma U_gamma satisfy

    beta_a(c)v=v beta_b(c) for every c in A.

Take c=f in C_0(X_S). Comparing label gamma gives

    f(a^-1 x)v_gamma(x)
             =v_gamma(x)f(b^-1 gamma^-1 x) for all x and f.

At any x where v_gamma(x)!=0, separation of points by C_0 functions
forces a^-1 x=b^-1 gamma^-1 x. If v_gamma is nonzero, its nonvanishing
set is open. Points with every coordinate nonzero are dense in X_S,
so that open set contains such a point. Coordinatewise cancellation
then forces a=gamma b. Hence all coefficients vanish unless a b^-1
is a diagonal positive S-unit; in that case only its unique label gamma
can occur.

Write v=h U_gamma in that case. Both external automorphisms fix every
U_delta, so the intertwining identity with c=U_delta gives

    alpha_delta(h)=h for all delta in Gamma_S.

Every such invariant h in C_0(X_S)+C1 is constant. To prove this, write
h=f+c with f in C_0(X_S), and choose an integer prime p in S_f. For
any x with nonzero real coordinate, p^n x leaves every compact subset
as n tends to infinity, because its real coordinate is unbounded.
Invariance gives f(x)=f(p^n x), whose right side tends to zero.
The nonzero-real-coordinate set is dense, so f=0 everywhere.

Consequently

    Hom_(A-A)(E_beta_a,E_beta_b)
      =C U_gamma if a=gamma b for gamma in Gamma_S,
      =0 otherwise.                            (1)

The converse follows from beta_(gamma b)=Ad(U_gamma) beta_b. These
are topological identifications: the nonzero Hom is a one-dimensional
closed subspace with its usual topology. The same conclusion holds
for the integrable unital subalgebra of P123.053, since its coefficients
embed in the same C_0-plus-constants algebra and it contains the same
U_gamma and sufficiently many compact coefficient tests.

Thus after choosing one lift of each external class, the additive
category generated by the twists is a finite-support family of finite
dimensional vector spaces indexed by external classes: different classes
have no morphisms, and each endomorphism algebra of a single generator
is C. In the bounded complex category, differentials are ordinary finite
complex matrices within each class. Each such complex splits as its
cohomology and a contractible complex by finite-dimensional linear
algebra. Therefore taking bounded cones of these twists does not create
new indecomposable mixed objects beyond shifts and finite sums of the
original external-class generators.

This corrects the possible interpretation of P123.064–074: their dg,
cyclic and kernel constructions are valid on the specified sector,
but stability alone did not produce the geometrically mixed sector
required by A.2, A.6 and A.8. A faithful kernel realization of individual
twists does not change the classification (1) of their bimodule morphisms.

To continue toward full A, the source category must contain additional
noninvertible/localized bimodules or geometric kernels, with their
composition and trace proved. Merely adding more external labels,
completing the scalar Hom spaces, or taking more cones in the present
sector cannot supply that enlargement. This is a specific proved
restriction on the current candidate, not a proof that a mixed source
cannot exist.
