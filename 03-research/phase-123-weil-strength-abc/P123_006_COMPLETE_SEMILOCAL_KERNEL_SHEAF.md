## 1. Why the completion changes the coefficient space

The imported algebraic sheaf has sections

```text
S(A_S) rtimes Gamma_S.
```

An infinite rapidly decreasing sum of rational dilations of a compactly
supported nonarchimedean function need not remain compactly supported.
Consequently the infinite label completion cannot be asserted inside the
Bruhat--Schwartz space.

The natural complete ambient coefficient algebra is instead

```text
B_S=C_0(A_S),
```

with the supremum norm.  Bruhat--Schwartz functions form a dense
subalgebra of `B_S`, and every rational scaling acts isometrically.

## 2. Rapid crossed-product completion

Let `Gamma_S` be the positive `S`-unit group and let

```text
alpha_q(f)(x)=f(q^-1 x).
```

Define

```text
K_S^rapid={F=(f_q)_(q in Gamma_S):
           ||F||_k=sum_q ||f_q||_infinity H(q)^k<infinity
           for every k>=0}.                           (2.1)
```

The crossed-product operations are

```text
(F star G)_t=sum_(qr=t) f_q alpha_q(g_r),              (2.2)
(F^dagger)_q=alpha_q(conjugate(f_(q^-1))).             (2.3)
```

### Theorem 2.1

`K_S^rapid` is a complete Frechet dagger algebra and

```text
||F star G||_k<=||F||_k ||G||_k,
||F^dagger||_k=||F||_k.                               (2.4)
```

The algebraic crossed product `S(A_S) rtimes_alg Gamma_S` is dense in it.

### Proof

The action `alpha_q` is isometric for the supremum norm.  Combining this
with `H(qr)<=H(q)H(r)`, Tonelli gives (2.4) for multiplication.  Inversion
preserves the height and `alpha` is an action, which proves (2.3), the
anti-multiplicative dagger law and its isometry.  The projective limit of
the Banach crossed-product spaces with norms `||-||_k` is complete and
Frechet.

To prove density, first truncate the label height.  The discarded tail
tends to zero in every fixed weighted norm.  Only finitely many
coefficients remain, and each can be approximated uniformly by a
Bruhat--Schwartz function on the finite product of local fields `A_S`.
A diagonal choice of truncation and approximation converges in every
Frechet seminorm.  QED.

No nuclearity is claimed for `K_S^rapid`: the Banach coefficient algebra
`C_0(A_S)` is not a nuclear Frechet space in general.  Nuclearity belongs
to the label algebra and to the later trace-class subcategory, not to every
ambient kernel.

## 3. Multiplier correspondences and the diagonal

For `q in Gamma_S`, let `U_q` be the multiplier determined by

```text
U_q(f_r U_r)=alpha_q(f_r)U_(qr),
(f_r U_r)U_q=f_r U_(rq).                              (3.1)
```

Then

```text
U_q U_r=U_(qr),
U_q^dagger=U_(q^-1),
U_1=1_M,                                               (3.2)
```

where `1_M` is the identity of the multiplier algebra.  It is not an
element of `K_S^rapid`, because the constant function `1` is not in
`C_0(A_S)`; this is the standard distinction between an identity
correspondence and the trace/test ideal.

Every `c in C_S` from P123.005 defines the strictly convergent multiplier

```text
U(c)=sum_q c_q U_q.                                    (3.3)
```

Indeed left and right multiplication satisfy the same weighted `l^1`
bounds as (2.4).  Equations (3.2)--(3.3) give a continuous dagger-algebra
map from `C_S` to the multiplier algebra of `K_S^rapid`.

Thus the internal presentation category with one object, trace ideal
`K_S^rapid` and gauge-multiplier arrows generated by the `U_q` has:

* an actual identity/diagonal `U_1`;
* associative composition;
* source-defined transpose `U_q^dagger=U_(q^-1)`;
* a complete two-sided test-kernel ideal.

## 4. Strict Zariski descent

For `S subset S'`, write

```text
A_(S')=A_S x product_(v in S' minus S) Q_v,
R_(S',S)=product_(v in S' minus S) Z_v.
```

Define restriction by

```text
f_q |-> (f_q tensor 1_R(S',S)),
Gamma_S -> Gamma_(S').                                (4.1)
```

The map on `C_0` is an isometric algebra embedding.  It intertwines the
action because every `q in Gamma_S` is a unit at the newly added places,
so `alpha_q(1_R)=1_R`.

### Theorem 4.1

The assignment

```text
U_S |-> K_S^rapid                                      (4.2)
```

is a strict sheaf of Frechet dagger algebras.  The multipliers `U_q` and
the label actions of P123.005 satisfy descent on every chart where the
label is a unit.

### Proof

Embed all terms for a finite cover in the algebra for
`T=union_i S_i`.  Unique expansion in the rational group labels reduces
compatibility to each coefficient separately.  The common labels are

```text
intersection_i Gamma_(S_i)=Gamma_(intersection_i S_i)=Gamma_S.
```

For such a label, the intersection of the coefficient images

```text
C_0(A_(S_i)) -> C_0(A_T)
```

consists exactly of functions pulled up from `A_S` and multiplied by the
characteristic functions of all added maximal compact factors.  This is
checked one new place at a time by slicing: membership in the image says
that the function vanishes outside `Z_v` and is constant in the `v`
coordinate on `Z_v`.  Imposing all cover conditions leaves precisely the
common base variables and all required compact factors.

The maps are isometric in every `||-||_k`; hence the algebraic equalizer is
a topological equalizer and the gluing map is a homeomorphism.  Zariski
quasi-compactness reduces arbitrary covers to finite covers.  Formulas
(2.2)--(2.3) and (3.1) commute with restriction.  QED.

## 5. Covariant representation and diagonal distribution

On

```text
H_S=L^2(A_S,dx_S)
```

let

```text
(V_q xi)(x)=|q|_S^(-1/2) xi(q^-1 x).                  (5.1)
```

Then `V_q` is unitary and

```text
V_q M_f V_q^-1=M_(alpha_q(f)).                        (5.2)
```

The formula `fU_q |-> M_f V_q` is therefore a covariant
representation.  A faithful representation of the completed crossed
product is obtained by the standard regular covariant representation on
`l^2(Gamma_S) tensor H_S`; the group is abelian and hence amenable.

For `q!=1` and `f in S(A_S)`, the half-density operator has distributional
kernel

```text
k_(f,q)(x,y)=f(x)|q|_S^(-1/2) delta(y-q^-1 x).
```

Restriction to the diagonal gives the source-defined local distribution

```text
Tr_dist,S(fU_q)
 =|q|_S^(-1/2) f(0)/|1-q^-1|_S
 =|q|_S^(1/2) f(0)/|q-1|_S.                          (5.3)
```

This is the ordinary fixed-point Jacobian calculation on the additive
semilocal transverse space.  At `q=1`, the fixed locus is all of `A_S` and
the trace diverges; its phase-space renormalization is part of the imported
semilocal trace theorem, not of (5.3).

Formula (5.3) is compatible with dagger after conjugating the distribution
and reversing `q`.  It is not yet the sum of local Weil distributions on
the quotient by `Gamma_S`: that comparison includes isotropy strata and
the cutoff subtraction and belongs to row C.

## 6. Row-A conclusion and remaining bridge

On the semilocal action-groupoid carrier, A now has a single sheaf with:

```text
internal gauge kernels U_q
  -> exact composition U_q U_r=U_qr
  -> exact transpose U_q^dagger=U_(q^-1)
  -> complete test-kernel ideal K_S^rapid
  -> dense Bruhat-Schwartz trace core
  -> source-defined fixed-point distribution before quotient descent,
     away from q=1.
```

This is a genuine **internal groupoid-kernel** calculus rather than a
product category of four decorations.  P123.013 corrects its typing: the
`U_q` are gauge arrows of the quotient presentation, not the external
idele-class correspondences whose character enters the trace formula.

Row A remains open because the external idele-class bimodule calculus and
the following later comparison have not been built:

```text
semilocal action-groupoid kernel sheaf
          |
          |  OPEN determinant/cohomology comparison
          v
intrinsic periodic Deligne cohomology on the spherical square.
```

That comparison, not composition or dagger, is now the live A gate.
