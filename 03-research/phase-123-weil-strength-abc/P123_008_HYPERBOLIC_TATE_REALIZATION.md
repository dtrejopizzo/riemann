# P123.008 — the hyperbolic Tate realization

## Status

`PROVED-IN-REPOSITORY` for the scalar-augmentation no-go, the continuous
two-character Tate realization of the dagger-complete rational gauge-label
algebra, its extension to internal semilocal kernels, Zariski compatibility
and the exact algebraic recovery of a hyperbolic plane.

`OPEN` for descent of this representation to the external idele-class
dynamics (P123.013 proves it cannot descend by identifying gauge arrows
with physical scalings), derived base-change/Beck--Chevalley compatibility,
and the determinant of the resulting relative Tor object.  Those are the
remaining ingredients needed to promote the realization to mixed
Riemann--Roch.

The construction is independent of the von Mangoldt function, the selected
family `Gamma_n`, the explicit formula, zeta zeros and every sign claim.

## 1. Why the old scalar augmentation cannot survive dagger

The one-sided Dirichlet algebra used in the old Deligne--nuclear pullback
has the augmentation

```text
epsilon_+(delta_1)=1,
epsilon_+(delta_n)=0  (n>1).                           (1.1)
```

It is multiplicative because a product of positive integers is one only
when both factors are one.  P123.003 proved that closure under transpose
forces the inverse label `delta_(1/n)`.

### Proposition 1.1 — scalar augmentation no-go

There is no unital algebra homomorphism from `C_Q` to a nonzero unital
algebra which extends (1.1).

### Proof

For `n>1`, multiplicativity would give

```text
1=epsilon(delta_1)
 =epsilon(delta_n delta_(1/n))
 =epsilon(delta_n)epsilon(delta_(1/n))=0.              (1.2)
```

QED.

Thus replacing the one-sided coefficient algebra by its dagger completion
inside the old scalar homotopy pullback is impossible.  A larger real
fiber, rather than another scalar normalization, is forced.

## 2. The two Tate characters

Let

```text
B_T=C direct-sum C
```

with componentwise multiplication, the antilinear involution

```text
(z_-,z_+)^sharp=(conjugate(z_+),conjugate(z_-)),       (2.1)
```

and trace `tau_T(z_-,z_+)=z_-+z_+`.  The associated Hermitian form is

```text
beta_T(z,w)=tau_T(z w^sharp)
           =z_- conjugate(w_+)+z_+ conjugate(w_-).    (2.2)
```

It is the hyperbolic plane, not a positive Hilbert form.

For `q in Q_+^times`, set

```text
rho_T(delta_q)=(q^(-1/2),q^(1/2)).                    (2.3)
```

For `c=(c_q) in C_Q`, define

```text
rho_T(c)=
 (sum_q c_q q^(-1/2), sum_q c_q q^(1/2)).             (2.4)
```

### Theorem 2.1

`rho_T:C_Q -> B_T` is a continuous unital algebra homomorphism and

```text
rho_T(c^dagger)=rho_T(c)^sharp.                        (2.5)
```

### Proof

Both components of (2.3) are multiplicative characters.  Since

```text
q^(1/2)<=H(q)^(1/2),   q^(-1/2)<=H(q)^(1/2),          (2.6)
```

both series in (2.4) converge absolutely and are bounded by the first
weighted seminorm of P123.003.  This proves continuity and permits the
Cauchy-product proof of multiplicativity.  Finally,

```text
sum_q conjugate(c_(q^-1))q^(-1/2)
 =conjugate(sum_r c_r r^(1/2)),
```

and the other component is the conjugate identity with the signs
exchanged.  This is (2.5).  QED.

Equivalently, on `C^2` put

```text
J=[[0,1],[1,0]],
D_q=diag(q^(-1/2),q^(1/2)).                           (2.7)
```

Then `D_q^sharp=J D_q^* J=D_(q^-1)`.  The transpose is therefore an
honest Krein/Rosati adjoint on the two Tate jets.

## 3. Extension from labels to semilocal kernels

For a finite-prime chart `S`, an element of P123.006 is

```text
F=sum_(q in Gamma_S) f_q U_q.
```

The additive origin is fixed by every rational dilation, so

```text
(alpha_q f)(0)=f(0).                                  (3.1)
```

Define

```text
pi_(T,S)(F)=
 (sum_q f_q(0)q^(-1/2), sum_q f_q(0)q^(1/2)).         (3.2)
```

### Theorem 3.1

`pi_(T,S):K_S^rapid -> B_T` is a continuous algebra homomorphism satisfying

```text
pi_(T,S)(F^dagger)=pi_(T,S)(F)^sharp.                 (3.3)
```

It extends to the unitization and, separately, to the multiplier subalgebra
generated by `K_S^rapid` and the rapid label multipliers; on that subalgebra
it sends `U_q` to `D_q`.

### Proof

Evaluation at zero is a continuous character of `C_0(A_S)` and (3.1)
makes it invariant under the action.  Apply it coefficientwise to (2.2)
of P123.006; the crossed-product convolution becomes ordinary group
convolution, after which Theorem 2.1 applies.  The estimate

```text
||pi_(T,S)(F)|| <= 2 sum_q ||f_q||_infinity H(q)
```

proves continuity.  Formula (3.3) follows from (2.5) and the crossed-product
dagger.  Unitization sends the adjoined unit to `(1,1)`.  The same weighted
estimate applies to the multiplier series of P123.006 (3.3), and on a pure
label multiplier the extension gives (2.7).  QED.

## 4. Descent compatibility

For `S subset S'`, P123.006 restricts a coefficient by

```text
f |-> f tensor 1_(R_(S',S)).
```

The characteristic function of the added maximal compact factors equals
one at their origin.  Hence

```text
pi_(T,S') restriction_(S',S)=pi_(T,S).                (4.1)
```

The maps (3.2) therefore define a morphism from the strict **internal**
semilocal structure-kernel sheaf to the constant hyperbolic Tate algebra.
It joins the gauge coefficient calculus to two algebraic Tate directions;
P123.013 shows why a separate external bimodule realization is still
required before calling these the physical ruling degrees.

## 5. Exact ruling plane

Let

```text
e_-=(1,0),   e_+=(0,1).
```

Then (2.2) gives

```text
beta_T(e_-,e_-)=beta_T(e_+,e_+)=0,
beta_T(e_-,e_+)=beta_T(e_+,e_-)=1.                    (5.1)
```

Thus the matrix in this basis is exactly

```text
[[0,1],[1,0]],                                        (5.2)
```

the intersection matrix of the two rulings.  Moreover the two components
of `D_q` are the two Tate degree characters.  Dagger exchanges them exactly
as transposition exchanges vertical and horizontal degree.

This is more than a formal direct sum at the internal coefficient level:
both directions are fibers of one algebra representation and their
hyperbolic pairing is induced by the same Rosati involution.  P123.013
shows that it is **not yet** the ruling realization of the external physical
correspondence; the latter must be constructed as an idele-class bimodule.

## 6. The induced perfect realization

Let `Ktilde_S` denote the unitization of `K_S^rapid`.  Algebraic base change
along (3.2) gives an exact functor on finite-cell perfect right modules,

```text
R_T,S:Perf(Ktilde_S) -> Perf(B_T),
R_T,S(M)=M tensor^L_(Ktilde_S) B_T.                   (6.1)
```

A finite-cell module remains a finite-cell `B_T`-module, so its two
Knudsen--Mumford determinant lines exist.  The sharp involution exchanges
the two determinant factors and supplies the hyperbolic Deligne pairing.
Restriction compatibility follows from (4.1).

This closes the **existence and typing** of the internal
coefficient/determinant realization.  It neither descends the gauge labels
as physical scalings nor proves compatibility with composition of external
bivariant kernels.

## 7. The exact remaining derived gate

For convolution/base-change compatibility one needs the multiplication map

```text
B_T tensor^L_(Ktilde_S) B_T -> B_T                   (7.1)
```

to have a controlled perfect cone.  If (7.1) were an equivalence, the Tate
realization would be a homological epimorphism and derived base change
would commute with kernel composition without correction.  There is no
reason to assume this: the common fixed point has nontrivial isotropy, so
relative Tor is expected and is exactly where a Lefschetz/RR correction
must live.

Define the relative contact object

```text
C_(T,S)=Cone(B_T tensor^L_(Ktilde_S) B_T -> B_T).      (7.2)
```

The next theorem must establish:

1. the relevant localized/truncated part of `C_(T,S)` is perfect;
2. its determinant is compatible with `S subset S'`;
3. its determinant cross-effect equals the correction between the source
   trace intersection of P123.007 and the hyperbolic ruling determinant;
4. no selected arithmetic weight is inserted into (7.2).

This is a concrete derived calculation, not the previous vague request for
a “comparison functor.”  It isolates mixed Riemann--Roch as the determinant
of the failure of the Tate fiber functor to be monoidal.
