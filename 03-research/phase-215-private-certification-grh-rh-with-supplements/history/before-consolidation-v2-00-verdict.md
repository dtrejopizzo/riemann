# Current verdict

**The proof of GRH/RH cannot be certified as written.** The identity `pcu:product` of 53 omits the factor α(det g)α(det y)β(det l). The error is used inside a period and affects the route of the exchange and of the covolume. No global repair preserving the required factors has been reconstructed. This does not refute RH, nor does it prove that the statement of the exchange is false.

The audit reconstructed numerous independent blocks and both finite-window bounds. The 371 statements reproduced in the reviewed appendices preserve the sources; fidelity of copying does not automatically validate their proofs. There are additional local repairs, described with lines and constants, and delimited (c) items still pending.
