## Outside the kernel of the conclusion

The reproduced finite windows, the auxiliary finite comparisons without proof of 46, the rows of 43–45 and the bibliographic priority are not premises of the automorphic chain. Their statuses must not be turned artificially into an obstruction to that chain nor into sufficient evidence for it. The stronger classification of the phase of Paper 49 can be replaced by the two proved consequences, with that explicit supplement.
