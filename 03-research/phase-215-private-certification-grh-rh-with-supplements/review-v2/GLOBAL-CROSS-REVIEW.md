# Adversarial cross-review of the assembly V2

Rereading: GLOBAL-ASSEMBLY, CLOSURE-MAP and 46:3390–3510,5015–5055,5190–5400; collation with REVIEW-55-56-V2. The Weil representation is not audited again here.

**Verdict (a) for the abstract implication from the stated premises.** I found no counterexample to the height step and no illicit exchange of quantifiers. This does not by itself certify the premises SF/Sp/Cv.

1. **One twist against two embeddings.** With ι fixed, ξ=θ_ι is used in both columns of the pairing. θ_(barι) is not used. The definition of the index demands a matrix u_ι whose vectors belong to the lattice for **all** the twists permitted at the prime ℓ. Hence u_(barι) also serves the same ξ. Hypothesis (ii) on auxiliaries has precisely the same universal quantifier. It is enough that admissibility depend on the prime; neither θ_ι=θ_(barι) nor regularity of ξ for barι is required. Consequently there is no simultaneity condition that the Selmer construction of 55 has omitted.

2. **Mixed determinant.** The integral matrix is bar(v′)Gram(Θ_ξw)vᵗ. Its determinant produces |barγγ|_ι^(n′)|Γ(ξ)|_ι≤ℓ^(2e_ℓ). The complex number |γ|² is exactly barγγ; an archimedean norm is not improperly transported to an ℓ-adic norm. The cost is 2δ and not δ. The proof asks neither for u=u′ nor for algebraic coefficients for the original vectors.

3. **Height principle.** If the multiplier H were transcendental, choosing an isomorphism ι₀ and a transcendental H′, and extending H↦2^(−n)H′ to an automorphism of C, produces |H|_ι arbitrarily large on composing with ι₀. This contradicts the bound for all ι above 2. Thus H is algebraic. All its embeddings extend to those of the definition; the rational norm and the product over the finite set of primes supporting it give c≥0. The requirement "every finite set T" suffices: no unjustified infinite sum is introduced.

4. **Archimedean conjugation.** σ′=cσc gives σ(bar z)=bar(σ′z). Since G is rational, σΓ(1) is a mixed exterior pairing between the families σ′w and σw. Cauchy–Schwarz, Fischer and Hadamard give |σΓ(1)|≤exp(c+n′b_short). It has not been assumed that σ commutes with c, nor that σ preserves the coordinate subspace: G is defined and positive on the ambient space. Injectivity of Θ₁ guarantees Γ(1)≠0, a condition indispensable for using the product.

5. **Cv and principal cancellation.** The quotients Z,Δ,H keep the exponents d and j; n′≤4d/b² absorbs the cost of the auxiliaries by ≤db. In SF+Sp+Cv only n≤d is used, for n·h_main≤d·h_main, permitted because h_main≥0. The term −n·m·rem is kept without replacing n by d. The case rem≤0 is separated before dividing by n; in the other case Sp guarantees d>0 and n≥d/2. I found no wrong sign and no loss of a factor m.

6. **Thresholds and characters.** The one-sided bound fixes q₀,d,ε before the data are chosen for each large integer x. The PNT terms are evaluated at x₁, not at x. The passage to reals costs O(1/x), which can be absorbed. To exclude a ρ, one fixes β between 1/2 and Reρ and then applies the criterion to all the primes of the class; a threshold uniform in d is not required. The Euler factors on inducing χ to modulus 18480q introduce no zeros in that half-plane. The functional equation is applied only afterwards, to the primitive character.

## Premises: what the map can assert

The review of 55/56 closes I/II/III→regularity under the declared inputs and the level supplement; there is no new joint requirement on ι and barι. Green has been collated with its primary source in REVIEW-55-56-V2 §8: the two rows of the map that still call it "in progress" and "quantifiers under review" can be updated.

The source inputs that the assembly **cannot discharge by itself** are: existence of admissible data; SF with the same principal constant and norms for all σ; Sp with d>0 and almost full rank; and inj/aux/factor/onelift/hecke/mult needed for Cv. The ledgers individualise those inputs, including the phase supplement, the corrected level, the calibration of the exchange and their ranges. In this rereading I found no extra input missing from that inventory. The specialised review of splitting/exchange must still close its own proof; the abstract computation here cannot replace it.

Therefore GLOBAL-ASSEMBLY correctly formulates a checked implication. A verdict on the argument **as literally written** must keep the identified V2 errata; a verdict on the argument **reconstructed with explicit supplements** may use them, without treating a desynchronisation of citations as a new mathematical obstruction and without confusing it with the proof of a substantive premise.
