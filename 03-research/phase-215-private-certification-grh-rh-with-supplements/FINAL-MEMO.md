# Closure of phase 215 — private mathematical certification of the GRH/RH chain with supplements

**Status: closed. Closure date: 28 November 2026.**

The phase is identified as `phase-215-private-certification-grh-rh-with-supplements`.

## Final declaration

**In phase 215 we have certified, by pure-mathematics review, the reconstructed chain that leads to GRH for Dirichlet L-functions and, in particular, to RH, with the corrections and supplementary proofs expressly incorporated into the private verdict.**

The object of this certification is the set of sources identified in `review-v2/sources-v2.json`, read together with the [mathematical supplement](review-v2/MATHEMATICAL-SUPPLEMENT.md) and the proofs linked in the [final report](FINAL-REPORT.md). The conclusion of the dossier is favourable for that reconstruction. It is not to be confused with a literal approval of every version of the PDFs, a formal Lean certification or an endorsement by external specialists.

The closure keeps the distinctions of the verdict: the old objection to the residual character of the exchange of 53 was withdrawn; the later corrections were identified and justified, among them νflat of 54, the auxiliary level of 55 and the sufficient properties of the phase of 49. The inventory and the supplements remain visible. Changes to the manuscripts later than the audited hashes are not automatically certified by this memo; this closure organises the result already obtained and does not constitute a new audit of those versions.

## A new research horizon

A universe of mathematical possibilities opens up to be developed: extracting reusable tools from the relations between CM divisibility, automorphic integral structures, comparison of determinants and analytic bounds. This opening is a research programme, not the assertion that all its generalisations are already proved nor that its bibliographic priority has been established.

The acknowledgement of 57 attributes to Alpöge the central mathematics organised by SF/Sp/Cv. Future research will keep that attribution and will distinguish the formulation of the series, the antecedents and the new results actually obtained.

## Planned handover to phase 216

**Proposed objective: to develop three autonomous works in depth.**

1. **Integral compatibility between twists.** Begin with one lift and then compose two, keeping visible the same global determinant, the indices and the local losses.
2. **Kernels realisable by means of CM weights.** Begin with three levels of weights and prove the analytic identity, the admissibility and their costs.
3. **Independent extraction of paper 56.** Study exponents of cup products, optimality and applications to congruences outside this proof.

The technical basis and the success criteria are in the linked programme. Following the decision to put phases 214 and 215 in order first and to report, **phase 216 is planned, not yet opened or started**. No results are created and no research is begun under that number in this closure.
