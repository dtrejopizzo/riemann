## Verdict (≤15 lines)

Core novelty holds up: no paper found proves LP-duality non-attainment for Weil's functional (item 1), the flat-top/Theorem-Z equivalence (item 2), the finite-zero-set non-certification result (item 5), the Toeplitz lattice-class ceilings (item 3), or the Schur-reduction certified value (item 7). These are the paper's real contributions and nothing in the 2025–2026 literature overlaps them. Zhu 2026 (arXiv:2608.24827) is correctly cited and correctly distinguished (window 1.6 vs. our log2/½log3, envelope+comb vs. dual-certificate method) — no overclaim detected. Two items need stronger citation, not correction: (4) sub-comb negativity should explicitly credit Bombieri2000 Thm. 12 and Yoshida1992 Lemmas 2–3 as the quantitative ancestor of "primes matter past a threshold," and (6) the archimedean-alone thresholds T*∞/T*∞,P should be framed against Connes–Consani 2021 (arXiv:2006.13771) as the closest prior study of archimedean-only positivity, since that paper is already cited elsewhere but not tied to this specific claim. Item 5 should add a one-line nod to Kreĭn extension theory (via Suzuki's screw-function papers, already cited) since the finite-data-can't-certify-forever phenomenon is classically a Kreĭn-extension fact. Groskin 2026 (arXiv:2607.02828) and Suzuki's screw-function series are the closest "adjacent methods" throughout but do not anticipate any specific theorem. No item found in status ALREADY KNOWN that isn't already correctly cited in main.tex. Recommend: add Bombieri2000/Yoshida1992 pointers to §8's SC discussion and a one-sentence Connes–Consani 2021 comparison near the archimedean-threshold claims; no other changes needed.
