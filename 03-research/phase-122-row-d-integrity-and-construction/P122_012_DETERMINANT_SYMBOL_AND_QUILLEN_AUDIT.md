# P122 -- determinant symbols and Quillen metrics: exact import boundary

## Status

`IMPORTED` for the external theorems identified below;
`PROVED-IN-REPOSITORY` for the hypothesis comparison and no-automatic-bridge
conclusion.  Determinant/Contou--Carrere geometry supplies a credible
algebraic orientation carrier, and Quillen/Deligne geometry supplies
nonflat Hermitian curvature on genuine projective families.  No cited
theorem combines them on the repository's spherical `Spec Z` carrier.

This is not a rejection of determinant geometry.  It identifies the exact
additional construction needed before it can be called `D_candidate`.

## 1. Algebraic determinant symbols

Osipov--Zhu construct the two-dimensional Contou--Carrere symbol on
double-loop units and prove reciprocity laws on algebraic surfaces.  Their
symbol is realized by generalized commutators in a categorical central
extension.  Braunling--Groechenig--Wolfson construct the higher-dimensional
symbol from boundary maps in algebraic K-theory and prove higher
Parshin--Kato reciprocity.

Primary sources:

* D. Osipov and X. Zhu,
  [The two-dimensional Contou--Carrere symbol and reciprocity laws](https://arxiv.org/abs/1305.6032),
  especially Proposition 4.3, Theorem 5.9 and Theorem 6.1;
* O. Braunling, M. Groechenig and J. Wolfson,
  [A generalized Contou--Carrere symbol and its reciprocity laws in higher dimensions](https://arxiv.org/abs/1410.3451).

These results genuinely provide three ingredients relevant to D:

1. an orientation-sensitive, pre-scalar central extension;
2. boundary maps in K-theory rather than an operator square root chosen
   from `B_nuc`;
3. a global reciprocity theorem when an actual algebraic surface and its
   flags are present.

They do not provide a Hermitian sign.  In characteristic zero the explicit
infinitesimal formula in Osipov--Zhu is

\[
 (1+\epsilon f,1+\epsilon g,1+\epsilon h)
 =1+\epsilon^3\operatorname {Res}(f\,dg\wedge dh)  \tag{1.1}
\]

over `k[epsilon]/(epsilon^4)`.  Its leading invariant is alternating and
trilinear.  It is neither a sesquilinear norm nor a positive square.
Complex conjugation, a Hilbert completion and a curved metric are not
conclusions of the algebraic reciprocity theorem.

## 2. Deligne pairing and Quillen curvature

For a smooth projective surjective morphism `X -> S` of relative dimension
`n` between integral complex schemes, line bundles on `X` have a Deligne
pairing on `S`.  With Hermitian and relative Kahler input, determinant lines
carry Quillen metrics; the standard comparison identifies the Deligne and
determinant metrics up to the stated normalization.

Primary sources used here:

* I. Biswas and G. Schumacher,
  [Deligne pairing and Quillen metric](https://arxiv.org/abs/1501.02539),
  Theorem 1.1 and Sections 3--5;
* I. Biswas and G. Schumacher,
  [Deligne pairing and determinant bundle](https://arxiv.org/abs/1106.0255),
  for the underlying canonical line-bundle isomorphism;
* J.-M. Bismut and G. Lebeau,
  [Complex immersions and Quillen metrics](https://www.numdam.org/item/PMIHES_1991__74__1_0/),
  for the analytic-torsion/Quillen framework behind the curvature and
  anomaly formulae.

The curvature is not generated by the bare multiplicative product formula.
It depends on geometric input: a proper holomorphic family, elliptic
complexes or line bundles on its fibers, Hermitian metrics, and the
appropriate Kahler data.  This is precisely why it evades the flatness
calculation of P122.010, but also why it cannot be imported for free.

## 4. Why simply composing the two theories fails

There is no functor in the cited results

```text
formal oriented Euler K2 germ
    -> 2-Tate determinant gerbe
    -> Hermitian Quillen line with critical residue curvature.
```

Even if the first arrow were constructed, algebraic reciprocity would say
that a product of symbols is one.  By P122.010 its flat logarithmic norm
has zero mixed curvature.  Passing to a Quillen metric changes the metric
and creates an anomaly; proving the anomaly formula then requires a source
elliptic complex and its local index density.  Choosing the metric so that
the anomaly equals the already known critical square would be circular.

The determinant central extension may preserve the two orientations and so
pass the port-swap test.  The metric comparison is the still-missing arrow.

## 5. The admissible determinant blueprint

The audit leaves a concrete, noncircular blueprint, not yet a D candidate.
It has five independently checkable construction obligations:

1. **2-Tate realization.**  Construct from each oriented Euler factor a
   canonical object in a restricted/double-loop determinant category,
   including exact prime-power weights before scalar trace.
2. **Global carrier.**  Construct a genuine global flag or an analytic
   replacement and prove a reciprocity theorem on it; do not call the
   spherical smash product an algebraic surface by analogy.
3. **Hermitian enhancement.**  Produce the Real structure and metric from
   the source complex.  The two causal ports must remain distinct.
4. **Local anomaly theorem.**  Compute its curvature and prove, rather than
   define, that the critical fixed-point contribution is
   `2 pi m_rho^2 |K_F(i gamma)|^2` per orientation.
5. **Global equality/sign theorem.**  Show that a primitive first-contact
   state has zero total anomaly, or that the remaining contributions have
   a sign forcing the critical curvature to vanish.

Items 1 and the local part of 3 have a plausible operator model in the
Hardy determinant line: the rank-one Hankel commutator of
`P121_LOCAL_HANKEL_ROOT_OBSERVABLE_AUDIT.md` is already the right local
shape and distinguishes the shifted degree-two pair.  Items 2, 4 and 5 are
not supplied by the literature cited here and remain construction work.

## 6. Verdict

Determinant-symbol geometry remains the best-matched live architecture
because it can retain orientation before trace and can, after a genuine
Hermitian enhancement, carry curvature.  The exact status is:

\[
 \boxed{\text{algebraic determinant skeleton: available in the correct
 ambient theories; global Hermitian realization on A: not constructed.}}
\]

It may therefore guide the construction of D, but invoking
“Contou--Carrere + Quillen” is not itself a proof and supplies no present
arrow to row (d).
