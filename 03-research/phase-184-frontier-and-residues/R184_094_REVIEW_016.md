# R184_094 — Review of R184_016 (A1 selector check)

**Status:** review. Pure mathematics, no numerics, no web. R184_016 is not modified.
Sources cross-checked: R184_016; R167_101 §2.2–§4 (Lemma Q, Lemma O, Thm B, Thm C, Prop E); R166_802 (table, Thm 3);
R169_091 Prop 3.2; R169_000 §2; CROSSWORD-TO-D §3.2–§4.1; Paper 46 cor:RH.

**Overall verdict.** No FATAL. The core survives: *literal A1 is a statement about the witness (vacuous under RH) and a
linear bound in Λ over tests G(·+γ)²k excludes a zero only at the points where it is evaluated* — that is E1-local and is
correctly seen. But there are four MAJOR items: Thm 1(ii) and Thm 2 are stated as "⟺" and only a sandwich is proved; Thm 5(ii) invokes R166_802
Thm 3 outside its domain (asymptotic in Y) and reads Thm C as sufficiency; the conclusion "only blank square = O166.802.1"
ignores OPEN squares of route B of the crossword itself. Proposed label for the verdict "A1 is E1": **PROVED in the
weak form (A1 ⟹ absence of zeros in Z; RH ⟹ A1 on Reg⁺), not as an equivalence**.

## Item 1 — the three declared weak points

**(1a) R163_401 (3.1) only gives existence of the quartet in the cone. MINOR (confirmed, with a nuance).**
R167_101 §2.2(i): "The witness identity (3.1) of R163_401 forces at least one such quartet, since P_+ > 0. Nothing more is
known." Thm B: "Assume the witness, with the quartet at t_q in its cone". The inequality of Thm B uses only Lemma Q (cone +
rotation (ii)), Lemma O and (Dom_θ). Lemma 1 is a correct reading. Nuance: in Lemma 1 the cone is cos 2Φ_F(γ,δ) < 0 with
Φ_F = arg G(γ+iδ), while Lemma Q uses Θ_q = arg G(t_q), t_q = γ − iδ. For real G, G(t̄) = conj G(t), so cos 2Θ_q =
cos 2Φ_F; correct, but it must be written out. Also k (via ϑ) depends on (γ,δ) and G, not on the zero: fine, but then
Reg and Reg⁺ depend on G (see item 4).

**(1b) Lemma O holds for G_a. MINOR (confirmed).** The proof of Lemma O uses only Ψ ≥ 0 on ℝ, Trudgian, Bernstein
(‖G′‖_∞ ≤ T‖G‖_∞) and ‖G‖_∞ < ∞; it does not use |w| ≤ w(0) nor the witness normalization. sin(T(z−a)) is bounded on ℝ and of type T:
it applies. (It is not Paley–Wiener in L², as Prop 3 states — correct to: "Bernstein class of type T"; Φ = G²k is indeed integrable.)

**(1c) Exact condition of Thm C. MAJOR (affects Thm 5(ii)).** R167_101 l.~180: η < δ is **necessary** ("can only hold if"),
not sufficient; the exact condition is η < α(F,K) ≤ δ plus an inequality with the constants 2/δ, c_Θ, (1−θ), E_K/4.
R184_016 Thm 5(ii) says "weakening B to X^η with η < δ **still excludes** (Thm C)": false as stated; only "η ≥ δ does not exclude"
is what Thm C gives.

**(1d) Not declared: Lemma 0 uses Lemma O in the wrong direction. MINOR.** Lemma O is an **upper** bound on O_K, which gives
a **lower** bound on (L/π)I − O_K. Lemma 0 needs the upper bound on 4Re𝒮, i.e. O_K ≥ (L/π)I − (counting term). This is fixed:
under the absence of zeros off the line in the zone, O_K = O^all up to tails, and the Stieltjes identity from Lemma O's proof is
an equality, giving |O^all − (L/π)I − ε′_K| ≤ 2C_S(2T+Δ)‖G‖²_∞‖k‖₁ in both directions. The equality should be cited,
not "Lemma O". Also, "no zeros in the zone" does not give O_K = O^all exactly (zeros outside the zone from other quartets):
the difference goes into tails ε′_K, to be declared.

## Item 2 — Thm 1(i), "vacuous under RH"

**MINOR (incorrect citation, correct derivation).** The argument "W_T ≥ 0 for every T under RH ⟹ no first contact ⟹
image of [S] = ∅" is correct, but the correct reference is Paper 46 cor:RH, direction (a)⟹(c) (via Paper 45 Thm 8.4),
or directly Weil positivity (Weil 1952 / Bombieri 2000), not a generic "(Weil; CITED)". The direction "first
contact ⟹ zero off the line" is the contrapositive and should be cited the same way. Precision: if "contact" is defined as
λ^W_T = 0 (not < 0), one also needs W_T > 0 strictly under RH at finite type (a G of finite type does not annihilate all the
γ_ρ, by N(T) ≫ T); one line.

**Thm 1(ii). MAJOR.** Stated as ⟺; the proof itself admits that ⟸ requires reading A1 on Reg⁺ and requires no quartets
off the line in the localization zone of *every* point of Γ_S(F) \ {t_q}. That is not "no witness in Reg": it is an
additional zero-free-region hypothesis. What is proved is: **A1 ⟹ ¬(witness with t_q ∈ Reg)** (given (Dom_θ)), and (i). Downgrade:
(ii) ⟹ PROVED given (Dom_θ); (ii) ⟸ CONDITIONAL[on absence of zeros off the line in N(Γ_S(F) ∩ Reg⁺)]. This does not alter
the E1 conclusion (which only uses ⟹).

## Item 3 — Thm 2: free region or "no zeros at those heights"?

**MAJOR (statement), the E1-local conclusion survives.**
(a) Lemma 1 excludes a zero exactly at (γ,δ) ∈ Γ_S(F) ∩ Reg⁺ for a given G ∈ 𝔉; A1^𝔉 ⟹ "Z(𝔉) contains no zeros". Z(𝔉)
is a union of open sets (cos 2Φ_F < 0, B > E_K/4 are open conditions) — for a single G it is a comb of bands
{|tan T(γ−a)| < tanh Tδ} in the sine case. For a **countable or window-dependent** family (e.g. one eigenvector per
T over a bounded range of T), Z(𝔉) is an open set that can be sparse (comb of density < 1, with no interior in the projection to
γ of selected heights if 𝔉 is chosen at scattered heights). "Zero-free region" is correct in the technical sense of
open, but misleading: the content is "no zeros in Z(𝔉)", an E1 statement *localized to Z*, not a dlVP-type
region. If 𝔉 and the evaluated heights formed a discrete set of (γ,δ), the content would be exactly "no zeros at those
points", even weaker; the argument still holds: it excludes a witness only if its position falls into that set.
(b) The converse requires the absence of zeros in a **localization neighborhood** N(Z) ⊋ Z (so that Q_K = R_K = 0) and reading on
Reg⁺. Hence it is a sandwich: zero-free(N(Z)) ⟹ A1^𝔉 ⟹ zero-free(Z) [given (Dom_θ) uniform]. Not "⟺". Downgrade the "⟺" to
"sandwich PROVED given (Dom_θ)".
(c) The dichotomy (a)/(b) remains intact in sandwich form: if the witness's position ∉ Z, useless; if ∈ Z, A1^𝔉 at that
point already is the absence of the zero. But the sentence "it is the force-(N′) bound … (R166_802 Thm 2, transfer)" conflates: what
is obtained is the pointwise transfer (Thm 2), not exponent strength (N′) (see item 5).
(d) (Dom_θ) "uniform in 𝔉" is a hypothesis about zeros quantified over all G; for 𝔉^{sin} with all a it is
a hypothesis about the local density of quartets off the line. It should be stated as such; it is not "technical" in the same sense as
at the single witness.

## Item 4 — Prop 3: are the sine-translated tests admissible?

**MAJOR (scope), the mathematics of Prop 3 is correct.**
- G_a is not a witness, nor a lowest eigenvector of W_T, nor primitive; it is not in the image of [S]. Hence Prop 3 says
  **nothing about A1 as literally written** (which quantifies only over the image of the selector, ∅ under RH). It says something about A1^{𝔉sin},
  a different strengthening. The initial verdict ("the sine-translation family already does so: A1 is RH in the
  regime") and the Corollary ("the relevant image is dense", "closes U2(a)") conflate both things. Correct: "the
  natural witnessless strengthening with 𝔉^{sin} is E1 on Reg⁺(sin)"; U2(a) (density of the **selector's image**) is not
  resolved by Prop 3 — it is resolved *in a different sense*: under RH the image is ∅ and under ¬RH it is whatever the witness gives.
- "Z(𝔉^{sin}) = Reg⁺" is ill-defined: Reg⁺ depends on G (|G(t)|², ‖G‖_∞, k). For G_γ, |G(t)|² = sinh²(Tδ), ‖G‖_∞ = 1,
  and Prop E requires Y^δ ≳ C_S(γ)(2T+Δ): Reg⁺(sin) excludes small δ at bounded T (δ ≲ log log γ / (2T+Δ)). One should write
  Z(𝔉^{sin}) = Reg⁺(𝔉^{sin}) := {(γ,δ): (γ,δ) ∈ Reg⁺(G_γ)} and compare it with the witness's Reg, which need not
  coincide. "Covers the witness's regime" only holds on the intersection.
- Type: if the crossword's admissibility requires real-rooted type ≤ 2T_c, G_a is real-rooted of type T, compatible; what
  fails is primitivity/eigenvector status, which is exactly what is meant to be dropped. Acceptable if labeled as a strengthening.
Downgrade: Prop 3 PROVED (for 𝔉^{sin}, with Reg⁺ corrected); Corollary "closes U2(a)" → withdrawn; "A1 is RH in the regime"
→ "A1^{𝔉sin} is E1 on Reg⁺(sin), modulo (Dom_θ) uniform".

## Item 5 — Thm 5(ii): "B polylog ⟹ force (N′)"

**MAJOR.**
- R166_802 Thm 3 is asymptotic: A_γ(Y) = O(Y^{Θ−½+ε}), Ω(Y^{Θ−½−ε}) as Y → ∞ at fixed γ; Θ is the **global supremum** of
  real parts, not "Θ at height γ_q" (Thm 3 stresses that the frequency γ_q does **not** isolate the quartet asymptotically).
  "Polylog at height γ_q beyond X = e^{2T_c} ⟺ Θ ≤ ½ at that height" is not a statement of Thm 3; it is a category
  error.
- In the regime of A1, T is bounded (Prop E: 2T+Δ ≲ 11.4 c_Θ e^{δΔ}), Y is finite and "polylog" is in γ, not in Y. At a
  finite scale there is no exponent; the correct tool is the pointwise transfer R166_802 Thm 2 / the sandwich of Thm 2 of
  R184_016: the bound at (γ,δ) is equivalent (given (Dom_θ), with the reading of 1d) to the absence of the zero there. That is E1-local, without
  going through (N′).
- The X^η version: see 1c; only "η ≥ δ does not exclude" is proved.
- Conclusion "at no point of the useful range is the bound weaker than 'there is no zero there'": survives, but via Thm 2
  (pointwise), not via Thm 3. Thm 5(i) (uniformity in X): correct; "∀X = union of regions" is a sandwich, as in item 3.
Downgrade: Thm 5(ii) PROVED → part "η ≥ δ does not exclude" PROVED-CITED (Thm C); "B polylog ⟺ (N′)" WITHDRAWN; replace with
"pointwise equivalence (R166_802 Thm 2)".

## Item 6 — "the only blank square is O166.802.1"

**MAJOR (overstatement).**
- Thm 2 of R184_016 covers only inequalities **linear in Λ, evaluated on tests of the form G(·+γ)²k, within the
  witness/exclusion architecture of Thm B, given (Dom_θ)**. That does not exhaust the crossword.
- CROSSWORD §4.1 lists route B with its own OPEN squares: B1 = O2601.1 ("OPEN, no candidate" ⟹ C_B) and B2 = L8 (m < ∞,
  forces Lindelöf). §2 of the crossword also has C_B (non-equivalence with RH NOT-REFUTED), C_T (RH ⟹ C_T OPEN),
  (M)/(E′_loc), C1 at O(1). None of these is witness architecture; A1's E1 does not touch them.
- What does follow: **within route A** (witness architecture + linear bound), the square with no meta-theorem is
  O166.802.1 (this was already so in CROSSWORD §3.3 last paragraph and in the addendum, l.~563). R184_016 adds nothing on that point
  except removing A1 as an intermediate node.
- Also (Dom_θ) and (P_gap) remain OPEN and route A is now "A1(E1) ∧ (Dom_θ) ∧ (P_gap)": the sentence "The conjunction is RH
  split into two regions" is correct only if it is stated that (Dom_θ) is an additional hypothesis about zeros (the conjunction
  could be apparently strictly stronger than RH, even though implied by RH only vacuously).
Downgrade: "only blank square" → "only blank square of route A (witness architecture)".

## Additional minor items

- §4 "(Dom_θ) under RH is vacuous (S^+_K = 0 = Q_K)": 0 ≤ θ·0 holds; fine. But then Lemma 1 "is read without a quartet" makes
