## R184_005 (C1, θ = 0 rigidity)

**N1: correct (clarification of the target).** The doubt was whether ℕ ∪ √2ℕ, as the free monoid on {primes} ∪ {√2}, counts 2
twice (as 2 and as √2·√2). That would happen with generators {2,3,5,…,√2}, which give ζ·(1−2^{−s/2})^{−1} and
N = Σ_j⌊x/2^{j/2}⌋ = ρx + O(log x). But the document refers to the *substitution* 2 ↦ √2 of L3 (generators
{√2,3,5,7,…}). There the integers are √2^j·m with m odd: j even gives all of ℕ and j odd gives √2ℕ, with multiplicity 1. Then
ζ_𝒫 = ζ(1+2^{−s/2}) and N = ⌊x⌋+⌊x/√2⌋ = (1+1/√2)x + O(1). **N1 stands.**
- MINOR-1: §3 says "it is a free monoid (L3)" without naming the generators. {√2} ∪ {p ≥ 3} must be written explicitly, because
  this is exactly where a reader would go wrong. Also, the "non-vanishing distances" argument is unnecessary:
  n·c ∉ ℤ for every n ≥ 1 (c is irrational), so ℕ ∩ (1/c)ℕ = ∅, which is trivially stronger.

**L1: what the table states is correct; the prose overreaches.** The representation ζ_𝒫 = s∫_1^∞N x^{−s−1}dx is valid because
N(x)=0 for x<1 (all generalized integers are ≥ 1) and there is no boundary term. ∫_1^∞ρx·x^{−s−1} = ρ/(s−1) and
|s∫R x^{−s−1}| ≤ C|s|/σ. This gives holomorphy in Re s>0 except at s=1 and boundedness of ζ_𝒫(σ) as σ→0⁺: PROVED.
- MINOR-2 (overstatement): "the continuation to Re s = 0 admits at most simple-pole-type singularities at it₀ and
  **none** at 0" **is not proved**. Re s = 0 is the boundary: the O(1/σ) bound gives no continuation beyond the boundary nor the type of
  singularity. Bounding along the real axis also does not exclude a singularity at 0 (the bound C|s|/σ is not bounded under
  non-radial approaches). The sentence should be deleted or labeled HEURISTIC. It does not affect L2: there ζ_𝒫 is an explicit
  finite product, meromorphic on ℂ, and only the real limit σ→0⁺ is used.

**L2: correct.** Each factor has a simple pole or zero at 0, independent of the arithmetic of q. ζ(0) = −½ ≠ 0 is the only
input. The poles at 2πik/log q lie on the boundary and do not enter.
- MINOR-3: if T is a multiset with p repeated, the "system" has negative multiplicity (it stops being a Beurling system). T must be required
  to be a set, or the multiplicity of p in T must be ≤ 1.

**L3: correct** (N = Σa_d⌊x/d⌋).

**Prop 4.1: correct, with error O(log x) (not O(log log x)).** There are at most 2^{K(x)} sets J with d_J ≤ x, with
K(x) ≤ log₂log₂x², hence ≤ 2log₂x. Each contributes an error ≤ 1. The tail splits as follows: (a) the J whose maximal √p_k is > x
contribute ≤ ρ·Σ_{√p_k>x} x/√p_k = O(1) by the doubly-exponential growth; (b) the J with every √p ≤ x but
d_J > x number ≤ 2^{K} and each term is < 1, hence O(log x). The target's suspicion of O(log log x) counts factors, not
subsets. The correct bound is O(log x), as the document states. Survives.
- MINOR-4: the variant O(log^A x) is marked ARGUED, which is correct. But the formula "p_k ≥ 2^{k^{1/A}}·…" is incomplete and should be
  written out or deleted.

**N2: correct** (C ≥ ½ follows from the jumps ≥ 1, and the window of width ≥ 1/c covers ℝ).

## R184_008 (the factor/discrepancy route)

**Lemma AC: correct for infinite Q.** MINOR-1: for finite Q, θ_Q = −∞ by the definition, but the abscissa of Σa_n n^{−s} is 0
(N_Q ≍ log^k x). The claim "θ_Q is also the abscissa…" is false in that degenerate case. It should be restricted to θ_Q ≥ 0 or
redefined. It does not affect Theorem F, which is trivial for finite Q.

**Theorem F: correct.** Landau holds for generalized Dirichlet series Σa_k e^{−λ_k s} with a_k ≥ 0 and λ_k ↑ ∞. The
Q-integers are discrete because N_Q ≤ N_P. The abscissa of convergence is θ_Q by Lemma AC. ζ_P/ζ_B is meromorphic in Re s > θ and continues to
ζ_Q. If σ_Q < 1, it is holomorphic in a disk around σ_Q because ζ_B(σ_Q) ≠ 0. If σ_Q = 1, the singularity is removable (quotient of simple poles with
residues c, c_B > 0). The contradiction is valid and there is no gap. §2.3 (division variant) is correct: the conclusion "forced real zero" is
what follows.
- MINOR-2: the label "Theorem (multiplicative rigidity)" is generous for a two-line positivity argument. It should be labeled a Lemma.

**Prop M (model):** Franel in a window (1/(12pq) plus an error O(pq)), Bessel and the Gram bound are correct. The Gram bound is correct in the form
"rank-one part (Y/12)vvᵀ, v_p = 1/p, plus O(Q³)". A row-wise Schur bound would give (Y/12q)Σ1/p ~ loglogQ, which is not uniform. The
rank-one argument should be written out.
- MINOR-3 (exponent): the three conditions of (d) are q ≪ Y^{a/(1+a)}, the choice of P₁, and q^{2(1+a)/a} ≪ Y. The last comes from
  (1+a)(2−a)/a + 1 + a = 2(1+a)/a. Its minimum is **η = a/(2(1+a))**, not a/((1+a)(3−a)). Since 3−a > 2, the declared η is *smaller*.
  The statement remains valid (it is weaker) but the sentence "the minimum of the exponents gives η" is arithmetically false. It should be corrected, and
  δ_M improves.
- MINOR-4: in (b), the error O(Y/q) relative to the main term (Y/12)ε_q q^{−a} is negligible only for q ≥ q₀(C). Small q must be excluded
  (no effect on Σq^{−2a}). The equidistribution of q^a mod 1 over primes is CITED/FLAGGED, as already declared.
