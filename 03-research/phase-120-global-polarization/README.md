# Phase 120 — Global polarization

The phase attempts to close row (d) by assembling a chain of place-resolved, Gamma-Frobenius, and
Green-identity constructions on the source (arithmetic) side, then tests a long series of candidate
"positive lift" architectures (P3-numbered notes) to turn this assembly into the global positivity
statement row (d) needs. It ends by diagnosing why the whole family of candidates terminates in the
same unresolved contraction, and retires that family as a pattern rather than continuing to vary its
vocabulary.

## Results
- A chain of source-side constructions is completed and retained as proved: place-resolved Frobenius towers with a common half-density carrier; the quarter twist and positive Gamma reservoir; exact place-Poisson resummation of every prime-power tower; the D.247 four-port conservation eliminating local contact ports; causal Gamma and prime-delay state equations; a universal causal half-line / slit-disk Gamma–Frobenius carrier; the coefficient-one radial Green current; the global determinant scattering multiplier K_T; the zero-residual signed Green identity; the exact von Mangoldt modular-Hessian identity; a relative Tomita modular-Hamiltonian interpretation of the same Green multiplier; and a common-failure (locality–phase–sign trilemma) theorem. PROVED (each on its stated core).
- The remaining target — a global square inequality P_T E_T*(K_T*QK_T − Q)E_T P_T ≥ 0 for every support window T, followed by a cofinal passage — is explicitly not proved. OPEN.
- Diagnosis: after an exact bank decomposition −B_nuc,T(F,F) = ‖X_T F‖² − ‖Y_T F‖², every linear realization of the "build a positive object, descend it, identify its norm with −B_nuc" pattern requires the same terminal contraction Y_T = Θ_T X_T, regardless of which language it is phrased in (Schur/Douglas contraction, inner scattering/de Branges, KYP storage, Hodge/Rosati polarization, Higgs stability, homological vanishing, positive determinant curvature, Fock/CAR descent, modular inclusion/entropy Hessian, cofinal sign persistence). NO-GO — the whole family of candidate architectures is retired as equivalent terminal forms of one obstruction, not separate future options.
- No phase-120 candidate is promoted to the paper-42 writeup as a proof of row (d).
