    Delta_T=P_T E_T^*(K_T^*QK_T-Q)E_TP_T.           (1)

## 2. Local generalized-Schur columns

For each prime, D.253 gives on the punctured disk

    K_(c_p)(w,z)=u_p(w)^*u_p(z)-v(w)^*v(z),         (3)

with

    u_p(z)=sqrt(1-p^(-1))/(z(1-p^(-1/2)z)),
    v(z)=z^(-1).                                    (4)

Thus the negative state is exactly the free-delay port and the positive
state is exactly the Poisson/contact port. The direct sum of (3) is not
shorted. Apply the D.247 isometry first:

    (odd tangent,degree) --U_DC-->
       (even tangent,contact).                      (5)

The coherent/primitive contact split of D.256 is retained in (5). This is
the matrix operation that a scalar Euler product cannot perform.

At infinity retain the paired determinant scattering

    S_Gamma=diag(b_infty,b_infty^(-1))              (6)

on the two Real orientations. It is `J`-unitary and its oscillator
realization is the quarter-Gamma state system already constructed.

## 3. Retired formal Potapov--Redheffer lifting

and the Real Bohr matching on the resonant complements. Form the feedback
relation

    S_T^mat=S_Gamma star V_DC star
      (direct-sum_(p in S_T) S_(c_p)),              (7)

where every free-delay output is connected to its D.247 contact input and
the coherent degree output is connected only after the global prime sum.
No local degree short is performed.

If (7) were a well-posed inner operator-valued transfer on the upper
half-plane, its de Branges--Rovnyak kernel

    K_T^mat(w,z)=
      [I-S_T^mat(w)^*S_T^mat(z)]/[i(conjugate(w)-z)] (8)

is positive. Its boundary diagonal is the positive Wigner--Smith operator

    W_T^mat(tau)=(1/i)S_T^mat(tau)^*
                         dS_T^mat(tau)/dtau >=0.    (9)

The source port map `J_T` is fixed by the local Poisson states, the D.247
degree/contact coordinates and the two Tate constraints. The desired
factor is then

    D_T=K_T^mat(.,.)^(1/2) J_T                     (10)

in its canonical RKHS realization, not as a square root of (1).

## 4. Exact comparison that the formal lifting would require

The calculation has only one acceptable endpoint:

    J_T^*W_T^mat J_T
      =P_TE_T^*(K_T^*QK_T-Q)E_TP_T=Delta_T.        (11)

The logarithmic derivative calculation already proves equality of the
uncompressed signed local symbols. What is still required for (11) is that

the source landing without an extra state term.

Write the literal discrepancy

    M_T=Delta_T-J_T^*W_T^matJ_T.                   (12)

The route succeeds only by deriving from the state equations that `M_T=0`.
It is forbidden to alter `J_T`, the feedback order or the metric in order to
force this equality.
