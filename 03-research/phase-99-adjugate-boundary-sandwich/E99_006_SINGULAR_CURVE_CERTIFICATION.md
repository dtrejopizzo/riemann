# E99.006 - Singular characteristic-curve certification

## 1. Test geometry

A real symmetric matrix with simple spectrum was shifted by its lowest
eigenvalue, producing

```text
K=H-mu I,
det K=0,
partial_mu chi !=0.                                  (1.1)
```

An independent symmetric direction, a bordered minor and a complex safe row
were used at seventy-decimal precision.

## 2. Results

The direct characteristic Jacobian response and the corrected cofactor
sensitivity formula differed by

```text
1.13e-72.                                            (2.1)
```

The normalized characteristic adjugate satisfied

```text
Tr[adj(K)/partial_mu chi]+1=0                         (2.2)
```

to the working precision, while

```text
|partial_mu chi|=41.94.                              (2.3)
```

## 3. Conclusion

The certification confirms the sign and normalization in E99.001 on the
singular physical curve.  It also distinguishes the admissible normalized
adjugate from the nonexistent characteristic inverse.
