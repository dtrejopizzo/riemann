## Synthesis of round 1

The four lines converge on **a single question**, O164.101 ≡ O164.102.1:

> Does a **positive** Beurling system exist (prime measure ≥ 0) with ζ's exact window ((Λ(n))_{n<X}, X = e^{2T_c}),
> Γ(s/2), ζ's FE and simple pole, and an off-line zero in a cone of its witness?

Facts already proved about it: such a system has the same witness as ζ (Prop W); it must move **infinitely many**
of ζ's zeros (Thm H); it cannot have frequencies on log ℕ (Prop I, Hamburger); its tail cannot be a modification
with no new pole (Cor L.1: added positive mass with no pole creates no zeros, so the tail must **subtract** Euler
mass from ζ above X, ν₋ ≠ 0, with ν₋ ≤ μ_ζ). If it exists, L1 dies and D closes only by identifying ζ. If it does
not exist, it is a class lemma (S11) implying RH. Round 2 attacks it from both sides.
