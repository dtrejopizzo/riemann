## 2. The two findings that required independent re-derivation

### F1 — confirmed, and sharpened

Re-derived without using the audit's algebra. `s_C(γ,θ) = \max_n|\cos(nθ_u+θ)|/\cosh(n\log q/2)` with
`θ_u := γ\log q`. Minimising over `θ`, the binding entries at the optimum are `n = 0` (value
`|\cos θ|`, increasing as `θ → 0`) and `n = 1` (decreasing there), so the optimum is their balance:

    |\cos θ^*| = \frac{|\cos(θ_u+θ^*)|}{\cosh(\tfrac12\log q)}
    \;\Longrightarrow\;
    \tan θ^* = \frac{\cosh(\tfrac12\log q)+\cos θ_u}{\sin θ_u} = \frac{q+1+a}{\sqrt{4q−a^2}} ,

using `\cos θ_u = a/(2\sqrt q)`, `\sin θ_u = \sqrt{4q−a^2}/(2\sqrt q)` and
`\cosh(\tfrac12\log q) = (q+1)/(2\sqrt q)`. Then `(s_C^*)^2 = \cos^2θ^* = 1/(1+\tan^2θ^*)`, i.e.

    (s_C^*)^2 = \frac{4q−a^2}{(4q−a^2)+(q+1+a)^2} ,\qquad d_0^* = \frac1{1−(s_C^*)^2}
              = \frac{(4q−a^2)+(q+1+a)^2}{4q−a^2} .

**Agrees with the audit's closed form.** For the curve of record `q = 2, a = −1`: `\tan θ^* = 2/\sqrt7`,
`s_C^* = \sqrt{7/11} = 0.797724035217466`, `d_0^* = 11/4` exactly. The independently computed value
`0.79779 at θ ≈ 2.494` is the same optimum at the mirror point `π−θ^*` (`π − 0.647285 = 2.494308`),
to within its quoted precision; the exact value is `\sqrt{7/11} = 0.7977240352…`.

**Sharpening beyond the audit.** The audit's derivation balances `n ∈ {0,1}`, which makes the formula
valid only if no `|n| ≥ 2` entry exceeds it at `θ^*` — a side condition the audit did not state. I ran
the check: the side condition **holds**, and at this curve the supremum is an **exact triple tie**, at
`n = −2, 0, +1`. Proof in closed form (now Prop. D.41.12′): `\cos 2θ_u = −3/4`, `\sin 2θ_u = −\sqrt7/4`,
`\cosh(\log 2) = 5/4`, so `|\cos(−2θ_u+θ^*)|/\cosh(\log 2) = \tfrac{5\sqrt7}{4\sqrt{11}}\cdot\tfrac45
= \sqrt{7/11}`. The remaining entries are `≤ 0.620453`, and `|n| ≥ 5` gives `≤ 1/\cosh(\tfrac52\log2)
= 0.1754`. The side condition is therefore **printed as a hypothesis** of Prop. D.41.12′ and **verified**
for the record curve, rather than left implicit.

**F1(b) accepted in full and turned into MISMATCH 11.** The audit is right that the round-1 table
compared an optimum with a non-optimum, and right about why: on the line, `\min_θ s(γ_1,θ) = s(γ_1,π/2)
= s_1` because the nearest peak of `|\cos(γ_1u+θ)|` to `u = 0` is pushed furthest out at `θ = π/2`,
where `\cosh(u/2)` is largest; on the lattice `n = 0` is a sample point, `|\cos θ|` is in the max, and
`θ = π/2` — which kills that entry while raising the `n = ±1` entries — is no longer optimal. This is a
genuine and previously unrecorded discreteness effect and is now **MISMATCH 11**.

### F3 — confirmed, and (P145) withdrawn

Re-proved independently, frame-internally, with no model input; it is now **Proposition D.42.5** with a
converse. If `𝔈` is a **closed**, `𝓝_∞`-saturated convex cone containing `𝓔` and satisfying the degree
gate `E ∈ 𝔈, E ∉ 𝓝_∞ ⟹ \deg E > 0` (the property Paper 46 Theorem 12.3 Step 4 consumes, Thm 11.13(i)),
then

    [\,I_∞(D,D) > 0,\ \deg D > 0 \Rightarrow D ∈ 𝔈\,] \iff \textbf{RH} .

(⟹) contrapositive: if RH fails, Paper 45 Thm 10.4 gives a primitive `P ∉ 𝓝_∞` with `q = I_∞(P,P) > 0`;
`\deg(P+εK) = ε > 0` and `I_∞(P+εK,P+εK) = q+ε^2/2 > 0` (Thm 11.8: `I_∞(P,K) = ½\deg P = 0`,
`I_∞(K,K) = ½`), so `P+εK ∈ 𝔈` for every `ε > 0`; closedness gives `P ∈ 𝔈` with `\deg P = 0` — the gate
fails. (⟸) under RH, a closed-positive-cone class of degree `0` is primitive with
`I_∞(D,D) = −Σ_ρ|Φ_D(ρ)|^2 ≥ 0`, hence `= 0`, hence in `𝓝_∞` by Thm 11.11; so
`𝔈 = \overline{\mathrm{Pos}}+𝓔` witnesses both. ∎

**Two further defects of (P145) that the audit lists, both accepted and repaired in (P145′):** clause 4
concluded `D ∈ \overline{𝔈}` where `(b′)` needs an actual element (V.1.8 gives `nD` effective, i.e.
`D ∈ 𝔈⊗Q`); and (P145) never carried Theorem 11.14(ii)'s `C_F < ∞`, which Steps 1–2 need for ampleness
over the enlarged cone and which is itself a signature statement over `\mathrm{Pos}` — now clause (4c),
and opened as **O-39** with the explicit warning that it may be the binding constraint rather than (4a).

**Also accepted:** without the gate, `𝔈 := D^W` satisfies round-1 clauses 1–4, and only clause 5 blocked
it — the auditor's "sure sign the problem is mis-scoped". **(P145′) does not have this defect**:
`𝔈 = D^W` supplies no `H^0` and fails (4b).

**Not contested, and kept verbatim:** the auditor explicitly confirms the round-1 negative that
