### MAJOR 2 — D.51.6(ii) is true but trivial; the Epstein machinery and O-47.2 are not needed for it
**Location:** §0 item 3; **Theorem D.51.6(ii)**, and the blockquote calling it "the round's sharpest
general finding"; D.54; README.

**Defect.** "`𝓔` contains a class of positive degree whose `Φ` has infinitely many zeros in
`\mathrm{Re}\,s > 1`" needs neither Epstein, nor `h > 1`, nor Davenport–Heilbronn, nor any numerics, and

    E := 3φ + τ_{\log 2}φ ∈ D^W ,  E > 0 pointwise, \deg E > 0 ,
    Φ_E(s) = Φ_φ(s)\,(3 + 2^{\,s−½}) ,

with `Φ_φ` entire and zero-free (a Gaussian transform). Hence `Φ_E` vanishes exactly where

diagnosis (Paper 47 §19.9, "the image of the nonnegative cone … is a **moment cone**"): pointwise
nonnegativity constrains `Φ_E` **only on the real axis** (the round's own §D.51.5(e)), and says nothing
off it.

**Repair (required).** Replace the proof of D.51.6(ii) by the two-line witness; remove the dependence
on O-47.2 and on `D = 15`; demote the "sharpest general finding" framing. The Epstein membership table
(D.51.5) remains interesting on its own and should be kept — but as a table about `K_Q`, not as the
proof of a triviality.

### MAJOR 4 — two carriers are conflated throughout; the headline overclaims, and three statements are ill-typed
**Location:** §0 (Lemma D.50.2 as displayed); D.50.0; D.50.1; **D.50.2**; D.50.3(ii),(iii); D.50.9(i);

correspondence theory of Papers 43–44 whose carrier is `D_A = C_c^∞(C_S)`. "The carrier is radial" is a

The corpus is explicit that these are not the same: Paper 47 **Theorem 14.3(i)**, "`D_A^∞` contains
non-radial tests, **which `D^W` does not model**"; Paper 44 §3bis, "The radial family is not the only
one available. The **Frobenius family** … is **not** radial"; `R140_001:374`, "What exists is the
**action**: `Frob_n` acts on radial classes by the modulus."

So the true statement is about the **radial realization map** `R : \mathrm{Corr}(C_{K,S}) →
\mathrm{End}(D^W)`: `R(Γ_σ) = \mathrm{id}`. As correspondences `Γ_σ ≠ Δ`, `\mathrm{Frob}_{σ𝔫} ≠
\mathrm{Frob}_𝔫`, and `𝓔_{G,\mathrm{Frob}} ⊋ 𝓔_{\mathrm{Frob}}`. Consequences in the text:

* **D.50.3(ii)** pairs operators as if they were classes: `Γ_σ = Δ` is an identity of *operators*

* **D.50.9(i)**, "`𝓔_{\mathrm{Frob}} + 𝓔 = 𝓔`", requires `𝓔_{\mathrm{Frob}} ⊆ 𝓔`, which is false as
  typed: `\mathrm{Frob}_𝔫` realizes radially as a point mass at `\log N𝔫`, not as an element of `D^W`.
  (The inequality the round needs survives: the point-mass ratio is `|\cos(γu+θ)|/\cosh(u/2)` at
  `u = \log N𝔫`, so `s(𝓔_{\mathrm{Frob}}) ≤ \sup_u(\cdot) = s(γ,θ)`; but it must be proved that way.)
* **D.50.1(iv)** vs **D.50.3(iii)**: `Γ_σ⋆\mathrm{Frob}_𝔫⋆Γ_σ^{-1} = \mathrm{Frob}_{σ𝔫}` is a genuine

### MAJOR 6 — the `\cos(π/m)` lemma of D.53.5(ii) is proved wrongly and is false as an equality for odd `|G|`
**Location:** **Theorem D.53.5(ii)** and its proof; D.53.5(iii); D.54 bullet 1; §5 row 8.

**Defect.** The proof asserts: "`\min_θ\max_{0≤k<m}|\cos(θ+2πk/m)|` is attained at `θ = π/(2m)`, value
`\cos(π/m)`". Both halves are wrong. Because `|\cos|` has period `π`, the `m` angles reduce mod `π` to

`m = 4`: `θ^* = π/4`, which is what the script prints, **not** `π/8 = π/(2m)`) — and to `m` points
spaced `π/m` when `m` is odd, minimum `\cos(π/(2m))`. Recomputed:

| `m` | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 | 6 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| true `\min_θ\max_k` | 0.000000 | **0.866025** | 0.707107 | **0.951057** | 0.866025 |
| claimed `\cos(π/m)` | 0.000000 | 0.500000 | 0.707107 | 0.809017 | 0.866025 |

The stated **inequality** `s(𝓔_{\mathrm{Aut,Frob}}) ≥ \cos(π/|G|)` survives (it is weaker), and the
model's value `1/\sqrt2` at `m = 4` is correct; but D.53.5(iii)'s quantitative claim "the gain is
bounded away from `1` by the finiteness of `G`" is substantially weaker than advertised for odd `m`
(`\cos(π/(2m)) → 1` twice as fast), and the argmin printed in the proof is wrong.

**Repair (required).** State the lemma as: `\min_θ\max_{0≤k<m}|\cos(θ+2πk/m)| = \cos(π/m)` for `m`
