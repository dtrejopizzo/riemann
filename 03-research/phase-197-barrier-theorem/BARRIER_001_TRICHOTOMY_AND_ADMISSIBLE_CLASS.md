## 1. Setting (definitions)

Fix T>0, N=N_T=⌈e^{2T}⌉. A *comb* is a ∈ ℝ^{ℕ≥2}; its window part is π_N a=(a(n))_{n<N}. For a comb, put
v_n(a)=a(n)/√n and
  𝔞_T^{(a)}(F) = m_T(F) − Σ_{n<N} v_n(a) Re c_F(log n),   λ_A(T;a)=inf{𝔞_T^{(a)}(F): F∈P_T, ‖F‖=1},
with m_T the archimedean+polar part and c_F the autocorrelation, exactly as in Paper 46 (def:W, eq:nuw). WP_T(a) means λ_A(T;a)≥0.
Λ is the von Mangoldt comb; the target is WP_T(Λ) for every T.

**Definition 1 (method).** A method at window T is a pair 𝔐=(S,Ψ):
- S ⊆ ℝ^{ℕ≥2}, Λ∈S, the *premise class* = all combs satisfying the premises the method uses (a finite conjunction of atoms
  from the catalogue 𝒜 of §2, or any set);
- Ψ:S→[−∞,∞) the *conclusion*, with soundness Ψ(a) ≤ λ_A(T;a) for every a∈S.
𝔐 *decides* T if Ψ(Λ)≥0. 𝔐 is *uniform* if Ψ is constant on S (a deduction "premises ⇒ WP_T", the thm:shape setting).
𝔐 is *linear-inference* (fragment 𝓛_lin) if Ψ(a) is the value of a finite combination of shift-multiplier identities and
Bochner squares ‖A∗F‖²≥0 and a pointwise symbol bound, i.e. of the form inf_τ[s_T^{(a)}+ν̂+β·ĝ] (lem:shape-lin(i): these
are exactly measure identities on (−2T,2T) plus Bochner positivity; N_018).

**Allowed operations as closure properties.** The method calculus is generated by: (O-lin) affine operations in a;
(O-conv) Dirichlet convolution/inverse, multiplication by ℂ(s); (O-place) place-wise products and place-wise identities;
(O-cone) membership of log ζ_a or −ζ_a′/ζ_a in a cone; (O-∂log) passage through ∂log; (O-id) exact identities (Green, gauge,
explicit formula); (O-sq) Cauchy–Schwarz/Bochner; (O-est) main term + remainder. §2 assigns each its premise invariance.

**Falsifier family 𝔉_T.** a_P (P>N, prop:shape-eulerdel), cΛ (c≠1, O_4), the zero comb 0, generic Beurling systems reaching the
line (O_2 family), Epstein Ξ_Q (prop:shape-inputs(ii)). λ_A(T;f)<0 is PROVED for a_P on [0.60, ½log P], for cΛ (c large,
T>½log2; c small, T≥0.60), for 0 at T≥0.60 (cor:Tarch); CITED for Ξ_Q for the complete form.

## 3. The barrier theorem

**Theorem 3 (trichotomy).** Let T≥0.60 and 𝔐=(S,Ψ) a method whose premise class is a finite conjunction of atoms of 𝒜, and
whose conclusion is uniform or in 𝓛_lin. Then exactly one holds:
(i) **comb-generic**: π_N S contains π_N f for some f∈𝔉_T with λ_A(T;f)<0; a uniform 𝔐 does not decide T.
(ii) **dual certificate**: Ψ∈𝓛_lin; then Ψ(Λ) ≤ λ_P(T) (prop:weak), and sup over such Ψ equals λ_P(T) (thm:D1); the gauge-
identity methods are this case (thm:strip-T4, INV_T T1/T4).
(iii) **REF-D**: π_N S={π_N Λ} and Ψ is uniform: the method's statement "WP_T on S" is WP_T(Λ) itself, i.e. 𝔞_T≥0 on P_T.
Labels: PROVED for T≥0.60 given Lemma 2, except the A2∧loose-FE sub-case (ARGUED) and A8, A9 (CONDITIONAL/CITED).

*Proof.* If Ψ∈𝓛_lin, by lem:shape-lin(i) Ψ(a) is the value of a triple (ν,β) certificate for the comb a, so Ψ(Λ)≤λ_P≤λ_A by
weak duality and sup=λ_P by thm:D1: (ii). Otherwise Ψ is uniform, Ψ≡c, and soundness gives c ≤ inf_{a∈S} λ_A(T;a). By Lemma
2(d), either (a)/(b): then f=a_P with P>e^{2T} (λ_A(T;a_P)<0 for T∈[0.60,½log P], PROVED) or Ξ_Q (CITED) lies in S, so c<0: (i).
Or (c): π_N S={π_NΛ}, so by window collapse inf_S λ_A=λ_A(T;Λ) and "c≥0" is the statement WP_T(Λ): (iii). ∎

Remark (T<0.60). On (½log2,0.60) the falsifier is cΛ (c large) for A2/A3-type classes (PROVED, lem:shape-scale); a_P and 0 do
not fail there. The theorem is stated for T≥0.60, which is where row D is open beyond the Paper 46 certificates.

Remark (estimates). Ψ of the form main term − remainder is either uniform (covered) or, if its main term is exact, in 𝓛_lin; a
remainder with slack is bounded by the margin (B′, lem:shape-est(i)–(iii)): no separate outcome.

## 4. Minimality and sharpness of the closure set

**Proposition 4.** Each of the four generators of 𝔉_T is necessary: for each there is a natural atom conjunction whose only
falsifier in 𝔉_T is that one.
- a_P: S = A3(𝒞_01) ∧ pole at 1 ∧ A7 (zeros of ζ). Excludes 0 (pole), cΛ c≠1 (𝒞_01), Ξ_Q (Euler from 𝒞_01 + ζ-zeros fails for
  Epstein) — contains a_P. PROVED (from I2, prop:shape-eulerdel).
- cΛ: S = A2 ∧ "a(p^k)/log p constant in p" — contains cΛ only. PROVED (trivially; shows O_4 cannot be dropped below 0.60? no:
  at T≥0.60 also c small fails; sharpness PROVED for T∈(½log2,0.60) where only O_4 acts).
- Ξ_Q: S = A9 ∧ Γ(s/2)-type of the complete form, no Euler. CITED.
- 0 / Beurling: S = A3(𝒞_01) without pole contains 0; A8 classes contain generic Beurling systems. PROVED / CONDITIONAL.

**Sharpness (does any method violating exactly one closure escape?).** Violating closure under O_6 alone (adding an atom that
a_P fails) forces an FE atom (a_P breaks only FE); then Lemma 2(b),(c) send it to Ξ_Q or to pinning. So **no** method violating
only O_6-closure escapes (PROVED modulo the ARGUED sub-case). Violating O_4 alone: A2 classes with c restricted to a
neighbourhood of 1 — escapes O_4 for large/small c but meets O_4^ε when κ_T≠0 (CONDITIONAL, lem:shape-scale). Violating
uniformity alone (non-uniform, non-𝓛_lin Ψ) **does** escape the theorem: that is door (D2) of §6. Violating "conjunction of
atoms of 𝒜" alone (a premise outside the catalogue) escapes: door (D1). Hence the minimal forcing set is
{conjunction-of-𝒜, uniform-or-𝓛_lin}; nothing smaller suffices (PROVED: §6 exhibits Ψ=λ_A(T;·) itself as a sound non-uniform
method on S=ℝ^N, which escapes (i),(ii) and is (iii) only because it is the target; any computable surrogate is door D2).

## 5. Quantitative localisation of any escaping premise class (new, PROVED)

**Theorem 5 (half-space).** Let F⋆ be a real unit minimizer of 𝔞_T^{(Λ)}, w_T=(c_{F⋆}(log n)/√n)_{n<N}. Any uniform method
deciding T has π_N S ⊆ H_T := {π_N a : ⟨π_N a−π_NΛ, w_T⟩ ≤ λ_A(T;Λ)}, and for T≥1, λ_A(T;Λ) ≤ 4·10⁹ T e^{−15.449T}.
*Proof.* lem:degen: λ_A(T;a) ≤ λ_A(T;Λ) − ⟨a−Λ,w_T⟩ for all a (supergradient of the concave map a↦λ_A). If a∈S, uniform
soundness with c≥0 gives 0 ≤ λ_A(T;a), hence the inequality. The bound is lem:shape-margin(i). ∎
Consequence: a decisive premise class may be large (up to codimension one) but must be pinned in the single direction w_T to
precision e^{−15.4T}, at every T simultaneously; a_P, cΛ, 0 are outside H_T at T≥0.60 (consistent with (i)).

## 6. The complement: the admissible class (constructive specification)

Theorem 3 leaves exactly two doors, and Theorem 5 constrains the first.

**(D1) Intermediate premise atoms.** An atom 𝔄 (not in 𝒜) such that the class S_𝔄 = {a : 𝔄(a)}:
 (D1.a) contains Λ, excludes π_N of every f∈𝔉_T (in particular separates Λ from a_P, so 𝔄 involves the global FE at
 conductor 1 — a_P breaks nothing else, PROVED);
 (D1.b) is not pinning: π_N S_𝔄 has positive dimension (otherwise (iii), cf. window Hamburger R163_1001c);
 (D1.c) π_N S_𝔄 ⊆ H_T for all T≥0.60 (Theorem 5): a codimension-≥1 constraint aligned with w_T to precision e^{−15.4T};
 (D1.d) is non-linear in a and not a cone, not coefficientwise, not sparse-stable, not place-wise (else it is in A1–A6 and
 Lemma 2(a) applies).
 Concretely: an **FE-defect inequality on the truncated comb**: a computable functional Δ_T(a) ≥ 0 of π_N a built from the
 global FE (e.g. from D_{S_N}=ξ_S(s)−ξ_S(1−s), INV_P P1/P2, whose low band separates Λ from a_P — ARGUED there), with Δ_T(Λ)
 small, and {Δ_T ≤ ε_T} ⊆ {λ_A(T;·) ≥ 0}. This is a *stability form of Hamburger rigidity with a sign*: rigidity says
 Δ=0 pins ζ; the missing statement is that small defect controls the bottom in the direction w_T. OPEN.

**(D2) Non-uniform, non-certificate conclusions.** Ψ(T;a), sound on a class containing a_P (so Ψ(a_P)<0), with Ψ(Λ)≥0,
not of the form inf_τ[s_T+ν̂+β·ĝ] (else (ii)), not main-term+remainder with slack (B′). Ψ must be non-affine in π_N a
(an affine sound Ψ on a class with a_P and Λ is a certificate by lem:shape-lin/N_018 — PROVED for convolution-calculus
derivations; ARGUED in general) and must read the global FE (to separate a_P). Concrete form:
  λ_A(T;a) ≥ −C_T · Δ_T(a)  for all Euler-product combs a near Λ (A4 class),
a *Łojasiewicz/stability inequality for the bottom along the FE defect*. With Δ_T(Λ)=0 it decides every T. OPEN.

**6.3 Why this is sharper than prop:spec and different from the index.** prop:spec (1)–(6) are separate necessary
conditions, ARGUED as a set; here: (1)–(4),(6) are absorbed into Lemma 2 (atom types A1–A6, PROVED except one sub-case);
clause (5) becomes (D1.a) with the extra quantitative constraint (D1.c) and the non-pinning constraint (D1.b), which no clause
of prop:spec states. Not a retry: R162_2801 is quantitative rigidity on the zero side for off-line quartets (dies on
Beurling); R163_1001c is window Hamburger as identity (pinning = (iii)); here the object is a comb-side defect inequality
that is by construction non-pinning and aligned with w_T.

**Missing mathematics, one sentence.** A non-linear inequality, uniform in T, that bounds the bottom of the window form of a
truncated Euler-product comb from below by (minus) a defect of the global functional equation at conductor 1, i.e. a
quantitative, signed Hamburger theorem in the direction of the minimizer's autocorrelation.
