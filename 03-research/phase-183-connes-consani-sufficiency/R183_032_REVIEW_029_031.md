  diagonal is f̃(1) = f(1). The trace log(1/b)·f(1) has the **correct sign**: the space grows
  as b decreases. It matches CC (43)'s 2log(1/λ)f(1) for b = λ².

  HS, but M_{f_i} is not HS on all of ℝ. One must write M_{g_i}1_L = 1_{L'}M_{g_i}1_L, with
