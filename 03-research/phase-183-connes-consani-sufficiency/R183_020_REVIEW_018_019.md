# R183.020 — Review of R183.018 and R183.019

## Verdict

The two builders, independently and via different mechanisms
(R183.018: pairs of primitive bumps at distance log 2; R183.019: Toeplitz
symbol ~(¼+s²)cos(s log p) in the ξ metric), prove the same NO-GO:

    If T>½log 2 and S contains a prime p with log p<2T, the remainder
    R_{S,T} = E_S − Son has infinite negative index on the primitive
    tests, even within ker(h↦∫h).

Hence **R183.017's obligation 2 is false as stated**: no form
R ≥ −b|v><v| + c(I−|v><v|) exists with the entire Sonin trace as the
positive part. Connes–Consani's mechanism (Lemma 6.9, Theorem 6.11 in v1)
is exhausted exactly at T=½log 2 for a structural reason, not a
numerical limitation: the prime term is a truncated, non-compact
Wiener–Hopf operator, with eigenvalues of both signs of infinite
multiplicity, and Son does not absorb it if subtracted whole.

This does NOT refute E_S≥0 nor CC's Conjecture 4.1: E_S = Son + R can be
positive on the same subspaces (Son grows like w^{-3}log(1/w), the prime
part like w^{-3}).

## The only point short of PROVED

Boundedness of ε on [1,e^{2T}] for large T. CC only control ε
explicitly on [1,2] (Prop. 5.5). But by (84) and Lemma 5.4,

    ε(ρ) = Σ_n λ(n)²/(1−λ(n)²) · ρ^{1/2}∫_{ρ^{-1}}^1 ξ_n^{a_n}(x)ξ_n^{a_n}(ρx)dx,

and Cauchy–Schwarz gives |ρ^{1/2}∫…| ≤ ‖ξ_n^{a_n}‖₂², uniform in ρ≥1. Hence

    sup_{ρ≥1}|ε(ρ)| ≤ Σ_n λ(n)²/(1−λ(n)²)‖ξ_n^{a_n}‖₂²,

which converges by the same superexponential decay of λ(n) that CC
invoke via (100). This reduces the weak point to a one-line lemma
(Lemma ε-L∞), granted as PROVED modulo convergence of that series,
which is the same one CC use. Final label for Thm 5.1: **PROVED**
(with Lemma ε-L∞), consistent across both files.
