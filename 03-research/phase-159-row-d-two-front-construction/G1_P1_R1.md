## §1 G1_B2 — laminations, leafwise L², the suspension wall

### 1.1 What was checked (`PROVED` here)
* **Prop 1 (conformal relocation).** Correct. In real dimension 2, `ω ∧ ⋆ω̄` depends only on the conformal class, and
  orientation-preserving conformal maps commute with `⋆` on 1-forms. The result is classical (conformal invariance of
  the middle-degree Dirichlet form). The memo rightly claims no literature novelty.
* **Theorem C on `Y_K`, `d = 2`.** I re-derived (ii) on the character `χ_v`:
  * `dω = 0` gives `v b_v = v̄ a_v`;
  * `d*ω = 0` gives `v̄ a_v + v b_v = 0`;
  * hence `a_v = b_v = 0` for `v ≠ 0`.
  Only constant forms are harmonic. Correct.
* **Theorem D (suspension along a dense length group).** (i)–(iii) are correct. The mixing step in Cor D1 is correct:
  * for fixed `v, v′` at most one `κ` has `κv = v′`;
  * `N(K^×)` is dense in `R_{>0}`;
  * so distinct `κ_n` with `log N(κ_n) → 0` exist, and the matrix coefficients vanish eventually.

### 1.2 Lens 1
The construction reads no zero and no `Λ(n)`, so it is not circular. It is prime-blind, so OT-4 fails. Its positive
form is prior (OT-5′ "vacuous pass"), but by the memo's own Cor D1 the positive space contains no zero. This is a
**proved exclusion of the candidate carrier**, not an implication toward RH.

### 1.3 A local error that worsens the exclusion (`PROVED`)
For `d = 1` (`K = Q`), Cor D1 states: "`V_c = C` in degrees 0 and 1 (top), with `Θ = 0, 1`". This is false for the
memo's own normalisation `u_1(κ) = N(κ)^{−1}m_κ^*`:
* `m_κ^*(dx) = κ dx`, so on the constant 1-form `u_1(κ) = κ/|κ| = sgn κ`;
* `κ = −1 ∈ ker ℓ` acts by `−1`, so `H^{ker ℓ} = 0` in degree 1, and `V_c = 0` there.

The same sign spoils the claimed modulus in Construction 3, "`|κ_∞|^{(1)} = |κ|`". For oriented leaves `R`, `m_{−1}` is
orientation-reversing: `C_ν ∘ m_κ^* = κ·C_ν`, so `δ` is not `R_{>0}`-valued, and Prop 1 does not apply (its leaves also
have the wrong dimension). So the suspension of `Y_Q` retains **only `H⁰` (`Θ = 0`)**, and not even the pole.

The polar pair for `Q` could be restored only with an orientation twist: densities `|dx|` in place of forms, i.e. the
sign character. That is an extra choice the memo does not make. The error is not load-bearing for the verdict, but the
§4 table ("`Y_Q` gives the polar pair only") must be corrected.

### 1.4 Remaining weak points
* N-d is `HEURISTIC` outside suspensions of Koopman representations of compact abelian solenoids. That is the memo's
  own scope, and I confirm it.
* Prop 2's correction of Doc 130 is plausible. In the CM case, `|ξ|² = deg(Frob) = q` is the positivity of the norm
  form of an imaginary quadratic field, i.e. Rosati on `End⁰(E)`: geometric and prior. But it rests on `CITED`
  canonical-lift facts, and it concerns `F_q`, not `Spec Z`. It should not enter the record as "the example is
  Weil-shaped" until someone re-reads math/0204194 against the hypotheses on `E`.

**Verdict: EXCLUDED.** Fatal step: Cor D1 (`H¹ = 0` after suspension). Salvageable: Prop 1; Theorem D as a filter
(N-d, `HEURISTIC` scope); the modular-solenoid test proposed for round 2 (Hecke correspondences, non-abelian), which
Theorem D does not cover.

### 2.2 Lens 1 (the classification)
* **Theorem C is Lemma T(⇒) (Serre's argument) with unitarity weakened to polynomial growth.** Its conclusion is
  "complete Lefschetz clause ⟹ RH", i.e. OT-2 is RH-strength on this class. For Connes' `L²_δ` that is already on record
  as A17 (the global trace formula ⟺ RH; Connes 1999 §VIII Thm 5). R158_004 and G1_B6 Thm 7 contain the same statement.
  **Class (ii).**
* **"OT-5′ holds automatically" is a redefinition.** OT-5′ (R158_001 §3.4) asks for a *positive form*, P-HS or P-HI,
  compatible with the flow. A tempered-growth bound on an ambient space has neither property:
  * it gives no hermitian square, so Cor T′ (`W_T(F) = ‖F(U)‖²_{HS}`) is unavailable;
  * it gives no per-window positivity;
  * it only places eigenvalues *once OT-2 has put them there*.

  must not be recorded as "OT-5′ PASS". The correct entry is "OT-5′ replaced by a weaker condition, and OT-2 RH-strength".
* **Cor S1 ("N-b is an artifact of isometry").** R158_005 already restricts N-b to "the definite/unitary route only".
  Cor S1 changes route; it removes nothing. On the slope route the multiplicity cap is a real price, since `S₃` fields
  have double zeros, so OT-10 needs `N` to grow with `K`, as the memo states.
* **Remark 2.2 (Connes' cap).** Lemma S gives chains `k ≤ 1 + δ/2` from `‖U_t‖ ≍ (1+|t|)^{δ/2}`. Connes' cap, as
  verified in R158_004 row 14, is `k < (1+δ)/2`, which is sharper by about ½. So Lemma S is *weaker* than Connes' cap,
  not "its abstract form". The mismatch comes from the Hilbert structure: the `log^{k−1}` eigenfunctional must be
  square-integrable against the weight. Minor.

**Verdict: RESTATEMENT.** Fatal step: Theorem C(iv) and §6 count OT-5′ as passed after redefining it; with the actual
OT-5′, nothing is passed, and OT-2 = Connes Thm 5. Salvageable:
* the local statements L1–L3, a clean elementary answer to the archimedean half of the Outlook question of CC
  2606.06604;
* the "Completeness problem (C_δ)" stated as a named RH-equivalent, so that no one resubmits it.

### 3.3 Local overclaim (`PROVED`)
Cor 8.1(iii) says the bound implies "RH ∧ simple zeros ∧ Riesz basis, strictly more than RH". A uniformly bounded group
forces **semisimplicity** (no Jordan blocks), not simplicity. On a non-cyclic `H¹`, a double zero can appear as a

(Meyer) `H¹` (rows 7, 11). So "simple zeros" needs cyclicity as an extra hypothesis. The same qualification applies to
the `S₃` failure argument in Construction 9, which the memo correctly marks "modulo cyclicity". The correct statement is
"RH ∧ semisimple ∧ Riesz basis (if pure point), and simple zeros if `H¹` is cyclic".

The (P2) converse "`ℓ_ρ ⊥ V ⟹ ξ(ρ) = 0`" is standard Tate, and I accept it as `CITED`. Note that `ℓ_ρ` are eigenvectors of
the *adjoint* flow on `(H¹_w)^*`, not eigenvectors in `H¹_w`. "Every strip zero visible" is therefore a statement about
the dual.

**Verdict: RESTATEMENT.** Fatal step: Construction 9 (O3) ⟺ a uniform bound that implies RH; (O1)/(O2) OPEN; Thm 2 =
