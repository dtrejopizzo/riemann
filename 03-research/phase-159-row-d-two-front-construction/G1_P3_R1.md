by switching to densities `|dx|` (orientation twist), which the memo does not do.

## §2 G1_B3_R1 — Lemma S, Theorem C, Lemma N, local Tate carriers

**Checked and correct.**

* Theorem C(i): the quotient-seminorm computation uses `U_tW_2 = W_2`.
* Lemma N. For `h = 1_{[1,∞)}f` one has `U_λh = 1_{[λ,∞)}U_λf`; isometry then gives constant tail norms, and order

**Load-bearing defect (why this is a restatement and not an OT-5′ pass).** §0.1 and the §6 row claim "OT-5′ holds
automatically, in the relaxed slope-½ form". R158_001 OT-5′ asks for a **positive form** (P-HS or P-HI) "defined from
geometry of `𝒪` alone", with its compatibility proved. A polynomial growth bound is not a positive form, and it gives
no `W_T(F) = ‖F(U)‖²` identity (OT-8 is only "partial" in the memo's own table). With OT-5′ read as the record
defines it, Theorem C says:
* a prior growth bound on the ambient space, together with (D2) on a subquotient, implies RH.

That is Lemma T(⇒), i.e. Serre's argument (R158_004 row 11: "(a)⇒RH is Serre's argument"), with isometry replaced by
temperedness. Connes 1999 Thm 1 already works exactly this way (`L²_δ`). The conclusion "OT-2 is RH-strength in this
class" is correct, and it is the content of R158_003 A17 (Connes' first failure is OT-2).

**Overclaim.** Cor S1, "N-b is an artifact of isometry", presents as new something R158_005 §2 already scopes: "N-b:
for the definite/unitary route only". It is a correct remark with no new content.

**Unverified.** The match of Remark 2.2 with Connes' multiplicity cap. Connes caps multiplicity `< (1+δ)/2`, while
order `N = δ/2` allows chains `≤ δ/2 + 1`. The two are consistent but do not coincide. Not load-bearing.

**Verdict: restatement.**
**Salvageable.** The completeness problem (C_δ) is correctly stated as OPEN and RH-strength, equivalent to Connes Thm 5,
and it is useful as a do-not-resubmit marker. Lemma N is a clean, correct lemma. Props L1–L3 are correct and elementary.
