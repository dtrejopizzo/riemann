## 0. Normalisations (they differ between memos, and they agree)

The memos define the rate κ in two ways:
- **B1 and B9:** `κ := limsup T^{−1} log⁺(−λ_T)`. The theorem reads: `λ ≥ −Ce^{κT}` ⟹ no zeros in `Re s > ½ + κ/2`.
- **B2, B3 and B5:** `κ := limsup (2T)^{−1} log⁺(−λ_T)`. The theorem reads: `λ ≥ −Ce^{2εT}` ⟹ no zeros in
  `Re s > ½ + ε`.

These are the same statement. The sandwich is `2Θ−1 ≤ κ ≤ 1` in the first normalisation and `Θ−½ ≤ κ ≤ ½` in the
second. All five memos also agree that P5_R3's heuristic exponent (`e^{4εT}`) was off by a factor 2. Whether B1 or B3
is right about which direction the factor goes, the proved statement is the same. **Any integration into Paper 47 must
fix one normalisation.**

## 1. The graded-converse cluster: B1, B2 (Thm G1), B3, B5, B9

### 1.1 Common structure (checked)

All five proofs follow the same three steps.
1. **Bottom bound ⟹ pointwise bound on a smoothed error.**
  - Two bumps at `±x/2`, or `±T`, with a gap of order `x`, lie in `P_T`. The form `𝔞_T + M⟨·,·⟩` is positive
  semidefinite (PSD) there, so Cauchy–Schwarz for the PSD form gives `|g(x)| ≤ g(0) + M(T)‖·‖²`.
  - B3 uses only the one-sided version of this bound, and then needs Landau's theorem.
2. **Laplace transform on `Re s > ½`.** It gives `Φ(s)Φ(−s)·(ζ′/ζ)(½+s)` plus terms that are holomorphic on
  `Re s > −½`.
3. **Continuation to `Re s > κ/2`.** A zero `ρ₀` with `Re ρ₀ > ½ + κ/2` gives a pole of `ζ′/ζ`. A bump with
  `Φ(ρ₀−½)Φ(½−ρ₀) ≠ 0` exists, because a real vector space is not a union of two proper subspaces, and `ρ₀ ≠ 0, 1`.

I re-derived the load-bearing identities of each proof against P47's conventions. Everything checks.

- **B1.**
  - `𝔞_T(ψ_{−x/2}, ψ_{x/2}) = g_ψ(x)`, since `F̂ \overline{Ĝ} = e^{−iτx}|ψ̂|²`.
  - `⟨F,G⟩ = c_ψ(x) = 0` for `x ≥ 2ε`.
  - Every contact with `c_ψ(x − log n) ≠ 0` has `log n < x + 2ε < 2T = x + 4ε`, so it lies in the window.
  - The Lévy term off the diagonal is `k_ψ = −½∫c_ψ(x−u)K(u)du = O(e^{−x/2})`.
  - `∫c_ψ e^{−zv} = Ψ(z)Ψ(−z)`.
- **B2 (Lemma T, Thm G1).**
  - `A(Dh(·−c)) = 0`, since `De^{±t/2} = 0`.
  - `Γ̃(s) = (¼−s²)²H(s)H(−s)`.
  - The identity `∫_2^∞Γ(log n − x)e^{−sx}dx = n^{−s}Γ̃(s)` needs `log n ≥ 3`, hence `n ≥ 21`. That bookkeeping is right.
- **B3.**
  - `c_{F_T} = 2c_f + c_f(·−2T) + c_f(·+2T)`, and `A(F_T) = 2cosh(T/2)A(f)`.
  - Lemma 1, `k_∞ = −e^{−|y|/2}/(1−e^{−2|y|})` off 0: I checked that this equals `−½K(|y|)`, with
  `K = ½/sinh(u/2) + ½/cosh(u/2)`, as P47 eq:A requires.
  - Landau's theorem is applied correctly. `φ = g_f + Ke^{εx} ≥ 0`. The right-hand side has no real singularity on
  `(ε, ∞)`, because ζ has no real zeros in `(0,1)` (CITED), `E` is entire and `H` is holomorphic on `Re s > −½`.
- **B5.**
  - The primitive PSD form `⟨P²(u_Λ + M_Aδ), G⋆H̃⟩` is correct, because `c_{PG} = P²c_G`: the Fourier multiplier is
  `(τ²+¼)²`. Also `(P²c_G)(0) = ‖PG‖²`.
  - Step 0, rescaling one bump so that `G₁(cs₀) ≠ 0`, is valid.
- **B9.**
  - Lemma J: `W(F_±) = 2S(0) ± 2S(x)` and `‖F_±‖² ≤ 4‖φ‖²` give `|S(x)| ≤ S(0) + 2λ^−‖φ‖²`.
  - The Theorem G bookkeeping holds: `Σ|b|n^{−½−σ} = σ∫e^{−σX}B_b(X)dX`.

### 1.2 Lens 1 verdict for the cluster

- **(iii) Circularity: none.** No zeros and no RH-strength input enter the hypothesis. The zeros appear only as the
  object of the conclusion.
- **(i) A genuine new implication toward the target: none.** Every one of these theorems is the **converse** arrow:
  lower bound on the bottom ⟹ zero-free half-plane. G2 asks for the direct arrow, from exact Λ to ∀T `λ ≥ 0`.
- **(ii) Equivalent reformulation.** Each memo's Corollary Q1 / G2 / G1 states that `RH ⟺ κ = 0 ⟺ −λ_T = e^{o(T)}`.
  - This is Weil's criterion (P45 Thm 10.4, Bombieri 2000 Thm 1) made robust by von Koch–Landau. On the prime side it
  is exactly `ψ(x) − x ≪ x^{½+ε}`, or, as B3 says precisely, Landau's one-sided version.
  - The graded statements for `κ ∈ (0, ½)` or `(0, 1)` are genuine implications, but they lead to quasi-RH
  conclusions. No known method proves their hypotheses, and R157_000 O2 excludes quasi-RH inputs as a route. The
  memos' Cor Q3 / Q1″ is the output-side mirror of O2, and I accept that reading.
  - **EQUIVALENT (Weil + von Koch–Landau).** All five memos say so themselves.

### 1.3 The "sign normalisation" claim (B3 §4, B5 §4, B9 §5)

These memos claim that O3 (`rem:resweights`), `thm:margin`, `cor:void` and `thm:finmod` bind only the *sign* target,
and not the EQUIVALENT rate target `κ = 0`.

- **As a statement about implications, this is PROVED and correct.** B3's Prop I and B5's Q2 show that `κ` is
  invariant under `S_b = X^{o(1)}` perturbations. So an estimate with `e^{o(T)}` slack, if one existed, would give RH,
  and then Weil would give the exact sign.
- **The claim does not reopen G2.** The rate target is literally the classical problem:
  - F4 (Ξ_a, Epstein, DMV/Zhang) and T1 still bind it in full;
  - O2 still binds every intermediate exponent;
  - no known prime-side method gives `κ < 1` (B9 normalisation).
- **Note (HEURISTIC).** The G2 brief's clause "must be an exact identity, not an estimate" should be read
  as a filter on arguments that aim at the *per-window sign*. For an asymptotic growth argument the operative filters
  are O2, F4 and T1. This is a clarification of the brief, not a new route.
