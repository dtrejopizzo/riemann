## G1_B2_R1 — excluded

**Checked.**
* Prop 1 (conformal invariance of the 1-form L² pairing in leaf dimension 2) is the classical conformal invariance of
  the middle-degree Dirichlet form. `PROVED` (trivial).
* Prop 2(i) is correct: `μ̃`-invariance is a lattice index.
  * Prop 2(ii)–(iii): "Hasse enters only via `H²` as a degree identity". This is literally the classical proof of Hasse
  for elliptic curves: `deg` is a positive-definite quadratic form on `End(E)`, and `deg π = q` gives `|ξ|² = q`.
  * So the "prior positivity" in Deninger's example is the Rosati/degree positivity, which was known before Deninger
  wrote it down.
  * The correction to Doc 130 is fair. It is not new mathematics.
* Thm C (leafwise L² cohomology of `A_K/K` = constant forms) is standard Pontryagin-duality bookkeeping. `PROVED`.
* Thm D:
  * (ii) is the correct description of `C(R,H)`-equivariant sections along a dense subgroup;
  * (iii) is the standard "weak mixing kills the continuous extension" argument.
  * Cor D1: the matrix coefficient `⟨u(κ_n)χ_v, χ_{v′}⟩` is nonzero for at most one `n`. `PROVED`.

**Literature.**
* Compact adelic solenoids with 1-dimensional leaves, and their leafwise cohomology, are the standard examples in:
  * Deninger, *Arithmetic geometry and analysis on foliated spaces*, arXiv:math/0505354;
  * Deninger, *Number theory and dynamical systems on foliated spaces*, math/0204110, Rem 2–3 (dense length group);
  * Leichtnam, arXiv:1307.3851 (foliated trace formulas);
  * Morishita, arXiv:2508.15971, relating Deninger systems to Connes–Consani adelic spaces (abstract checked).
* In those works the observation that a dense `log N(K^×)` has no Hausdorff suspension is exactly C5.
* The complex-leaf variant `A_K/K` for imaginary quadratic `K` is a routine extension. I found no statement of it, and I
  accept "not in the corpus". Its failure mode is the known one.

**Fatal step (for G1).** Cor D1 itself: the suspended carrier has `H¹ = 0`, so OT-1 and OT-2 fail and OT-4 fails
(prime-blind). This is an exclusion by the record's C5/C16, now with a proof on this carrier.

**Objection to the headline.**
* N-d is proved necessary only for suspension carriers, and shown to fail only for Koopman actions on compact abelian
  solenoids.
* It is a heuristic filter, not a theorem about all objects. It must not enter the record as a proved necessary
  condition.
* The non-abelian (Shimura/Hecke) case is untested (`OPEN`). The expected outcome there, control of Satake parameters
  (Ramanujan-type) and not of ζ-zeros, is itself a known phenomenon: Petersson positivity gives unitarity of the Hecke
  action, which says nothing about `L`-zeros.

**Salvageable.** Theorem D(iii) is a clean "mixing kills suspension" lemma. It is worth recording as a method
constraint.

## G1_B3_R1 — restatement

**Checked.**
* Lemma S(i)–(ii): the growth argument is correct.
* Theorem C(i): the quotient seminorm estimate is correct.
* Lemma N: support shifting plus order continuity. This is correct, and classical (dilations on `L^p`, `p < ∞`, have no
  eigenfunctions).
* Prop L1 (Liouville on `E_p`) and Prop L3 (translations act trivially on cohomology) are correct and elementary.
* Prop M is a tautology, as the memo says.

**Literature.**
* Theorem C(iii) is the abstract content of Connes, Selecta 1999 (math/9811068), Thm 1: on a polynomially weighted
  carrier only critical zeros appear, and "all zeros appear" ⟺ RH (§VIII Thm 5).
* Lemma S is the standard growth-bound/Jordan-chain fact.
* The FF remarks (§5) are readings of Colmez–Fontaine and Fargues–Fontaine, correctly labelled `HEURISTIC`.

**Fatal step.** None is false. The candidate is by its own §6 verdict a RESTATEMENT: OT-2 in the class is RH ∧ a
multiplicity cap.

**Objection.** Cor S1's slogan "N-b is an artifact of isometry" overreaches.
* A fixed-order-`N` tempered norm on a carrier that realises all zeros would impose `m_ρ ≤ N+1` on ζ. That is unknown,
  so the relaxed OT-5′ is still stronger than RH on such a carrier.
* For field uniformity it forces `N` to grow with `K`, as the memo admits.
* So N-b is weakened, not removed.

**Salvageable.** The "conservation law" (Theorem C + Lemma N) is a useful map statement. It says that in the
subquotient class the RH content sits in completeness, i.e. OT-2. It duplicates Theorem 7 of B6.

## G1_B6_R1 — restatement

**Checked.**
* Thm 1: the archimedean log divergence against the `‖c_F‖_{L¹}` bound for a state is correct.
* Thm 3: the invariant projection commutes with `U`. `PROVED`, one line.
* Thm 4: the scaling-measure argument, including the `a ≠ 0` total-mass case, is correct.
* Thm 7 is correct, with one convention slip: `‖U_s‖² = ess sup w(u−s)/w(u)`, not `w(u+s)/w(u)`. It is harmless.
* Thm 8 is Sz.-Nagy 1947 (a uniformly bounded ℝ-representation is similar to a unitary one), `CITED`, and correctly
  applied.

**Gaps.**
1. **Thm 2(ii), "dense range".** The finite interpolation argument does not by itself give density in `ℓ²(m)`.
  * Interpolating the values `Φ_f(ρ_j)` on `J` does not control the tail.
  * The correct argument: the closure of the image is a closed subspace invariant under multiplication by all
  `e^{iγs}`. By Wiener's lemma for atomic measures it is `L²(μ_Z|_S)`, and every atom is charged.
  * Fixable, but not written.
2. **Cor 8.1(iii) and §0 item 6, "the bound ⟹ RH ∧ simple zeros".** Uniform boundedness gives only *semisimplicity*
  (no Jordan blocks).
  * A multiple zero can still sit as a multi-dimensional eigenspace.
  * Simplicity needs cyclicity of `H¹`, which is `ANALYSIS` in R158_004.
  * The headline claim is overstated. The correct form is RH ∧ semisimple (∧ SZ if `H¹` is cyclic).
3. Construction 9's (P2) converse and O1–O3 are open, as declared.

**Literature.**
* Construction 9 is in substance a Hilbertian version of Meyer's exponentially weighted carrier: R. Meyer, *On a
  representation of the idele class group related to primes and zeros of L-functions*, Duke Math. J. 127 (2005),
  arXiv:math/0311468. There all strip zeros appear and a nuclear Lefschetz formula is proved, while the positivity is
  missing.
* "Similarity to a unitary group ⟺ uniformly bounded" is the standard reformulation of a Hilbert–Pólya realisation
  (R157_002 row 32, EQUIV).
* Thm 4 is folklore: a flow that scales a trace has no `L^p` eigenoperators.

**Fatal step.** (O3) is RH-strength, as the memo states. The positive form exists iff one uniform bound holds, and that
bound implies RH.

**Salvageable.** Thm 3 (null inheritance) and Thm 4 should be recorded as exclusions of the dual-trace/`L²(C_Q)` rows.
Thm 7 (growth type ½ is required) is a good design constraint.

## G1_B7_R1 — restatement

**Checked.**
* M1: (P2), (P4) and Weil/Bombieri. `PROVED`.
* M3: discriminant plus Hilbert reciprocity give `s₋ mod 4`, sharp. `PROVED`, classical (Serre, *Cours*, Ch. IV).
* M5: Gelfond–Schneider, correct.
* M4: Jensen gives `O(R)` zeros of an exponential-type `g` in a disc of radius `R`.
  * Against this, `≫ R log R` *distinct* ζ-zeros are needed. Conrey (or any positive-proportion simple-zeros theorem)
  supplies them, which is correct.
  * With only the multiplicity bound `m_ρ ≪ log γ`, one gets just `≫ R` distinct zeros. That is insufficient, so the
  citation is indeed load-bearing.
  * It is folklore that a compactly supported test cannot annihilate all but finitely many zeros.

**Literature.**
* "The motive of `Spec Z`, whose Hodge standard conjecture is RH" is the programme of Deninger and of
  Connes–Consani–Marcolli.
* The Rosati/trace-form ⟺ RH statement is CCM Cor 6.3, as recorded in R158_002 (I did not re-read the source).
* Ancona's reciprocity mechanism (arXiv:1806.03216) is correctly described.

**Fatal step.** M1 is an EQUIVALENT reformulation. No object passes OT-5′.

**Scope objection.** The §3.5 exclusion covers only lattices whose Lefschetz numbers are `Λ(n)/√n` up to a single real
scale. A period-matrix carrier is `OPEN`, as the memo admits. Ancona's own argument uses `p`-adic periods, so the
exclusion of S4 is partial, not complete.

**Salvageable.** M3–M5 as a method constraint on "real signature from local invariants".

## G1_B8_R1 — restatement

**Checked.**
* (A2) step 4: `s − ρ₀ = −i(z − z₀)`, `ψ ≈ im/(z−z₀)`, and along `z₀ − ir`, `−2ηψ ≈ 2ηm/r → +∞`. Correct.

  * That derives ¬RH, not a contradiction, so it does not determine the sign.
  * The sign must be read off the contour orientation directly.
  * Not load-bearing for (A2), but (A5) as stated is not fully proved.
2. The `o_f(η)` term is non-uniform. The memo correctly draws only a liminf consequence.
3. Laplace–Parseval for distributional `K_η` with `η ≤ 1` is plausible (Vladimirov §9) but not verified.

**Literature (checked).**
* `Re ξ′/ξ(s) > 0` on `Re s > ½` ⟺ RH is due to Hinkkanen, with Lagarias, Acta Arith. 89 (1999) 217–234 (see

* `|exp(−2ηψ)| ≤ 1` on `C₊` is exactly that criterion.
* Boundedness of `exp(−2ηψ)` ⟺ no off-line zero is the trivial essential-singularity remark.

zero-free factor, so the criterion is blind to them. That confirms the criterion is a pure zero-location statement.
It is not evidence of a prime-side lever. It is precisely OT-4 failure.

## G1_B10_R1 — restatement

**Checked.**
* (i)⟹(ii) of Theorem D.
  * The prime atoms give `−Λ(n)n^{−s}` with `s = ½+ε+z`.
  * The polar term gives `1/(s−1)` + holomorphic, since `e^{2(1−s)}/(s−1) − 1/(s−1)` is entire.
  * Lévy term: `K(u) = 2Σ_j e^{−(4j+1)u/2}`, so the term is `−Σ_j∫χe^{−(s+2j)u}`, holomorphic on `Re s > 0`.
  * All of this is correct, and so is the temperedness-plus-Laplace argument (Landau-type).
* (ii)⟹(i): `2Re k_ε(x+iδ) = 2[P_{ε−δ}(x) + P_{ε+δ}(x)]`, checked by partial fractions.
* E.1–E.4 are correct.
* E.5 constant slip:
  * Plancherel gives `λ^{W,ε}_T ≥ inf S_ε = 2 inf_τ Re ξ′/ξ(½+ε+iτ)`, not `π^{−1} inf`.
  * The stated bound is weaker, hence still true.
  * For `σ` in a zero-free region, `inf_τ Re ξ′/ξ(σ+iτ) = ξ′/ξ(σ)` (Lagarias for `σ > 10`; Garunkštis below that). This
  makes `c(ε)` explicit for `ε > ½`.

**Literature (checked).**
* `Re ξ′/ξ(σ+it) > 0` for all `t` ⟺ no zeros with `β > σ`: Hinkkanen; Lagarias 1999; Garunkštis;
  arXiv:2201.08599, 2509.18963.

## Panel note

**The seven memos converge on one statement**, each in its own language: whenever the positive form is defined before
the spectrum, one of three things happens.
* It lives on a space that cannot carry zeros (B2, B6 Thm 3–4).
* The zeros' membership or realisation is RH (B3, B6 Thm 8, B7, B8, B9, B10).
* It reaches only an RH-silent rung (B10, `ε ≥ ½`).

This is Lemma T / N-a of the record, re-derived six ways. None of it is new literature-level mathematics, apart from
small packaging results (Thm D of B2, Thm 4(a) of B9, Thm 3–4 of B6, M3–M5 of B7).

**Recommendation:**
* record the correct exclusions as method constraints;
* do **not** promote N-d (B2) or "N-b removed" (B3) to proved necessary conditions;
* correct the "simple zeros" claim of B6 to "semisimple (SZ under cyclicity)".
