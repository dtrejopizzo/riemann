### 1.3 The fatal defect: Theorem T(iii) and Corollary T.1 are false as stated

The claims:
* T(iii): any cone `𝓜^ext_+ ⊊ 𝔈′ ⊊ D^W/𝓝_∞` with the degree gate that contains `ℓ_{E_w}` "satisfies the

  is Prop 21.21 bigness, i.e. RH".

**Counterexample (`PROVED`).** Let `𝔚` be the set of all witnesses of Cor 19.25b, and put

  𝓒 := 𝓜^ext_+ + { Σ_i c_i ℓ_{E_{w,i}} : c_i ≥ 0, E_{w,i} ∈ 𝔚, finite sums } .

* Every nonzero generator has positive degree. For `𝓜^ext_+` this is `∫(1+ρ)dσ + α₁ + α₂ > 0`. For a witness,
  `deg E_w = d > 1` (Cor 19.25b, l.6525).
* `deg` is linear on classes (`deg ℓ = ℓ(2K)`, Thm 11.13(iv)). So every nonzero element of `𝓒` has `deg > 0`.
* Hence the degree gate holds, `𝓒` is salient (`ℓ, −ℓ ∈ 𝓒` would force `deg ℓ > 0` and `deg(−ℓ) > 0`), and `2K` is
  ample on `𝓒` in the sense of Thm 11.14(iii) (`I_∞(ℓ,2K) = deg ℓ > 0`).
* `𝓒 ⊋ 𝓜^ext_+`, because `ℓ_{E_w} ∉ 𝓜^ext_+` (G1_B1_R1 Cor C.1). `𝓒 ≠ D^W/𝓝_∞`, because `𝓒` is salient.

This holds **unconditionally**, and `𝓒` does not satisfy (SG). If RH fails, (SG) at `α = β = 0` would put into `𝓒` a
primitive `P` with `W(P) < 0` and `deg P = 0`, which the gate forbids. So "exists iff RH" is false. T(iii)'s claim

slices, and in particular at `α = β = 0` on the slices where `W < 0`. A witness class `F + (d/2t)(e₀+e₁)` is one
(SG)-admissible point on a slice with `W_F > 0`.

**What is true.** The conditions of Cor T.1 are satisfiable by installation. The cone `𝓒` escapes Cor 19.25b because
the witness classes were put into it. That is the OT-3 failure in cone form, and it proves nothing. The correct demand
on the Riemann–Roch route is (SG) under the gate (G1_B1_R1 Thm E, which is RH), or the full `h⁰` hypothesis of
Thm 12.3 on a linear domain. It is not "a salient cone containing the witnesses". So Cor T.1 does not replace N-c by
RH. It replaces N-c by a condition that is just as vacuous as the one it criticises.

**Verdict: refuted** as a statement of three horns. The trichotomy's third clause is false.
