## §0 Verdict

**No object is built. Status: partial new result, a proved exclusion.** It closes the residual that the panel called
the sharpest open item of G1 in round 4: the (E-orb) gluing problem of G1_B2_R4 §5, for Deninger's conformal
mechanism DK_α with finite trace. It is **not** an RH-equivalence and **not** circular. It uses no zeros and no `Λ`.

1. **Theorem F (flat wall; `PROVED`).** Let `(X,𝓕,g,φ,μ) ∈ DK_α` with `α ≠ 0`, with finite trace
  `m(X) = C_μ(vol_g) < ∞`, and with the integration-by-parts property of `C_μ`. Then:
  * (i) `m`-almost every leaf is flat, and the leafwise curvature tensor vanishes on `Y = supp m`. **No compactness or
  boundedness hypothesis is used.** The proof is two lines: the flow scales curvature by `e^{−αt}` and preserves the
  finite measure `m`.
  * (ii) Every leafwise-harmonic `k`-form is leafwise **parallel** on `Y` (Bochner).
  * (iii) If `Y` has a dense leaf (Deninger's route to `H̄⁰ = R`), then the positive Hilbert space `Ĥ^k_μ` has
  dimension `≤ C(d,k)`. In particular `dim Ĥ¹_μ ≤ d`, which is 2 for Deninger's Riemann-surface leaves.
2. **Corollary F1 (`PROVED`).** No DK_α carrier with finite trace, of any transversal type (manifold, profinite,
  adelic, type II or III), has a Lefschetz formula on its positive cohomology equal to ζ's explicit formula. The
  alternating trace is a finite exponential polynomial, hence smooth, while ζ's formula has atoms at every
  `k log p`. **OT-2 fails on the whole class, and (E-orb) under DK_1 is closed.**
3. **Answer to the panel's ratio-set question (`PROVED`, with a `CITED` input).** The type does not decide it:
  * Leichtnam proves type III_{1/q} with ratio set `q^Z` for the one-prime model. His proof is read and cited as a
  realised, non-obstructed case.
  * Theorem F excludes every transversal type at once, by a **leafwise** mechanism (curvature and parallelism), not a
  transverse one.
4. **Theorem E (Euler wall, class-level; `PROVED`, with Connes' measured Gauss–Bonnet `CITED`).** Take any foliated
  system with a foliated flow and `H̄² = R[λ]`, where `Θ = α ≠ 0` on `H̄²`, and let `C_μ` factor through `H̄²`. Then
  the Euler class of `T𝓕` is `0` in `H̄²`, so `χ_Co(𝓕,μ) = 0`.
  * This holds with **no** pointwise conformality, so it also covers the Serre / Deninger–Singhof / Leichtnam
  class-level relaxation `(φ^t)^*[λ_g] = e^t[λ_g]`.
  * Consequence: Deninger's expectation `χ_Co(𝓕,μ) = −log|d_K| < 0` for ramified `K` (DLS §7 Rem 7) is **inconsistent
  with his own `H̄²` axiom**, unconditionally. G1_B2_R4 Cor H2 needed Candel's hypothesis; Theorem E does not.
5. **What remains open (`OPEN`, precise).** Three escapes, all outside Theorem F:
  * (X1) infinite trace, `m(X) = ∞`. Then `1 ∉ Ĥ⁰` and `vol_g ∉ Ĥ²`. This is Connes' II_∞ situation (A17, EQUIV).
  * (X2) class-level conformality only. This is the P-HI shape, and Theorem E still binds it.
  * (X3) failure of integration by parts at a singular set of positive capacity.
  None of the three has a candidate here.

## §1 Setting (B2's class DK_α, with the two hypotheses made explicit)

**Definition 1.1.** `(X,𝓕,g,φ,μ) ∈ DK_α` is as in G1_B2_R4 Def 1.1:
* `X` is a second-countable, locally compact foliated space, locally `R^d × T`.
* `g` is a leafwise metric. **Regularity (R):** `g` is leafwise `C^∞`, and its leafwise derivatives up to order 2 are
  continuous on `X`. So the leafwise curvature tensor `Rm` is continuous on `X`.
* `φ` is a flow that maps leaves to leaves and satisfies `(φ^t)^*g = e^{αt}g` on `T𝓕`.
* `μ` is a holonomy-invariant transverse Radon measure with (D2), `C_μ(φ^{t*}β) = e^{dαt/2}C_μ(β)`.

Put `m := vol_g ⊗ μ`. By G1_B2_R4 Lemma 1.2(b) (`PROVED` there, re-checked): **`m` is `φ^t`-invariant.** Put
`Y := supp m`. It is closed, saturated and `φ`-invariant (B2 Cor M1(a), proof).

Hypotheses:
* **(FT) finite trace:** `m(X) = C_μ(vol_g) < ∞`.
  *Reason (`PROVED`):* Deninger's `H̄^d = R[vol_g]` lies in the Hilbert space with `(vol_g, vol_g) = C_μ(vol_g) = m(X)`,
  and the trace `tr[vol_g] = m(X)` is finite (B2 Lemma 1.2(a)). The constant function `1 ∈ H̄⁰` has norm² `m(X)`. So
  (FT) is exactly the requirement that `H̄⁰` and `H̄^d` lie in the positive Hilbert space. It holds automatically when
  `X` is compact.
* **(IBP):** `C_μ(d_𝓕η) = 0` for the leafwise `(d−1)`-forms `η` used below: `η = ⋆_𝓕 d_𝓕 f` with `f = |h|²_g`,
  `h` harmonic.
  *Reason (`CITED` std., Ruelle–Sullivan; Candel–Conlon I §10):* this is the closedness of the Ruelle–Sullivan current,
  i.e. the fact that `C_μ` factors through `H̄^d`. It holds for every compactly supported `η`, hence for all `η` when
  `X` is compact.
* **(D) dense leaf in `Y`.** This is Deninger's own route to `H̄⁰ = R` (DLS Thm 4.1 proof: "Because we have a dense
  leaf, `H̄⁰ = H⁰` consists only of constant functions"). G1_B2_R4 §5(1) demands `supp μ = S`, so for that carrier
  (D) is Deninger's hypothesis. The measure-theoretic variant (D′), ergodicity of `m`, works equally well (Remark 2.3).
* **(Hodge):** `H̄^k ≅ Harm^k := {h leafwise smooth, derivatives continuous, Δ_𝓕 h = 0}`. This is DLS Thm 4.1's
  standing identification, and it is how the pairing is defined on classes.

The positive form is `(h,h′) = ∫_X ⟨h,h′⟩_g dm`, i.e. `C_μ(h ∧ ⋆h̄′)` for `k = 1`, `d = 2`. The positive Hilbert space
`Ĥ^k_μ` is the Hausdorff completion of `Harm^k` for this form.

## §2 Theorem F (the flat wall)

**Lemma 2.1 (curvature scaling; `PROVED`).** For `x ∈ X` and `t ∈ R`: `|Rm|_g(φ^t x) = e^{−αt}|Rm|_g(x)`, and for
`d = 2` the Gaussian curvature satisfies `K(φ^t x) = e^{−αt}K(x)`.
*Proof.* `φ^t: (L_x, e^{αt}g) → (L_{φ^t x}, g)` is an isometry, because `(φ^t)^*g = e^{αt}g`. Under a constant rescaling
`g ↦ cg`, the `(1,3)` curvature tensor is unchanged, its `g`-norm becomes `c^{−1}|Rm|_g`, and `K_{cg} = c^{−1}K_g`.
Isometries preserve curvature. ∎

**Theorem F (`PROVED`).** Let `(X,𝓕,g,φ,μ) ∈ DK_α` with `α ≠ 0`, satisfying (R) and (FT).
(i) `Rm = 0` on `Y`. So `m`-almost every leaf, and every leaf contained in `Y`, is flat.
(ii) Assume also (IBP) for the relevant `η`. Every `h ∈ Harm^k` satisfies `∇h = 0` on `Y`.
(iii) Assume also (D). For any `h_1,…,h_N ∈ Harm^k`, the pointwise Gram matrix `G_ij(x) = ⟨h_i,h_j⟩_x` is **constant**
on `Y`, and has rank `≤ C(d,k)`. Hence `dim Ĥ^k_μ ≤ C(d,k)`. If the form is definite on `Harm^k` (Deninger's Thm 4.1
needs this in order to act on `H̄^k` itself), then `dim H̄^k ≤ C(d,k)`. In particular `dim H̄¹ ≤ d`.
(iv) `φ^{t*}` preserves `Harm^k`. On `Ĥ¹_μ` the generator `Θ` has at most `d` eigenvalues, counted with multiplicity.

*Proof.*
(i) Put `F(s) := m{x : |Rm|(x) > s}` for `s > 0`.
* By Lemma 2.1, `φ^{−t}{|Rm| > s} = {x : |Rm|(φ^t x) > s} = {|Rm| > e^{αt}s}`.
* Since `m` is `φ`-invariant, `F(s) = F(e^{αt}s)` for all `t ∈ R`. As `α ≠ 0`, `F` is constant on `(0,∞)`.
* `F` is bounded by `m(X) < ∞` (FT). `|Rm|` is finite everywhere, so the sets `{|Rm| > s}` decrease to `∅` as
  `s → ∞`. Continuity from above of the finite measure `m` gives `F(s) → 0`. So `F ≡ 0`, and `m{Rm ≠ 0} = 0`.
* `Rm` is continuous (R) and vanishes `m`-a.e., so it vanishes on `supp m = Y`. Every leaf `L ⊂ Y` is flat. Since `Y`
  is saturated and has full `m`-measure, `m`-a.e. point lies on such a leaf.

(ii) Use the Weitzenböck identity on leafwise `k`-forms: `Δ = ∇^*∇ + 𝓡`, where `𝓡` is linear in `Rm` (std.; for
`k = 1`, `𝓡 = Ric`). Use also the pointwise identity `⟨∇^*∇h,h⟩ = |∇h|² + ½Δ_0|h|²`, with `Δ_0 = d^*d = −div grad`
(std.). For `h ∈ Harm^k`:
`0 = ⟨Δh,h⟩ = |∇h|² + ½Δ_0|h|² + ⟨𝓡h,h⟩` pointwise.
* For a function `f`, `(Δ_0 f)vol_g = −d_𝓕(⋆_𝓕 d_𝓕 f)`, so `∫Δ_0 f dm = −C_μ(d_𝓕 ⋆ d_𝓕 f) = 0` by (IBP).
* By (i), `𝓡 = 0` on `Y ⊇` the support of `m`.
* Integrating against `m` therefore gives `∫_X |∇h|² dm = 0`.
* `∇h` is continuous and vanishes `m`-a.e., hence on `Y`.

(iii) The connection is metric, so on `Y`: `d_𝓕 G_ij = ⟨∇h_i,h_j⟩ + ⟨h_i,∇h_j⟩ = 0`.
* `Y` is saturated and leaves are connected, so `G_ij` is constant on each leaf in `Y`. It is continuous by (R).
* A dense leaf of `Y` then forces `G_ij ≡ c_ij` on `Y`.
* At any `x ∈ Y` (`Y ≠ ∅` because `m ≠ 0`), `c = (⟨h_i(x),h_j(x)⟩)` is the Gram matrix of `N` vectors in
  `Λ^k T*_x𝓕`, which has dimension `C(d,k)`. So `rank c ≤ C(d,k)`.
* The Hilbert Gram matrix is `(h_i,h_j) = ∫_Y c_ij dm = m(X)c_ij`, of the same rank. Hence any `N > C(d,k)` elements
  of `Harm^k` are linearly dependent in `Ĥ^k_μ`. If the form is definite, they are dependent in `Harm^k`.

(iv) `φ^{t*}g = e^{αt}g` gives `d^*_{φ^*g} = e^{−αt}d^*_g` on 1-forms, so `Δ_g φ^{t*} = e^{αt}φ^{t*}Δ_g`, and `ker Δ_g`
is preserved. The action descends to the completion, since B2 Lemma 1.2(c), extended to any `d`, gives
`‖φ^{t*}h‖² = e^{kαt}‖h‖²` for `k`-forms:
* `|φ^*h|_g = e^{kαt/2}|h|_g∘φ`;
* `vol_g = e^{−dαt/2}φ^*vol_g`;
* `C_μ` scales by `e^{dαt/2}` under (D2).
The dimension bound then gives at most `d` eigenvalues. ∎

**Remark 2.2 (what is and is not used).** Nothing requires:
* compactness of `X`, completeness of leaves, or regularity of the lamination (the argument is `m`-a.e.);
* any hypothesis on the transversal `T`, which may be profinite, adelic, of any von Neumann type, or of any ratio set;
* `d = 2`.
Nothing about ζ enters. The single dynamical input is: **a finite invariant measure cannot be invariant under a
nontrivial scaling of a finite-valued function.** This is why flatness comes for free; it is the scaling version of
Poincaré recurrence.

**Remark 2.3 (variants; `PROVED`).**
(a) Replace (D) by ergodicity of `m` for `𝓕` (D′). The `G_ij` are leafwise-constant `L¹(m)` functions, hence
`m`-a.e. constant, and the proof of (iii) goes through unchanged.
(b) Without (D) or (D′), (iii) becomes a module statement. The Gram matrix of `Harm^k` takes values in the algebra `𝔅_Y`
of continuous leafwise-constant functions on `Y`, with pointwise rank `≤ C(d,k)`. So `Ĥ^k_μ` has **local rank `≤ C(d,k)`
over the basic algebra.**

**Corollary F1 (OT-2 fails on DK_α with finite trace; `PROVED`).** Assume (R), (FT), (IBP), (D) and (Hodge), with
`α ≠ 0`. Whenever the Lefschetz distribution `Σ(−1)^k Tr(φ^* | Ĥ^k_μ)` is defined, it is a finite exponential polynomial
`Σ_{j≤M} ±e^{θ_j t}` with `M ≤ 2^d`, hence smooth on `R`. ζ's explicit formula (P46 `eq:W`, `thm:Z(i)`; R158_001 OT-2)
has prime side `Σ_p Σ_k log p·δ_{k log p}`, with nonzero atoms. The two cannot be equal as distributions on `R ∖ 0`.
Equivalently, `1 − Σ_ρ e^{ρt} + e^{t}` needs infinitely many `ρ` (P46 `thm:finZ` context; N(T) ~ (T/2π)log T), but
`Ĥ¹_μ` has at most `d` eigenvalues.
This is Deninger's own remark after DLS Thm 5.3 ("if the right hand side … is non-zero, at least one of the cohomology
groups must be infinite dimensional. Otherwise the alternating sum of traces would be a smooth function"). Theorem F
proves that under conformality and finite trace the positive `H̄¹` is **finite-dimensional**. ∎

**Corollary F2 (the (E-orb) gluing problem of G1_B2_R4 §5 has no solution; `PROVED`).** Suppose a lamination `S`
satisfies B2 §5 (1)–(3): `H̄⁰ = R` and `H̄² = R[vol_g]` with `supp μ = S`, the local models at every `p` and at `∞`,
and ζ's explicit formula as its Lefschetz formula. Then it satisfies (FT), because `vol_g` has finite trace, and (D),
by B2 §5(1) read through Deninger's dense-leaf route. If it also satisfies (IBP) and (R), Corollary F1 contradicts (3).
So the only ways out are (X1)–(X3) of §3. **The ratio-set / type-III question the panel proposed is not needed**
(§3.1). ∎

## §3 The escapes, the ratio set, and Theorem E

### 3.1 The ratio-set question (panel G1_P2/P3/P4/P5_R4), answered

* **Type III is realised, not obstructed (`CITED`, read).** Leichtnam (math/0603576, Prop 2 part 4 and proof) takes the
  one-prime lamination `S = (L × R_{>0})/q^Z`. There `W(L,𝓕)` is II_∞, `τ_Λ∘φ^t_* = e^{−t}τ_Λ`, the Connes invariant is
  `S(W ⋊ R) ∩ R_{>0} = q^Z`, and the crossed product is **type III_{1/q}**.
* With germs of modulus `p^{−1}` at every `p` inside one ergodic lamination, the natural expectation (`HEURISTIC`: the
  ratio set of an ergodic quasi-invariant measure class is a closed subgroup; recurrence at every `x_p` is not proved
  here) is that the ratio set contains every `p^Z`, and hence is `R_{>0}`, i.e. III_1. That would be the type of Connes'
  and BC-type systems. Nothing forbids type III_1, so **the type is not the obstruction**.
* Theorem F closes the question by another route. Leafwise flatness plus Bochner kills `H̄¹` in every transversal type
  at once. The obstruction is **leafwise and metric**, not transverse and ergodic.

### 3.2 Escape (X1): infinite trace

If `m(X) = ∞`, then `F(s)` may be `≡ ∞` and (i) fails; flatness is no longer forced. The price (`PROVED`):
* `1 ∉ Ĥ⁰_μ` and `vol_g ∉ Ĥ²_μ`, so the polar pair (OT-2's `Θ = 0, 1`) is **not** carried by the positive Hilbert
  space and must be restored by hand.
* This is the configuration of Connes' adèle class space: infinite invariant measure (R158_001 row "Connes"; the
  II_∞ factor of R158_002 §4.5), with the poles removed through the cokernel.
* There the global trace formula is RH-equivalent (A17, EQUIV), and `Ĥ¹` for the leafwise-form reading has no carrier
  (R158_002 Lemma A).
So (X1) leads into the EQUIV family of record unless a new infinite-measure carrier is found. `OPEN`, with no candidate.

### 3.3 Escape (X2): class-level conformality; Theorem E

Deninger and Leichtnam already weakened `(φ^t)^*g = e^t g` to `(φ^t)^*[λ_g] = e^t[λ_g]` in `H̄²` (Leichtnam l.966ff:
"Deninger pointed out to us that the condition … was probably too strong for being generalized"). Positivity then
comes from the Serre / Deninger–Singhof Hodge–Riemann form `⟨α₁,α₂⟩ = −(α₁ ∪ Jα₂; C(Λ))`, which is the P-HI shape.
Theorem F says nothing about this class: there is no pointwise curvature scaling. What survives is class-level.

**Theorem E (Euler wall; `PROVED`; Connes' measured Gauss–Bonnet `CITED` (std.): A. Connes, *Sur la théorie non
commutative de l'intégration*, LNM 725 (1979); Moore–Schochet, *Global Analysis on Foliated Spaces*).** Let
`(X,𝓕,φ)` be a foliated space with an oriented leafwise tangent bundle of rank `d` and a flow mapping leaves to leaves.
Assume:
* `H̄^d = R[λ]`;
* `Θ = α ≠ 0` on it, i.e. `φ^{t*}[λ] = e^{αt}[λ]`;
* `C_μ` is a linear functional on `H̄^d`, i.e. the trace of Deninger's axioms.
Then the Euler class `e(T𝓕)` has image `0` in `H̄^d`, and `χ_Co(𝓕,μ) = C_μ(e(T𝓕)) = 0`.

*Proof.*
* `Tφ^t: T𝓕 → T𝓕` is an oriented bundle isomorphism covering `φ^t`. Orientation is preserved because `t ↦ φ^t` is
  continuous from the identity.
* The leafwise Chern–Weil Euler form of any leafwise metric changes by a leafwise-exact form under a change of metric
  (transgression, std.). So `φ^{t*}e(T𝓕) = e(T𝓕)` in `H^d_𝓕`, hence in `H̄^d`.
* Write the image as `c[λ]`. Then `c e^{αt}[λ] = c[λ]` for all `t`, so `c = 0`.
* Connes' measured Gauss–Bonnet gives `χ_Co(𝓕,μ) = ⟨C_μ, e(T𝓕)⟩`. ∎

**Corollary E1 (`PROVED`).** In any Deninger-type system satisfying the hypotheses of Theorem E, whether pointwise
conformal or not:
* The δ₀-term of DLS Thm 5.3/(23) is `χ_Co(𝓕,μ)δ₀ = 0`.
* Deninger's expectation `χ_Co(𝓕,μ) = −log|d_{K/Q}|` (DLS (29), Rem 7.6(7)) **fails for every ramified `K`**. In
  G1_B2_R4 Cor H2 this was conditional on Candel's hypothesis; here it is unconditional.
* For `K = Q` there is no conflict.

For OT-10 (the family `𝒪_K`, §28.4 clause (6)), this is a constraint:
* The discriminant term `log|d_K|·h(0)` of the explicit formula for `ζ_K` cannot come from `χ_Co`.
* It must come from the fixed-point distributions `W_x` at archimedean places. `HEURISTIC`: their natural extensions
  across `t = 0` depend on local data (`κ_x`, `ε_x`), which see only `(r₁,r₂)` and not `d_K`. If so, `log|d_K|` has no
  carrier in Deninger's list.
* **Also `PROVED` from Theorem E plus Candel's converse direction (std.).** No such system admits a continuous
  leafwise metric of curvature `≤ −c < 0` on `supp μ`, since that would give `χ_Co ≤ −c·m(X)/2π < 0`. So the
  hyperbolic-leaf laminations (Boutet de Monvel's example in Leichtnam's remark after (iv); Deroin's quadratic
  differentials) are excluded **as carriers of a finite trace with `Θ ≠ 0` on `H̄²`**.

What remains in (X2) is `OPEN`: class-level conformality, positivity of Hodge–Riemann type, parabolic-on-average leaves,
and `dim H̄¹ = ∞`. Theorem F does not reach it. Leichtnam's Assumption (iv), which he needs for the trace class
property, forces `dim H¹_L = 2g < ∞` in his class (his §1), with "no example … with `g ≥ 2`" known. That is consistent
with Theorem F in the conformal subcase, where the rank is `≤ 2`.

### 3.4 Escape (X3): integration by parts fails

(IBP) can fail only through a boundary contribution at a singular set, for example the archimedean singularity of
Leichtnam §4.4. If that set has zero leafwise capacity and `h, ∇h ∈ L²(m)`, then (IBP) holds by the standard cut-off
argument (std.; `HEURISTIC` in this generality, not written out). A carrier escaping via (X3) would need its
**cohomology to be created by a singular set of positive capacity**, which no model on record has. `OPEN`.

### 4.2 Obstructions of record

* **Lemma T** (R158_001 §3.1): not invoked. Theorem F is a necessary condition that no object can meet, so it is not
  circular. If a DK_1 object with OT-1+OT-2 existed, Deninger's Thm 4.1 would give RH. Theorem F shows the premise is
  empty at finite trace.
* **C5 / N11 / G1_B2_R4 Cor M1:** Theorem F strictly extends them. C5 (compact manifold, `α = 0` forced) and Cor M1
  (codimension one, closed orbits outside `supp μ`) exclude **manifold** transversals. Theorem F excludes **every**
  transversal at finite trace, laminations included.
* **G1_B2_R4 Theorem H / Lemma H0:** these gave flatness only on complete leaves through periodic or fixed points.
  Theorem F(i) gives flatness `m`-a.e. with no completeness, removing the weakest step the panel flagged (completeness,
  Cor H1/H2). It is consistent with Theorem H.
* **A17 (Connes, EQUIV), R158_002 Lemma A:** the only exit Theorem F leaves in the pointwise class is (X1), which is
  that configuration.
* **`thm:finZ`, `cor:void`, `thm:margin`, `thm:arena`, `lem:Rrig`, `thm:SC`, T1, T2:** not engaged. There is no window
  and no finite zero set. The walls are zero-blind and Λ-blind, so they cannot decide a sign, consistent with
  `cor:void`.

### 4.3 Inverse checks: realised systems must not be wrongly excluded

* **Deninger's CM-elliptic system** (DLS 7.7–7.8; `X = (C ×_Γ T_πΓ) ×_{p^Z} R_{>0}`, compact, flat leaves `C`):
  * (FT) holds. (D) **fails**: continuous leafwise-constant functions are the functions of `r ∈ R_{>0}/p^Z`, i.e.
  `H̄⁰ = L²`-functions on a circle, the function-field `H⁰`.
  * Remark 2.3(b) gives local rank `≤ 2` over that basic algebra, which is exactly `2g = 2` (eigenvalues
  `ρ_j + 2πik/log p`, `j = 1,2`). ✓ Not wrongly excluded.
* **Leichtnam's laminations:** conformal only under the `q`-action (his (ii)), and class-level for the flow (his Prop
  2.2). They are in (X2), and Theorem F does not apply. His (iv) gives `2g < ∞`. ✓
* **Riemannian foliations with `α = 0`** (Deninger–Singhof, Kim's FDS3): outside the hypothesis `α ≠ 0`, and Lemma 2.1
  gives nothing. ✓
* **Horospherical foliations of `T¹(Γ∖H³)`, `Γ` cocompact** (strong unstable foliation, flat leaves `C`, geodesic flow
  conformal with `α = 2`, finite Liouville measure, dense leaves):
  * Theorem F predicts `dim Ĥ¹ ≤ 2`.
  * Independently (`CITED` std., Moore ergodicity / Lebesgue spectrum of the horospherical `N ≅ C`-action on
  `L²_0(Γ∖G)`): leafwise-harmonic `L²` functions are `N`-invariant, hence constant, so harmonic 1-forms have constant
  coefficients. ✓
  * Its Selberg-type zeros live in non-`L²` (distributional or resonance) leafwise cohomology (Juhl, *Cohomological
  Theory of Dynamical Zeta Functions*, 2001; `CITED`, not re-read). That is the (X1)/(X3)-type space, not the
  positive `Ĥ¹_μ`. This is a useful calibration: **even the model case where "RH" holds (Selberg) does not carry its
  zeros in DK positivity.**

## §5 What is genuinely new, and the weakest step

**New (to the corpus; literature status noted).**
1. **Theorem F(i)**: `m`-a.e. flatness from finite trace and `α ≠ 0`, with no completeness or compactness. The
  argument (scaling invariance of a finite invariant measure) is standard in rigidity theory. I found no statement of
  it in Deninger (DLS, math/0204110), Leichtnam (math/0603576), Kopei or Kim. It turns Deninger's and Leichtnam's
  remark that pointwise conformality is "probably too strong" into a theorem: **at finite trace it is fatal.**
2. **Theorem F(ii)–(iii) and Corollaries F1–F2**: the positive `Ĥ^k_μ` has rank `≤ C(d,k)` over the basic algebra. The
  (E-orb) gluing problem, which the panel called the sharpest open item of G1, is closed for DK_α at finite trace.
3. **Theorem E / Cor E1**: `χ_Co = 0` under Deninger's `H̄²` axiom, class-level and unconditional. It refutes the
  expectation `χ_Co = −log|d_K|` for ramified `K`, and excludes hyperbolic-leaf carriers with finite trace. It
  replaces the Candel-conditional G1_B2_R4 Cor H2.
4. §3.1: the ratio-set/type question is answered: not an obstruction, and not needed.

**Not claimed.** No object, no trace formula for ζ, no sign at any window, nothing for `T > 0.8`, no progress on the
"for all `T`" arrow. The primitive and complete forms are both untouched.

**Weakest steps.**
* **(IBP) and (Hodge) on non-compact or singular carriers.** For compact `X` both are standard (closedness of
  Ruelle–Sullivan currents; Deninger's standing Hodge identification). For a carrier that is singular at archimedean
  fixed points, (IBP) needs a capacity-zero argument that I have labelled `HEURISTIC` (§3.4). A carrier whose `H̄¹` is
  produced by boundary terms at a positive-capacity singular set is the unexcluded (X3).
* **Regularity (R).** The continuity of `Rm` is used only to pass from "`m`-a.e." to "on `Y`". Without it, (i) still
  holds `m`-a.e. and the Bochner integral argument still works. (iii) then needs (D′) ergodicity instead of (D).
* **(D) versus Deninger's `H̄⁰ = R`.** Deninger derives `H̄⁰ = R` from a dense leaf. A carrier with `H̄⁰ = R` but no
  dense leaf in `supp μ` and non-ergodic `m` is covered only by the module form, Remark 2.3(b). There `Ĥ⁰_μ` would have
  dimension `> 1`, which conflicts with the single polar term of OT-2. That conflict is `HEURISTIC` (cancellations in a
  distributional trace are not excluded by this memo).
* **Theorem E** uses Connes' measured Gauss–Bonnet and leafwise transgression (std., CITED, not re-proved). Cor E1's
  claim that `W_x` cannot carry `log|d_K|` is `HEURISTIC`.
* **(X1) and (X2) are wide open.** Theorem F moves the (E-orb) residual out of DK_α at finite trace. It does not move
  it out of Connes' infinite-measure world (EQUIV of record) or out of the Hodge-index (P-HI) world. In the latter,
  Theorem E is the only constraint proved.

**Suggested next step.** Record Theorem F + Cor F1 as the canonical closing of (E-orb)/DK_α, and
Theorem E as a filter on every Deninger-type candidate (OT-10 constraint). The unique non-EQUIV residual of the Deninger
angle is now **(X2)**. A compact lamination with finite trace, class-level conformality, Hodge–Riemann positivity, and
`dim H̄¹ = ∞`, necessarily with leaves that are "flat on average" (`χ_Co = 0`, Theorem E) but not flat, and failing
Leichtnam's (iv). The sharp question: does `χ_Co(𝓕,μ) = 0` together with `φ^{t*}[λ] = e^t[λ]` force parabolic leaves
`μ`-a.e.? If so, does it force a finite-rank `H̄¹` through a Theorem F-type argument on the uniformised flat metric?
