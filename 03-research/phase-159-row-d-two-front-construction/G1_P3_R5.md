## §1 G1_B1_R5 — orbit-closure law

### 1.1 Checked and correct
* **Lemma 2.1.**
  * The covector norm `|ξ∘Tφ^t|_g = e^{αt/2}|ξ|_g` follows from `|Tφ^t v| = e^{αt/2}|v|`.
  * Hence `|h|(φ^t x) = e^{(Re ρ−α/2)t}|h|(x)`.
  * The `m`-invariance of G1_B2_R4 Lemma 1.2(b), with `d = 2` (`vol` scales by `e^{αt}` and `μ` by `e^{−αt}`), gives
  `(h,h) = e^{2(Re ρ−α/2)t}(h,h)`.
  * Correct.
* **Lemma 2.2.**
  * `f = φ^{−l}|_{L_x}` scales intrinsic leaf distance by `c < 1`. Curvature transforms as `K(y) = c²K(f y)`, so
  `K(y) = c^{2n}K(fⁿy) → 0`.
  * This holds in fact on all of `L_x`, not only on `U`, since `d(fⁿy, x) = cⁿd(y,x)`.
  * On a flat convex disc, a map that scales all distances by `c` is `c` times an isometry. Orientation then gives
  `z ↦ λ^{−1}z`.
  * For fixed points, a continuous semigroup in `C^×` is exponential.
  * Correct.
* **Theorem O1(i)–(iii).**
  * `|h|` is constant on `U` by contraction and continuity.
  * Comparing coefficients of `aλ dz + bλ̄ dz̄ = e^{ρl}(a dz + b dz̄)` is correct.
  * In the orbifold case, `aζ = a` and `bζ̄ = b` with `ζ ≠ 1` force `a = b = 0`.
* **Theorem O2.** A continuous φ-invariant function is constant on orbit closures. Correct.
* **Theorem O3.**
  * (a): both `ρ, ρ′` lie in the two-point set `{α/2 ± iθ_x}`.
  * (b): `e^{iδ_∓ l} = 1`.
  * (c): pigeonhole places 3 periods in 2 cyclic groups, and two nonzero multiples of `2π/|δ|` have rational ratio.
  * (d): two progressions with incommensurable steps meet in at most one point, times 4 sign choices.
  * Correct.
* **Corollary O4.**
  * (i): a measurable a.e.-invariant `|h|` is constant a.e. under ergodicity (the standard strict-invariant version).
  If `m(X) = ∞`, then `c = 0`.
  * (ii): `{|h| ≠ c}` is open and null, so it misses `supp m`. O1 then applies **directly** at each named periodic
  point of `supp m`, and O3(b),(c) run pointwise.
  * Correct.
* **Cor O5.** The claims follow from O4 with `N(T) → ∞` and `ζ(½) ≠ 0`. The ζ-application is a correct implication
  under the stated hypotheses. (Ergodic, (C), smooth germs.)
* **Lemma O6.**
  * `E_s` is open, invariant and of finite measure (Chebyshev).
  * `F(s′) = F(e^{αt}s′)` holds by invariance, so `F` is constant, and it tends to 0. Correct.

### 1.2 Defects (not fatal)
* **Lemma 2.3 proves more hypothesis than it is given.** It assumes `dh = 0` and `d⋆h = 0`, but (C) defines `Harm¹` by
  `Δ_𝓕 h = 0`. On a non-compact disc these differ without IBP. For example `h = x dx` has `dh = 0`, `δh = −1`, and
  `Δh = dδh = 0`.
  * *Repair (one line, so not fatal):* in flat coordinates `Δ` acts componentwise, so `f, g` are harmonic functions.
  Then `Δ(|f|²+|g|²) = 2(|∇f|²+|∇g|²)`, and constancy of the norm forces `f, g` constant.
  * The conclusion `h = a dz + b dz̄` and all of O1 stand.
* **The notation of the §4 CM-elliptic check** (`f_j(t)dz` "leafwise constant") is loose. With `|dz|_g = e^{−t/2}`, the
  correctly normalised eigenform is `e^{ρt}dz`, well defined on the `p^Z`-quotient exactly when `p^ρ = π` (or `π̄`,
  depending on the convention).
  * The check itself (O1 returns the `H¹` spectrum of `E/F_p`) is right.
  * It is not load-bearing.
* **Scope, as the memo flags.**
  * (C) is a theorem only on compact Riemannian foliated manifolds, where B2_R4 Cor M1 already empties `supp m` of
  closed orbits. For laminations it is a standing assumption.
  * φ-ergodicity of `m` is a hypothesis, not a consequence of Deninger's `H̄⁰ = R`.
  * So the exclusion covers a class, and the class may be empty of ζ-candidates for other reasons.

### 1.3 Verdict: **excluded**
