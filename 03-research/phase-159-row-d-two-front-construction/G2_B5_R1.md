# G2_B5_R1: the window parameter as the time of a de Branges canonical system

Group G2 (the mechanism), track 5 of 10, round 1.
Angle: treat the window `T` as the time of a canonical system `J Y' = z H Y` whose Hamiltonian is built from the exact
`Λ(n)` and from Γ. The hope was that positivity of the Hamiltonian for all time would give `∀T`.

**Verdict, stated first.** The construction exists and is exact. It is also a restatement. Specifically:

* **(i) PROVED, assembled from CITED de Branges theory.** Wherever `λ^W_T > 0`, the complete Weil form makes the window
  space a de Branges space, and these spaces form an isometric chain in `T`. So there is a canonical system in window
  time, with `H ≥ 0`, that exists up to a blow-up time `T*`. Its Hamiltonian on `[0,T]` depends exactly on Γ and on
  `{Λ(n) : n < e^{2T}}`.
* **(ii) PROVED.** `T* = ∞` ⟺ RH. This is `thm:KEP3`(b) and `rem:nonzero` in canonical-system language: it is
  **EQUIVALENT**, not a step.
* **(iii) PROVED.** The evolution law, meaning the map from the datum to the Hamiltonian, is the same for every comb. So
  "`H ≥ 0` for all time" can never come from the dynamics. It has to come from a property of the input sequence
  `v_n = 2Λ(n)/√n`, and by `cor:void`/`thm:finmod` that property cannot be a class property. This is the wall of
  record, not a way around it.

What is new relative to the record is in §6: the polar-term invariance lemma, the unconditional shifted chain, the
blow-up alternative in trace-normed time, and the atomic structure of every extension at a degenerate window.
**No sign is claimed at any window.**

Forms: every statement is for the **complete** form `W_T` (`eq:W`) unless it says **primitive** (`𝔞_T` on `P_T`).

## 1. Set-up (Paper 47 notation)

* `F̂(z) = ∫F(t)e^{izt}dt`. `D_T` is the form domain `{F ∈ L²(I_T) : ∫log(2+|τ|)|F̂|² < ∞}`.
* `S_T(τ) = m(τ) − Σ_{n∈N_T} v_n cos(τ log n)`. `𝔞_T(F) = (2π)^{−1}∫S_T|F̂|²dτ`, and
  `W_T(F) = 𝔞_T(F) + 2Re(A(F)conj B(F))`.
* The Tate moments are `A(F) = F̂(−i/2)` and `B(F) = F̂(i/2)`.
* For `λ ∈ R`, put `W_T^λ(F) := W_T(F) − λ‖F‖²`. Let `T*(λ) := sup{T : λ^W_T > λ} ∈ (0,∞]`. By `lem:monoT`, this
  is the first time the bottom reaches `λ`.
* Record facts:
  * `λ^W_T` is nonincreasing and continuous (`lem:monoT`, `lem:contT`).
  * RH ⟺ `λ^W_T > 0` for every `T` (`rem:nonzero`; the proof of `cor:RH`).
  * Hence **RH ⟺ `T*(0) = ∞`**.
  * `T*(0) ≥ 0.8` (Zhu, CITED, not re-verified).

## 2. The window spaces are de Branges spaces

**Lemma 1 (the polar term is invariant under de Branges division; PROVED).** Let `w ∉ R` and
`b_w(z) = (z − w̄)/(z − w)`. Then `b_w(z̄)·conj(b_w(z)) = 1` for every `z ≠ w, w̄`. Consequently, for every entire `G`
and every `z₀`, the quantity `(G b_w)(z̄₀)·conj((G b_w)(z₀))` equals `G(z̄₀)·conj G(z₀)`.

*Proof.* `conj b_w(z) = (z̄ − w)/(z̄ − w̄) = 1/b_w(z̄)`. ∎

Apply this with `z₀ = i/2`, so `z̄₀ = −i/2`. The polar summand `2Re(F̂(−i/2)conj F̂(i/2))` of `W_T` is invariant
under `F̂ ↦ F̂·b_w`. This is the one point where the complete form might have failed de Branges's axiom (H1). It does
not fail, because the two polar points `±i/2` are complex conjugates.

**Theorem A (window de Branges space; PROVED from CITED de Branges theory).** Fix `T` and `λ < λ^W_T`. Let
`𝓔_T^λ = {F̂ : F ∈ D_T}` carry the norm `‖F̂‖² := W_T^λ(F)`. Then `𝓔_T^λ` is a de Branges space `H(E_T^λ)` for a
Hermite–Biehler function `E_T^λ` with no real zeros. `E_T^λ` is unique up to right multiplication of `(A,B)` by
`SL(2,R)`.

*Proof.* We check de Branges's axioms (de Branges 1968, Thm 23, CITED).

1. **Hilbert space.** Uniformly in `|τ|`, `m(τ) = log(|τ|/2π) + O(1)`, hence `m(τ) ≥ log(2+|τ|) − C₂`. The prime
  part is bounded by `A_T‖F‖²`, and the polar part by `2e^{T}·2T·‖F‖²` (Cauchy–Schwarz, `|e^{±t/2}| ≤ e^{T/2}` on
  `I_T`). So `W_T ≥ N(F) − C‖F‖²` with `N(F) = (2π)^{−1}∫log(2+|τ|)|F̂|²`. Together with
  `W_T^λ ≥ (λ^W_T − λ)‖F‖²` this gives
  `W_T^λ ≥ ε N(F)` for `ε(C + |λ|) ≤ (1−ε)(λ^W_T − λ)`.
  The reverse inequality `W_T^λ ≲ N(F)` is immediate. So the norm is equivalent to `N`, and `D_T` is complete for
  `N`: it is a closed subspace, by Paley–Wiener, of the weighted `L²` of `F̂`.
2. **(H2), bounded point evaluation.** `|F̂(w)| ≤ ‖F‖·‖e^{iwt}‖_{L²(I_T)}`.
3. **(H3), the reflection `F̂ ↦ F̂^#`.** Here `F̂^#(z) = conj F̂(z̄)` is the transform of `F̄(−t)`, which lies in `D_T`.
  `S_T` is even, so `𝔞_T` and `‖·‖` are preserved. `A(F̄(−·)) = conj B(F)`, so `Re(A conj B)` is preserved.
4. **(H1), division.** Suppose `F̂(w) = 0` with `w ∉ R`, and let `G = F̂·b_w`.
  * `G` is entire. `|G| = |F̂|` on `R`. `G` has exponential type `≤ T` in both half-planes, since `b_w` is bounded
  off a neighbourhood of `w` and `b_w → 1`.
  * So, by Paley–Wiener, `G = Ĝ₁` with `G₁ ∈ D_T`, and `N(G₁) = N(F)`.
  * Every real-line integral in `W_T^λ` depends only on `|F̂|²` on `R`. This includes the truncated comb, which is
  exact because `c_{G₁}` also vanishes off `[−2T,2T]`.
  * The polar term is invariant by Lemma 1.
  * Hence `‖G‖ = ‖F̂‖`.

Finally, `E_T^λ` has no real zeros because, for each real `x`, some `F̂` has `F̂(x) ≠ 0`. ∎

**Remark (primitive form).** The same proof works for the primitive form: use `Φ = F̂/(z² + ¼)` with the weight
`S_T(τ)(τ² + ¼)²`, on `P_T` wherever `λ^A_T > λ`. Lemma 1 is not needed there. For the primitive form, "de Branges
space" was essentially known (Suzuki). Lemma 1 is what lets the **complete** form enter without discarding the pole.

## 3. The chain: window time as canonical-system time

**Theorem B (window-time canonical system; PROVED from CITED de Branges theory).** Fix `λ`, and let
`0 < T < T′ < T*(λ)`.

1. **Isometric nesting.** Extension by zero embeds `𝓔_T^λ` isometrically into `𝓔_{T′}^λ`. This is the computation
  of `lem:monoT`: a contact in `N_{T′} \ N_T` sits at `log n ≥ 2T`, where `c_F = 0`; and `A`, `B`, `‖·‖` are
  unchanged.
2. **The family `{H(E_T^λ)}_{T < T*(λ)}` is a continuous chain.**
  * `∪_{s<T} 𝓔_s` is dense in `𝓔_T`: the dilates `F(t/r)`, `r ↑ 1`, as in `thm:KEP2`, converge in `N`.
  * `∩_{s>T} 𝓔_s = 𝓔_T`: a function of type `≤ s` for every `s > T` has type `≤ T`.
3. **Canonical system.** By de Branges's structure theorems (1968, Thms 35, 40; Remling, *Spectral Theory of Canonical
  Systems*, 2018, ch. 5–6, CITED), there are a trace-normed Hamiltonian `H^λ(s) ≥ 0` on `[0, L^λ)` and an increasing
  homeomorphism `σ : [0,T*(λ)) → [0,L^λ)` such that `H(E_T^λ)` is the de Branges space `H_{σ(T)}` of
  `J ∂_s Y(s,z) = z H^λ(s) Y(s,z)`, `Y(0,z) = I`, with `E_{σ(T)} = (1, −i)Y(σ(T), z)` up to normalisation.
  * The theorems are applied to `H(E^λ_{T′})` for each `T′ < T*(λ)`, whose isometrically contained de Branges
  subspaces are totally ordered.
  * The systems for different `T′` agree on their common range, by uniqueness of the trace-normed Hamiltonian.
  * The chain has no jumps (item 2), so `H^λ` has no singular interval of positive length inside `σ((0,T*))`.
4. **Causality.** `H^λ` on `[0, σ(T)]` is a function of Γ, of `λ` and of `(Λ(n))_{n < e^{2T}}` only.
  * The input it uses is the datum `g` of `eq:gdatum` restricted to `(−2T,2T)`, minus `λδ₀`.
  * The contact `n` first influences `H^λ` at window time `T_n = ½log n`.
5. **Explicit recipe.** Let `R_T^λ : D_T → D_T^*` be the form operator of `W_T^λ` (invertible for `T < T*(λ)`), and
  `ē_w(t) = e^{−i w̄ t}`.
  * The reproducing kernel is `K_T(w,z) = ⟨(R_T^λ)^{−1} ē_w, ē_z⟩`.
  * `E_T^λ` is recovered from `K_T` by the standard formula
  `K(w,z) = [E(z)conj E(w) − E^#(z)conj E^#(w)]/(2πi(w̄ − z))`.
  * With `Y(s,0) = I`, the trace-normed time obeys `K_T(0,0) = π^{−1}∫_0^{σ(T)} H^λ_{11}(s)ds`.
  * Here `K_T(0,0) = sup_F |∫F|²/W_T^λ(F)`, which is nondecreasing in `T`.

So the "Hamiltonian built from the exact `Λ(n)` and Γ" exists and is unique, but only up to `T*(λ)`: its existence
**is** positivity of the datum on the window. That is why the positivity statement (§4) is a restatement.

**Corollary B′ (unconditional finite-time chains; PROVED).** For every `T₁ < ∞`, choose `λ < λ^W_{T₁}`. This is
possible by `thm:margin` and `lem:contT`: `λ^W_{T₁}` is a finite real number. Then the shifted datum `g − λδ₀`
generates an honest positive canonical system on window time `[0,T₁]`. No hypothesis is involved.
Suzuki's per-window shifted device (2606.09096) is the fixed-`a` case. The chain in `T` needs `λ` to be **uniform** on
`[0,T₁]`, which is exactly what `lem:monoT` gives.

## 4. Blow-up alternative, extensions at the blow-up time, and the `T = ∞` end

**Theorem C (blow-up alternative; PROVED).** Suppose `T* := T*(λ) < ∞`. Then:

1. **The bottom is reached.** `λ^W_{T*} = λ`, by continuity (`lem:contT`), and a unit minimiser `F*` of `W_{T*}`
  exists (Bombieri 2000 Thm 3 for `μ_±`, CITED; equivalently, the compact-sublevel argument of `thm:O`/`rem:resweights`
  transposed to `W`).
2. **The trace-normed length is exhausted at a finite window.** `σ(T) → L^λ = ∞` as `T ↑ T*`.
  * Pick `w` with `F̂*(w) ≠ 0`. The dilates `F_r := r^{−1/2}F*(·/r) ∈ D_{rT*}`, `r ↑ 1`, satisfy
  `W^λ_{rT*}(F_r) → W^λ_{T*}(F*) = 0` by the three estimates in the proof of `lem:contT`, while `F̂_r(w) → F̂*(w)`.
  * Hence `K_{rT*}(w,w) ≥ |F̂_r(w)|²/W^λ_{rT*}(F_r) → ∞`.
  * On the other hand, if `L^λ < ∞`, then `tr H^λ = 1` makes `Y(·,w)` bounded on `[0,L^λ]`, and
  `K_s(w,w) = π^{−1}∫_0^s Y^*HY` would stay bounded. Contradiction.
3. **Every extension at `T*` is atomic.** Suppose `ν` is a positive symmetric tempered measure with `ν̂ = g − λδ₀`
  on `(−2T*,2T*)`. Then `supp ν ⊂ Z_R(F̂*)`, a discrete set. Moreover such `ν` **exist**: vague limits of solutions
  of `KEP_λ(T)`, `T ↑ T*`, are solutions.
  * *Existence.* For `T < T*`, `KEP_λ(T)` is solvable (§3 and de Branges Thm 22, or `thm:KEP3`(a) for `λ = 0`).
  Take `h = φ ⋆ φ̃` with `φ ∈ C_c^∞(I_{T₀})`, so `ĥ = |φ̂|² ≥ 0`. For `T ≥ T₀`, the local masses satisfy
  `∫ĥ(τ − x)dν_T(τ) = ⟨g − λδ₀, h e^{ix·}⟩ = O(log(2+|x|))`, uniformly in `T`. That gives vague compactness and a
  uniform tempered bound, so every Schwartz-tail pairing passes to the limit.
  * *Support.* `∫|F̂*|²dν = W^λ_{T*}(F*) = 0`. For `F*` in `D_{T*}` rather than `C_c^∞`, extend the identity by a
  Plancherel–Pólya step:
  `Σ_k sup_{[k,k+1]}|F̂|² ≲_T ∫|F̂|²` for type-`T` functions, applied with the local-mass bound `O(log)`. This
  makes `F ↦ ∫|F̂|²dν` continuous in the norm `N`.
  * Hence `ν` is carried by the real zero set of `F̂*`.

**Remark C1 (reading).** If RH fails, then at `T*(0) < ∞` the explicit-formula datum on the window has **only**
"phantom zero measures": positive, purely atomic, carried by the real zeros of one entire function of exponential
type `T*`, with Nyquist density `≍ T*/π` and weights `≍ log|x|` (by the local-mass law). This is the continuum
version of `thm:finDet`, and it matches the boundary case of `thm:KEP3`(a).
*Determinacy of `KEP(T*)` is OPEN here.* The duality argument
`∫ F̂* conj F̂*/((τ−x_j)(τ−x_k)) dσ = σ_j|F̂*′(x_j)|²δ_{jk}` needs the test `h` with support in the **closed**
window `[−2T*,2T*]`, while `KEP` prescribes `ν̂` only on the open one. The edge is not controlled.

**Theorem D (the `T = ∞` end, conditional; PROVED under RH, CITED de Branges).** Assume RH. Then:

* `T*(0) = ∞`, and the window-time system of Theorem B is defined on `[0,∞)`.
* It is in the **limit-point case** at `∞`, and its unique spectral measure is the zero measure
  `μ_ζ = Σ_ρ δ_{γ_ρ}`.
* *Why.* Every `H(E_T)` sits isometrically in `L²(μ_ζ)` (`thm:Z`(ii)). Any measure `μ` with this property for every
  `T` has `μ̂ = g` on `R`, so `μ = μ_ζ` by Fourier uniqueness for tempered distributions. Uniqueness of the spectral
  measure is the limit-point case (de Branges §§40–44).
* The self-adjoint operator of this system has simple spectrum `{γ_ρ}`. This is a Hilbert–Pólya realisation whose
  Hamiltonian on `[0,T]` is computable from `Λ(n)`, `n < e^{2T}`.
* **Circular as a proof:** `H ≥ 0` on `[0,∞)` is the hypothesis. Its unconditional counterpart is Suzuki's
  conjecture (2606.09096, Cor. 1.6).

## 5. Why positivity of the Hamiltonian cannot be the mechanism

**Proposition E (universality of the evolution law; PROVED).** Let `v′` be any nonnegative comb, such as the modified
combs of `thm:finmod`/`cor:void`, and `g′` its datum. Theorems A–C hold verbatim for `g′`, with `T*(v′)` in place of
`T*`.
*Proof.* No property of `v` was used beyond `v_n ≥ 0`, `Σ_{N_T} v_n < ∞`, and the fact that `log n` is discrete. The
proofs use only the structure of `m`, the pole and the window. ∎

**Consequence.** `cor:void` gives combs that keep ψ up to a constant, keep all the zeros and the pole, and still fail.
For those combs, `T*(v′) < ∞`. So the property "`H^λ(s) ≥ 0` for every `s`" is not implied by anything the dynamics
`g ↦ H` knows. The dynamics is **comb-universal**. Every T-uniform statement about the Hamiltonian must therefore be
an **exact identity for the input `v`**. By O3 (`rem:resweights`, R157), at windows `T > 1.51` a change
`|δv_{n₀}| ≲ e^{−14.4T}` moves `λ^W_T` across 0, i.e. it moves `T*`. In Hamiltonian terms, the map
`v ↦ H|_{[0,σ(T)]}` near a blow-up has sensitivity `≳ e^{14.4T}`. No perturbative or Lyapunov-function argument on
`H` survives this. A conserved quantity would have to be exact in `v`.

**Where the sign lives (HEURISTIC).** In the continuous Kreĭn/Schur picture (Kreĭn systems; Denisov, IMRS 2006,
CITED as background), a locally integrable coefficient `A(t)` gives a positive datum on every window, **with no
constraint**. The analogue of `|α_n| < 1` for OPUC disappears in the continuum. So in the canonical-system picture
the sign becomes a **global-existence (non-blow-up)** question for the nonlinear Gelfand–Levitan map `g|_{(−2T,2T)} ↦
H`. Two further points:

* The increments of `g` at the window edge are positive between thresholds (`d_*(u) = 2cosh(u/2) − ½K(u) > 0`
  for `u ≥ 0.3`) and negative atoms `−v_n/2` at `T_n`. But the edge sign does not control existence
  (compare `thm:SC`, `lem:Rrig`).
* The phase-64 von Mangoldt system is the **Born (first-order) linearisation** of this map. There, the atoms become
  nonnegative impulses and positivity is free. The sign is entirely in the nonlinear remainder, which is why phase 64
  found "positive but not Ξ-realising".

**Classification: EQUIVALENT (row-39 cluster of R157_002), with three proved additions that carry no sign.**

**Is anything left for a mechanism? (OPEN, one-round test for round 2.)** The only non-restating question this angle
produces is a rigidity statement about the phantom measures of Thm C(3):

> *(Q)* Can a positive atomic measure carried by the real zero set of one entire function of exponential type `T`

makes it equivalent to the existence of the minimiser with value 0, i.e. again to `λ^W_{T*} = 0`. **Expected
outcome: restatement.** I record it only so that no one resubmits it as new.

## 7. Weakest step

1. **Theorem B(3): applying de Branges's chain theorems (Thms 35, 40) to a family defined without a priori measure.**
  Each `H(E_{T′})` is an honest de Branges space, and its isometrically embedded de Branges subspaces are ordered.
  What was not checked line by line is that every `H(E_T)`, `T < T′`, belongs to the chain of the canonical system
  of `H(E_{T′})`, and not merely to its set of subspaces. By Thm 35 the set of isometrically embedded de Branges
  subspaces of a given `H(E)` is totally ordered and equals the chain of `E`'s system. Our spaces are among them, so
  this should hold, but I read only secondary statements (Remling ch. 5–6) of the ordering theorem's hypotheses
  (`E_T/E_{T′}` of bounded type, no real zeros).
2. **Theorem C(3):** the Plancherel–Pólya continuity step, which extends `∫|F̂|²dν = W(F)` from `C_c^∞` to the form
  domain.
3. **Status of the whole angle:** no sign at any window. The construction is **EQUIVALENT** to row D.
