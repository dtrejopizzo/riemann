### 1.2 Lemma L (the memo's declared weakest step)
**Direct statement.** Write `P := −∂²+¼`. Since `P^kβ ∈ 𝒮′`, the hypothesis is really just `P^k u_Λ ∈ 𝒮′`. The memo
should say so: the slack `β` is cosmetic.
* **Step 1.** `θ ∈ O_M`, so `θv ∈ 𝒮′` with support in `[0.1,∞)`. The function `χe^{−s·}` lies in `𝒮` and depends
  holomorphically on `s` for `Re s > 0`. **PROVED / CITED** (Schwartz VIII; Hörmander I, 7.4).
* **Step 2.** Two parts.
  * *Supports.* `θP^kw − P^k(θw)` vanishes wherever `θ` is locally constant, so `r` is supported in `[0.1,0.5]` and `ℒr`
  is entire. `P^k(χe^{−su}) − (¼−s²)^kχe^{−su}` is supported in `supp χ′ ⊂ [0.02,0.05]`, which is disjoint from
  `supp θ ⊂ [0.1,∞)`. Correct.
  * *Gap (repairable, not fatal).* The memo pairs `P^k(θu_Λ)` with the non-compactly-supported `χe^{−su}` as if `θu_Λ`
  were tempered. It is **not** known to be tempered: that is the whole point. Repair: put
  `ψ_R := χ₁(u/R)χe^{−su}`.
  * `ψ_R → χe^{−s·}` in `𝒮` for `Re s > 0`, so `⟨θv,ψ_R⟩ → ℒ(θv)(s)`.
  * For each `R`, the decomposition `θv = P^k(θu_Λ) − P^k(θβ) + r` can be paired with the compactly supported `ψ_R`.
  * On `[0.1,∞)` one has `P^kψ_R = χ₁(u/R)(¼−s²)^ke^{−su} + E_R`. Here `E_R` is supported in `[R,2R]` and satisfies
  `|E_R| ≤ C_s R^{−1}e^{−σu}`.
  * Since `∫e^{−σu}d|θu_Λ| < ∞` for `σ > ½`, dominated convergence gives
  `⟨θu_Λ,P^kψ_R⟩ → (¼−s²)^k∫e^{−su}d(θu_Λ)`.
  * The `θβ` term converges directly in `𝒮′ × 𝒮`.

  So the identity in step 2 holds on `Re s > ½`. The memo's own truncation remark in the preamble of §2 is the
  right tool. It should be applied here explicitly and not only to tempered `w`.
* **Step 3.** `∫_{0.5}^∞ e^{(½−s)u}du = e^{−(s−½)/2}/(s−½)`, and likewise for `e^{−u/2}`. `K = O(e^{−u/2})` gives
  `A(s)` holomorphic on `Re s > −½`. On the comb, `θ ≡ 1` since `log 2 > 0.5`. The Dirichlet series is
  `−ΣΛ(n)n^{−½−s} = (ζ′/ζ)(½+s)`. **PROVED.**
* **Step 4.** On `Re s > 0` the right side is meromorphic with poles only at `s = ½`. A zero with `Re ρ > ½` would
  give a pole at `ρ−½ ≠ ½`. The functional equation handles `Re ρ < ½`. **PROVED.**

**Converse.** Weil + Lemma 1.2 + Bochner–Schwartz. **PROVED / CITED.**

**Remark 2.1.** The identity "`2cosh(u/2) − Σ… = e^{−u/2}d(e^u − ψ(e^u))`" drops the `e^{−u/2}` half of `2cosh(u/2)`.
Also, "temperedness = polynomial growth of smoothings" is informal. Both are HEURISTIC and not load-bearing. The

### 1.6 Remark 3.4: fatal to that remark only (not load-bearing)
The claim is: "`D_b^+ = ε·2πμ_Λ` is impossible, because `σ̌ = ε(k_∞+2cosh(u/2))` off `±log ℕ`, and `2cosh(u/2)` is not

* **The inference is invalid.** A tempered distribution can agree, off a discrete set, with an exponentially growing
  smooth function. Under RH, `u_Λ` itself is an example: it is tempered (Lemma L, converse) and equals
  `−½K + 2cosh(u/2)` off `±log ℕ ∪ {0}`.
* **The conclusion is not proved either.** Put `σ̌ := εu_{Λ′}` for a hypothetical comb `Λ′ ∈ 𝒮^W` with `Λ′−Λ ∈ 𝒯` and
  `μ_{Λ′} ⊥ μ_Λ`. Then `β_b = ε(u_Λ − u_{Λ′})` is comb-supported, and `D_b^+ = 2πεμ_Λ`. So the "proportional"
  candidate reduces to exactly the existence question the memo leaves OPEN. It is not excluded by the pole argument.
* The remark also presupposes RH, since `μ_Λ` is only defined under RH, but it is labelled PROVED with no condition.
* **Fix:** relabel Remark 3.4 and §6 item 6 as OPEN (under RH).

This does not affect Theorem R′: its hypothesis `b ∈ 𝒯_meas` excludes any `D_b` that charges `Γ_ζ`.
