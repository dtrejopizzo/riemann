## §0 Verdict

1. **The graphs of the sums `Γ_{m+nπ}` do not close the gap** (Theorem 2, `PROVED`). For every ordinary `E/F_q`, the
  cone spanned by the graphs of **all** nonzero endomorphisms, their transposes and the rulings has
  `min_θ σ_θ/σ_θ(Eff‾) ≤ 2√2/3 < 1`. For the curve of record (`y²+xy = x³+1` over `F_2`), its support function
  equals the frame's **in every direction**, so its minimum is `√(7/11)`. This corrects the reading in R145_005 §2
  ("the missing effective classes are the graphs of the sums"). Those classes are missing, but adding them changes
  nothing.
2. **What does close it is Frobenius–Verschiebung rebalancing** (Theorem 3, `PROVED`). The bigraphs
  `B_{k,l} = (V^k, F^l)_*[E]` have class `(q^k, q^l; π^{k+l})`: they carry the H¹ datum of `F^{k+l}` with the polar
  (ruling) weights moved. At `k = l` the cosh penalty vanishes, and `σ_θ = ½ = σ_θ(Eff‾)` for every `θ`, for every
  ordinary `E`. **`√(7/11)` is a lattice artefact of the Frobenius-only cone.** The structure that closes the gap is
  the coupling between H¹ and the polar part, which the frame fixes as `(e^{−u/2}, e^{u/2})`.
3. **`q → 1`** (Theorem 4, `PROVED`). The Frobenius-cone constant converges uniformly in `θ` to P47's continuum
  constant `s(γ,θ)`, with error at most `(γ+½)η/2`. At `γ_1` its minimum over `θ` tends to `s_1 = 0.99846…`, not to
  `√(7/11)` and not to `1`. The rebalanced constant is `1` for every `η`.

## §1 Setting (g = 1)

`E/F_q` is ordinary, with Frobenius `F = π`, Verschiebung `V = π′ = q/π = a − π ∈ End(E)`, `α` a Frobenius eigenvalue
with `|α|² = q` (Weil, `CITED` P47 (X27)), and `θ_u = arg α`. The rulings are `F_1 = {0}×E` and `F_2 = E×{0}`.

**Fact 1.1 (`CITED`).** `NS(E×E) ≅ Z ⊕ Z ⊕ End(E)` via `D ↦ (A, B; f) = (D·F_1, D·F_2; f_D)`, with
`D·D′ = AB′ + A′B − 2⟨f, f′⟩`, where `⟨f,f⟩ = deg f`. References: Kani, *The moduli spaces of Jacobians isomorphic to
a product of two elliptic curves* (Collect. Math. 2016), §2, and Mumford, *Abelian Varieties* §20–§23 in any
characteristic. Under `f ↦ λ_f` (the eigenvalue on the `α`-line, a ring map `End(E) → C` with `π ↦ α`), we have
`deg f = |λ_f|²` (`|α|² = q` together with ordinarity). This recovers P47's coordinates (21.8),
`I = 2ab − 2(x²+y²)` with `x + iy = λ`, and P47's datum `Φ_D(ρ_1) = λ_f`. The class `Γ_{π^n}` is `(1, q^n; π^n)`, and
the frame generator `q^{−n/2}Γ_{π^n}` is `(q^{−n/2}, q^{n/2}; α^n q^{−n/2})`.

**Lemma 1.2 (the ratio of a class; `PROVED`).** For `D = (A, B; λ)` with `A, B > 0`, `λ ≠ 0`:

  |Re(e^{iθ}λ)|/deg D = ½ · |cos(arg λ + θ)| · (|λ|/√(AB)) / cosh(½ log(B/A)) , deg D = A + B .

*Proof.* `A + B = 2√(AB) cosh(½log(B/A))`. ∎

For a cone generated by a family `𝒢`, `σ_θ(Cone 𝒢) = sup_{g∈𝒢} |Re(e^{iθ}λ_g)|/deg g`. This is the supremum of a ratio
of a linear functional to a positive linear functional, which is attained on generators (`PROVED`, one line). For
classes with `D² = 0`, `|λ|² = AB`, so the middle factor is `1`.

## §2 The endomorphism-graph cone does not close the gap

Definition: `𝓔_End := Cone{F_1, F_2, Γ_φ, Γ_φ^t : 0 ≠ φ ∈ End(E)}`. We have `Γ_φ = (1, N; φ)` and
`Γ_φ^t = (N, 1; φ′)` with `N = deg φ = |λ_φ|²`. By Lemma 1.2 each generator contributes
`½|cos(arg λ + θ)|/cosh(½ log N)` (or the conjugate phase), and the rulings contribute 0. Also `𝓔_C ⊂ 𝓔_End`.

**Theorem 2 (`PROVED`).**
(i) For every ordinary `E/F_q`, `min_θ σ_θ(𝓔_End)/σ_θ(Eff‾) ≤ max(2√2/3, √3/2) = 2√2/3 = 0.9428… < 1`.
(ii) For the curve of record (`q = 2`, `a = −1`, `End(E) = Z[π] = O_{Q(√−7)}`), `σ_θ(𝓔_End) = σ_θ(𝓔_C) = ½s_C(γ_1,θ)`
for **every** `θ`. In particular `min_θ σ_θ(𝓔_End)/σ_θ(Eff‾) = √(7/11)`.

*Proof.* (i) `σ_θ(Eff‾) = ½` (P47 Thm 21.17(iii), `g = 1`). Generators with `N ≥ 2` contribute at most
`½/cosh(½log 2) = ½·2√2/3`. The units of an imaginary quadratic order are `±1`, `±i` or the sixth roots of unity.
Modulo `π` their phases form at most three classes spaced `π/3`, or two classes spaced `π/2`, or one class. Choosing
`θ` so that one class sits at `π/2` gives `max_u |cos(arg u + θ)| ≤ sin(π/3) = √3/2`.
(ii) The norm form is `N(m + nπ) = m² − mn + 2n²`, so `4N = (2m − n)² + 7n²`.
- `N = 1`: `λ = ±1`.
- `N = 2`: `±α`, `±ᾱ` (note `1 + α = −ᾱ`).
- `N = 3`: none (`(2m−n)² + 7n² = 12` has no solution).
- `N = 4`: `(2m−n)² + 7n² = 16` gives `±2`, `±(2+α) = ∓α²`, `±(α−1) = ±ᾱ²`, using `α² = −α − 2` and `ᾱ = −1 − α`.

So every element with `N ≤ 4` has, modulo `π`, the phase of a Frobenius power of the same norm (`n = 0, ±1, ±2`), or
phase 0 with norm 4, which contributes `½·0.8|cos θ| < ½|cos θ|`, the frame's `n = 0` entry. Its contribution
therefore does not exceed the frame entry. Every element with `N ≥ 5` contributes at most
`½·2√5/6 = ½·0.7454 < ½√(7/11) ≤ ½s_C(γ_1,θ)` (P47 Prop 21.13: `min_θ s_C = √(7/11)`). Hence
`σ_θ(𝓔_End) = σ_θ(𝓔_C)`. ∎

*Check (scratch script, not load-bearing):* sweeping `|m|,|n| ≤ 40` and `θ` on a grid of `2000` points gives
`max_θ (σ_θ(𝓔_End) − σ_θ(𝓔_C)) = 2·10^{−16}` and `min_θ = 0.79779`, versus `√(7/11) = 0.79772`; the difference is
grid resolution.

**Correction to the record (proposed).** R145_005 §2, "the missing effective classes are the graphs of the sums
`Γ_{m+nπ}`", should read: *they are missing from `𝓔_C`, but adjoining them leaves the support function unchanged on
the curve of record, and below `2√2/3` on every ordinary `E`. The additive structure of `End(E)` alone does not close
the gap.*

## §3 Frobenius–Verschiebung rebalancing closes it

Definition: `ι_{k,l} := (V^k, F^l) : E → E×E` and `B_{k,l} := (ι_{k,l})_*[E]` for `k, l ≥ 0`.
`𝓔_{FV} := Cone{B_{k,l}, B_{k,l}^t}`. Since `B_{0,n} = Γ_{π^n}`, we have `𝓔_C ⊂ 𝓔_{FV} ⊂ Eff(E×E)`.

**Lemma 3.1 (`PROVED`).** `B_{k,l} = (q^k, q^l; π^{k+l})`. In frame-normalised form this is
`(q^{(k−l)/2}, q^{(l−k)/2}; e^{i(k+l)θ_u})`, and `B²_{k,l} = 0`.

*Proof.* By the projection formula, `B·F_1 = deg(pr_1∘ι) = deg V^k = q^k` and `B·F_2 = deg F^l = q^l`. The Hom
component is determined by the intersections with `Δ` and `Γ_π` (together with `F_1`, `F_2` they give a basis of
`NS ⊗ Q`, rank 4).

`B·Δ = #{P : V^kP = F^lP} = deg(F^l − V^k)`. For `k ≥ 1` this map is separable, because `d(F) = 0` and `d(V) ≠ 0`
for ordinary `E`, so the intersection is transversal. Its value is `q^l + q^k − 2Re(α^{k+l})`, using `λ_V = ᾱ` and
`|α|² = q`. Fact 1.1 gives `D·Δ = A + B − 2Re λ_f`, so `Re λ_f = Re α^{k+l}`.

Likewise `B·Γ_π = deg(F^l − FV^k) = q^l + q^{k+1} − 2q Re(α^{k+l−1})`. Fact 1.1 gives `D·Γ_π = qA + B − 2Re(λ_f ᾱ)`,
so `Re(λ_f ᾱ) = q Re α^{k+l−1} = Re(α^{k+l}ᾱ)`. Since `ᾱ ∉ R`, the two real equations give `λ_f = α^{k+l}`.

`B² = 2q^{k+l} − 2|α^{k+l}|² = 0`. (Also: the image is an elliptic curve on an abelian surface.) The cases `k = 0`
and `l = 0` are the graphs themselves. ∎

**Lemma 3.2 (`PROVED`).** For ordinary `E`, `α^n ∉ R` for every `n ≥ 1`, so `θ_u/π ∉ Q`.

*Proof.* `α^n ∈ R` gives `α^n = ᾱ^n`, hence `F^n = V^n` in `End(E)`. But `F^n` is purely inseparable and `V^n` is
separable (ordinary), a contradiction. ∎

**Theorem 3 (`PROVED`).** For every ordinary `E/F_q` and every `θ`, `σ_θ(𝓔_{FV}) = ½ = σ_θ(Eff‾)`. The supremum is
approached along `B_{m,m}` (`m → ∞` on a subsequence) and is attained only when `θ ≡ −2mθ_u` (mod `π`) for some `m`.

*Proof.* By Lemmas 1.2 and 3.1, `B_{k,l}` contributes `½|cos((k+l)θ_u + θ)|/cosh(½(l−k)log q)`. At `k = l = m` this is
`½|cos(2mθ_u + θ)|`. By Lemma 3.2 the sequence `(2mθ_u)` is dense modulo `π`, so the supremum is `½`. The upper bound
`½` is P47 Thm 21.17(iii). ∎

**Structure that closes the gap (`PROVED` reading of Lemma 3.1).** `B_{k,l}` and the frame generator `Γ_{π^{k+l}}`
carry the **same** H¹ datum `α^{k+l}`. They differ only in the polar weights `(A, B)`:
`[B_{k,l}] = [Γ_{π^{k+l}}] + (q^k − 1)F_2 − (q^{k+l} − q^l)F_1` (since `F_1 = (0,1;0)`, `F_2 = (1,0;0)`), a
combination of the rulings, one of whose coefficients is **negative** for `k ≥ 1`. The frame locks the polar weights to the
datum, `(e^{−u/2}, e^{u/2})` at position `u`. That lock is what forces the cosh penalty, and hence `s < 1`. The
elementary instance is the pair `Γ_V = (1, q; V)` and `Γ_F^t = (q, 1; V)`: they have the same datum, their polar
weights are swapped, and they differ by a ruling combination. Composition makes the contrast plain:
`Γ_{F^l} ∘ Γ_{F^k}^t = q^{min(k,l)}Γ_{F^{|l−k|}}`, which collapses like the frame's inverse-semigroup law
`Frob_m ⋆ Frob_n^† = Frob_{m/n}`, whereas `Γ_{F^l} ∘ Γ_{V^k}^t = B_{k,l}` does not collapse. The resource is **`V` as
an endomorphism, as opposed to `F^t` as a transpose**.

**Remark 3.3 (`HEURISTIC`, genus).** `V ∈ End(C)` requires `C = Jac(C)`, i.e. `g = 1`. For `g ≥ 2` the bigraphs live
on `J×J`, not on `C×C`, and whether `σ(Eff‾(C×C)) = ½` remains P47's open O-37. The rebalancing mechanism is
therefore a genus-one mechanism.

## §4 The limit q → 1: which constant survives

Fix `γ > 0`. Put `f_θ(u) := |cos(γu + θ)|/cosh(u/2)` and, for mesh `η > 0`, `s_η(γ,θ) := sup_{n∈Z} f_θ(nη)`. This is
the constant of the Frobenius cone of a lattice model with `η = log q` and `θ_u = γη`. P47's `s(γ,θ) := sup_{u∈R} f_θ(u)`.

**Theorem 4 (`PROVED`).**
(i) `s(γ,θ) − (γ+½)η/2 ≤ s_η(γ,θ) ≤ s(γ,θ)`, uniformly in `θ`. Hence `min_θ s_η(γ,·) → min_θ s(γ,·)` as `η → 0`.
(ii) At `γ = γ_1` the limit is `s_1 = 0.99846…`: P47 §19/§21 table, `CITED`, which records the continuum optimum at
`θ = π/2`. So **`√(7/11)` does not survive**. It is the value at `(η, γ) = (log 2, θ_u/log 2 = 2.788…)`, and the
lattice optimum `θ^* ≠ π/2` (MISMATCH 11) is an artefact of the sample point `n = 0`, which disappears in the limit.
(iii) The rebalanced constant `sup_{|t|≤|u|} |cos(γu+θ)|/cosh(t/2)` (over `u ∈ ηZ`, or over `u ∈ R`) equals `1` for
every `η` with `γη/π ∉ Q`, and for the continuum. The gap between the Frobenius cone and the rebalanced cone is
`1 − s(γ,θ)` in the limit, and is `η`-independent in character.

*Proof.* (i) `|f_θ′(u)| ≤ γ + ½`, since `|d/du sech(u/2)| ≤ ½ sech(u/2)`, uniformly in `θ` (where `f_θ` is not
differentiable, the one-sided derivatives obey the same bound). Every `u` lies within `η/2` of `ηZ`, and `ηZ ⊂ R`
gives the upper bound. Uniformity in `θ` gives convergence of the minimum. (iii) Take `t = 0`; density of `γu` modulo
`π` (Lemma 3.2 for the lattice; trivial for the continuum). ∎

Numerical check (scratch): at `γ_1` the errors are `0.0314`, `0.0023` and `1.7·10^{−5}` for `η = 0.1, 0.01, 0.001`,
all below the bound, and consistent with `O(η²)` at a smooth maximum.

**Answer to the angle's question.** Of `(√(7/11), E×E)` the `q → 1` transport keeps: (a) the **Frobenius-cone
constant**, which becomes P47's `s(γ,θ)`; (b) the **rebalanced family**, which becomes `𝓦` below (zero-free); and not
(c) the **mechanism that makes (b) effective and bounded**: `V ∈ End(E)`, `B² = 2 − 2g = 0`, and `|λ_V| = √q`.
Section 5 shows that over `Spec Z`, (c) is exactly RH.

## §5 The arithmetic transport: the rebalanced cone 𝓦 (Horn B, row B6)

Notation of P47 §11. `D^W`, `Φ_F(s) = ∫F(t)e^{(s−½)t}dt`, `deg F = Φ_F(0) + Φ_F(1)`, `I_∞` given by (11.6), and
`w_X(u)` of (11.16), so that `I_∞(E,X) = ∫E w_X` (Thm 11.14(i)). `𝓔_+` is the cone of nonnegative elements of `D^W`.

**Definition 5.1.**
- Ruling classes: `ε_0 := K + 2K′` and `ε_1 := K − 2K′`, where `K` is P47's class with `Φ_K = ξ`, `K ∈ D^W_4`, so
  `K′ ∈ D^W`. Since `Φ_{g′}(s) = −(s−½)Φ_g(s)`, we get `Φ_{ε_0} = 2(1−s)ξ(s)` and `Φ_{ε_1} = 2sξ(s)`. Hence
  `Φ_{ε_0}(0) = 1`, `Φ_{ε_0}(1) = 0`, `Φ_{ε_1}(0) = 0`, `Φ_{ε_1}(1) = 1` (since `ξ(0) = ξ(1) = ½`), and both vanish on
  `Z(ξ)`. **No zero is used**: `ξ` is defined by the Γ-factor and the Euler product.
- For `u, t ∈ R` with `|t| ≤ |u|`:
  `W_{u,t} := δ_u + (e^{−t/2} − e^{−u/2})ε_0 + (e^{t/2} − e^{u/2})ε_1`,
  as a linear functional on `D^W` through `I_∞(δ_u, X) := w_X(u)`. This is the weak extension of Thm 11.14(i). Then
  `Φ_W(0) = e^{−t/2}`, `Φ_W(1) = e^{t/2}` and `Φ_W(ρ) = e^{u(ρ−½)}`. The case `t = u` is the frame's Frobenius
  point `δ_u`.
- `𝓦 := Cone{W_{u,t}}`.

**Theorem 5 (`PROVED`).**
(i) *(zero-free)* `𝓦` is defined from `δ_u`, `K` and `K′` alone.
(ii) *(gate)* `deg W_{u,t} = 2cosh(t/2) > 0`. The cone `𝓔_+ + 𝓦` is salient and does not contain `−K`.
(iii) *(support)* At every on-line zero `ρ = ½+iγ`, `γ ≠ 0`, and every `θ`,
`sup_{W∈𝓦} |Re(e^{iθ}Φ_W(ρ))|/deg W = ½`, attained at `t = 0`, `γu + θ ∈ πZ`. This is the value of Pos (P47 Thm
21.17(ii)), against `½s(γ,θ)` for `𝓔_+` (Lemma 19.17).
(iv) *(admissibility = RH)* Clause (T2_cone) of P47 Thm 19.30 for `𝓔′ = 𝓔_+ + 𝓦`, namely
`sup_{E′∈𝓔′} |I_∞(E′,X)|/deg E′ < ∞` for every `X ∈ D^W`, **holds if and only if RH holds**.
(v) *(Castelnuovo shape = RH)* `|Φ_W(ρ)| ≤ ½deg W` for all `W ∈ 𝓦` and all zeros `ρ` if and only if RH holds.
(vi) *(falsifiers)* For `Ξ_a` (off-line zeros) (iv) and (v) fail; for a DMV Beurling system, or Epstein `Ξ_Q`, with
off-line zeros they fail too. No false sign is produced, because none is produced.
(vii) *(self-intersection)* Formally, `I(W_{u,t}, W_{u,t}) = 2Φ_W(0)Φ_W(1) − Σ_ρ Φ_W(ρ)Φ_W(1−ρ) = 2 − Σ_ρ 1`,
independent of `(u,t)` and with no hypothesis on the zeros. It equals `2 − 2g = 0` in the `g = 1` model (Lemma 3.1)
and diverges to `−∞` for ζ (Hardy). This is P44 Thm 8bis.2, `Δ·Δ = −∞`, seen on the rebalanced family.

*Proof.* (i)–(ii): as defined. Every generator of `𝓔_+ + 𝓦` has positive degree (Thm 11.13(i)), so `deg` is a
strictly positive functional on the cone, and `−K` has degree `−1`.

(iii) `|Φ_W(ρ)| = |e^{iγu}| = 1`, `deg = 2cosh(t/2) ≥ 2`, with equality at `t = 0`.

(iv) `I_∞(ε_0,X) = Φ_X(1)` and `I_∞(ε_1,X) = Φ_X(0)` by (11.6), since the zero sum vanishes. Hence
`I_∞(W_{u,t},X) = Φ_X(1)e^{−t/2} + Φ_X(0)e^{t/2} − f_X(u)` with `f_X(u) := Σ_ρ Φ_X(1−ρ)e^{(ρ−½)u}`.

(⇐) Under RH, `|f_X(u)| ≤ Σ_ρ|Φ_X(1−ρ)| < ∞` by (11.17). So each `W` term is bounded by `C_X deg W`, and `𝓔_+` is
Thm 11.14(ii).

(⇒) Take `X(t) = e^{−t²} ∈ D^W`, so `Φ_X(s) = √π e^{(s−½)²/4}`, which vanishes nowhere. Put `c_ρ := Φ_X(1−ρ) ≠ 0`,
with `Σ|c_ρ| < ∞`. (T2) at `t = 0` gives `sup_{u≥0}|f_X(u)| = M < ∞`. For `Re z > ½`,
`L(z) := ∫_0^∞ f_X(u)e^{−zu}du = Σ_ρ c_ρ/(z − w_ρ)` with `w_ρ = ρ − ½`, by dominated convergence, since
`|e^{w_ρ u}| ≤ e^{u/2}`. Boundedness makes `L` holomorphic on `Re z > 0`.

The series `G(z) = Σ c_ρ/(z − w_ρ)` converges locally uniformly off the closed discrete set `{w_ρ}`. It is
meromorphic on `C`, with a simple pole at each `w_0` of residue `m_0c_{ρ_0} ≠ 0`. Since `G = L` on `Re z > ½` and
`{Re z > 0} ∖ {w_ρ}` is connected, `G` has no pole in `Re z > 0`. So no zero has `Re ρ > ½`. By `ρ ↦ 1 − ρ̄`, none has
`Re ρ < ½` either.

(v) (⇐) `|e^{u(ρ−½)}| = 1 ≤ cosh(t/2)`. (⇒) Take `t = 0`: `e^{u(β−½)} ≤ 1` for all `u ∈ R` forces `β = ½`.

(vi) (iv)(⇒) and (v)(⇒) use only an entire `Φ_X` without zeros and the Hadamard/explicit-formula structure (11.6).
Both are available for `Ξ_a` and for the other falsifiers.

(vii) `Φ_W(ρ)Φ_W(1−ρ) = e^{u(ρ−½)}e^{u(½−ρ)} = 1`. ∎

**Classification.** `𝓦` is a **new Horn-B candidate** (P47 §19.9 table, row "B6: rebalanced Frobenius classes"). Its
verdict: passes the support test (iii), which B1–B3 and B5 do not; zero-free (i); gated (ii); **admissibility
EQUIVALENT to RH** (iv). Theorem 19.30 is a necessary test. Even with (T2) granted, Theorem 12.3 still needs an `h⁰`
with (a), (b′), (c) on the enlarged cone, which is not constructed. Whether the witness family of Thm 19.23 lies in
`𝓔_+ + 𝓦 + 𝓝_∞` (the quantity `C′_{F_0}` of Thm 19.30) is `OPEN` (not computed). Its answer cannot remove the RH
content of (iv).

**What (iv) says, in geometric terms.** In the model the bound on `𝓔_{FV}` is `|λ_{F^{2m}}| = q^m = deg`, i.e.
`|α| = √q`. Over `Spec Z` it is `sup_u |Σ_ρ c_ρ e^{(ρ−½)u}| < ∞`. Both are RH of the respective zeta function. On
`E×E` it is **supplied** by Hodge index (Weil), or here more cheaply by the fact that `B_{m,m}` is a curve with
`B² = 0` on an abelian surface. The arithmetic counterpart of that supply is excluded by (vii): the rebalanced class
has self-intersection `−∞`, so no Hodge-index or "effective ⟹ nef" argument can be run on any finite span containing
one `W`. Consequently the rebalancing, which is the one structure the transport isolates, **does not carry its own
positivity over `Spec Z`**: N-a fails.
