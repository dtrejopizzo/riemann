## §1 G1_B2_R4 — Deninger's conformal class DK_α

### 1.1 Checked and correct
* **Lemma 1.2(b).** `m = vol_g ⊗ μ` is `φ`-invariant. Plaques scale by `e^{dαt/2}`, so
  `μ(φ^tτ) = e^{−α_d t}μ(τ)`. Correct for every Borel transversal.
* **Thm M(i).** `φ^l` fixes the segment `σ ⊂ γ` pointwise, so `μ(σ) = e^{−α_d l}μ(σ)` and `μ(σ) = 0`. Saturation is
  null because holonomy is a countably generated pseudogroup of `μ`-preserving maps. Correct.
* **Thm M(ii).** In codimension one the orbit segment is itself a transversal through `x`. Every small transversal
  through `x` is holonomy-equivalent to it near `x`, so `x ∉ supp μ`. Correct.
* **Thm M(iii), the modulus law.**
  * From `μ(h((a,b)×B)) = e^{−α_d a}μ(h((0,b−a)×B))`, the measure `e^{α_d s}h^*μ` is translation-invariant in `s`,
  which gives `h^*μ = e^{−α_d s}ds⊗ν`.
  * The sheared image `Φ((a,b)×B)` has measure `(∫_a^b e^{−α_d s})∫_{P(B)} e^{−α_dσ∘P^{−1}}dν`. This uses injectivity
  of `P` on small `B` (local homeomorphism).
  * Equating with `e^{−α_d l}m(E)` gives the two-sided bound.
  * Whitney–Bebutov local sections are CITED.
* **Thm M(iv), Cor M1(a)–(c).** Correct. `supp μ` is saturated and `φ`-invariant, so `U` is too. Cor M1(d) is
  conditional on (L), as labelled.
* **Lemma H0.** Classical (Kobayashi–Nomizu). Each step checks:
  * a bijective homothety scales distance;
  * Banach's fixed point;
  * the curvature scaling `K(p,σ) = c^{2n}K(fⁿp,dfⁿσ) → 0`;
  * the loop contraction argument gives `π₁ = 1`;
  * Killing–Hopf.
* **Thm H(i)–(iii).** For (iii): `φ^t x′` is fixed by `φ^{t′}|_L`, hence equals `x′`, hence equals the unique fixed
  point `x` of `φ^t|_L`. Correct.
* **Prop K, Prop F.**
  * Prop K: `vol(L) = e^{dαt/2}vol(L)`.
  * Prop F: `F_±(e^{κt}r) = e^{−α_d t}F_±(r)` gives `F_± = c r^{−α_d/κ}`, and finiteness needs `−α_d/κ > 0`.
  * The real-place arithmetic `κ = −2 ⟺ β = −½` is correct.
* **Cor H2.** Given Candel (CITED), every leaf is conformally hyperbolic. A `g`-complete leaf through an orbit is
  isometric to `R²`, hence conformally `C`. That is a contradiction. The corollary is conditional, as labelled.
* **Falsifier checks (Rem M3).** The CM-elliptic Haar modulus `p^{−1}` and Leichtnam's Lemma 6.1 fit the modulus law.
  Nothing true is excluded.

### 1.2 Defects (not fatal)
* **The novelty of "ε = +1 derived" (Thm H(iv), §0 item 3, §7 item 3) is overstated.** Conformality at the fixed point
  already gives `T_xφ^{kl}|_{T_x𝓕} = e^{kαl/2}O` with `O ∈ O(2)`. The flow is isotopic to the identity, so
  `O ∈ SO(2)`, and `det(1 − e^{kαl/2}O) = |1 − λ^k|² > 0` follows **at the point**, with no completeness and no Lemma
  H0. This is exactly Deninger's "Fact" (§7 Rem 5) read under his own conformality axiom. What Theorem H adds is
  linearity **on the whole leaf**, and that needs completeness. The sign itself was never in doubt in DK_α.
* **Completeness** (the memo's own weakest step). This is correct as flagged. Leichtnam's §4.4 needs a singular
  archimedean point, so in the only case of interest the leaves through the primes may be incomplete. Cor H1 is then
  unproved for the relevant laminations.
* §5's "forced local normal form" is `PROVED` only as a list of necessary conditions. The memo itself says that the
  slice `(V_p, ν_p, P_p)` is not forced to be `Z_p` (`HEURISTIC`), and that is correct.

### 1.3 Verdict: **excluded**
The results are correct necessary conditions and an unconditional exclusion of codimension-one foliated manifolds
(Cor M1). No object is built, OT-1 fails, and the gluing problem is `OPEN` with no candidate.
*Salvageable:* the modulus law `ν(PB)/ν(B) → e^{−α_d l}` is a clean, citable filter. The gluing sub-question of §7
