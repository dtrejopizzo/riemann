## 1. Step check (audit; only the steps my verdict uses)

**Lemma R (resonator identity). Correct. PROVED, primitive form.**
- `A(ψ)=B(ψ)=0` follows from `ψ=(−∂²+¼)χ_ε`: the same integration by parts as in `prop:finsupp`.
- Separation: for `nk ≠ m` with `m,k ≤ M`, `|log(nk/m)| ≥ log(1+1/M) ≥ 1/(2M) ≥ 4ε`. So `c_{F_b}(log n)` picks out
  exactly the pairs `m = nk`, which is (c).
- (d): the pointwise bound `m(τ) ≤ log(1/ε)+ℓ(ετ)+½log 2` integrates against `|F̂_b|² ≥ 0`. The off-diagonal kernel is
  `O(ε^{−3}(ε/u)²)`, which is `ℓ¹`-summable in `|m−k|` (`2ζ(2)`), so Young applies. Correct.

**Lemma S (Schur test). Correct. PROVED (Mertens CITED).** Take weights `w_k = k^{−½}`. The row sum over divisors
`k | m` is `Σ_{d|m}Λ(d) = log m`, exactly. The row sum over multiples is `Σ_{j≤M/m}Λ(j)/j ≤ log(M/m)+E`. Total
`≤ log M + E`. This is the right order: the test vector `b_m = m^{−½}` gives `G_M(Λ) ~ log M`.

**Proposition P. Correct. PROVED, unconditional, every `T`, on `ℛ_M` only (both forms coincide on `P_T`).**
- *Diagonal:* `Arch(ψ) ≥ (log(1/ε) − C'_χ)‖ψ‖²` by `lem:mbounds`.
- *Off-diagonal:*
  - The polarised `eq:A` at separation `u_0 ≥ 4ε` loses the local terms.
  - `K(u) ≤ 1/u + ½` holds because `½/sinh(u/2) ≤ 1/u`.
  - `‖ψ‖_1² / ‖ψ‖² = O(ε)`.
  - The `1/|h|` kernel costs `2H(M) ≤ 2(1+log M)` in Young. The `½` part costs `εM` by Cauchy–Schwarz.
  - Together, `≤ C·εM(2+log M) = C/(8K)`.
- The gap `log(8K(2+log M)) − C'_χ − C''_χ/K − E` is positive for `K ≥ K_0`. I re-derived each inequality, and the
  constants are consistent (not optimised, as the memo says).

**Theorem D and Cor D1. Correct.**
- `D_a(σ) = (σ−1)∫S(x)x^{−σ}dx`, and `S ≤ 2log x + O(1)` gives `D_a(σ) ≤ 2/(σ−1)+O(1)`. So Theorem Q′'s double pole is
  cut to a simple one.
- Hardy–Littlewood–Karamata (with `a ≥ 0`, so `S` is monotone) gives `S ~ r log x`. Then
  `G_M ≥ (2/(log M+1))∫_1^M S dx/x = (r+o(1))log M`, and Theorem D forces `r ≤ 1`.
- Input: Theorem Q′ with `κ = 0`, panel-verified in round 4 (G2_P3_R4 §1.3).

**Theorem R. Correct, given CITED Soundararajan Thm 2.1 (lower bound), which I checked in the primary text.**
- I extracted arXiv:0708.3990 (Math. Ann. 342, 2008) from the PDF. Theorem 2.1 reads
  `max_r Σ_{mk≤N} r(m)r(mk)/√k / Σ_{n≤N}|r(n)|² = exp(√(log N/log log N) + O(√log N/log log N))`.
- The lower bound uses a multiplicative, squarefree-supported `f` with `f(p) = L/(√p log p)` on
  `L² ≤ p ≤ exp((log L)²)`, where `L = √(log N log log N)`, and Rankin's trick.
- The memo's sum `Σ_{nk≤N} r(k)r(nk)n^{−½}` is the same sum after renaming. The truncations `mk ≤ N` and `n ≤ N`
  match `b = r·1_{[1,N]}`.
- The `n = 1` term is exactly the denominator, so `Q_a/‖b‖² ≥ 2c(Ψ_N − 1)`.
- Every ratio `n` that occurs is a squarefree product of resonator primes, and those exceed `P_0` for large `N`. So
  `a_n ≥ c` wherever it matters.
- `log N = 2T − 1 + o(1)` gives the stated rate. Correct.

**Theorem T. Correct. PROVED.**
- Contour shift from `Re w = c > ½` to `Re w = 0`. The pole at `w = ½` has order `≤ 2`.
- Its residue involves only `𝒞(½) = A(F)B(F)` and `𝒞′(½) = A′B − AB′`. Both vanish on `P_T`.
- The growth hypothesis kills the horizontal segments.
- (b): the upper bound is the modulated `prop:finsupp` test. The lower bound is `inf S_s`, because the form is a
  T-free Fourier multiplier on nested spaces.

**Cor T2.** (i) and (ii) are correct (Hardy 1914 for (ii)). (iii) is a restatement: EQUIVALENT to RH, and labelled so.

## 3. Why the candidate is EXCLUDED as a mechanism (the fatal step)

The only positive assertion is Prop P. Its inputs are exactly two:
- the divisor row sum `Σ_{d|m}a(d) ≤ log m + O(1)`;
- the multiple row sum `Σ_{j≤x}a(j)/j ≤ log x + O(1)`.

Both are preserved under the two finite modifications of `thm:finmod`:
- deletion `Λ1_{n≥N}` (since `0 ≤ a ≤ Λ`);
- amplification `Λ + c1_{n=2}` (each row sum grows by `≤ c`, which is absorbed by `K ≥ K_0 e^{O(c)}`).

So Prop P holds for members of `𝒞_BT`, `𝒞_PNT` and `𝒞_zeros` that fail `RD_T`. By `cor:void`, and by `rem:twohalves`
clause (a), no derivation whose prime input is only these row sums decides any window `T > ½log 2`.

The deeper reason:
- By `thm:arena`, finite compressions do not decide the even Schur complement.
- The resonators' spectrum `|τ| ≍ KM log M` lies in the archimedean-dominance regime. That regime is exactly where
  `rem:resweights` shows the sign is insensitive to `O(1)` weight changes. The near-minimisers carry the
  `e^{−14.4T}` sensitivity (O3), and they are not in `ℛ_M`.

The memo states all of this in §5. I confirm it independently.

Grading against the filters:
- **F1:** the proof uses an exact identity (`Λ∗1 = log`), but only as an inequality, so the conclusion is class-level.
- **F2:** uniform in `T`, but on a subspace only.
- **F3:** respected.
- **F4:** β-blind; not a separator.
- **F5:** n/a.
- **O3 and `cor:void`:** fails. The candidate does *not* fail for modified combs, which is the disqualifying signature.

## 4. Salvageable ideas

1. **Lemma R as a record tool (PROVED).** It is an exact dictionary between P47's window form and multiplicative
  Toeplitz / GCD-sum theory. It turns every GCD-sum lower bound into an upper bound on bottoms of combs: a clean
  *destructive* instrument for falsifier design (Cor R1: spread-mass flips need neither `thm:margin` nor the OPEN
  non-degeneracy).
2. **Remark R2 (OPEN direction, HEURISTIC value).** On resonators, Λ and dense models are separated by the support of
  Λ, i.e. by `μ²`-type vanishing on products of two or more primes. A test family that is *not* in the
  archimedean-dominance regime, but still sees the support, would be the object to look for. Lemma R cannot supply
  it: in Lemma R the bump width is tied to `1/M` by separation, which forces `|τ| ≳ M`.
3. **Cor D1 (PROVED).** A tidy record item: ∀T-positivity of a nonnegative comb caps the residue at `1`.
