**Literature (Deninger, DLS notes, re-read in full text this phase).**

  Corollary 5.5 which actually implies α = 0". It then asks for "phase spaces more general than manifolds … where

  1906.02424.
* **ε = +1 is not new in substance.**
  * DLS §7 Rem 5 already shows, under conformality with factor `e^t`, that `e^{−kl/2}T_xφ^{kl}` is orthogonal.
  Deninger's "Fact" is that `ε = +1` iff it lies in `SO`.
  * If `T𝓕` is oriented (Deninger's leaves are Riemann surfaces), continuity of `s ↦ φ^s` puts it in `SO`
  automatically.
  * So "derived rather than assumed" means orientation plus Deninger's Fact.

* **Cor H2 is close to Deninger Rem 7.** Deninger already infers, via Candel [9], that for ramified `K` every leaf is

  from Deninger's text.
* **Prop F.** `β = −½` with the ramified double cover reproduces Leichtnam's §4.4 singular structure, as the memo says.

* **Theorem M.** No source states the modulus law as such. In every realised example (CM-elliptic, Leichtnam
  laminations, ALKL simple foliated flows 1906.06753 / 2402.06671, which are codimension one with `α = 0`) it is

**Fatal step (for G1).** None mathematical. No object: the global gluing (§5 (1)–(3)) is `OPEN`. The class is not
excluded, since Deninger-type laminations with Haar `Z_p`-germs pass every law proved. Hence **unproved**, not

(math/0603576, Prop 2.4) computes the ratio set `q^Z` in the one-prime case. A lamination carrying modulus-`p^{−1}`

answer (`HEURISTIC`) is that the only such object on record is the adèle class space, which is not a lamination

**Literature and duplication.**

  in B4 is the strip clause (b). I recommend recording it as a corollary of W♯(b).

OT-3). Remark 5.2 (the residual must read the zeros) is `HEURISTIC`, correctly labelled.

  ([PDF](https://swc-math.github.io/dls/DLSDeninger.pdf)); read: §6 after (31), Fact 6.1, §7 Rem 4–7.
* F. Kopei, *A remark on a relation between foliations and number theory*
  ([math/0605184](https://arxiv.org/html/math/0605184v1)). It has no statement about α or measures, so it gives no
  priority for Thm M.
* Álvarez López–Kordyukov–Leichtnam, *Simple foliated flows* ([1906.06753](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1906.06753)) and
  *A trace formula for foliated flows* ([2402.06671](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.06671)).
* J. Kim ([1906.02424](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1906.02424)); E. Leichtnam ([1307.3851](https://arxiv.org/abs/1307.3851);
  math/0603576).
* R. Meyer, *On a representation of the idele class group related to primes and zeros of L-functions*
  ([math/0311468](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0311468)).
* A. Connes, C. Consani, *Spectral triples and zeta-cycles* ([2106.01715](https://arxiv.org/abs/2106.01715));
  A. Connes, C. Consani, H. Moscovici, *Zeta zeros and prolate wave operators*
  ([2310.18423](https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.18423)).
* N. Makarov, A. Poltoratski, *Etudes for the inverse spectral problem* ([2204.08395](https://arxiv.org/abs/2204.08395)).
* J. Lagarias, *Hilbert spaces of entire functions and Dirichlet L-functions* (math/0010324).
* Standard: von Neumann (Compositio 1939); Kakutani (1948); Arveson (JFA 1974); Pontryagin (1944); Kobayashi–Nomizu I;
  Candel (Ann. ÉNS 1993); Schur test; PNT.
