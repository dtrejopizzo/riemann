# G2_B9_R1 — G2 (the mechanism), round 1: jump calculus in T

Angle: at each contact `T_n = ½log n` the window forms are supposed to change by an explicit rank-≤2 perturbation of
weight exactly `w_n = Λ(n)/√n`. The plan was to derive an exact interlacing or jump inequality from this, and then a
quantity that is conserved or monotone across all jumps and certifies `∀T`.

Notation is that of Paper 46 (`eq:symbol`, `eq:form`, `eq:W`): `I_T = (−T,T)`, `c_F(u) = ∫F(t)F(t−u)dt`,
`v_n = 2Λ(n)/√n = 2w_n`, `N_T = {n ≥ 2 : log n < 2T}`, `P_T = {A = B = 0}`,
`‖F‖_Q² = (2π)^{-1}∫log(2+|τ|)|F̂|²`. Every statement says whether it concerns the **primitive** form (`𝔞_T` on `P_T`) or
the **complete** form (`W_T` on `L²(I_T)`).

**Verdict, stated first.** The angle gives **no mechanism**. No sign is claimed at any window.

* **(a)** The premise is false in the continuous window model. A contact does not produce a rank-≤2 jump. At `T = T_n`
  the forms do not change at all. For `T = T_n + ε/2` the increment is `−w_n(σ_x ⊗ 1)` on `L²(0,ε) ⊗ ℂ²` (the two
  boundary layers). It has infinite rank, spectrum `{−w_n, 0, w_n}`, and a norm that is independent of `ε`
  (Prop. 1, PROVED). What is rank 2 is the *matrix* `σ_x`, not the operator.
* **(b)** Contacts enter softly. Uniformly on near-minimisers, the new contact changes the bottom of either form by at most
  `C·w_n / log(1/ε)` (Thm 2, PROVED). So a jump inequality has nothing to act on at the entry point. The influence of
  `Λ(n)` on the sign is zero at the entry and grows with `T`, up to the O3 sensitivity `e^{−14.4T}`. That is the opposite
  of a local jump law.
* **(c)** The exact "jump + drift" bookkeeping for the complete form is the running Chebyshev remainder
  `E(u) = Σ_{log n≤u} v_n − 8 sinh(u/2)`. Each contact raises it by exactly `v_n`, and the polar term is exactly the
  drift (Thm 3, PROVED; the complete-form companion of `rem:Cheb`). `E ≪_ε e^{εu}` for every ε is RH (Prop. 4, PROVED
  via a classical argument). So the conserved/monotone quantity attached to the jumps is the size of `ψ(x) − x`:
  **EQUIVALENT**.
* **(d)** No-go (Thm 5, PROVED from `thm:finmod` and `rem:resweights`). Any certifying quantity whose jump rule
  preserves nonnegativity under a one-sided change of a single weight is contradicted by amplified or deleted combs.
  The set `𝒮 = {v ≥ 0 : λ^A_T(v) ≥ 0 ∀T}` is convex. Under RH and a non-degeneracy hypothesis (ND, OPEN), the comb of ζ lies on its **boundary** (Cor. 6).
  So a "for all T" argument must be a supporting-hyperplane identity at an exact point, and in the limit the supporting
  functionals are zero-side data (`thm:Z`). That is the record's wall E3/E4, reached from the jump side.

## §1 The contact increment is not finite rank

Fix `n ≥ 2` with `T_n = ½log n`, and let `T = T_n + ε/2` with `0 < ε < log n`, so that `T < log n`. Put
`L = (−T, −T+ε)` and `R = (T−ε, T)`. These intervals are disjoint (`ε < T`) and `R = L + log n`. On `L²(I_T)`, the
compressed shift `S_n F(t) := 1_{I_T}(t) F(t − log n)` maps `L²(L)` isometrically onto `L²(R)` and kills `L²(I_T \ L)`.
The contact term of `𝔞_T` and `W_T` is `−v_n c_F(log n) = −w_n ⟨(S_n + S_n^*)F, F⟩` for real `F`.

**Proposition 1 (PROVED; both forms).**
(i) Let `U : L²(0,ε) ⊗ ℂ² → L²(L) ⊕ L²(R)`, `U(f ⊗ e_1 + g ⊗ e_2)(t) = f(t+T)1_L + g(t−T+ε)1_R`. `U` is unitary and
`U^*(S_n + S_n^*)U = 1 ⊗ σ_x`, where `σ_x = [[0,1],[1,0]]`. `S_n + S_n^*` vanishes on `L²(I_T \ (L∪R))`. Hence the
increment `−w_n(S_n + S_n^*)` has spectrum `{−w_n, 0, w_n}`, and `±w_n` have infinite multiplicity for every `ε > 0`.
Its norm is `w_n` for every `ε > 0`, while it is `0` at `ε = 0`. So the contact is not a rank-≤2 perturbation. It is
"rank 2 per point of the layer", and there is no Cauchy interlacing between `T_n` and `T`. (In any case the
domains `L²(I_{T_n}) ⊂ L²(I_T)` differ by infinite codimension.)
(ii) Parity. Put `f(s) = F(−T+s)`, `0 < s < ε`. If `F` is even, then `c_F(log n) = (f*f)(ε) := ∫_0^ε f(s)f(ε−s)ds`.
If `F` is odd, then `c_F(log n) = −(f*f)(ε)`. So the contact contributes `−v_n(f*f)(ε)` to the even sector and
`+v_n(f*f)(ε)` to the odd sector.

*Proof.* (i) `S_n` restricted to `L²(L)` is the translation by `log n`, which is onto `L²(R)`. In the coordinates `U` it
is `f ⊗ e_1 ↦ f ⊗ e_2`, because `t ∈ L ↦ t + log n = t + 2T − ε ∈ R` and `(t+T) = (t + log n) − T + ε`. Hence
`S_n + S_n^* = U(1 ⊗ σ_x)U^*` on the layers, and it is `0` elsewhere. The spectrum of `σ_x` is `{±1}`, with eigenvectors
`e_1 ± e_2`.
(ii) `c_F(log n) = ∫_R F(t)F(t − log n)dt`. With `t = T − ε + s`, `t − log n = −T + s`, and `F(T−ε+s) = ±F(−T+ε−s) = ±f(ε−s)`. ∎

Remark (PROVED, trivial). In the dilated frame of `lem:contT` (fixed interval `I_T`, contacts at `log n / r`) the entry
is again an operator of norm `w_n` on a layer of width `→ 0`. So a change of frame does not produce a finite-rank jump
either. The only setting with a genuine rank-2 step is a lattice (Toeplitz) model, in which all `log n` lie in `hℤ`. There
the entry of a lag is the corner pair `(1,N),(N,1)`, and positivity for all `N` is `|α_N| < 1` for the Verblunsky
coefficients (Szegő–Verblunsky, CITED). For ζ, `log 2 / log 3 ∉ ℚ` (unique factorisation, PROVED). So the contacts lie in
no lattice. Lattice sampling of the problem is `thm:AL` (RETRACTED → AL′, `Θ(1/M)` deficit), and truncation decides no
window (`thm:arena`). The discrete jump calculus is the Kreĭn/KEP restatement (`thm:KEP3`; R157_002 row 39; G2_B5_R1):
**EQUIVALENT**.

## §2 Soft entry: the contact is invisible to resolution `1/log(1/ε)`

**Lemma 2a (layer mass; PROVED).** For `F ∈ L²(ℝ)` with `‖F‖_Q < ∞`, an interval `J` of length `ε`, and any `Ω > 0`:
```
‖1_J F‖ ≤ (εΩ/π)^{1/2} ‖F‖ + ‖F‖_Q / log(2+Ω)^{1/2}.
```
*Proof.* Split `F = F_{≤Ω} + F_{>Ω}` by the Fourier cut `|τ| ≤ Ω`. Then
`‖F_{≤Ω}‖_∞ ≤ (2π)^{-1}∫_{|τ|≤Ω}|F̂| ≤ (2π)^{-1}(2Ω)^{1/2}(2π)^{1/2}‖F‖ = (Ω/π)^{1/2}‖F‖`, so
`‖1_J F_{≤Ω}‖ ≤ (εΩ/π)^{1/2}‖F‖`. Also `‖F_{>Ω}‖² = (2π)^{-1}∫_{|τ|>Ω}|F̂|² ≤ ‖F‖_Q²/log(2+Ω)`. ∎

**Theorem 2 (soft entry; PROVED; both forms).** Fix `T_0 > 0`, `n` with `T_n < T_0`, and `T = T_n + ε/2 ≤ T_0` with
`0 < ε ≤ ε_0 < 1/e`. Let `W_T^{(−n)}` and `𝔞_T^{(−n)}` be the forms with `v_n` replaced by `0`. Then there is `C = C(T_0)`
such that
```
|λ^W_T − λ^W_T[W^{(−n)}]| ≤ C w_n / log(1/ε), |λ^A_T − λ^A_T[𝔞^{(−n)}]| ≤ C w_n / log(1/ε).
```
*Proof.* Take `F` real and unit (the forms are real, so real near-minimisers suffice). By the proof of `lem:contT`,
`‖F‖_Q² ≤ W_T(F) + C'‖F‖²`, where `C' = C_1 + Σ_{n<e^{2T_0}}v_n + 4sinh T_0`. The same bound holds for `W^{(−n)}` and,
without the polar term, for `𝔞` and `𝔞^{(−n)}`. For a unit `F` with form value `≤ λ + 1`, where `λ` is the smaller of
the two bottoms and `|λ| ≤ C'`, we get `‖F‖_Q² ≤ Q_0 := 2C' + 1`. The two forms differ by `−v_n c_F(log n)`, and
`|c_F(log n)| = |⟨1_R F, S_n 1_L F⟩| ≤ ‖1_L F‖‖1_R F‖`. Apply Lemma 2a with `Ω = 1/(ε log(1/ε))`. For `ε ≤ ε_0` both terms
are `≤ C''/log(1/ε)^{1/2}`, so `v_n|c_F(log n)| ≤ C w_n/log(1/ε)`. Evaluate the minimising sequence of one form in the
other, in both directions. ∎

**Reading.** At the entry point the sign of either form is insensitive to `Λ(n)`, to resolution `1/log(1/ε)`. By
`rem:resweights`, at a window `T ≫ T_n` the sign can be flipped by a change `≈ e^{−14.4T}` of the same `Λ(n)`. So the
dependence on `Λ(n)` is not created at the jump. It accumulates over the whole later history `T > T_n`, through the
growth of the layers into all of `I_T`. A jump inequality at `T_n` therefore cannot carry the arithmetic: there is
nothing at the jump to carry. Sharpness of the modulus `1/log(1/ε)`, and whether `λ^A` has a kink at `T_n`, are **OPEN**.
Boundary traces of minimisers of log-order forms are also OPEN. HEURISTIC: they vanish like `(log 1/δ)^{-1/2}`, which
would make the modulus sharp.

## §3 The exact jump + drift identity for the complete form

Let `S(u) = Σ_{log n ≤ u} v_n` (right-continuous, with jumps of exactly `v_n` at `u = log n`) and
```
E(u) := S(u) − 8 sinh(u/2), u ≥ 0, E(0) = 0.
```

**Theorem 3 (PROVED; complete form; companion of `rem:Cheb`).** For real `F ∈ C_c^∞(I_T)`:
```
W_T(F) = (2π)^{-1}∫ m |F̂|² − ∫_{(0,2T)} c_F dE = (2π)^{-1}∫ m |F̂|² + ∫_0^{2T} c_F'(u) E(u) du.
```
*Proof.* `A(F)B(F) = ∫∫F(s)F(t)e^{(s−t)/2}ds dt = ∫_ℝ c_F(u)e^{u/2}du = ∫_0^{2T} c_F(u)·2cosh(u/2)du`, because `c_F` is
even. So `2AB = ∫_0^{2T} c_F d(8 sinh(u/2))`. The comb term is `Σ_{N_T} v_n c_F(log n) = ∫_{(0,2T)} c_F dS`. Subtract.
`c_F ∈ C_c^∞(−2T,2T)` and `E(0) = 0`, so integrating by parts gives the second form. ∎

So in the complete form, the polar term is **exactly** the continuous drift that compensates the jumps. Every contact
raises `E` by exactly `v_n = 2Λ(n)/√n`, and between contacts `E` decreases at the rate `4cosh(u/2)`. The remainder is
`E(u) = 2∫_1^{e^u} y^{−1/2} d(ψ(y) − y) − 4 + 4e^{−u/2}`.

**Proposition 4 (PROVED; classical in substance).** RH ⟺ for every `ε > 0`, `E(u) = O_ε(e^{εu})`.
*Proof.* (⇐) For `Re s > ½`, `∫_{(0,∞)} e^{−su}dS(u) = Σ v_n n^{−s} = −2(ζ′/ζ)(s+½)`, and
`∫_0^∞ e^{−su}4cosh(u/2)du = 2/(s−½) + 2/(s+½)`. So `G(s) := ∫e^{−su}dE = −2(ζ′/ζ)(s+½) − 2/(s−½) − 2/(s+½)` for
`Re s > ½`. If `E = O(e^{εu})`, integration by parts shows that `G(s) = s∫_0^∞ e^{−su}E(u)du` converges absolutely and is
holomorphic on `Re s > ε`. This gives a continuation of `−2ζ′/ζ(s+½) − 2/(s−½)` holomorphic on `Re s > ε`, so ζ has no
zeros with `Re ρ > ½ + ε`. (⇒) Under RH, `ψ(y) − y ≪ y^{1/2}log²y` (von Koch, CITED), and
partial summation gives `E(u) ≪ u³`. ∎

So the quantity that is naturally conserved or monotone across the jumps is the running remainder `E`, and its
sub-exponential size is RH: **EQUIVALENT**. The window form reads `E` exactly on `(0,2T)`, through `c_F'`. That is
`prop:finite` / E4: the finite datum `(Λ(n))_{n<e^{2T}}`.

## §4 No-go for a jump-monotone certifying quantity

Consider weights `a ≥ 0` on `n ≥ 2`, with `v_n(a) = 2a(n)/√n`. Write `λ^A_T(a)` and `λ^W_T(a)` for the corresponding
bottoms, and `RD_T(a)` for `λ^A_T(a) ≥ 0`.

**Theorem 5 (PROVED, from `thm:finmod` and `rem:resweights`).** Let `(Q_T)_{T>0}` be functionals of the comb with
(Q1) locality: `Q_T(a)` depends only on `a|_{N_T}` (and Γ-data);
(Q2) certification: `Q_T(a) ≥ 0 ⟹ RD_T(a)`;
(Q3⁺) one-sided jump monotonicity: if `a′ = a + c1_{n_0}` with `c ≥ 0`, then `Q_T(a) ≥ 0 ∀T` implies `Q_T(a′) ≥ 0 ∀T`.
Then `Q_T(Λ) ≥ 0 ∀T` is false. The same conclusion holds with (Q3⁻), the version with `c ≤ 0`, `a′ ≥ 0`, applied to
the deleted combs `Λ1_{n≥N}`.
(b) Quantitative form. Let `F_⋆` be a real unit minimiser of `𝔞_T` on `P_T` with `c_{F_⋆}(log n_0) ≠ 0`, and let `s` be
its sign. Then any `Q` satisfying (Q1)–(Q2) and certifying `Λ` at `T` must fail to certify `Λ + sε1_{n_0}` for every
admissible `ε > ε_*(T,n_0)`. Here `ε_*(T,n_0) ≤ 2·10⁹ T e^{−14.449T}/|c_{F_⋆}(log n_0)|` for `T ≥ 1`.
*Proof.* (Q3⁺): take `n_0 = 2` and `c > c_*(T,2)` for some `T > ½log2` (`thm:finmod`(ii)). If `Q_T(Λ) ≥ 0` for all `T`,
then (Q3⁺) gives `Q_T(Λ + c1_2) ≥ 0` for all `T`, so `RD_T(Λ + c1_2)` holds by (Q2). This contradicts `thm:finmod`(ii).
(Q3⁻): removing the weights `n < N` one at a time is a finite chain of one-sided decreases, and `RD_T(Λ1_{n≥N})` fails
on `[0.60, ½log N]` (`thm:finmod`(i)). (b) is `rem:resweights` read through (Q2). ∎

So a certifying quantity for ζ cannot be monotone in any single jump weight, in either direction. At window `T` it must
distinguish `Λ(n_0)` from `Λ(n_0) ± e^{−14.4T}` (conditional on the non-degeneracy of `rem:resweights`, which is OPEN).
As `T → ∞` it must see the exact value. That is F1 and O3, and the jump structure does not supply it.

**Corollary 6 (primitive form).**
(i) **PROVED.** `𝒮 := {a ≥ 0 : RD_T(a) ∀T}` is convex and closed in the product topology.
(ii) **PROVED under RH plus hypothesis (ND).** (ND) says: there are `κ > 0` and `T_k → ∞` such that some real unit
minimiser `F_k` of `𝔞_{T_k}` on `P_{T_k}` has `|c_{F_k}(log n_k)| ≥ κ` at some admissible contact `n_k ∈ N_{T_k}`.
"Admissible" means `Λ(n_k) > 0` or `c_{F_k}(log n_k) > 0`. Under RH and (ND), `Λ ∈ 𝒮 ∩ ∂𝒮` in the weighted-`ℓ¹`
metric `d(a,b) = Σ_n 2|a(n) − b(n)|/√n`. That is, for every `δ > 0` there is `a ≥ 0` with `d(a,Λ) ≤ δ` and `a ∉ 𝒮`.
Whether (ND) holds is **OPEN**. Its failure at all large `T` is the degenerate case of `lem:degen`.

*Proof.* (i) `V_T` is concave and finite on `ℝ^{N_T}` (`lem:degen`), hence continuous, so `{a : V_T(v(a|_{N_T})) ≥ 0}`
is closed, convex, and depends on finitely many coordinates. `𝒮` is the intersection of these sets over `T`.
(ii) Under RH, `Λ ∈ 𝒮` (E2) and `λ^A_T(Λ) → 0` (`thm:margin`). Given `δ`, choose `k` with
`λ^A_{T_k}(Λ) < δκ`, and let `s` be the sign of `c_{F_k}(log n_k)`. Let `a` be `Λ` with the weight at `n_k` moved so that
`v_{n_k}` changes by `sδ′`, where `δ′ = δ` if `s = +1`, and `δ′ = min(δ, v_{n_k}(Λ))` if `s = −1`. The supergradient
inequality of `lem:degen` gives `V_{T_k}(a) ≤ λ^A_{T_k}(Λ) − δ′κ`. This is `< 0` whenever `δ′ = δ`. In the case `s = −1`
with `v_{n_k}(Λ) < δ`, the same inequality with `δ′ = v_{n_k}(Λ)` needs `λ^A_{T_k}(Λ) < v_{n_k}κ`, which is ensured by
choosing `k` large, since `v_{n_k} ≥ 2 log 2 · e^{−T_k}` whereas `λ^A ≤ 4·10⁹ T e^{−15.449T}`. So `a ∉ 𝒮`, and
`d(a,Λ) ≤ δ`. ∎

**Reading.** A "for all `T`" argument must certify that one specific point lies in a convex set on whose boundary it
sits. By `lem:degen`, the supporting functionals are the autocorrelations of the minimisers on the contacts. By
`thm:Z` / `cor:zeros`, their limits are zero-side data. So the exact identity that the jump calculus would have to
supply is a supporting-hyperplane identity for `𝒮` at `Λ`. The only such identity on record is the explicit formula
(`thm:Z`, E3), and no finite zero set supplies it (`thm:finZ`).
