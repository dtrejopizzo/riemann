## §0 Verdict in one paragraph

The operator-algebra angle does **not** produce an object passing OT-5′. It does produce five proved statements that
fix exactly where such an object cannot be, and one reformulation that turns OT-5′ into a uniform estimate.

1. **Positivity is never the missing ingredient in operator algebras.** A positive functional (state, weight, trace)
  is positive by definition. The whole content moves into the Lefschetz identity OT-2.
2. **Any carrier that passes OT-2 is a Hilbert–Pólya model** (Thm 2, GNS collapse). Its flow-cyclic GNS part is
  canonically `L²(μ_Z)`, where `μ_Z` is the zero measure. The positive functional also has to be an infinite weight of
  logarithmic density: no state works (Thm 1).
3. **Inherited forms are null** (Thms 3–4). Suppose the ambient Hilbert space carries a unitary normalised flow with no
  point spectrum. Then every form that `H¹ = H/V` inherits from it vanishes identically on every eigenclass, on or off
  the line. This covers:
  * `L²(C_Q)`;
  * `L^p(X, μ)` for any measure scaled by the flow;
  * the II_∞ Takesaki core with its dual trace (for example Bost–Connes at `β ≤ 1`, or Connes' foliation factor).

  The II_∞ dual trace is conformal, `τ∘θ_s = e^{−s}τ`, which is exactly Deninger's `α = 1`. **It carries no L^p
  eigenvector at all** (Thm 4). This is a proved obstruction, stronger than the "restatement" verdict of R158_004 A23(iii).
4. **Bost–Connes at `β > 1`** has an inner flow with one-sided spectrum `log N`, and its states are finite. It is
  excluded by Prop 6 and Thm 1.
5. **Visibility** (Thm 7). A carrier of polynomial growth only ever sees critical eigenvalues, so on it OT-2 is
  RH-strength. A carrier that can see every zero of the strip must have growth type `½`.
6. **The bridge G1 ↔ G2** (Thm 8, Sz.-Nagy). On any Hilbertian `H¹`, OT-5′ in the P-HS form holds **if and only if**
  `sup_{s∈ℝ} ‖e^{−s/2}φ^s|_{H¹}‖ < ∞`. The positive form is then an invariant mean, defined before the spectrum.
  * Each finite window `|s| ≤ T` is bounded automatically.
  * The content is the uniformity in `s`.

  Construction 9 gives a zero-free Hilbertian carrier of growth type `½` (a weighted Meyer–Connes quotient). On it,
  OT-5′ is exactly that uniform bound. By Lemma U / Lemma T, the bound implies RH ∧ simple zeros ∧ a Riesz-basis
  property. So it is RH-strength, and stronger than RH. By R158_004's `S₃` falsifier its field-uniform version is false
  (modulo cyclicity).

## §1 Setting: operator-algebraic carriers

Conventions of P47: `Φ_F(s) = ∫F(t)e^{(s−½)t}dt`; for real `F`, `W_T(F) = Σ_ρ Φ_F(ρ)Φ_F(1−ρ)` (`eq:W`). For
`f ∈ C_c^∞(ℝ)` write `W(f) := Σ_ρ Φ_f(ρ)`, the Weil distribution. Here `f̃(t) = \overline{f(−t)}` and
`c_F = F * F̃` is the autocorrelation, and `W(c_F) = W_T(F)` for real `F ∈ C_c^∞(I_T)`. This holds because
`Φ_{F̃}(s) = Φ_{\bar F}(1−s)` and Φ is multiplicative on convolutions. The **normalised flow** is `U_t = e^{−t/2}φ^t`.
On a `ρ`-eigenvector `U_t` acts by `e^{(ρ−½)t}`, and `F(U) := ∫F(t)U_t dt` acts by `Φ_F(ρ)`.

**Definition 1.1 (OA-carrier).** An *operator-algebraic carrier* is a quadruple `𝒪 = (𝔄, u, φ, ℐ)` where:
* `𝔄` is a *-algebra (in the cases below, a von Neumann algebra or a C*-algebra with its multipliers);
* `u : ℝ → 𝔄` is a unitary group (the normalised flow), strongly continuous in a representation;
* `u(f) := ∫ f(t)u_t dt`, so that `u : (C_c^∞(ℝ), *, ~) → 𝔄` is a *-homomorphism;
* `φ` is a positive functional (`φ(x^*x) ≥ 0`) on a *-subalgebra containing `u(C_c^∞)`. It may be a state, or a
  normal semifinite weight restricted to its definition ideal;
* `ℐ ⊂ C_c^∞(ℝ)` is the test class: `ℐ = C_c^∞` for the **complete** form, or `ℐ = {f : Φ_f(0) = Φ_f(1) = 0}` for the
  **primitive** form.

The **Lefschetz identity** of `𝒪` (its OT-2) is `φ(u(f)) = W(f)` for all `f ∈ ℐ * ℐ̃`.

**Remark 1.2 (why this is the right formalisation of the angle; `HEURISTIC`).** A trace or state on a crossed product
`C_0(Y) ⋊ ℝ` or `M ⋊_σ ℝ` restricts, through the canonical unitaries `λ_t` implementing the action, to a functional
on `u(C_c^∞)`. So "a trace on a crossed product whose value on the flow is the explicit formula" is exactly Def 1.1.
The only other option is a trace on a *quotient* (a cohomology) of a module over the crossed product. That is treated
in §3.

## §2 Theorems on OA-carriers

### Theorem 1 (no state; the weight has logarithmic density) — `PROVED`

*(a)* There is no OA-carrier with a **bounded** positive functional (a state, up to scale) satisfying the complete
Lefschetz identity.

*(b)* More precisely, fix `χ ∈ C_c^∞(−1,1)` real, even, `χ ≥ 0`, `∫χ = 1`, and put `F_ε(t) = ε^{−1}χ(t/ε)`. Then
`‖c_{F_ε}‖_{L¹} ≤ 1`, while

  `W(c_{F_ε}) = W_ε(F_ε) ≥ (‖χ̂‖²_2 / 2πε)·(log(1/ε) − C) → ∞` as `ε ↓ 0`.

*(c)* The same divergence holds for the primitive form, after a bounded two-dimensional correction.

*Proof.*
* **Setup.** For `ε < ½log 2` the window `I_ε` has no contacts (`N_ε = ∅`, since `log n ≥ log 2 > 2ε`). So
  `s_ε = m`, and by `eq:form`, `eq:W`:
  `W_ε(F_ε) = (1/2π)∫ m(τ)|χ̂(ετ)|²dτ + 2A(F_ε)B(F_ε)`.
* **The archimedean term.** `m(τ) = Re ψ(¼ + iτ/2) − log π ≥ log(1+|τ|) − C_1` for all real `τ`. This follows from
  Stirling, `Re ψ(z) = log|z| + O(1/|z|)` for `Re z = ¼`, and continuity on compacts. Substituting `σ = ετ`:
  `(1/2π)∫ m|χ̂(ετ)|² dτ ≥ (1/2πε)∫ (log|σ| + log(1/ε) − C_1)|χ̂(σ)|² dσ`.
  Since `log|σ||χ̂(σ)|²` is integrable, this gives the displayed bound.
* **The Tate moments.** `A(F_ε), B(F_ε) → 1`.
* **Contradiction with a state.** For a bounded positive `φ` and the *-homomorphism `u`,
  `|φ(u(c_F))| ≤ ‖φ‖‖u(c_F)‖ ≤ ‖φ‖ ‖c_F‖_{L¹} ≤ ‖φ‖ ‖F‖²_{L¹} = ‖φ‖`.
  This contradicts the divergence.
* **Primitive case.** Fix `G_0, G_1 ∈ C_c^∞(I_{T_0})`, `T_0 < ½log 2`, dual to `(A, B)`. Put
  `F' = F_ε − A(F_ε)G_1 − B(F_ε)G_0`. The coefficients are bounded, and `‖F'‖_{L¹} ≤ 1 + O(1)`. Write `m = m_+ − m_−`
  with `0 ≤ m_− ≤ |C_0|`. Cauchy–Schwarz for the positive form `∫ m_+|·̂|²` gives `𝔞(F') ≥ 𝔞(F_ε) − O(𝔞(F_ε)^{1/2}) −
  O(1) → ∞`. ∎

*Reading (`PROVED` + `HEURISTIC`).* The functional must be an **unbounded weight**. The divergence rate
`(1/2π)log(|τ|/2π)` is the archimedean multiplier, i.e. the Riemann–von Mangoldt density of the zeros. The weight must
see an infinite, logarithmically dense spectrum. This matches `thm:finZ`: a finite zero set gives a finite measure,
hence a bounded functional (`HEURISTIC` link; `thm:finZ` itself is about flat-top certificates, a different
statement). It also matches the logarithmic growth of CC's RR dimension (MAP_5).

### Theorem 2 (GNS collapse: a carrier that passes OT-2 is a Hilbert–Pólya model) — `PROVED`; content `CLASSICAL`

Let `𝒪` be an OA-carrier satisfying the complete Lefschetz identity. Then:

*(i)* `W(f * f̃) ≥ 0` for every `f ∈ C_c^∞`, hence RH holds (Weil's criterion; P47 §1; P45 Thm 10.4).

*(ii)* Let `K_φ` be the closure of `{u(f)Ω_φ}` in the GNS space of `φ`, with the action `u_s`. It is unitarily equivalent
to `L²(ℝ, μ_Z)`, `μ_Z = Σ_γ m_γ δ_γ`, with `u_s` acting as multiplication by `e^{iγs}` (sign as in P47). In particular
`u` has pure point spectrum on `K_φ`, equal to the set of ordinates, and the spectrum is simple (multiplicity enters as
the weight `m_γ`).

*(iii)* The same holds for the primitive identity on `ℐ` (GNS of `ℐ`), with RH by Bombieri 2000 Thm 1 / P45 Thm 10.4.

*Proof.*
* **(i)** `φ(u(f)^*u(f)) = φ(u(f̃ * f)) = W(f̃ * f) ≥ 0`, which is Weil positivity on all tests.
* **(ii) The inner product.** `⟨u(f)Ω, u(g)Ω⟩ = φ(u(g̃ * f)) = W(g̃ * f)`, and under RH this equals
  `Σ_γ m_γ Φ_f(½+iγ)\overline{Φ_g(½+iγ)}`. So `u(f)Ω ↦ (Φ_f(½+iγ))_γ` is isometric into `ℓ²(m) = L²(μ_Z)`.
* **(ii) Dense range.** For any finite set of ordinates, `C_c^∞` functions interpolate arbitrary values of `Φ_f`. That
  is Paley–Wiener, plus the fact that `f ↦ (Φ_f(ρ_j))_j` is onto `ℂ^J`, because exponentials are independent.
* **(ii) Intertwining.** `u_s u(f) = u(f(·−s))`, and `Φ_{f(·−s)}(½+iγ) = e^{iγs}Φ_f(½+iγ)`.
* **(iii)** is identical on `ℐ`. ∎

**Corollary 2.1 (conservation law; `PROVED`).** In the operator-algebra angle, OT-5′ as "a positive form defined before
the spectrum" holds for free: every state and every weight is one. The Lefschetz identity OT-2 then carries all of RH.
Any proof of it constructs, via (ii), a Hilbert–Pólya realisation (R157_002 row 32: EQUIV). **So the OA angle can
contribute only through a proof of OT-2 for a positive weight, and such a proof is RH-strength.** This is Lemma T in
GNS form (R158_001 §3.1). It is not new in content; it is the precise reason why "a trace on a crossed product" is not
by itself progress.

**Remark 2.2 (primitive vs complete; `PROVED`).** Thm 2(i) is the complete form. It needs `φ` positive on all of
`u(C_c^∞)`. The primitive version needs positivity only on `u(ℐ)`. Per window the two are not interchangeable
(`thm:parityschur`, `cor:convfail`, C22). Globally both are RH.

**Remark 2.3 (differences of weights; `PROVED`, standard).** Suppose OT-2 holds in the form
`φ_1∘u − φ_2∘u = W` with `φ_1, φ_2 ≥ 0`. Examples: Connes' `Tr_X − Tr_Y` (CC 1805.10501 l.887); the S-local cutoff
`Tr(R_Λ ·) − 2 log Λ·δ`. Then RH ⟺ `φ_2∘u ≤ φ_1∘u` on squares. By the Radon–Nikodym theorem for positive functionals
dominated by a positive functional (standard: Dixmier, *C\*-algèbres* 2.5.1; Pedersen–Takesaki for weights), this is
equivalent to a positive contraction `h`, in the commutant of the GNS representation of `φ_1`, with
`φ_2(u(f)^*u(f)) = ⟨h u(f)Ω_1, u(f)Ω_1⟩`. This is D.114's "contractive conditional expectation ⟺ D" in commutant
language. `RESTATEMENT`.

## §3 Inherited forms and flows without point spectrum

The one freedom Def 1.1 leaves is to take `H¹` as a **quotient** `H/V` of an ambient space by flow-invariant
"coboundaries". This is Connes' cokernel (1999), Meyer's `H¹`, and CCM's distillation. The natural positive form is
then the one **inherited** from the ambient Hilbert space.

### Theorem 3 (null inheritance) — `PROVED`

Let `H` be a Hilbert space, `U` a strongly continuous unitary group on `H` with **empty point spectrum**, and `V ⊂ H`
a `U`-invariant linear subspace (not necessarily closed). Let `q([x]) = inf_{v∈V}‖x − v‖` be the inherited seminorm on
`H/V`. Call `[x]` an *eigenclass* if `U_s x − c(s)x ∈ V` for all `s` and some function `c`. Call it a *generalised
eigenclass of order k* if `(U_s − c(s))^k x ∈ V` in the natural sense. Define this recursively: `U_s x − c(s)x − y_s ∈ V`
with each `y_s` in the span of generalised eigenclasses of order `k−1`. Then:

*(i)* `q` vanishes on every generalised eigenclass and on their linear span, **whatever `c` is**, on or off the unit
circle.

*(ii)* Consequently every sesquilinear form on `H/V` that is continuous for `q` (in particular the inherited inner
product) vanishes identically on the span of the eigenclasses.

*Proof.*
* **The projection.** `V̄` is closed and `U`-invariant, so `V̄^⊥` is `U`-invariant (`U` is unitary). The orthogonal
  projection `P` onto `V̄^⊥` commutes with every `U_s`, and `q([x]) = ‖Px‖`.
* **Order 1.** `U_s Px = c(s)Px` for all `s`. If `Px ≠ 0`, then `c(s+t) = c(s)c(t)`, `|c(s)| = 1` (unitarity), and `Px`
  is an eigenvector of `U`. That contradicts empty point spectrum. So `Px = 0`.
* **Order k.** By induction `P y_s = 0`, so `U_s Px = c(s)Px`, and the order-1 argument applies.
* **Spans and forms.** Spans follow by linearity of `P`. (ii) follows from (i) and continuity. ∎

**Proposition 3.1 (reflexive `L^p`; `PROVED`, standard argument).** The conclusion "the closed quotient `H/V̄` has no
eigenvector" holds when `H` is reflexive, `U` is uniformly bounded, and neither `U` nor the adjoint group `U^*` has
point spectrum.

*Proof.* Let `[x] ≠ 0` be an eigenvector of the induced action on `Q = H/V̄`, with character `c`, `|c| = 1`
(boundedness). Pick `ξ ∈ Q^*` with `ξ([x]) ≠ 0`. The averages `η_S = (2S)^{−1}∫_{−S}^{S} c(s)U_s^*ξ ds` are bounded in
the reflexive space `Q^* = V^⊥ ⊂ H^*`. A weak cluster point `η` satisfies `η([x]) = ξ([x]) ≠ 0`. By the Følner property
of `[−S,S]`, `U_s^*η = \overline{c(s)}η`. So `η ∈ H^*` is an eigenvector of `U^*`, a contradiction. ∎

(It fails for `L¹`: the characters `f ↦ f̂(γ)` are bounded on `L¹(ℝ)`, and `L¹/ker` is one-dimensional. `L¹` quotients
carry no Hilbert form, and they see **every** frequency, so they select nothing.)

### Theorem 4 (trace-scaling flows have no `L^p` eigenvectors) — `PROVED`

Let `M` be a von Neumann algebra with a faithful normal semifinite trace `τ`. Let `θ : ℝ → Aut(M)` be a σ-weakly
continuous flow with `τ∘θ_s = e^{−κs}τ` for some `κ ≠ 0`, or with `κ = 0` and `θ` ergodic on projections of finite
trace. Let `0 < p < ∞`. If `x ∈ L^p(M, τ)` satisfies `θ_s(x) = c(s)x` for all `s`, then `x = 0`.

*Proof.*
* **Reduction to a measure.** `θ_s` extends to `τ`-measurable operators and commutes with the polar decomposition.
  So `θ_s(|x|) = |c(s)||x|`, and `c(s) = e^{zs}` (continuous character). Put `a = Re z`. For Borel `E ⊂ (0,∞)` let
  `m(E) = τ(1_E(|x|))`. This is a positive measure, finite on `(ε, ∞)` because `τ(1_{(ε,∞)}(|x|)) ≤ ε^{−p}‖x‖_p^p`.
* **The scaling law.** `θ_s(1_E(|x|)) = 1_E(e^{as}|x|) = 1_{e^{−as}E}(|x|)`. Applying `τ`:
  `m(e^{−as}E) = e^{−κs}m(E)`.
* **Case `a = 0`.** Then `m(E) = e^{−κs}m(E)` for all `s`. If `κ ≠ 0`, `m(E) ∈ {0, ∞}`, and finiteness on `(ε, ∞)` gives
  `m = 0`. If `κ = 0`, the projections `1_{(ε,∞)}(|x|)` are θ-invariant with finite trace, so ergodicity gives `0`. In
  both cases `|x| = 0` by faithfulness.
* **Case `a ≠ 0`.** Put `μ = r^p dm(r)`. It is a finite measure of total mass `‖x‖_p^p`, and it satisfies
  `μ(λE) = λ^{p+κ/a}μ(E)` for all `λ > 0`. The total mass is scale-invariant, so either `μ = 0` or `p + κ/a = 0`. In the
  second case `μ` is dilation-invariant, and every dyadic shell `(2^k, 2^{k+1}]` has the same mass `μ((1,2])`. Finite
  total mass then forces `μ((1,2]) = 0`, hence `μ = 0`, hence `x = 0`. ∎

*Remarks (`PROVED`).*
* `κ ≠ 0` needs no ergodicity.
* The commutative case `M = L^∞(Y, ν)` with `ν∘θ_s = e^{−κs}ν` is the classical fact that a flow scaling a measure has
  no `L^p` eigenfunctions.
* The norm relation `‖θ_s x‖_p^p = e^{−κs}‖x‖_p^p` shows that a would-be eigenvector must have `Re z = −κ/p`. This is
  **Serre's argument, automatic**: conformality alone places every eigenvalue on one line. Thm 4 says that on the
  trace-scaling carrier there is nothing to place.

### Corollary 5 (the II_∞ dual trace, `L²(C_Q)`, Bost–Connes at `β ≤ 1`) — `PROVED` modulo `CITED` standard facts

*(a) The Takesaki core.* Let `N` be a factor of type III_1 with faithful normal state `ω`, and let `M = N ⋊_{σ^ω} ℝ` be
its core. Then `M` is a II_∞ factor with trace `τ` and dual action `θ`, `τ∘θ_s = e^{−s}τ` (Takesaki, Acta Math. 131
(1973); Takesaki, *Theory of Operator Algebras II*, Ch. XII; `CITED`, standard). Two facts follow.
* **The dual action.** The normalised dual action `U_s = e^{s/2}θ_s` is unitary on `L²(M, τ)` and has no eigenvector
  (Thm 4 with `κ = 1`, `p = 2`). In particular, the conformal positive form that the dual trace supplies, i.e. exactly
  (D3) with `α = 1`, lives on a space with **no point spectrum**. By Thm 3 its inheritance to any `H¹ = L²(M,τ)/V`
  vanishes on every zero-eigenclass. By Prop 3.1 this extends to `L^p`, `1 < p < ∞`.
* **The implemented flow.** The flow `λ_t = h^{it}` implementing `σ^ω` inside `M` is `τ`-preserving. Left multiplication
  by `λ_t` on `L²(M,τ)` also has no eigenvector. An eigenvector would satisfy `x = e x`, where `e = 1_{\{γ\}}(log h) ∈ M`.
  But `τ` restricted to `{h}''` is `ω(1)·e^{−x}dx` in the variable `x = log h`, which is Lebesgue class (dual-weight
  formula `τ = ω̃(h^{−1}·)`, `CITED` standard). So `τ(e) = 0`, and `e = 0` by faithfulness.

*(b) Bost–Connes at `0 < β ≤ 1`.* The unique KMS_β state generates a III_1 factor (Bost–Connes, Selecta 1995; for the
type, Neshveyev and Laca; `CITED`, not re-read), and its modular group is the rescaled BC time evolution. The BC time
evolution is outer there, so it cannot be implemented inside the factor. Its only operator-algebraic implementation is
the core of (a), and (a) applies. **This is Connes' foliation II_∞ factor situation** ("the dual action scales the
trace in the same manner", essay l.700, quoted in R158_002 §4.5). The dual trace therefore fails as an OT-5′ carrier:
* it fails **OT-1**: no `H¹` of eigenvectors in any `L^p`, `0 < p < ∞`;
* it fails **OT-5′**: every inherited form on a quotient `H¹` is null.

*(c) `L²(C_Q)`.* `C_Q = ℝ_+^* × Ẑ^*`, and the scaling flow is the regular representation of `ℝ` tensored with the
identity. It has Lebesgue spectrum, hence no point spectrum. By Thm 3, every `H¹` taken as a quotient of `L²(C_Q)` with
the inherited norm has a null form on its eigenclasses. This is why Connes' Hilbert-space picture shows the zeros as an
**absorption** spectrum (a cokernel) and not as eigenvectors. Thm 3 is the one-line reason: the eigenclasses have
inherited norm zero.

### Proposition 6 (Bost–Connes at `β > 1`) — `PROVED`

For `β > 1` the extremal KMS_β states are Gibbs states `Tr(e^{−βH}π_u(·))/ζ(β)` on `ℓ²(ℕ)`, with `Hε_n = (log n)ε_n`
(`CITED`, Bost–Connes 1995; D.116 (1.1)–(1.3)). The time evolution is inner, `σ_t = Ad e^{itH}`, so `u_t = e^{itH}`
lies in the von Neumann algebra `B(ℓ²(ℕ))`.
* **Weights.** Every normal semifinite weight on `B(ℓ²)` has the form `Tr(ρ^{1/2}·ρ^{1/2})` with `ρ ≥ 0` (standard). Its
  pull-back is `f ↦ Σ_n ρ_{nn} f̂(log n)`, a positive measure supported in `log ℕ ⊂ [0,∞)`.
* **The zero measure is symmetric.** `μ_Z` is symmetric under `γ ↦ −γ` (functional equation and reality), and it has an
  atom at `−γ_1 < 0`, because ζ has a zero with `γ_1 = 14.13… > 0`. Uniqueness of Fourier transforms of tempered
  measures shows that no such weight satisfies the Lefschetz identity. Also, states are bounded, which Thm 1 excludes.
* **Summary.** BC positivity at `β > 1` is the positivity of the **prime measure on the frequency side**:
  `Σ_n n^{−β}|f̂(log n)|² ≥ 0`, together with its log-derivative `Σ Λ(n)n^{−β}|f̂(log n)|²` (D.116 (3.1)). Weil
  positivity needs the sign of the prime measure against the **autocorrelation on the time side**, `−Σ w_n c_F(±log n)`.
  The explicit formula is the Fourier duality exchanging the two, and BC sits on the wrong side of it. ∎

**Verdict on the two rows of R158_004 A23.**
* **II_∞ dual trace:** upgraded from "RESTATEMENT" to **excluded by a proved obstruction** (Cor 5(a)–(b)). The
  conformal factor is right. The space has no point spectrum. Inherited forms are null.
* **Bost–Connes:**
  * `β > 1`: excluded (Prop 6: one-sided spectrum, finiteness);
  * `β ≤ 1`: excluded through the core (Cor 5(b));
  * `β = ½` (D.116): neither Gibbs nor an L²-eigen carrier.

## §4 Growth type, visibility, and the Sz.-Nagy bridge

### Theorem 7 (visibility) — `PROVED` (classical spectral-radius argument)

Let `Q` be a Banach space with a strongly continuous group `U_s`, `‖U_s‖ ≤ C e^{a|s|}(1+|s|)^N`.

*(i)* Every eigenvector `U_s x = e^{(ρ−½)s}x` has `|Re ρ − ½| ≤ a`.

*(ii)* In particular, if `a = 0` (polynomially bounded), every eigenvalue is critical. If `H¹` is a Hilbert or Banach
quotient of such a carrier, and OT-2 is stated as "the spectrum of the generator on `H¹`, with algebraic multiplicities,
is the multiset of non-trivial zeros" (Leichtnam 6], (D2) of Lemma T), then OT-2 implies RH. **On every polynomially
bounded carrier, OT-2 is RH-strength.**

*(iii)* In the weighted model `H = L²(ℝ, w(t)dt)` with translations, the character `ℓ_z(f) = ∫f e^{zt}dt` is continuous
⟺ `e^{Re z·t}w^{−1/2} ∈ L²`. If `w` is polynomially bounded above and below, as it must be when `U` is polynomially
bounded, this forces `Re z = 0`. So **off-line zeros are invisible by construction**.

*Proof.*
* **(i)** `c(s) = e^{(ρ−½)s}` satisfies `|c(±s)| ≤ ‖U_{±s}‖`. So `|Re(ρ−½)||s| ≤ a|s| + O(log|s|)` for all `s`.
* **(ii)** is (i) with `a = 0`.
* **(iii) The weight.** `‖U_s‖² = ess sup_u w(u+s)/w(u)` by the change of variables. Polynomial boundedness of `U` gives
  `c(1+|t|)^{−2N} ≤ w(t)/w(0) ≤ C(1+|t|)^{2N}` (a.e., after the usual Lebesgue-point argument).
* **(iii) The dual.** `L²(w)^* = L²(w^{−1})` under the `dt` pairing, and `∫e^{2xt}(1+|t|)^{−2N}dt = ∞` for `x ≠ 0`. ∎

*Reading.* This is the mechanism behind Connes 1999 Thm 1. In the polynomially weighted `L²_δ`, the critical zeros
appear, with multiplicity `< (1+δ)/2` (verified verbatim in R158_004 row 14). The global trace formula is equivalent
to RH (R158_001 A17). A Weil-type proof needs the opposite arrangement:
* the carrier must a priori admit **every** zero of the strip (growth type `a = ½`), so that OT-2 can be proved
  zero-blind;
* the positivity must then be a theorem that forces growth `0`.

That is exactly Weil's arrangement. `H¹_ét` a priori admits any Frobenius eigenvalue. Rosati positivity, proved from
ampleness, forces `|α| = √q`.

### Theorem 8 (the Sz.-Nagy bridge: OT-5′ ⟺ a uniform-in-time bound) — `PROVED` (from `CITED` Sz.-Nagy 1947)

Let `H¹` be a Hilbertian space (a Hilbert space whose inner product is fixed only up to equivalence) with a strongly
continuous flow `φ^s`, and put `U_s = e^{−s/2}φ^s`. The following are equivalent:

*(a)* There is an inner product `⟨·,·⟩′`, equivalent to the given one, with `⟨φ^s x, φ^s y⟩′ = e^s⟨x, y⟩′`. This is
(D3) / P-HS / OT-5′ in Deninger's form with `α = 1`.

*(b)* `M := sup_{s∈ℝ} ‖U_s‖ < ∞`.

Moreover, under (b) one choice is `⟨x,y⟩′ = LIM_s ⟨U_s x, U_s y⟩`, where `LIM` is an invariant mean on `C_b(ℝ)`.
It satisfies `M^{−2}‖x‖² ≤ ‖x‖′² ≤ M²‖x‖²`. For every finite window, `sup_{|s|≤T}‖U_s‖ < ∞` holds automatically
(Banach–Steinhaus + strong continuity).

*Proof.*
* **(a)⟹(b).** `U` is unitary for `⟨·,·⟩′`, and equivalent norms give `‖U_s‖ ≤ const`.
* **(b)⟹(a).** `s ↦ ⟨U_s x, U_s y⟩` is bounded and continuous. An invariant mean on the amenable group `ℝ` exists
  (Markov–Kakutani / Day). `⟨·,·⟩′` is sesquilinear and positive. The norm bounds `M^{−1}‖x‖ ≤ ‖U_s x‖ ≤ M‖x‖` pass to
  the mean. Invariance of `LIM` gives `⟨U_t x, U_t y⟩′ = ⟨x, y⟩′`. This is Sz.-Nagy's theorem (*Acta Sci. Math.
  Szeged* 11 (1947) 152–157) for `ℝ`; Day and Dixmier (1950) for amenable groups; `CITED`, standard.
* **Finite windows.** For each `x`, `s ↦ U_s x` is continuous on the compact `[−T, T]`. ∎

**Corollary 8.1 (what OT-5′ is on a Hilbertian `H¹`; `PROVED`).** On any Hilbertian `H¹` on which the generator has
the zeros as spectrum (OT-2 in the form (D2)), OT-5′ in the P-HS form is **one** estimate:
`sup_s e^{−s/2}‖φ^s|_{H¹}‖ < ∞`.

*(i)* The positive form is then produced by an invariant mean, which is a definition that precedes the spectrum.

*(ii)* Every finite window is bounded for free. The content is the uniformity in `s`.

*(iii)* Under this bound: every zero is critical and semisimple (Thm 7 with `a = 0`, plus Jordan blocks growing
polynomially). If the spectrum is pure point, the normalised eigenvectors form a Riesz basis, because a unitary group
with pure point spectrum has an orthonormal eigenbasis, and the similarity maps it to a Riesz basis. So the bound
implies **RH ∧ simple zeros ∧ Riesz basis**, strictly more than RH. This is consistent with R158_004 Lemma U.

*Reading (`HEURISTIC`).* This is the precise sense in which G1 and G2 are the same problem in operator-algebra
language.
* "Each window is decidable, the uniformity is RH" (the diagram) has an exact counterpart for the object:
  "each window is bounded, the uniform bound is OT-5′".
* The time `s` of the flow is the support variable of the tests: `F ∈ L²(I_T)` sees `U_s` only for `|s| < T`.
* The identification of this `s`-uniformity with P47's `T`-uniformity (`prop:uniform`) is structural, not a theorem.
* `thm:margin` does not exclude such a bound. A bound `M < ∞` is qualitative, not a margin.
* O3 / `cor:void` suggest, without proving it, that any proof of the bound must use exact `Λ(n)`. For a
  `Λ + εδ_{n₀}` perturbation, zeros leave the line and the bound fails.

### Construction 9 (a zero-free Hilbertian carrier of growth type ½) — definitions `PROVED`; key properties `OPEN`

*Data.* Take:
* on the log-scale `t = log|x|` of `C_Q = ℝ_+^* × Ẑ^*`, the weight `w(t) = cosh(t)(1+t²)^{1+δ}`, `δ > 0`;
* `H_w = L²(C_Q, w dt ⊗ d^*u)`;
* `U_s` = normalised scaling, `‖U_s‖_{H_w} ≍ e^{|s|/2}` (growth type exactly `½`);
* `V = E(𝒮(A_Q)_0)`, where `E f(x) = |x|^{1/2}Σ_{q∈Q^*} f(qx)` and `𝒮(A_Q)_0 = {f : f(0) = f̂(0) = 0}` (Connes 1999
  §VII; `CITED`). `E f` decays faster than any power of `|x|^{±1}` (Connes' lemma, `CITED`), so `V ⊂ H_w` for every
  weight of exponential type;
* `H¹_w := H_w / V̄`, with the induced Hilbertian structure and flow. It is **defined without the zeros** (OT-3 ✓).

*Proved properties.*
* **(P1)** For `0 < Re ρ < 1`, the functional `ℓ_ρ(g) = ∫ g(x)|x|^{ρ−½}d^*x` (trivial character on `Ẑ^*`) is continuous
  on `H_w`. Proof: `∫ e^{2(Re ρ−½)t}w^{−1}dt < ∞`, since `2|Re ρ − ½| < 1`.
* **(P2)** `ℓ_ρ(Ef) = (Tate) ζ-factor(ρ)·(local integral of f at ρ)`. The Mellin transform of `Ef` is the Tate zeta
  integral (`CITED`, Tate/Connes). So `ℓ_ρ ⊥ V` whenever `ξ(ρ) = 0`. Conversely, if `ℓ_ρ ⊥ V`, then the Tate integrals
  `Z(f, ρ)` vanish for all `f ∈ 𝒮_0`, which forces `ξ(ρ) = 0` (choose `f` with nonvanishing local factor).
* **Consequence of (P1)–(P2).** **Every** zero in the open strip, on or off the line, gives a nonzero eigen-functional
  on `H¹_w`. The carrier is zero-blind in the sense Thm 7 requires. Visibility is not RH-strength here.

*Open properties.*
* **(O1) Spectral synthesis:** `V^⊥` is the closed span of the `ℓ_ρ` and of their derivatives at multiple zeros. This is
  the "density of `H¹_alg`" issue of R158_004 row 11. It is `OPEN`, possibly RH-adjacent.
* **(O2) Lefschetz:** `U(f)` is trace class on `H¹_w` for `f ∈ C_c^∞`, with Lidskii trace equal to `W(f)`, including
  the polar terms from the edge characters `ℓ_0, ℓ_1`, which are continuous on `H_w` (`∫e^{±t}w^{−1} < ∞`). This is the
  weighted-Hilbert analogue of Meyer's nuclear theorem. `OPEN` (ANALYSIS).
* **(O3) OT-5′ on `H¹_w`:** by Thm 8, OT-5′ is **equivalent** to `sup_s ‖U_s|_{H¹_w}‖ < ∞`, which by Cor 8.1(iii)
  implies RH ∧ SZ ∧ Riesz basis. `OPEN`, RH-strength.

*Why Construction 9 is the correct shape, and why it still fails (`PROVED` + `CITED`).*
* **The shape.** It is the only arrangement compatible with Thms 3, 4 and 7:
  * the ambient space must not be unitary (Thm 3 would null the form);
  * it must not be polynomially bounded (Thm 7 would make OT-2 RH-strength);
  * so it must have growth type `½`, which forces the positive form on `H¹` to be **new**, not inherited. Thm 8 then
  says that the new form exists iff one uniform bound holds.
* **The failure.** The bound implies simple zeros, and the construction is uniform in the number field: replace `Q` by
  `K` in Tate's thesis. The `S₃`-field falsifier (R158_004 row 12′: `ζ_K = ζ·L(sgn)·L(ρ)²` has infinitely many double
  zeros) then shows that the `K`-version of the bound is **false** whenever `H¹_{w,K}` is cyclic at those zeros. A
  cyclic `H¹` at a double zero has a Jordan block, which gives polynomial growth, hence unboundedness. Cyclicity is
  ANALYSIS in R158_004 (A20). So by T1 (P46 Thm 18.1, Rem 18.1a; C15), a proof of (O3) must use a property separating
  `ζ` from the `S₃` Dedekind zeta functions. Unconditionally, the P-HS route on this carrier can deliver at most a
  **non-field-uniform** statement. This is N-b of R158_005, now located precisely.
* **The fallback is circular.** The primitive/P-HI route needs only semidefiniteness, which tolerates Jordan blocks.
  On `H¹_w` it would be a semidefinite `Θ`-compatible hermitian form. By R158_004 Fact 4.6a.7 (corrected) such forms are
  `B_Φ` with `Φ` entire. By Prop H, the definite ones have multipliers of infinite type, and CCM's `B_{ξ′}` is Weil's
  form itself. The fallback is therefore RESTATEMENT (4.3 of R158_004).

## §6 What is genuinely new (for the programme)

1. **Thm 4 + Cor 5.** The II_∞ dual-trace carrier is excluded by a **proof**, not a restatement. Its conformal trace
  (the right factor, `α = 1`) lives on a space with no `L^p` eigenvectors. This also covers BC at `β ≤ 1` through its
  core, and it is the exact form of the phase-15-arithmetic-hodge-index M8 remark "continuous spectrum, infinite invariant measure".
2. **Thm 3 (null inheritance).** Any positive form inherited from a unitary ambient space without point spectrum
  vanishes identically on every zero-eigenclass, on or off the line. This is the one-line reason for Connes'
  absorption picture. It turns N-a into a proved exclusion (N-a′) for `L²(C_Q)`, the II_∞ core and scaled-measure
  spaces.
3. **Thm 7 (visibility) + Thm 8 (Sz.-Nagy bridge).**
  * A carrier must have growth type `½` for OT-2 not to be RH-strength.
  * On a Hilbertian `H¹`, OT-5′ is **equivalent** to a single uniform-in-time bound, and the positive form is an
  invariant mean.
  * This is the operator-algebra form of the "nexus": finite windows are bounded for free, and the uniformity
  is the whole content.
  * The corpus named "the unitarizability problem" (D.115) but never stated this criterion.
4. **Construction 9.** A zero-free, Hilbertian carrier of growth type `½` on which every strip zero is visible, and on
  which OT-5′ is exactly that bound. Its P-HS form implies RH ∧ SZ ∧ Riesz basis, and its field-uniform version is
  false modulo cyclicity. So the construction also shows that **the unitary (P-HS) shape is the wrong target** for a
  field-uniform proof. Over `ℚ`, the target should be a semidefinite (P-HI) form, and on this carrier that is
  RESTATEMENT (§4).
5. **Thm 1.** The positive functional must be an infinite weight of logarithmic density (no state). **Thm 2** is the
  GNS form of Lemma T: every carrier that passes OT-2 **is** a Hilbert–Pólya model.

Items 2, 4 and 5 are elementary. Their mathematical content is classical or folklore, and no priority is claimed for
it. The novelty is their placement in the object test.

## §7 The weakest step

* **Prop 6 and Cor 5.** These rest on `CITED` standard facts not re-read this phase:
  * the form of the core trace (`τ∘θ_s = e^{−s}τ`, `τ = ω̃(h^{−1}·)` with Lebesgue-class restriction to `{h}''`);
  * the type III_1 property of BC at `β ≤ 1`;
  * the Gibbs form of BC KMS states at `β > 1`.

  Thms 3, 4 and 7 themselves are fully proved above.
* **Construction 9 (the most fragile).**
  * (P2)'s converse, "`ℓ_ρ ⊥ V ⟹ ξ(ρ) = 0`", needs the choice of `f` with nonvanishing local factors at `ρ`. That is
  standard in Tate's thesis but not written out here.
  * (O1) and (O2) are open.
  * The claim that a cyclic `H¹_{w,K}` has Jordan blocks at the double zeros of an `S₃` field inherits R158_004's
  ANALYSIS flag (cyclicity).
* **Overall.** The status of Construction 9 is "correct shape, OT-5′ reduced to a uniform bound", not "object
  constructed".
* **A reader should check:**
  * that Thm 8's equivalence is used only on a Hilbertian `H¹` with its topology **fixed** before the spectrum;
  * whether the weight `w` can be chosen so that (O2) holds at the edge characters `ℓ_0, ℓ_1`, where `w` is exactly
  critical.
