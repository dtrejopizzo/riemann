## §2 The class my angle supplies: Hodge-type polarizations

The brief asks for "a Hodge-type polarization" on an analytic/condensed carrier for `Spec Z ∪ {∞}`. In the
Scholze/Fargues–Fontaine school the archimedean analogue of the Fargues–Fontaine curve is Simpson's **twistor line**
`P¹_tw` (`CITED`: Fargues–Fontaine, *Courbes et fibrés vectoriels en théorie de Hodge p-adique*, Astérisque 406, intro;
Simpson, *Mixed twistor structures*, alg-geom/9705006; Fargues, Bourbaki exposé 1150 (2018), §1). Its dictionary,
`CITED` (Simpson): `C^×`-equivariant vector bundles on `P¹_tw` with the antipodal real structure `σ(z) = −1/z̄`
↔ real Hodge structures; arbitrary bundles ↔ (mixed) twistor structures; a bundle is **pure of weight `n`** iff it is
isomorphic to `O(n)^{⊕r}`; a **polarization** is a `σ`-hermitian pairing `E ⊗ σ^*Ē → O(n)` whose induced form on the
fibre at a point is positive definite, which for Hodge structures is the Hodge–Riemann form `Q(Cx, ȳ)`, `C` the Weil
operator. This is a positivity **defined before and without any spectrum**, derived from the geometry of `P¹` and the
real structure: it is exactly what OT-5′ asks for. The question is what flow it is compatible with, and what its
Lefschetz formula says.

**Definition 2.1 (Hodge-type carrier, class `𝓗`).** A carrier `(H, φ^t, Q)` belongs to `𝓗` if one of the following
holds:

* **(𝓗-eq) Equivariant / algebraic.** `H` is a complex vector space with a real structure, carrying an algebraic
  representation of the Deligne torus `S = Res_{C/R} G_m` (equivalently, `H = ⊕_{(p,q) ∈ Z²} H^{p,q}` with
  `H^{p,q}` bar `= H^{q,p}`, `z ∈ C^× = S(R)` acting on `H^{p,q}` by `z^{−p} z̄^{−q}`); each weight space
  `H_n := ⊕_{p+q=n} H^{p,q}` is finite-dimensional or, more generally, `H` is a direct sum (or filtered colimit) of
  finite-dimensional sub-Hodge structures. The **flow** is the restriction of the torus action to `R_{>0} ⊂ C^×`:
  `φ^t := ρ(e^{t})`, so `φ^t|_{H_n} = e^{−nt}`, and `Θ|_{H_n} = −n`. `Q` is a polarization (Hodge–Riemann form) on
  each finite-dimensional piece.
* **(𝓗-tw) Twistor.** `H = ⊕_i Γ-data of finite-rank holomorphic bundles E_i on P¹_tw with σ-structure`, each
  `E_i ≅ ⊕_j O(n_{ij})` (Grothendieck–Birkhoff, `CITED` std.), with the flow acting on the `O(n)`-isotypic part by
  `e^{−nt}` (the natural `C^×`-action on `O(n)` restricted to `R_{>0}`, or any lift of the weight grading), and `Q` a
  polarization in Simpson's sense.

The sign convention of `Θ` is immaterial below; what matters is `Θ ∈ Z`. The class `𝓗` is the class the FF/twistor
school actually produces: `p`-adic Hodge theory produces filtered `φ`-modules whose archimedean shadow is a Hodge
structure; Simpson's non-abelian Hodge theory produces twistor structures; Deninger–Singhof produce (on compact
Kähler–Riemann foliations) *real polarizable Hodge structures* in the same sense (Doc 129 §1.1, verbatim source).

**Construction T (the candidate carrier of this round, for the record).** For `Spec Z ∪ {∞}` take
`H := ⊕_{n ∈ Z} H_n` with `H_n` the "weight-`n` part" of any Hodge-type cohomology of `Spec Z̄` (Weil–Arakelov,
Deninger's `H^i_{dyn}` fibres at integers, motivic cohomology with real coefficients, or Beilinson's absolute Hodge
cohomology of `Spec Q`), polarised by Hodge–Riemann, with the flow `e^{−nt}` on `H_n`. It is a genuine object of
`𝓗-eq`, its positivity is prior to any spectrum and compatible with the flow: `Q(φ^t x, φ^t y) = e^{−2nt} Q(x,y)` on
`H_n`, the (D3)-type conformal law with factor `e^{−2nt}`. **Theorem W says what its Lefschetz formula is, and it is
not the explicit formula.**

## §3 Theorem W: the integer-weight wall

### 3.1 Statement

**Theorem W (`PROVED`).** Let `(H, φ^t, Q) ∈ 𝓗`.
(i) The `Θ`-spectrum (point spectrum of the generator of `φ^t`) is contained in `Z`. In particular no `ρ = ½ + iγ`
with `γ ∈ R` is an eigenvalue: the normalised generator `Θ − ½` has spectrum in `Z − ½ ⊂ R`, disjoint from `iR`.
(ii) Suppose the Lefschetz trace `L(h) := Tr ∫ h(t) U_t dt` (with `U_t = e^{−t/2}φ^t`) exists, as a convergent sum of
eigenvalue contributions `Σ_n m_n ∫ h(t) e^{(−n−½)t} dt` with `m_n := dim H_n ∈ Z_{≥0} ∪ {∞}`, for every
`h ∈ C_c^∞(R)`. Then `m_n < ∞` for all `n`, `m_n = 0` for all but finitely many `n`, and `L` is given by integration
against the **smooth function** `ℓ(t) = Σ_n m_n e^{(−n−½)t}`.
(iii) Let `D ∈ 𝒟′(R)` be the spectral side of the explicit formula in Deninger's normalisation,
`D(h) = Σ_ρ ĥ((ρ−½)/i)` summed over the nontrivial zeros with multiplicity (the distribution whose value on `h` is the
zero side of P47 `eq:W` / `thm:Z(i)`, polar pair included as the two terms `ρ ∈ {0,1}`). Then `D ≠ L` for every
`(H, φ^t, Q) ∈ 𝓗` satisfying the hypothesis of (ii). Hence **OT-2 fails on the class `𝓗`**, unconditionally.

### 3.2 Proof

*(i), case 𝓗-eq.* Every algebraic character of `S` over `C` is `z ↦ z^{−p} z̄^{−q}` with `(p,q) ∈ Z²`
(`Hom(G_m, G_m) = Z`, `CITED` std.; `S_C ≅ G_m × G_m`). An algebraic representation is a direct sum of weight spaces
for these characters; the flow `e^t ∈ R_{>0}` acts on `H^{p,q}` by `e^{−(p+q)t}`. Since `H` is a direct sum (or
filtered colimit) of finite-dimensional sub-representations, every eigenvector of the flow lies in a finite-dimensional
invariant subspace, on which the generator is diagonalisable with eigenvalues `−(p+q) ∈ Z`. No other eigenvalues exist:
if `φ^t v = e^{λt} v` for all `t` with `v ≠ 0`, decompose `v = Σ v_n` into weight components; then
`Σ (e^{−nt} − e^{λt}) v_n = 0` for all `t`, so `e^{−nt} = e^{λt}` for each `n` with `v_n ≠ 0`, i.e. `λ = −n ∈ Z`.

*(i), case 𝓗-tw.* By Grothendieck–Birkhoff (`CITED` std.: every finite-rank holomorphic vector bundle on `P¹` is
isomorphic to `⊕ O(n_j)`, uniquely up to order), each `E_i` is graded by integers `n_{ij}`, and the flow acts on the
`O(n)`-isotypic part by `e^{−nt}`. The remainder of the argument is identical.

The second sentence of (i): `−n − ½ ∈ Z − ½ ⊂ R ∖ {0}`, while `(ρ − ½) = iγ ∈ iR`. The two sets meet only if
`iγ = −n − ½`, impossible for real `γ` since the right side is real and nonzero.

*(ii).* Fix `R > 0` and choose `h ∈ C_c^∞(R)`, `h ≥ 0`, `h ≠ 0`, supported in `[R, R+1]`. Every term
`m_n ∫ h(t) e^{(−n−½)t} dt` is `≥ 0`, so the convergent sum is absolutely convergent and each term is finite; hence
`m_n < ∞` for every `n` with `∫ h e^{(−n−½)t} > 0`, i.e. for every `n`. For `n ≤ −1`, `∫ h(t) e^{(−n−½)t} dt ≥
e^{(|n|−½)R} ‖h‖_1`, so `Σ_{n ≤ −1} m_n e^{(|n|−½)R} < ∞` for every `R`; as `m_n ∈ Z_{≥0}`, this forces `m_n = 0`
for all but finitely many `n ≤ −1` (if infinitely many `m_n ≥ 1`, the sum diverges for `R = 1`). Symmetrically, an
`h ≥ 0` supported in `[−R−1, −R]` gives `m_n = 0` for all but finitely many `n ≥ 0`. So `ℓ(t) = Σ_n m_n e^{(−n−½)t}`
is a finite sum of real exponentials, a smooth function, and `L(h) = ∫ h ℓ` for every `h ∈ C_c^∞(R)` by linearity.

*(iii).* By the explicit formula in the form of record (P47 `eq:W`, `thm:Z(i)`, both `PROVED`/classical; Weil 1952,
`CITED`), for every `h ∈ C_c^∞(R)`
`D(h) = ĥ(archimedean term m(τ)) + Σ_{n ≥ 2} (Λ(n)/√n) (h(log n) + h(−log n))` (up to the record's normalisation of
`m` and of the polar pair), where the archimedean term is integration against a locally integrable function `m(τ)` in
the dual variable, i.e. against a **continuous function of `t`** away from `t = 0` (`CITED`: the archimedean
distribution `−(Γ′/Γ)`-type kernel is `C^∞` on `R ∖ {0}`; P47 §1). Hence the singular support of `D` contains
`{±log n : Λ(n) ≠ 0}`: for `t_0 = log p^k`, `D − (Λ(p^k)/p^{k/2})δ_{t_0}` is a continuous function on a neighbourhood
of `t_0`, so `D` is not a continuous function there. A finite sum of real exponentials is `C^∞` on `R`. Two
distributions on `C_c^∞(R)`, one of which is `C^∞` near `t_0 = log 2` and the other not, differ. So `D ≠ L`. ∎

### 3.3 Corollaries and scope

**Corollary W1 (`PROVED`).** No carrier in `𝓗` satisfies OT-2, whether or not RH holds, and whether or not its
`H¹` is infinite-dimensional. If it satisfies OT-5′ (it does: Hodge–Riemann is prior and derived) then OT-5′ is
satisfied **vacuously**: the object has no `Θ`-eigenvalue off `Z`, so Lemma T(⇒) (R158_001 §3.1) applies to an empty
set of zeros. This is the C5 pattern (Deninger math/0204110 Cor 3.5 Rem 2: derived positivity forces `α = 0`).

**Corollary W2 (`PROVED`; supersedes G1_B3_R2 Prop WA).** Every object of `𝓗` (in particular Weil–Arakelov
cohomology, Deninger's integer fibres `H^i_{dyn}(Θ − n)`, motivic/absolute Hodge cohomology of `Spec Q` with their
Hodge polarizations) carries `ζ` only through `Θ ∈ Z`, i.e. through special values, never through zeros. The
conditional hypotheses of Prop WA (Flach–Morin Conj. 2.3, 4.1) are not needed: the conclusion follows from the
grading alone.

**Corollary W3 (the falsifier check; `PROVED`).** For `Ξ_a` (P46 Prop 12.4B), for `ζ_K`, for `Ξ_Q` and for every
modified comb (`cor:void`), the same argument gives `D_* ≠ L`: Theorem W is blind to the location of the zeros and to
the arithmetic of the comb. It excludes the class `𝓗`, not a truth about `ζ`. (OT-4 is therefore not applicable: no
object exists to be rerun.)

**Proposition W4 (scope; `PROVED` parts and one `OPEN`).**
(a) The hypothesis "direct sum / filtered colimit of finite-dimensional sub-Hodge structures" in `𝓗-eq` is
automatic for algebraic representations of `S` on any vector space (an algebraic representation is by definition
locally finite; `CITED` std.). So `𝓗-eq` is the **whole** class of algebraic `S`-representations, of any dimension.
(b) For `𝓗-tw` the finite-rank hypothesis on each `E_i` is used through Grothendieck–Birkhoff. Whether an
infinite-rank holomorphic Hilbert bundle on `P¹` can carry a `σ`-polarization with a flow whose weights are
**not** integers is **`OPEN`** here and **not claimed** either way. Any such object would in any case need a flow, and the
flow on a twistor structure comes from the `C^×`-action on `P¹`, which fixes `0` and `∞` and has integer weights on
the fibres there (`T_0 P¹`, `T_∞ P¹` carry weights `±1`); so the natural flow is algebraic and (a) applies to the
fibre representations. The escape would have to be a flow **not** induced from `P¹`.
(c) Theorem W(ii) assumes only that the trace exists termwise for nonnegative `h`; conditional-convergence devices
(as in Deninger's `H¹`, where the trace is a distribution and the sum over zeros converges only after pairing) do not
help, because with real exponents and nonnegative test functions all terms have one sign.

### 3.4 Relation to phase-15-arithmetic-hodge-index M5/M8 and to C5

M5 (phase 15) proved, under **LI** (linear independence of the ordinates over `Q`), that no lowest-weight
integer-graded `sl₂` acts on the zero spectrum, and read this as "arithmetic spectrum and HR-carrying grading are
LI-incompatible" (`HEURISTIC` as a general law; it concerned one `sl₂` on `L²(R, dr)`). M8 F1 refuted a claimed
integration to `SU(2)` because Connes' scaling flow has continuous spectrum and infinite invariant measure.

Theorem W is different in three respects. (1) **No LI**: the wall is that characters of an algebraic torus are
integers, and that `iγ ∉ Z − ½` for real `γ ≠ 0`; the arithmetic independence of the ordinates plays no role.
(2) **Twistor structures included**: Simpson's non-equivariant class has integer weights through Grothendieck–Birkhoff
rather than through a torus action; M5 did not treat it. (3) **The prime side kills, not the zero side**: part (iii)
uses only that `eq:W` has atoms at `log p^k`. This is the premise (3) in negative form: the prime side of the
explicit formula is a *singular* distribution, so a Lefschetz formula whose spectral side is a smooth function of `t`
cannot be the explicit formula. Any carrier hoping to pass OT-2 must have a flow whose "spectral side" is itself
singular at every `log p^k`, i.e. must carry the closed orbits of length `log p` as genuinely singular data
(Deninger's axiom 2], Leichtnam §4.1), which a torus-graded object never does.

Consistency with C5 (Deninger–Singhof): on a compact Kähler–Riemann foliation the leafwise Hodge structure is
polarizable and `α = 0` (no scaling), which is the case `Θ ∈ Z` with a trivial flow on `H¹`. Theorem W is the
statement that **allowing an algebraic scaling** (weights `n ≠ 0`) does not change the conclusion: one gets
`Θ ∈ Z`, still no zeros. The only known derived-positivity mechanism with non-integer `Θ` is none: Deninger's
"`(φ^t)^*g = e^t g` might be too strong" (Leichtnam, Comment 6, `CITED` via R158_001) is exactly the demand for a
non-algebraic conformal flow with a *derived* positivity, which is OT-5′ itself.

## §4 Theorem F: the Fargues–Fontaine product class falls under B2's Corollary H

### 4.1 The local carriers (recalled from G1_B3_R1, `PROVED` there)

For each prime `p`, the local FF-type carrier at the archimedean place is the complex Tate curve `E_p = C^×/p^Z`
(Connes–Consani 2606.06604 attach these to `Spec Z` via the `F₁`-curve; `CITED`), with Frobenius `φ_p` the
multiplication by `p` on `C^×`, and the archimedean analogue `B_∞^{φ=p^d}` of the FF period space. R1 Props L1–L3:
`B_∞^{φ=p^d}` is a line; the smooth `φ_p`-eigenspaces on functions on `C^×` realise exactly the Euler factor
`1 − p^{d−s}`; the Riemann form on `H¹(E_p)` is a polarization defined before any spectrum on which the scaling flow
acts trivially (Lefschetz number `0`). In operator terms: on `L²(C^×, d^×z)` the normalised Frobenius
`T_p := p^{−1/2}(φ_p)_*` is **unitary** (the Haar measure `d^×z` is scale-invariant), and its eigenvectors with
eigenvalue `p^{iγ}` are the homogeneous functions `|z|^{iγ}` times a character of the compact part; the Euler factor
is the spectral trace of `T_p` on the smooth part (Prop L2).

### 4.2 The global product and its Frobenius (`PROVED`)

The only way the FF school has to pass from the local carriers to a global one for `Spec Z` is the restricted tensor
product (adelic / Euler-product principle, as in `π_f = ⊗′_p π_p` for automorphic representations, and as in
Connes–Consani's `F₁`-curve gluing of the `E_p`): `H := ⊗′_p (H_p, ξ_p)` with `H_p = L²(C^×)` or `H¹(E_p)` or
`B_∞^{φ=p^d}`, a unit vector `ξ_p` (the spherical/invariant vector), and `u(p) := T_p` acting on the `p`-th factor.

**Lemma 4.1 (`PROVED`).** `(H, (u(p))_p)` is an **Euler-factorizable family of commuting unitary (hence normal)
operators** in the sense of G1_B2_R2 §3 (`P_0 = ∅`, `|B_j| = 1`) and of G1_B2_R3 Theorem E\* (normal family with the
eventually separating local shape `γ ↦ (p^{iγ})_p`). The Hilbert norm of `H` is the product norm, i.e. a positive form
defined before the spectrum (OT-5′-type input), and the local shape of a putative zero-eigenvector is `p^{iγ}` by
Prop L2.
*Proof.* Each `T_p` is unitary on its factor and acts as the identity on the other factors; the von Neumann incomplete
tensor product through `⊗ ξ_p` is separable and the `u(p)` commute. The local shape is Prop L2. ∎

**Theorem F (`PROVED` given G1_B2_R3 Corollary H and Theorem E\*, which are round-3 statements not yet audited by the
panel; Hardy's theorem `CITED`).** No carrier of the FF product class — Hilbert norm, Frobenius the Euler-factorizable
unitary family of Lemma 4.1, each critical zero realised as an eigenvector of the family with local shape `p^{iγ}` — can
exist: by B2's Theorem E (as corrected: tail set `Γ_tail(ξ)`, `|Γ_tail| ≤ 1` by Lemma P) the suspended flow has at most
**one** on-line eigenvalue, while Hardy gives infinitely many critical zeros. Two critical zeros already contradict the
hypothesis. **The exclusion is unconditional on RH.**
*Proof.* Lemma 4.1 places the family in the hypotheses of B2_R3 Cor H (a)–(c). Its conclusion is the statement. ∎

**Remark 4.2 (what Theorem F adds to B2; `PROVED` parts).** B2's wall is stated for abstract factorizable families.
Theorem F identifies the FF/Tate-curve product as an instance and supplies the local shape (Prop L2), so that the
FF product class need not be resubmitted. In particular the cheapest FF-style object, "`H¹` of the `F₁`-curve of
Connes–Consani 2606.06604 as the product of the `H¹(E_p)`", has `T_p = id` on `H¹(E_p)` (Prop L3), so the suspended
spectrum is `{0}` (only `Θ = 0`, the `H⁰`-type point) — it is the `P_0 = ∅` degenerate case where `Γ_tail = {0}`.

**Corollary F1 (`PROVED` given Theorem F).** Entanglement across primes (N-e₁ of B2, renamed per the panel) is
**necessary** for any FF-derived global carrier. The FF school's gluing principle (restricted product of local
objects) violates it by construction. A non-factorizable FF-type global object would need a global period ring for
`Spec Z` that is not a product of local ones; the condensed `Spec Z ∪ {∞}` gives a *space*, not a Frobenius (§5).

### 4.3 Proposition H: the Habiro ring with its Adams operations (Scholze school; new to the corpus)

`CITED`: Habiro, *Cyclotomic completions of polynomial rings* (Publ. RIMS 40, 2004) defines
`Ĥ := lim_n Z[q]/((q;q)_n)`, the Habiro ring, with ring endomorphisms `ψ_p : q ↦ q^p` (Frobenius lifts / Adams
operations) commuting for all `p`; Garoufalidis–Scholze–Wheeler–Zagier (2024–25, arXiv 2412.04241, abstract) use it
as the ring of "functions on `Spec Z` near all roots of unity", with `Ĥ ⊗ Z_p`-completions matching `Z_p`-points.
In the phase-159 dictionary this is the Scholze-school candidate for an "`F₁`-like Frobenius on `Spec Z ∪ {∞}`"
(`q → 1` playing the archimedean place, `HEURISTIC` dictionary).

**Proposition H (`PROVED` on the monomial model `Z[q] ⊂ ℓ²(Z_{≥0})`; the extension to `Ĥ` is `OPEN`).** On
`ℓ²(Z_{≥0})` with basis `e_k := q^k`, the operators `Ψ_p e_k := e_{pk}` (the Hilbert-space form of `ψ_p`) are
commuting **isometries** with `Ψ_p Ψ_q = Ψ_{pq}`, forming an Euler-factorizable multiplicative semigroup
`(Ψ_n)_{n ≥ 1}`. Their joint unitary part is `H_u = C e_0` (the constant `1`), on which every `Ψ_n` acts trivially. Hence
the only on-line "eigenvalue" is the trivial character (`Θ = 0`-type point), and no critical zero is realised; the
normalised family `n^{−1/2}Ψ_n` has empty unimodular point spectrum on `e_0^⊥`. The Habiro Frobenius is a
Bost–Connes-type shift family, exactly as B2_R3 §5.1 found for Bost–Connes and B7_R3 Prop BL for Borger's `Z[Q/Z]`.
*Proof.* `Ψ_p` maps the orthonormal basis injectively into itself, so it is an isometry; `Ψ_pΨ_q e_k = e_{pqk}`. The
unitary part of a family of commuting isometries is `∩_{n} ran(Ψ_n^{m})` over all `n, m` (Wold; `CITED` std.):
`ran Ψ_p^m = span{e_k : p^m | k}`, and `∩_{p,m} span{e_k : p^m | k} = C e_0`. On `e_0^⊥` each `Ψ_p` is a pure shift, so
`n^{−1/2}Ψ_n` is a strict contraction there with no unimodular eigenvalue. ∎

The extension from `Z[q]` to `Ĥ` (where the monomials are not a basis) changes the Hilbert structure and is not
claimed; it is noted as `OPEN` and, by B2_R3 Theorem I (contractions: on-axis eigenvectors lie in the unitary part),
unlikely to escape, but that is not a proof. **Even granting an escape, the class is Euler-factorizable, so Theorem F
applies.**

## §5 Proposition D: the two sources of derived positivity in the school, and the one escape

**Proposition D (`HEURISTIC` as a completeness claim; each clause `PROVED` where labelled).** In the
Scholze / Fargues–Fontaine / p-adic Hodge school, positivity that is *derived* (not postulated) comes from exactly two
mechanisms:

| source | what it is | why it cannot carry ζ's zeros on a flow carrier | label |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Compactness** | invariant lattices / Galois representations / weak admissibility; slopes as `p`-adic valuations; Iwasawa lattices | the acting group must be compact; every continuous `R → Gal(Q̄/Q)` is trivial (G1_B4_R1 Prop 4.3(ii)); forcing the flow through a compact group gives boundedness + almost periodicity, RH-strength on an `H¹` realising the zeros | R2 Thm I (`PROVED` given `CITED`) |
| **Algebraic weights** | Hodge–Riemann on Hodge / twistor structures (the twistor `P¹` = archimedean FF curve) | `Θ ∈ Z`; smooth Lefschetz trace; the prime-side atoms `δ_{log p^k}` are invisible | R3 Thm W (`PROVED`) |

The union is claimed to exhaust the school's derived positivity: FF slope theory (Harder–Narasimhan on `X_FF`) is
valuation-positivity, a compactness statement (`H⁰(O) = Q_p`, slope-0 bundles ↔ Galois representations,
Fargues–Fontaine Thm, `CITED`); the twistor/Hodge side is algebraic weights. I know no third derived positivity in
this literature; the claim is `HEURISTIC`.

**The escape (`OPEN`, RH-strength as far as the record knows).** A **continuous, non-algebraic `R`-action** with a
Hodge–Riemann-type positivity derived without an algebraic grading. On record this is exactly (a) Deninger–Singhof
on compact Kähler–Riemann foliations (C5: compactness forces `α = 0` and no fixed points, so it is the `Θ ∈ Z`, trivial
flow, corner of Theorem W), and (b) any carrier in the Def B.1 class of G1_B6_R2, where the resolution law N-e₂ shows
the norm must build the Riemann–von Mangoldt density into its topology before the spectrum. Note the tension with
Theorem W(iii): the escape must have a spectral side singular at every `log p^k`, so it cannot be a *smooth* family of
weights either; it must be a genuine `R`-action with **continuous or dense spectrum on the flow direction**, which is
Connes' cokernel picture (A17 of record: its global trace formula is RH-equivalent) or Deninger's unconstructed `H¹`.

**On the condensed `Spec Z ∪ {∞}` (`OPEN`, stated as an absence, not a theorem).** Clausen–Scholze's analytic-stack
compactification of `Spec Z` (liquid real point glued to the `Spec Z_p`; `CITED` at the level of the lecture notes
*Condensed Mathematics and Complex Geometry*, 2022, and *Analytic Stacks*, 2023–24) provides a *space* with a
well-defined archimedean point and a six-functor formalism. I know of **no** Frobenius or flow defined on it at the
archimedean point in the published material, and no positivity. Its arithmetic content is again at integer twists
(special values through `RΓ(Spec Z ∪ {∞}, Z(n))`-type objects), i.e. inside Cor W2 if a Hodge polarization is added.
Nothing further is claimed.

## §6 Remark T: the twistor real form and the archimedean pair (`HEURISTIC` dictionary)

`P¹_tw` carries the antipodal real structure `σ(z) = −1/z̄`, which has **no real points** (`CITED` std.: the real
form is the conic `x² + y² + z² = 0`). The `C^×`-action `z ↦ λz` fixes `0` and `∞`, and `σ` swaps them. In Deninger's
dictionary (Leichtnam axioms 7]–8], R158_001 table) an archimedean place is a fixed point of the flow, and the factor
`−2` at `t > 0` versus the `t < 0` riddle is resolved formally by the covering `S_{Q[i]} → S_Q` with a mild singularity
at `x_∞` (R158_003 A6). The twistor picture reproduces this shape: the real place of `Q` is not a real point of the
archimedean FF curve but a **conjugate pair** `{0, ∞}` exchanged by the real structure, i.e. a complex place of `Q[i]`
folded by `σ`. This supports the reading that the archimedean term `m(τ)` of `eq:W` is the trace of a fixed-point pair
with an orbifold quotient, consistent with OT-12 as amended. No proof weight; it changes no label.

## §8 What is genuinely new, what is not, and the weakest step

**New (to the corpus and, in form, to the literature as far as I know).**
* Theorem W as an **LI-free** integer-weight wall covering algebraic `S`-representations of any dimension and
  Simpson's twistor structures; its part (iii), which kills through the **singular support of the prime side**
  (`δ_{log p^k}`) rather than through the zeros. The argument is elementary; the novelty is the identification of the
  class `𝓗` as the whole Hodge-type toolkit of the FF/twistor school and the unconditional OT-2 failure on it.
* Cor W2: Weil–Arakelov / integer-twist cohomologies are zero-blind unconditionally (R2's Prop WA needed Flach–Morin
  conjectures).
* Theorem F: the FF/Tate-curve product is an instance of B2's Cor H, with the local shape supplied by R1 Prop L2; the
  `F₁`-curve gluing of Connes–Consani 2606.06604 is the degenerate `Γ_tail = {0}` case.
* Prop H: the Habiro ring Frobenius (Scholze school) is a Bost–Connes-type shift family with trivial unitary part.

**Not new / classical.** Grothendieck–Birkhoff; characters of `G_m`; Simpson's twistor dictionary; Wold decomposition;
the explicit formula's singular support (Weil 1952). Phase-15 M5/M8 anticipated the integer-weight dichotomy under LI
for one `sl₂`; §3.4 states the difference.

**What the angle has produced over three rounds.** A closed negative map of the Scholze/FF/p-adic Hodge school for
G1: (R1) tempered/normed subquotient class → OT-5′ is Lemma T in tempered form; local FF carriers = Euler factors;
(R2) compactness positivity cannot act on the flow; Weil–Arakelov zero-blind (conditional, now unconditional);
completeness `(C_δ)` is an RH-equivalent; (R3) algebraic-weight positivity is zero-blind and OT-2-incompatible with
the prime side; the FF product class is unconditionally excluded via B2's wall. **Recommendation:** close the
FF / condensed / prismatic / twistor angle in the G1 map with Theorems I, W and F as its record; reopen only with a
carrier whose flow is a non-algebraic `R`-action, entangled across primes, with a derived positivity — the
Deninger residual, RH-strength by Lemma T on record.

**Weakest step.** Proposition D's completeness claim ("compactness and algebraic weights exhaust the school's derived
positivity") is `HEURISTIC`; a third mechanism would reopen the angle. Within the proved statements, the weakest is
Prop W4(b): Theorem W for twistor structures uses finite rank in each summand (Grothendieck–Birkhoff), and I have
**not** proved that an infinite-rank holomorphic Hilbert bundle on `P¹_tw` with a `σ`-polarization and a flow *not*
induced from the `C^×`-action on `P¹` is impossible; it is marked `OPEN`. Theorem F depends on G1_B2_R3 Cor H, which is
a round-3 result not yet checked by the panel; if Cor H falls, Theorem F reverts to the panel-confirmed round-2 form
(Theorem E with `Γ_tail`: zeros can enter only by installation, Prop R), which still fails OT-3/OT-4 but is no longer
an unconditional exclusion.
