## 1. G2_B2_R2 — the sieve flow `Ψ`

### Literature and corpus
- **The recursion.** `Λ(n) = log n − Σ_{d|n,1<d<n}Λ(d)` is Chebyshev's identity `Λ⋆1 = log` (Möbius-inverted, `Λ = μ⋆log`)
  written as a triangular linear system. That `Λ` is its unique solution with `a(1) = 0` is unique factorisation; it is the
  first thing any text proves about `Λ` (Hardy–Wright Thm 296 in the form `Σ_{d|n}Λ(d) = log n`). The "short-annulus
  locality" (Ψ1) is the trivial observation that a proper divisor is `≤ n/2`. Nothing here is new as mathematics; the
  memo says so ("Not new" list, §7).
- **The propagated defect** `b = δ_{n₀}⋆μ`, Dirichlet series `εn₀^{−s}/ζ(s)` (Ψ5(1)) is Möbius inversion, classical.
  The residue expansion Ψ5(2) is the standard Perron/Landau contour for `1/ζ` (Titchmarsh Ch. XIV, the `M(x)` explicit
  formula under simple zeros, Thm 14.27 in the 2nd ed.; Ingham Ch. IV). Its application to the *window form* is new to the
  corpus, and I accept it as a record lemma; it is not a mechanism and does not claim to be.
- **Row 30 of R157_002** ("identity-level use of `Λ = μ⋆log` has no formulation; EXCL per window, EQUIV globally"). The
  memo formulates it and lands exactly where row 30 said: Prop Ψ4 shows the `ℕ`-specific content of `Ψ` is `ζ(½+it)` plus
  the pole, so an exact `Ψ`-invariant is a functional of `ζ` on the critical line. That is E3. The memo's own label is
  EQUIVALENT. I concur.
- **DMV / Epstein instances** (Ψ3): the generic recursion `−L′ = L·(−L′/L)` for any normalised Dirichlet series is textbook
  (it is how `a_L` is defined). F4 placement is correct and honest.

### Step check (audit)
- **Ψ1, Ψ2.** Correct (trivial).
- **"Not subject to `cor:void`" (after Ψ2).** Formally true, but vacuous: the class of `Ψ`-orbits is `{Λ}`, and `cor:void`
  is by construction a statement about classes with at least two members. Any argument whose *only* prime input is "`a = Λ`"
  escapes `cor:void`; the question is what it then uses about `Λ`, and Ψ4 answers: `ζ(½+it)`. So the claim "avoids W15 and
  `cor:void` in substance" is a relabelling of "uses `ζ` itself", which is the situation of every EQUIV row. This is why the
  verdict is *restatement* rather than *excluded*.
- **Ψ4 (Mellin inversion).** Correct: `Φ` decays faster than any power in vertical strips; `ζ(½+s)` polynomial growth; residue
  at `s = ½` is `Φ(½)`. Standard.
- **Ψ5(2), contour.** The inverse-Laplace step `c_F(log n) = (2πi)^{−1}∫Φ_F(s)n^{−s}ds` is correct; `Φ_F(s) = ĉ_F(−is)`, so
  `Φ_F(iγ) = ĉ_F(γ) = |F̂(γ)|²` for real `F`. Residues and the absence of poles at `s = ½` (`1/ζ(1) = 0`) and `s = −½`
  (`ζ(0) = −½`) are right. The vertical-line bound via the functional equation is right (the `Γ` lower bound
  `|Γ(x+iy)| ≥ Γ(x)e^{−π|y|/2}` for `x ≥ 1` is classical). The `k`-series convergence: `|Φ_F(−2k−½)| ≤ ‖c_F‖₁e^{(2k+½)2T}`
  against `|ζ′(−2k)| ≍ (2k)!/(2π)^{2k}`: absolutely convergent. Correct.
- **The CITED horizontal bound (memo's weakest step).** The *statement* "there is `T′ ∈ [T,T+1]` with
  `1/ζ(σ+iT′) = O(T^A)` uniformly for `−1 ≤ σ ≤ 2`" is classical and true. The mechanism is Titchmarsh Thm 9.6(B)
  (`log ζ(s) = Σ_{|t−γ|≤1}log(s−ρ) + O(log t)`) plus an *average* over `t ∈ [T,T+1]` of `Σ_{|t−γ|≤1}log(1/|t−γ|)`, which is
  `O(log T)` because `∫_0^1 log(1/x)dx < ∞` and there are `O(log T)` zeros; a pointwise "min distance `≥ c/log T`" argument
  would only give `T^{A log log T}`, which would **not** suffice against a merely superpolynomial decay of `Φ_F` for general
  `C_c^∞` tests. So the citation must be to the averaged version, which is what Titchmarsh §9.7 does. I could not open the
  primary text (no open copy of the 2nd edition; Internet Archive has only the 1930 tract), so the theorem *number* stays
  "to re-check" as the memo says; the *statement* is fine and the proof of Ψ5(2) is sound with it. Ψ5(1), Ψ6, §2 do not
  depend on it, as claimed.
- **Ψ6.** `W^{(a)}(F_*) = λ^W_T − 2εM(F_*)` is Ψ5(1) plus finiteness of the comb in the window. Correct. The non-degeneracy
  `M(F_*) ≠ 0` is the OPEN hypothesis of `rem:resweights`, carried over honestly.
- **Ψ5-rel** (relative bound with `Q_T(F) = Σĉ_F(γ)/|ζ′(ρ)|`) is CONDITIONAL and labelled so. Fine.

### Verdict: restatement
- **Fatal step for the mechanism:** (M-Ψ)(ii) has no candidate `J`; by Ψ4 any exact `J` is a functional of `ζ(½+it)`, so
  (M-Ψ) is a positivity statement about `ζ` on the line of RH strength — EQUIVALENT (row 30, row 17 for the "`J = λ^W`"
  choice, which is `lem:monoT` in the wrong direction).
- **Salvageable:** Ψ1/Ψ2 as the formal statement of row 30; Ψ5 (the defect-response residue expansion on `W_T`) and Ψ6 as
  record lemmas quantifying O3 along the orbit; Ψ3′ as a sharpening of F4 ("the inputs A3 and A8 must enter through
  `r(n) = 1`").

## 2. G2_B4_R2 — edge-profile SOS certificates and Theorem R

### Literature and corpus
- **Flat-top forces sign-changing `ŵ`.** A positive-definite continuous function equal to `1` on a neighbourhood of `0` is
  identically `1` (classical; `lem:Rrig` in P47). So no Fejér-type `ŵ ≥ 0` certificate exists in `𝒲_T` — this is why the
  angle needs the edge profile at all, and the memo is right that the family runs between two excluded ends (Prop F(iv)).
- **Riesz means** `min(1,(x/n)^ε)` as weights in explicit formulae are classical (Riesz typical means; Ingham Ch. IV).
  Their use as flat-top dual certificates is P47 def:W (exponential tails admitted). Not new; not claimed new.
- **Almost periodicity in the window parameter.** I found no open-literature source treating the *window length* as the
  a.p. variable of the Weil functional. Bohr–Jessen-type almost periodicity is used in `τ`/`t` (log ζ on vertical lines;
  in the corpus G1_B10_R2 Thm 8(b), G2_B7). The `T`-a.p. structure `ŵ^φ_T(x) = 2Re[e^{2iTx}Ψ(x)]` is a direct consequence of
  the flat top (the `2sin(2Tx)/x` term cancels against the boundary term of `∫φ`, which I re-derived: correct). So
  Theorem R is new to the corpus and, as far as I can find, to the literature. It is modest in depth (a page of Bohr
  theory) but it is a real statement about the "for all `T`" arrow.
- **Lagarias `Re ξ′/ξ > 0 ⟺ RH`, Hamburger** are used as context only and are on record (phase 16, phase 46). Fine.

### Step check (audit)
- **Lemma 3.2.** Re-derived; correct. `Φ_1(0) = ∫φ′ = −1`, so `Ψ(z) = iΦ_1(z)/z` has the simple pole at `0` claimed.
- **Lemma Z′.** The mollified cut-off `h_k`, uniform `(1+|z|)^{−2}` bound on `|Im z| ≤ ½` from the uniformly bounded
  weighted total variation of `h_k″` (including the two atoms of mass `|φ′(0⁺)|` at `±2T`), `Σ(1+|γ|)^{−2} < ∞`, dominated
  convergence on all four sides. Standard and correct. Only subtlety: the explicit formula for `C_c^∞` even tests is
  Weil's; IK Thm 5.12 is one standard statement of it. The hypotheses (compact support, smoothness) are met by `h_k`. I
  accept Lemma Z′; it fills the `𝒲_T` vs `𝒲^{reg}_T` gap of `thm:Z(ii)` and is worth recording.
- **Theorem R, ¬RH branch.** Weil ⟹ some `λ^W_{T₁} < 0`; `lem:monoT` (re-read in P47 l.483, zero-extension proof, sign-free)
  ⟹ `λ^W_T < 0` for `T ≥ T₁`; weak duality (Prop F(ii)) ⟹ `inf f ≤ λ^W_T < 0`. Correct.
- **Theorem R, RH branch.** Step 1: uniform absolute convergence in `T` ⟹ Bohr a.p. Correct. Step 2: exponents
  `±2(τ−γ)` distinct and nonzero for `τ ∈ E`; mean zero. Correct. Step 3: a.p. `≥ 0` on a half-line ⟹ `≥ 0` on `ℝ` (almost
  periods relatively dense in both directions); nonnegative a.p. with mean `0` ⟹ `≡ 0`. Correct. Step 4: all Bohr
  coefficients vanish ⟹ `Ψ(τ−γ₁) = 0` on a dense set ⟹ `Ψ ≡ 0` on `ℝ∖{0}` ⟹ `Φ_1 ≡ 0` ⟹ `φ′ = 0` ⟹ `φ ≡ 1`, not admissible.
  Correct. Step 5 (relatively dense recurrence at depth `η`): correct. **Theorem R is PROVED**, complete form.
- **Cor. R2.** Linearity in `φ` plus the uniform-in-`T` bound `C_{τ_*}N_δ(φ−φ′)`. Correct.
- **Scope honesty.** The memo correctly says: (i) compact `T`-dependent families and `T`-scaled profiles are OPEN (Q-R);
  (ii) primitive form not covered (multipliers `∼e^T`, not a.p.); (iii) under RH some `T`-dependent `w_T` does work
  (`cor:RH`), so the obstruction is to *uniform formulas*, not to certificates. All correct.
- **Lemma P** (primitive temperedness) and the S3(a) correction: checked, correct; they repair round-1 gaps and do not
  bear on the mechanism.

### Verdict: excluded
- **Fatal step for the mechanism:** the angle's natural uniform family is killed by its own Theorem R; the surviving branch
  is the free choice of `w_T` per `T`, which is `thm:Zexh`/`cor:RH` (EQUIV, R157_002 rows on flat-kernel form). The FE is
  used in Lemma Z′ only to *validate an identity* (`0 < β < 1`), not to produce a sign — the memo says so.
- **Salvageable:** Theorem R + Cor. R2 (record: "a `T`-uniform certificate rule of edge type must carry the zero frequencies
  `e^{2iTγ}` in its `T`-dependence"); Lemma Z′; Remark R3 (zero-tuning necessary condition, `Φ_1(γ) = 0 ∀γ`) as the SOS

  positive answer for a `𝒦` containing the `thm:Zexh` optimisers would contradict RH, so the expected answer is "fails for
  large `𝒦`".

## 3. G2_B6_R2 — the Nyquist horizon and Theorem M

### Literature and novelty (the point of this lens)
- **Zhu, arXiv:2608.24827** (abstract read ). Title "Weil positivity in compact windows: a finite reduction,
  certified two-sided bounds, and a Landau–Widom decay law". Contents relevant here: certified lower bound
  `Q(f) ≥ 8.9·10^{−18}‖f‖²` on `[−0.8,0.8]`; **unconditional upper bounds only numerical** (`λ_min(2) ≈ 3.2·10^{−283}`);
  **conditional on RH:** `λ_min(L) ≤ exp(−Le^L)` for large `L`; an *empirical* law `−ln λ_min(L) ∼ 2π²N(T*)/ln N(T*)`;
  and the remark that a one-stroke certificate must resolve frequencies up to `2πe^{A_L}`, `A_L ∼ 4e^L`. **No unconditional
  doubly-exponential upper bound is claimed there.** So the memo's comparison (§3.9) is accurate, and Theorem M —
  unconditional `λ^A_T ≤ exp(−e^{(2−ε)T})` — is not in Zhu. Consistency: Zhu's empirical law at the Nyquist height
  `T* ≍ 2πe^{2T}` gives `−ln λ ≍ 2π²e^{2T}`; Theorem M gives `≥ e^{(2−ε)T}`; compatible, as the memo states.
- **Landau–Widom / prolate literature.** The idea "count degrees of freedom of `PW_T` against zeros up to height `X`" is the
  Landau density heuristic (Landau 1967; Landau–Widom 1980) and appears in the corpus as HEURISTIC (P47 `subsec:where`,
  R153_012/013). The *theorem* — annihilate every zero below `2πe^{2θT−1}` by a dimension count on an exactly orthogonal
  Nyquist lattice, then bound the tail by a Gevrey window — I did not find in the open literature (searches: Weil
  positivity / smallest eigenvalue / Landau–Widom / Paley–Wiener zeros; Bombieri's *Remarks on Weil's quadratic
  functional*, Yoshida 1992, Connes–Consani 2021 semi-local positivity all treat small supports or lower bounds). The
  novelty check is not exhaustive, as the memo says; I could not falsify the novelty claim.
- **Beurling sampling theorem / Littlewood's `S(t)` bound** (Prop. T) are CITED correctly (Seip 2004 Ch. 2; Titchmarsh
  §14.13).

## 4. G2_B9_R2 — rigidity of the jump weights at `T = ∞`

### Literature and corpus
- **Bochner–Schwartz** (positive-definite distributions are tempered and are Fourier transforms of positive tempered
  measures; Schwartz, *Théorie des distributions*, Ch. VII §9) is applied correctly: the form `W^{(a)}` on `C_c^∞` is
  translation-covariant with kernel `u_a` (archimedean kernel + `2cosh(u/2)` − comb), positivity for all `T` is positivity
  on `C_c^∞`, so `u_a` is positive definite. The automatic temperedness handles the exponentially growing comb.
- **Rigidity of Weil positivity under comb perturbation.** I found no open-literature statement of the form "under RH,
  `Λ + b` fails Weil positivity for every nonzero `b ∈ ℓ¹_w`". The folklore that "Dirichlet series without the right
  functional equation do not satisfy Weil positivity" is about their *own* explicit formulas (extra zeros off the line);
  Theorem R keeps `ζ`'s archimedean and polar data and moves only the comb, which is the row-D object. In the corpus,
  G2_B4_R1 S1 uses the same atomicity of `μ_Z` for certificates; B9 credits it. So Theorem R is new to the corpus and, as
  far as I can find, to the literature; its proof is short (one page) and correct.
- **Hamburger's theorem** (Riemann's FE with an `ℕ`-Dirichlet series forces `ζ` up to a constant) is CITED via G2_B3_R2;
  standard (Hamburger 1921–22; Titchmarsh §2.13). Its role here is only the rigidity table.
- **`ζ_odd = (1−2^{−s})ζ`.** The object is classical; as a *row-D falsifier* with 0/1 coefficients, rational Euler product
  and `0 ≤ Λ_odd ≤ Λ` it is new to the corpus and useful: it kills "weight = position", integrality and the Euler product
  as standalone inputs (F1′ door of G2_B4). Not known-to-fail in the literature because nobody set up this window form.

- **Lemma 2.1.** Under RH, `W(F) = Σ_γ|F̂(γ)|²` for real `F ∈ C_c^∞` (absolutely convergent), so `μ_Λ = Σm_γδ_γ`. Correct.
