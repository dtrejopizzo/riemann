## §1 G1_B2_R2 — Kronecker line, Theorem E, Cor D2

### 1.1 Checked (`PROVED` here)
* **Theorem S.** I checked the argument:
  * uniform continuity on the dense subgroup `log G`;
  * Bochner;
  * Fourier–Stieltjes uniqueness on the compact group `Ĝ = ∏_p T`, which gives `μ_w = ι_*ν`;
  * Lusin–Souslin for the Borel structure of `L`.

  The converse is dominated convergence. Correct. This is the spectral-measure form of the classical fact that the
  characters of `Q^×_{>0}` that are continuous for the archimedean topology are the `n^{iγ}`. It has no RH content.
* **Lemma P.** Correct. Suppose two distinct `γ, γ′` both carry positive product mass. Then the local masses must tend
  to 1 at both points. The points differ in all but at most one block, since `(γ−γ′)log p, (γ−γ′)log q ∈ 2πZ` would
  force `log p/log q ∈ Q`. So both points carry mass `> ½` in a single probability space, which is a contradiction.
* **Cor E1, Cor D2.** Correct modulo the `CITED` inputs.
  * An `SL₂(Q_p)`-invariant vector would span an invariant subspace that is also `GL₂`-invariant, because `SL₂` is
  normal. So an infinite-dimensional `π_p` has none.
  * Howe–Moore then gives decay along `a_p^{2k}`, and the odd powers follow.
  * Wiener's lemma makes the local spectral measure atomless.
  * Cor E1 kills `V_c`.

### 1.2 A gap in the proof of Theorem E (not fatal; repair supplied)
In the case `P_0 = ∅` the memo says "take `ν` directly on `Ĝ = T′`". But then `L = ι′(R)` is uncountable, and the
"countable section ⟹ sum of point masses" step does not apply.

**Repair (`PROVED`).** Move one block `B_1` into `P_0`. For `|B_1| ≥ 2` the section `{γ : ι_{B_1}(γ) = χ_0}` is at
most one point, and for `|B_1| = 1` it is a lattice, so it is countable in both cases. The Fubini argument then runs as
written, and Lemma P applies to the remaining tail `(B_j)_{j≥2}`.

(Equivalently, for independent `X, Y` the event `g(X) = g′(Y)` has positive probability only at common atoms.) The
statement of Theorem E stands.

### 1.3 Lens 1
* No zero and no `Λ(n)` is read, so the memo is not circular.
* It proves no equivalence of RH.
* It proves that a whole class of carriers **cannot** host the zeros, except by installing them one summand at a time
  (Prop R). That is Lemma T(⇐) inside the class, and the memo says so correctly.
* The candidate object M (Hecke suspension) is **excluded by its own Cor D2**.

The negative result is a valid addition to the map, with scope limited to unitary `Q^×_{>0}` representations and
their suspensions. It is not an object.

### 1.4 Notes for the record
* **Name clash.** B2's "N-e" (infinite entanglement across primes) and B6's "N-e" (super-critical resolution) are
  different conditions with the same label. They should be renamed, for example N-e(ent) and N-e(res).
* **Scope.** The "three-way conflict" of §5 is `HEURISTIC`, as the memo states. It must not be recorded as a
  theorem outside the suspension class. The escape routes the memo names are:
  * correspondence-valued Frobenius (`Ψ(λ)` with `Id_ε`);

  Both are genuinely open.

**Verdict: excluded.** The fatal step is Cor D2 (`V_c ∩ H¹_{(2)} = 0`): the object has nothing in `H¹`, so OT-1 and
OT-2 fail.
