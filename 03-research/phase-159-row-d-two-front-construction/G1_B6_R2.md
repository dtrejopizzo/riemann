## §0 Verdict

**Round 2 settles the open item (O3) of Construction 9, and settles it negatively and unconditionally.** On Construction 9,
OT-5′ in the P-HS form is not "RH-strength". It is **false**, whether or not RH holds (Theorem A). The window
constants `M_T = sup_{|s|≤T}‖U_s|_{H¹}‖` grow at least linearly in `T`, and the cause is the Riemann–von Mangoldt
density of the zeros, not the position of any zero.

The mechanism is general (Theorem B, the **resolution law**). On any quotient carrier `H¹ = H/V̄` with a flow, a uniform
bound forces the normalised adjoint eigenvectors at distinct zeros to be `2/(M+1)`-separated (Lemma S). In a carrier
whose topology is fixed before the spectrum, these eigenvectors form a differentiable family `λ ↦ h_λ`. The zeros then
have to be resolved by the topology at their own spacing, `≍ 2π/log γ`. Write `R(λ)` for the relative Lipschitz
constant of the family (Def B.1). Then:
* `R(λ) = o(log λ)` excludes the carrier unconditionally;
* `R(λ) = O(log λ)` excludes it under the small-gap hypothesis (SG), which follows from Montgomery's pair-correlation
  conjecture.

Consequences:
1. **Construction 9, and every time-weighted carrier, is excluded** (Cor B1). So is every carrier obtained by adding a
  Fourier-side "archimedean energy" `∫μ|F̂|²` with `μ = o(log²)` (Cor B2).
2. **Weil's form is not continuous** on any of these carriers unless `μ ≳ log` (Prop C).
3. Together these give a new necessary condition **N-e** for any P-HS object. The topology must be **super-critical**:
  its resolution exceeds the zero density `(1/2π)log(λ/2π)`, which is itself fixed by the Γ-factor. So it can be
  supplied without zeros, but it has to be built in.

  The critical (sampling-rate) topology is exactly the corpus's "critical space" (Day 12; M10.3), where the P-HS
  question is the Riesz-basis / `A_2` / `S(T)`-regularity problem already on record as RH-strength. The resolution
  law excludes that space too, under (SG).

**Status: partial new result (a proved exclusion theorem and a proved necessary condition). No object is built. No
sign is claimed.** The corrections the panel asked for (E2, E3, Thm 2(ii) density, the Thm 7 sign slip, the Cor 5(b)

## §1 The carrier (Construction 9′ = Construction 9 corrected per E3)

**Definition 1.1.**
* **The weight.** Fix a measurable weight `w : ℝ → (0,∞)` with
  `n_w² := ∫ w(t)^{−1}dt < ∞`, `K_w² := ∫ t²w(t)^{−1}dt < ∞`, `sup_t w(t+s)/w(t) < ∞` for every `s`,
  and `∫ e^{2a|t|}(1+t²) w(t)^{−1}dt < ∞` for every `a < ½`.
  The round-1 choice `w(t) = cosh(t)(1+t²)^{1+δ}`, `δ > 0`, qualifies.
* **The ambient space.** `H_w := L²(ℝ, w(t)dt)` (the log line `t = log x` of `ℝ_+^*`), with the translation group
  `U_sF = F(·−s)`, strongly continuous, `‖U_s‖ = (ess sup_t w(t+s)/w(t))^{1/2}`. (This is the corrected form of the
  round-1 formula: `‖U_sF‖² = ∫|F(u)|²w(u+s)du`, as P4 noted.)
* **The coboundaries.** For `g ∈ 𝒮_0 := {g ∈ 𝒮(ℝ) even : g(0) = 0, ∫g = 0}` put
  `(Eg)(t) = e^{t/2} Σ_{n≥1} g(ne^t)`.
  Let `V` be the linear span of `{U_s Eg : s ∈ ℝ, g ∈ 𝒮_0}`, and `V̄` its closure in `H_w`.
* **The carrier.** `H¹_w := H_w / V̄`, with the quotient norm and the induced group, still written `U_s`.

**Lemma 1.2 (`CITED` standard, Riemann's second proof / Tate).** For `g ∈ 𝒮_0`:
* `Eg` is smooth, and it and all its derivatives decay faster than every `e^{−N|t|}` at both ends. At `+∞` this is
  Schwartz decay of `g`. At `−∞`
  it is Poisson: `Σ_{n≥1}g(nx) = x^{−1}Σ_{k≥1}ĝ(k/x)`, using `g(0) = ĝ(0) = 0`. So `Eg ∈ H_w`.
* `Φ_{Eg}(ρ) = ζ(ρ)·Mg(ρ)` on `0 < Re ρ < 1`, where `Mg(ρ) = ∫_0^∞ g(x)x^ρ d^*x` is holomorphic on `Re ρ > −2`.

**Consequence 1.3 (`PROVED` from 1.2).** For each non-trivial zero `ρ` of multiplicity `m`, and each `0 ≤ j < m`, the
functional `ℓ_ρ^{(j)}(F) := ∫F(t)t^j e^{(ρ−½)t}dt` is continuous on `H_w` and vanishes on `V̄`.
* **Continuity.** Cauchy–Schwarz and the last condition on `w`, since `|Re ρ − ½| < ½`.
* **Vanishing.** `ℓ_ρ^{(j)}(U_sEg) = ∂_ρ^j[e^{(ρ−½)s}ζ(ρ)Mg(ρ)]`, which is `0` because `ζ` vanishes to order `m`.

Under the pairing `⟨F, h⟩ = ∫Fh dt`, identify `H_w^* = L²(w^{−1}dt)`. Then `ℓ_ρ^{(j)} ↔ t^je^{(ρ−½)t}`, and on the line,
`ρ = ½+iλ`, `h_λ(t) := e^{iλt}`. The adjoint group is `U_s^*h = h(·+s)`, so `U_s^*h_λ = e^{iλs}h_λ`.

**Remark 1.4 (E3, `PROVED`).** Round 1's `H_w = L²(C_Q, w dt⊗d^*u)` has the Ẑ*-isotypic decomposition. Its `χ`-part
carries the zeros of `L(s,χ)`, so its Lefschetz target is `Σ_χ W_χ`, not `W`. Definition 1.1 is the trivial-character
part. **Theorem A below holds for both versions**, because it uses only the ζ-functionals of 1.3, which lie in `V̄^⊥`
in either case.

**Remark 1.5 (`HEURISTIC`).** For `w = cosh`, Paley–Wiener for the strip identifies `H_w` with the Smirnov space
`E²(S)`, `S = {|Im ζ| < ½}`. `V̄` is then plausibly `B_Z E²(S)`, with `B_Z` the Blaschke product of the zeros. That
would make `H¹_w` the model space `K_{B_Z}` with the compressed exponentials, and it would settle (O1) (kernels at the
zeros of `B` are complete in `K_B`). The step "`[ξ]` has no singular inner factor at the two ends of the strip" is only
sketched here, so (O1) remains `OPEN`. It is not needed below.

## §2 Lemma S (separation) — `PROVED`

Let `X` be a Banach space and `(U_s)_{s∈ℝ}` a group of bounded operators on `X` (no continuity needed), with
`M := sup_s ‖U_s‖ < ∞`.

*(a) Critical.* If `U_s e = c(s)e` with `e ≠ 0`, then `|c(s)| = 1` for all `s`.

*(b) Semisimple.* If `U_s e = e^{iγs}e` and `U_s e′ = e^{iγs}(e′ + s e)` for all `s`, then `e = 0`.

*(c) Separated.* If `U_s e = e^{iγs}e` and `U_s e′ = e^{iγ′s}e′` with `γ ≠ γ′` real, then
`‖e − e′‖ ≥ 2 max(‖e‖, ‖e′‖)/(M+1)`. In particular, unit eigenvectors for distinct eigenvalues are `2/(M+1)`-apart,
**however close `γ` and `γ′` are**.

*Proof.*
* **(a)** `|c(s)|^{±k}‖e‖ = ‖U_{±ks}e‖ ≤ M‖e‖` for all `k ∈ ℕ`.
* **(b)** `‖U_se′‖ ≥ |s|‖e‖ − ‖e′‖`, and this is at most `M‖e′‖` for all `s`.
* **(c)** Take `s = π/(γ′−γ)`. Then `U_s(e − e′) = e^{iγs}(e + e′)`, so `‖e+e′‖ ≤ M‖e−e′‖`. Then
  `2‖e‖ ≤ ‖e+e′‖ + ‖e−e′‖ ≤ (M+1)‖e−e′‖`, and symmetrically for `e′`. ∎

*Reading.* (a)+(b) are Thm 7 / Lemma U without cyclicity. (c) is the new ingredient. **A uniformly bounded flow cannot
host arbitrarily close eigenvalues with arbitrarily close eigenvectors.** In a finite-dimensional `H¹` (Weil) this is
empty: there are finitely many eigenvalues. Over `Spec Z` the eigenvalues accumulate with density `(1/2π)log λ`, and (c)
becomes a constraint on the topology.

## §3 Theorem A (Construction 9′ fails OT-5′, unconditionally) — `PROVED`

Let `M_T := sup_{|s|≤T} ‖U_s|_{H¹_w}‖`, with `n_w`, `K_w` as in Def 1.1. Then there is `T_0` such that for all
`T ≥ T_0`

  `M_T ≥ (2n_w/(πK_w))·T − 1.`

In particular `sup_s ‖U_s|_{H¹_w}‖ = ∞`. So:
* by Sz.-Nagy (round 1 Thm 8), `H¹_w` carries **no** inner product equivalent to its norm for which
  `φ^s = e^{s/2}U_s` is conformal;
* **OT-5′ in the P-HS form fails on Construction 9 and 9′.**

This does not depend on RH, and it holds for every admissible weight.

*Proof.* Dualise. The quotient `H_w → H¹_w` identifies `(H¹_w)^*` isometrically with `V̄^⊥ ⊂ H_w^*` (Hahn–Banach), and
`‖U_s|_{H¹_w}‖ = ‖U_s^*|_{V̄^⊥}‖`. By 1.3 the functionals `t^je^{(ρ−½)t}` lie in `V̄^⊥`. There are three exhaustive
cases.

*Case 1: some zero `ρ_0 = β_0+iγ_0` has `β_0 ≠ ½`.* Put `h = e^{(ρ_0−½)t} ∈ V̄^⊥`. Then `U_s^*h = e^{(ρ_0−½)s}h`, so
`M_T ≥ e^{|β_0−½|T}`. This exceeds `(2n_w/πK_w)T − 1` for `T ≥ T_0(β_0)`.

*Case 2: RH holds and some zero `½+iγ` is multiple.* Put `h = h_γ` and `h′ = t e^{iγt}`, both in `V̄^⊥`. Then
`U_s^*h′ = (t+s)e^{iγ(t+s)} = e^{iγs}(h′ + s h)`. With `‖h‖ = n_w` and `‖h′‖ = K_w`:
`M_T ≥ ‖U_T^*h′‖/‖h′‖ ≥ (Tn_w − K_w)/K_w ≥ (2n_w/πK_w)T − 1`.

*Case 3: RH holds and all zeros are simple.* By Riemann–von Mangoldt (`CITED` standard),
`N(2X) − N(X) = (X/2π)log X·(1+o(1))`. Since the ordinates are distinct, `(X, 2X]` contains two consecutive ordinates
with gap at most `X/(N(2X)−N(X)−1) → 0`. So for every `T` there are ordinates `γ < γ′` with `δ := γ′−γ ∈ (0, π/T]`.
Put `x = h_γ − h_{γ′} ∈ V̄^⊥`.
* **Upper bound on `x`.** `‖x‖² = ∫|e^{iγt} − e^{iγ′t}|²w^{−1} ≤ δ²K_w²`.
* **Lower bound on `U_T^*x`.** `U_T^*x = e^{iγT}[(1 − e^{iδT})h_γ + e^{iδT}x]`, so
  `‖U_T^*x‖ ≥ |1−e^{iδT}|n_w − δK_w = 2sin(δT/2)n_w − δK_w ≥ (2/π)δTn_w − δK_w`,
  using `sin u ≥ 2u/π` on `[0, π/2]`.
* **Dividing** by `‖x‖ ≤ δK_w` gives `M_T ≥ (2n_w/πK_w)T − 1`. ∎

*Remarks.*
* **(i) Sanity check against Connes 1999.** Take a polynomial weight `w = (1+t²)^{δ/2}` with `δ > 3`. Off-line zeros
  are then invisible, so Case 1 is unavailable. Cases 2–3 use only on-line zeros, and they give `M_T ≳ T` (`PROVED`
  under RH). Unconditionally, run Case 3 on the simple zeros on the line: they have positive proportion (Conrey 1989,
  `CITED`), so their gaps also tend to `0`. The ambient bound is `‖U_s‖ ≤ C(1+|s|)^{δ/2}`, so there is no contradiction. For `δ ≤ 3`,
  `K_w = ∞` and the argument does not apply, which is consistent with Connes' `L²_δ`, where critical zeros appear with
  multiplicity `< (1+δ)/2` (R158_004 row 14).
* **(ii) The picture, made quantitative for the object (`PROVED` + `HEURISTIC` reading).**
  * Each window is bounded (`M_T < ∞`, Banach–Steinhaus). The uniformity is exactly what fails, at a linear rate.
  * **The rate is forced by the zero density (Case 3) whether RH is true or false.** Case 3 is the RH-true case, and
  it still fails.
  * So on this carrier the "para todo T" is not blocked by an off-line zero. It is blocked by the carrier's inability
  to separate eigenvalues closer than `~1/T` at window `T`.
  * This is the same resolution limit that makes `thm:finZ` hold: a window-`T` test sees zeros only at resolution
  `1/T`. The link to `thm:finZ` is `HEURISTIC`; the theorems are different.

## §4 Theorem B (the resolution law) — `PROVED`; parts (iii), Cor B3 `PROVED under (SG)`

**Definition B.1 (zero-free quotient carrier with an eigenfamily).** A quadruple `(H, U, V, h)` where:
* `H` is a Banach space, `U` a group of bounded operators on `H`, and `V ⊂ H` a closed `U`-invariant subspace;
* `λ ↦ h_λ ∈ H^*` (`λ ∈ ℝ`) is a norm-`C¹` map with `U_s^*h_λ = e^{iλs}h_λ` and `h_λ ≠ 0`.

It is **defined before the spectrum** if `H`, `U` and `h` are defined without the zeros. The zeros enter only through
the requirement that `V` realises them:
* **(Z1)** `h_γ ∈ V^⊥` for every ordinate `γ` of a zero on the line;
* **(Z2)** `∂_λh_λ|_γ ∈ V^⊥` at every multiple zero on the line;
* **(Z3)** for every off-line zero `ρ`, `V^⊥` contains a nonzero `U^*`-eigenvector with character `e^{(ρ−½)s}`.

This is the **full-visibility** condition, which round 1 Thm 7 showed is necessary for OT-2 not to be RH-strength.
The **resolution function** is
`R(λ) := sup_{|μ−λ|≤1} ‖∂_μh_μ‖ / ‖h_λ‖`.

**Theorem B.** Let `(H, U, V, h)` satisfy (Z1)–(Z3), and suppose `M := sup_s ‖U_s|_{H/V}‖ < ∞`. Then:

*(i)* RH holds and all zeros are simple.

*(ii)* For every pair of consecutive ordinates `γ < γ′` with `γ′ − γ ≤ 1`: `R(γ)·(γ′ − γ) ≥ 2/(M+1)`.

*(iii)* `liminf_{X→∞} (1/log X)·max_{γ∈(X,2X]} R(γ) ≥ 1/(π(M+1))`. In particular:
* if `R(λ) = o(log λ)`, then `M = ∞`. This is **unconditional**;
* if `R(λ) = O(log λ)` and (SG) holds, then `M = ∞`, where
  **(SG)**: `liminf_n (γ_{n+1} − γ_n) log γ_n = 0`.

*Proof.*
* **(i)** Lemma S(a) with (Z3), and Lemma S(b) with (Z1)–(Z2). Differentiating `U_s^*h_λ = e^{iλs}h_λ` in `λ` gives
  `U_s^*∂h_γ = e^{iγs}(∂h_γ + is h_γ)`, a Jordan pair.
* **(ii)** By the mean-value inequality, `‖h_γ − h_{γ′}‖ ≤ (γ′−γ)·sup_{[γ,γ′]}‖∂h‖ ≤ (γ′−γ)R(γ)‖h_γ‖`. Lemma S(c)
  gives `‖h_γ − h_{γ′}‖ ≥ 2‖h_γ‖/(M+1)`.
* **(iii)** By (i) the ordinates are distinct, so as in Case 3 of Theorem A, `(X,2X]` contains consecutive
  `γ < γ′` with `γ′−γ ≤ 2π(1+o(1))/log X`. Apply (ii).
* **The (SG) clause.** If `R(λ) ≤ C log λ`, then (ii) gives `(γ′−γ)log γ ≥ 2/(C(M+1))` for all consecutive pairs,
  contradicting (SG). ∎

**About (SG) (`CITED`, `OPEN`).** (SG) is unproved, even under RH. It follows from Montgomery's pair-correlation
conjecture, whose density `1 − (sin πu/πu)²` is positive near `u = 0`, together with the standard uniformity used to
derive gap statements (Montgomery 1973; Conrey–Ghosh–Gonek). It is also what the GUE model predicts. It would follow at
once from the existence of a multiple zero, but that case is already killed by (i). I use (SG) only to locate the
boundary. **Nothing in this memo depends on it except Cor B3.**

### Corollaries

**Cor B1 (`PROVED`).** Construction 9′, and Construction 9 over `C_Q`, have `R ≤ K_w/n_w` bounded. So they are
excluded by (iii). Theorem A is the quantitative form.

**Cor B2 (archimedean energy does not rescue time-weighted carriers; `PROVED`).**
* **Set-up.** Let `μ ≥ 0` be measurable and polynomially bounded, and put `μ*(λ) := sup_{|τ|≤2|λ|+2}μ(τ)`. Let `H` be
  the completion of `C_c^∞(ℝ) + V` under `‖F‖_H² := ∫|F|²w + (1/2π)∫μ(τ)|F̂(τ)|²dτ`, with `w` as in Def 1.1 and `V` as
  in 1.1. Since `Eg` is Schwartz on the log line (Lemma 1.2), `V ⊂ H`.
* **Claim.** If `μ*(λ) = o(log²λ)`, then `sup_s‖U_s|_{H/V̄}‖ = ∞`.

*Proof.*
* **The group and (Z1)–(Z3).** Translation preserves `|F̂|`, so `‖U_s‖_H ≤ ‖U_s‖_{L²(w)}`. Because
  `‖·‖_H ≥ ‖·‖_{L²(w)}`, the dual norms satisfy `‖·‖_{H^*} ≤ ‖·‖_{L²(w)^*}`. So all functionals of 1.3 stay continuous,
  (Z1)–(Z3) hold, and `‖∂h_μ‖_{H^*} ≤ K_w`.
* **Lower bound on `‖h_λ‖`.** Fix a real `φ ∈ C_c^∞` with `∫φ = 1` and put `F_λ = e^{−iλt}φ`. Then `h_λ(F_λ) = 1`, and
  `F̂_λ(τ) = φ̂(τ−λ)`. Using the Schwartz decay of `φ̂` for `|τ| > 2|λ|+2`:
  `‖F_λ‖_H² ≤ ∫φ²w + (1/2π)∫μ(τ)|φ̂(τ−λ)|²dτ ≤ C_φ(1 + μ*(λ))`.
  So `‖h_λ‖_{H^*} ≥ C_φ^{−1/2}(1+μ*(λ))^{−1/2}`.
* **Conclusion.** `R(λ) ≤ K_wC_φ^{1/2}(1+μ*(λ+1))^{1/2} = o(log λ)`. Apply (iii). ∎

**Prop C (Weil's form needs archimedean energy in the norm; `PROVED`, from `eq:form`/`eq:W`).** In the setting of
Cor B2, suppose `μ*(λ) = o(log λ)`, which includes `μ = 0`, i.e. Construction 9′. Then there is no constant `C` with
`W(c_F) ≤ C‖F‖_H²` for all `F ∈ C_c^∞(−ε, ε)`, `ε < ½log 2`. The complete Weil form is not continuous on the carrier.

*Proof.* Take `F = F_λ` with `supp φ ⊂ (−ε,ε)`. The window has no contacts (`log n ≥ log 2 > 2ε`), so by `eq:form` and
`eq:W`:
`W_ε(F_λ) = (1/2π)∫m(τ)|φ̂(τ−λ)|²dτ + 2Re(A\bar B)`.
* **The Tate moments.** `A(F_λ)`, `B(F_λ)` are Fourier transforms of `φe^{±t/2}` at `∓λ`, so they are `O(λ^{−∞})`.
* **The archimedean term.** `m(τ) ≥ log(1+|τ|) − C_1` (round 1 Thm 1, from Stirling). This gives
  `W_ε(F_λ) ≥ (log λ − C′)‖φ‖_2²`.
* **The norm.** `‖F_λ‖_H² ≤ C_φ(1+μ*(λ)) = o(log λ)`. The ratio diverges. ∎

(Since `‖[F]‖_{H¹} ≤ ‖F‖_H`, a bound by the quotient norm fails too.)

**Cor B3 (the critical space; `PROVED under (SG)`).** Every zero-free carrier of Def B.1 whose resolution is at the
sampling rate, `R(λ) = O(log λ)`, fails P-HS under (SG). This covers:
* Cor B2 with `μ* ≍ log²`;
* the "critical space `K ≍ 1/ρ`" of Day 12, if it is realised with a flow as in Def B.1.

**Remark B.4 (the window of design; `PROVED` pieces, `HEURISTIC` synthesis).** In the class of Cor B2:
* Prop C needs `μ* ≳ log` for Weil's form to be continuous;
* Cor B2 needs `μ* ≳ log²` to escape exclusion unconditionally, and Cor B3 needs `μ*/log² → ∞` along the relevant heights
  under (SG).

In that regime `W(c_{F_λ})/‖F_λ‖² → 0`, so the Weil form is small relative to the carrier norm at high frequency. The
conformal inner product that OT-5′ demands would then be a different form from Weil's, and OT-8 (the Hermitian square
`W(c_F) = ‖F(U)‖²_{HS}`) would have to come from a trace identity, not from the norm. This is `HEURISTIC`: I have not
proved that OT-8 fails there. The other way out of the class is a time weight that **relaxes at high frequency**, a
phase-space weight `Ω(t,τ)` with plateau `|t| ≲ L(τ)`. Theorem B then asks for `L(λ)/log λ → ∞` along the zeros (under
(SG)). Both escapes are super-critical.

## §5 The new necessary condition N-e, and the object test

**N-e (resolution; `PROVED` in the class of Def B.1, `HEURISTIC` beyond).** A P-HS object whose `H¹` is a quotient of a
zero-free ambient space with a flow, and which sees every strip zero (full visibility), must have a topology whose
resolution at height `λ` beats the zero spacing.
* Unconditionally, `R(λ)/log λ` cannot tend to `0`.
* Under (SG), `R(λ)/log λ` must be unbounded.

The zero density `(1/2π)log(λ/2π)` is the derivative of the Riemann–Siegel phase, i.e. it comes from Γ. So N-e can be
met without the zeros, but only by building the archimedean density, amplified, into the carrier's topology. This
refines N-a ("the object must deliver the count"). **The object must deliver the count as a resolution, before the
spectrum.** In Weil's case `H¹` is finite-dimensional and N-e is empty. This is a structural difference between curves
and `Spec Z` that no finite-dimensional analogy captures (`HEURISTIC`).

## §7 What is genuinely new

1. **Theorem A.** On the zero-free, fully visible, growth-type-½ carrier of round 1, the P-HS form of OT-5′ is
  **false**, proved without RH. The window constants grow at least linearly, and the cause is the zero density.
2. **Theorem B (resolution law) and Lemma S.** Among zero-free quotient carriers with a flow and full visibility, P-HS
  requires resolution `R(λ) ≫ log λ` (`≠ o(log λ)` unconditionally; unbounded over `log λ` under (SG)).
3. **Cors B1–B3, Prop C.**
  * Time-weighted carriers are excluded, and so are carriers with sub-`log²` archimedean energy.
  * Weil's form is discontinuous on sub-`log` carriers.
  * The corpus's critical space (Day 12, M10.3) is excluded under (SG).
4. **N-e**, a necessary condition that is absent from the OT list and from N-a/N-b/N-c: the object has to carry the
  Riemann–von Mangoldt density as a super-critical resolution of its topology.

What is **not** new: Lemma S is elementary. The Riesz-basis / sampling / `A_2` view of zeros is Pavlov–Nikolski–Seip,
and is used in M10.3. (SG) is Montgomery's.

## §8 The weakest step

* **Scope of Theorem B.** It covers carriers of Def B.1: quotient `H¹`, an ambient `C¹` eigenfamily `h_λ` defined for
  all real `λ`, and full visibility (Z1)–(Z3).
  * A carrier whose adjoint eigenvectors at zeros are **not** the restriction of a zero-free differentiable family
  escapes. Hilbert–Pólya `L²(μ_Z)` is the extreme case, but that is defined from the zeros.
  * A carrier whose `H¹` is not a quotient (a subspace, or a subquotient with the topology changed after quotienting)
  also escapes.
  * Whether a zero-free carrier can avoid a `C¹` eigenfamily **and** keep OT-2 is `OPEN`. This is where an audit
  reader should push.
* **(SG)** is unproved. Only Cor B3 and the "unbounded" clause of N-e depend on it. Theorem A, Cors B1 and B2, and
  Prop C are unconditional.
* **Citations.** Lemma 1.2 (Poisson / Riemann's second proof) and Riemann–von Mangoldt are `CITED` standard. The
  inequality `m(τ) ≥ log(1+|τ|) − C_1` comes from Stirling, as in round 1 Thm 1.
* **Cor B2** assumes `μ` is polynomially bounded, to control the tail of `∫μ|φ̂(τ−λ)|²`.
