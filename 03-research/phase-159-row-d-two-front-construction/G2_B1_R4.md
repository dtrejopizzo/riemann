## 2. The graded converse

**Setup (PROVED; P47 definitions).** Fix `ε = 1/10`, so `5ε < log 2`. Let `ψ ∈ C_c^∞((−ε,ε))` be real with
`A(ψ) = B(ψ) = 0`. For real `x`, put `ψ_x(t) := ψ(t − x)`. Then:
(a) `A(ψ_x) = e^{x/2}A(ψ) = 0` and `B(ψ_x) = e^{−x/2}B(ψ) = 0`, so `ψ_x ∈ P_T ∩ C_c^∞(I_T)` whenever `|x| + ε < T`. The
primitive subspace is translation-invariant within the window.
(b) Put `Ψ(z) := ∫ψ(t)e^{−zt}dt` (entire) and `Φ(z) := ∫c_ψ(v)e^{−zv}dv = Ψ(z)Ψ(−z)`. Here `c_ψ(v) = ∫ψ(t)ψ(t−v)dt`,
and substituting `v = t − w` gives `∫∫ψ(t)ψ(w)e^{−zt}e^{zw}`. Moreover `Ψ(½) = B(ψ) = 0` and `Ψ(−½) = A(ψ) = 0`.
(c) Define `g_ψ(x) := (2π)^{-1}∫ s_T(τ)|ψ̂(τ)|² e^{−iτx}dτ` for any `T` with `|x| + 2ε < 2T`. This is independent of
such `T`. Indeed `(2π)^{-1}∫cos(τL)|ψ̂|²e^{−iτx}dτ = ½[c_ψ(x−L) + c_ψ(x+L)]`, because `ĉ_ψ = |ψ̂|²` and `c_ψ` is even. A
contact `L = log n ≥ 2T > |x| + 2ε` gives `|x ± L| > 2ε`, so it contributes nothing. With the Lévy form `eq:A`
(Fubini is justified since `c_ψ(x) − ½[c_ψ(x−u)+c_ψ(x+u)] = O(u²)` and `K(u) = O(1/u)` at `0`), we get, for `x ≥ 3ε`,
```
g_ψ(x) = k_ψ(x) − Σ_{n≥2} Λ(n) n^{−½} c_ψ(x − log n), k_ψ(x) = −½∫_{x−2ε}^{x+2ε} c_ψ(x−u)K(u)du ,
```
with `|k_ψ(x)| ≤ C_ψ e^{−x/2}`. Here the integration variable satisfies `u ≥ x − 2ε ≥ ε`, and on `u ≥ ε`,
`K(u) ≤ e^{−u/2}/(1 − e^{−ε}) + e^{−u/2}`.
(d) For `F = ψ_{−x/2}`, `G = ψ_{x/2}`, the sesquilinear form gives `𝔞_T(F,G) = (2π)^{-1}∫s_T F̂ \overline{Ĝ} = g_ψ(x)`,
because `F̂ = e^{−iτx/2}ψ̂` and `\overline{Ĝ} = e^{−iτx/2}\overline{ψ̂}`. Also `𝔞_T(ψ) = g_ψ(0)` and `⟨F,G⟩ = c_ψ(x)`.

**Theorem Q (graded Weil converse; PROVED; primitive form, hence also complete form).** Suppose there are
`C ≥ 0`, `κ ≥ 0` and `T_0` with
```
λ^A_T ≥ −C e^{κT} for all T ≥ T_0 .
```
Then `ζ(s) ≠ 0` for `Re s > ½ + κ/2`. The same conclusion holds if `λ^W_T ≥ −Ce^{κT}` for `T ≥ T_0`, since
`λ^W_T ≤ λ^A_T` (P47 l.143).

*Proof.* Step 1 (Cauchy–Schwarz, no positivity of `u` needed). Fix `ψ` as in the Setup and `x ≥ 3ε`, and put
`T := x/2 + 2ε`, taking `x` large enough that `T ≥ T_0`. Then `F := ψ_{−x/2}` and `G := ψ_{x/2}` lie in
`P_T ∩ C_c^∞(I_T)`, by (a), since `x/2 + ε < T`. On real `P_T` the real symmetric bilinear form
`H := 𝔞_T + Ce^{κT}⟨·,·⟩` is positive semidefinite. This uses the hypothesis, plus the fact that the real and complex
bottoms agree because `s_T` is even. By Cauchy–Schwarz for `H` and translation invariance of the norm and of `𝔞_T`
(d), and since `⟨F,G⟩ = c_ψ(x) = 0` for `x ≥ 2ε`,
```
|g_ψ(x)| = |H(F,G)| ≤ H(F,F)^{½}H(G,G)^{½} = g_ψ(0) + C e^{κ(x/2+2ε)}‖ψ‖² .
```
Hence `|g_ψ(x)| ≤ C_1 e^{κx/2}` for all `x ≥ 3ε`, where `C_1` depends on `ψ, C, κ, T_0`. On a compact `x`-range, use
continuity.

Step 2 (Laplace transform). Put `𝒢(z) := ∫_{3ε}^∞ g_ψ(x)e^{−zx}dx`. By Step 1 it is holomorphic on `Re z > κ/2`. For
`Re z > ½` compute it from (c). Every `c_ψ(x − log n)` is supported in `(log n − 2ε, log n + 2ε) ⊂ (3ε, ∞)`, because
`log 2 > 5ε`. The sum `Σ Λ(n)n^{−½−Re z}‖c_ψ‖_1 e^{2ε|z|}` is finite, so Fubini gives
```
𝒢(z) = 𝒦(z) − Φ(z) Σ_{n≥2} Λ(n) n^{−½−z} = 𝒦(z) + Φ(z)·(ζ′/ζ)(½ + z) (Re z > ½),
```
with `𝒦(z) := ∫_{3ε}^∞ k_ψ(x)e^{−zx}dx` holomorphic on `Re z > −½`.

Step 3 (continuation). By Steps 1–2, `Φ(z)(ζ′/ζ)(½+z) = 𝒢(z) − 𝒦(z)` extends holomorphically to `Re z > κ/2`, when
`κ < 1`. For `κ ≥ 1` the conclusion is the trivial `Re ρ < 1`. Suppose `ζ(ρ_0) = 0` with `Re ρ_0 > ½ + κ/2` and put
`z_0 := ρ_0 − ½`. Then `(ζ′/ζ)(½+z)` has a simple pole at `z_0` with residue equal to the multiplicity `≥ 1`.
Moreover `z_0 ≠ ±½` since `0 < Re ρ_0 < 1`. The functionals `ψ ↦ Ψ(w)` for `w ∈ {z_0, z̄_0, −z_0, −z̄_0, ½, −½}` come
from the exponentials `e^{−wt}` on `(−ε,ε)`. Distinct exponentials are linearly independent, so `Re Ψ(±z_0)` and
`Im Ψ(±z_0)` are not in the real span of `Ψ(½), Ψ(−½)`. Hence the real codimension-2 space `P_ε ∩ C_c^∞` contains a
`ψ` with `Ψ(z_0)Ψ(−z_0) ≠ 0`: the set where this holds is the complement of finitely many proper closed subspaces.
For that `ψ`, `Φ(z_0) ≠ 0`, so `Φ(ζ′/ζ)(½+·)` has a pole at `z_0`, which lies in its half-plane of holomorphy. This is
a contradiction. ∎

**Remark Q.0 (what the proof uses).** The proof uses only three facts: (a) translation invariance of `P_T`; the
T-independence of `g_ψ`, which is `lem:monoT`'s mechanism; and one Cauchy–Schwarz. It uses no temperedness, no
Bochner–Schwartz, and no functional equation. The same proof, with `Σ a_n n^{−½−z}` in place of `−ζ′/ζ(½+z)`, gives
the following.

**Theorem Q′ (general combs; PROVED; primitive form).** Let `a = (a_n)_{n≥2}` be real with
`Σ|a_n|n^{−σ} < ∞` for all `σ > 1`, and let `λ^A_T(a)` be the bottom on `P_T` of `eq:form` with weights
`v_n(a) = 2a_n/√n` and the same `m`. Suppose `λ^A_T(a) ≥ −Ce^{κT}` for `T ≥ T_0`. Then `D_a(s) := Σ a_n n^{−s}`
continues holomorphically to `{Re s > ½ + κ/2} ∖ {1}`, with at most a pole of order `≤ 2` at `s = 1`. The order bound
holds because for generic `ψ`, `Φ` vanishes to order exactly 2 at `z = ½`: `Ψ(z)` vanishes simply at `½`, and `Ψ(−z)`
vanishes simply at `½` since `Ψ(−½) = 0`. *Proof.* As above. ∎

**Corollary Q1 (PROVED; both forms).**
(i) If `λ^A_T ≥ −C_η e^{ηT}` for every `η > 0` (a *subexponential* lower bound), then RH. With `κ = 0`, a uniform
lower bound `λ^A_T ≥ −C` already gives RH.
(ii) For `b ∈ ℓ¹_w := {Σ|b_n|/√n < ∞}`, `D_b` is holomorphic on `Re s > ½`. So by Theorem Q′,
`λ^A_T(Λ+b) ≥ −Ce^{κT}` implies that `ζ` has no zero in `Re s > ½ + κ/2`. With `κ = 0` this is G2_B9_R3 Theorem N(i)
for `b ∈ ℓ¹_w`, with a proof that avoids the Lemma L step-2 transposition flagged by the round-3 panel.
(iii) (EQUIVALENT) The following are equivalent: RH; `∀T λ^A_T ≥ 0`; `inf_T λ^A_T > −∞`; `λ^A_T ≥ −e^{o(T)}`. The same
holds for `λ^W`. The last two conditions are robust: changing finitely many `Λ(n)`, or adding any `b ∈ ℓ¹_w`, moves
`λ^A_T` by at most `2Σ|b_n|/√n`, uniformly in `T`, because `|c_F| ≤ ‖F‖²`.

**Proposition T (trivial exponent; PROVED; both forms).** For every `T`,
`λ^A_T ≥ C_0 − 𝖠_T = −(4+o(1))e^T`, and `λ^W_T ≥ C_0 − 𝖠_T − 2 sup_{‖F‖=1}|A(F)B(F)| ≥ −(5+o(1))e^T`.
*Proof.* By `eq:A`, `‖F‖² − c_F(u) ≥ 0` and `|c_F(log n)| ≤ ‖F‖²`. Also `|A(F)|² ≤ ‖F‖²∫_{−T}^T e^t dt` and the same for
`B`, so `|AB| ≤ 2sinh(T)‖F‖²`. ∎
Theorem Q at `κ = 1` returns exactly the trivial `Re ρ ≤ 1`. The trivial bound and the trivial zero-free region
calibrate each other.

**Proposition Q2 (sharpness of the exponent; PROVED, conditional and possibly vacuous).** Suppose `ζ` has only
finitely many zeros off the critical line, `N` of them with multiplicity, and let `Θ := max Re ρ > ½`. Then
`λ^A_T ≥ −2NT e^{(2Θ−1)T}` for all `T`. Together with Theorem Q, the exponent
`κ* := limsup_{T→∞} T^{−1}log⁺(−λ^A_T)` then equals `2Θ − 1` exactly.
*Proof.* For real `F ∈ P_T ∩ C_c^∞(I_T)`, `lem:roughEF` gives `𝔞_T(F) = Σ_ρ Φ_F(ρ)Φ_F(1−ρ)`, with
`Φ_F(s) = ∫F e^{(s−½)t}` and the sum absolutely convergent since `F` is smooth. A zero on the line contributes
`|F̂(γ)|² ≥ 0`. An off-line zero `ρ = ½ + δ + iγ` has `|Φ_F(ρ)Φ_F(1−ρ)| ≤ (∫|F|e^{|δ||t|})² ≤ 2Te^{2|δ|T}‖F‖²`. Summing
gives the bound on the smooth subspace. It extends to all of `P_T` by the density Lemma 1.3 of G2_B9_R3 (panel-checked)
and continuity of `𝔞_T` in the form norm. ∎
**Reading.** A zero at `½ + δ` can depress the bottom by at most about `e^{2δT}`, and Theorem Q says it must depress
it at least that much, infinitely often. The zero-free abscissa `½ + κ/2` of Theorem Q therefore cannot be improved
to `½ + κ/4` (G2_P5_R3's heuristic form), since that would contradict Prop. Q2 in the finite-exception scenario.
**OPEN:** the unconditional inequality `κ* ≤ 2Θ − 1` in general (infinitely many off-line zeros). The proof above
needs `Σ_{off}|Φ_F(ρ)Φ_F(1−ρ)| ≲ e^{(2Θ−1+η)T}‖F‖²` uniformly over rough `F`, and a zero-density estimate does not
give this without smoothness of `F`. Proved so far: `2Θ − 1 ≤ κ* ≤ 1`.

## 3. The Tao reading: error budgets of structure/randomness splits

**Definition S.** A *split* is `Λ = s + r` with real `s, r` supported on `n ≥ 2`, where `s` satisfies the growth
hypothesis of Theorem Q′. The *structured form* is `𝔞^{(s)}_T` (P47's form with weights `2s_n/√n` and the full `m`).
The *pseudorandom functional* is `ℓ_T^{(r)}(F) := Σ_{n∈N_T} (2r_n/√n) Re c_F(log n)`, so that
`𝔞_T = 𝔞^{(s)}_T − ℓ^{(r)}_T` exactly, by linearity of `eq:form` in the weights. The *one-sided absolute budget* is
`E^+_T(r) := sup{ℓ^{(r)}_T(F) : F ∈ P_T, ‖F‖ = 1}`, the harmful direction.

**Corollary S (budget = zero-free half-plane; PROVED; primitive form).**
(i) Suppose that for some split, `λ^A_T(s) ≥ 0` for `T ≥ T_0` and, for some `0 ≤ η < 1`,
`ℓ^{(r)}_T(F) ≤ η 𝔞^{(s)}_T(F) + Ce^{κT}‖F‖²` on `P_T`. Then `λ^A_T ≥ −Ce^{κT}`, so by Theorem Q, `ζ ≠ 0` on
`Re s > ½ + κ/2`. The absolute case is `η = 0`, `E^+_T(r) ≤ Ce^{κT}`.
(ii) Contrapositive: if `ζ(½ + δ + iγ) = 0` with `δ > 0`, then every split satisfying the structured hypothesis of
(i) has error exponent `κ ≥ 2δ`.
(iii) Under RH the trivial split `s = Λ`, `r = 0` has `κ = 0`. Every split with `κ < 1` proves a zero-free half-plane
`Re s > ½ + κ/2 < 1`, which is not known (quasi-RH). No unconditional split with `κ < 1` exists in the literature.
*Proof.* (i) `𝔞_T = 𝔞^{(s)} − ℓ^{(r)} ≥ (1−η)𝔞^{(s)} − Ce^{κT}‖F‖² ≥ −Ce^{κT}‖F‖²`. (ii)–(iii) follow from (i) and
Theorem Q. ∎

**Proposition K (Liouville/Kronecker obstruction to absolute budgets; PROVED, with CITED inputs PNT with de la
Vallée-Poussin error and Selberg–Delange).** Let `s` be periodic (any modulus `q`) and put `r = Λ − s`. Then
```
E^+_T(r) ≥ (4 − o(1)) e^T / T (T → ∞).
```
Compare `𝖠_T = (4+o(1))e^T`. No periodic structured part (in particular no Green–Tao `W`-model `s_W`, R3 Def. G) brings
the absolute one-sided error below the trivial bound by more than the Fejér factor `T`. The absolute Tao split
therefore has exponent `κ = 1`, the trivial one.

*Proof.* Let `X = e^{2T}` and `w(n) := 1 − log n/(2T)`. Fix `θ ∈ (0, π/2)`.
1. *Test functions.* The numbers `{log p}_{p<X}` are ℚ-linearly independent. By Kronecker's theorem (CITED,
  Hardy–Wright Thm 442), for every `η' > 0` and `N` there is `τ_0 > N` with `p^{−iτ_0}` within `η'` of `e^{iθ}` for
  all `p < X`. Hence `|n^{−iτ_0} − e^{iθΩ(n)}| ≤ η'Ω(n)` for `n < X`. Put `F_0(t) := (2T)^{−½}e^{−iτ_0 t}1_{I_T}`. Then
  `‖F_0‖ = 1` and `c_{F_0}(u) = e^{−iτ_0u}(1 − |u|/2T)_+`, so `Re c_{F_0}(log n) → w(n)cos(θΩ(n))` as `η' → 0`. Also
  `|A(F_0)|, |B(F_0)| ≤ 2e^{T/2}/((2T)^{½}|τ_0|) → 0` as `τ_0 → ∞`. Subtracting the orthogonal projection onto
  `span{e^{t/2}, e^{−t/2}}` gives `F ∈ P_T` with `‖F − F_0‖ → 0`. Since
  `|ℓ^{(r)}(F) − ℓ^{(r)}(F_0)| ≤ Σ_{n<X}(2|r_n|/√n)(‖F‖+‖F_0‖)‖F−F_0‖`, we get
  `E^+_T(r) ≥ V_θ := Σ_{n<X}(2r_n/√n) w(n) cos(θΩ(n))`.
2. *The Λ-part.* Primes give `2cosθ Σ_{p<X}(log p)p^{−½}w(p)`. With `θ(x) = x + O(xe^{−c√log x})` (CITED), partial
  summation against `x^{−½}w(x)`, which vanishes at `X` and has derivative `≪ x^{−3/2}`, gives
  `∫_1^X x^{−½}w(x)dx + o(√X/log X) = 4√X/log X + o(√X/log X)`. Prime powers `k ≥ 2` contribute
  `≪ Σ_{p<√X}log p/p ≪ log X`. So the Λ-part equals `(8cosθ + o(1))√X/log X = (4cosθ + o(1))e^T/T`.
3. *The s-part.* A `q`-periodic `s` is a finite combination of `n ↦ 1_{d|n}χ(n/d)` with `d | q` and `χ` a
  character mod `q/d`. Writing `n = dm` gives `e^{iθΩ(n)} = e^{iθΩ(d)}e^{iθΩ(m)}` and `w(dm) = w_{X/d}(m) + O(log d/T)`.
  So it suffices to treat one character. For a character `χ`, `f(n) := χ(n)e^{iθΩ(n)}` is
  multiplicative with `f(p) = χ(p)e^{iθ}`. By Selberg–Delange (CITED: Tenenbaum, *Introduction to analytic and
  probabilistic number theory*, II.5, Thm 5.2 and Cor.), `S_f(x) := Σ_{n≤x}f(n) = c_f x(log x)^{e^{iθ}−1}(1+O(1/log x))`
  for `χ = χ_0`, and `S_f(x) ≪ x(log x)^{−2}` for `χ ≠ χ_0`, because `L(s,χ)^{e^{iθ}}` is holomorphic and zero-free
  near `s = 1`. Partial summation against `x^{−½}w(x)`, using the same kernel as in step 2, gives
  `Σ_{n<X}f(n)n^{−½}w(n) ≪ (log X)^{cosθ−1}·√X/log X + √X(log X)^{−2} = o(√X/log X)`, since `cosθ < 1`. So the
  s-part is `o(e^T/T)`.
4. Hence `E^+_T(r) ≥ (4cosθ − o(1))e^T/T` for every fixed `θ ∈ (0,π/2)`, and `θ → 0` gives the claim. ∎

**Reading of Prop. K.** The harmful test functions of step 1 sit at Kronecker frequencies `τ_0`. These are
astronomically large, `τ_0 ≥ exp(c·π(X))` for alignment of all `p < X`, far above the band `[2πe^{2T}, 2πe^{𝖠_T}]`.
There `m(τ_0) ≈ log τ_0` dominates, so `𝔞^{(s)}(F)` is huge and a *relative* budget (`η > 0` in Cor. S(i)) absorbs
them. Prop. K therefore says that the Tao split never helps in absolute terms: the positivity must be carried by
`m` plus the exact comb in the band, and never by the structured comb. Once `m` is allowed to absorb, the required
estimate is Cor. S(i) with `η > 0`, and for `κ < 1` that is a zero-free half-plane. This is the quantitative form of
R3 Remark B: the only structure in `Λ` beyond the pole is the zero spectrum. Here that statement is measured, since
the error exponent is `2(Θ − ½)` (Theorem Q with Prop. Q2).

**Remark K.1 (HEURISTIC; not load-bearing).** The Selberg–Delange step is the only place where `s` must be
low-complexity. For nilsequence structured parts, one would need `Σ λ-type·e^{iθΩ(n)}·(nilsequence)` cancellation at
rate `o(1/log X)`. That is a Möbius-disjointness statement of Green–Tao type (Ann. Math. 175 (2012)) for the twisted
function `e^{iθΩ(n)}`, which is not in the cited statements as I know them. So Prop. K is proved for periodic `s` only.

## 5. What is genuinely new; weakest step; status

**New to the record (PROVED):**
* Theorem Q and Q′, the graded Weil converse: `λ^A_T ≥ −Ce^{κT}` gives no zeros in `Re s > ½ + κ/2`. This holds in
  primitive and complete form and for general combs. The proof is elementary (translation invariance of `P_T` plus
  Cauchy–Schwarz plus Landau's Laplace step). It supplies a gap-free proof of G2_B9_R3 Theorem N(i) on
  `Λ + ℓ¹_w` (Cor. Q1(ii)).
* Prop. Q2: the exponent `κ/2` is sharp. It corrects the salvage heuristic of G2_P5_R3 (`κ/4`).
* Prop. T: trivial calibration, `κ* ≤ 1` against `Θ ≤ 1`.
* Cor. Q1(iii): the robust EQUIVALENT target `λ^A_T ≥ −e^{o(T)}`, and why O3 and `thm:margin` do not act on it.
* Cor. S: a split's error exponent equals a zero-free half-plane.
* Prop. K: for every periodic structured model, the absolute one-sided pseudorandom error is `≥ (4−o(1))e^T/T`.

**Not achieved:** no mechanism, and no sign for any `T > 0.8`. `κ* ≤ 2Θ − 1` in general is OPEN.

**Weakest step.** Among the load-bearing steps, the weakest is step 3 of Prop. K, the Selberg–Delange asymptotic for
`χ(n)e^{iθΩ(n)}`. It is CITED in its standard form (Tenenbaum II.5), and the partial summation against
`x^{−½}(1 − log x/log X)` is routine, but I did not re-derive the uniformity of the `O(1/log x)` term. Prop. K is not
needed for Theorem Q, which is self-contained. Step 3 of Theorem Q (existence of `ψ ∈ P_ε` with `Φ(ρ_0 − ½) ≠ 0`)
rests on linear independence of six distinct exponentials on `(−ε, ε)`. I checked it, and `z_0 ≠ ±½` is guaranteed by
`0 < Re ρ_0 < 1`.

**Status: dead end for the G2 mechanism, with a proved graded converse and an obstruction.** The ∀T arrow is not
touched. Row D keeps no label.
