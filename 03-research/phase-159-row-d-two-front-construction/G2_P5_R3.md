## §5 Addendum (independent re-check, same date): one sign slip, one extra falsifier

**(a) Sign slip in Theorem N(i), harmless.** The memo writes "`a = Λ − β` with `β := −β_b`". Since
`u_{Λ+b} = u_Λ − β_b`, the distribution entering Lemma L is `u_a = u_Λ − β` with `β = +β_b`. The memo mixes the comb
variable and the distribution variable here. `𝒮′` is closed under negation, so nothing changes. It should still be fixed
in the text.

**(b) Transposition in Lemma L step 2 (re-derived).** `⟨(−∂²+¼)^k(θu_Λ), χe^{−s·}⟩ = ⟨θu_Λ, (−∂²+¼)^k(χe^{−s·})⟩` pairs
a non-tempered measure with a test that is not compactly supported. It holds for `Re s > ½`: truncate with `χ_1(u/R)`.
The boundary terms are `O(R^{c}e^{−(Re s−½)R}) → 0` because `d|θu_Λ|` has growth `e^{u/2}`. Also,
`(−∂²+¼)^k(θu_Λ) = θ(v + (−∂²+¼)^kβ) + (compactly supported)` is itself tempered, so its distributional Laplace
transform is defined on `Re s > 0` and agrees with the classical one on `Re s > ½`. VERIFIED; the step is sound.

**(c) Extra falsifier: a modified comb whose zeros are all on the line under RH.** `ζ·E` with the factor
`E(s) = 1 − 2^{½−s}`. Its extra zeros `½ + iτ_m`, `τ_m = 2πm/log 2`, lie on `Re s = ½`, so this is a harder test
than `ζ_odd`. The comb perturbation is `b_{2^k} = −log 2 · 2^{k/2}`, so `b_n/√n ≡ −log 2` on powers of 2. Then
`b ∉ ℓ¹_w`, but `β_b = −log 2 (Σ_{k∈ℤ} δ_{k log 2} − δ_0)` is tempered. By Poisson,
`D_b = log 2 − 2π Σ_m δ_{τ_m}`, a locally finite measure. If no `τ_m` (`m ≠ 0`) is an ordinate of ζ (not known; this
membership in `𝒯_meas` is therefore conditional), Theorem R′(i) says that `ζ·E` is not ∀T-positive. Under RH the direct
computation gives `û_{Λ+b} = 2πμ_Λ + 2πΣ_m δ_{τ_m} − log 2`, whose continuous part `−log 2 < 0` rules out positive
definiteness. So negativity is true, and `sup D_b = log 2 > 0` is the constant `E` contributes through its own explicit
formula, which the archimedean term of `u_Λ` does not absorb. The theorem asserts negativity where negativity is true.
PASS. Even when some `τ_m` does hit an ordinate, the constant `−log 2` still breaks positivity. The obstruction is
robust on this example, but the memo's `𝒯_meas` hypothesis cannot be checked for it; this is a scope remark, not a gap.

**(d) Reverse Lemma-L logic on the falsifiers.** Landau's argument in step 4 needs only a Dirichlet series with
meromorphic continuation. For `Ξ_a`, `Ξ_Q` (class number > 1) and DMV systems with off-line zeros, the analogue says
"bounded-below primitive bottoms ⟹ no zeros right of ½", and its conclusion is false for each of them. So each of them
must have unbounded-below bottoms. That agrees with the known failure of positivity. No contradiction and no false
positivity. PASS.
