## §1 G1_B2_R2 — Kronecker line, tail-factorizable Frobenius, Hecke/Shimura suspension

**Checked and correct.**
* §1 correction: for `G=Q^×`, `m_{−1}^*dx=−dx`, so `V_c=0` in degree 1; for `Q^×_{>0}` the constant form survives. Correct.
* Theorem S(i)–(ii): `|φ(s)−φ(s′)| ≤ ‖w‖‖u(n/n′)w−w‖` gives uniform continuity on the dense `log G`; Bochner gives `ν`;
  Fourier–Stieltjes uniqueness on compact `Ĝ` gives `μ_w=ι_*ν`, so `w=E(L)w`. Converse by Lusin–Souslin (CITED) and
  dominated convergence. Correct.
* Lemma P, "at most one point": `ι_{B_j}(γ)=ι_{B_j}(γ′)`, `γ≠γ′`, needs `(γ−γ′)log p∈2πZ∖{0}` for every `p∈B_j`; two
  distinct primes cannot both satisfy it (`log p/log q∉Q`). Two distinct points with mass `→1` is impossible. Correct.
* Cor E1: for fixed `χ′∈T^{P∖{p}}` the `L`-section is at most one point (`ι_{P∖{p}}` injective, ≥2 primes remain);
  atomless `μ_{w_p}` gives 0; density of elementary tensors. Correct.
* Cor D2: `SL_2(Q_p)`-invariants of irreducible unitary `π_p` form a `GL_2`-stable subspace (`SL_2` normal); nonzero ⟹
  everything ⟹ `π_p` factors through `det`, one-dimensional, contradicting genericity. Howe–Moore on `SL_2(Q_p)` via
  `a_p^{2k}=central·diag(p^k,p^{−k})` gives vanishing matrix coefficients, so `U_p` has Rajchman spectral measure,
  atomless by Wiener. Cor E1 then applies since `u(p)` acts on the `p`-th factor only. Correct modulo CITED inputs
  (Flath, JL genericity, Howe–Moore, description of `H¹_{(2)}`).

**False statement (PROVED false; not fatal to the verdict, fatal to Theorem E(i) as written).**
Theorem E(i): "if `Γ(ξ)=∅` then `V_c=0`", with `Γ(ξ):={γ: ∏_{j≥1}μ^{(j)}_{ξ_j}({ι_{B_j}(γ)})>0}` over **all** `j`.
The proof asserts "`A_w=Γ(ξ)` because `w_j=ξ_j` for `j>J`". False: `A_w` depends on the first `J` factors of `w`, not of `ξ`.

*Counterexample.* `P_0=∅`, `B_j={p_j}`; `H_1=C²`, `u_1(p_1)=diag(p_1^{iγ_0},−p_1^{iγ_0})`, `ξ_1=e_2`; `H_j=C`,
`u_j(p_j)=p_j^{iγ_0}`, `ξ_j=1` for `j≥2`. Then `μ^{(1)}_{ξ_1}=δ_{−p_1^{iγ_0}}`, which has mass 0 at `ι_{B_1}(γ_0)`;
the tail (two primes suffice) forces `γ=γ_0` for any positive product; hence `Γ(ξ)=∅`. But
`w=e_1⊗ξ_2⊗ξ_3⊗⋯` lies in the incomplete tensor product (differs from `⊗ξ_j` in one factor) and `u(n)w=n^{iγ_0}w`,
so `w∈V_c≠0`. Lemma P's "tail statement" is misphrased the same way: `A` is *contained in* a tail-determined set, not
equal to it.

*Repair (PROVED).* `Γ_tail(ξ):={γ: ∃j_0, ∏_{j≥j_0}μ^{(j)}_{ξ_j}({ι_{B_j}(γ)})>0}`. It depends only on the equivalence
class of `(ξ_j)` (as the incomplete tensor product does); Lemma P gives `|Γ_tail|≤1`; every `A_w⊂Γ_tail`. With
`Γ_tail` in place of `Γ(ξ)`, E(i)–(ii) hold, and so do "at most one eigenvalue per summand", Cor E2, Prop R, N-e.
E(ii) as stated is in fact true (`Γ(ξ)={γ_0}` ⟹ `Γ_tail={γ_0}`). Cor D2 uses only E1 and is unaffected.

**Other remarks.**
* Name collision: "N-e" is used by B2 (entanglement) and B6 (super-critical resolution) for different conditions.
* Scope: N-e is PROVED only in the suspension class (unitary `Q^×_{>0}` representation + its suspension). Nothing on
  correspondence-valued Frobenius (finite-level `T_p`, Connes–Consani `Ψ(λ)`). The three-way conflict is HEURISTIC.

**Verdict: excluded.** The one carrier constructed (Shimura lamination with Hecke suspension) has zero suspended `H¹`
(Cor D2, correct modulo CITED), so it fails OT-1, OT-2, OT-4 by the memo's own theorem. Theorem E(i) as stated is
false and must be repaired before anything enters the record.

**Salvageable.** Theorem S, repaired Theorem E, Cor E1, Cor D2: a correct wall for the Deninger-suspension class. The
dilation question (does a correspondence-valued Frobenius dilate to a unitary representation?) is the right next test.
