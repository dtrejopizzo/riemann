## 2. The renormalisation structure: three exact statements

### B1 — one `T`-free functional over nested convex sets (complete form)

**Lemma B1** *(PROVED; a restatement of `thm:Zexh`(i))*. Put `Φ(w) := inf_{τ∈ℝ} f_w(τ)` for even `w ∈ C_c^∞(ℝ)` with `w(0)=1`.
Then:
1. `Φ` does not depend on `T`, and it is concave and upper semicontinuous for uniform convergence of `w` together with
  `‖w‖_{L¹(K du + cosh)}` and `Σ v_n|w(log n)|`.
2. For every `T ≥ ½log 2`: `λ^W_T = sup{Φ(w) : w ∈ 𝒲_T^reg}`.
3. **(Exact annulus renormalisation.)** For `½log2 ≤ T < T′`, `𝒲_{T′}^reg = {w ∈ 𝒲_T^reg : w ≡ 1 on the annulus 2T ≤ |u| ≤ 2T′}`.
  Hence
  `λ^W_{T′} = sup{ Φ(w) : w ∈ 𝒲_T^reg, w|_{2T≤|u|≤2T′} ≡ 1 }.`

*Proof.* (1) In eq:fw, `T` appears nowhere. `f_w(τ)` is affine in `w`, so `Φ` is an infimum of affine functionals,
hence concave. Upper semicontinuity: each `w ↦ f_w(τ)` is continuous for the stated norms, because
`|1 − w cos| K ≤ (|1−cos| + |1−w|)K` and `K ∈ L¹` away from 0, and an infimum of continuous functions is usc.
(2) is `thm:Zexh`(i). (3) is the definition of `𝒲^reg`: flatness on `[−2T′, 2T′]` is flatness on `[−2T, 2T]` plus
flatness on the annulus. ∎

*Reading.* The **entire `T`-dependence of row D (complete form) is a constraint set**: `w ≡ 1` on a growing interval.
The functional equation asked for by the angle is (3). Its content is that the upward step is an *equality
constraint*, not an inequality. Imposing `w = 1` on the annulus switches on, **with the exact weight `v_n`**, every new
contact `n` with `2T ≤ log n < 2T′`, through the term `−v_n w(log n)cos(τ log n)`. It also switches on the polar mass
`2cosh(u/2)` on the annulus and switches off `½(1−w)K` there. (Compare `ν_w` in eq:nuw with `w = 1`: this is exactly the
arithmetic annulus measure `κ = 2cosh(u/2)𝟏_A du − Σ_{log n ∈ A}(v_n/2)(δ_{log n}+δ_{−log n})`.)

**Corollary B1′ — the exact form of W15** *(PROVED)*. The certificate sets `𝒞_T(c) := {w ∈ 𝒲_T^reg : Φ(w) ≥ c}` satisfy
`𝒞_{T′}(c) = 𝒞_T(c) ∩ {w|_A ≡ 1}`. They form a *decreasing* family. There is no map `𝒞_T → 𝒞_{T′}`. Any
`w ∈ 𝒞_T(c)` that is not identically 1 on `A` gives no information about `𝒞_{T′}(c)`, and by `thm:O` (non-attainment)
the optimisers of `sup Φ` over `𝒲_T` do not exist. So "the optimal certificate at `T`" is not an object that can be
propagated. *Proof:* B1(3) and `thm:O`. ∎

**Remark B1″ — O3 in this language** *(PROVED, immediate)*. For `w ∈ 𝒲_{T′}`, changing one `v_{n₀}` (`log n₀ < 2T′`) by `ε`
changes `f_w` by at most `|ε|` uniformly, and so changes `λ^W_{T′}` by at most `|ε|`. Together with
`rem:resweights` (a change of size `≈ e^{−14.4T}` flips the sign) and `thm:margin`, any proof of `sup Φ ≥ 0` over
`𝒲_{T′}` must be exact in each `v_n` to precision `λ^W_{T′} ≤ 4·10⁹ T′ e^{−15.449T′}`.

### B2 — scale covariance (the renormalisation group proper)

**Proposition B2** *(PROVED)*. For `T ≥ ½log2` and `r ≥ 1`:

  `λ^W_{rT} = r · sup_{w ∈ 𝒲_T^reg} inf_{σ∈ℝ} Σ_ρ ŵ(σ − rγ_ρ).`

In words, enlarging the window by `r` is the same as keeping the window and dilating the zero set by `r`, then
multiplying by `r`.

*Proof.* `w ↦ w_r := w(·/r)` is a bijection `𝒲_T^reg → 𝒲_{rT}^reg`, and `ŵ_r(t) = r ŵ(rt)`, an entire function of
exponential type, so the formula holds at complex `γ_ρ` as well. By `thm:Z`(ii),
`f_{w_r}(τ) = r Σ_ρ ŵ(rτ − rγ_ρ)`. Take the infimum over `τ` (equivalently over `σ = rτ`), then the supremum over
`w`, and apply `thm:Zexh`(i) at the window `rT ≥ ½log2`. ∎

*Reading.* The RG flow acts on the zero set, `Z ↦ rZ`. It has no fixed point, since the zero density
`(2π)^{−1}log(γ/2π)` is not scale invariant. The certificate at a large window sees `rZ` through a kernel of fixed
resolution. The dense regime (many zeros per resolution cell), where the archimedean mean density wins, and the
sparse regime, where `thm:finZ`-type negativity lives, are separated by a band that moves with `r`. This is
`prop:band` / `prop:align` seen from the zero side. **HEURISTIC** for the band description; the identity itself is
PROVED.

### B3 — one-sided dilation stability of minimisers (complete form; no regularity)

**Theorem B3** *(PROVED)*. Let `F_T` be a minimiser of `W_T` on the unit sphere of `L²(I_T)`. It exists by
Bombieri 2000 Thm 3 (CITED, as used in P47 `cor:RH`). Put `g(r) := W_{rT}(U_rF_T)`, `r > 0`. Then

  `g(r) ≥ g(1) = λ^W_T` for every `r ∈ (0, 1]`.

Consequently, whenever the left derivative exists, `g′(1⁻) ≤ 0` (the **one-sided Pohozaev inequality**). For every
`r > 0`, `λ^W_{rT} ≤ g(r)`.

*Proof.* `U_r` maps the form domain onto the form domain unitarily (`lem:contT`). Hence
`g(r) ≥ λ^W_{rT}`, and for `r ≤ 1`, `λ^W_{rT} ≥ λ^W_T` by `lem:monoT`. For `r < 1`, `(g(r) − g(1))/(r − 1) ≤ 0`. ∎

**Corollary B3′ — the virial expression** *(PROVED under hypothesis (Reg′); the formula is on record, D.133 and
R153_012 §5.1, there for the primitive form on a smooth core)*.

(Reg′): the autocorrelation `c_{F_T}` has a right derivative at each `log n`, `n ∈ 𝒩_T`.

Under (Reg′), `g′(1⁻)` exists and equals

  `V_T(F_T) = −(1/2π)∫ τ m′(τ)|F̂_T(τ)|² dτ + Σ_{n∈𝒩_T} v_n (log n) c′_{F_T}(log n⁺) + 2Re(A B̄) + Re(A₁B̄ − A B̄₁),`

with `A₁ = ∫ tF e^{t/2}` and `B₁ = ∫ tF e^{−t/2}`. Hence `V_T(F_T) ≤ 0`.

*Proof.* `g(r) = (1/2π)∫ m(τ/r)|F̂|² − Σ_n v_n c_F(log n / r) + 2Re(A(U_rF)\overline{B(U_rF)})`. The comb term can be
summed over all `n`, since `c_F = 0` on `|u| ≥ 2T`, and for `r < 1` contacts with `log n ≥ 2T` contribute 0.
- Archimedean term: `|(m(τ/r) − m(τ))/(r−1)| ≤ M₁|log r|/|r−1|`, with `M₁ = sup|x m′(x)| < ∞` (`lem:contT`(i)), which
  is bounded near `r = 1`. Dominated convergence gives the derivative `−(1/2π)∫ τ m′|F̂|²`.
- Comb term: (Reg′) and the chain rule, `d/dr[−c_F(log n/r)]_{r=1⁻} = (log n) c′_F(log n⁺)`.
- Polar term: `A(U_rF) = r^{1/2}∫F(s)e^{rs/2}ds` is smooth in `r` for `F ∈ L²(I_T)`, with
  `A′(1) = ½A + ½A₁`, and likewise `B′(1) = ½B − ½B₁`. Expanding gives the stated term.

Then apply B3. ∎

*Zero-side reading* (CONDITIONAL on RH and on hypothesis (b) of `lem:roughEF` for `F_T`). Here
`g(r) = r Σ_γ |F̂_T(rγ)|²`, and B3 says that compressing the zeros towards the origin never lowers the minimiser's
energy: `Σ_γ r|F̂_T(rγ)|² ≥ Σ_γ |F̂_T(γ)|²` for `r ≤ 1`. Formally `V = Σ_γ ∂_γ(γ|F̂_T(γ)|²) ≤ 0`.

**What is new in B3 relative to the record.** D.133 and R153_012 give the *two-sided* rate `dλ/dT` at simple
eigenvalues on a smooth core, and R153_012 measures that the endpoint form vanishes at one minimiser. B3 needs no
simplicity, no differentiability of `λ` and no regularity of `F_T`. It gives a sign-definite constraint on every
minimiser at every `T`, unconditionally: `g ≥ λ^W_T` on `(0,1]`. It is **not** a sign of `λ`.

## 3. The candidate mechanism built from B1–B3, and why it closes

### 3.1 The integrated renormalisation identity

**Proposition R** *(PROVED; tautological by design)*. For `T < T′ = rT`, let `F_{T′}` be a minimiser at `T′` and set
`D_r(T) := W_T(U_{1/r}F_{T′}) − λ^W_{T′} ≥ 0`. Then

  `λ^W_{T′} = λ^W_T − [λ^W_T − W_T(U_{1/r}F_{T′})] − D_r(T)`, where the bracket is `≤ 0`.

So `λ^W_{T′} ≥ λ^W_T − D_r(T)`, with equality exactly when `U_{1/r}F_{T′}` minimises at `T`.

*Proof.* `W_T(U_{1/r}F_{T′}) ≥ λ^W_T` by the variational principle, and `D_r ≥ 0` by B3 applied at `T′` with
dilation `1/r ≤ 1`. ∎

This is the "functional equation between `T` and `T+δ`". **It is not an induction.** It needs the *future*
minimiser `F_{T′}`, and `D_r(T)` is the dilation defect of that future minimiser. `D_r(T) ≥ λ^W_T − λ^W_{T′}` always
holds, so the bound is sharp only if the future is known. Differentiated (under (Reg′) and Lipschitz continuity of
`λ^W`, OPEN in general, see §6), it is the record's rate formula `T·dλ^W/dT = V_T(F_T)`.

### 3.2 The Grönwall form

**Proposition G** *(EQUIVALENT to RH; PROVED as an equivalence)*. Assume Zhu's `λ^W_{0.8} > 0` (CITED, Zhu 2026
Thm 1.2) and that `λ^W` is absolutely continuous on compact subsets of `[0.8, ∞)` (hypothesis (AC), OPEN). Put
`c(T) := −(λ^W_T)′/λ^W_T` wherever `λ^W_T > 0`. Then:

  RH ⟺ for every `T₁ > 0.8`, `∫_{0.8}^{T₁} c(s) ds < ∞`.

*Proof.* (⇐) Under (AC), on any interval where `λ > 0` we have `log λ(T) = log λ(0.8) − ∫ c`, which stays bounded below
on `[0.8, T₁]`. Therefore `λ` cannot reach 0 at a finite first time `T_c` (continuity, `lem:contT`), because
`∫^{T_c} c = +∞` would be needed there. So `λ^W > 0` for all `T`, which is RH by Weil. (⇒) Under RH, `λ^W_T > 0` for
every `T` (P47 `cor:RH` proof, strictness). By (AC) and continuity, `log λ` is finite and absolutely continuous on
compacta, so `∫ c < ∞`. ∎

This is the only way the bootstrap produces a monotone invariant: `J(T) := log λ^W_T + ∫_{0.8}^T c` is constant.
**This is circular.** The integrability of `c` is exactly the non-vanishing of `λ`, i.e. R157_002 row 17 (first
crossing / clopen, EQUIV).

### 3.3 Monotone invariants with a structural rate: excluded or conjecturally false

A *structural* monotone invariant would be `J_κ(T) = λ^W_T · e^{K(T)}` with an explicit `K` whose monotonicity is proved
without knowing the sign. That monotonicity means `c(T) ≤ K′(T)`.

- **Constant rate `K = κT`.** By R153_012 §6 and phase-46 Prop. 143.E (CITED from the record), a uniform modulus is
  equivalent under RH to LP-143 (vertical zero repulsion, `liminf (γ_{n+1}−γ_n)log γ_n > 0`). GUE predicts LP-143 is
  **false**, and Conrey–Ghosh–Gonek give an unconditional normalised `liminf ≤ 0.5172` under RH. So `J_κ` is
  **conjecturally not monotone** for any `κ`. (For the primitive form this is phase 46 §4. The complete form inherits
  the same Lehmer-pair mechanism. Label HEURISTIC for the transfer to `λ^W`.)
- **Any explicit `K` derived from class properties of the comb.** This is EXCLUDED by `cor:void` / `thm:finmod`. The
  modified combs of `thm:finmod`(ii) keep every classical property and have a first crossing `T_c` of `λ^W(a)` with
  `λ^W(a) → 0`, so `c_a(T) → +∞` as `T ↑ T_c` for them (or `λ^W(a)` has a singular drop). No `K′` bound that holds for
  the whole class can hold. (PROVED, from the cited theorems and B1″.)
- **A bound on `c(T)` from the rate formula term by term.** EXCLUDED by the measured `10⁵–10⁶` cancellation
  (R153_012 §5.1, DIAGNOSTIC) and by D.133 (PROVED there: unbounded endpoint form on the form domain, no uniform
  signed Hadamard induction).

## 4. How W15 is — and is not — avoided (the angle's explicit demand)

W15 (P47 `cor:mono`, `lem:monoT`, PATH-TO-D W15): a certificate at `T` says nothing about `T′ > T`.

1. **Formally avoided.** B1(3), Proposition R and Proposition G never use "certificate at `T` ⟹ certificate at `T′`".
  They use (i) an *equality constraint* on the annulus (B1), (ii) the *future* minimiser (R), or (iii) a differential
  identity holding pointwise in `T` (G). None of these is an induction.
2. **Not avoided in content.** In each case the quantifier `∀T` reappears inside the needed input:
  - (B1): for each `T′`, a `w` flat on the annulus with `Φ(w) ≥ 0`. This is the sign at `T′`.
  - (R): control of `D_r(T)`, which needs `F_{T′}`.
  - (G): `c ∈ L¹_loc` on `[0.8, ∞)`, i.e. `∀T`.

  The per-`T` input is decidable at each window (`prop:finite`, `prop:decide`), exactly as the sign is. It is **the
  same decision**: at a would-be first crossing `T_c`, `c(T) → ∞` if and only if `λ^W_{T_c} = 0`.
3. **Conclusion for the angle.** There is no functional equation between `T` and `T+δ` that transfers a *lower* bound
  upward using only the new contacts. The new contacts enter B1(3) with exact weights, but they enter as a constraint
  on the unknown optimiser, and the optimiser is never attained (`thm:O`). So "the optimal certificate at `T`" is not
  a well-defined object to renormalise.

## 7. What is genuinely new, and the weakest step

**New (small, PROVED):**
- (a) The `T`-free concave functional `Φ` and the exact annulus renormalisation `𝒲_{T′} = 𝒲_T ∩ {w|_A ≡ 1}`. This
  makes W15 an exact statement about nested convex constraint sets, with non-attainment (`thm:O`) as the reason no
  optimal certificate propagates.
- (b) The scale covariance B2: the RG acts on the zero set as `Z ↦ rZ`.
- (c) The one-sided dilation stability of minimisers, B3, which needs no regularity or simplicity. It gives the
  Pohozaev sign `V_T(F_T) ≤ 0` unconditionally at every `T`.

**Not new:** the rate/virial formula (D.133, R153_012), the equivalence "uniform rate ⟺ RH with a modulus ⟺ LP-143"
(phase 46 doc 143, R153_012 §6), and first crossing / clopen (R157_002 row 17).

**Weakest step (of the angle, not of the lemmas):** the angle requires a structural per-`T` bound on the renormalisation
defect (`c(T)` or `D_r(T)`) using only Γ and the exact new contacts.
- §3.3 shows that no class-level bound exists (`cor:void`).
- A constant-rate bound is conjecturally false (LP-143, GUE).
