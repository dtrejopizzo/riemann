## §1 Theorem S — the adelic stratification law

### 1.1 Setting

`A_Q = ℝ × A_f`. The **archimedean scaling flow** is `x·t := (e^t x_∞, x_f)` for `t ∈ ℝ`. On `C_0(A_Q)` it acts by
`(α_t f)(x) := f(x·(−t))`, a strongly continuous action by automorphisms. Let `G` be a locally compact group acting on
`A_Q` by homeomorphisms `x ↦ g x` such that:

  (G1) `g(x·t) = (gx)·t` for all `g, t, x`, and (G2) `g Y₀ = Y₀`, where `Y₀ := {x ∈ A_Q : x_∞ ≠ 0}`.

Examples:
* `G = Q^×` (diagonal multiplication);
* `G = Ẑ^×`, `Q^×_{>0}`, or products of these;
* `G = A_Q^×` (multiplication by ideles).

All satisfy (G1) because multiplication is commutative and associative, and (G2) because the archimedean component of
a unit is nonzero.

`Y₀` is open (it is `ℝ^× × A_f`). `Z := A_Q ∖ Y₀ = {0} × A_f` is closed. Both are `G`- and flow-invariant. The flow
**fixes every point of `Z`**.

A **covariant representation** of the system is a quadruple `(H, π, u, U)` with the following properties:
* `π` is a nondegenerate representation of `C_0(A_Q)`;
* `u` is a strongly continuous unitary representation of `G` with `u_g π(f) u_g^* = π(g·f)`, where
  `(g·f)(x) = f(g^{−1}x)`;
* `U` is a strongly continuous unitary representation of `ℝ` with `U_t π(f) U_t^* = π(α_t f)` and
  `U_t u_g U_t^* = u_g`.

Such quadruples correspond exactly to the nondegenerate representations of the iterated full crossed product
`𝒞 := (C_0(A_Q) ⋊ G) ⋊_α ℝ`, where `α` fixes `G` (CITED standard; Williams, *Crossed products of C\*-algebras*, 2.40).
Through the GNS construction they correspond to **all positive functionals on `𝒞`**, normal or not. Flow values
`ψ(u(f)^*u(f))` are read on the canonical multipliers `u(f)`, as in R4 Rem 1.1a.

### 1.2 Statement and proof

**Theorem S (`PROVED`, modulo `CITED` Stone–von Neumann).** Let `(H, π, u, U)` be a covariant representation. Put
`H_arch := [π(C_0(Y₀))H]` and `H_inert := H_arch^⊥`.

*(i)* Both subspaces reduce `π`, `u` and `U`.

*(ii)* `U|_{H_arch}` is unitarily equivalent to `λ ⊗ 1_𝒦` (the regular representation of `ℝ`, some multiplicity). In
particular every vector of `H_arch` has an absolutely continuous spectral measure.

*(iii)* On `H_inert`: `π(f) = 0` for `f ∈ C_0(Y₀)`, so `π` factors through restriction `C_0(A_Q) → C_0(Z)`. Moreover
`U_t` commutes with `π(C_0(A_Q))` and with `u(G)`.

*(iv)* For every vector `ξ`, `ν_ξ = ν_{P_arch ξ} + ν_{P_inert ξ}`. The first term is absolutely continuous. Every atom
of `ν_ξ` is an atom of `ν_{P_inert ξ}`.

*Proof.*
* **(i).** `C_0(Y₀)` is a closed ideal of `C_0(A_Q)`. It is invariant under `f ↦ g·f` (by (G2)) and under `α_t` (the
  flow preserves `Y₀`).
  * `π(h)π(f)ξ = π(hf)ξ` with `hf ∈ C_0(Y₀)`.
  * `u_gπ(f)ξ = π(g·f)u_gξ`.
  * `U_tπ(f)ξ = π(α_tf)U_tξ`.

  So `H_arch` is invariant under the self-adjoint sets `π(C_0(A_Q))`, `u(G)` and `U(ℝ)`, and hence so is its
  orthocomplement.
* **(ii), the log-coordinate.** `ℓ(x) := log|x_∞|` is continuous on `Y₀`, and `ℓ(x·t) = ℓ(x) + t`. **It need not be
  `G`-invariant, and it is not used with `G` at all.**
* **(ii), the Weyl pair.**
  * `π₀ := π|_{C_0(Y₀)}` is nondegenerate on `H_arch` by definition. It therefore extends uniquely to a unital
  representation `π̄₀` of the multiplier algebra `C_b(Y₀)` on `H_arch`, with `π̄₀(g)π₀(f)ξ = π₀(gf)ξ` (CITED
  standard).
  * Put `V_s := π̄₀(e^{isℓ})`, a unitary representation of `ℝ` on `H_arch`.
  * **Strong continuity.** For `f ∈ C_c(Y₀)`, `ℓ` is bounded on `supp f`, so `e^{isℓ}f → f` uniformly as `s → 0`, and
  `V_sπ₀(f)ξ → π₀(f)ξ`. These vectors are total, and `V` is unitary.
  * **Covariance.** `α_t(e^{isℓ}f)(x) = e^{is(ℓ(x)−t)}f(x·(−t)) = e^{−ist}e^{isℓ(x)}(α_tf)(x)`. Hence
  `U_tV_sπ₀(f)ξ = π₀(α_t(e^{isℓ}f))U_tξ = e^{−ist}V_sπ₀(α_tf)U_tξ = e^{−ist}V_sU_tπ₀(f)ξ`, i.e.
  `U_tV_s = e^{−ist}V_sU_t` on `H_arch`.
  * By the Stone–von Neumann theorem (CITED; Folland, *A course in abstract harmonic analysis*, §6.5; valid in any
  Hilbert space, the representation being a direct sum of Schrödinger representations), `(U, V)` is a multiple of
  the Schrödinger pair. There `U` acts by translation on `L²(ℝ, 𝒦)`, whose spectral measures are
  `(2π)^{−1}‖ξ̂(τ)‖²dτ`.
* **(iii).**
  * **`π` vanishes on the ideal.** For `f ∈ C_0(Y₀)`, `π(f)H_inert ⊂ H_inert` by (i), and `π(f)H ⊂ H_arch` by
  definition. So `π(f)H_inert = 0`.
  * **`π` factors through `Z`.** For `f ∈ C_0(A_Q)`, `α_tf − f` vanishes on `Z`, because the flow fixes `Z`
  pointwise. A function in `C_0(A_Q)` vanishing on the closed set `Z` lies in `C_0(Y₀)`. Hence on `H_inert`,
  `U_tπ(f)U_t^* = π(α_tf) = π(f)`.
  * **`U` commutes with `u`.** `U_tu_gU_t^* = u_g` is part of covariance.
* **(iv).** The decomposition is orthogonal and `U`-invariant, and spectral measures add. Apply (ii). ∎

**Remark S0 (where Connes' objects sit; `PROVED` for the placement, `CITED` for the objects).**
* `L²(C_Q)`, `L²` of ideles, and every representation obtained by restricting functions to ideles are supported on
  `Y₀`: ideles have `x_∞ ≠ 0`. Theorem S therefore contains R4 Cor Δ3(b) in its application to `C_Q`.
* The periodic orbits of `A_Q/Q^×` with `x_p = 0` (length `log p`; Connes 1999 §VII, CITED) have `x_∞ ≠ 0`, so they
  lie in `Y₀`. The prime contributions of the trace formula therefore come from the **Lebesgue** part. That is
  consistent: they are distributional traces in `t`, not atoms in `τ`.
* The inert stratum `Z` is the fixed-point set of the whole archimedean `ℝ^×_+`. In Connes' local trace formula that
  is where the archimedean term `∫′_{ℝ^×}h(u^{−1})/|1−u|d^×u` originates (`HEURISTIC` identification: I did not
  re-derive the localisation of the archimedean distribution to `Z`).

**Remark S1 (number fields; `PROVED` by the same proof).** For a number field `K` of degree `n`, let the flow act on
every archimedean coordinate by `x_v ↦ e^{t/n}x_v`, so the idele norm scales by `e^t`. Put
`N(x) := Σ_{v|∞}|x_v|_v^{1/n_v}`, which scales by `e^{t/n}`, and `ℓ := n·log N` on `Y₀ := {x : x_∞ ≠ 0}` (some
archimedean coordinate nonzero). Then `ℓ` is continuous and equivariant, and Theorem S holds verbatim with
`Z = {0} × A_{K,f}`. Theorem S is therefore **field-uniform**. It is a statement about spectral type and never mentions
zeros (OT-10 row).

### 1.3 Theorem S′ — the Maharam law (general form)

**Theorem S′ (`PROVED`, modulo `CITED` Stone–von Neumann).**

*(C\*-form)* Let `X` be a locally compact space with a continuous flow `x·t` and a group `G` of homeomorphisms
commuting with it. Let `X₀ ⊂ X` be an open, flow- and `G`-invariant set carrying a continuous `ℓ : X₀ → ℝ` with
`ℓ(x·t) = ℓ(x) + t`. The function `ℓ` need not be `G`-invariant. Then in every covariant representation of
`(C_0(X) ⋊ G) ⋊ ℝ`, the flow is Lebesgue on `[π(C_0(X₀))H]`. If moreover the flow fixes `X ∖ X₀` pointwise, the
complement is inert in the sense of S(iii).

*(von Neumann form)* Let `(X, m)` be a standard measure space with a non-singular flow and a countable group `G` of
non-singular maps commuting with it. Let `ℓ` be a measurable function with `ℓ(x·t) = ℓ(x) + t` a.e. Then in every
covariant representation `(π, u, U)` of `(L^∞(X, m) ⋊ G, ℝ)` **with `π` normal**, `U` is a multiple of the regular
representation.

*Proof.*
* **C\*-form.** The proof of Theorem S uses only the ideal `C_0(X₀)`, `ℓ`, and the fact that `Z` is fixed pointwise.
* **von Neumann form.**
  * `V_s := π(e^{isℓ})`. Normality of `π` gives strong continuity: `e^{isℓ} → 1` boundedly a.e. as `s → 0`, hence
  σ-strongly in `L^∞`, and `π` is σ-strongly continuous.
  * The same computation gives `U_tV_s = e^{−ist}V_sU_t`. Then apply Stone–von Neumann. ∎

**Example (the core of a Krieger factor).** `X = Z × ℝ` with measure `ν ⊗ e^s ds`, and `G` acting by
`g(z,s) = (gz, s − log D_g(z))`, where `D_g = d(ν∘g)/dν`. This is the Maharam extension. The flow translates `s`, and
`ℓ = s`. The von Neumann form says that every normal covariant representation of the core `L^∞(Z×ℝ) ⋊ G`, with its
dual (trace-scaling) flow, has Lebesgue flow. This generalises R1 Cor 5 (no eigenvector of the normalised dual action on
`L²(M, τ)`), which has priority, to every normal covariant representation.

## §2 Proposition S2 — the inert stratum is free

**Proposition S2 (`PROVED`).**

*(a)* Let `ρ` be any representation of `C_0(Z) ⋊ G`, for example a point evaluation at `0 ∈ A_Q` with `u ≡ 1`. Let
`W` be any strongly continuous unitary group on a Hilbert space `L`. Then
`(H_ρ ⊗ L, ρ∘res ⊗ 1, u_ρ ⊗ 1, 1 ⊗ W)` is a covariant representation with `H_arch = 0`.

*(b)* Every tempered positive measure `σ` on `ℝ̂` is the flow measure of a flow-finite datum inside such a
representation.

*(c)* Conversely, on `H_inert` every covariant representation is a representation of the C\*-algebra
`(C_0(Z) ⋊ G) ⊗ C*(ℝ)`, i.e. two commuting representations. The flow measures of vectors are the `C_0(ℝ̂)`-marginals
of its positive functionals. For every state `ω` of `C_0(Z) ⋊ G` and every probability `σ` on `ℝ̂`, the product state
`ω ⊗ σ` exists. Its flow measure is `σ`, whatever `ω` is.

*Proof.*
* **(a).** Covariance is immediate: `α_t` is trivial on `C_0(Z)` (flow fixes `Z`), and `1 ⊗ W` commutes with
  `ρ ⊗ 1`.
* **(b).** Take `L = L²(σ)`, `W_t = e^{iτt}`, and vectors `ξ_j = v ⊗ 1_{[j,j+1)}` with `‖v‖ = 1`. Then
  `Σ_j ν_{ξ_j} = σ`, which is flow-finite since `σ` is tempered.
* **(c).** By S(iii), `(ρ, u)` and `U` commute on `H_inert`. Commuting representations of `A` and `B` are
  representations of `A ⊗_max B`, and `⊗_max = ⊗_min` because `C*(ℝ) ≅ C_0(ℝ̂)` is abelian, hence nuclear (CITED
  standard). Product states exist on `⊗_min`. ∎

**Corollary S3 (placement of the zeros; `PROVED`).** Let `(𝔇₁, 𝔇₂)` be a finite split for ζ, complete or primitive,
whose dominant term consists of vectors in a covariant representation of the adèle class system of §1.1, for any
admissible `G`. Then:
* RH holds (R4 Δ(i));
* **all** mass `≥ m_γ` at every ordinate is carried by `P_inert ξ_k^{(1)}`, i.e. by the stratum on which the flow
  commutes with `π(C_0(A_Q))` and `u(G)`. In particular it commutes with every `u_p`, `p` prime.

On that stratum, by S2(c), the joint law "(arithmetic, flow)" of the dominant term can be a product law, and the flow
marginal is unconstrained. So **the arithmetic of the system imposes no condition on the part of the dominant term that
carries the zeros.** This is the proved content of "the zeros are not produced by the primes" for this class. The
positivity, `ν₁ ≥ μ_Z`, is the positivity of a freely chosen measure. That is OT-3 (Lemma T) and OT-5′ failure by
structure.

*Proof.* R4 Δ(iii) together with S(iv): the atoms of `ν₁ = Σ_kν_{ξ_k}` are atoms of `Σ_kν_{P_inert ξ_k}`. ∎

*What is not claimed.*
* It is not claimed that a Lefschetz *trace* formula on `H_inert` is impossible. It is `HEURISTIC` that such a trace
  cannot produce `Σ_nΛ(n)n^{−1/2}δ_{±log n}`, since the flow has no fixed-point structure there beyond that of `G`
  alone.
* Theorem S is about positive functionals. Connes' distributional trace is a different object, and its global form is
  `EQUIV` (A17).

## §3 Proposition G — Bost–Connes as the inert remnant

## §4 Theorem D — the discrete-spectrum law (BC at every `β`)

**Theorem D (`PROVED`, modulo the `CITED` facts named).** Let `(A, σ)` be a C\*-dynamical system over `ℝ`. For
`λ ∈ ℝ` let `A_λ := {x : σ_t(x) = e^{iλt}x ∀t}`. Suppose `span ⋃_{λ∈Γ}A_λ` is dense in `A` for a countable set
`Γ ⊂ ℝ`. Let `ω` be a `σ`-invariant state, with GNS triple `(H_ω, π_ω, Ω)`, and let `U_tπ_ω(x)Ω := π_ω(σ_tx)Ω` be its
canonical implementation. Then:

*(i)* `U` has pure point spectrum, with eigenvalues in `Γ`. Every flow measure of every family of vectors in `H_ω`,
with flow `U_{ct}` (`c > 0`), is atomic and supported in `cΓ`.

*(ii)* **Bost–Connes.** `Γ = log Q^×_{>0}` for the BC system. Every KMS_β state, for every `β ∈ (0, ∞]`, is
`σ`-invariant. So (i) applies at every `β`:
* the Gibbs and extremal states for `β > 1` (type I_∞);
* the unique state for `0 < β ≤ 1` (injective III_1 factor);
* the ground states at `β = ∞`.

The modular group is `Δ^{is} = U_{−βs}`, so `log Δ` has spectrum in `β·log Q^×_{>0}`.

*(iii)* A finite split for ζ with such a dominant term, i.e. vectors in `H_ω` with flow `U_{ct}`, exists only if

  **(BC-mod′)** `γ ∈ c·log Q^×_{>0}` (equivalently `e^{γ/c} ∈ Q_{>0}`) for every ordinate `γ`, for one `c > 0`;

and then only with `Σ_k‖P_γξ_k‖² ≥ m_γ`, where `P_γ` is the `γ/c`-eigenprojection.

*Proof.*
* **(i).** `U` is well defined and unitary because `ω∘σ_t = ω`, and strongly continuous because `σ` is (standard).
  For `x ∈ A_λ`, `U_tπ(x)Ω = e^{iλt}π(x)Ω`. The vectors `π(x)Ω`, for `x` in the dense span, are total in `H_ω`
  (cyclicity). Eigenvectors for distinct eigenvalues are orthogonal. So `H_ω = ⊕_{λ∈Γ}H_λ`, and every spectral
  measure is `Σ_λ‖P_λξ‖²δ_λ`.
* **(ii).** The monomials `μ_ne(r)μ_m^*` (with `σ_t(μ_n) = n^{it}μ_n`, `σ_t(e(r)) = e(r)`) span a dense `*`-subalgebra,
  with eigenvalue `log(n/m)`. CITED: Bost–Connes, Selecta Math. 1 (1995), Prop. 18 and §§4–7; Laca–Raeburn. KMS
  states are invariant (CITED standard; Bratteli–Robinson II, Prop. 5.3.3). The modular relation
  `σ^ω_s = σ_{−βs}` is the KMS condition (standard).
* **(iii).** R4 Δ(iii) and (i). ∎

*Status of (BC-mod′): `OPEN`.*
* It asks that every ratio `γ/γ′` lie in `log Q^×_{>0}/log Q^×_{>0}`. No transcendence result about the ordinates
  reaches this: even the irrationality of `γ₁` is not known to me to be proved.
* The six-exponentials theorem needs two independent `x`'s with algebraic `e^{x_iγ_j}`, and (BC-mod′) supplies only

* `HEURISTIC`: it is false.
* Even if it were true, the choice of eigenvectors reads the zeros (Lemma T; OT-3). The canonical vector `Ω` has flow

## §5 Theorem N — singular functionals are free

**Theorem N (`PROVED`, modulo `CITED` state extension).** Let `𝔐` be a unital C\*-algebra containing a C\*-subalgebra
`C ≅ C_0(ℝ̂)`, generated by `λ(f) ↦ f̂`, `f ∈ L¹(ℝ)`. Examples are:
* a von Neumann crossed product `N ⋊ ℝ`, where `λ_t ⊗ 1` is a multiple of the regular representation, so `C*(λ)` is
  faithfully `C*_r(ℝ) = C_0(ℝ̂)`;
* the multiplier algebra `M(B ⋊ ℝ)` of a C\*-crossed product admitting a regular covariant representation.

Then for every tempered positive measure `σ` on `ℝ̂` there is a weight `ψ` on `𝔐` (a sum of positive functionals,
neither normal nor lower semicontinuous in general) with `ψ(λ(f)^*λ(f)) = ∫|f̂|²dσ` for all `f ∈ C_c^∞(ℝ)`.

*Proof.*
* **Decompose.** Write `σ = Σ_{j∈ℤ}σ_j` with `σ_j := σ|_{[j,j+1)}`, which is finite.
* **Extend.** `σ_j` defines a positive functional on `C`, and hence on the unital algebra `C + ℂ1`, with value
  `σ_j(ℝ)` at `1`. That is a positive functional of norm `σ_j(ℝ)` on a unital C\*-subalgebra of `𝔐`. By the state
  extension theorem (CITED standard; Kadison–Ringrose I, Thm 4.3.13) it extends to a positive functional `ψ_j` on `𝔐`
  with the same norm.
* **Sum.** `ψ := Σ_jψ_j` is additive and positively homogeneous on `𝔐_+`, with values in `[0, ∞]`. On `λ(f)^*λ(f)`,
  which lies in `C` and corresponds to `|f̂|²`, it gives `Σ_j∫|f̂|²dσ_j = ∫|f̂|²dσ`. This is finite because `σ` is
  tempered and `|f̂|²` is Schwartz. ∎

*Consequence.* R4 Theorem Δ uses only the flow measure `ν₁` and its temperedness, so it applies to such `ψ`. The class
of non-normal weights realises **every** tempered measure, `μ_Z` included under RH. It therefore contains no
constraint, and a dominant term drawn from it is a choice of `σ ≥ μ_Z`: installation (Lemma T). This closes R4 §5.3,
first bullet, as a **void class**. The Dixmier-trace `HEURISTIC` of R4 §5.3 becomes moot: whether particular singular
traces are atomless no longer matters, because the class as a whole is free.

## §6 Theorem Θ — growth law and broken dual symmetry

**Theorem Θ (`PROVED`).** Let `(𝔇₁, 𝔇₂)` be a finite split for ζ, complete or primitive. Then:

*(i)* `ν₁ ≥ μ_Z` as positive Radon measures, so `ν₁([−T, T]) ≥ 2N(T) = (T/π)log(T/2πe) + O(log T)`.

*(ii)* **No dual symmetry.** For every `c ≠ 0`, `ν₁∘τ_c ≠ ν₁`, where `τ_c(E) = E + c`. Equivalently, `φ_{𝔇₁}` is not
invariant under the dual modulation `f ↦ e^{ic·}f`. On a crossed product, the dominant term is not invariant under the
dual action `θ̂_c` for any `c ≠ 0`.

*(iii)* More generally, `ν₁([−T, T]) = O(T)` is impossible.

*Proof.*
* **(i).** By R4 Δ(ii), `ν₁ = μ_Z + ν₂` with `ν₂ ≥ 0`. For the Riemann–von Mangoldt formula see Titchmarsh, Thm 9.4
  (CITED classical).
* **(iii).** Immediate from (i), since `N(T)/T → ∞`.
* **(ii).** If `ν₁∘τ_c = ν₁`, then `ν₁([−T,T]) ≤ (2T/|c| + 2)ν₁([0, |c|])`, which is `O(T)` because `ν₁` is Radon.
  That contradicts (iii).
* **Equivalence in (ii).**
  * (⟸) If `φ(e^{ic·}f) = φ(f)` for all `f`, then `∫|f̂(τ − c)|²dν₁ = ∫|f̂|²dν₁`, since `(e^{ic·}f)^ = f̂(· + c)`, up to
  the sign convention.
  * The distributions `∫ĥ dν₁` and `∫ĥ d(ν₁∘τ_{±c})` therefore agree on every `c_f`, `f ∈ C_c^∞`, and by polarisation
  on every `f ⋆ g̃`.
  * The approximate-identity argument of R4 Lemma 1.3 and Riesz uniqueness give `ν₁∘τ_{±c} = ν₁`.
  * For a crossed product, `θ̂_c(u(f)) = u(e^{ic·}f)`, and `ψ∘θ̂_c = ψ` gives exactly this invariance. ∎

**Corollary Θ1 (uniform exclusions; `PROVED`).** None of the following is the dominant term of any finite split for ζ,
with any subtracted term, in either form:
* **(a) dual-invariant functionals.** This includes every **dual weight** `ω̃` on `N ⋊ ℝ`, because `ω̃∘θ̂ = ω̃`
  (Takesaki's construction `ω̃ = ω∘T`, with `T` the operator-valued weight obtained by integrating `θ̂`; CITED
  standard). It also includes every functional built from the crossed-product structure alone. R4 Cor Δ3(a2) (dual
  weights) is a special case, now with a one-line proof independent of normality;
* **(b) periodic flow measures.** This includes the canonical trace of any **Frobenius suspension**: a unitary `F`
  suspended with period `log q`, for which `ν` is invariant under `2π/log q`;
* **(c) flow measures of linear growth.** Examples are bounded-density absolutely continuous measures, and atomic
  measures with uniformly bounded mass per unit interval.

*Inverse check (function fields; `CITED` Weil).* For a curve over `F_q`, a finite split exists. `ν₁` is the Frobenius
point measure, which is periodic with period `2π/log q`, and `N_C(T) = (2g/log q)·T + O(1)` is linear. Theorem Θ
excludes nothing true there: the growth `T log T` is exactly what ζ adds. In operator form, the dual symmetry of period
`2π/log q` that every function field has **must be broken completely** for ζ. This is the proved content of the
"`q → 1`" heuristic (G1_B9_R4) at the level of positive functionals. Theorem Θ itself is elementary. Its use is as a
one-line uniform filter.

*Falsifiers.* Theorem Θ is conditional on a finite split. For `Ξ_a`, `Ξ_Q`, and Beurling or Epstein data with off-line
zeros, no finite split exists (R4 Lemma 2.1; E-P5.12). So no false positivity arises, and nothing true is excluded.

## §7 Proposition R — the ratio-set question on (E-orb)

The panels on G1_B2_R4 (P2, P4, P5 salvage) asked the following. Can one transverse measure, scaled by `e^{−t}`
under the flow, carry germs of modulus `p^{−1}` at orbits of length `log p` for every `p`? Or does the ratio set /
type III structure of the crossed product obstruct it?

### 7.1 The canonical system

`A_f` carries its Haar measure `ν_f` (normalised by `ν_f(Ẑ) = 1`). `Q^×_{>0}` acts by multiplication.

**Proposition R (`PROVED`, except (iii), which is `CITED`).**

*(i)* `ν_f∘q = q^{−1}ν_f`, i.e. `ν_f(qE) = |q|_fν_f(E) = q^{−1}ν_f(E)` for `q ∈ Q^×_{>0}`. The Radon–Nikodym cocycle
is constant along each group element: `D_q ≡ q^{−1}`. In particular `×p` fixes `0 ∈ A_f`, and on the balls
`p^kẐ_{(p)}` its germ has modulus exactly `p^{−1}`, for **every** `p` at once. This is B2's modulus law, Thm M(iii),
with `α_d = 1` and return time `log p`.

*(ii)* **The Maharam extension is the archimedean stratum.** The Maharam extension of `(A_f, ν_f, Q^×_{>0})` is
`A_f × ℝ` with measure `ν_f ⊗ e^s ds` and action `q(x, s) = (qx, s − log D_q) = (qx, s + log q)`. Translation in `s`
scales the invariant measure by `e^t`. Under `(x, s) ↦ (e^s, x) ∈ ℝ_{>0} × A_f`:
* it is isomorphic to `({x_∞ > 0} ⊂ A_Q, Haar)` with the **diagonal** `Q^×_{>0}`-action;
* the Maharam flow becomes **archimedean scaling**;
* `ℓ = s = log x_∞`.

So the canonical carrier of B2's modulus germs at every prime, with one transverse measure and the scaling flow, is
exactly the archimedean stratum `Y₀⁺` of Theorem S.

*(iii)* (`CITED`, not re-read.) The `Q^×_{>0}`-action on `(A_f, ν_f)` is ergodic of type III_1. The ratio set is the
closed subgroup generated by the `q^{−1}`, which is `ℝ^×_+` because `log 2/log 3 ∉ Q`. Its core (the Maharam crossed
product) is the II_∞ factor `L^∞(A_Q^{x_∞>0}) ⋊ Q^×_{>0}`, and the dual flow is archimedean scaling. This matches
Connes' statement that `Q^×` acts on `A_Q` with a type II_∞ crossed product whose dual action scales the trace
(R158_002 l.395), and the type III_1 of BC at `β ≤ 1` (Bost–Connes 1995).

*Proof of (i).* Haar on `A_f = ∏′Q_p` is the restricted product of the Haar measures `μ_p` on `Q_p`, and
`μ_p(qE_p) = |q|_pμ_p(E_p)`. The product formula gives `∏_p|q|_p = |q|_∞^{−1} = q^{−1}`. The balls: `×p` maps
`p^kZ_p × ∏_{ℓ≠p}Z_ℓ` onto `p^{k+1}Z_p × ∏_{ℓ≠p}Z_ℓ`, because `p` is a unit at `ℓ ≠ p`. The measure ratio is `p^{−1}`.

*Proof of (ii).* Invariance:
`(ν_f ⊗ e^sds)(q(E × [a,b])) = ν_f(qE)∫_{a+log q}^{b+log q}e^sds = q^{−1}ν_f(E)·q∫_a^be^sds`. The change of variables
`x_∞ = e^s` gives `e^sds = dx_∞`, so the measure is Haar on `ℝ_{>0} × A_f`, and `q` acts by `(x_∞, x) ↦ (qx_∞, qx)`. ∎

### 7.2 The answer

* **No ratio-set obstruction** (`PROVED` for existence, `CITED` for the type). Modulus-`p^{−1}` germs at every prime
  coexist under one transverse measure. The resulting type is III_1, and its canonical core is the archimedean
  stratum of the adèle class space. The panel's expected answer (P4: "the only such object is the adèle class space")
  is confirmed in the existence direction. Uniqueness is not proved (`OPEN`).
* **The OA consequence** (`PROVED`). By Theorem S′ (both forms, `ℓ = log x_∞`), every covariant representation of this
  Maharam crossed product, and every normal one of its von Neumann core, has **Lebesgue** flow. Realised as a positive
  functional on the crossed product, the modulus data carry no atoms. They carry the primes, and only in the
  Lebesgue part (Remark S0).

### 7.3 What this does not decide (`HEURISTIC` / `OPEN`)

B2's (E-orb) object is a lamination with leafwise reduced cohomology `H̄¹`, and a positive form from leafwise
harmonic forms. Theorem S′ concerns representations of the crossed product. The multiplication operators `e^{isℓ}` lie
in the algebra, but they do **not** preserve leafwise harmonic forms, because `ℓ` is not leafwise constant. So Theorem
S′ does not apply to the `W(S,F)`-module `H̄¹`. It is `HEURISTIC` that a Deninger `H̄¹` over this transversal would
inherit Lebesgue spectrum. The contrary scenario, point spectrum on `H̄¹` from harmonicity, is exactly Deninger's
programme, and it remains `OPEN`. I record the dictionary:

Laca's corner; Riemann–von Mangoldt; von Koch–Landau (inside R4 Δ).

### Weakest steps
* **Theorem S(ii): the multiplier extension and strong continuity of `V_s`.** The argument: `π₀` is nondegenerate on
  `H_arch`; it extends to `C_b(Y₀)`; `e^{isℓ}f → f` uniformly for `f ∈ C_c(Y₀)`. `A_Q` is locally compact, `Y₀` is
  open, and `C_c(Y₀)` is dense in `C_0(Y₀)`, so this is standard. What should be re-checked is the extension for a
  **non-separable** `H`. Stone–von Neumann holds without separability (decomposition into cyclic Weyl
  representations), but I cite it rather than re-prove it.
* **Cor S3's reading "the zeros are not produced by the primes".** What is `PROVED` is the commutation
  (`U|_{H_inert} ∈ π(𝒞)′`) and the freeness of the flow marginal (S2). That no *trace-type* (as opposed to
  positive-functional-type) Lefschetz formula can live on `H_inert` is `HEURISTIC`.
* **Prop R(iii) (type III_1, ergodicity) and Prop G(ii) (Laca's corner)** are `CITED` and were not re-read. The
  load-bearing parts, R(i)–(ii) and the proofs of S and S′, do not use them.
* **(BC-mod′)** is `OPEN`, with no known leverage. It is also inert as a route, because the eigenvector choice installs
  the zeros.
* **§7.3.** The gap between crossed-product representations and a Deninger leafwise `H̄¹` is not bridged. Theorem S′
  says nothing about harmonic forms. This is the one place where the operator-algebra closure does not reach G1_B2's
  residual.
* **Mathematical weight.** Each proof is short, from classical tools. The value is the placement: in positive operator
  algebra over `A_Q`, the primes live on the Lebesgue stratum and the zeros only where the atoms are chosen.
