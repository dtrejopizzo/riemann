## 2. Why Prop. P is excluded (the fatal step for G2)

Let `𝒞_P := {a ≥ 0 : Σ_{d|m} a(d) ≤ log m + C_1 ∀m, Σ_{j≤x} a_j/j ≤ log x + C_2 ∀x}`. The proof of Lemma S uses only
these two row-sum bounds (the Schur test with weights `k^{−½}`). So Prop. P holds for every `a ∈ 𝒞_P`, with `K_0`
depending on `C_1 + C_2`.
* **Deletion.** `a = Λ1_{n≥N}` has `0 ≤ a ≤ Λ`, so both row sums are at most Λ's, and `a ∈ 𝒞_P`. This is the whole of
  `𝒞_BT`.
* **Amplification.** `a = Λ + c1_{n=2}` adds `c` to row `m` from the entry `k = m/2`, and `c/2` from the entry `k = 2m`.
  The total is `3c/2`, not "at most `c`" as §5 of the memo says. That is a harmless slip: it is absorbed by
  `K ≥ K_0 e^{O(c)}`.

So `𝒞_P ∋ Λ` is closed under both operations of `thm:finmod`. By `cor:void`, a derivation of `RD_T` whose only prime
input is `𝒞_P`-membership would prove `RD_T(a)` for the deletion comb at `T > T_0(0)`, and for the amplification comb
at `T > ½log 2`. Both are false by `thm:finmod`(i),(ii). **Prop. P therefore cannot be the load-bearing step of a
G2 mechanism, at any window.**

It also fails the other filters.
* **O3 (`rem:resweights`).** The gap of Prop. P on `ℛ_M` is `log(8K(2+log M)) − C`, which is insensitive to `O(1)`
  weight changes. A window sign needs precision `e^{−14.4T}` in one weight. This is the class-level signature.
* **`thm:arena`.** `ℛ_M` is an `M`-dimensional compression for each `M`. No finite compression decides the even
  Schur complement, and in any case `ℛ_M` avoids the near-minimisers. This is consistent with `thm:margin`, as the
  memo notes.

The memo correctly reports this exclusion himself (§5, item `cor:void`).

## 3. Verification of the load-bearing steps (checked against P46's definitions)

1. **Normalisation.** Per `eq:symbol`/`eq:form`, `𝔞_T(F) = (2π)^{−1}∫m|F̂|² − Σ_{n∈N_T} v_n c_F(log n)`, with
  `v_n = 2Λ(n)/√n`. The cosine gives `c_F(log n)` for real `F`, so the memo's `Q_a(b) = 2Σ a_n n^{−½}Σ_k b_{nk}b_k
  = b^⊤(𝕄_a + 𝕄_a^⊤)b` has the right factor 2. **OK.**
2. **Lemma R(a)–(c).** `A(ψ) = B(ψ) = 0` holds by two integrations by parts. The support of `F_b` lies in
  `(−½log M − ε, ½log M + ε) ⊂ I_T`. For the separation, `nk ≠ m` with `m, k ≤ M` gives
  `|log(nk/m)| ≥ log(1 + 1/M) ≥ 1/(2M) ≥ 4ε`. The pair `nk < m` uses `m/(nk) ≥ 1 + 1/(nk) ≥ 1 + 1/M`. At `u = log n`
  only `m = nk` contributes. Contacts `n ≤ M ≤ e^{2T}` all lie in `N_T`. **OK.**
3. **Lemma R(d).** For `|τ| ≥ 1`, `m(τ) ≤ log(1+|τ|)` follows from `lem:mbounds`(ii): `−log 2π + 1 + ½log(5/4) < 0`.
  For `|τ| < 1` it follows from (i) and `m(1) < 0`. For the off-diagonal terms, two integrations by parts in `J`
  give `(ε/u)²`. The family `G_ε` is uniformly bounded in `W^{2,1}`, and `Σ_{h≠0} h^{−2} = 2ζ(2)`. **OK.**
4. **Lemma S.** Row `m` of `𝔸` has two parts. The divisor part is exactly `Σ_{d|m}Λ(d) = log m`. The multiple part is
  `Σ_{2≤j≤M/m}Λ(j)/j ≤ log(M/m) + E`. The Schur test with positive weights for a nonnegative symmetric matrix is
  standard. The bound `λ_max ≤ log M + E` is **OK**.
5. **Prop. P, archimedean lower bound.**
  * For all `τ ≠ 0`, `m(τ) ≥ log(|τ|/2π) − 4`. For `|τ| ≥ 1` this is `lem:mbounds`(ii). For `|τ| < 1`,
  `m ≥ C_0 = −5.372 > −5.84`.
  * The substitution `x = ετ` gives `log(1/ε) + log|x|`, and `(log|x|)^−x⁴|χ̂|²` is integrable.
  * Off-diagonal, via polarised `eq:A`. The cross inner product vanishes, and the cross-correlation vanishes on
  `|u| < u_0 − 2ε`, so the divergent `∫K` terms cancel. What remains is bounded by
  `sup_{(u_0/2, 2u_0)}K · ‖ψ‖_1²`, with `K ≤ 1/u + ½`.
  * `‖ψ‖_1 = O(ε^{−1})` and `‖ψ‖_2² ≍ ε^{−3}`, which gives the ratio `O(ε/u_0 + ε)`.
  * The Hilbert-type sum is `Σ_{m≠k}|b_m b_k|/|m−k| ≤ 2H(M)‖b‖²`, and the constant part is `ε(Σ|b_m|)² ≤ εM‖b‖²`.
  * With `ε_M = 1/(8KM(2+log M))`, the total is `O(1/K)`, and the bracket is
  `log(8K(2+log M)) − C' − C''/K − E ≥ c_0 > 0` for `K ≥ K_0`.
  **OK (PROVED).** The constants are unoptimised but correct in structure.
6. **Thm D / Cor. D1.** Take `b_m = m^{−½}`. Then `Σ_k b_{nk}b_k = n^{−½}H(M/n)` and `‖b‖² = H(M)`, and (1.2) at
  `T = ½log M + ε` gives the ceiling. (i) uses `a ≥ 0` to drop the terms with `n > √M`. (ii) uses Theorem Q′ of
  G2_B1_R4, which panel R4 verified (P1, P3, P5 R4), and Hardy–Littlewood–Karamata for
  `Σ (a_n/n) n^{−(σ−1)} ~ r/(σ−1)`. The comparison `∫_1^M S(x)dx/x ~ r(log M)²/2` gives `r ≤ 1`. **OK.**
  Note the direction: this is a *necessary* condition extracted from ∀T-positivity. It is useless as a G2 arrow.
7. **Thm R.**
  * `b = r1_{[1,N]}` reproduces Soundararajan's numerator `Σ_{mk≤N} r(m)r(mk)k^{−½}` exactly. The truncation matches
  because `nk ≤ N` forces `k ≤ N`.
  * The support argument holds: `n = (nk)/k` is a squarefree product of primes `> P_0`, so `a_n ≥ c` and every term
  is nonnegative.
  * `T ≥ ½log N + ε` holds, since `½log N = T − ½`.
  **OK, given CITED Soundararajan (2008), Thm 2.1, lower bound.** I did not re-derive the Rankin error term either. It
  is load-bearing only for Thm R, which is an obstruction, not a mechanism.
8. **Thm T.** For `Re w = c > ½`, Laplace/Fourier inversion gives `Σ s_n n^{−½} c_F(log n)`. The shift to `Re w = 0` is
  legal by the growth hypothesis and because `𝒞` decays faster than any power. At the pole `w = ½`,
  `𝒞(½) = A(F)B(F) = 0` and `𝒞′(½) = A′B + AB′ = 0` on `P_T`, so a pole of order at most 2 leaves no residue.
  (b) has three parts:
  * monotonicity is `lem:monoT`, whose proof is comb-independent;
  * the lower bound comes from density in the form domain, where the comb part is a finite, bounded perturbation;
  * the upper bound comes from the modulated `prop:finsupp` test.
  **OK.**
9. **Cor. T1.** `s^W ≥ W/φ(W)` on squarefree `n` coprime to `W`, so Thm R applies and `inf S_{s^W} = −∞`. **OK.**

**Inaccuracy, not load-bearing.** The memo says (§ intro, Remark R.1) that `prop:band` places the decisive band at

the negative set is a finite union of bounded intervals starting at 0. The lower end `2πe^{2T}` is the memo's
gloss, not a statement of the record. It does not affect any proof.
