## G1_B5_R3 — excluded

**Checked (`PROVED`).**

  `p ≠ q` (double commutation); induction to coprime `a, b`. `H_u = ⋂S_nH` reducing with `S_p|H_u` unitary. Correct.
* Theorem 1: `φ_w(n/(n−1)) = ⟨S_{n−1}^†S_nw, w⟩ = ⟨S_nS_{n−1}^†w, w⟩ = ⟨F_{n−1}w, F_nw⟩` by double commutation of the
  coprime pair; Cauchy–Schwarz with `‖F_{n−1}‖ ≤ 1` gives `liminf‖F_nw‖ ≥ ‖w‖`; monotonicity `Q_{jd} ≤ Q_d` along
  multiples gives `Q_dw = w`. Correct, and genuinely short.
* Theorem 2(a),(b), Prop D: correct. Theorem 2(c) sharpness by `e_s`: correct.
* Cor 4(i): `μ_nμ_n^* = (1/n)Σ_{ns=0}e(s)` on `H_u` equals `1`, and strict convexity of the Hilbert norm forces
  `e(s)ξ = ξ`. Correct modulo the `CITED` presentation (which is the standard Laca–Raeburn one).
* Prop N: `⟨S_ng, e_{s̄}⟩ = n^{−s}G(s)`. Correct.

**Literature (the lens-4 findings for this memo).**
* **HWD = isometric Nica-covariant representation of `N^×`.** The relations `μ_nμ_m = μ_{nm}`,
  `μ_n^*μ_m = μ_mμ_n^*` for `(n,m) = 1` are exactly the Laca–Raeburn presentation of the BC algebra as a semigroup
  crossed product (J. London Math. Soc. 54 (1996)), i.e. Nica covariance for the quasi-lattice ordered pair
  `(Q^×_{>0}, N^×)`. Lemma 1.2's converse is that presentation read backwards. The memo grep'd "Nica" (0 corpus hits)
  but does not connect to the literature; it should.
* **BC ↔ Witt vectors is in the literature.** Connes–Consani, *On the arithmetic of the BC-system*, J. Noncommut.
  Geom. 8 (2014) 873–945 (arXiv:1103.4672), realise the integral BC endomorphisms `σ_n` and the additive maps
  `ρ̃_n` with `ρ̃_n∘σ_n = n` (Frobenius/Verschiebung relations) and relate the integral BC system to Witt vectors
  `W_0(F̄_p)`; Borger–de Smit, *Lambda actions of rings of integers* ([arXiv:1105.4662](https://arxiv.org/abs/1105.4662))
  and Yalkınoğlu (Invent. Math. 2013, [arXiv:1105.5022](https://arxiv.org/abs/1105.5022)) build BC systems from
  Λ-structures; B. Yalkınoğlu, *Bost–Connes systems and periodic Witt vectors*
  ([arXiv:2507.23759](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.23759), 2025) states that all BC systems are described by generalised
  Witt vectors and that the integral BC system is the Witt ring of `F̄_1`. So the memo's "Witt datum = BC datum"
  identification is known algebraically; the Hilbert-space form (Rosati compatibility `V = pF^†` = isometry) is a
  one-line translation. New to the corpus, not to the literature.
* **`H_u = ⋂S_nH` is the unitary part of a Wold decomposition.** For doubly commuting isometries the Wold decomposition
  is Słociński (1980, pairs) and Sarkar (*Wold decomposition for doubly commuting isometries*, Linear Algebra Appl.
  2014, `n`-tuples); for Nica-covariant isometric representations of general semigroups there is a substantial recent
  literature (e.g. Wold decompositions for odometer semigroups, product systems). Theorem 1's new content is only the
  continuity-to-unitary-part implication via Farey neighbours; that implication is elementary and I found no source
  for it, so I accept "new" for that step alone.
* Prop N: Nyman–Beurling / Báez-Duarte in `𝓗²` (Nikolski 1995; HLS 1997), EQUIV, correctly labelled.

**Fatal step.** None; the class is excluded by its own Theorem 1/2. The `OPEN` Kreĭn escape O-B5.3 is correctly
labelled RH-strength once a zero-carrying `H¹` exists (Lemma T).

**Salvageable.** Nothing beyond the record's row 43. Recommend the memo add the CC 2014 / Borger–de Smit /
Yalkınoğlu citations so the corpus does not re-derive the BC–Witt dictionary a third time.
