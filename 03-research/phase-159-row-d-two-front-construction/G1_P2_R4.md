## §1 G1_B2_R4 — Deninger's conformal class DK_α

  `μ(σ) = e^{−α_d l}μ(σ)` forces `μ(σ) = 0`. (iii) The flow-box computation
  `μ(ΦE) = (∫_a^b e^{−α_d s}ds)∫_{P(B)}e^{−α_dσ(P^{−1}w)}dν(w)` is correct (change of variables `s′ = s + σ(v)`), and
  gives the modulus law `ν(P(B))/ν(B) → e^{−α_d l}`. For ζ: `p^{−1}`, the Haar modulus of `×p` on `Z_p` — consistent
  with Deninger's CM example and Leichtnam's Lemma 6.1 (Rem M3). (ii),(iv): in codimension one the orbit segment **is**
  a local transversal, so `x ∉ supp μ`. **PROVED** (local-section theorem CITED).
* **Cor M1(a)–(c).** Follow from M(ii). Unconditional: on a codimension-one foliated manifold, every closed orbit lies

  fixed points). **PROVED.** (d) is conditional on (L), as labelled.

  is Euclidean); the sketch (curvature `K = c^{2n}K(fⁿ·)`, loops shrink, Killing–Hopf) is correct. H(iii) (commuting
