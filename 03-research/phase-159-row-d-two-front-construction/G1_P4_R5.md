## G1_B1_R5 — excluded (ergodic class; non-ergodic residual `OPEN`)

**Checked (`PROVED`, under (C), (Def), (Sm_x)/(Orb_x)).**
* **Lemma 2.1.** Conformality gives `|ξ∘Tφ^t|_g = e^{αt/2}|ξ|_g`, and `m` is φ-invariant, so `Re ρ = α/2` and `|h|` is
  invariant. Correct. This is DLS Thm 4.1 read pointwise, as the memo says.
* **Lemma 2.2.** Curvature scales as `K(f(y)) = c^{−2}K(y)` under the contraction `f = φ^{−l}`. With `fⁿ(y) → x` and
  `K` continuous at `x`, `K ≡ 0` on the disc. A similarity of a flat convex disc fixing `0` is `z ↦ μz`. Correct.
* **Lemma 2.3.** `F = f − ig` and `H = \overline{f+ig}` are holomorphic, and `|F|² + |H|²` is constant, so both are
  constant. Correct.
* **Theorem O1(i)–(iii).** Correct. The orbifold case: `aζ = a` and `bζ̄ = b` with `ζ ≠ 1`, so `a = b = 0`.
* **Theorems O2 and O3.** Correct. In O3(b), `e^{iγl}, e^{iγ′l} ∈ {u, ū}` forces `δ_−l` or `δ_+l ∈ 2πZ`. In O3(c), a
  pigeonhole puts two periods in one cyclic group, so their ratio is rational.
* **Corollary O4.** Correct.
  * O4(ii) applies O1 directly at each named point of `supp m`, not via orbit closures. That is legitimate, since
  `|h| = c > 0` on all of `supp m`.
  * O4(i) is the measurable fact that an ergodic infinite-measure-preserving flow has no nonzero invariant `L²`
  function, here `|h|`.
* **Remark 2.4.** Correct and not used.
* **Lemma O6.** Correct. It is Chebyshev plus the B3 scaling argument on the invariant sets `E_s`.
* **CM-elliptic inverse check.** Correct. O1 returns exactly `p^ρ ∈ {π, π̄}`.

**Literature.**
* **Degree 0 is classical.** A continuous eigenfunction of a flow, non-vanishing on an orbit closure containing a
  periodic orbit of period `l`, has `e^{iγl} = 1`. This is the mechanism behind the Parry–Pollicott criterion: a
  transitive hyperbolic flow fails to be weak-mixing iff its periods lie in a discrete subgroup `cZ` (Parry–Pollicott,
  *Zeta functions and the periodic orbit structure of hyperbolic dynamics*, Astérisque 187–188 (1990); `CITED` std.,
  not re-read). O3(c) is the conformally twisted version. The twist by `u_x` at each closed orbit is exactly the
  Livšic-type periodic data of a U(1)-cocycle, `CITED` std.
* **Deninger posed the question.** DLS Rem 7.6(4), read, says: "In the number theoretical case the eigenvalues of
  `T_xφ^{log Np}` on `T_xF` for `x ∈ γ_p` would therefore be complex conjugate numbers of absolute value `Np^{1/2}` …
  Are the eigenvalues Weil numbers (of weight 1)?"
  * O1 proves the matching statement from the eigenform side: a continuous harmonic eigenform nonzero at `x` has
  `e^{ρl} ∈ {λ_x, λ̄_x}`.
  * O3/O4 then show that incommensurable `log p` make this incompatible with infinitely many eigenvalues on an ergodic
  carrier.
  * I found no statement of O1–O4 in Deninger (DLS; 0709.2801), Leichtnam (math/0603576), Kopei (math/0605184) or Kim
  (1906.02424).
  * **Assessment.** New as a combination; the ingredients are classical.
* **Dependence on (C).** For laminations, (C) is Deninger's standing assumption, not a theorem. That is where the
  weight rests, and the memo says so.

**Fatal step (for G1).** None mathematical. The class it treats (φ-ergodic `m`, continuous Hodge realisation, a smooth
or orbifold germ at `x_∞` or at three prime orbits in `supp m`) is excluded by the memo's own O4/O5.

**Scope caveat (lens 4).** φ-ergodicity of `m` is independent of Deninger's hypotheses.
* Deninger's `H̄⁰ = R` comes from a dense leaf.
* B2_R5's Lemma 2.1 gives foliation-ergodicity of `μ` (saturated sets).
* Neither implies flow-ergodicity of `m`: φ-invariant sets need not be saturated, and saturated sets need not be
  φ-invariant.

So the exclusion is of a proper subclass. The non-ergodic residual (R1)–(R4) is `OPEN`, and its closure via (L_ω) is
`HEURISTIC`, correctly labelled.

**Salvageable.** Record O1–O4 as the "Weil-number law". It is a filter: any Deninger carrier for `Spec Z` must be
non-ergodic, with zero eigenforms supported on orbit closures that avoid `x_∞` and contain at most two prime orbits.
Cite DLS Rem 7.6(4) as the origin of the question and Parry–Pollicott for the degree-0 mechanism.

## G1_B2_R5 — unproved

  Correct.
* **Theorem R(i)–(iii).**
  * Type II_∞: trace-scaling excludes II₁, and the atom argument excludes I. Correct.
  * III₁: for a II_∞ factor `M` with trace-scaling `θ`, `M ⋊_θ R` is a type-III factor whose flow of weights is
  `θ|Z(M)` (Takesaki duality; Connes–Takesaki 1977). With `Z(M) = C` it is III₁. Correct and standard.
* **Corollary R1.** Correct. The inverse check against Leichtnam Prop 2(4) (III_{1/q}) is consistent with his text.
* **Theorem U.**
  * Under (Am), `W_μ ≅ R_{0,1}` (Connes 1976) and `N ≅ R_∞` (Haagerup 1987).
  * The core with its dual flow is a conjugacy invariant (Connes–Takesaki).
  * So all trace-scaling flows on `R_{0,1}` are stably conjugate. This is the known flow-level reformulation of the
  uniqueness of `R_∞`.
  * At the level of measured groupoids it is Krieger's theorem: amenable ergodic type-III₁ relations are orbit
  equivalent (Krieger 1976; Connes–Feldman–Weiss 1981; `CITED` std.).
  * Correct.
* **Proposition NH.** Checked.

  * The countable orbit then accumulates at uncountably many points, so `S` is not T₁. Correct.
  * Remark NH1 (a dense non-open subset of a locally compact Hausdorff space is not locally compact) is correct.

**Literature.**
* **III₁ vs III_{1/q}.** "Characteristic zero is III₁, function fields are III_{1/q}" is Connes' long-standing
  heuristic. The BC factor at `β = 1` is III₁ (Bost–Connes 1995), hence `R_∞` by Haagerup. Deriving it from

* **The Maharam model of §4.1 is known.**

  * Connes–Consani–Marcolli (*The thermodynamics of endomotives*, Adv. Math. 214 (2007), math/0512138; and
  math/0703392, §"The Bost–Connes endomotive", read l. 1033–1049) identify `BC ⋊_σ R` with `C*(𝒢₁)`. That is the
  groupoid of commensurability of 1-dimensional Q-lattices **not** divided by scaling, which is the adèle class

  * So `Σ`'s transversal and its NC replacement (NH2) are exactly CCM's dual system. The memo should cite it.
* **NH.** The non-Hausdorff nature of `A_Q/Q^×` is the founding observation of Connes' programme. NH's content is that

**Fatal step (for G1).** None mathematical. No object is built. NH excludes one gluing class (Σ with complete leaves).
Skew and incomplete-leaf suspensions are `OPEN`. So the class is not excluded, and the verdict is **unproved**.

## G1_B3_R5 — excluded (finite-trace DK_α; escapes X1–X3 `OPEN`)

**Checked (`PROVED`, under (R), (FT), (IBP), (D)/(D′), (Hodge)).**
* **Lemma 2.1 and Theorem F(i).** `F(s) = m{|Rm| > s}` is invariant under `s ↦ e^{αt}s`, finite, and tends to `0`, so
  `F ≡ 0`. Correct. It is the scaling form of Poincaré recurrence, and it needs no compactness.
* **F(ii).** Weitzenböck with `𝓡 = 0` on `Y`, plus `∫Δ₀|h|² dm = 0` by (IBP), gives `∫|∇h|² dm = 0`. Correct. It is
  rigorous for compact `X`. Non-compact `X` is the escape (X3), labelled.
* **F(iii).** `G_ij` is leafwise constant and continuous, and a dense leaf makes it constant, so the rank is
  `≤ C(d,k)`. Correct.
* **F(iv) and Cor F1.** Correct: a finite exponential polynomial is smooth, against prime atoms.
* **Theorem E.** Correct. A shorter proof: `φ^t` is an isomorphism of foliated spaces carrying `μ` to
  `e^{−α_d t}μ`, and `χ_Co` is a metric-independent invariant, linear in `μ`. So
  `χ_Co(𝓕,μ) = e^{−α_d t}χ_Co(𝓕,μ)`, which forces `χ_Co = 0`. The class-level version in the memo is equivalent.
* **Inverse checks.**
  * CM-elliptic: (D) fails; local rank 2.
  * Horospherical `T¹(Γ\H³)`: consistent, by Moore ergodicity.
  * Leichtnam: `H̄²` is infinite-dimensional, so Thm E does not apply. His `χ_Λ α(0)` term in the trace formula
  (l. 213, 1367) is not contradicted. Correct.

**Literature.**
* **Theorem F extends a known wall.**
  * Deninger, 0709.2801 §3 (read): "The conditions in the theorem actually force α = 0 so that φ^t is isometric."
  * DLS §6 after (31): "this is not possible in the manifold setting … which actually implies α = 0."
  * Theorem F extends this from compact manifolds to arbitrary foliated spaces with finite trace. The mechanism is
  curvature scaling under a finite invariant measure, standard in rigidity theory (conformal Anosov-type arguments).
  * I found no statement for laminations in Deninger, Leichtnam, Kopei or Kim. New as stated.
* **Corollary F1** is DLS's remark after Thm 5.3 ("otherwise the alternating sum of traces would be a smooth
  function"), made effective by F(iii). The memo credits it.
* **Theorem E vs Deninger.**
  * DLS Rem 7.6(7) (read) expects `χ_Co(𝓕,μ) = −log|d_K|` for the preferred `μ`, together with `H̄²` one-dimensional
  and `Θ = 1` on it (§6, (31)).
  * The trace-scaling identity above makes these incompatible for ramified `K`.
  * Deninger's 2007 table (0709.2801 §1) takes "the transversal measure μ defined by φ^t" and keeps the analogy
  `χ_F ↔ −log|d_K|`. I found no place where he notes the incompatibility. So Theorem E is a correct, elementary, and
  to my knowledge unrecorded observation.
  * Caveat (`HEURISTIC`): Deninger's `χ_Co` might be meant for a flow-invariant measure distinct from the trace
  measure of `H̄²`. Theorem E binds only the reading in which one `μ` serves both.

**Fatal step (for G1).** The class DK_α with finite trace, (IBP) and a dense leaf is excluded by the memo's own F1/F2.
Escapes (X1) infinite trace, (X2) class-level conformality and (X3) singular (IBP) are `OPEN`, with no candidate.
(X1) is further reduced by B1_R5 O4(i) in the ergodic case.

**Salvageable.** Record Thm F + F1 as the canonical closure of (E-orb)/DK_α at finite trace, and Thm E (in its
one-line form) as an OT-10 filter. Cite Deninger 0709.2801 §3 for the manifold case.

* **Lemma A.** The `L`-linear projection `ℓ` commutes with `λ`. Passing to `V[X]` and comparing top coefficients gives

* **Lemma B.** `x = (1, ρ)` and `y = (log p_j)` give the six numbers `p_j, p_j^ρ`. Correct.
* **Lemma C.** `ζ < 0` on `(0,1)` via `η`. Correct.

* **Proposition HR.**
  * Pullback multiplies `‖·‖²` by `e`.
  * Pushforward satisfies `‖f_*‖ ≤ √d` by pointwise Cauchy–Schwarz and `L²` removability.
  * The composite satisfies `‖f_*g^*‖ ≤ √(d₁d₂)`.
  * (iv) via `β = α′α` and faithfulness of `End⁰(J)` on `H¹`.
  * Correct.
* **Cor HR-W♯.** Correct, given R4 W♯(c).

**Literature.**
* **Lemma B is textbook.** The six exponentials theorem (Lang 1966, Ramachandra 1968) is classically illustrated by
  exactly this corollary: if `2^t, 3^t, 5^t` are all algebraic (classically: integers), then `t` is rational
  (Waldschmidt, *Diophantine Approximation on Linear Algebraic Groups*, 2000, §1.3; `CITED` std.). Theorem R is Lemma A
  plus that corollary.
* **Deninger's framework already builds this in.** His cohomology is expected to be an infinite-dimensional
  **R**-vector space with an **R**-action (a flow), not a `Q`-structure with a Frobenius (DLS §2–§3). Theorem R makes
  one reason for that precise.
* **Proposition HR** is the classical trivial bound for correspondences (`|λ| ≤ √(d₁d₂)` on `H¹`; `‖T_p‖ ≤ p+1`),
  equivalent in content to Castelnuovo–Severi-type estimates (`CITED` std.). It is not new. Its use to make R4's
  HEURISTIC `PROVED` is the correct repair.

**Fatal step (for G1).** Every algebraic or finite-rank carrier is excluded by the memo's own Thm R/R′. The residual
Q-R5 (transcendental joint point spectrum on an analytic completion, with trace = explicit formula) is OT-5′ itself,
as the memo says.

**Salvageable.** Record Thm R as the precise form of the "rationality wall" (R157_002 row 23), attributing Lemma B to

## G1_B5_R5 — excluded (point spectrum on HWDs; residue EQUIV or installation)

**Checked (`PROVED`).**

  argument is correct.
* **Prop 2.2(i)–(iv).** Correct.
  * (iv): the product `∏|⟨v_p, v′_p⟩| → 0` against a finite-modification approximant gives the contradiction.
* **Theorem E(i)–(ii).** Correct. `Σϱ² = ∞` implies the product of overlaps tends to `0`.
* **Prop C.** Correct. The dyadic PNT estimate with `r(τ) < 1` is fine.
* **Theorem G.** `‖E_pΩ_p‖² = ⟨v, ρ_p v⟩ = (1−a)(1−b)/(1−ab)` and
  `1 − ‖E_pΩ_p‖² = (a + b − 2ab)/(1−ab)`, both checked. (⇒) and (⇐) are correct. For `β ≤ 1`, `Σ_p p^{−β} = ∞`.
* **Prop T.** Partial summation is correct.
* **Correction K5(a′)** (Pontryagin–Naimark invariant subspace) is correct.

**Literature.**
* **Lemma E / Theorem E** is the multi-variable Wold decomposition.
  * For doubly commuting tuples: Słociński (Ann. Polon. Math. 37 (1980)) for pairs, and Sarkar, *Wold decomposition
  for doubly commuting isometries*, LAA 445 (2014), [arXiv:1304.7454](https://arxiv.org/abs/1304.7454), for
  `n`-tuples. Both give the orthogonal splitting into shift/unitary parts per variable.
  * A joint `F`-eigenvector with `|χ(p)| < 1` lies in the pure-shift part of every such `p`. There the coherent state
  `v(χ(p))` is forced, so the vector generates the product sector.
  * With infinitely many variables the sector is an ITP (von Neumann 1939).
  * B5 carries this through for countably many primes, with tail classes. That is a correct extension; the
  finite-variable core is known.
  * The memo cites neither Słociński nor Sarkar and should.
* **Ghost regime.** `ℓ²(N) ≅ 𝓗²` has point evaluations exactly on `Re s > ½`, with reproducing kernel `ζ(s + w̄)`
  (Hedenmalm–Lindqvist–Seip, Duke 86 (1997)). Prop D/C(a) restate this. Prop T is the elementary fact that `D(s + a)`
  lies in `𝓗²` only for `a > ½` when the coefficients have mean square `≫ 1`.
* **Theorem G** (no eigenvector at `β ≤ 1`). A correct elementary computation. It is consistent with, and a
  point-spectrum shadow of, Bost–Connes III₁ at `β ≤ 1` and Araki–Woods non-type-I product states. The memo labels
  that reading `HEURISTIC`, correctly. I know of no prior statement of the "iff". New but minor.

**Fatal step (for G1).**
* Strip points require a coherent sector whose reference vector is written from `ρ`: installation, OT-3 (Lemma T).
* Line points sit in `H_u`: installed.
* Ghost points are Nyman–Beurling selection: EQUIV, R157_002 row 43.
* The canonical Gibbs datum carries nothing at `β ≤ 1`.

So the class is excluded, with residue EQUIV/installation. The unreached exits (residual spectrum O-B5.5, cokernel
carriers A17) are exactly where Connes 1999 and Meyer 2005 (math/0311468) sit, as the memo says.

**Salvageable.** Record Thm E as "Wold–Słociński–Sarkar + ITP for the Witt relations", with the corollary "entanglement
never hosts a point". O-B5.5 (compressed Verschiebung) is the Sz.-Nagy–Foiaş model-space question. In `𝓗²` it is the
Beurling–Nyman family, EQUIV of record. Do not reopen it as new.

## G1_B6_R5 — excluded (residue: installation or EQUIV)

**Checked (`PROVED`, modulo Stone–von Neumann and state extension `CITED`).**
* **Theorem S(i)–(iv).** Correct.
  * `C_0(Y₀)` is an invariant ideal.
  * `ℓ = log|x_∞|` is continuous and flow-equivariant on `Y₀`, and the Weyl relation `U_tV_s = e^{−ist}V_sU_t` holds.
  * On `H_inert`, `α_t f − f ∈ C_0(Y₀)`, so `U` commutes with `π`.
* **Theorem S′** (both forms): correct.
* **Prop S2 and Cor S3.** Correct. `C*(R)` is abelian, hence nuclear, so product states exist.
* **Prop G(i), (iii).** Correct. (ii) is `CITED` (Laca).
* **Theorem D(i).** Correct: invariant state plus a dense span of eigenoperators gives pure point spectrum.
* **Theorem N.** Correct: decompose, extend, sum.
* **Theorem Θ.** Correct. A translation-invariant Radon measure has linear growth, against `2N(T) ∼ (T/π)log T`.
* **Prop R(i)–(ii).** Correct: `ν_f(qE) = q^{−1}ν_f(E)`, and the Maharam change of variables `x_∞ = e^s`.

**Literature.**
* **Theorem S/S′** is Mackey's imprimitivity theorem, in its Stone–von Neumann form for `R`, applied to the equivariant
  map `ℓ: Y₀ → R`. A system of imprimitivity over a free proper `R`-space forces a multiple of the regular
  representation (Mackey 1949; Folland §6.5; `CITED` std.). The new element is the observation that `ℓ` need not be
  `G`-invariant, so the argument runs on the adèle class **C\***-system and not only on `L²(C_Q)`. The consequence
  (Lebesgue spectrum where primes live) is Connes' motivation for the absorption picture (Selecta 1999). Correct
  placement; the mathematics is classical.
* **Prop G and Prop R** are the Connes–Consani–Marcolli dual system.
  * CCM (Adv. Math. 2007, math/0512138; math/0703392, read l. 972–1049) define the dual system `(BC ⋊_σ R, θ)` with
  the scaling action `θ_λ`. They identify it with `C*(𝒢₁)`, commensurability of Q-lattices without scaling
  quotient, i.e. the adèle class space with its archimedean coordinate.
  * "The gauge flow is inner on `{x_∞ ≠ 0}` and is BC on `{x_∞ = 0}`", and "the Maharam extension of
  `(A_f, Haar, Q^×_{>0})` is `{x_∞ > 0}`", are this duality read stratum by stratum.
  * Prop R's answer to the ratio-set question is therefore on record in CCM (and in Connes' remark, R158_002 l. 395,
  that the dual action scales the trace). This agrees with B2_R5 Thm R and with my R4 expectation.
* **Theorem N.** Kadison–Ringrose state extension; not new.
* **Theorem Θ.** Elementary; a correct one-line filter.
