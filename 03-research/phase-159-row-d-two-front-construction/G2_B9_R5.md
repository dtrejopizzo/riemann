## Verdict, stated first

In round 4 the panel scored all five graded-converse memos, mine included, as restatements. The ∀T content was
`κ = 0 ⟺ RH` (von Koch), and the upper half `κ_W(Λ) ≤ 2Θ* − 1` stayed OPEN except under B2's hypothesis (BD). This round

one at a time, with exact weights. What happens when **all** of them are resummed at once, instead of being tracked
contact by contact? And does any *estimate* on the resummed jump train, as opposed to an identity, control the bottoms?

1. **Theorem S (exact resummation of the jump train). PROVED, both forms.** For real `F ∈ C_c^∞(I_T)` and every
  `σ ∈ (Θ*, 1)`,
  ```
  W_T(F) = Arch_T(F) + (1/π) ∫_ℝ (ζ′/ζ)(σ+iτ) · ĝ_σ⁺(τ) · conj(ĝ_σ⁻(τ)) dτ, g_σ^± := e^{±(σ−½)t} F,
  ```
  where `Arch_T(F) := (2π)^{−1}∫ m|F̂|²` is the archimedean part of P46 `eq:form`. On `P_T` the same formula holds for
  `𝔞_T(F)`. When `σ > 1` the integrand is the Laplace transform of the jump train, and the identity is P46 `eq:W`
  contact by contact. When `σ` crosses `1`, the polar term `2AB` is exactly the residue at `s = 1`: the drift is
  absorbed. When `σ < 1` every jump is resummed into `ζ′/ζ`. There is also a complete-ξ version (S′): with `ξ′/ξ` in
  place of `ζ′/ζ` it needs no `Arch` term.
2. **Theorem L (log-absorption balance). PROVED, conditional on a pointwise hypothesis stated as such.** Suppose that on
  one line `Re s = σ ∈ (Θ*, 1)` there are `C` and `θ ∈ [0,1)` with `|ζ′/ζ(σ+iτ)| ≤ C·log(2+|τ|)^θ`. Then
  ```
  λ^A_T ≥ λ^W_T ≥ −C′ e^{2(σ−½)T/(1−θ)} for all T ≥ 1, i.e. κ_W ≤ 2(σ−½)/(1−θ).
  ```
3. **Corollary L1 (the tie). PROVED, given the CITED three-circle bound.** Insert the best pointwise bound known under
  the hypothesis "no zeros in `Re s > Θ*`", which is `θ(σ) = (1−σ)/(1−Θ*) + ε` (Littlewood; Titchmarsh §14.2 with ½
  replaced by `Θ*`). The output of Theorem L is then `≥ 1` for **every** `σ`. This is the trivial exponent `κ_W ≤ 1`
  (Chebyshev). The excess over 1 is exactly `(1−σ)(Θ*−½)/(σ−Θ*)` in the limit `ε → 0`. **Under RH the output is
  exactly 1 at every `σ ∈ (½,1)`**, although the truth under RH is `κ = 0` (Weil). So pointwise prime-side information,
  of the only kind known, ties *exactly* with the trivial bound. The positivity lives only in the sign structure of
  `Re ξ′/ξ` (the Herglotz property), and that is EQUIVALENT to RH (R157_002 row 39).
4. **Consequence for the angle (PROVED implications, HEURISTIC reading).** The jump calculus has now been run in all
  three regimes.
  * Contact by contact: the entry modulus is `≤ C w_n/log(1/ε)` (R1 Thm 2; P124.004 §5). There is no jump.
  * Cumulatively: the Chebyshev remainder `E(u)` is controlled (R1 Thm 3/Prop 4, EQUIVALENT), and the growth converse
  holds (R4 Thm G, EQUIVALENT).
  * Fully resummed: Theorem S. Absolute-value estimates tie with the trivial exponent (L1), and the sign is Herglotz
  positivity (EQUIVALENT).

  In none of the three is a proved sign transported. **Dead end.**

What is new: Theorem S in P46's normalisation for both forms, with the jump bookkeeping explicit (§2); Theorem L with its

## §1 Setting (P46 conventions, re-read in `main.tex` l.114–146 and l.416–520)

`I_T = (−T,T)`. For `F ∈ L²(I_T)` extended by zero, `F̂(τ) = ∫F(t)e^{iτt}dt` and `c_F(u) = ∫F(t)F(t−u)dt` (F real).
`Φ_F(s) = ∫F(t)e^{(s−½)t}dt`, `A(F) = Φ_F(1)`, `B(F) = Φ_F(0)`, and `P_T = {A = B = 0}`. Also
`m(τ) = Re ψ(¼ + iτ/2) − log π`, `v_n = 2Λ(n)/√n`, and
```
𝔞_T(F) = Arch_T(F) − Σ_{log n<2T} v_n c_F(log n), Arch_T(F) := (2π)^{−1}∫ m(τ)|F̂(τ)|²dτ, W_T(F) = 𝔞_T(F) + 2A(F)B(F)
```
for real `F` (P46 `eq:form`, `eq:W`; `(2π)^{−1}∫cos(τ log n)|F̂|² = c_F(log n)` because `c_F` is even). Here
`Θ* := sup{Re ρ}` is taken over the nontrivial zeros, and `κ_W := limsup_T T^{−1}log⁺(−λ^W_T)`; similarly `κ_A`.
Put `L(τ) := log(2+|τ|)`.

**Lemma 1.1 (core). PROVED.** `C_c^∞(I_T)` is dense in the form domain `Q_T = {F ∈ L²(I_T) : ∫L|F̂|² < ∞}` for the norm
`‖F‖²_Q = ∫L|F̂|²`. Both `W_T` and `𝔞_T` are continuous for this norm. So `λ^W_T` is the infimum over real unit
`F ∈ C_c^∞(I_T)`. The same holds for `λ^A_T` over `C_c^∞(I_T) ∩ P_T`.

*Proof.* Let `F ∈ Q_T`.
* *Dilation.* The dilations `U_r F(t) = r^{−1/2}F(t/r)`, `r < 1`, have compact support in `I_T`. They satisfy
  `‖U_rF − F‖_Q → 0` as `r ↑ 1`. This is dominated convergence on the Fourier side: `\widehat{U_rF}(τ) = r^{1/2}F̂(rτ)`
  and `L(τ/r) ≤ L(τ) + log 2`, the same bound as in the proof of P46 `lem:contT`.
* *Mollification.* Mollifying a compactly supported element of `Q_T` at scale `η → 0` converges in `‖·‖_Q`: the
  Fourier multiplier is `ρ̂(ητ) → 1` boundedly.
* *Continuity.* The comb term is bounded on `L²` by `Σ v_n‖F‖²`, `|m| ≤ L + C` gives continuity of `Arch_T`, and
  `A, B` are `L²`-continuous.
* *Primitive part.* For `P_T`, correct an approximant by the two fixed interior smooth functions `b_±` with
  `(A,B)(b_σ)` invertible, as in P124.004 §1. The correction is `O(|A(F_k)| + |B(F_k)|) → 0` in `Q`. ∎

## §2 Theorem S: the jump train resummed

**Theorem S (PROVED; complete form, and primitive form on `P_T`).** Let `F ∈ C_c^∞(I_T)` be real, and put
`h_F(s) := Φ_F(s)Φ_F(1−s)`. For every `σ` with `Θ* < σ < 1`,
```
W_T(F) = Arch_T(F) + (1/π) ∫_ℝ (ζ′/ζ)(σ+iτ) h_F(σ+iτ) dτ, h_F(σ+iτ) = ĝ_σ⁺(τ)·conj(ĝ_σ⁻(τ)), g_σ^± = e^{±(σ−½)t}F. (S)
```
If `F ∈ P_T` the left side is `𝔞_T(F)`. For `c > 1` the same identity holds with `(ζ′/ζ)` on `Re s = c` and with the
extra term `+2A(F)B(F)` removed from the left side, i.e.
`𝔞_T(F) = Arch_T(F) + (1/π)∫(ζ′/ζ)(c+iτ)h_F(c+iτ)dτ`.

*Proof.*
1. *Laplace form of `h_F`.* `h_F(s) = ∫∫F(t)F(u)e^{(s−½)(t−u)}dtdu = ∫c_F(y)e^{(s−½)y}dy`. Since `c_F ∈ C_c^∞`,
  `h_F` is entire and `|h_F(x+iτ)| ≤ C_{N,K}(1+|τ|)^{−N}` uniformly for `x` in a compact `K` (integrate by parts in
  `y`). The two-sided Laplace inversion `c_F(y) = (2πi)^{−1}∫_{(c)} h_F(s)e^{−(s−½)y}ds` holds on every vertical
  line. At `y = log n` it gives `(2πi)^{−1}∫_{(c)} n^{−s}h_F(s)ds = n^{−½}c_F(log n)`.
2. *Jump train as a Dirichlet integral, `c > 1`.* The sum `Σ_n Λ(n)n^{−c} < ∞` and the decay of `h_F` justify
  exchanging sum and integral:
  ```
  Σ_n v_n c_F(log n) = 2Σ_n Λ(n)n^{−½}c_F(log n) = (2/2πi)∫_{(c)} (−ζ′/ζ)(s) h_F(s) ds.
  ```
  (Only `log n < 2T` contribute, since `c_F` vanishes on `|y| ≥ 2T`. So the "contacts not yet entered" are killed
  automatically by `c_F`, which is `lem:monoT` in Laplace form.) Hence `𝔞_T(F) = Arch_T(F) + (1/π)∫(ζ′/ζ)(c+iτ)h_F(c+iτ)dτ`
  (`ds = i dτ`). This is the `c > 1` clause.
3. *Shift to `σ ∈ (Θ*,1)`.* In the strip `σ ≤ Re s ≤ c` the function `−ζ′/ζ` is meromorphic, and its only pole is at
  `s = 1`, simple with residue `1`, since there are no zeros with `Re s > Θ*`. There is a sequence `T_k → ∞` with
  `|ζ′/ζ(x ± iT_k)| ≪ log²T_k` uniformly for `x ∈ [−1, 2]` (CITED: Titchmarsh–Heath-Brown Thm 9.6(B) and its
  corollary). Combined with the decay of `h_F`, the horizontal sides vanish. So
  `(2πi)^{−1}∫_{(c)}(−ζ′/ζ)h_F = (2πi)^{−1}∫_{(σ)}(−ζ′/ζ)h_F + h_F(1)`, and `h_F(1) = Φ_F(1)Φ_F(0) = A(F)B(F)`. Hence
  `Σ v_n c_F(log n) = 2A(F)B(F) + (2/2πi)∫_{(σ)}(−ζ′/ζ)h_F`, and
  `W_T(F) = 𝔞_T(F) + 2AB = Arch_T(F) + (1/π)∫(ζ′/ζ)(σ+iτ)h_F(σ+iτ)dτ`.
  On `P_T`, `AB = 0` and `W_T = 𝔞_T`.
4. *Factorisation.* `Φ_F(σ+iτ) = ∫F(t)e^{(σ−½)t}e^{iτt}dt = ĝ_σ⁺(τ)`. Also
  `Φ_F(1−σ−iτ) = ∫F(t)e^{−(σ−½)t}e^{−iτt}dt = conj(ĝ_σ⁻(τ))`, because `g_σ^−` is real. ∎

**Theorem S′ (complete-ξ version; PROVED).** For real `F ∈ C_c^∞(I_T)` and every `σ > Θ*`,
`W_T(F) = (1/π)∫_ℝ (ξ′/ξ)(σ+iτ)h_F(σ+iτ)dτ`, where `ξ(s) = ½s(s−1)π^{−s/2}Γ(s/2)ζ(s)`.

*Proof.* `W_T(F) = Σ_ρ h_F(ρ)` (P46 l.137–142, CITED there from Weil and Paper 45 Thm 9.4). Integrate `(ξ′/ξ)h_F` over the
rectangle with vertical sides `Re s = σ` and `Re s = 1−σ` and horizontal sides at `±T_k`. The zeros of the entire function `ξ`
are the nontrivial zeros, all inside when `σ > Θ*`. The functional equation gives `ξ′/ξ(1−s) = −ξ′/ξ(s)`, and
`h_F(1−s) = h_F(s)`. The substitution `s ↦ 1−s` turns the left side into `−∫_{(σ)}`, so
`Σ_ρ h_F(ρ) = (2/2πi)∫_{(σ)}(ξ′/ξ)h_F`. The horizontal sides vanish because `(Γ′/Γ)(s/2) ≪ log|s|` in the strip. ∎

**Remark 2.1 (the three regimes of the jump calculus; PROVED as bookkeeping).**
* `σ > 1`: the integrand is `−Σ_n Λ(n)n^{−s}`, the Laplace transform of the jump train. Each contact contributes its
  exact weight, and (S) is P46 `eq:form` contact by contact.
* `σ` crossing `1`: the pole of `ζ` produces exactly `2AB`, the polar/Tate term. The "drift" `8sinh(u/2)` of R1 Thm 3 is
  the residue.
* `σ < 1`: the jumps are no longer visible individually. Their exact values are encoded in the analytic continuation of
  `ζ′/ζ`.

The primitive form differs from the complete form only by the residue at `s = 1`. That residue is invisible on `P_T`,
the interpolation condition `Φ(0) = Φ(1) = 0` (cf. P124.013).

## §3 Theorem L: the log-absorption balance

**Hypothesis H(σ, θ, C).** `Θ* < σ < 1`, `0 ≤ θ < 1`, and `|ζ′/ζ(σ+iτ)| ≤ C·L(τ)^θ` for all `τ ∈ ℝ`.

**Theorem L (PROVED under H; both forms).** Under H(σ,θ,C), put `δ := σ − ½ ∈ (0,½)`. There is `C′ = C′(σ,θ,C)` such that
for all `T ≥ 1`
```
λ^A_T ≥ λ^W_T ≥ −C′ e^{2δT/(1−θ)}, hence κ_A ≤ κ_W ≤ 2(σ−½)/(1−θ).
```

*Proof.* By Lemma 1.1 it suffices to take real `F ∈ C_c^∞(I_T)` with `‖F‖ = 1`. Write `g_± = g_σ^±`.

1. *Energy equivalence (PROVED, elementary).* Put `k(u) := 1_{|u|≤1}/|u|` and `E(g) := ∫∫|g(t)−g(s)|²k(t−s)dsdt`. By
  Plancherel, `E(g) = (2π)^{−1}∫M(τ)|ĝ(τ)|²dτ`, where `M(τ) = 4∫_0^1(1−cos τu)u^{−1}du`. Also `M ≥ 0`, and
  `M(τ) = 4log|τ| + 4γ + O(1/|τ|)` for `|τ| ≥ 1`. Hence there are absolute constants with
  `a_1∫L|ĝ|² ≤ E(g) + ‖g‖² ≤ a_2∫L|ĝ|²` (with `‖g‖² = (2π)^{−1}∫|ĝ|²`).
2. *Multiplier bound (PROVED).* Use `φ(t)F(t) − φ(s)F(s) = φ(t)(F(t)−F(s)) + F(s)(φ(t)−φ(s))`. Only pairs with
  `|t−s| ≤ 1` and one point in `supp F ⊂ I_T` count, so only `φ|_{I_{T+1}}` matters. With `φ = e^{±δt}`,
  `sup_{I_{T+1}}(|φ| + |φ′|) ≤ 2e^{δ(T+1)}`. Then
  `E(g_±) ≤ 2e^{2δ(T+1)}E(F) + 2δ²e^{2δ(T+1)}∫|F(s)|²∫_{|u|≤1}|u|du ds ≤ 2e^{2δ(T+1)}(E(F) + ‖F‖²)`.
  By step 1, `∫L|ĝ_±|² ≤ a_3 e^{2δT}∫L|F̂|²`, and trivially `‖g_±‖² ≤ e^{2δT}`.
3. *Archimedean control (PROVED).* `m(τ) = log(|τ|/2) + O(τ^{−2})` as `|τ| → ∞`, and `m` is continuous and even. So
  `m ≥ L − b` for an absolute `b`, and `(2π)^{−1}∫L|F̂|² ≤ Arch_T(F) + b`. Also `m ≥ m(0)`, since `m` is increasing
  in `|τ|` (P46 `thm:S`: `m − m(0) = φ(τ²)` with `φ` Bernstein). So `Arch_T(F) ≥ m(0)`.
4. *Young's inequality.* For `0 < η ≤ 1`, `L^θ ≤ ηL + η^{−θ/(1−θ)}`. (For `θ = 0`, take the bound `1 ≤ η^0`.) By (S),
  H, Cauchy–Schwarz and steps 2–3,
  ```
  |W_T(F) − Arch_T(F)| ≤ (C/π)∫(ηL + η^{−θ/(1−θ)})|ĝ_+||ĝ_−|
  ≤ (C/2π)[η(∫L|ĝ_+|² + ∫L|ĝ_−|²) + η^{−θ/(1−θ)}(∫|ĝ_+|² + ∫|ĝ_−|²)]
  ≤ C a_3 η e^{2δT}·2(Arch_T(F) + b) + 2C η^{−θ/(1−θ)} e^{2δT}.
  ```
5. *Choice of `η`.* Take `η := min(1, e^{−2δT}/(4Ca_3))`. The coefficient of `Arch_T(F) + b` is then `≤ ½`. Also
  `Arch_T(F) + b ≥ 0`, because `m + b ≥ L ≥ 0`. Writing `W_T ≥ Arch_T − ½(Arch_T + b) − R = ½(Arch_T + b) − b − R ≥ −b − R`
  with `R := 2Cη^{−θ/(1−θ)}e^{2δT}` gives
  ```
  W_T(F) ≥ −b − 2C(4Ca_3)^{θ/(1−θ)} e^{2δT} e^{2δTθ/(1−θ)} = −b − C″e^{2δT/(1−θ)}.
  ```
  The primitive statement follows from `λ^A ≥ λ^W`, or directly from (S) on `P_T`. ∎

**Remark 3.1 (where the loss sits; PROVED).** The chain loses in exactly two places, and both are sharp for this chain.
* **(a) The factor `e^{2δT}`.** It is attained up to constants by the two-endpoint tests
  `F = φ_0(· − T + 1) + φ_0(· + T − 1)`: `∫e^{2δt}F² ≍ ∫e^{−2δt}F² ≍ e^{2δT}`. This is the same family that realises
  `κ ≥ 2(Θ*−½)` in R4 Lemma J.
* **(b) The log absorption.** The positive part of the form is only logarithmic (P124.008: no Sobolev gain). So a
  multiplier of size `L^θ` in (S) must be paid for from `Arch_T ≍ ∫L|F̂|²`, at the Young price `η^{−θ/(1−θ)}`. If the
  domain gave one derivative, the price would be polynomial in `T`. This is the precise sense in which the P124.004
  "domain wall" (the translation generator is first order, the form is logarithmic) becomes an exponent loss.

## §4 Corollary L1: the tie with the trivial exponent

**Input (CITED, method).** Suppose `ζ(s) ≠ 0` for `Re s > Θ*`. Then for `Θ* < σ < 1` and every `ε > 0`,
`ζ′/ζ(σ+iτ) ≪_{σ,ε} L(τ)^{(1−σ)/(1−Θ*)+ε}`. This is Littlewood's argument as in Titchmarsh–Heath-Brown Thm 14.2 (stated
there for `Θ* = ½`, exponent `2−2σ+ε`, for `log ζ`). The steps are:
* Borel–Carathéodory, which gives `log ζ ≪ log t` on `Re s ≥ Θ* + ε′`;
* Hadamard's three circles between that line and `Re s = 1 + ε′`, where `log ζ = O(1)`;
* Cauchy's estimate on a disc of radius `(σ−Θ*)/2`, which passes from `log ζ` to `ζ′/ζ` at the cost of `ε`.

The general-`Θ*` form is the same proof with ½ replaced by `Θ*`. I did not re-derive it in full this phase. This is the
weakest input; see §8.

**Corollary L1 (PROVED given the input).** With `θ(σ) = (1−σ)/(1−Θ*) + ε`, Theorem L gives
```
κ_W ≤ 2(σ−½)(1−Θ*) / (σ − Θ* − ε(1−Θ*)) for every σ ∈ (Θ*, 1).
```
In the limit `ε → 0`, the right side equals `1 + 2(1−σ)(Θ*−½)/(σ−Θ*)`, which is `≥ 1`. Equality holds iff `Θ* = ½` or
`σ → 1`. So the output never beats the trivial bound `κ_W ≤ 1` (R4 §1, from Chebyshev). **Under RH it equals 1 for every
`σ ∈ (½,1)`**, whereas the truth under RH is `κ_W = 0` (Weil: `λ^W_T ≥ 0`).

*Proof.* The identity is `2(σ−½)(1−Θ*) − (σ−Θ*) = 2(1−σ)(Θ*−½)`. Expand:
`2σ − 2σΘ* − 1 + Θ* − σ + Θ* = σ − 1 − 2σΘ* + 2Θ* = (1−σ)(2Θ* − 1)`. ∎

**Remark 4.1 (what an ideal pointwise input would give; HEURISTIC).** Unconditionally,
`log ζ(σ+it) = Ω((log t)^{1−σ−ε})` for `½ < σ < 1` (CITED: Titchmarsh–Heath-Brown §8.12; Montgomery's refinement). If
the same barrier holds for `ζ′/ζ`, which I have not verified, the best conceivable `θ` is `1−σ`. With that input,
Theorem L would give `κ_W ≤ inf_{σ>Θ*} 2(σ−½)/σ = 2(Θ*−½)/Θ*`. This is within a factor `1/Θ* ∈ [1,2)` of the proved lower
bound `2(Θ*−½)` (R4 Thm G). Under RH it would give `κ_W = 0`, with no sign. The input is far beyond what is known: even
under RH, the known exponent is `2−2σ`, which gives exactly the tie of L1.

**Reading (HEURISTIC; the implications are PROVED).** For the *size* of the negativity, the round-4 panel isolated the
upper half of the growth law as a Carleson-measure question (B2's (BD)). Corollary L1 shows that the only other natural
route, pointwise bounds on the resummed jump train `ζ′/ζ` off the line, contributes **nothing** beyond the trivial
exponent with any known input. The information that the upper half needs is in the *distribution* of the off-line zeros,
not in the size of `ζ′/ζ`.

## §5 Where the sign sits in Theorem S′ (EQUIVALENT; recorded, not resubmitted)

`h_F(σ+iτ) = ĝ⁺·conj(ĝ⁻)` is a cross term for `σ ≠ ½`, and it is `|F̂(τ)|² ≥ 0` only at `σ = ½`. The sign of the
integrand of S′ is therefore carried by `Re ξ′/ξ` as `σ ↓ ½`. By Hadamard,
`Re ξ′/ξ(σ+iτ) = Σ_ρ (σ−β)/|σ+iτ−ρ|² > 0` for `σ > Θ*` (CITED; Lagarias, Acta Arith. 89 (1999)). Moreover, RH holds iff
this holds on all of `Re s > ½`, in which case `Re ξ′/ξ(½+ε+iτ)dτ → πΣ_γ m_γδ_γ` weakly, and S′ tends to Weil's
`Σ|F̂(γ)|²`.
