## G2_B1_R1: structure vs randomness (zero-free completions, centre identity)

**Lens 1.** This is not a mechanism, and it is not presented as one. The memo proves that the angle fails. Q1
(strong duality in the zero-free class) would be the zero-free reformulation "RH ⟺ ∀T ∃D: `Re D(½+iτ) ≤ ½m + a_1`".
That is category (ii) (EQUIV), as the memo says.

**Audit.**
* **Prop. 1** (PROVED for completions satisfying Def. 1). I checked the Mellin shift and the bookkeeping:
  `Σ a_n n^{−½} c(log n) = α‖F‖² + ½Σ v_n c(log n) = (2π)^{-1}∫Re D |F̂|² + r_D AB`, which gives
  `𝔞_T = (2π)^{-1}∫σ_D|F̂|² − 2r_D AB`. Correct for the Selberg `ζM_R`, which has a simple pole with residue `M_R(1)`.
  **Wrong for the Heath-Brown `D_{k,z}`**, which has a pole of order `k+1` at `s=1` (see the table). Remove them from
  the list of examples, or generalise the residue term.
* **Lemma 2** (`Re ζ′/ζ(½+iτ) = −½m(τ)`, so `C_0 = −2ζ′/ζ(½)`) is classical and correct. It follows from reality of
  `ξ` on the line and `Re[1/s + 1/(s−1)] = 0`.
* **Thm 3.** I checked (a)–(d).
  * (b): `F_T = (−∂²+¼)g_0(·/T) ∈ P_T`, and the Rayleigh quotient tends to `σ^str_R(0)` by dominated convergence. The
  bound `|σ^str_R| ≪ (1+|τ|)^{1/4+ε}` against the Schwartz `ĝ_0` suffices.
  * (c): the Laplace transform is `Σ μ(d)d^{−½−s}/s² = 1/(s²ζ(s+½))`, and the double and simple poles at 0 cancel by
  Lemma 2.
  * (d): Landau's theorem applies, because the finite initial segment gives an entire contribution. `ζ(σ) ≠ 0` on
  `(−2,1)`, so `G` is real-analytic on `(−5/2, ∞)`. The pole at `iγ_1` gives the contradiction.
  * **Correct, unconditional.**
  * Reading: fixed-`R` `Λ_R` differs from `Λ` beyond `R`. So Thm 3 is a new member of the `thm:finmod`/`cor:void`
  family, a modified comb with no zeros that fails. It is not a statement about ζ's form.
* **Prop. 5(ii)–(iii)** are method-level arguments, labelled "arg.", not theorems. That is acceptable for an exclusion.
  Regime Q2 (`R = e^{2θT}`) is untreated, but closing it would only strengthen the exclusion. It is not a door to a
  mechanism, because by Prop. 4 and O3 any structured part must reproduce `λ^A_T` to within `e^{−15.4T}`.

**Verdict: EXCLUDED** (as a mechanism: F2, F4, O3/E3). Salvage: Prop. 1 for simple-pole completions, Lemma 2 as the
exact criticality of the centre, and Thm 3 as a zero-free falsifier.

## G2_B4_R1: SOS / Positivstellensatz

**Lens 1.** Every branch is closed. The translation-covariant SOS is the zero side (S1). Windowed SOS equals the Paper
47 certificates, and existence for all T equals RH (S2, category ii). The algebraic Positivstellensatz is impossible
(S3). Inequality certificates are margin-type (Cor. S3.3). Category: EXCLUDED angle, not a mechanism.

**Audit.**
* **S1(c)** is correct.
  * Temperedness: `|χ̂| ≥ cos(1)∫χ` on `[−1,1]`, and `g` has finite order on `[−2,2]`.
  * Uniqueness: polarization, then `Ḡ̂ν = 0`. The primitive-case codimension argument is right, since
  `αe^{t/2}+βe^{−t/2}` is tempered only if `α = β = 0`.
* **S1(b)** is correct: `𝒟′_{L²}` by closed graph, local division by one of two entire transforms, and an a.c. measure
  cannot equal atomic `μ_Z`. It is unconditional because the certificate's existence already gives `W ≥ 0`, hence RH,
  hence (EF). The content is modest: the spectral measure of the Weil distribution is `μ_Z`.
* **S2(c)**: the soft `lim λ^W ≤ 0` is correct, subject to the compactly written tail-uniformity step. That step is
  standard.
* **S3(a)**: the Baker input (β_0 + Σβ_i log α_i ≠ 0 for ℚ-independent logs) is quoted correctly. The linear algebra
  of prime-power relations is correct, subject to the `V_n = 0` correction in the table.
* S3(b) is correct: `𝒩_T = {2}` and `√2 log 2` is transcendental. S3(c) (Schanuel) is correctly labelled conditional.
* **Audit point on Def. S3.1.** Requiring positivity **identically on the real variety** `V_ℝ(I)` builds `cor:void`
  into the definition. S3 then says that algebra over ℚ̄ cannot separate `Λ` from a failing member of the same
  variety. That is true, but no serious proof was ever expected to have this form: Paper 42's proof is an inequality.
  So S3 excludes an architecture, not a live candidate. It is still a correct theorem.

**Verdict: EXCLUDED** (no mechanism). Salvage: S1 as the formal closure of R157_002 row 51. The "weight = position"
remark (§4.3.3) marks the only door the SOS angle leaves: an analytic identity that couples `Λ(p) = log p` to the atom
position `log p`. The only known such identity is the explicit formula (OPEN otherwise).

## G2_B7_R1: Bohr–Jessen / Kronecker; Haar-null Weil positivity

**Lens 1.** Not a mechanism. The memo proves that every mean-value, ergodic or almost-sure prime input is blind to the
point `θ = 0`. Category: EXCLUDED angle.

**Audit of Thm B** (the step the memo flagged).
* Step 0: `c_{F_T}(u)/‖F_T‖² = r_T(u/T)` follows from `F̂_T(τ) = (τ²+¼)Tĝ(Tτ)`. Correct.
* **Sanity check at θ = 0.** For ζ itself, `X_T(0)` is not large. `F_T ∈ P_T` kills `∫e^{u/2}c_F`, which is the main
  term of the comb, and what remains is the `ψ(x) − x` fluctuation. So nothing contradicts RH. The twist destroys that
  cancellation, and this is why the variance `≍ T²` appears.
* Step 4:
  * `Var(v_p cos θ_p r) = 2log²p/p·r²`.
  * The primes in `(y, y²]` with `y = e^{x₀T_j/2}` lie in `B_j` once `T_j ≥ 4T_{j−1}/x₀`. This holds because
  `T_j/T_{j−1} = 2^{2j−1}`.
  * `r ≥ ½` there, which gives `s_j² ≥ x₀²T_j²/8·(1+o(1))`.
  * `E R_j² ≪ T_{j−1}²` by orthogonality of distinct `(p,k)`.
  * **Correct.**
* Step 5:
  * The Berry–Esseen error `≤ C·1.48/s_j` holds for bounded non-identical summands.
  * `A_j` depends only on `Y_j`, so the `A_j` are independent across `j`. No independence between `R_j` and `Y_j` is
  used.
  * `Σ μ(D_j) ≪ Σ 4^{−(2j−1)} < ∞`.
  * **Correct.**
* Step 6 (Borel–Cantelli, both halves) is correct.
* Thm C is Wintner-type and correct: Kolmogorov maximal inequality plus Borel–Cantelli. It is classical in substance
  (random Euler products) and could be CITED.
* The Cor. A′ gap is described in the table. It must be restated for the complex twist.

**Verdict: EXCLUDED** (no mechanism). Salvage: Thm B is the strongest new obstruction of the round. For almost every
twist, `liminf λ^θ_T/T ≤ −c`, and almost every such twist has `ψ_θ ≪ x^{½+ε}`. Together these prove that
square-root cancellation of prime sums does not imply Weil positivity unless the Γ-factor is matched to the Euler
product (functional equation). They also give G2 the concrete **a.e.-twist test**: a surviving mechanism must fail for
almost every θ, and must therefore use the functional equation at a load-bearing line.

## Consolidated recommendation

1. Round 1 of G2 produces **no mechanism**. It gives three proved falsifier families, all valid for every `T` past a
  fixed threshold:
  * zero-free structured combs (B1 Thm 3);
  * twists by unimodular characters, both the Haar-mean version (B3/B7 Thm A) and the almost-sure version with rate
  (B7 Thm B);
  * the explicit real-coefficient flip at 2 (B3 Thm B).
2. Together they sharpen F1/F4 into one checkable **twist test**. A candidate must visibly use, at a load-bearing
  step, the phase and positivity of the local atoms coupled to the archimedean place, i.e. the functional equation.
  Any argument that also runs for `Λχ` with almost every χ, or for `Λχ_2`, is unsound. Round-2 memos should be
  given this test up front.
3. No label changes. Row D stays open. The premises of record (nothing proved for `T > 0.8`, `thm:finZ`, positivity
  only from the prime side) are respected by all seven memos. None claims a sign.
