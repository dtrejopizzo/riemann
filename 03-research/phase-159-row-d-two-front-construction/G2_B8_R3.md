## §1 The stability dictionary at the window

**Definitions (all from Paper 47).** `I_T = (−T,T)`; `c_F(u) = ∫F(t)\overline{F(t−u)}dt`; `𝒩_T = {n ≥ 2 : log n < 2T}`;
`v_n = 2Λ(n)/√n`; `m(τ) = Re ψ(¼ + iτ/2) − log π = C_0 + ∫_0^∞(1 − cos τu)K(u)du`, `C_0 = ψ(¼) − log π = −5.3721834…`,
`K(u) = 2e^{3u/2}/(e^{2u} − 1) > 0` (lem:K). For real `F` in the form domain (thm:A, eq:Aform):

  `𝔞_T(F) = C_0‖F‖² + Lév(c_F) − Σ_{n∈𝒩_T} v_n c_F(log n)`, `Lév(c_F) = ∫_0^∞ (c_F(0) − c_F(u))K(u)du = ½∫_0^∞‖F − F_u‖²K(u)du ≥ 0`,

with `F_u = F(·−u)`. The **archimedean form** is `𝔄_∞(F) := 𝔞^0_T(F) = C_0‖F‖² + Lév(c_F)` (empty comb). The **local
prime form** at `p` is `Q_p(F) := −Σ_{k≥1, k log p<2T} v_{p^k} c_F(k log p)` (cor:Qp), so that `𝔞_T = 𝔄_∞ + Σ_{p<e^{2T}} Q_p`
on the form domain. `P_T = {F : A(F) = B(F) = 0}` with `A(F) = ∫F e^{t/2}`, `B(F) = ∫F e^{−t/2}`; the complete form is
`W_T = 𝔞_T + 2Re(A\bar B)`, and `W_T = 𝔞_T` on `P_T`.

**Stability language.** The Euler factor `(1 − p^{−s})^{−1}` is "stable" in three classical senses: (S1) it is
zero-free on `Re s > 0` (Lee–Yang / Hurwitz sense); (S2) its logarithmic derivative on `Re s = ½`,
`τ ↦ log p Σ_k p^{−k/2}p^{−ikτ}`, is the Fourier transform of the positive measure `log p Σ_k p^{−k/2}δ_{k log p}`
(Bochner sense); (S3) it is a completely monotone function of `σ = Re s` on `σ > 0` (Bernstein sense). The
archimedean factor `Γ_ℝ(s) = π^{−s/2}Γ(s/2)` is stable in sense (S1) (zero-free) but its *reciprocal* carries the trivial
zeros, and its logarithmic derivative on the line is `−m(τ)`-type: conditionally **negative** definite (thm:S).

**Remark B (the sign of the stable term). PROVED (elementary).** For every prime `p < e^{2T}` and every `T`, the local
prime symbol `σ_p(τ) := Σ_{k: k log p<2T} v_{p^k}cos(kτ log p)` is a positive definite function of `τ ∈ ℝ` (it is the
Fourier transform of the symmetric positive measure `Σ_k (v_{p^k}/2)(δ_{k log p} + δ_{−k log p})`), and so is the whole
prime symbol `Σ_{n∈𝒩_T}v_n cos(τ log n)`. The archimedean symbol `m(τ) − C_0 = φ(τ²)` is conditionally negative
definite (thm:S(ii): Lévy–Khintchine with Lévy measure `½K(|u|)du`). The window symbol is
`σ_T = C_0 + [cond. neg. def.] − [pos. def.]`, and `𝔞_T(F) = (1/2π)∫σ_T|F̂|²`.

*Consequence (PROVED as stated).* Any operation `𝒪` on Dirichlet series that preserves (S1), (S2) or (S3) at every
finite place preserves the positive definiteness of the prime symbol, i.e. the sign structure of the term that enters
`σ_T` **negatively**. The positivity of `𝔞_T` on `P_T` is the statement that the negative-definite archimedean energy
dominates the positive-definite prime energy on `P_T`, up to the constant `C_0 < 0`. It is not a stability property of
the primes; it is a *domination* of the primes by the archimedean place. Schur products, Dirichlet convolutions
(= products of L-functions, which **add** combs), Asano contractions and Pólya–Schur multiplier sequences act on the
dominated term. None can raise the dominating one. This is row 42's "wrong direction" stated at the window. ∎

The one construction this leaves is: dominate each `Q_p` separately by a share of `𝔄_∞`. Theorem L kills it.

## §2 Theorem L: no shared-budget local certificate

**Definition (shared-budget local decomposition).** Fix `T` and nonnegative scalars `(α_p)_{p prime, p<e^{2T}}` with
`Σ_p α_p ≤ 1`. The associated **local forms** are `q_p := α_p 𝔄_∞ + Q_p`. (If `Σα_p = 1`, then `𝔞_T = Σ_p q_p`; if
`Σα_p < 1`, the remainder `(1 − Σα_p)𝔄_∞` may be assigned to the archimedean place as `q_∞`.) A decomposition
**certifies** `RD_T` if every `q_p ≥ 0` on `P_T ∩ (form domain)`.

**Theorem L. PROVED, unconditional.** Let `T ≥ 4.70`. For every choice of shares with `Σ_p α_p ≤ 1` there is a prime
`p ≤ 109` and a real `F ∈ P_T ∩ H¹_0(I_T)` with `q_p(F) = α_p𝔄_∞(F) + Q_p(F) < 0`. Hence no shared-budget local
decomposition certifies `RD_T`, for the primitive form on `P_T`; and since `W_T = 𝔞_T` on `P_T` and any share of the
polar term `2Re(A\bar B)` vanishes on `P_T`, the same holds for the complete form restricted to `P_T`.

Constants: `C'' := 4.65`, `ℓ_p := ½log(1 + 1/p)`, `D_p := 2C_0 + 4C'' + 2log(1 + 9π²/ℓ_p²)`. Then `D_p > 0` for all `p`
(`D_2 = 23.213…`, `D_3 = 24.586…`, increasing in `p`), and

  **(L)** `Σ_{p ≤ 109} v_p/D_p = 1.00400… > 1`, `v_p = 2 log p/√p`.

*Proof.* Throughout `p ≤ 109` is prime and `T ≥ 4.70 > log 109 = 4.6913…`, so `log p ≤ T`.

*Step 1 (the tests).* Put `u_0 = log p`, `ℓ = ℓ_p`, `J = (−T, −T + ℓ)`. Check the constraints of lem:cpos:
`ℓ < log(1 + 1/p) ≤ log(3/2) < log 2 ≤ u_0`, and `2T − u_0 ≥ T ≥ 4.70 > ℓ`. So `J`, `J + u_0`, `J + 2u_0` are pairwise
disjoint and `J ∪ (J + u_0) ⊂ I_T`. Let `ψ_j(x) = √2 sin(jπx)` on `(0,1)`, `j = 1,2,3`, and `φ_j(t) = ℓ^{−1/2}ψ_j((t + T)/ℓ)`,
so `(φ_j)` is orthonormal in `L²(J)`, `φ_j ∈ H¹_0(J)`, and `(φ_j′)` is orthogonal with `‖φ_j′‖² = j²π²/ℓ²`. For
`c ∈ ℝ³`, `|c| = 1`, put `φ_c = Σc_jφ_j` and `F_c = φ_c + φ_c(·−u_0)`. Then `‖φ_c‖ = 1`, `‖F_c‖² = 2` (disjoint supports),
and `‖φ_c′‖² = Σ c_j² j²π²/ℓ² ≤ 9π²/ℓ²`. The map `c ↦ F_c` is linear and injective into `L²(I_T)`, and `A`, `B` are
continuous linear functionals, so `{c : F_c ∈ P_T}` is a subspace of `ℝ³` of dimension `≥ 1`. Fix a unit `c` in it and
write `φ = φ_c`, `F = F_c ∈ P_T`. `F ∈ H¹_0(I_T)` lies in the form domain (`∫τ²|F̂|² < ∞`), so eq:Aform applies.

*Step 2 (only one contact is touched).* `c_φ` is supported in `[−ℓ, ℓ]`. Expanding `F = φ + φ_{u_0}`,
`c_F(u) = ⟨φ,φ_u⟩ + ⟨φ_{u_0},φ_{u_0+u}⟩ + ⟨φ,φ_{u_0+u}⟩ + ⟨φ_{u_0},φ_u⟩ = 2c_φ(u) + c_φ(u + u_0) + c_φ(u − u_0)`.
So `supp c_F ⊂ [−ℓ,ℓ] ∪ ±[u_0 − ℓ, u_0 + ℓ]`, and at `u = u_0`: `2c_φ(u_0) = 0` (`u_0 > ℓ`), `c_φ(2u_0) = 0`, and
`c_φ(0) = ‖φ‖² = 1`; hence `c_F(u_0) = 1`.
Contacts in the support: `log n ≤ ℓ` is impossible for `n ≥ 2` (`ℓ ≤ ℓ_2 = 0.2027 < log 2`). If `|log n − log p| ≤ ℓ`
with `n ≠ p`, then `|log(n/p)| ≥ min(log((p+1)/p), log(p/(p−1))) = log(1 + 1/p) > ℓ`, a contradiction. Hence
`Σ_{n∈𝒩_T}v_n c_F(log n) = v_p c_F(log p) = v_p`, and in particular `Q_p(F) = −v_p` (the higher powers `k ≥ 2` do not
meet the support, as in cor:Qp) while `Q_{p′}(F) = 0` for `p′ ≠ p`.

*Step 3 (the archimedean energy of the test).* For real `G`, `c_G(0) − c_G(u) = ½‖G − G_u‖²`, so
`Lév(c_G) = ½∫_0^∞‖G − G_u‖²K(u)du`. Since `F − F_u = (φ − φ_u) + (φ_{u_0} − φ_{u_0+u})`, the triangle inequality
gives `‖F − F_u‖ ≤ 2‖φ − φ_u‖`, hence `Lév(c_F) ≤ 4Lév(c_φ)`. By eq:m and Plancherel (proof of thm:A),
`Lév(c_φ) = (1/2π)∫(m(τ) − C_0)|φ̂(τ)|²dτ`.

*Bound on the symbol.* Claim: `m(τ) − C_0 ≤ ½log(1 + τ²) + C''` for all `τ ∈ ℝ`, with `C'' = 4.65`. By lem:mbounds(ii),
for `τ > 0`: `m(τ) ≤ log(τ/2π) + 1/τ + ½log(1 + 1/(4τ²))`. For `τ ≥ 1`, using `log τ ≤ ½log(1 + τ²)`,
`m(τ) − C_0 − ½log(1+τ²) ≤ −log 2π − C_0 + 1 + ½log(5/4) = 4.6458…`. For `0 ≤ τ ≤ 1`, lem:mbounds(i) (`m` increasing)
gives `m(τ) − C_0 ≤ m(1) − C_0 ≤ 4.6458…` and `½log(1+τ²) ≥ 0`. `m` is even. (Numerically the sharp constant is the
limit `−log 2 − ψ(¼) = 3.5343…`, approached from below; the rigorous value from the cited lemma is what is used.)

*Jensen.* `|φ̂|²dτ/(2π‖φ‖²)` is a probability measure with second moment `‖φ′‖²/‖φ‖²`; since `x ↦ ½log(1 + x)` is
concave, `(1/2π)∫½log(1 + τ²)|φ̂|² ≤ ‖φ‖²·½log(1 + ‖φ′‖²/‖φ‖²) ≤ ½log(1 + 9π²/ℓ²)`. Therefore
`Lév(c_φ) ≤ ½log(1 + 9π²/ℓ²) + C''` and

  `𝔄_∞(F) = C_0‖F‖² + Lév(c_F) ≤ 2C_0 + 4C'' + 2log(1 + 9π²/ℓ_p²) = D_p`.

*Step 4 (pigeonhole).* Suppose `q_p(F) ≥ 0` for the test `F = F^{(p)}` of Steps 1–3, for every prime `p ≤ 109`. Then
`α_p D_p ≥ α_p𝔄_∞(F^{(p)}) ≥ −Q_p(F^{(p)}) = v_p`, i.e. `α_p ≥ v_p/D_p` (`D_p > 0`). Summing,
`1 ≥ Σ_{p<e^{2T}}α_p ≥ Σ_{p≤109}v_p/D_p = 1.0040 > 1` by (L). Contradiction. So some `q_p(F^{(p)}) < 0`. ∎

**Remarks on Theorem L.**
1. *Inputs.* eq:Aform (thm:A), lem:mbounds(i)(ii), the lem:cpos construction, and the numerical sum (L) (39 terms,
  elementary; script of §8). No zero, no functional equation, no value of `Λ` beyond `Λ(p) = log p` at the 29 primes
  `≤ 109`. Unconditional.
2. *What it adds to the record.* `cor:Qp` is the case `α_p = 0` (each `Q_p` alone indefinite). `thm:SC` gives a
  single sub-comb the **whole** budget and needs mass `< 5.37`; it does not bound sums of shares. Theorem L is the
  statement that the archimedean budget cannot be *distributed* by scalars so that every local factor is dominated:
  the cost of dominating `p` alone is `≥ v_p/D_p ≍ p^{−1/2}`, and `Σ_p p^{−1/2}` over `p < e^{2T}` is `≍ e^T/T`, far
  above the unit budget. The threshold `109` (`T ≥ 4.70`) is an artefact of `C''` and of the `H¹_0` test; a sharper
  symbol bound or a smoother profile lowers it, but no value of `T` in `(0.8, 4.70)` is claimed.
3. *Both forms.* Only `P_T` is used, where `W_T = 𝔞_T`. For the complete form on all of `L²(I_T)` one would also
  distribute the polar term; on `P_T` it vanishes identically, so the contradiction is unchanged.
4. *What Theorem L does not cover (OPEN).* Non-scalar splittings of the archimedean place: `K = Σ_p K_p`,
  `C_0 = Σ_p C_p`, `q_p = C_p‖F‖² + Lév_{K_p}(c_F) + Q_p`. The obstacle is real: `∫_0^∞ K(u)du = ∞` (`K ∼ 1/u` at 0),
  so the small-scale archimedean budget is infinite and can be sliced among infinitely many primes. The test of
  Steps 1–3 sees only the part of `K_p` at scales `≳ ℓ_p`. I do not claim Theorem L for such splittings. Any
  mechanism that survived through this door would still have to pass F1 (it would be a class-level statement about
  `Λ ≥ 0`, hence excluded by `cor:void`), so the door is not a route to the sign either.

## §3 Theorem NM: the four stability-type derivations, each excluded

The notion "derivation of `RD_T(Λ)` whose only prime input is membership in a class `𝒞 ∋ Λ`" is P46 Thm 18.1(ii) /
P47 `cor:void`. Write `v^0 = v_T(Λ)`, `V_T(v) = λ_min(A^{(v)}_T|P_T)` (lem:degen; concave in `v`).

**Theorem NM. PROVED (assembled); a statement about derivations, not about ζ.** Let `T ≥ 0.60`. A derivation of
`RD_T(Λ)` proves a false statement if it is valid for a class `𝒞` of nonnegative combs with any one of:
* (a) **monotone in the comb** — `𝒞` closed under deletion of finitely many terms (then fails for `T > T_0(0) = 0.749`)
  or under amplification of one term (`T > ½log2`): `cor:void`, `thm:finmod`. Lee–Yang / GKS-type monotonicity in the
  coupling cone is (a).
* (b) **homogeneous in the coupling** — `𝒞 ∋ βΛ` for some `β > β_+(T)` or `β < β_−(T)`: G2_B8_R2 Theorem R(2). The
  positive set along the ray is a compact interval missing `β = 0`; for integer `β = k > β_+(T)` the member `ζ^k` has
  an Euler product, an FE and ζ's zeros (Cor R′).
* (c) **equivariant under Satake rotation** — `𝒞 ⊇ {v(θ) : θ ∈ 𝕋_T}`: G2_B8_R1 Theorem S(1) / G2_B7_R2 Thm A (the
  Haar mean of the rotated bottoms is `≤ λ^∞_P(T) < 0`, so the failure set has positive Haar measure), and G2_B7 Thm B.
  Asano contractions and every zero-side stability argument are equivariant.
* (d) **local with scalar archimedean shares** — the derivation certifies `q_p = α_p𝔄_∞ + Q_p ≥ 0` separately:
  Theorem L, `T ≥ 4.70`.

*Proof.* Each clause cites a proved failure inside the class; a derivation valid on the class would prove `RD_T`
for the failing member. ∎

**Reading (HEURISTIC).** (a)–(d) exhaust the ways a Lee–Yang / Pólya–Schur argument uses its hypotheses: order,
scale, phase, locality. Round 2 added the conductor (Prop K: FE-extendable data are dense in `𝕋_T`, the FE pins the
scalar `log q`). What all of them fail to see is the same object: the exact scale of `Λ` against `Γ_ℝ` (Theorem R),
the exact phase `θ = 0` (Theorem S), the exact conductor `1` (Prop K), and the exact collective balance of the primes
against the archimedean place (Theorem L). Hamburger's theorem (G2_B3_R2 Thm H) says that fixing these is fixing ζ.
The positivity content of "the function is ζ" is the explicit formula and its one-sided smoothing (thm:Z, cor:RH): E3.
**EQUIV.**

## §4 The remaining stability-side candidates of the literature, at the window

All are on record as EQUIV (R157_002 rows 39–42). I add only what a G2 reader needs: where the exact `Λ(n)` would
enter, and why they do not.

* **Herglotz / Hermite–Biehler (row 39).** RH ⟺ `Re(ξ′/ξ)(s) > 0` on `Re s > ½` (Hinkkanen; Lagarias 1999, CITED).
  The unconditional half, `Re(ξ′/ξ)(s) > 0` for `Re s ≥ 1`, follows from `ζ(1 + it) ≠ 0`, whose prime-side proof is
  Mertens's `3 + 4cos θ + cos 2θ ≥ 0` applied to `Σ Λ(n)n^{−σ}cos(t log n)`. That inequality uses **only** `Λ ≥ 0`: a
  class property, so by `cor:void` it decides no window `> ½log2`. The Poisson-kernel tests that express the Herglotz
  property are not compactly supported (window `T = ∞`), and moving the half-plane from `Re s ≥ 1` to `Re s > ½` by a
  stability-preserving flow is row 41 (arithmetic-blind, P13 Thm N5).
* **Laguerre–Pólya / Jensen / Turán (row 40).** Each finite order is unconditional (GORZ), the full order is RH, and
  P15 `thm:hr` shows the limit form is degenerate (no uniform gap) — `thm:margin` in another dress. P14 `prop:blind`:
  a stability-*preserving* operator cannot distinguish ζ from `cos t`.
* **de Bruijn–Newman (row 41).** `Λ_dBN ≥ 0` (Rodgers–Tao, CITED) says ζ is *exactly at* the boundary of the LP class,
  the zero-side twin of Theorem R(3) (`β = 1` within `margin/|D_T|` of `∂I_T`). Both are the no-margin statement F2.
* **Lee–Yang / Kurasov–Sarnak (row 42).** Remark B: the Bochner/Lee–Yang sign lives on the dominated term.

No stability-side candidate is left that is not (i) EQUIV to RH by a cited theorem, or (ii) excluded by Theorem NM.
