## 2. The arithmetic structured part

**Definition G (`W`-periodic model; CITED for its role, PROVED as a definition).** For `W ≥ 1` put
```
s_W(n) := (W/φ(W))·1_{(n,W)=1} (n ≥ 1), r_W := Λ − s_W .
```
This is the structured part of `Λ` in Green–Tao "Linear equations in primes" (Ann. Math. 171, 2010, §5, with
`W = Π_{p ≤ w}p`), before the Siegel correction; the Siegel-corrected model differs by a term
`−(W/φ(W))χ_S(n)·(…)` supported on a possible exceptional character, which we treat in Remark G2. The theorem of
Green–Tao–Ziegler (Ann. Math. 175 (2012), with Green–Tao "Möbius…nilsequences", Ann. Math. 175 (2012)) is that
`r_W` restricted to `[N]` (after the `W`-trick rescaling) has small Gowers `U^k` norms for every fixed `k`: CITED,
quantitatively `o_{w→∞}(1)`, ineffective/qualitative in the strength needed below.

**Lemma G1 (Dirichlet series of the model; PROVED).** For `Re s > 1`,
```
S_W(s) := Σ_n s_W(n) n^{-s} = (W/φ(W))·ζ(s)·Π_{p|W}(1 − p^{-s}) .
```
`S_W` is `ζ` times a Dirichlet polynomial; its only pole is at `s = 1`, simple, with residue
`(W/φ(W))Π_{p|W}(1−1/p) = 1`; its zeros are the zeros of `ζ` together with the zeros of `Π_{p|W}(1−p^{-s})`
(`s = 2πik/log p`, on `Re s = 0`). *Proof.* `1_{(n,W)=1} = Σ_{d|(n,W)}μ(d)`, so
`Σ 1_{(n,W)=1}n^{-s} = Σ_{d|W}μ(d)d^{-s}ζ(s) = ζ(s)Π_{p|W}(1−p^{-s})`. ∎

**Theorem G (the arithmetic structured part is a rigid `ζ`-type completion and never reproduces the window; PROVED).**
In the notation of R2 §2 (`a_1 = α`, `a_n = Λ(n)` for `2 ≤ n < X = e^{2T}`; a `ζ`-type completion is `D = ζ·P`
with coefficients `a = 1*b`):
(i) `S_W = ζ·P_W` with `b^W_d = (W/φ(W))μ(d)1_{d|W}`: `s_W` is a `ζ`-type completion supported on `d | W`.
(ii) For every `W` and every `X > 2`, `s_W` does **not** reproduce the window comb: by R2 Theorem R a reproducing
`ζ`-type completion has `b_d = μ(d)(α − log d)` for all `d < X`, which is nonzero at every squarefree `d < X`
with `log d ≠ α`; while `b^W_d = 0` for `d ∤ W`. Taking any squarefree `d < X`, `d ∤ W`, `log d ≠ α` (exists as
soon as `X > 3`) gives the contradiction. Concretely `s_W(n) ≠ Λ(n)` already at any prime `p ∤ W`, `p < X`,
`p ≠ W/φ(W)`.
(iii) The same holds for every periodic structured part: if `s` is periodic mod `q` (any `q`), then `Σ s(n)n^{-s}`
is a linear combination of `L(s,χ)`, `χ mod q`, times finite Euler factors, hence of the form `ζ·P + (zero-free-
at-`s=1` remainder)`; the `ζ`-component is rigid by R2 Theorem R and the `L(s,χ)` components (`χ ≠ χ_0`) have
coefficients of mean zero, so no periodic `s` reproduces `Λ` on `[2, X)` for `X` beyond the period's least prime
non-divisor. *(Elementary: `Λ` is not periodic on any window `[2,X)` containing two primes `p < p′` with `p′ − p`
a multiple of `q` plus a composite.)* ∎

**Reading.** The Tao decomposition at the arithmetic level is exactly the analytic one of R1–R2 seen through
`d | W` instead of `d ≤ R`: both structured parts are `ζ × (Dirichlet polynomial)`, and R2 Theorem R is the
statement that no such object equals the window comb. Whatever "structure" one extracts from `Λ` on the band, it is
a different comb on the band, and its window form is that of a modified comb (`thm:finmod` / `cor:void` family),
never a summand of ζ's own form on `P_T` with a controlled complement.

**Remark G2 (Siegel correction; PROVED as a statement about poles).** The Siegel-corrected model adds to `s_W` a
term whose Dirichlet series is `(const)·L(s,χ_S)·(finite Euler product)` for a real character `χ_S`; this is
regular at `s = 1`, has zeros at the zeros of `L(s,χ_S)`, and is again a `ζ`-free, pole-free component. It does not
change (ii)–(iii) of Theorem G (it is not a `ζ`-type completion at all and contributes no `Λ(p)` for `p ∤ W`
beyond its mean-zero periodic pattern), and it does not change Prop. Z below except by adding the zeros of
`L(s,χ_S)` (with negative residues) to the structured side — i.e. the Siegel correction moves zeros from the
pseudorandom side to the structured side, which is the wrong direction for a positivity mechanism.

## 3. The exact split: pole to the structured side, zero measure to the pseudorandom side

**Proposition Z (exact explicit formula of the split; PROVED; both forms).** Let `T > 0`, `F ∈ C_c^∞(I_T)` real,
and let `𝔞_T^{(a)}(F)` denote the primitive window form with the comb `a` in place of `Λ` (R1 Def. 1 / Prop 1),
so that `𝔞_T = 𝔞_T^{(s_W)} + 𝔞_T^{(r_W)}` by linearity (this is the *only* exact identity the split provides).
Then:
(i) (structured side, empty zero side) `𝔞_T^{(s_W)}(F) = (2π)^{-1}∫σ_W(τ)|F̂(τ)|²dτ/… − 2A(F)B(F)` with the
`T`-independent symbol
```
σ_W(τ) = m(τ) + 2(W/φ(W)) − 2(W/φ(W))·Re[ ζ(½+iτ)Π_{p|W}(1−p^{-½-iτ}) ] ,
```
(the term `2W/φ(W)` is the `n = 1` coefficient `s_W(1)`, counted as in R1 Prop 1 through `a_1 c_F(0)`; the
residue `r_D = 1` of Lemma G1 gives the polar term `−2AB`). This is Prop 1 of R1 for `D = S_W`, whose hypotheses
(a),(c),(d) hold by Lemma G1 (convexity bound `|ζ(½+iτ)| ≪ (1+|τ|)^{1/4+ε}` for the shift of contour).
(ii) (pseudorandom side, pole-free, all zeros) The Dirichlet series of `r_W` is
`R_W(s) = −ζ′/ζ(s) − S_W(s)`, **regular at `s = 1`** (residues `1 − 1 = 0`), with poles exactly at the zeros of
`ζ` (residue `−m_ρ` at `ρ`, cf. R2 §1 item 2) and at the trivial zeros. Hence, on `P_T`,
```
𝔞_T^{(r_W)}(F) = W_T(F) − (2π)^{-1}∫σ_W|F̂|² (F ∈ P_T),
```
and by `thm:Z` (Paper 47) `W_T(F)` is the zero-side sum. The pseudorandom part carries the **whole** zero measure
and *no* polar term; the structured part carries the pole and *no* zero.
(iii) (complete form) `W_T(F) = (2π)^{-1}∫σ_W|F̂|² + 𝔞_T^{(r_W)}(F)` for all real `F ∈ C_c^∞(I_T)`: the polar
term `+2AB` of `eq:W` is exactly the residue of the structured side.

*Proof.* (i) is R1 Prop 1 with `D = S_W`, the `n = 1` term handled as in R1 (the panel checked that step, G2_P3_R1).
(ii): residue bookkeeping from Lemma G1 and `−ζ′/ζ`; the identity follows by subtracting (i) from
`𝔞_T = W_T − 2AB` (`eq:W`, real `F`) and using that on `P_T` the polar term vanishes (`A(F) = B(F) = 0`). (iii) is
(i) + linearity without the `P_T` restriction. ∎

**Reading (E3).** Prop. Z is the exact content of "`Λ` = structured + Gowers-uniform" for the window form: the
structured part is an *explicit* pole-plus-archimedean symbol, the pseudorandom part is *exactly* the zero measure.
The sign question `𝔞_T ≥ 0 on P_T` becomes `(2π)^{-1}∫σ_W|F̂|² + [zero sum] ≥ 0`; the second summand is the
object whose positivity is RH's content (`thm:Z`, E3), and the first is a fixed symbol. Nothing has been gained,
and the split is exactly R1 Prop 5(i) with `Λ_R` replaced by `s_W`.

**Corollary Z1 (the structured form fails for infinitely many `W`, and its symbol is not one-signed; (a) PROVED,
(b) HEURISTIC).**
(a) Centre value: `σ_W(0) = C_0 + 2(W/φ(W))[1 − ζ(½)Π_{p|W}(1−p^{-½})] = C_0 + 2(W/φ(W))[1 + 1.4603545…·Π_{p|W}(1−p^{-½})]`.
For `W = 1`: `σ_1(0) = C_0 + 2 + 2.9207 = −0.4514 < 0`, so by the `prop:finsupp` argument (R1 Thm 3(b) with the
`T`-independent symbol `σ_1`) `limsup_{T→∞}λ_min(A_T(s_1)|P_T) ≤ −0.45 < 0`: the form of the trivial model
`s_1 ≡ 1` is negative on `P_T` for all large `T`. For `W ≥ 2`, `σ_W(0) > 0` (e.g. `W = 2`: `+0.34`; `W → ∞`
along primorials: `σ_W(0) ~ 2W/φ(W) → +∞` since `Π(1−p^{-½}) → 0`), so the centre carries no obstruction and
no sign.
(b) For `W ≥ 2` the symbol `σ_W` is expected to be unbounded below in `τ`: `m(τ) ~ log|τ|` while `Re[ζ(½+iτ)·
Π_{p|W}(1−p^{-½-iτ})]` exceeds `exp(c√(log τ/log log τ))` on a set of `τ` of positive lower density in
suitable ranges (large values of `ζ` by the resonance method — Soundararajan, Math. Ann. 342 (2008); Bondarenko–Seip,
Duke 166 (2017) — combined with Kronecker alignment of the finitely many phases `p^{-iτ}`, `p | W`, to fix the sign
of the real part). The alignment step for the *real part* with a twist is not in the cited papers as stated; hence
HEURISTIC. If (b) holds, `inf_τ σ_W < 0` and the form of `s_W` fails on `P_T` for all large `T` for every `W`
(`prop:finsupp` argument). Not load-bearing; it would only strengthen a falsifier.

## 4. Why Gowers uniformity cannot enter, exactly

**Proposition E (every use of uniformity is an estimate, and estimates are excluded; PROVED as a reduction).**
(i) The window form is a *linear* functional of the comb, `𝔞_T^{(a)}(F) = α‖F‖² − Σ_{n∈N_T} (2a_n/√n)c_F(log n) +
(2π)^{-1}∫m|F̂|²`. A bound on `|𝔞_T^{(r_W)}(F)|` from Gowers uniformity of `r_W` is a bound on a single linear
correlation of `r_W` with the smooth weight `n ↦ c_F(log n)/√n`; such correlations are controlled by the `U²`
(indeed by the Fourier/`U¹`-type major-arc) behaviour of `r_W`, i.e. by `R_W(½+iτ) = −ζ′/ζ(½+iτ) − S_W(½+iτ)` on
the critical line — which is the zero side again (Prop. Z(ii)). No `U^k`, `k ≥ 3`, information is ever used by a
quadratic form in `F` that is linear in `a`.
(ii) Any such bound has the shape `|𝔞_T^{(r_W)}(F)| ≤ ε(W,T)·‖F‖²_{(something)}` with `ε` an *estimate*
(qualitative `o(1)` in GTZ; at best a power of `log` saving unconditionally). By `thm:margin`
(`λ^A_T ≤ 4·10⁹·T·e^{−15.449T}`), R1 Prop 4 (every exact split inherits the margin) and B6_R2 Theorem M
(`λ^W_T ≤ exp(−e^{(2−ε)T})`, panel-verified), the structured side would have to be shown `≥ |error|` with an error
term at doubly exponential precision. No uniformity estimate has, or could have, that precision: a change of one
`Λ(n_0)` by `≈ e^{−14.4T}` flips the sign (`rem:resweights`, O3) while changing every Gowers norm of `r_W` by an
amount invisible at the scale of any known bound. Hence (F2, F3, O3) the branch is EXCLUDED.
(iii) (F4) Lemma G1, Theorem G and Prop. Z use only: complete additivity of `log`, Möbius inversion, and the
residue of the polar term. All hold verbatim for any Beurling system with a pole (the DMV falsifier included, with
`ζ_𝒫` in place of `ζ`); the archimedean `m` enters only additively in `σ_W`. No line uses A3 + A7/A8/A9
load-bearingly for a sign. F4 fails, as in R1–R2.

**Remark B (the common wall, HEURISTIC as a synthesis, each cited piece PROVED in its own memo and panel-checked).** Under RH with
simple zeros and `Σ_ρ 1/(|γ||ζ′(ρ)|) < ∞`-type convergence, the Selberg structured centre `g(y)` of R1 Thm 3 has
Laplace transform with simple poles at `ρ − ½ = iγ` (R2 Prop. S2), so `y ↦ g(y)` is (formally) a Bohr
almost-periodic function with spectrum `Ord` and mean zero — the comb-side twin of B4_R2 Theorem R (`T ↦ f_{w_T}(τ)`
a.p. with spectrum `2(τ − Ord)`, mean zero) and of B9_R2 Theorem R (`Λ` the unique `∀T`-positive comb in `Λ+ℓ¹_w`).
The three say the same thing from three sides: **the only structure in `Λ` beyond the pole is the zero spectrum**,
and a uniform-in-`T` sign must encode it. In the Tao dichotomy, the "structured part" that would carry the sign for
all `T` is not a low-complexity object (nilsequence, periodic model, mollified `ζ`) but the almost-periodic function
with frequencies `Ord` — that is, the zero side itself (E3). This is the precise reason the angle cannot work and
the reason it was worth closing with proofs rather than by analogy.
