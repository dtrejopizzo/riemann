## §6 Round-2 position of lens 5

Across the five memos no argument's positivity step goes through for a falsifier where the positivity is false. The
reason is structural, not accidental. Every proved positivity in this round has one of three properties:
* it sits on an object that contains no zero (B2 after suspension; B6's exclusion);
* it holds only at a damped or shifted parameter, where it is RH-silent (B8 with `δ′ > Θ* − ½`; B10 at `ε ≥ 1`);
* it is the one-sided input itself, which is RH (B9).

The round-2 record therefore adds two proved walls for G1:
* the **suspension wall**: B2 Theorem E with E5/E6 corrected, plus Cor D2;
* the **resolution wall**: B6 Theorems A and B.
