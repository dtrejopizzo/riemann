## §0 Verdict

**Status: dead end for the object on the motivic angle. The closing theorem is new and `PROVED`.**

In Weil's proof the polarization is Rosati positivity. It is a positive trace `τ` on the algebra generated by the
Frobenius correspondences and their transposes, whose value on the identity is `τ(1) = 2g`, the **genus**. In rank one
this is the whole proof (Prop 0): on `C[q^Z]`, a positive trace with finite genus that carries the Lefschetz numbers of
`C/F_q` exists **iff** RH holds for `C`.

For `Spec Z`, the Frobenius correspondences generate the discrete group algebra `A = C[Q^×_{>0}]`, which has
**infinite rank**. The explicit formula prescribes the Lefschetz numbers at prime powers: `τ(δ_{p^k}) = −log p·p^{−k/2}`.
Two things are free: the genus `c = τ(1)`, and the "mixed" values `τ(δ_x)` at the other `x`. I prove:

1. **Theorem A (orbit-faithful traces have infinite genus; `PROVED`).**
  * *Setting.* Say the trace is **orbit-faithful** if `τ(δ_x) = 0` whenever neither `x` nor `1/x` is a prime power.
  These are the Lefschetz numbers of correspondences with no closed orbit. They are the entries of the record's
  pure Frobenius Gram `G^{Frob}` (P47 (19bis.9), Thm 19bis.12(A)) and of Landau's formula.
  * *Claim.* An orbit-faithful trace is positive for **no** finite genus.
  * *Quantitatively.* On `C[{1,…,N}]`, positivity needs `c ≥ λ_max(A′_N) ≥ log N − O(1)`, where `A′_N` is the matrix
  of P47 (5.4). The same bound holds with the opposite sign.
  * *Robust form (A′).* Positivity with finite genus fails even when the mixed prime-ratio values are arbitrary, as
  long as their Gram block stays bounded in operator norm.
  * *What it closes.* This is the first `PROVED` form of the record's "infinite genus" (P47 Rem 5.9 and Rem 21.23;
  R158_001 Rem 1.1, which is `HEURISTIC`). The record says `G^{Frob}` "has no diagonal at all" (P47 19bis.12(ii)).
  Theorem A shows that no diagonal can be supplied.
2. **Theorem B (the exact minimal genus is `c_* = 2(√2+1)log 2 = 3.3468…`, and it is attained by a Künneth trace that
  carries no zero; `PROVED`).**
  * Every positive trace with the prescribed prime-power values has genus `≥ c_*`. The bound comes from the subgroup
  `2^Z` alone (Herglotz).
  * The **Künneth trace** `τ(δ_x) = c·∏_p m_p(v_p(x))` is positive exactly for `c ≥ c_*`.
  * Its GNS carrier is `L²(T^P, ⊗_pν_p)` with absolutely continuous local measures. It has no Kronecker eigenvalue,
  and its spectral measure gives **zero mass to the whole Kronecker line** `ι(R)`.
  * So a finite-genus polarization carrying the explicit formula's prime atoms exists unconditionally. It is local
  (Künneth over the primes), and it carries no zero.
3. **Proposition G (Lefschetz-faithful ⟹ infinite genus; weighted ⟹ `EQUIV`; `PROVED`).**
  * If the spectral side of the trace is the sum over the on-line zeros with their multiplicities, the genus is `∞`
  (Hardy, `CITED`).
  * If the zeros enter with summable weights on the Kronecker line, the trace is the restriction to `log Q_{>0}` of a
  continuous Toeplitz kernel. Its positivity is P47 Restatement 19bis.13 (`EQUIV`).
4. **Proposition L (count genus is void; `PROVED` modulo `CITED` Landau–Gonek and Riemann–von Mangoldt).**
  * *Setting.* Truncate the zero sum at height `T`. Then `τ_T(1) = N(T)`, and the off-diagonal is `(T/2π)×`
  (orbit-faithful atoms) `+ O(log T)`.
  * *Claim.* `τ_T` is positive on each `C[S]`, `S` finite, for `T ≥ T_0(S)`. This holds unconditionally, and it is
  stable when finitely many zeros are moved off the line. It is therefore zero-blind (`cor:void` schema).
  * *Credit.* This is the Landau-regularized twin of P47 Thm 5.8 / Rem 5.9, and I credit it as such.

**Corollary M (the motivic trichotomy; `PROVED`).** Let a "motive of Spec Z" have `H¹` carrying a positive trace on its
discrete Frobenius correspondence algebra that reproduces the explicit formula's Lefschetz numbers at prime powers.
Then exactly one of the following holds:
* (a) it is orbit-faithful, and then the genus is infinite (Thm A). Once the count supplies the genus, the positivity is
  void (Prop L);
* (b) it is not orbit-faithful with finite genus `≥ c_*`, and then the mixed Lefschetz numbers must be invented with
  unbounded Gram norm (Thm A′). The canonical invention, Künneth, carries no zero (Thm B);
* (c) its zeros are carried with weights, and then it is Restatement 19bis.13 (`EQUIV`, Prop G).

**Answer to the angle's question.** Identify `H¹` and the polarization:
* **the polarization of a motive of Spec Z cannot live on the discrete Frobenius correspondence algebra;**

## §2 Setting

**Definition 2.1.** Let `Q^×_{>0}` be the positive rationals, a free abelian group on the primes. Let
`A := C[Q^×_{>0}]` have basis `δ_x`, product `δ_xδ_y = δ_{xy}` and involution `δ_x^† := δ_{1/x}`. In Weil's
dictionary, `δ_n` for `n ∈ N` is the normalized Frobenius correspondence `n^{−1/2}Frob_n`, and `δ_{1/n}` is its
transpose.

A **trace datum** is a function `φ : Q^×_{>0} → C` with `φ(1/x) = conj φ(x)`. Its **genus** is `φ(1)`. Its Hermitian
form is `τ_φ(u†u) := Σ_{x,y} u_x ū_y φ(x/y)` on finitely supported `u`. The datum is **positive** if `τ_φ(u†u) ≥ 0` for
all `u`, i.e. `φ` is positive-definite on the discrete group.

**Definition 2.2 (the prescribed data).** Put `w(p^k) := log p · p^{−k/2}` for primes `p` and `k ≥ 1`.
* `φ` **carries the prime atoms** if `φ(p^{±k}) = −w(p^k)` for all `p, k`. The sign is Weil's: the prime side enters
  `W` with `−Λ(n)n^{−1/2}`. Theorems A and B are sign-blind, since only `|w|` enters.
* `φ` is **orbit-faithful** if moreover `φ(x) = 0` for every `x ≠ 1` such that neither `x` nor `1/x` is a prime power.
  The Lefschetz number of a correspondence with no closed orbit vanishes.
* `φ` is **Künneth** if `φ(x) = c∏_p m_p(v_p(x))` with `m_p(0) = 1`.

*Consistency with the record (`PROVED` by comparison).* On `{1,…,N}` the orbit-faithful form is `cI − A′_N`, where
`(A′_N)_{mn} = Λ(r)r^{−1/2}` if `m/n = r^{±1}` with `r` a prime power, and `0` otherwise. This `A′_N` is exactly P47
(5.4). It is also P47 (19bis.9) with chart factors `c_N ≡ 1`. For chart factors bounded below by `c_0 > 0`, every bound
below holds with `w` replaced by `c_0w`.

**Proposition 0 (rank one is Weil; `PROVED`, curve facts `CITED` (std.), P47 (X27)).** Let `C/F_q` have genus `g`, and
let `ω_j = α_j/√q` (`1 ≤ j ≤ 2g`) be its normalized Frobenius eigenvalues. On `C[q^Z]`, put
`φ(q^k) := Σ_jω_j^k` (`k ∈ Z`), so that `φ(1) = 2g`. Then `φ` is positive iff `|ω_j| = 1` for all `j` (RH for `C`).

*Proof.* (⇐) `φ(q^k) = Σ_j ω_j^k` with `|ω_j| = 1`, and `ω_j^{−1} = ω̄_j`. So
`τ(u†u) = Σ_j|Σ_k u_kω_j^k|² ≥ 0`.

(⇒) The Gram matrix on `{1, q^k}` is `[[2g, φ(q^k)],[conj φ(q^k), 2g]]`, and it is psd. So `|φ(q^k)| ≤ 2g` for all `k`.
Then `Σ_{k≥0}φ(q^k)z^k = Σ_j(1−ω_jz)^{−1}` has radius of convergence `≥ 1`. The right side is a rational function whose
poles `1/ω_j` carry positive integer multiplicities, so they cannot cancel. Hence `max|ω_j| ≤ 1`. The multiset `{ω_j}`
is stable under `ω ↦ 1/ω` (`α ↦ q/α`), so `min|ω_j| ≥ 1`. ∎

*Reading.* In rank one, a positive finite-genus trace carrying the Lefschetz numbers is **exactly** RH. The group `q^Z`
has one generator, so orbit-faithfulness is automatic: every ratio is a power of `q`.

## §3 Theorem A: orbit-faithful traces have infinite genus

**Theorem A (`PROVED`; Euler `Σ1/p = ∞` and Mertens `CITED` (std.)).**
* (i) *(Star threshold.)* Let `S_X = {1} ∪ {p ≤ X}` and `v_X := (w(p))_{p≤X}`. For every trace datum carrying the prime
  atoms with `φ(p/q) = 0` for distinct primes `p, q ≤ X`, positivity on `C[S_X]` holds **iff**
  `φ(1) ≥ ‖v_X‖₂ = (Σ_{p≤X}(log p)²/p)^{1/2}`.
  The right side tends to `∞`; it is `~ log X/√2` by Mertens. Hence **no orbit-faithful trace datum is positive, for
  any finite genus.**
* (ii) *(Robust form A′.)* Let `φ` carry the prime atoms, with **arbitrary** mixed values `φ(p/q)`. Let `E_X` be the
  Hermitian zero-diagonal matrix `(φ(p/q))_{p≠q≤X}`. If `φ` is positive with genus `c`, then
  `⟨E_Xv_X, v_X⟩ ≥ ‖v_X‖⁴/c − c‖v_X‖²` for every `X`.
  In particular `‖E_X‖_{op} ≥ ‖v_X‖²/c − c → ∞`. A finite-genus positive completion must invent mixed Lefschetz
  numbers whose Gram norm diverges like `Σ_{p≤X}(log p)²/p`.
* (iii) *(Two-sided growth on `{1,…,N}`.)* `λ_max(A′_N) ≥ log N − O(1)` and `λ_min(A′_N) ≤ −log N + O(1)`. So
  `cI ∓ A′_N ⪰ 0` requires `c ≥ log N − O(1)`, whichever sign convention is used for the atoms.

*Proof.*

(i) Order `S_X` as `1, p_1, …`. The Gram matrix is `G = [[c, v^T],[v, cI]]`, where `v = −v_X` is real, and the
`p_i/p_j` entries vanish by hypothesis.
* If `c > 0`, then `G ⪰ 0` iff the Schur complement `cI − vv^T/c ⪰ 0`, i.e. iff `c² ≥ ‖v‖²`.
* If `c = 0`, psd forces `v = 0`, which is false.
* If `c < 0`, the `(1,1)` entry is negative.

Divergence: `Σ_p(log p)²/p ≥ (log 2)²Σ_p 1/p = ∞` (Euler). The asymptotic `(log X)²/2` is Mertens (`CITED`).

(ii) Put `u = δ_1 − s·Σ_p v_pδ_p` with `s ∈ R` and `v = −v_X`. Then
`τ(u†u) = c − 2s‖v‖² + s²⟨(cI+E_X)v, v⟩`. This is `≥ 0` for all real `s` iff `‖v‖⁴ ≤ c⟨(cI+E_X)v, v⟩`, which is the
claim; `c > 0` is forced as in (i). The operator-norm consequence follows from `⟨Ev,v⟩ ≤ ‖E‖‖v‖²`.

(iii) Take `u_n = n^{−1/2}` on `{1,…,N}`. Then
`⟨A′_Nu, u⟩ = 2Σ_{m<n≤N, n/m = r prime power} Λ(r)r^{−1/2}(mn)^{−1/2} = 2Σ_{m≤N} m^{−1} Σ_{r≤N/m} Λ(r)/r`.
* By Mertens, `Σ_{r≤y}Λ(r)/r = log y + O(1)` (`CITED`). So the sum is `2Σ_{m≤N} m^{−1}(log(N/m) + O(1)) = (log N)² + O(log N)`,
  using `Σ_{m≤N} log m/m = ½(log N)² + O(1)`.
* `‖u‖² = Σ_{n≤N}1/n = log N + O(1)`.
* So the Rayleigh quotient is `≥ log N − O(1)`.

For `λ_min`, take `u_n = λ(n)n^{−1/2}`, with `λ` Liouville's function. For `n = mr`, `λ(m)λ(n) = λ(r) = (−1)^k` when
`r = p^k`, so the inner sum becomes `Σ_{p^k≤y}(−1)^k log p/p^k = −log y + O(1)`, since the terms with `k ≥ 2` converge
absolutely. ∎

**Corollary A.1 (no diagonal can be supplied to `G^{Frob}`; `PROVED`).** P47 Thm 19bis.12(ii) records that the pure
Frobenius Gram has no diagonal. Theorem A shows that **no finite value** of the diagonal makes it positive or negative
semidefinite on `{1,…,N}` for all `N`. Any value that works on `{1,…,N}` is `≥ log N − O(1)`.

**Corollary A.2 (sharpness of the regime of P47 Thm 5.8(b); `PROVED` for the leading matrix).** The leading matrix of
P47 (5.4), `−(log(1/ε) − C_φ)I + A′_N`, is negative definite **iff** `log(1/ε) − C_φ > λ_max(A′_N)`.
* By A(iii) this needs `log(1/ε) ≥ C_φ + log N − O(1)`, i.e. `ε ≲ e^{−C_φ}/N`.
* The record's sufficient condition uses the Gershgorin radius `r_N`. Up to constants, `r_N` equals the first row sum
  `Σ_{r≤N}Λ(r)r^{−1/2} ~ 2√N`.
* So the true threshold of the "unconditionally uninformative" regime lies between `e^{−O(√N)}` and `O(1/N)`. The
  unconditional damping regime shrinks **at least polynomially** in `N`.
* The leading order in (5.4) is for fixed `N`. The statement is about that matrix, and I claim no uniformity in `N`
  for the `o(1/ε)` term.

## §4 Theorem B: the exact minimal genus, and the Künneth trace

**Theorem B (`PROVED`; Herglotz/Bochner on `Z`, Poisson kernel, and Kolmogorov's product measure `CITED` (std.)).** Put
`c_* := sup_p 2log p/(√p − 1) = 2log 2/(√2−1) = 2(√2+1)log 2 = 3.34681…`. The supremum is attained at `p = 2`:
`f(x) = 2log x/(√x−1)` decreases on `[2,∞)`, since `f′ < 0` iff `log x > 2(1 − x^{−1/2})`, which holds at `x = 2`
(`0.693 > 0.586`) and persists. Numerically, `f(3) = 3.0015` and `f(5) = 2.604`.
* **(i) Lower bound, for every completion.** If `φ` carries the prime atoms and is positive, then `φ(1) ≥ c_*`.
* **(ii) Attained, by a Künneth trace.** For `c ≥ c_*`, put `m_p(k) := −(log p/c)p^{−|k|/2}` for `k ≠ 0` and
  `m_p(0) = 1`, and `φ(x) := c∏_p m_p(v_p(x))`. Then `φ` is positive, carries the prime atoms, and has genus `c`. Its
  mixed values are `φ(p^aq^b⋯) = c∏ m`, e.g. `φ(p/q) = log p·log q/(c√(pq)) > 0`. It is not orbit-faithful, and
  `E_X = (v_Xv_X^T − diag(v_X²))/c`, as Theorem A(ii) requires.
* **(iii) The GNS carrier.** Let `ν_p` be the probability measure on `T` with Fourier coefficients `m_p(k)`, and
  `μ = c·⊗_pν_p` on `T^P = (Q^×_{>0})^∧`. The carrier is `H = L²(T^P, ⊗ν_p)`, `U_x = ` multiplication by `χ ↦ χ(x)`,
  cyclic vector `Ω = 1`, and `φ(x) = c⟨U_xΩ, Ω⟩`. Then:
  * (a) every `ν_p` is absolutely continuous, so `U_p` has no eigenvalue;
  * (b) the joint Kronecker point spectrum is empty, `Γ(U) = ∅`;
  * (c) `μ(ι(R)) = 0`, where `ι(γ) = (p^{iγ})_p` is the Kronecker line. So the spectral measure charges no part of the
  line, atomic or continuous.

*Proof.*

(i) Restrict `φ` to the subgroup `2^Z`. It stays positive-definite (restriction to a subgroup), and
`φ(2^k) = −log 2·2^{−|k|/2}` for `k ≠ 0`. By Herglotz, `φ|_{2^Z}` is the Fourier–Stieltjes sequence of a positive
measure on `T` of mass `c = φ(1)`. Its formal density is
`f(θ) = c − log 2·(P_r(θ) − 1)`, with `r = 2^{−1/2}` and `P_r(θ) = Σ_{k∈Z}r^{|k|}e^{ikθ} = (1−r²)/(1−2r cos θ+r²)`.
The series converges absolutely, so `f` is continuous and is the density. Positivity forces `min f ≥ 0`. The minimum is
at `θ = 0`, where `P_r(0) = (1+r)/(1−r)`. So `c ≥ log 2·(2r/(1−r)) = 2log 2/(√2 − 1)`.

The same computation at every `p` gives `c ≥ 2log p/(√p−1)`, and the supremum is the value at `p = 2`.

(ii) The density of `ν_p` is `f_p(θ) = 1 − (log p/c)(P_{r_p}(θ) − 1)`, with `r_p = p^{−1/2}`. It is `≥ 0` iff
`c ≥ 2log p/(√p−1)`, which holds for all `p` when `c ≥ c_*`. So each `ν_p` is a probability measure (`f_p ≥ 0`,
`∫f_p = 1`), and the infinite product `⊗ν_p` exists (Kolmogorov).

For `x = ∏p^{a_p}`,
`∫χ(x) d(⊗ν_p) = ∏_p ∫z^{a_p} dν_p = ∏_p m_p(a_p)`,
by independence of the coordinates (only finitely many `a_p ≠ 0`). So `φ = μ̂` is positive-definite. It carries the
prime atoms because `c·m_p(k) = −log p·p^{−|k|/2}`.

(iii)
* (a) `U_p = M_z ⊗ 1` on `L²(T,ν_p) ⊗ L²(∏_{q≠p})`. If `M_zf = λf` in `L²(ν_p)`, then `f` is supported on `{λ}`, which
  is `ν_p`-null. So `M_z` has no eigenvalue, and `ker(M_z ⊗ 1 − λ) = ker(M_z − λ) ⊗ (⋯) = 0`.
* (b) follows from (a) with one prime; this is round-3 Cor C1, and B4 Thm W covers it too.
* (c) The pair `(χ(2), χ(3))` has the absolutely continuous law `f_2 dθ ⊗ f_3 dθ` on `T²`. The set
  `{(2^{iγ}, 3^{iγ}) : γ ∈ R}` is a one-parameter curve, a Lebesgue-null σ-compact set. So
  `μ(ι(R)) ≤ c·P((χ(2),χ(3)) ∈ curve) = 0`. ∎

*Remark B.1 (`HEURISTIC`, no proof weight).*
* At the minimal genus `c = c_*`, the 2-local density `f_2` vanishes exactly at `θ = 0`, the trivial character (the
  pole).
* The Künneth measure is singular with respect to Haar measure on `T^P`, by Kakutani:
  `∏∫√f_p = 0`, because `1 − ∫√f_p ≍ (log p)²/(c²p)` is not summable. I record this without using it.

*Reading (`PROVED` content, `HEURISTIC` phrasing).* Theorem B is the counterpoint to premise 3 of R159_000. The
**prime-side data alone admit an unconditional, finite-genus, positive completion.** That completion is **local**: a
product of local positive measures, the C16/C17 type, which fails OT-6. It carries **no zero**: `μ(ι(R)) = 0`.
"Positivity from the prime side" is meaningful only when the prime side is read **orbit-faithfully**, and then
Theorem A forbids a finite genus.

## §5 Proposition G and Proposition L: the two remaining genera

**Proposition G (`PROVED`; Hardy 1914 `CITED`; P47 Restatement 19bis.13).** Let `φ` be positive, with spectral measure
`μ` on `T^P` (Bochner on the discrete group; `μ(T^P) = φ(1)`).
* (i) *(Lefschetz-faithful ⟹ infinite genus.)* Suppose every on-line zero `½+iγ` is an atom of `μ` at `ι(γ)` with
  mass at least its multiplicity. This is the spectral side of a Lefschetz trace: a sum over eigenvalues with
  multiplicities. Then `φ(1) = ∞`.
  *Proof.* `ι` is injective (unique factorization). Hardy gives infinitely many distinct on-line ordinates, so the
  masses are `≥ 1` at infinitely many distinct points. ∎
* (ii) *(Weighted ⟹ `EQUIV`.)* Suppose `μ = ι_*m` with `m = Σ_ρ A_ρδ_{γ_ρ}` a finite positive measure on `R`. Then
  `φ(x) = κ(log x)`, where `κ(t) := Σ_ρ A_ρe^{iγ_ρt}` is continuous. A continuous function is positive-definite on the
  dense subgroup `log Q_{>0}` iff it is positive-definite on `R`: approximate any finite configuration and use the
  continuity of the quadratic form. For `A_ρ = Φ_{E_0}(ρ)Φ_{E_0}(1−ρ)` this is P47 Restatement 19bis.13(ii)–(iv), whose
  positivity is the RH-type statement of record. Any other choice of weights is a choice made from the zeros, which is
  installation (Lemma T, OT-3). ∎

**Proposition L (count genus is void; `PROVED` given `CITED` inputs).** Inputs:
* Riemann–von Mangoldt: `N(T) = (T/2π)log(T/2πe) + O(log T)`;
* Landau's formula in Gonek's form: for fixed real `z > 1`,
  `Σ_{0<γ≤T} z^ρ = −(T/2π)Λ(z) + O_z(log T)`, with `Λ(z) = 0` unless `z` is a prime power. This is unconditional,
  summed over all nontrivial zeros. (Landau 1912; S. M. Gonek, Contemp. Math. 143 (1993).)

Define `τ_T(δ_z) := Σ_{ρ: 0<Im ρ≤T} z^{ρ−½}` (with multiplicity).
* (i) `τ_T(δ_1) = N(T)`, and `τ_T(δ_{1/z}) = conj τ_T(δ_z)`. The second holds because the zero set in the strip is
  stable under `ρ ↦ 1−ρ̄`, and `z^{½−ρ̄} = conj((1/z)^{ρ−½})`. So `τ_T` is a trace datum.
* (ii) Under RH, `τ_T(u†u) = Σ_γ|Σ_x u_xx^{iγ}|² ≥ 0` for every `T`.
* (iii) Unconditionally, for `z ≠ 1`, `τ_T(δ_z) = −(T/2π)·w̃(z) + O_z(log T)`, where `w̃` is the orbit-faithful atom
  datum (`w(r)` at `z = r^{±1}`, `r` a prime power; `0` otherwise). Hence, for each finite `S ⊂ Q^×_{>0}`,
  `τ_T|_{C[S]} = N(T)I − (T/2π)A′_S + O_S(log T)`.
  This is positive definite as soon as `log(T/2πe) > λ_max(A′_S) + o(1)`, i.e. for `T ≥ T_0(S)`.
* (iv) The conclusion of (iii) is unchanged if finitely many zeros are moved (symmetrically) off the line. Each moved
  pair changes each entry of the finite matrix by `O_S(1)`, uniformly in `T`. So (iii) cannot distinguish RH from its
  negation.

*Proof.* (i) and (ii) are as shown. For (iii), apply Landau–Gonek to `z` and `1/z` using (i). The count term is
Riemann–von Mangoldt. Positivity follows because the diagonal `N(T) ~ (T/2π)log T` beats the off-diagonal `(T/2π)A′_S`
by a factor of `log T` (the same mechanism as P47 Thm 5.8). (iv) is as shown. ∎

*Reading.* The count supplies the genus (N-a of R158_005), and with it a positivity that is void: `cor:void` schema,
blind to the falsifier. By Theorem A(iii), `T_0(S)` for `S = {1,…,N}` must satisfy `log T_0 ≳ log N`. To leading order,
certifying Rosati positivity on the Frobenius up to `N` needs zeros up to height `≍ N` (`HEURISTIC` as a quantitative
statement, since the `O_S(log T)` term is not uniform in `S`).

*Relation to `thm:finZ`.* No conflict. Prop L is about the discrete algebra, not about flat-top certificates of `W_T`.
It uses the zeros up to `T`, with `T → ∞` as `S` grows, and it certifies nothing about any window.

## §6 Corollary M: the motivic trichotomy and the answer to the angle

**Corollary M (`PROVED`: Theorems A and B, Props G and L).** Consider a trace datum `φ` on `A = C[Q^×_{>0}]` carrying
the prime atoms. Such a datum is the "Rosati polarization of the discrete Frobenius correspondence algebra of a motive
of Spec Z" that reproduces the explicit formula's Lefschetz numbers at prime powers. If `φ` is positive, then:
* `φ(1) ≥ c_*` (Thm B(i));
* `φ` is not orbit-faithful (Thm A(i));
* its mixed values have diverging Gram norm (Thm A(ii));
* its spectral measure either (a) charges `ι(R)` with finite total mass, and then any zero content is weighted
  (Prop G(ii): `EQUIV` / installation), or (b) does not charge the atoms of the zeros with their multiplicities
  (Prop G(i)).

The canonical minimal completion (Künneth) has `μ(ι(R)) = 0` (Thm B(iii)). The orbit-faithful datum becomes positive
only with the count genus, where it is void (Prop L).

**Answer to the angle ("identify `H¹` and the polarization").**
* **H¹.** Let the would-be `H¹` be the GNS space of the discrete Rosati trace.
  * For a finite-genus polarization it is either zero-free (Künneth, Thm B) or carries the zeros with installed
  weights (Prop G(ii)).
  * For a Lefschetz-faithful one it has infinite genus (Prop G(i)). This is the `PROVED` form, on the discrete algebra,
  of Deninger's "`H¹` is infinite-dimensional" and of P47's "infinite genus".
* **Polarization.** The Hodge-type standard conjecture of such a motive, stated on the discrete correspondence
  algebra, is:
  * false at finite genus in the orbit-faithful reading (Thm A);
  * true but zero-free in the Künneth reading (Thm B);
  * void in the count reading (Prop L);
  * `EQUIV` in the weighted reading (Prop G(ii)).
  The only remaining place for it is the continuous completion, `W` on `C_c^∞(R)`: Weil's criterion, CCM Cor 6.3,
  `EQUIV` (round 1 of this angle, `G1_B7_R1` M1).

**§7.2-type reading (`HEURISTIC`, no proof weight).** On the continuous completion, the genus is supplied by the
archimedean place. The non-integrable `1/|t|` singularity of the archimedean kernel at `t = 0` (P47 (5.4), the
`log(1/ε)` diagonal) is the continuous counterpart of `φ(1) = ∞`, and its Fourier transform `≍ log|ξ|` is the
Riemann–von Mangoldt density. So the discrete algebra has no archimedean place to supply the genus. Its infinite
rank demands one (Thm A). Rank one does not (Prop 0). That is the precise sense in which "the polarization of Spec Z is
archimedean and continuous, never discrete".

## §8 What is new, what is not, the weakest step

**Not new.**
* Prop 0 is Weil/Serre (the power-sum step is classical).
* Herglotz, Bochner, the Poisson kernel, Kolmogorov, Kakutani, Mertens, Euler, Hardy, Landau–Gonek and Riemann–von
  Mangoldt are all standard.
* Prop L is the Gonek–Fujii mean value read as a trace (phase 29 doc 41), and has the same moral as P47 Thm 5.8.
* The weighted branch is P47 Restatement 19bis.13.
* The conclusion that the polarization lives on the continuous completion and is Weil's criterion is round 1 of this
  angle (`EQUIV`).

**New to the record (`PROVED`).**
1. **Theorem A:** no finite genus makes the orbit-faithful Frobenius Gram positive.
  * The exact star threshold is `‖v_X‖₂`.
  * The robust form A′ covers arbitrary bounded mixed data.
  * Two-sided growth: `λ_max(A′_N) ≥ log N − O(1)`, `λ_min(A′_N) ≤ −log N + O(1)`.
  * Corollaries: A.1 (`G^{Frob}` admits no diagonal) and A.2 (the regime of P47 Thm 5.8(b) needs `ε ≲ e^{−C_φ}/N`).
  * This is the first proved form of the record's "infinite genus".
2. **Theorem B:** the exact minimal genus `c_* = 2(√2+1)log 2` of any positive trace carrying the explicit formula's
  prime atoms. It is attained by the Künneth trace, whose carrier has spectral mass `0` on the Kronecker line. This is
  a proved instance of a finite-genus, prime-side, unconditional positivity that is zero-free.
3. **Corollary M:** the trichotomy on the discrete correspondence algebra. It upgrades the round-3 `HEURISTIC` Prop Ψ(b)
  ("Frobenius = flow") to a theorem at the level of traces, and gives the filter N-g.

**Weakest step.**
* *Scope: the discrete algebra.* Everything is proved for traces on `C[Q^×_{>0}]`, i.e. for motives whose Frobenius
  correspondences form a discrete group with a pointwise Lefschetz trace. A carrier whose correspondences are only
  **smeared** (Frobenius = flow, CCM, Connes) is outside the theorems. There the positivity is Weil's, `EQUIV` by
  record (R158_002/004 A17), so it is classified rather than excluded by this memo.
* *Orbit-faithfulness is a modelling hypothesis.* It is natural: fixed points of a correspondence of ratio `x` are closed
  orbits of length `|log x|`, which exist only for prime powers. It is also the record's (19bis.9), and it is forced
  asymptotically by Landau (Prop L(iii)). But it is not derived from an axiomatic object. Thm A(ii) covers every
  completion with bounded mixed data, and Thm B shows unbounded mixed data can give positivity (zero-free).
* *`OPEN`.* Whether **every** positive completion of finite genus (not only Künneth) has spectral measure with
  `μ(ι(R)) = 0`, or at least carries on `ι(R)` only summable, installed weights. The Kronecker line is Haar-null and
  dense. I have no example of a finite-genus completion charging it, and no proof that none exists. By Prop G, a
  positive answer would still be installation or `EQUIV`, so this `OPEN` item cannot hide an object.
* *`CITED` inputs.* Landau–Gonek (fixed `z`, unconditional), Riemann–von Mangoldt, Mertens, Hardy, and standard
  harmonic analysis. The numerical value `c_* = 3.34681…` is closed form, `2(√2+1)log 2`.
* **The G1 objective is not met. No sign is proved or claimed.**
