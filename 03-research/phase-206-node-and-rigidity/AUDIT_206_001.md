  M = −2∫_0^{2T} e^{u/2}c_F(u)du.

### A.3 N.4 (class NO-GO of 𝒦_NL): ARGUED, class poorly delimited

**Exact class, as written.** Steps Q ≥ 0, Q = R − L(Λ), with

### B.7 Lemma V: correct, trivial; correction of a detail

- **Formula.** The Poisson formula on ℤ² for x ↦ |g|²(Q(x)), with Q positive definite, is correct: the Fourier transform of a function of Q(x) is a Hankel transform J₀ evaluated at Q*. The main term (2π/√D)∫|g|² is the area of the ellipse Q ≤ x, which equals 2πx/√D. The FE of Z_Q follows from this, so Voronoi for r_Q exists for every h, whether h = 1 or h > 1. PROVED (standard CITED).
- **"Does not use E"?** Correct, for the simplest reason: the positivity of the left-hand side uses r_Q ≥ 0 and |g|² ≥ 0, and nothing else. The FE is an identity that does not contribute to the sign. The lemma is **tautological**: the inequality "dual ≥ −main" is the identity plus "LHS ≥ 0." Its usefulness is as a NO-GO for the route "Voronoi + positive test." It is legitimate, but does not require Epstein: it is killed by any nonnegative sequence with FE, or even without one. For example, for cΛ "the positivity of the left-hand side" also holds.
- **Detail error.** "For ζ² (d = r for the 'hyperbolic' form xy…)" is incorrect: xy is indefinite and r_{xy}(n) over ℤ² is infinite. d(n) counts pairs (a,b) ∈ ℕ² with ab = n, and Voronoi for d is not Poisson on ℤ² for a form. It should be rewritten as "for d = 1∗1, Voronoi is the FE of ζ² with kernels K₀/Y₀." The conclusion does not change.
- Label: **PROVED (trivial)**.
