### Lemma K (Kronecker resonance): (WS) uniform in γ is FALSE even in finite range
For N = Xe^{2Δ} fixed, 𝒮(γ) = Σ_{n≤N} Λ(n)n^{−½+iγ}w(log n) is a finite Dirichlet polynomial; by Kronecker–Dirichlet (ℚ-linear independence of {log p}_{p≤N}) there exist arbitrarily large γ with p^{iγ} ≈ e^{iφ_p} for all p ≤ N simultaneously, with phases φ_p of choice. Then sup_γ |𝒮(γ)| ≥ Σ_p (log p/√p)|w(log p)|(1−ε) − (higher powers) ≍ X^{1/2}‖w‖: the trivial bound is attained. It is the real-frequency realization of the b_θ of R167_102 Thm B. **Consequence:** (WS) with η < δ < ½ cannot hold for all γ > H; it could only hold for γ = γ_q specifically, and the only property distinguishing γ_q from a Kronecker-resonant frequency is being the ordinate of a zero of ζ ⇒ circular (R167_102 Thm C, R166_802 Thm 3). Label: PROVED in structure (standard simultaneous Dirichlet constants); pending review. **(WS) is DEAD as a uniform statement; as a statement at γ_q it is localized RH.**
