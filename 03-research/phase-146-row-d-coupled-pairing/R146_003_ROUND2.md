## D.47 — The diagonal, computed; and the single-prime tower

### D.47.1 `δ_m − δ_n`, exactly

**Definition D.47.1.** Fix a chart `S` with `S_f ≠ ∅`, a level `N ≥ 1`, and a unit-height even profile
`φ_ε ∈ C_c^∞([−ε,ε])`, `φ_ε(0) = 1`, `ε < ½\log 2`. For `n ≥ 2` with `P(n) ⊆ S_f` write, following Paper 44
Theorem 8bis.1b,

    𝔡_{ε,N}(n) \;:=\; T_∞\big(Δ^{(n),[φ_ε,N]}\big)
      \;=\; -\!\!Σ_{r∈S_f∖P(n)}\!(\log r)\Big[\tfrac{r−2}{r−1}+(N−1)\Big] \;-\; \log\tfrac1ε \;+\; c_φ ,   (147.1)

with `c_φ = γ+\log 4π + O(1)` depending on `φ` **only** — not on `n` (Thm 8bis.1b(iv): the archimedean
profile of `Δ^{(n),[φ_ε,N]}` is `φ_ε`, with every finite coordinate `1`, irrespective of `n`). The Gram
diagonal proper is `δ_n := I_A(Frob_n^{sm},Frob_n^{sm}) = V_N(n)·𝔡_{ε,N}(n)` in the bare convention and
`V_N(n)^{−1}𝔡_{ε,N}(n)` in the `♭` convention, with `V_N(n) = ∏_{r∈S_f∖P(n)}((r−1)r^{N−1})^{−1} > 0`
(Corollary 8bis.1b′, (8bis.0)).

**Theorem D.47.2 (the difference of diagonals; `PROVED` from Thm 8bis.1b).** For `m,n ≥ 2` with
`P(m),P(n) ⊆ S_f`:

 (i) **the archimedean divergence cancels identically** — not to `O(1)`, but exactly, since the term
     `−\log(1/ε)+c_φ` of (147.1) is the same number for every `n`; and

     𝔡_{ε,N}(m) − 𝔡_{ε,N}(n)
       = Σ_{r∈P(m)∖P(n)}(\log r)\Big[\tfrac{r−2}{r−1}+N−1\Big]
         − Σ_{r∈P(n)∖P(m)}(\log r)\Big[\tfrac{r−2}{r−1}+N−1\Big] ,                             (147.2)

     **supported on the symmetric difference `P(m) △ P(n)`**, with no `ε` and no place outside it;
 (ii) each summand is `≥ 0`, and `> 0` except in the single case `r = 2`, `N = 1` (where
     `\frac{r−2}{r−1} = 0` and `N−1 = 0`). Hence for `N ≥ 2`:
     **`𝔡_{ε,N}(m) = 𝔡_{ε,N}(n) ⟺ P(m) = P(n)`**; for `N = 1` the same holds modulo the prime `2`;
 (iii) `V_N(n)` depends on `n` through `P(n)` only, so `P(m) = P(n)` gives `V_N(m) = V_N(n)` and therefore

          P(m) = P(n) \quad\Longrightarrow\quad δ_m = δ_n \ \ \textbf{exactly, at every } (ε,N),
          \textbf{ in both conventions};                                                        (147.3)
 (iv) off that regime the difference **diverges linearly in the chart parameter `N`**, with slope
     `Σ_{r∈P(m)∖P(n)}\log r − Σ_{r∈P(n)∖P(m)}\log r`.

*Proof.* (i)–(ii) subtract the two instances of (147.1); `S_f∖P(n)` minus `S_f∖P(m)` is `P(m)∖P(n)`, and
the stated positivity is Theorem 8bis.1b(iii)'s bracket. (iii) is (8bis.0) and (ii). (iv) is the `N`-linear
term of (147.2). ∎ `PROVED` (from a cited computation; nothing is re-derived).

> **What this replaces.** Round 1 asserted `δ_n = n(2−2g) = −∞`. The truth at a fixed chart is: `δ_n` is a
> **finite negative number** (both divergences in Thm 8bis.1b are negative, and the bracket is negative for
> every `(ε,N)`), it is **not proportional to any degree of `Frob_n`**, and its variation in `n` is
> **entirely supported on `P(m) △ P(n)`**. The infinite genus is still there — it is the `ε → 0` and
> `N → ∞` behaviour (Thm 8bis.2, Cor 8bis.1b′, cited, not re-derived) — but it is a property of the
> *absolute* diagonal, and (147.3) says it **cancels exactly** between two Frobenii with the same prime
> support. That is the door M2(c) pointed at, and it opens onto the following object.

### D.47.2 The single-prime tower: the first arithmetic diagonal that is not an assumption

**Definition D.47.3.** Fix a prime `p ∈ S_f` and let `M_p := Z⟨Frob_{p^k} : k ≥ 1⟩ ⊂ M`. Since
`P(p^k) = \{p\}` for every `k ≥ 1`:

* the chart constant is the **same** for every pair: `c_N(p^j,p^k) = ∏_{s∈S_f∖\{p\}}\mathrm{vol}(1+s^NZ_s)
  =: c_p > 0`, independent of `(j,k)` (by (8bis.2b), `P(p^j)∩P(p^k) = \{p\}`);
* the diagonal is the **same** for every `k`: `δ_{p^k} = δ_p` for all `k ≥ 1`, exactly, by (147.3).

Hence the half-density Gram of the tower is, with `ρ := p^{−1/2} ∈ (0,1)` and `T(ρ)_{jk} := ρ^{|j−k|}`,

    G^{(p)}_{jk} = c_p\,(\log p)\,ρ^{|j−k|}\ (j ≠ k),\qquad G^{(p)}_{kk} = δ_p ,
    \boxed{\,G^{(p)} \;=\; c_p(\log p)\,\Big[\,T(ρ) \;+\; (τ_p−1)\,I\,\Big],\qquad
           τ_p := \frac{δ_p}{c_p\,\log p}\,}                                                    (147.4)

**Lemma D.47.4 (`PROVED`).** `c_p(\log p) > 0`, so **the signature of `G^{(p)}` depends on the chart, the
level, the smearing and the convention through the single real number `τ_p` and through nothing else.**
Every convention-dependence that M8 objected to in §§D.44–D.45 collapses, on `M_p`, into one parameter. ∎

### D.47.3 Is it a count? (off-diagonal yes, diagonal no — and now for a better reason)

**Proposition D.47.5 (`PROVED`, from cited computations).**

 (i) **Off-diagonal: a count.** For `j ≠ k`, `G^{(p)}_{jk}/c_p = Λ(p^{|j−k|})\,p^{−|j−k|/2}` is the
     Haar measure `\log p` of the fixed-point shell of `Frob_{p^j}⋆Frob_{p^k}^† = Frob_{p^{|j−k|}}`

 (ii) **Diagonal: not a count, and now demonstrably so.** `δ_p < 0` at every `(ε,N)` (Thm 8bis.1b: both
     contributions are negative). A Haar measure of a fixed-point set is `≥ 0`; a cardinality is `≥ 0`.
     So the tower's diagonal **exists at a fixed chart and is negative**.

*Proof.* (i) as cited; the prefactor computation is one line. (ii) is the last sentence of Theorem 8bis.1b

`PROVED`

### D.47.4 The signature of the tower form (a computed negative)

**Theorem D.47.6 (the trichotomy; `PROVED`).** With `ρ = p^{−1/2}` and `τ_p` as in (147.4), for the
bi-infinite tower:

    G^{(p)} \succeq 0 \iff τ_p ≥ τ_+(p) := \frac{2ρ}{1+ρ} = \frac{2}{\sqrt p+1} ,
    G^{(p)} \preceq 0 \iff τ_p ≤ τ_-(p) := -\frac{2ρ}{1-ρ} = -\frac{2}{\sqrt p-1} ,              (147.5)

and `G^{(p)}` is indefinite strictly between. Moreover, for every finite truncation
`\{Frob_{p^k}\}_{k=1}^{K}` with `K ≥ 2`, positive semidefiniteness requires
`τ_p ≥ 1 − λ_{\min}(T_K(ρ)) > 0`, since `λ_{\min}(T_K) < \mathrm{tr}\,T_K/K = 1`.

**Corollary D.47.7 (`PROVED`).** Because `δ_p < 0` (Proposition D.47.5(ii)) we have `τ_p < 0 < τ_+(p)`, so

> **the computed single-prime tower form is not positive semidefinite — at any chart, at any level, at any
> smearing, in either convention, and already at `K = 2`.**

*Proof of D.47.6.* `T(ρ)` is the Kac–Murdock–Szegő/Toeplitz matrix with symbol
`f_ρ(θ) = Σ_kρ^{|k|}e^{ikθ} = (1−ρ^2)/(1−2ρ\cosθ+ρ^2)`, whose range is `[\frac{1−ρ}{1+ρ},\frac{1+ρ}{1−ρ}]`
(minimum at `θ = π`, maximum at `θ = 0`). The bi-infinite form (147.4) is PSD iff
`τ_p−1+\min f_ρ ≥ 0`, i.e. `τ_p ≥ 1−\frac{1−ρ}{1+ρ} = \frac{2ρ}{1+ρ}`; NSD iff `τ_p−1+\max f_ρ ≤ 0`. For a
finite section, `λ_{\min}(T_K) ≤ \mathrm{tr}/K = 1` with equality iff `T_K = I`, false for `K ≥ 2`. ∎
`PROVED`. Values `CERTIFIED`, `scripts/tower_gram.py` §§[1]–[2]:
`τ_+(2) = 0.828427`, `τ_+(3) = 0.732051`, `τ_+(7) = 0.548584`; `τ_-(2) = −4.828427`;
`λ_{\min}(T_K(2^{−1/2})) = 0.1974, 0.1779, 0.1732` at `K = 4, 8, 16`.

> **W-a compliance, explicitly.** This is a **failure** of positivity, computed. The complementary
> statement — "`τ_p ≥ 2/(\sqrt p+1)`", i.e. a *positive* diagonal exceeding an explicit threshold — is
> what positivity would require, and nothing in Paper 44 or here produces it; a Haar-measure diagonal is
> negative. No positivity is claimed and none is needed. **Relation to prior art:** this is a sharpening,
> with an explicit threshold and an explicit regime, of Paper 44 **Theorem 8bis.4a** (*positivity fails on
> the pure Frobenius primitive radial sector at every finite chart*, `CORPUS_CHECK_R146b` §4) — it does not
> contradict it and does not claim to be independent of it.

### D.47.6 Comparison with the model's tower — and where the missing block is

**Theorem D.47.9 (`PROVED`; values `CERTIFIED` §[4]).** For the model curve `E/F_2: y^2+xy = x^3+1`
(`q = 2`, `a = −1`, `g = 1`), the tower Gram entry is `κ(k) = q^{−k/2}N_k` with `N_k = q^k+1−S_k`, i.e.

    κ(k) \;=\; q^{k/2} + q^{−k/2} − q^{−k/2}S_k \;\sim\; q^{k/2} \;\longrightarrow\; ∞ ,

while the arithmetic tower entry is `(\log p)\,p^{−k/2} → 0`. Numerically
`κ(k) = 2.83, 4.00, 1.41, 4.00, 7.78, 7.00, 10.25, 18.00` for `k = 1..8` against
`0.490, 0.347, 0.245, 0.173, 0.123, 0.087, 0.061, 0.043`. Consequently:

 (i) **no rescaling identifies the two.** A positive constant times a summable decaying kernel is never an
     unbounded growing one; the two Toeplitz symbols are not conjugate (one is a bounded positive function
     on the circle, the other is not a function at all).
 (ii) **the difference is exactly one block, and it is named.** The model's `κ` is the **whole** Weil
     kernel — the model has no archimedean place and its entire archimedean side is the single integer
     `κ(0) = N_0 = 2−2g = χ_{\mathrm{top}}(C)` (`R145_001` `MISMATCH 5`) — whereas the arithmetic tower
     Gram is the **finite half `W_{fin}` only**: the pure Frobenius family carries **no archimedean block**
     (Paper 44 Corollary 8bis.1d; `R140_001` Theorem D.23.12 row (A)). The growing part `q^{k/2}` of `κ` is
     the polar/ruling term `Γ·F_2 = q^k`, which is exactly what the half-density normalization removes and
     what `W_{fin}` does not contain.
 (iii) **the object that does contain it exists and is cited**: the unit-averaged family
     `Γ^{rad}_n = Frob_n⋆Δ^{rad}` carries the **full** kernel `W_{fin}+ω_∞`, exactly and at every level
     (Paper 44 Theorem 8bis.1e; `R140_001` D.23.12 row (B)) — at the cost of forgetting the factorization
     (Prop. 3ter.5). **No object carries both** (`R140_001` D.23.12(iii); phase 136's meta-theorem).

*Proof.* The displayed asymptotics and the numerics; (ii)–(iii) are the cited rows. ∎ `PROVED`
