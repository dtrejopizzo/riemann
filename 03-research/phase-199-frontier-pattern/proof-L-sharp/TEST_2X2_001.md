## 1. Method (`TEST_2X2_001_galerkin.py`, arb/mpmath 800 bits)
B_J = rung basis (d×J), M = BᵀB, Q = I − BM⁻¹Bᵀ (L²). a_J, c_J, θ_J, θ′_J are generalized J×J eigenproblems (Cholesky in
mpmath). b_J and (QGQ)⁻¹ via the deflated matrix K = QGQ + 10·P (spec = spec(QGQ|V^⊥) ∪ {10}), arb inverse + power iteration
(cross-checked by double eigvalsh on an orthonormal complement: agreement 13 digits at J = 20, 50; double is noise for J ≤ 5).
The naive route G⁻¹ − G⁻¹B(BᵀG⁻¹B)⁻¹BᵀG⁻¹ loses the O(1) block under the 1/λ* ≈ 2·10⁶⁹ component of G⁻¹ (observed failure at
