# Phase 37 — Physics-Style Invariants

This phase looks for "physics-style" invariants — mode-counting arguments, the argument principle, spectral flow, and almost-periodicity/universality properties of $\zeta$ — that could force the de Bruijn–Newman-type parameter $\Lambda$ to zero or bound the number of off-critical zero quadruples, including a direct attack on the dichotomy "finitely many off-critical quadruples $\Rightarrow$ zero off-critical quadruples."

## Results
- Critical anchoring principle (what forces $\Lambda=0$): formulated as a guiding question; no closing argument established. (OPEN)
- Invariants that count modes one by one (argument principle, spectral flow, "inertia reader"): specified as a toolset, not shown to close the program. (OPEN)
- Target L8 (unconditional bound on the number of off-critical quadruples): assessed as currently out of reach with available tools. (OPEN)
- Dichotomy $m<\infty \Rightarrow m=0$ (rigorous attack on "finitely many off-critical quadruples implies none"): direct attack carried out; the needed non-Stepanov-almost-periodicity property of $\zeta$ in the strip and related claims are scrutinized, with gaps identified relative to the literature (Bagchi/Voronin apparatus, Besicovitch almost-periodic classes). (OPEN / partial NO-GO, see audits in Phase 38)
- Comparison of the LP-112 approach against the literature: no precedent found for "sequential self-approximation without strong recurrence" of the type needed. (NO-GO for finding a precedent; leaves the approach unsupported)
