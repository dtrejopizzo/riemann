# Phase 160 — Survey, brief, and round 1–2 construction attempts for row D beyond T > 0.8

After phase 159's two-front run closed with walls on both sides, this phase first runs a full
five-part survey over the entire prior corpus (phases 0–159, all papers) and writes a
consolidated brief (definitions, walls as a checklist, dead routes, live ingredients), then
launches a construction effort of 20 memos per round, each round checked by independent audits, aimed at either an object (G1) or a
mechanism (G2) proving the row-D positivity for T beyond the proved range (T ≤ 0.8). The run is
resource-limited: round 1 (18 memos) completed and was audited; round 2 selected five follow-up
routes (D1–D5), of which at least D2 and D4 were written up; the work was then paused
before further rounds, with an explicit status note for resumption.

## Results

- Stage 1 (five-part corpus survey, phases 0–159 and all papers) — **DONE**, consolidated into `R160_001_BRIEF.md`.
- Round 1 (18 of 20 planned memos; M11 not written, M12 partial): **no memo reached G1 or G2**; no memo accepted as complete on audit; highest score 3/10 (C3, Meyer H¹ route).
- Structural lesson of round 1: the most frequent audit objection across almost every route — the proposed identity holds verbatim for known non-ζ comparison objects (Λχ₂, Ξ_a, Davenport–Heilbronn, comb models), i.e. it is not ζ-specific — **PROVED/ARGUED per route**, repeatedly.
- Two theorems from round 1 pin down what any future carrier must violate: C1's Theorem Z (a holomorphic flow with one pole carries at most one pair of L²-zeros) and C3's Theorem H (every continuous Θ-compatible positive form is blind to off-line zeros) — **PROVED**; together they imply a successful carrier must be non-holomorphic up to isotopy (a Torelli failure) or carry a non-homogeneous positivity, neither of which has a known candidate.
- Round 2, route D2 (non-homogeneous positive structure on Meyer's H¹): **closed with a sharp wall** — every arithmetic non-homogeneous positive structure on H¹ is either blind to off-line zeros or a free (automatically positive) square; every transfer of such a structure to the Weil form through a natural flow operator is Weil's criterion itself; the whole carrier reduces G1 to G2 — **PROVED**, no sign claimed.
- Round 2, route D4 (separating-datum mechanism): builds an explicit polar-boundary theorem and completion identity (Theorems 2.1–2.2, 3.4–3.5, 4.3, 5.1–5.2) — **PROVED/PARTIAL_NEW with a negative core** — the route does not supply the needed mechanism but leaves a reusable exact toolkit (none of it sign-bearing).
- Round 1's new unaudited quantitative fact later folded into phase 169: Theorem M (G2_B6, this phase's predecessor work carried into R160_C-series) bounding the per-window margin doubly-exponentially — referenced here and formalized across phases.
- The construction effort (rounds 3–6) was never completed: the phase was stopped before resumption; the formal `R160_098_STRONGEST_CONSTRUCTION.md` / `R160_099_SYNTHESIS.md` deliverables were never produced.
