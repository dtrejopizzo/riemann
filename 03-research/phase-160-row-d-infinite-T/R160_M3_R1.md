## §1 Definitions (P46 conventions, main.tex:115–147)

* `I_T = (−T,T)`; `F ∈ L²(I_T)` real, extended by zero; `f(τ) := F̂(τ) = ∫F(t)e^{iτt}dt`; `c_F(u) = ∫F(t)F(t−u)dt`
  (even, real, continuous, `supp c_F ⊂ [−2T,2T]`, `ĉ_F = |f|²`, `|c_F(u)| ≤ c_F(0) = ‖F‖²`).
* `m(τ) = Re ψ(¼+iτ/2) − log π`, `v_n = 2Λ(n)/√n`, `ℓ_n := log n`, `N_T = {n≥2: ℓ_n<2T}`, `A_T = Σ_{N_T}v_n`,
  `σ_T(τ) = m(τ) − Σ_{n∈N_T}v_n cos(τℓ_n)`.
* Form domain `𝒟_T := {F ∈ L²(I_T) real: ∫log(2+|τ|)|f(τ)|²dτ < ∞}`, norm `‖F‖²_𝒟 := ∫(1+log(2+|τ|))|f|²dτ`.
* `𝔞_T(F) = (1/2π)∫σ_T|f|²` [prim-carrying]; Tate moments `A(F) = ∫Fe^{t/2}`, `B(F) = ∫Fe^{−t/2}`;
  `Φ_F(s) = ∫F(t)e^{(s−½)t}dt` (so `Φ_F(1) = A(F)`, `Φ_F(0) = B(F)`, `Φ_F(½+iτ) = f(τ)`, and `Φ_F(\bar s) = \overline{Φ_F(s)}`);
  `W_T(F) = 𝔞_T(F) + 2A(F)B(F)` (eq:W, real `F`); for complex `G`: `W_T(G) = (1/2π)∫σ_T|Ĝ|² + 2Re[A(G)\overline{B(G)}]`.
  `λ^W_T := inf_{F∈𝒟_T∖0}W_T(F)/‖F‖²` (the complex and real infima coincide, Lemma 1.2).
* Lévy form (`thm:A`, main.tex:416–421, PROVED): `𝔞_T(G) = C_0‖G‖² + ∫_0^∞(c_G(0) − Re c_G(u))K(u)du − Σ_{N_T}v_n Re c_G(ℓ_n)`,
  `K(u) = ½/sinh(u/2) + ½/cosh(u/2) > 0`, `C_0 = ψ(¼) − log π`. Note `K(u) ≤ 1/u + ½` on `(0,∞)`.
* **Prime side functional.** For an even real `h` with `supp h ⊂ [−2T,2T]` and `∫_0^∞|h(0)−h(u)|K(u)du < ∞`:
  `𝔴(h) := C_0h(0) + ∫_0^∞(h(0)−h(u))K(u)du − Σ_{n≥2}v_n h(ℓ_n)`, and `ĥ(s) := ∫_R h(u)e^{(s−½)u}du`.
  Thus `𝔞_T(F) = 𝔴(c_F)`, `\widehat{c_F}(s) = Φ_F(s)Φ_F(1−s)`, and `2A(F)B(F) = 2∫_R c_F(u)cosh(u/2)du`.
* **Explicit formula (EF)** in this normalisation: for `G ∈ 𝒟_T` (complex), `W_T(G) = Σ_ρ Φ_G(ρ)\overline{Φ_G(1−\barρ)}`,
  summed as in P46 eq:W (main.tex:131–145; CITED there as Weil's formula, PROVED). For real `F`: `W_T(F) = Σ_ρ \widehat{c_F}(ρ)`.
* **Weil measure.** `μ_T := K(u)du|_{(0,∞)} + Σ_{n∈N_T}v_nδ_{ℓ_n}`, a positive Borel measure on `(0,∞)`; for any
  `φ` with `|φ(u)| ≤ Cu²` near 0, `∫φ dμ_T` converges absolutely on `(0,2T]`.
* **Deformation.** `E_a`: `(E_aF)(t) = e^{at}F(t)1_{I_T}(t)`, `a ∈ C`; `u_a := cosh(at)F`, `w_a := sinh(at)F`.
* **Falsifier.** `Ξ_a(s) = ξ(s+a)ξ(s−a)`, `0<a<½` (P47 main.md:52, 1220–1226): zero set `Z(Ξ_a) = {ρ±a}`; prime weights
  `v_n cosh(aℓ_n) = Λ(n)(n^a+n^{−a})/√n`; Tate exponents `±(½±a)`. Its complete window form is **defined** by the zero side:
  `W^{(a)}_T(F) := Σ_{ρ'∈Z(Ξ_a)}Φ_F(ρ')Φ_F(1−ρ') = Σ_ρ[\widehat{c_F}(ρ+a) + \widehat{c_F}(ρ−a)]`, and its bottom
  `λ^{(a)}_T := inf_{F∈𝒟_T∖0}W^{(a)}_T(F)/‖F‖²` [compl]. `T*(a) := inf{T>0: λ^{(a)}_T < 0} ∈ (0,∞]`,
  `a*(T) := sup{a∈[0,½): λ^{(a)}_T ≥ 0}`.

**Lemma 1.1 (stability of the form domain).** If `φ ∈ C^∞` on a neighbourhood of `[−T,T]` and `F ∈ 𝒟_T`, then `φF ∈ 𝒟_T`
and `‖φF‖_𝒟 ≤ C_φ‖F‖_𝒟`, where `C_φ` depends only on `‖ψ̂‖_{L¹}` and `∫|ψ̂(η)|log(2+|η|)dη` for a Schwartz extension `ψ` of `φ`.
*Proof.* `\widehat{ψF} = (2π)^{−1}ψ̂ * f`; by Cauchy–Schwarz `|ψ̂*f(τ)|² ≤ ‖ψ̂‖_1∫|ψ̂(η)||f(τ−η)|²dη`, and
`log(2+|τ|) ≤ log(2+|τ−η|) + log(2+|η|)` since `2+|τ| ≤ (2+|τ−η|)(2+|η|)`. Integrating,
`∫log(2+|τ|)|ψ̂*f|² ≤ ‖ψ̂‖_1[∫|ψ̂(η)|log(2+|η|)dη·‖f‖²_2 + ‖ψ̂‖_1∫log(2+|ξ|)|f(ξ)|²dξ] < ∞`. ∎

**Lemma 1.2 (real/complex splitting).** For `G = F_1 + iF_2` with `F_j` real in `𝒟_T`: `W_T(G) = W_T(F_1) + W_T(F_2)`.
*Proof.* `|Ĝ|² = |f_1|² + |f_2|² + 2Re(\overline{f_1}f_2)i·(−1)`… precisely `|f_1+if_2|² = |f_1|²+|f_2|² − 2Im(\overline{f_1}f_2)`; for real
`F_j`, `f_j(−τ) = \overline{f_j(τ)}`, so `τ ↦ Im(\overline{f_1}f_2)(τ)` is odd while `σ_T` is even: the cross term integrates to
zero. Polar: `Re[A(G)\overline{B(G)}] = A_1B_1 + A_2B_2` with `A_j = A(F_j)`, `B_j = B(F_j)` real. ∎
Consequently `inf` over complex `G` of `W_T(G)/‖G‖²` equals the infimum over real `F`, and the same holds for `W^{(a)}_T`.

## §2 Theorem 1 — the deformation identity

**Theorem 1.** Let `F ∈ 𝒟_T` be real and put `Q_F(a) := 2W_T(u_a) − 2W_T(w_a)` for real `a`. Then:

(i) (**autocorrelation law**) `c_{u_a}(x) − c_{w_a}(x) = cosh(ax)c_F(x)` for all real `x`, `a`.

(ii) (**prime side**) `Q_F(a) = 2𝔴(cosh(a·)c_F) + 4∫_R cosh(au)c_F(u)cosh(u/2)du`.

(iii) (**zero side; Theorem A in both forms**) `Q_F(a) = W^{(a)}_T(F)`, the complete Weil form of `Ξ_a`; its primitive-carrying
part `𝔞^{(a)}_T(F) := 2𝔴(cosh(a·)c_F) = 2[𝔞_T(u_a) − 𝔞_T(w_a)]` on all of `𝒟_T` [prim]; and the polar part is
`2[Φ_F(1+a)Φ_F(−a) + Φ_F(1−a)Φ_F(a)] = 2[A(u_a)B(u_a) − A(w_a)B(w_a)]·2`, vanishing exactly on `P^{(a)}_T`
(the four Tate conditions `Φ_F(±(½±a)+½)=0`), where (iii) reduces to R153_005's Theorem A.

(iv) (**measure form**) `Q_F(a) = 2W_T(F) − 2∫_{(0,∞)}(cosh(au)−1)c_F(u)dμ_T(u) + 4∫_R(cosh(au)−1)c_F(u)cosh(u/2)du`.

(v) (**holomorphy**) The right side of (iv) defines an even entire function of `a ∈ C` of exponential type `≤ 2T`, real on
`R` and on `iR`; it extends (ii)–(iii) to all `a ∈ C` (the zero side by analytic continuation of both sides).

(vi) (**imaginary axis = modulations**) `Q_F(iθ) = W_T(e^{iθt}F) + W_T(e^{−iθt}F)` for real `θ`; hence `Q_F(iθ) ≥ 2λ^W_T‖F‖²`
for all `θ`, and `Q_F(iθ) → +∞` as `|θ| → ∞` for every `F ≠ 0`.

*Proof.* (i) `cosh(at)cosh(a(t−x)) − sinh(at)sinh(a(t−x)) = cosh(ax)` (addition formula); multiply by `F(t)F(t−x)` and integrate.

(ii) By Lemma 1.1, `u_a, w_a ∈ 𝒟_T`. By the Lévy form and `2A(G)B(G) = 2∫c_G cosh(u/2)`, `W_T(G) = 𝔴(c_G) + 2∫_R c_G cosh(u/2)du`
for real `G ∈ 𝒟_T`; both terms are linear in `c_G`. Apply to `G = u_a, w_a`, subtract, and use (i).

(iii) By (EF), `W_T(G) = Σ_ρ\widehat{c_G}(ρ)`, linear in `c_G`; so `Q_F(a) = 2Σ_ρ\widehat{cosh(a·)c_F}(ρ)`. Now
`\widehat{cosh(a·)c_F}(s) = ½[\widehat{c_F}(s+a) + \widehat{c_F}(s−a)]`, hence `Q_F(a) = Σ_ρ[\widehat{c_F}(ρ+a)+\widehat{c_F}(ρ−a)]
= Σ_{ρ'∈Z(Ξ_a)}Φ_F(ρ')Φ_F(1−ρ') = W^{(a)}_T(F)` (the sums converge as the two sums `Σ_ρ\widehat{c_{u_a}}(ρ)`, `Σ_ρ\widehat{c_{w_a}}(ρ)`
of (EF) do). The prime part of (ii) is `2𝔴(cosh(a·)c_F) = 2C_0‖F‖² + 2∫_0^∞(c_F(0)−cosh(au)c_F(u))K(u)du − 2Σ_n v_n cosh(aℓ_n)c_F(ℓ_n)`,
i.e. the ζ prime side with weights `v_n cosh(aℓ_n)` and archimedean test `cosh(au)c_F`, which is P47's `Ξ_a` datum
(main.md:1220–1226: weights `Λ(n)(n^a+n^{−a})n^{−½}`, symbol `Σ_±Re ψ(¼±a/2+iτ/2) − 2log π`; the symbol identity is
`(1/2π)∫[m(τ+ia)+m(τ−ia)]|f|² = 2C_0‖F‖² + 2∫(c_F(0) − cosh(au)c_F(u))K du`, the Lévy representation applied to the entire
extension of `m`, R153_001 §1.2(ii)/R153_005 Lemma C1). Polar part of (ii): `4∫cosh(au)cosh(u/2)c_F du =
∫c_F[e^{(½+a)u}+e^{(½−a)u}+e^{(−½+a)u}+e^{(−½−a)u}]du = Σ_{±}[\widehat{c_F}(1±a) + \widehat{c_F}(±a)]
= 2[Φ_F(1+a)Φ_F(−a) + Φ_F(1−a)Φ_F(a)]`; and directly `A(u_a)B(u_a) − A(w_a)B(w_a) = ½[A(E_aF)B(E_{−a}F) + A(E_{−a}F)B(E_aF)]`
with `A(E_{±a}F) = Φ_F(1±a)`, `B(E_{∓a}F) = Φ_F(∓a)`, consistent. The four Tate exponents `±(½±a)` are those of P47.
On `P^{(a)}_T` all four `Φ_F(1±a), Φ_F(±a)` vanish, and (iii) is R153_005's `⟨A^{(a)}_TF,F⟩ = 2[⟨A_Tu,u⟩ − ⟨A_Tw,w⟩]`.

(iv) `𝔴(cosh(a·)c_F) − 𝔴(c_F) = −∫_0^∞(cosh(au)−1)c_F K du − Σ_{n}v_n(cosh(aℓ_n)−1)c_F(ℓ_n)` (the `h(0)` terms agree since
`cosh 0 = 1`; contacts with `ℓ_n ≥ 2T` contribute `c_F(ℓ_n) = 0`), which is `−∫(cosh(au)−1)c_F dμ_T`. Add the polar difference.

(v) In (iv) every integral is over `[0,2T]` (support of `c_F`), the integrands are entire in `a`, and near `u = 0`,
`|cosh(au)−1|K(u) ≤ (|a|²u²/2)cosh(|a|u)(1/u+½)` is integrable. Dominated convergence gives an entire function; the bound
`|cosh(au)−1| ≤ 2e^{2T|a|}` on `[0,2T]` gives exponential type `≤ 2T`. Evenness and reality on both axes are read off from
`cosh(au)` (`cosh(iθu) = cos θu`). Both sides of (iii) are entire in `a` on the region where the zero sum converges
locally uniformly (it does on compacts of `C`, as `Σ_ρ|\widehat{c_F}(ρ±a)|` is dominated by the (EF) sums for `e^{±(Re a)t}F`),
so (iii) holds for all `a ∈ C`.

(vi) With `G := e^{iθt}F`: `c_G(u) = e^{iθu}c_F(u)`, so `Re c_G = cos(θu)c_F` and by the Lévy form (complex version)
`𝔞_T(G) = 𝔴(cos(θ·)c_F)`; the same for `\bar G`. Polar: `4∫cos(θu)c_F cosh(u/2)du = 2Re[Φ_F(1+iθ)Φ_F(−iθ)] + 2Re[Φ_F(1−iθ)Φ_F(iθ)]
= 4Re[A(G)\overline{B(G)}]` (use `Φ_F(\bar s) = \overline{Φ_F(s)}`; `A(G) = Φ_F(1+iθ)`, `B(G) = Φ_F(iθ)`). So (iv) at `a = iθ`
reads `𝔞_T(G) + 𝔞_T(\bar G) + 2Re A(G)\overline{B(G)} + 2Re A(\bar G)\overline{B(\bar G)} = W_T(G) + W_T(\bar G)`, using
`A(\bar G) = \overline{A(G)}`. The bound follows from the definition of `λ^W_T` (Lemma 1.2). Growth: `σ_T(τ) ≥ log(2+|τ|) − C_T`
(`m(τ) = log(|τ|/2) + O(τ^{−2})`, comb bounded by `A_T`), and `∫σ_T(τ)|f(τ−θ)|²dτ ≥ ∫_{|τ|≥|θ|/2}(log(2+|τ|)−C_T)|f(τ−θ)|² − C_T∫_{|τ|<|θ|/2}|f(τ−θ)|²
≥ (log(2+|θ|/2) − C_T)(‖f‖² − o(1)) − C_T o(1) → ∞`, while the polar terms are `O(‖F‖²)`. ∎

**Remark 2.1 (what Theorem 1 says about O-151.4).** On the real `a`-axis `Q_F` is a *difference* of two ζ-forms (cross term,
numerator sign); on the imaginary axis it is a *sum* of two ζ-forms (summand sign). The same entire function carries both:
the axis "summand vs numerator" of R151_005 is the choice of a line through the origin of the `a`-plane. Under RH the
function is `≥ 0` on `iR`; on `R` it becomes negative for `T > T*(a)` (Theorem 4). No entire function of exponential type
is constrained in sign on one axis by its sign on the other (`sin²z` vs `−sinh²y`), so no Phragmén–Lindelöf argument
transports positivity between the axes — this closes the obvious "continuation" idea on this route.

## §3 Theorem 2 — the sandwich and the regularity of the falsifier bottom

Put `C_T := 2T² + ⅔T³ + 4T²A_T + 32T²sinh T` and `ε_T(a) := 2∫_{(0,2T)}(cosh(au)−1)dμ_T + 8∫_0^{2T}(cosh(au)−1)cosh(u/2)du`.

**Theorem 2.** For real `a` and every `T>0`:
(a) `|Q_F(a) − 2W_T(F)| ≤ ε_T(a)‖F‖²` for all real `F ∈ 𝒟_T`; hence `2λ^W_T − ε_T(a) ≤ λ^{(a)}_T ≤ 2λ^W_T + ε_T(a)`, and
`ε_T(a) ≤ a²cosh(2aT)C_T`.
(b) `T ↦ λ^{(a)}_T` is nonincreasing on `(0,∞)`, including across contact thresholds.
(c) `a ↦ λ^{(a)}_T` is locally Lipschitz on `R`, with `λ^{(0)}_T = 2λ^W_T`; `λ^{(a)}_T > −∞`.
(d) For `T ≤ 0.005`: `λ^{(a)}_T ≥ 2(0.599) − ε_T(a) > 0` for `|a| < ½` (so `T*(a) > 0.005`); more generally
`T*(a) ≥ T_0` whenever `ε_{T_0}(a) ≤ 2λ^W_{T_0}` and `λ^W_{T_0}>0`.

*Proof.* (a) In Theorem 1(iv), `|c_F| ≤ ‖F‖²`, `μ_T ≥ 0`, `cosh(au) − 1 ≥ 0`, `cosh(u/2) > 0`; take absolute values. For the
explicit bound use `cosh(x)−1 ≤ (x²/2)cosh x`, `∫_0^{2T}u²K du ≤ ∫_0^{2T}(u + u²/2)du = 2T² + ⅔T³`, `Σ_{N_T}v_nℓ_n² ≤ 4T²A_T`,
`∫_0^{2T}u²cosh(u/2)du ≤ 4T²·2sinh T`. The infima follow by taking `inf` over `F` in `2W_T(F) − ε‖F‖² ≤ Q_F(a) ≤ 2W_T(F) + ε‖F‖²`.
(b) For `T<T'` extend `F ∈ 𝒟_T` by zero: `c_F`, `‖F‖`, the Tate moments are unchanged, `c_F = 0` on `|u| ≥ 2T`, and the new
contacts `ℓ_n ≥ 2T` contribute nothing to (iv); so `Q_F(a)` is the same number for `T` and `T'`, and `𝒟_T ⊂ 𝒟_{T'}` isometrically
(this is P46 `lem:monoT`'s proof, main.tex:483–500, applied to the test `cosh(a·)c_F`). (c) From (iv),
`|Q_F(a) − Q_F(a')| ≤ ‖F‖²·|a−a'|·2T sinh(2T max(|a|,|a'|))·[μ_T((0,2T)) restricted… ]`; precisely
`|cosh(au) − cosh(a'u)| ≤ |a−a'|u sinh(u max(|a|,|a'|))`, and `u sinh(bu)K(u)` is integrable on `(0,2T)`, so the Lipschitz
constant is finite on compacts; `λ^{(0)}_T = 2λ^W_T` is (iv) at `a=0`; `λ^W_T > −∞` is P46 (the form is bounded below), so (a)
gives `λ^{(a)}_T > −∞`. (d) R157_004 Lemma E: `λ^W_T ≥ C_0 + log coth(T/2) − 4sinh T ≥ 0.599` for `T ≤ 0.005`; then (a). ∎

## §4 Theorem 3 — the falsifier direction is strictly downhill at second order (unconditional)

**Lemma 4.1 (discrete spectrum, bottom attained, finite multiplicity).** `𝒟_T ↪ L²(I_T)` is compact; `W_T` is a closed,
semibounded form on `L²(I_T)` with form domain `𝒟_T` and form norm equivalent to `‖·‖_𝒟`; hence the associated self-adjoint
operator has compact resolvent, `λ^W_T` is its least eigenvalue, attained, with finite-dimensional eigenspace `E_T`, and every
`F_T ∈ E_T` satisfies the variational equation `W_T(G,F_T) = λ^W_T⟨G,F_T⟩` for all `G ∈ 𝒟_T`.
*Proof.* Compactness: if `‖F_j‖_𝒟 ≤ 1`, the `f_j` are entire of exponential type `T` with `|f_j(τ)| ≤ √(2T)`, `|f_j'(τ)| ≤ T√(2T)`
(differentiate under the integral), so a subsequence converges uniformly on compacts (Arzelà–Ascoli), while
`∫_{|τ|>R}|f_j|² ≤ 1/log(2+R)` uniformly; hence `f_j` is Cauchy in `L²(R)`, i.e. `F_j` in `L²(I_T)`. Semiboundedness and
closedness: `σ_T(τ) ≥ log(2+|τ|) − C_T` and `|2A(F)B(F)| ≤ 4sinh T‖F‖²` give `W_T(F) ≥ (1/2π)‖F‖²_𝒟 − C'_T‖F‖²`, and
`W_T(F) ≤ C''_T‖F‖²_𝒟`. The rest is the standard theory of semibounded closed forms with compact form domain (Reed–Simon
XIII.64; attainment is also Bombieri 2000 Thm 3, CITED via R157_004 §1.2). The variational equation is the first-order
condition for the minimum of `W_T(F)/‖F‖²`. ∎

**Theorem 3.** Let `T > 0`, `λ := λ^W_T`, `E_T` the bottom eigenspace. Then:
(a) for every `F ∈ E_T`: `q_1(F) = −2[W_T(tF) − λ‖tF‖²] ≤ 0`;
(b) there exists `F_T ∈ E_T`, `‖F_T‖=1`, with `q_1(F_T) < 0`;
(c) consequently `λ^{(a)}_T ≤ 2λ^W_T − a²|q_1(F_T)| + R_T(a)` with `0 ≤ R_T(a) ≤ (a⁴/12)cosh(2aT)C'_T`,
`C'_T := ∫_0^{2T}u⁴dμ_T + 4∫_0^{2T}u⁴cosh(u/2)du`; in particular `λ^{(a)}_T < 2λ^W_T` for `0 < a < a_1(T)`, and
if `λ^W_T = 0` then `λ^{(a)}_T < 0` for all small `a > 0`.

*Proof.* (a) `t²F ∈ 𝒟_T` (Lemma 1.1), so the variational equation gives `W_T(t²F,F) = λ⟨t²F,F⟩ = λ‖tF‖²`. By Corollary 1.3,
`q_1(F) = 2W_T(t²F,F) − 2W_T(tF) = 2λ‖tF‖² − 2W_T(tF)`, and `W_T(tF) ≥ λ‖tF‖²` because `tF ∈ 𝒟_T` and `λ` is the bottom.
(b) Suppose `q_1(F) = 0` for every `F ∈ E_T`. Then `W_T(tF) = λ‖tF‖²` for every `F ∈ E_T`, i.e. `tF` attains the bottom, so
`tF ∈ E_T`: `E_T` is invariant under multiplication by `t`. Take `F ∈ E_T∖0`; then `F, tF, t²F, …` all lie in `E_T`, and they
are linearly independent (a polynomial `p` with `p(t)F(t) = 0` a.e. on `I_T` vanishes on a set of positive measure, so `p = 0`).
This contradicts `dim E_T < ∞` (Lemma 4.1). (c) By Theorem 1(iv) with `cosh(x) − 1 − x²/2 ∈ [0, (x⁴/24)cosh x]`:
`Q_{F_T}(a) = 2λ + a²q_1(F_T) + R`, `|R| ≤ (a⁴/24)cosh(2aT)·[2∫u⁴dμ_T + 8∫u⁴cosh(u/2)]‖F_T‖²`; then `λ^{(a)}_T ≤ Q_{F_T}(a)`. ∎

**Remark 4.2 (reading).** (1) The theorem is prime-blind in the sense of I-1: it holds for every comb, and in particular for

*Proof.* (a) `Ξ_a` has zeros off `Re s = ½`: by Hardy `ξ` has a zero `ρ` with `Re ρ = ½`, and `Re(ρ ± a) = ½ ± a`. P47

## §6 The mechanism (G2): what this route forces, what it gives, where it stops

### 6.1 Proposition 5 (the datum)
The deformation acts on the comb by `Λ(p^k) ↦ Λ_a(p^k) := Λ(p^k)·2cosh(ka log p)`, on the archimedean symbol by
`m ↦ Σ_±Re ψ(¼±a/2+iτ/2) − 2log π`, and on the Tate exponents by `{±½} ↦ {±(½±a)}` (P47 main.md:1220–1226). The deformed
data satisfy P0 (`Λ_a ≥ 0`), P1 (prime-power support), P2 (Hankel blocks PSD: `k ↦ 2cosh(kaℓ_p)` is the moment sequence of
`δ_{p^a}+δ_{p^{−a}}`) and the functional equation; what fails is unimodularity of the local roots (P47 Thm 7.1(iii): `p^{±a}`
instead of `1`), visible on the comb as the growth `Λ_a(p^k)/log p = 2cosh(kaℓ_p) → ∞` in `k`, against `Λ(p^k)/log p ≡ 1`.
**Consequence for G2.** Let `M` be any argument that proves `λ^W_T ≥ 0` for all `T` from `(Λ, m, {±½})`. By Theorem 4(a), for
every `a > 0` there is `T > T*(a)` with `λ^{(a)}_T < 0`; hence `M` applied to `(Λ_a, m_a, {±(½±a)})` must fail, for every
`a > 0`. Since `Λ_a − Λ = O(a²k²ℓ_p²)Λ` termwise, the failing line of `M` must be *quantitative in the k-growth of `Λ(p^k)`
at arbitrarily small deformation*, uniformly in `T` — no qualitative use of "`Λ ≥ 0`, prime-power support, PSD Hankel" can be
that line (I-7). Moreover a Beurling system with `Λ_B(p^k) = log p` and off-line zeros (T3, I-6) has `k`-bounded weights, so the
line must also use the additive structure of `Z` (P47 §28.4(5)). Both facts are on record separately; the deformation makes the
first one *metric*: the distance from `ζ` to the failing comb is `O(a²)` per contact and the window `T` resolves it only when
`a ≳ a*(T)`, which is super-exponentially small under RH (Remark 5.1). METHOD; not a sign.

### 6.2 Remark (the summand structure under RH; flagged S-6)
Under RH, `θ ↦ Q_F(iθ) = 2Σ_γ|f(γ+θ)|²` is an even, nonnegative, entire function of exponential type `2T` with
`Q_F(iθ) = O(log|θ|)`, hence `∫log⁺Q_F(iθ)/(1+θ²)dθ < ∞`, and by Akhiezer's theorem (Fejér–Riesz for entire functions of
exponential type; CITED: Akhiezer, *Lectures on approximation theory*, §6.? / Krein) `Q_F(iθ) = |k_F(θ)|²` for one entire `k_F`
of exponential type `T` zero-free in the upper half-plane; then `Q_F(a) = k_F(−ia)\overline{k_F(ia)}` for real `a`. The prime side
gives `Q_F(iθ)` as an explicit cosine transform of `c_F μ_T` plus a polar term (Theorem 1(iv)), but `k_F` is not
prime-computable: producing it from `(Λ, m)` would be a positive Hilbert realisation — the Douglas factorization of S-6 —
and is not attempted. No lift is claimed. Under ¬RH, `Q_F(iθ)` changes sign for suitable `F` (odd-order zeros of `Q_F` on

### 6.3 The G2 step in deformation coordinates, and why the route stops here
By Theorem 4(b), G2 on this route is:

> **(G2-def)** For every `T_0 > 0` there is `a_0 = a_0(T_0) > 0` with `λ^{(a)}_T ≥ 0` for all `T ≤ T_0` and `0 ≤ a ≤ a_0`.

Everything proved above brackets `a*(T)` from both sides (Theorems 2(a), 3(c)) but the lower bracket has the form
`a² ≤ 2λ^W_T/(cosh(2aT)C_T)`, i.e. it *presupposes* `λ^W_T > 0`. One might hope to replace `2λ^W_T` on the right by a quantity
computable on the prime side without knowing its sign — e.g. to prove (G2-def) directly by showing that the deformed form
`W^{(a)}_T` on the window is positive for small `a` by an argument that exploits the *smallness* of the deformation. But
Theorem 3 shows the deformation is a *descent* direction at every `T`, for every comb: the deformed bottom is `2λ^W_T − a²|q_1| + O(a⁴)`,
strictly below `2λ^W_T`. So positivity of the deformed form for small `a` is strictly *stronger* than positivity of `W_T`, and any
proof of (G2-def) contains a proof of `λ^W_T > 0`. The two ingredients that made Theorems 2–3 provable — the positivity of the
Weil measure `μ_T` (for the bound `ε_T(a)`) and the finite multiplicity of the bottom (for the strict descent) — are class-level
(I-1): they hold for `Λ1_{n≥N}` and `Λ + c1_{n=n₀}`. Hence the route, by itself, cannot supply the load-bearing line of Prop 5.
It does tell what that line must produce: **a lower bound `λ^W_T ≥ δ(T) > 0` from the k-boundedness of `Λ(p^k)/log p` and the
additive structure, with `δ(T)` allowed to be as small as `exp(−e^{(2−ε)T})`** (Theorem M) — an exact-arithmetic positivity
with no margin, which is row D itself (ROW-D-REDUCED-STATEMENT §1). The memo records this as the OPEN step without disguise.
