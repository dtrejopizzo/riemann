## §1 Exact definitions (quoted; file:line)

### 1.1 The window, symbol, contacts — P46 main.tex:115–124
> "Throughout, `T>0`, `I_T=(−T,T)`, and for `F∈L²(I_T)` (extended by zero) we write `F̂(τ)=∫F(t)e^{iτt}dt` and
> `c_F(u)=∫F(t)\overline{F(t−u)}dt` for the autocorrelation, so that `c_F` is continuous, supported in `[−2T,2T]`, and
> `ĉ_F=|F̂|²` on `R`. Put `m(τ)=Re ψ(¼+iτ/2)−log π`, `σ_T(τ)=m(τ)−Σ_{n∈N_T} v_n cos(τ log n)`, `v_n=2Λ(n)/√n`,
> where `N_T={n≥2: log n<2T}` is the set of *contacts* and `A_T:=Σ_{n∈N_T}v_n` the total mass of the prime comb."

### 1.2 The truncated (primitive-carrying) form `𝔞_T = A_T` — main.tex:125–131
> "`𝔞_T(F)=(1/2π)∫_R σ_T(τ)|F̂(τ)|²dτ`, defined on the form domain `{F∈L²(I_T): ∫log(2+|τ|)|F̂|²<∞}`; it is the
> quadratic form of the operator `A_T` of [Paper42], namely the archimedean multiplier `G_Γ−m_0` minus
> `Σ_{n∈N_T}w_n(S_{log n}+S_{−log n})`, `w_n=Λ(n)/√n`, compressed to `I_T`."

Lévy form (`thm:A`, main.tex:416–421, PROVED): `𝔞_T(F)=C_0‖F‖²+𝔈(c_F)−Σ_{n∈N_T}v_n c_F(log n)`,
`𝔈(Φ)=∫_0^∞(Φ(0)−Re Φ(u))K(u)du ≥ 0`, with (`lem:K`, main.tex:363–370) `m(τ)=C_0+∫_0^∞(1−cos τu)K(u)du`,
`C_0=ψ(¼)−log π=−5.3721834…`, `K` completely monotone (`thm:S`, main.tex:386).

### 1.3 Tate moments, `P_T`, the complete form `W_T` — main.tex:131–145
> "The two *Tate moments* are `A(F)=∫F(t)e^{t/2}dt` and `B(F)=∫F(t)e^{−t/2}dt`, and the *primitive subspace* is
> `P_T={F∈L²(I_T): A(F)=B(F)=0}`. The *complete Weil form* on the window is `W_T(F)=𝔞_T(F)+2Re(A(F)\overline{B(F)})`
> (eq:W), which by the Weil explicit formula equals `Σ_ρΦ_F(ρ)Φ_F(1−ρ)`, `Φ_F(s)=∫F(t)e^{(s−½)t}dt` … Weil's
> criterion says that RH is equivalent to `W_T≥0` for every `T`, and [Paper45, Thm 10.4] (see also [Bombieri2000,
> Thm 1]) that primitive tests suffice: `RH ⟺ ∀T: W_T≥0 on L²(I_T) ⟺ ∀T: 𝔞_T≥0 on P_T`."

### 1.4 The bottoms — main.tex:146–147, macros 38–40, `rem:Kprim` 451–456, `thm:A`
> "Write `λ_min(A_T|P_T)` for the bottom of `𝔞_T` on the unit sphere of `P_T` and `λ^W_T` for that of `W_T` on the
> unit sphere of `L²(I_T)`; `λ^W_T ≤ λ_min(A_T|P_T)` since the two forms agree on `P_T`."

`λ^prim_T := inf_{Φ∈𝒦^prim}[C_0+𝔈(Φ)−Σv_nΦ(log n)] = C_0−Λ_T ≤ λ^A_T` (`thm:A`), and `rem:Kprim`: "also
`λ^W_T ≤ λ^prim_T`". So always **`λ^W_T ≤ λ^prim_T ≤ λ^A_T`** (PROVED). `λ^prim_T = λ^A_T` is "not asserted".
`T ↦ λ^A_T, λ^prim_T, λ^W_T` are nonincreasing (`lem:monoT`, main.tex:483, PROVED); `λ^W_T` is continuous
(`lem:contT`, main.tex:504, PROVED; = Bombieri 2000 Thm 5).

### 1.6 Primal and dual — `def:PD`, main.tex:553–559
> "**(P)** `λ^prim_T=inf{∫σ_T dμ: μ an even probability measure on R, μ̌(u)=0 for |u|≥2T, ∫ĝ_0dμ=∫ĝ_1dμ=0}`.

### 1.7 The flat-top certificate — `def:W`, `eq:nuw`, main.tex:1219–1227; `thm:Z` 1234–1251
> "`𝒲_T` is the set of even measurable `w:R→R` with `w≡1` on `[−2T,2T]` and `|w(u)|≤Ce^{−(½+δ)|u|}` for `|u|≥2T`,

> measure `dν_w=[½(1−w(u))K(|u|)+2w(u)cosh(u/2)]1_{|u|≥2T}du−Σ_{n∉N_T}(v_n/2)w(log n)(δ_{log n}+δ_{−log n})`, and
> `f_w:=σ_T+ν̂_w+2ĝ_0`."

`(ν_w,2,0)` is dual-feasible, so `W_T ≥ inf f_w` on `L²(I_T)` (main.tex:1229–1233). **A flat-top certificate at `T`**
is a `w∈𝒲_T` with `f_w ≥ 0` (value `c` if `f_w ≥ c`). `thm:Z` (PROVED): (i) `f_w(τ)=C_0+∫_0^∞(1−w(u)cos τu)K(u)du
−Σ_{n≥2}v_n w(log n)cos(τ log n)+2∫w(u)cosh(u/2)cos(τu)du`, "the right-hand side of the Weil explicit formula for
the even test function `h_τ(u)=w(u)cos(τu)`"; (ii) for `w∈𝒲_T^reg`, `f_w(τ)=Σ_ρ ŵ(τ−γ_ρ)` (absolutely convergent):
a certificate is a one-sided smoothing of the zero measure. `thm:Zexh` (main.tex:1297, `T≥½log2`, PROVED):
`sup_{w∈𝒲_T^reg} inf f_w = λ^W_T` [compl]; with the `β`-correction the sup is `λ^prim_T` [prim]. `cor:RH`
(main.tex:1328): RH ⟺ for every `T≥½log2` some `w∈𝒲_T^reg` has `f_w≥0` (EQUIV). Alignment form (`def:Al`,
main.tex:3735): `Al_T(w)` ⟺ `f_w ≥ 0`; `thm:Al` (main.tex:3772): for `T≥½log2`, `[∃w: Al_T(w)] ⟺ λ^W_T>0` (EQUIV
