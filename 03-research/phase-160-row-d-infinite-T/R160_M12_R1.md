form, not to a sign. (1) The identity `Λ = μ∗log`, read on each prime tower, is *exactly* the statement that the

disc — its value at the centre and its value at the Euler point `p^{−1/2}` — weighted by `log p` (Theorem 2.1, both

of `prop:finsupp`/Doc 142, so the per-prime information is the known one; every further bit is cross-prime (§2.3).
(2) The "entropy-weighted `×p` construction" exists and is canonical: `Λ(p^k) = h_Leb(×p)·tr((×p)^{k*}|H⁰)`, the
comb is the `H⁰`-part of the Lefschetz formulas of the endomorphisms `×p^k` of the circle weighted by the entropy
(= Lyapunov exponent) `log p`, and the Ruelle zeta of the family is `ζ(s−1)/ζ(s)` (Theorem 3.3). (3) The `×p`
action reaches the window data in exactly two places: contraction of the window `T ↦ T/p` is `×p` on every prime
circle (Theorem 3.4), and de la Vallée Poussin's `3+4cos θ+cos 2θ` is a positivity along the `×2`-orbit `{θ,2θ}` on
every prime circle (Proposition 5.3). (4) Rigidity is sign-blind: Rudolph–Johnson classifies the positive-entropy
invariant measures, and those are exactly the Euler-neutral ones; window data are never invariant (Theorems 3.5,
3.6). (5) o-minimality sees a single window only, and the family over `T` is not definable (Proposition 4.1). The

what closing it would take. **No sign is claimed for any `T > 0.8`. No label moves.** Numerics: none.

## §1 Definitions

**1.1 Window objects (P46 main.tex:115–147).** `T > 0`, `I_T = (−T,T)`, `F ∈ L²(I_T)` extended by zero;
`F̂(τ) = ∫F(t)e^{iτt}dt`; `c_F(u) = ∫F(t)\overline{F(t−u)}dt`, continuous, supported in `[−2T,2T]`, `ĉ_F = |F̂|²`;
`m(τ) = Re ψ(¼+iτ/2) − log π`; `v_n = 2Λ(n)/√n`; `N_T = {n ≥ 2: log n < 2T}`;
`𝔞_T(F) = (1/2π)∫σ_T|F̂|²`, `σ_T = m − Σ_{n∈N_T}v_n cos(τ log n)`; `A(F) = ∫Fe^{t/2}`, `B(F) = ∫Fe^{−t/2}`;
`P_T = {A = B = 0}`; `W_T(F) = 𝔞_T(F) + 2Re(A(F)\overline{B(F)})` (eq:W); `λ^A_T`, `λ^W_T` the bottoms.
`(S_uF)(t) = F(t−u)` on `L²(R)`, unitary. `Q_p(F) = −Σ_{k≥1, k log p<2T} v_{p^k} Re c_F(k log p)` (`cor:Qp`).
`Π_T = Σ_p Q_p`, `A_∞ = 2Re(AB̄) + ∫m dμ_F`, `W_T = A_∞ + Π_T` (P46 §arch, brief §1.9).

**1.2 Spectral measure and Kronecker marginals.** `dμ_F(τ) := |F̂(τ)|² dτ/2π`, a finite positive absolutely continuous
measure on `R` of mass `‖F‖²` (Plancherel), with
`c_F(u) = ∫_R e^{−iuτ} dμ_F(τ)` (Fourier inversion of `ĉ_F = |F̂|²`), so `∫cos(uτ)dμ_F = Re c_F(u)`.
For a prime `p` let `π_p: R → S¹ := R/2πZ`, `π_p(τ) = τ log p mod 2π`, and `ν_{F,p} := (π_p)_*μ_F`, a positive
measure on `S¹` of mass `‖F‖²`, absolutely continuous (`π_p` is a local diffeomorphism). Its Fourier coefficients are
`ν̂_{F,p}(k) := ∫_{S¹}e^{−ikθ}dν_{F,p}(θ) = ∫_R e^{−ikτ log p}dμ_F(τ) = c_F(k log p)`, `k ∈ Z`.  (1.1)
The **Kronecker torus** is `𝕋_Q := Hom(Q^×_{>0}, S¹) ≅ ∏_p S¹` (dual of the free abelian group on the primes), the
**Kronecker map** `j: R → 𝕋_Q`, `j(τ) = (n ↦ n^{iτ})`, i.e. `j(τ)_p = e^{iτ log p}`; `μ_{F,∞} := j_*μ_F`, whose Fourier
transform on `Q^×_{>0}` is `r ↦ c_F(log r)`, supported in the band `{r: |log r| < 2T}`. `ν_{F,p}` is the `p`-th
coordinate marginal of `μ_{F,∞}`.

**1.3 Poisson integrals.** For `z = re^{iφ}`, `0 ≤ r < 1`: `P(z,θ) = (1−r²)/|e^{iθ}−z|² = Σ_{k∈Z} r^{|k|}e^{ik(θ−φ)} > 0`;
`P_r(θ) := P(r,θ)`. For a finite positive measure `ν` on `S¹`, `u_ν(z) := ∫P(z,θ)dν(θ)` is a positive harmonic
function on the disc with `u_ν(0) = ν(S¹)`, and on the radius `z = r ∈ [0,1)`:
`u_ν(r) = ν(S¹) + 2Σ_{k≥1} r^k Re ν̂(k)`  (absolutely convergent, `|ν̂(k)| ≤ ν(S¹)`).  (1.2)
Write `u_{F,p} := u_{ν_{F,p}}` and `r_p := p^{−1/2}`. The **Euler defect** of `F` at `p` is
`𝔢_p(F) := u_{F,p}(0) − u_{F,p}(r_p)`.

**1.4 The `×p` maps.** `T_p: S¹ → S¹`, `θ ↦ pθ mod 2π` (equivalently `x ↦ px` on `R/Z`). `(T_p)_*ν` has Fourier
coefficients `((T_p)_*ν)^(k) = ν̂(pk)`. For `F ∈ L²(I_T)` and a prime `p`, the **contracted test** is
`F^{(p)}(t) := √p F(pt) ∈ L²(I_{T/p})`, `‖F^{(p)}‖ = ‖F‖`.
