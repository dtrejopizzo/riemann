## §1 Definitions (from the papers; file:line)

**1.1 The Weil distribution (P47 `lem:K` main.tex:363, `thm:A` 416, `eq:W` 139; the KEP pairing 1658).** For real
`u>0`, `K(u)=1/(2sinh(u/2))+1/(2cosh(u/2))`, positive, completely monotone, `K(u)=u^{−1}+O(1)` at `0`,
`K(u)=2e^{−u/2}(1+O(e^{−u}))` at `∞`; `C₀=ψ(¼)−log π=−5.3721834…`; `v_n=2Λ(n)/√n`; write `w(n):=Λ(n)/√n=v_n/2`.
For an even real `h∈C_c^∞(R)` put
```
𝒲(h) := C₀h(0) + ∫_0^∞ (h(0)−h(u))K(u)du − Σ_{n≥2} v_n h(log n) + 2∫_R h(u)cosh(u/2)du .
```
For a general real `h∈C_c^∞(R)` put `𝒲(h):=𝒲(h_ev)`, `h_ev(u)=½(h(u)+h(−u))`. For real `F∈C_c^∞(R)` with
autocorrelation `c_F(u)=∫F(t)F(t−u)dt` (even, compactly supported),
```
W(F) := 𝒲(c_F) = 𝔞_T(F) + 2A(F)B(F) = W_T(F)      for every T with supp F ⊂ I_T ,
```
by `thm:A` (`𝔞_T(F)=C₀‖F‖²+𝔈(c_F)−Σ_{n∈N_T}v_nc_F(log n)`, `𝔈(Φ)=∫_0^∞(Φ(0)−Φ(u))K(u)du`), the identity
`∫c_F(u)(e^{u/2}+e^{−u/2})du=2A(F)B(F)` (Fubini; `A(F)=∫Fe^{t/2}`, `B(F)=∫Fe^{−t/2}`), and the fact that contacts
with `log n≥2T` contribute `0` since `supp c_F⊂[−2T,2T]`. `W` is translation invariant: `c_{F(·−s)}=c_F`.
**Lemma 1.1 (PROVED).** `K(u) ≤ u^{−1}+½` for `u>0`. *Proof.* `1/(2sinh y)≤1/(2y)` and `1/(2cosh y)≤½`. ∎

**1.2 Away from the identity.** For `t≠0` put `D₀(t):=2cosh(t/2)−½K(|t|)`. For even `h` supported in `R∖{0}`,

## §2 The extremal coupling: `eq:W` as a finite-genus trace datum on `C[Q_{>0}]`

**Theorem 2.1 (PROVED equivalence; the statement is EQUIV). [compl]**
* (a) `RH ⟺ M_N(ε)⪰0` for all `N≥1` and all `ε∈(0,ε₀)`. The right side may be restricted to a single sequence
  `ε_j→0`.
* (b) For every `T>0`: `λ^W_T≥0 ⟺` for every `ε∈(0,ε₀)` and every finite `X⊂Q_{>0}` with `diam(log X)<2(T−ε)`,
  `G_X(ε)⪰0`. Again a single sequence `ε_j→0` suffices on the right.

*Proof.* (b, ⟹). Let `X`, `a` be as stated. `F_a` is supported in an interval of length `<2T`; translate it into
`I_T` (`W` is translation invariant, §1.1). Then `a^TG_X(ε)a=W(F_a)=W_T(F_a(·−s))≥λ^W_T‖F_a‖²≥0`.

(b, ⟸). Fix `T'<T` and `F` in the form domain of `W_{T'}` (`F∈L²(I_{T'})`, `∫log(2+|τ|)|F̂|²<∞`). For
`ε<min(ε₀,T−T')`, `G:=F∗ψ^{(ε)}` is `C_c^∞` with support in `I_{T'+ε}`. Partition `[−T',T']` into intervals `J_j` of
length `δ`, pick `s_j∈J_j∩log Q_{>0}` (dense), and put `R_δ:=Σ_j(ε^{−1/2}∫_{J_j}F)ψ_ε(·−s_j)`. Since
`s↦ψ^{(ε)}(·−s)` is uniformly continuous from `R` into every `C^k`, and `F∈L¹`,
`‖G−R_δ‖_{C^k}≤‖F‖₁ sup_{|s−s'|≤δ}‖ψ^{(ε)}(·−s)−ψ^{(ε)}(·−s')‖_{C^k}→0` as `δ→0`, all supports in one fixed
`I_{T'+ε}`. `W` is continuous on `C_c^∞(I_{T'+ε})` in the `C^k` topology (`|f̂(τ)|≤C_k‖f‖_{C^k}(1+|τ|)^{−k}` and
`|σ(τ)|≤C log(2+|τ|)+A_{T'+ε}`). `R_δ` is a smeared Frobenius vector on `X_δ={e^{s_j}}` with
`diam(log X_δ)≤2T'<2(T−ε)`, so `W(R_δ)≥0` by hypothesis, hence `W(G)≥0`. Now let `ε→0`: with `T'':=T'+ε₀` fixed,
`W(G)=(2π)^{−1}∫σ_{T''}(τ)|F̂(τ)|²|ψ̂(ετ)|²dτ+2A(G)B(G)` (contacts with `log n≥2(T'+ε)` contribute `0`, so the
larger contact set `N_{T''}` is harmless), `|ψ̂(ετ)|²≤‖ψ‖₁²`, `σ_{T''}|F̂|²∈L¹`, and `A(G)=A(F)∫ψ^{(ε)}(s)e^{s/2}ds→A(F)∫ψ`,
likewise `B`. Dominated convergence gives `W(G)→(∫ψ)²W_{T'}(F)`. So `W_{T'}≥0` on its form domain for every `T'<T`,
i.e. `λ^W_{T'}≥0`, and `λ^W_T=lim_{T'↑T}λ^W_{T'}≥0` by `lem:contT` (P47 main.tex:504, PROVED).

(a). `RH ⟺ ∀T: λ^W_T≥0` (Weil; P47 §1). Every finite `X⊂Q_{>0}` has a common denominator, so `G_X(ε)` is a principal
submatrix of some `M_N(ε)`; conversely `M_N(ε)=G_{\{1,…,N\}}(ε)`. Apply (b) for all `T`. ∎

**Remark 2.1.1 (what is and is not new; R-1).** Theorem 2.1 is Weil's criterion, reparametrised. The record's
closest statements are Paper 42's operator `A_T` (compression of the multiplier to `I_T`) and G2_B1_R5 Lemma R / Prop P
(resonator subspaces `ℛ_M`). What is new is the *reading*: for fixed `ε`, `φ_ε` is a trace datum on the discrete
Frobenius correspondence algebra `A=C[Q_{>0}]` of G1_B7_R4 §2, of **finite genus** `φ_ε(1)=log(1/ε)+c_ψ+O(ε)`
(Theorem 2.2(i)), carrying the **exact prime atoms** (2.2(ii)), and with mixed values that are **not free** but forced
by the archimedean place (2.2(iii)). G1_B7_R4 Theorem A says an orbit-faithful datum (mixed values `0`) has infinite
genus; Theorem B says finite genus is attained only by Künneth data with invented positive mixed values; Corollary M

**Theorem 2.2 (structure of the coupling; PROVED). [compl]** Let `0<ε<ε₀` and `x∈Q_{>0}`, `u:=log x`.
* (i) *Genus.* `φ_ε(1)=𝔤(ε):=C₀+∫_0^∞(1−g_ε(t))K(t)dt+2∫g_ε(t)cosh(t/2)dt = log(1/ε)+c_ψ+O(ε)`, with
  `c_ψ=C₀+∫_0^1(1−g_1(s))s^{−1}ds+∫_0^1(K(t)−t^{−1})dt+∫_1^∞K(t)dt`. (Uncertified numerics, not used:
  `∫_0^1(K−1/t)≈0.4599`, `∫_1^∞K≈2.4973`, so `c_ψ≈−2.415+∫_0^1(1−g_1)/s`.)
* (ii) *Atoms.* If `x=r` is a prime power and `|log r'−log r|>2ε` for every other prime power `r'`, then
  `φ_ε(r)=−w(r)+(g_ε∗D₀)(log r)`, and `|(g_ε∗D₀)(u)|≤ε·max_{|t−u|≤ε}|D₀(t)|`.
* (iii) *Mixed values.* If `|u|>ε` then `φ_ε(x)=(g_ε∗D₀)(u)−𝔠_ε(u)`, where the **blurred comb** is
  `𝔠_ε(u):=Σ_{n≥2}w(n)[g_ε(u−log n)+g_ε(u+log n)]`. If moreover no prime power lies within `e^{±ε}` of `x^{±1}`,
  then `φ_ε(x)=(g_ε∗D₀)(u)=ε(∫ψ)²D₀(u)(1+o_{u}(1))` as `ε→0`. `D₀` is strictly increasing on `(0,∞)` from `−∞` to
  `+∞`, with a unique zero `u₀` (uncertified: `u₀∈(0.28,0.29)`); so the mixed Lefschetz numbers of the coupling are
  **negative** at separation `|log(x)|<u₀` and **positive** beyond.
* (iv) *Not Künneth.* For distinct primes `p,q` and `ε` small, `φ_ε(pq)φ_ε(1)≠φ_ε(p)φ_ε(q)`; the restriction of `φ_ε`
  to `⟨p,q⟩` is not a tensor product of positive-definite functions on `p^Z` and `q^Z`; and, unlike every Künneth datum
  of G1_B7_R4 Thm B(ii) (all mixed values `>0`), `φ_ε(p/q)<0` whenever `|log(p/q)|<u₀` (e.g. `p=11,q=13`).

*Proof.* (i) For `ε<ε₀` no `log n` lies in `[−ε,ε]`, so the comb term in (1.2) vanishes at `x=1`, and
`g_ε(·)_{ev}=g_ε`. Split `∫_0^∞(1−g_ε)K=∫_0^ε(1−g_1(t/ε))K(t)dt+∫_ε^∞K`. With `K=t^{−1}+K_0`, `K_0` bounded on
`(0,1]` (`lem:K`): the first integral is `∫_0^1(1−g_1(s))s^{−1}ds+O(ε)`, the second is

## §5 Below the decoupling scale: the Z-matrix structure of the truncated Gram

The complete form on resonators is `M_N(ε)=Z_N(ε)+P_N(ε)`, where `Z_N(ε):=[𝔞(ψ_ε(·−log m),ψ_ε(·−log n))]` is the

**Lemma 5.0 (PROVED).** `P_N(ε)=κ_ε²(uv^T+vu^T)` with `u=(n^{1/2})_{n≤N}`, `v=(n^{−1/2})_{n≤N}`,

*Proof.* `A(ψ_ε(·−log n))=n^{1/2}κ_ε`, `B(ψ_ε(·−log n))=n^{−1/2}κ_ε` (`ψ` even). ∎

  matrix `ρ(B)=min_{v>0}max_m(Bv)_m/v_m`, CITED (std.)). The bottom eigenvalue of `Z_N(ε)` is simple, with a positive
  eigenvector.
