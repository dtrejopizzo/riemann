## §0 Verdict

**No sign is claimed at any window, and no label of row D moves. Claim level: PARTIAL_NEW.**

The route asked what the bilinear machinery (Vaughan, Heath-Brown, Linnik, Type I/II sums with coupled ranges) can
do against `W_T` when only exact values of `Λ` are used. I built the operator calculus in which this question has a
precise meaning, and proved five structural theorems. They give three conclusions.

1. **Every Dirichlet-ring decomposition of the comb is an identity between compressions of Fourier multipliers
   on `L²(I_T)` (Theorem 1).** A Type II piece is `∫A_αA_β dμ_F`, where `μ_F=|F̂|²dτ/2π ≥ 0` and `A_α, A_β` are
   the two Dirichlet polynomials on the critical line (Theorem 5). The coupled range `mk<e^{2T}`, which Rem 27.15
   calls "the entire method", costs nothing here: it is the support of `c_F`. Bilinear cancellation against
   `W_T` can therefore only mean that `A_α` and `\bar A_β` are misaligned in `L²(μ_F)`. Added to the Type I part,
   this is the certificate problem, which is EQUIV (Prop. 5.2). The Friedlander–Iwaniec and Heath-Brown kernels
   carry a signed arithmetic spectral weight coming from `ℤ[i]` or `ℤ[∛2]`. No Weil form of `ζ` contains such a
   weight, since in `W_T` the weight is `|F̂|²` (Prop. 5.3). This answers the second half of P47 (42) *for Weil
   forms*: the door is not negative by an obstruction, but it is not inside the form.
2. **The window zeta operator.** `ζ_T := P_T(Σ_m m^{−½}S_{log m})P_T` is bounded and unipotent, and its
   nilpotent part has index `⌈2T/log2⌉`. It equals the compression of the unbounded critical-line multiplier
   `ζ(½−iD)` plus a rank-one polar operator (Theorem 2). Its powers are the compressions of the powers, up to an
   explicit operator of rank at most `j` (Theorem 3). So the truncated Toeplitz operators with symbol `ζ(½−iτ)^j`
   are bounded and multiplicative modulo finite rank. Linnik and Heath-Brown in the window then give the
   completion of G2_B1_R2 Prop 1′ at `z=1` (Cor. 3.1, credited).
3. **The window phase identity (Theorem 4).** `𝔞_T(F)=⟨F,i[t,Θ_T]F⟩` and `W_T=⟨·,i[t,Θ_T]·⟩+2Re(A\bar B)` hold
   exactly, where `Θ_T:=P_T·2θ(D)·P_T−2 Im log ζ_T` and `θ` is the Riemann–Siegel theta function. The formal
   symbol of `Θ_T` is `2π(N(τ)−1)`, a counting function. The truncated form is the derivative of the window phase
   along the vertical twist `n^{iε}`, which the archimedean frequency shift compensates exactly (Cor. 4.1). The
   ordinate-counting part of `W_T` is always `≥0`, and the sign sits in a depth defect `Δ` (Prop. 4.2). RH is
   equivalent to a positive-commutator estimate for every `T` (EQUIV).

**Why no construction is within reach on this route, stated rather than hidden.** Three results close off the
remaining options. Ring identities alone are twist-covariant (G2_B3_R3 Thm S(i)). Any all-`T` Hermitian square is
RH (G2_B3_R3 Thm K). Theorem 5 shows that bilinear structure adds nothing that is not already in the symbol. The
ingredients this memo isolates are Euler (E), Poisson (P) and functional equation (FE) (Prop. 6). They are exactly
Hamburger's datum `𝓗_FE` (G2_B3_R2 Thm H), which has no falsifier and yields no inequality. The sign is the
positive-commutator statement (O1), and (O1) is E1/E2. §8 records where the Friedlander–Iwaniec-type input would
have to live instead: on a carrier with additive structure. That is G1, and the adèle class space is wall O-12.

## §1 Definitions

**D1 (window).** `T>0`, `I_T=(−T,T)`, `X:=e^{2T}`. `L²(I_T)⊂L²(ℝ)` by extension by zero, and `P_T` is the orthogonal
projection onto it. `⟨F,G⟩=∫F\bar G`. `(S_uF)(t)=F(t−u)`, `(JF)(t)=F(−t)`, and `t` is multiplication by `t`,
bounded on `L²(I_T)`. `c_F(u)=⟨F,S_uF⟩=(2π)^{-1}∫|F̂(τ)|²e^{−iτu}dτ`, and `μ_F:=|F̂|²dτ/2π`. The Tate vectors are
`e_±(t)=e^{±t/2}1_{I_T}(t)`, so `A(F)=⟨F,e_+⟩` and `B(F)=⟨F,e_−⟩`. The higher moments are
`A_k(F)=∫F t^ke^{t/2}` and `B_k(F)=∫F t^ke^{−t/2}`.

**D2 (Dirichlet ring).** `𝔇` is the ring of functions `ℕ_{≥1}→ℂ` under `*`, with unit `δ=1_{n=1}` and `1` the
constant function. `(La)(n)=a(n)log n` is a derivation. `ℓ:=Λ/log` with `ℓ(1)=0`, `d_j:=1^{*j}` and
`t_j:=(1−δ)^{*j}`; `t_j` is supported on `n≥2^j`, with `t_j(2^j)=1`. `I_X:={a: a(n)=0 ∀n<X}` is an ideal
(`(a*b)(n)≠0` forces a divisor `≥X`), and `𝔇_X:=𝔇/I_X`.

**D3 (window operators).** For `a∈𝔇` and `F∈L²_{loc}(ℝ)` with `supp F⊂[c,∞)`, put
`(M_aF)(t):=Σ_{m≥1}a(m)m^{−½}F(t−log m)`, a finite sum for each `t`, and `𝐌_T(a):=P_TM_aP_T` on `L²(I_T)`. Further
`ζ_T:=𝐌_T(1)`, `N_T:=ζ_T−I=𝐌_T(1−δ)`, `S_p:=𝐌_T(δ_p)=p^{−½}P_TS_{log p}P_T`, `J_T:=⌈2T/log2⌉` and
`log ζ_T:=Σ_{1≤j<J_T}(−1)^{j−1}N_T^j/j`.

**D4 (multipliers).** For continuous `φ` of polynomial growth, `φ(D)` acts on `L²(ℝ)` by `(φ(D)G)^=φĜ`, with a
domain that contains `C_c^∞(ℝ)`. Define `𝒵:=ζ(½−iD)`. `θ` is the Riemann–Siegel theta function
`θ(τ)=Im log Γ(¼+iτ/2)−(τ/2)log π` (continuous branch, `θ(0)=0`, odd), and `X:=e^{2iθ(D)}` is unitary. Since
`θ′(τ)=½Re ψ(¼+iτ/2)−½log π`, we have **`m=2θ′`**.

**D5 (polar operator).** `(RG)(t):=e^{t/2}∫G(s)e^{−s/2}ds` for `G∈C_c^∞(ℝ)`. `R_T:=P_TRP_T=e_+⟨·,e_−⟩` has rank one,
and `‖R_T‖=‖e_+‖‖e_−‖=2sinh T`.

**D6.** `Z_T:=ζ_T−R_T`, the window zeta operator.

**D7 (phase operator, commutator form).** `Im Y:=(Y−Y^*)/2i`, and
`Θ_T:=P_T·2θ(D)·P_T−2 Im(log ζ_T)`, a symmetric operator on the domain `C_c^∞(I_T)`. For a symmetric `Θ` with
`C_c^∞(I_T)⊂dom Θ`, put `𝒞_Θ(F):=⟨F,i[t,Θ]F⟩:=2Im⟨tF,ΘF⟩`. For real `φ` one has `[t,φ(D)]=−iφ′(D)`, since
`(tG)^=−i∂_τĜ`, and hence `𝒞_{φ(D)}(F)=∫φ′dμ_F`.

**D8 (bilinear forms).** For real, finitely supported `α,β∈𝔇` put
`𝒯_{α,β}(F):=Σ_{m,k}α(m)β(k)(mk)^{−½}c_F(log mk)` and `A_α(τ):=Σ_mα(m)m^{−½−iτ}`.

**D9 (fixed-frequency tests).** For real `φ∈C_c^∞(−1,1)` and `γ∈ℝ`, put `F_{γ,T}(t):=e^{−iγt}φ(t/T)∈C_c^∞(I_T)`. Then
`F̂_{γ,T}(τ)=Tφ̂(T(τ−γ))` and `‖F_{γ,T}‖²=T‖φ‖²`.

## §2 The window Dirichlet calculus

**Theorem 1. PROVED.** Let `T>0` and `X=e^{2T}`.
1. `𝐌_T(a)` is bounded, with `‖𝐌_T(a)‖≤Σ_{m<X}|a(m)|m^{−½}`, and depends only on `a mod I_X`.
2. `𝐌_T(a*b)=𝐌_T(a)𝐌_T(b)` and `𝐌_T(δ)=I`. So `𝐌_T:𝔇_X→B(L²(I_T))` is a unital algebra homomorphism, and it is
   injective.
3. `[t,𝐌_T(a)]=𝐌_T(La)`.
4. `𝐌_T(a)^*=J𝐌_T(\bar a)J`.
5. `⟨F,𝐌_T(a)F⟩=Σ_{n<X}\bar a(n)n^{−½}c_F(log n)`. For real `a` this equals `∫D_a(½+iτ)dμ_F(τ)`, where
   `D_a(s)=Σ_{n<X}a(n)n^{−s}`; any finitely supported representative of `a mod I_X` gives the same value.
6. `N_T^j=𝐌_T(t_j)`, and `N_T^j=0 ⟺ 2^j≥X ⟺ j≥2T/log2`. `ζ_T` is invertible, with `ζ_T^{−1}=𝐌_T(μ)`.
   `log ζ_T=𝐌_T(ℓ)=−Σ_{p<X}log(I−S_p)` and `exp(log ζ_T)=ζ_T=∏_{p<X}(I−S_p)^{−1}`; the factors commute and each
   `S_p` is nilpotent.

*Proof.* (1) For `F∈L²(I_T)` and `t∈I_T`, a term `F(t−log m)≠0` forces `log m<t+T<2T`, i.e. `m<X`. So
`𝐌_T(a)=P_TΣ_{m<X}a(m)m^{−½}S_{log m}P_T`, and `‖S_u‖=1`.
(2) *Causality.* Let `F∈L²(I_T)` and `G:=M_bF`. Since `log m≥0`, `supp G⊂[−T,∞)`, so `G−P_TG=G1_{[T,∞)}`. The
function `M_a(G1_{[T,∞)})` is supported in `[T,∞)`, because every shift moves to the right, so it is killed by

## §8 Why a construction is not within reach here, and where the door leads

The goal was the full construction, audacity included. I state exactly what stops it.

1. The phase identity (Thm 4) contains every datum that the walls require: exact `Λ` through `𝐌_T`, the Euler
   product with root `1`, Poisson on `ℤ`, the functional equation with `ζ`'s own `X`, and the polar pair. The sign
   is then the **positive-commutator estimate** `i[t,Θ_T]≥0` on `P_T` for every `T`. That is E2, and by G2_B3_R3
   Thm K no structural rewriting of it (a square) is weaker than RH.
2. The bilinear machinery acts on this identity only through products of symbols against `μ_F≥0` (Thm 5,
   Prop 5.3). Whatever it produces is a presentation of `σ_T` modulo `{ν̂: supp ν⊂{|u|≥2T}}`, and the dual value
   `D_T=λ^prim_T` (`thm:D1`, main.tex:601) does not depend on the presentation. So bilinear decompositions cannot
   change the problem; they can only suggest certificates. The Vaughan representative is not one, since its symbol
   is unbounded below (S-2).
3. The one input that is neither twist-covariant nor presentation-level is the additive one: Poisson and FE, (P)
   and (FE). Inside `W_T` it enters only linearly, through `𝒵=X𝒵^*`. Prop G of G2_B3_R2 shows that squares
   linear in `ζ` are RH-blind. The Friedlander–Iwaniec kind of input, an arithmetic-not-metric bilinear
   coupling, needs a space on which the additive structure of the ring acts together with the multiplicative one,

   the adèle class space, is wall O-12 (R158_002, R158_004): a restatement unless the carrier is a different one.
