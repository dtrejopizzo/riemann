## 2. A further defect, found while repairing H7, and repaired

The audit's H7 asks that `(P139)` be stated over the class `𝒵` of all `Z` satisfying A1–A6 + A2⁺, so
that (AT-1) and (AT-2) denote something. Stated naively — *construct `h⁰_Z` satisfying (a),(b′),(c)
for every `Z ∈ 𝒵`* — the problem is **unsatisfiable**, and provably so: `Ξ_a ∈ 𝒵`, and Paper 46
Theorem 12.4 (`main.md:2945`) proves that **no** `h⁰_{Ξ_a}` with (a),(b′),(c) exists. `(P139)` would
then be a construction problem with no solutions, which is not a deliverable.

The repair, in the statement: the rule `Z ↦ h⁰_Z` must be **defined** on all of `𝒵` — that is what
makes "read the construction at `Z = Ξ_Q`" meaningful — while **(a),(b′),(c) are demanded only for
`Z = ξ_K`**. The paragraph "Why the requirement is split" states this and draws the consequence that
the mathematical content of the acceptance tests is **clause (N)**: not that the construction fails at
`Ξ_Q` and `Ξ_a` (it must, and that is a theorem — Theorem D.19.5 and Paper 46 Theorem 12.4 — not a
test), but that its author can **point at the line where it fails**. (AT-1)'s wording is corrected
accordingly: it no longer says "must fail" but "**does** fail …; what the test requires is that the
author name the line".
