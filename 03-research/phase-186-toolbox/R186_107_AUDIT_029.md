## 3. Question 5: what kind of tool remains, and proposed front

If Lemma H is taken in its one true sense (every lower bound of λ_A is ≤ λ_A), what distinguishes a route is
**how it is verified**: (A) exact identity (zero remainder = dual certificate; Paper 47 already has the format and thm:Z
identifies it with the explicit formula on flat-top tests); (B) structure of the minimizer (nonlinear in F; (★_cell)/(TC),
with (G),(N) of R166_501); (C) continuity arguments in T (lem:contA, lemma C of R157_004; inherit (N′) from R166_802).
Object defined in the corpus: only (A) (def:PD, thm:C, thm:log5A/W, prop:polarcap) and part (C) (PROVED continuity
of λ_A and λ_W). (B) has had no object since phase 169.

**Recommended front for round 15 (differs from 029):** not (d). Two options with an explicit inequality:

1. **Certificate of the complete form with exact β₀ = 2 as a structural, not numerical, object.** Statement: for
 T ∈ (½log2, ½log5], exhibit w ∈ 𝒲_cl (def. eq:nuw) such that f_w ≥ 0 is proved by an identity (thm:Z(i): f_w is the
 explicit formula in h_τ = w cos τu) and not by evaluation. What exists: thm:log5W verifies it by balls; what is missing: the
 structural reason. Falsifier: if for every w ∈ 𝒲_cl^reg the minimum of f_w is attained at a τ where the prime part
 −Σ v_n w(log n) cos(τ log n) is the only negative one and there is no reorganization into squares with O(1) coefficients (test: the
 Gram matrix of {cos(τ log n)}_{n ≤ e^{2T}} against the measure of m is not diagonally dominant), then the certificate is
 intrinsically numerical and the front closes as a structural NO-GO.
2. **Theorem T₁ of 029 (reserve (b)) promoted to a front**: D via the semilocal mechanism on [½log2, T₁] with the boundary
 bound |c_h(a)| ≤ B_T‖h‖²(2T − |a|)^{1/2}. It is the only proposal in 029 whose positivity inequality is written out.
 Falsifier: the one from 029 (B_T not uniform, or ‖κ_S‖_{L¹} > 0.5 at T = 0.36).
