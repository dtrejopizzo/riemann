### 2.2 Explanation (PROVED, two-line algebra): parity + Sherman–Morrison

Let W_T = 𝔞_T + 2AB (eq:W of Paper 47), A = t₊, B = t₋. The form 𝔞_T and W_T commute with the reflection x ↦ −x (K(|u|), 𝒞 symmetric, and the reflection swaps t₊ ↔ t₋). With u_e := (t₊ + t₋)/√2 (functional ∫F·√2 cosh(x/2), zero on odd functions) and u_o := (t₊ − t₋)/√2 (zero on even functions):

 t₊t₋ᵀ + t₋t₊ᵀ = u_e u_eᵀ − u_o u_oᵀ, hence 𝔞_e = W_e − u_e u_eᵀ, 𝔞_o = W_o + u_o u_oᵀ. (★)

That is: **the pole term is negative rank one on the even sector and positive rank one on the odd sector.** Immediate consequences:

(a) If W_T ≻ 0 on L²(I_T), then n₋(𝔞_o) = 0 and n₋(𝔞_e) = [w_e > 1] with w_e := u_eᵀW_e⁻¹u_e (determinant lemma). Since 𝔞_T(e^{x/2}1_I) < 0 (PROVED in the memo) and 𝔞_T(e^{x/2}1_I) = 𝔞_e(even part) + 𝔞_o(odd part) with 𝔞_o ⪰ 0, the even part is negative, hence w_e > 1 and **n₋(𝔞_T) = 1 exactly**. No numerics needed.
(b) Conversely n₋(𝔞_T) = 1 implies nothing about D on the window: n₋(𝔞_T|P_T) ≤ 1 only (interlacing), and D on the window (𝔞_T|P_T ⪰ 0) only gives n₋(𝔞_T) ∈ {0,1,2}. **n₋ = 1 is neither necessary nor sufficient for D on the window.**
(c) What "compensates": nothing mysterious. The complete archimedean form A_∞ = Γ_T + 2AB (prop:archneg) has n₋ = 2 (one even direction and one odd direction) from T ≈ 0.37 (odd) / 0.41 (even) onward in my Galerkin (λ_min(A_∞) = −0.21, −0.43, −0.71, −0.94 at 0.45, ½log3, 0.69, ½log5; the threshold 0.4608 of prop:archneg is the certificate, not the crossing), and by (★) that yields n₋(Γ_T) = 2–3. With primes, W_T ⪰ 0 (NUMERICAL, 10⁻¹³) and (★) forces n₋(𝔞_T) = 1. **The primes do not "reduce the inertia of 𝔞_T" by any mechanism intrinsic to 𝔞_T: they lift both negative directions (even and odd) of A_∞ in 0.2–0.9, i.e. they make W_T ⪰ 0 on the whole L²(I_T).** The memo observes this but attributes it to the poleless form; it is the wrong object.
(d) For T ≤ ½log2 (no contacts): W_T = A_∞ ⪰ 0 on L²(I_T) is the Connes–Consani theorem (CITED, cc Thm 6.11, as in R185_008 §4), hence n₋(Γ_T) = 1 is **PROVED** there by (a). For ½log2 < T ≤ ½log5, n₋(𝔞_T) = 1 is **CONDITIONAL on W_T ≻ 0 on L²(I_T)** (NUMERICAL on my mesh; undecidable in double precision from T ≈ 0.78 onward, λ_min(W_T) ≈ ±10⁻¹³). thm:C of Paper 47 has β₀ = 2.0026 ≠ 2 and thm:log5A is about P_T: neither certifies W_T ≥ c on the whole L²(I_T) (remark after eq:W: exact β₀ = 2 is required). **Honest new datum:** λ_min(W_T on L²) ≪ λ_A (7.6e-3 vs 0.69 at T = 0.3; 5.5e-8 vs 9.5e-4 at ½log3; 1e-12 vs 1.3e-7 at 0.69): the complete form on the whole L² is much closer to being singular than the primitive form, on the even sector. This is NOT in the memo and is the only new quantitative information in all of §2.3.

## 3. Lemma 2.4 (Haynsworth) and the scalar criterion

Correct algebra: with rank T_k = 2, In([[G,T_kᵀ],[T_k,0]]) = In(G) + In(−S), and n₋(bordered) = 2 + n₋(G|ker T_k) (Finsler/Maddocks). Hence n₋(G|E_k) = n₋(G) + n₊(S) − 2, and with n₋(G) = 1: G|E_k ⪰ 0 ⟺ n₊(S) = 1; G|E_k ≻ 0 ⟺ In(S) = (1,1,0) ⟺ det S < 0. PROVED. But:
- In the even/odd basis, S is **diagonal** (|S_eo| ≤ 10⁻¹⁵ in my Galerkin): S = diag(σ_e, σ_o) with σ_e = u_eᵀ𝔞_e⁻¹u_e, σ_o = u_oᵀ𝔞_o⁻¹u_o, and by (★) and Sherman–Morrison **σ_e = w_e/(1 − w_e), σ_o = w_o/(1 + w_o)**, w_• = u_•ᵀW_•⁻¹u_•. Verified to all digits (table in out026c.txt). The "scalar criterion" det S < 0 is therefore σ_eσ_o < 0, i.e. (given W_T ≻ 0) w_e > 1 and w_o > 0: a rewriting of W_T ≻ 0 plus the negative direction already proved. **It has no constructive content**: if W_T ≻ 0 on L² is known, G|E_k = W_T|E_k ≻ 0 trivially (the pole cancels on E_k).
- If G_k is singular: the lemma requires G invertible; the correct and free alternative is the inertia of the bordered matrix (no inverse). Numerically cond(G_k) = 10⁴–10⁶ and grows with k because λ₂(G_k) ↓ ≈ λ_A (3·10⁻¹² at ½log5): det(T_kG_k⁻¹T_kᵀ) in double precision is useless exactly where it matters. MAJOR as a tool, not as algebra.
- Dependence on normalization: scaling the Tate rows (s₊, s₋) multiplies det S by (s₊s₋)² and moves the eigenvalues (−1.000 → −2.018, −3.071, −0.250 with (2,1), (1,3), (½,½)); the "−1" is specific to t_± = M c_±, i.e. to the functionals A, B of Paper 47 as they stand.

## 4. The eigenvalue −1.000: explained, not OPEN

σ_e = w_e/(1 − w_e) = −1 − 1/(w_e − 1). With w_e = 136 (T = 0.3), 8.3·10², 7.6·10⁴, 2.3·10⁷, 1.5·10¹² (½log2, 0.45, ½log3, 0.69): σ_e = −1.007387, −1.001201, −1.000013, −1.000000, −1.000000, exactly as the formula predicts (and as the memo reports: −1.0004 at T = 0.36, k = 3; −1.00001 at ½log5, k = 13). **The −1 is the limit w_e → ∞**, i.e. W_e nearly singular with nonzero Tate component (λ_min(W_e) ≪ λ_A, §2.2(d)); the "converges to −1 as k grows" is just w_e growing with k toward the operator's value. The positive eigenvalue σ_o = w_o/(1 + w_o) ↑ 1 for the same reason on the odd sector (w_o = 0.035, 0.16, 7.6, 1.5·10³, 4·10⁷). The lead was correct in spirit (it is the form on span{e^{±x/2}1_I} via the pole) and the exact answer is (★): it is not a normalization artifact, it is an identity. **As a "potential falsifier for future identities" (memo §2.4) it does not work: it is itself an identity.**

Note: ‖G c₊ + t₋‖/‖t₋‖ = 1.15: there is no identity of the type 𝔞_T(e^{−x/2}1_I, ·) = −t₊ (ruled out).

## 8. Does n₋ = 1 deserve to be the next constructive target?

**No.** (i) It is not an artifact: robust across two bases and three dimensions. (ii) But it is a consequence of W_T ≻ 0 on L²(I_T) via (★), and it does not imply D on the window (only n₋(𝔞_T|P_T) ≤ 1). (iii) The object that does emerge is W_T on the **whole** L²(I_T), with margin λ_min(W_T) ≪ λ_A and concentrated on the even sector: if one wants a target derived from this memo, it is "W_T ≥ c on L²(I_T) with exact β₀ = 2" (certificate (ν, 2, 0) of Paper 47, remark after eq:W), which Paper 47 does not have for any T > ½log2 (thm:C uses β₀ = 2.0026). That is a legitimate constructive target, stronger per window than D; the memo does not formulate it. What remains of the memo for Paper 47: Lemmas 1.1–1.4 (closed form, with convention C1 verified) and Thm 3.3 corrected, as an exact upper-bound lemma for λ_A.
