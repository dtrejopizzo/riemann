## 1. Attack 1: what is N(T*) in Zhu? Reproduction and interpretation

**1.1 Literal definition (CITED, local text).** Abstract l. 43: "with N the zero-counting function"; §1.1 l. 68: "γ₁ ≤ γ₂ ≤ … the ordinates of the non-trivial zeros"; §1.3 l. 221: "Writing N(T) for the number of zeros of height at most T"; §12 l. 1427: Riemann–von Mangoldt with Backlund. **N(T*) is a count of zeros of ζ up to height T* = 2πe^{2L}**; it is not a Landau–Widom count of the negative set nor a node count. Mechanism (Zhu §9, l. 984–1000): T* is the height at which the zero density (1/2π)log(t/2π) equals the budget L/π of real zeros of a type-L function; the minimizer "interpolates" the zeros γ ≤ T* (|f̂(γ)| ≈ √λ*) and pays at the transition at T* (Fig. 4). Zhu §14.2 l. 1719: "the window floor being twice the squared smallest singular value of a low-zero sampling operator." The memo's claim in §1.1 is correct as a citation.

Result (M = 40): the nearly balanced directions are exactly those that annihilate F̂ at the low zeros. Same at log 2 and 0.75. That is: by lem:roughEF (𝔞_T(F) = Σ_ρ Φ_F(ρ)Φ_F(1−ρ), unconditional) **the Neg ≈ Pos cancellation in a direction is the vanishing of F̂ at the low zeros**, and the count of nearly balanced directions is the dimension of the subspace of 𝒫_T where the sampling operator F ↦ (F̂(γ_k))_{γ_k ≤ T*} is small. Dimension count (Zhu §9): degrees of freedom of type T below height H ≈ 2TH/π, constraints N(H); the maximum of 2TH/π − N(H) is at H₀ = T*/e and equals ≈ e^{2T−1}(2T + 2) ≍ N(T*): the count is ≍ N(T*) with a constant not determined by this argument, which explains the threshold dependence. **Answers:** (a) "does each direction ↔ a zero?" Not literally: the head block is the near-kernel of the sampling at the N(T*) low zeros; its eigenvalues 1 − β_j are (up to Pos(·) = O(1)) the squared singular values of that sampling over 𝒫_T, exactly the object of Zhu §14.2. (b) Consistent with thm:Z? thm:Z is the dual statement (certificate f_w = Σ ŵ(τ − γ)); the relevant primal is lem:roughEF, and yes: it is the explicit formula restricted to the block. (c) **Falsifier T = 0.9, 1.0:** N(T*) = 6 and 8. Zhu measures −ln λ*(1.0) = 67 (l. 1402: and the law 2π²N/ln N gives 76); if the staircase keeps steps of 4–6, the count at 10⁻³ at T = 1 would be ≈ (−log(1−β₁) − 7)/5 ≈ 12–14 ≈ 2N(T*) − 2 if −log(1−β₁) ≈ −log λ_W − O(T), or ≈ 8 under Lemma H. **Lemma H "K ≤ N(T*) + O(1)" and Conj. 12.1 with constant 2π² are incompatible with steps bounded by 6**: the rate dN(T*)/dT = 4Te^{2T} (≈ 16 per unit T at 0.8, matching the observed rate of entry of directions ≈ 1/0.06) grows as half the rate 2π²(dN/dT)/log N divided by π²/log N_Sh. The falsifier is, then, sharp: at T = 1, a count ≈ 8 refutes Zhu's constant 2π² for λ_A (or forces steps ≈ 10), a count ≈ 14 refutes Lemma H. Multiple precision is mandatory (λ_A ~ 10⁻³³; at T = 0.90 my M = 40 gives λ_A = −3.4·10⁻¹⁴: the float64 floor, Zhu's §10 false negative).

## 2. Attack 2: Lemma 2.1 (prolate tail)

(i)–(ii) PROVED (Neg ≤ s_-S_T as forms, min–max, Sylvester). With that, the **crude form** is PROVED: β_j < 1 for j > J_crude(T) := #{ν_j(Neg_T) > c_P} (R186_034 §3.3; my values 1,1,4,4,5,7,10,12,14 across 0.36 … 0.90). What fails:
(a) **LW requires fixed α and fixed sets (one dilated).** Here α = c_P/s_- depends on T (c_P = 0.64, 0.28, 0.22, 0.25, 0.24, 0.19, 0.18 and s_- = 6.4 → 9.8) and the set {σ_T < 0} changes shape and number of components at each contact (prop:band: finite union, ≤ 8 components at ½log5, more at each prime). No statement of LW 1980 (CITED-NOT-VERIFIED in `PROLATE-REFERENCES.md`, noted only for intervals) nor of Israel 2015 (general sets, dilation asymptotics) covers a set that varies with the parameter; the o(log N_Sh) is not uniform. (iii) is CONDITIONAL, not CITED.
(b) **(iv) contradicts §2.3(a):** "1 − λ₀(S_T) ≍ exp(−π²N_Sh/log N_Sh)" is the LW transition formula extrapolated to j = 0 (plateau), which the memo itself declares with no controlled relative error. For an interval the plateau asymptotic is Fuchs/Widom 1 − λ₀ ~ C c^{1/2}e^{−2c}, c = πN_Sh/2, i.e. e^{−πN_Sh}; with it log(s_-/c_P) ≲ πN_Sh and **J ≤ N_Sh + (log N_Sh/π²)·πN_Sh = N_Sh(1 + log N_Sh/π)**, not 2N_Sh + O(log). For unions there is no plateau asymptotic (R183_031, and the memo §2.3(b)). The "constant 2" is unjustified; correct label: **tail PROVED with computable J_crude(T); "J ≤ 2N_Sh + O(log N_Sh)" ARGUED.**
(c) "min_off σ_+" in (iv) is 0 (σ_+ vanishes at the endpoints of each component); the correct version is c_δ(1 − λ₀(S_{neg+δ})) with the multi-interval δ-neighborhood, whose λ₀ has no cited asymptotic.
(d) "BL ⟺ statement about the head block" is tautological: BL speaks of β₁, which is in every head block by definition. The reduction removes nothing from BL; its only content is that the near-kernel has finite dimension per T, which R186_034 §3.3 already gave.

## 4. Attack 4: N_Sh ≍ T²e^{2T²} versus N(T*) ≍ Te^{2T}

Definition R186_034: N_Sh = 2T|{σ_T < 0}|/π; the script uses 2T·2·med/(2π) = identical. Reproduced values: 1.58, 1.97, 2.41, 4.59, 5.13, 6.57, 7.05 (and 12.57 at 0.85, 13.31 at 0.90). The memo's Gaussian heuristic: variance ½Σv_n² ≈ 4T², P(comb > x) ≈ e^{−x²/8T²}, med ≈ 2π∫e^xe^{−x²/8T²}dx ≍ Te^{2T²} (maximum at x = 4T² = 2T standard deviations): the arithmetic is correct, the Gaussian approximation at 2T standard deviations with π(e^{2T}) non-independent terms is ARGUED, and **the data do not show it**: N_Sh/(Te^{2T}) = 2.1, 1.8, 1.5, 2.1, 1.9, 2.0, 1.8 (constant ≈ 2) while N_Sh/(T²e^{2T²}) = 9.4, 6.5, 4.4, 5.5, 4.1, 3.8, 3.0 (decreasing). In this range, |{σ_T < 0}| ≈ T*/2 = πe^{2T} (6.45, 9.4, 15.7 against 6.89, 11.64, 13.75): below T* the threshold m(τ) < 2T is less than one standard deviation of the comb and a fixed fraction of [0, T*] is negative; the Gaussian tail beyond T* only dominates when e^{2T²} ≫ e^{2T}, i.e. T ≳ 1.5. Conclusion: "N_Sh ≫ N(T*)" is ARGUED for large T and **false as a description of the computed range**, where N_Sh ≈ 2N(T*) + O(1). This invalidates the "two separate scales" reading of §2.4: with log N_Sh ≈ log N(T*) + log 2 there is no observable separation, and the "slope π²/log N_Sh" (5.05–6.04) is indistinguishable from π²/2 = 4.9 or from any constant ≈ 5 (measured steps 3.1–5.9; at T = 0.85, where N_Sh jumps to 12.6 and π²/log N_Sh = 3.9, the steps are 4.2–6.6 at M = 40 and 5.0–6.3 at M = 60: they do not go down, but there the Galerkin has not converged). The dismissal of L_Sh as the bilateral law survives only by (iii) of §3, not by the rate.

**Opinion (question 5).** The head block with the correspondence directions ↔ N(T*) **is not a new object: it is the explicit formula seen from the pencil.** My evaluation at the zeros (§1.3) shows this unambiguously: the directions with 1 − β_j ≪ 1 are the F ∈ 𝒫_T that annihilate F̂ at γ₁ … γ_{N(T*)}, and 1 − β_j are (up to Pos = O(1)) the squared singular values of the sampling of PW_T at the low zeros, which is literally Zhu's "low-zero sampling operator" §14.2 and, in the corpus, the primal reading of thm:Z / lem:roughEF. What the pencil adds to the explicit formula is the **Pos normalization**, which is O(1) and does not change the count. It therefore does not deserve a round of its own as an object; it does deserve a single multiple-precision run at T = 1 (§1.3(c)), because it decides between "K ≈ N(T*)" (Lemma H) and "K ≈ 2N(T*)" (Zhu's constant 2π² with bounded steps), and that number **is** new information about the law of λ_A that neither Zhu (who measures λ_W) nor the corpus has. That experiment is NUMERICAL by construction and does not touch the sign; its value is to fix the shape of BL before trying to prove it.
