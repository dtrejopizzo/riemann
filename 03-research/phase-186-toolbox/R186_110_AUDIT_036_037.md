### 2.5 Lemma D (net proof, reproduced). PROVED
Let w ∈ cl(𝓡 + C̄): a net r_α + c_α → w in ℝ^𝔉, r_α ∈ 𝓡, c_α ∈ C̄ ⊂ [0,∞)^𝔉. In [0,∞]^𝔉 (Tychonoff) a subnet c_β → c ∈
