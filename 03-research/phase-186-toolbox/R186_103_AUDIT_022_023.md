## 2. Contradictions 022 / 023 / 019

- **022 "peak ≈ 4/T" vs 023 "s·T not constant".** Different objects (s₀ = min Son_∞; h* = min E_S; h_a = min A_∞) with overlap ≥ 0.95 up to ½log3. In the common range [½log2, ½log3] both give peak·T ∈ [3.7, 4.2]; only **h_a** keeps ≈ 3.7/T up to T = 1.5 (9.4·0.40 = 3.76, 6.8·0.549 = 3.73, 4.65·0.805 = 3.74, 2.45·1.5 = 3.68: that one is indeed a 1/T law, NUMERICAL, which 023 understates by presenting it as a footnote). h* breaks away from h_a past 0.55. **Compatible**; 022 only overwrites "∝ 1/T" based on a local fit. The "single-mode model with all phases → 1" that 023 falsifies is not written down in 022 nor anywhere in the folder (only in an oral brief, "point 3b"): the falsification has an unrecorded target.
- **019 "cancellation confined to 4 boundary modes" vs 023 "growing rank k".** These are not the same measurement. 019 is a Schur complement: S = G_EE − G_E𝒱 G_𝒱𝒱⁻¹ G_𝒱E, 4×4 but with G_𝒱𝒱⁻¹ (all of 𝒱, infinite-dimensional) inside; the near-kernel has only 0.805 projection onto E. 023 restricts W by a Rayleigh quotient (no Schur) to the k lowest modes of A_∞: at ½log3 not even k = 12 (2.6e-3) comes down to λ_min (9.7e-4). Both numbers are correct and compatible: **"rank" is basis-dependent and a Schur complement hides dimension**. 023 should say this instead of contrasting its k with "a single fragile mode".
- **MINOR consistency issue in 023 §2:** the "½log3" row of the rank table uses "truth 1.5e-3", which is λ_min(0.54), not λ_min(½log3) = 9.7e-4 (R186_019: 9.4e-4).
- **README line 166 (022 summary): "= alignment-arc mechanism (Paper 46 prop:align) seen from Sonin" is FALSE** according to the paper's definitions: prop:align is for N_T = {2,3} (T ∈ (½log3, log 2]) and τ > τ_a = 23.34; the peak of s₀ is at 7.6–11 and in the {2} regime. 023 §4 itself denies this. 022 does not assert it in its text; correct the README.

## 3. Is AL-k well posed?

- **(b) "A_∞|V_k − G ≻ 0"** with G_ij = Σ v_p Re c_{φ_i,φ_j}(log p): by the definition of P_n in `build` this is exactly E_S|V_k ≻ 0, a **necessary** condition implied by W ≥ 0 on 𝒫_T. The "tail control on V_k^⊥ via Schur" added next is literally the equivalence W ≥ 0 ⟺ (W|V_k ≻ 0 and Schur complement ⪰ 0). Together: **AL-k(b)+tail = W ≥ 0 written in the eigenbasis of A_∞**. A rewriting; NO-GO as a route (not as a statement, which is trivially CONDITIONAL on W ≥ 0).
- **(a) "λ_min(E_S|V_k) = λ_min(1+o(1)) with k = O(N(T))"**: ill-posed. N(T) (Landau–Widom number) requires a bandwidth that σ does not have (it is not band-limited; thm:margin gives no N); with k = dim 𝒫_T^{(M)} the equality is exact, so without an explicit constant the statement is vacuous; and the data itself refutes it in the measured range: at ½log5, k = 12 gives 1.4e-6 vs 6.4e-12 (relative error 2e5), with no trend toward 1+o(1).
- Inconsistent notation: (b) sums over p, the "consequence" over p^m.
- The phrase "margin = λ_min ~ exp(−c T e^{…}) (thm:margin)" is not what thm:margin says: that theorem gives an upper bound B(T,k) ≈ T²(k/T)^{2k}[Σ_p(21) + e^T Σ_p(H_PT)]/(const·T) with k fixed, and only limsup λ ≤ 0. MINOR (imprecise citation).
