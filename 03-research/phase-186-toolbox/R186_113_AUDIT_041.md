## 5. Attack 5: "λ_A(T) = Gauss quadrature error of the explicit formula with nodes at the zeros ≤ T*"

**Incorrect as a statement and circular as an idea.** (1) What integrand, what measure? The only two measures at play
are the zero measure ν_Z = Σ_γ δ_γ (represents 𝔞_T on 𝒫_T via lem:roughEF, with real zeros CITED) and μ_X
(represents 𝔞_T − λ on V_X). On V_X: λ‖F‖² = Σ_γ|F̂(γ)|² − Σ_x w_x|F̂(x)|². With F = h* the second term is 0 and
**λ = Σ_γ|ĥ*(γ)|²: that is lem:roughEF, and it is R186_040 item 2** (λ(1+o(1)) = mass of |ĥ*|² at the zeros > T*). Calling
it "quadrature error with nodes at the zeros ≤ T*" is renaming Σ_{γ>T*}|ĥ*(γ)|² + Σ_{γ≤T*}(1−w_γ)|ĥ*(γ)|² (the second
term is ≈ 0 by 1.3). (2) The "Gauss remainder" has no sign by structure in this context: Gauss
theory (remainder = f^{(2N)}(ξ)/(2N)!·‖p_N‖², Markov–Stieltjes inequalities) requires a positive measure and functions
with derivatives of fixed sign; here the reference measure (σ_T−λ)dτ/2π is signed and 𝔞_T is indefinite on L²(I_T). The
sign of the "remainder" is the term-by-term positivity of Σ_γ|ĥ*(γ)|², which is "real zeros" (CITED, not structural) and
row D cannot rest on it as a proof. (3) Theories that do speak to this, and what they give: (i) Kreĭn
(extension of a positive-definite function from (−2T,2T)): the N-extremal measures are discrete and give exact
quadratures, but the datum here is not positive definite on L²(I_T) but on a subspace of codimension 2 (Pontryagin
case κ ≤ 2, Kreĭn–Langer), and the determinate/indeterminate dichotomy is **global**, not "at low height": the
hypothesis "Kreĭn determinacy at low height" in R41.2 is not a notion of that theory. What R155_040/041 say
about the moment problem (and which can legitimately be used) is that for T ≤ 0.8 the extension is indeterminate: there
are many representing measures, so the agreement μ_X ≈ ν_Z at low height **cannot** come from uniqueness; it comes
from 1.3. (ii) Lemma R (R155_010): a w ≡ 1 on [−2T,2T] with ŵ ≥ 0 is trivial, i.e. **no quadrature with nodes at the
zeros produces a positive term-by-term certificate**: the "signed remainder" by band-limited structure is
excluded from the certificate class of Paper 47. (iii) Quadrature for entire functions of exponential type
(Shannon/Boas sampling formulas, Plancherel–Pólya): these give exact identities with nodes of density ≥ T/π and
positive weights for functions in PW_T, but with Lebesgue measure, not with (σ_T−λ)dτ; there is no signed asymptotic
for λ_A. **Conclusion: the README's "reading" is neither a new statement nor a candidate law; it is lem:roughEF with
another name. Withdraw from the README.**

## 7. Opinion on R41.2

**It does not deserve a proof round; it deserves a paragraph, and not with the proposed hypothesis.** The correct
conditional statement is: *if λ := 𝔞_T(h*) and γ is a simple real zero with |G′| ≥ c in a neighborhood of radius r of γ where |G″| ≤ C,
and √λ/c < r, then there is a node x with |x − γ| ≤ (1+o(1))√(λ/2)/c and w_x = 1 + O(|x−γ|) + O(λ/(gap²c²))*. It is
CONDITIONAL[real zeros up to the height used, CITED] and its proof is 1.3 (lem:roughEF + R165_202 Thm 3.1). It
needs no "Kreĭn determinacy" (and that notion "at low height" does not exist in the theory; at T ≤ 0.8 the problem is
indeterminate by R155_040/041, so uniqueness cannot be the reason). All the information is in the hypothesis
"|G′(γ_k)| ≥ c_k," i.e. in the decay profile of G′ at the zeros (geometric, ×10 per zero), which is the
content of R186_040 (where the head lives) and which this theorem does not explain. It also does not explain T*: the
observable boundary is ≈ 1.4 T* at 10⁻² and depends on the threshold. No consequence for the sign.
