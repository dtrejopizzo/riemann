## 2. F_S = E F_∞ E⁻¹ and the measure (the critical point)

**Measure.** [CCM (42)]: w_S : L²(X_S) → L²(C_S) is unitary; C_S ≅ ℝ₊^* × K_S with K_S = ℤ₂^* and Haar
d^*u ⊗ dk. On K_S-invariants, L²(X_S)^{K_S} ≅ L²(ℝ₊^*, d^*u) (constant = vol(ℤ₂^*)). [CCM (45)–(46)]:
w_S η_S f(u) = u^{1/2} Σ_{k≥0} f(2^k u). Writing w_S Φ = u^{1/2} h, ‖Φ‖² = ∫|h|² u d^*u = ∫|h|² du.
Therefore **the model u with du is correct** (up to a global constant); there is no dyadic weight Σ2^{−k}:
the 2-adic weight disappears because the fundamental domain is ℤ₂^*×ℝ₊^* and the function is K_S-invariant.
P^S_λ = 1_{(0,λ]}(u) (|1×u|_S = u). R186.003 §1 and R186.006 are correct on this.

**Formula.** [CCM Prop 4.1(iii)] F_S∘η_S = η_S∘F_∞; in the model u, η_S = E (the normalization
L²(ℝ)^{ev} ≅ L²(ℝ₊,2du) is a constant). E bounded with bounded inverse E⁻¹ = 1 − D₁ (telescoping)
[CCM Prop 4.3(i)] ⟹ F_S = E F_∞ E⁻¹. ✓

**Unitarity (derived, not assumed).** Mellin U h(s) = ∫h(u)u^{½−is}d^*u is unitary L²(du) → L²(ds/2π).
U E U⁻¹ = multiplication by L₂(½−is) [CCM (47)]; U F_∞ U⁻¹ = γ(s)·(s↦−s) with |γ|=1. Then
U F_S U⁻¹ φ(s) = γ(s) L₂(½−is)/L₂(½+is) · φ(−s), and L₂(½+is) = conj L₂(½−is), so the factor
has modulus 1: **F_S is unitary in L²(du)** even though E is not (E^*E = multiplication by |L₂(½−is)|²,
≠ 1). There is no contradiction: E is not unitary but conjugation of F_∞ by E is, because the
multiplier of E is "real up to phase" under s↦−s. F_S² = 1, F_S = F_S^*, F_S h_S = h_S. ✓

**Kernel.** F E⁻¹ f(ξ) = ∫f(x)[2cos(2πξx) − cos(πξx)]dx ✓. Applying E and telescoping,
Σ_{k=0}^{K}[2cos(2π2^k ux) − cos(π2^k ux)] = Σ_{k=0}^{K}cos(2π2^kux) − cos(πux) + cos(2π2^Kux).
The memo's formula is that of the truncation with the term cos(2π2^K ux) removed. As an operator on

"distributional sense" should be replaced by: *P F_S P is the norm limit of the operators with
truncated kernel*. A correction of wording, not of content. (Outside P convergence is only strong.)

## 5. Halmos, compactness, and the question of similarity (DECISION)

Notation: P = 1_{(0,λ]}, A_S = P F_S P, A_∞ = P F_∞ P.

**Supporting facts.** E and E⁻¹ preserve ran P (if g ⊂ (0,λ], g(2^ku) ⊂ (0,2^{−k}λ]). But E does **not**
preserve ran(1−P): E(1−P) carries mass from (λ,2^kλ] to (λ2^{−k},λ]. Thus E_λ := PEP is invertible on ran P with
inverse PE⁻¹P, but E does not commute with P.

**Exact decomposition.** A_S = P E F E⁻¹ P = P E F P E⁻¹ P = E_λ A_∞ E_λ⁻¹ + R E_λ⁻¹, with
R := P E (1−P) F P = Σ_{k≥1} 2^{−k/2}δ_k P_{(λ,2^kλ]} F P_λ, Rf(u) = Σ_{k≥1, 2^ku>λ} (F f)(2^k u).
Each summand is HS, the series converges in norm ⇒ R compact ⇒ A_S compact (memo ✓). No eigenvalue 1
(Paley–Wiener on E⁻¹f) ✓; with compactness, ‖P P̂ P‖ < 1.

**Theorem (referee, PROVED). P F_S P is not Hilbert–Schmidt.** It suffices to see that R is not HS (E_λ A_∞ E_λ⁻¹ is HS and E_λ is
an isomorphism of ran P). If R were HS it would have a kernel k ∈ L²; on Q = (λ/2,λ)², for f,g ∈ C_c^∞,
⟨Rf,g⟩ = Σ_{k≥1}∫∫2cos(2π2^kux)f(x)g(u) (every k ≥ 1 satisfies 2^ku > λ for u > λ/2), so
k|_Q is the distributional sum T = Σ_{k≥1}2cos(2π2^kux). Let χ ∈ C_c^∞(Q), χ ≥ 0, χ ≢ 0. Non-stationary phase
(∂_x[(2^k±2^j)ux] ≥ 2^{j−1}λ/2) gives ⟨T, χcos(2π2^jux)⟩ = ∫χ + O(2^{−j}) → ∫χ ≠ 0.
But if k ∈ L²(Q), χk ∈ L¹ and Riemann–Lebesgue (phase ux with nonzero gradient on Q) gives → 0. Contradiction. ∎

**Corollary (decision requested).** A_∞ = P F_∞ P is trace class (Slepian: super-exponential eigenvalues),
so Σ μ_n(A_∞²) < ∞. If A_S were similar to A_∞ (or merely isospectral with multiplicities), being A_S
self-adjoint compact, ‖A_S‖²_HS = Σ μ_n(A_S)² = Σ μ_n(A_∞)² < ∞, contradicting the theorem.
**P F_S P is NOT similar to P F_∞ P.** The suspicion "A_S = E A_∞ E⁻¹" of R186_007 confuses E with E_λ and forgets
the leakage term R = PE(1−P)FP, which is precisely the same "outward dilation" phenomenon of §3.
Consequence: the "semilocal angle operator with new spectrum" **exists** (spectrum of P P̂ P not summable,
cos²θ_n not trace class), and the program does not have to be reformulated as "metric only." It should however be stated
precisely: principal part = similarity to the archimedean one; novelty = non-HS compact perturbation R.
