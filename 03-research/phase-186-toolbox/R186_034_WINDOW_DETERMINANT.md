## 1. The three candidates (NO-GO, with argument)

**1.1 (a) Semilocal chain.** By R186_009 L0, A_S = PF_SP is a self-adjoint compact operator, ‖A_S‖ = |λ₁| < 1, Σλ_n² = ∞ (A_S ∉ HS, R186_007 Thm 5). So ∏(1 − λ_n²) = 0: det(1 − A_S²) does not exist as a Fredholm determinant. det₂(1 − A_S²) = ∏(1−λ_n²)e^{λ_n²} exists if Σλ_n⁴ < ∞ (ARGUED |λ_n| ≍ n^{−1/2}: yes). But det₂ is a function of the spectrum of Q₁Q₂Q₁, the angle operator of the pair (P, P̂^S): it does not depend on any test h, nor on the window T except through λ. Row D in this chain is the sign of E_S(h) = Son_S(h) − ∫c_h ε′_S (CONDITIONAL on TC_S), a quadratic functional in h; a scalar invariant of the pair of projections cannot be equivalent to the positivity of a functional on an infinite-dimensional space. Additivity in S: the logarithm of det₂ is NOT additive in S (U = e^{iφ(D)} is multiplicative in S but A_S = PF_∞UP is not: P does not commute with U); the Szegő formula for the symbol m_∞U does not apply because PF_∞P is not a Wiener–Hopf operator (F_∞ is a Hankel/cosine-type involution, not a convolution). **NO-GO.**

**1.2 (b) Perturbation relative to the archimedean.** Two obstacles, each fatal.
(i) Γ_T := 𝔞_T without primes **is not ≥ 0 on 𝒫_T** for T ≥ 0.60 (Paper 46 subsec:arch (ii): λ^{∞,P}_min < 0 from cor:Tarch; R186_023: E_∞(h_a) crosses 0 at 0.597). At ½log5 there is no positive Γ_T^{−1/2}: the object is not defined at the second requested window.
(ii) **Theorem 1.2 (no Schatten class; PROVED).** Let Γ be a self-adjoint operator on L²(I_T) with eigenvalues γ_j ≍ log j (in particular R_T(m + c)(D)R_T with c > −C₀, by lem:mbounds and Weyl's law for monotone symbols: CITED Widom, *Asymptotic behavior of block Toeplitz matrices and determinants* II (1976) / Birman–Solomyak for R_Tf(D)R_T; rough NUMERICAL check in the script: γ_j/log(1+j) ∈ [2.5, 3.4] for j = 5…40 at ½log3, Legendre Galerkin M = 40), and let Π be a bounded operator whose diagonal in the eigenbasis {e_j} of Γ does not tend to zero: |⟨Πe_j,e_j⟩| ≥ c > 0 on a sequence of positive density (for compressed Π_T = −Σw_n(S_{log n}+S_{−log n}): ⟨S_ae_j,e_j⟩ ≈ (1 − a/2T)cos(πja/2T), which does not decay). Then K := Γ^{−1/2}ΠΓ^{−1/2} ∉ S_p for every p < ∞.
*Proof.* ⟨Ke_j,e_j⟩ = ⟨Πe_j,e_j⟩/γ_j, so Σ_j|⟨Ke_j,e_j⟩|^p ≥ c^p Σ_{j∈J}(log j)^{−p} = ∞. For K self-adjoint (or normal) the diagonal in any orthonormal basis is majorized by the eigenvalue sequence (Schur–Horn), so Σ|⟨Ke_j,e_j⟩|^p ≤ ‖K‖_p^p. ∎
Consequence: det(1 + K) does not exist, nor does det_p(1 + K) for any p: the logarithmic growth of the archimedean term is **too slow** to regularize the prime perturbation. No "Szegő–Widom with the comb as symbol" can get started. On E_k (R186_026) everything is a finite matrix and its determinant is ∏ of eigenvalues: it inherits λ_min(W|E_k) ≥ λ_A (R186_106). **NO-GO.**

**1.3 (c) Truncated Toeplitz/Wiener–Hopf with symbol σ_T.** The operator is exactly A_T = R_Tσ_T(D)R_T (thm:W): unbounded, eigenvalues → +∞ like log j; det does not exist; det(A_T·Γ_c^{−1}) does not either by Theorem 1.2 (A_T = Γ_c + (Π_T − c)). The citable theories — Kac–Akhiezer/continuous strong Szegő (Böttcher–Silbermann, *Analysis of Toeplitz Operators*, 2nd ed., §10.9, Thm 10.47: a − 1 ∈ L¹∩L², a > 0, log a ∈ B^{1/2}_{2,2}), Widom (1982, *On a class of integral operators with discontinuous symbol*, leading order for piecewise symbols), Basor–Widom (1983, Fisher–Hartwig with zeros/jumps) — require a **bounded** symbol, a − 1 integrable, and describe T → ∞ with fixed symbol; here σ_T is unbounded, σ_T − 1 ∉ L¹, and σ_T changes with T. On the sign: if a det existed, its sign would be (−1)^{n₋(𝔞_T|L²(I_T))} = −1 for every T ≥ 0.12 with an even negative direction (R186_028, PROVED under W_T ≥ 0 and NUMERICAL up to 0.85); the parity of n₋ over L²(I_T) is not n₋ over 𝒫_T. Widom's count N_−(T) = (2T/2π)|{σ<0}| + O(log T) has error ≥ the quantity that matters (0 or 1). "Does the sign of D count the sign crossings of σ weighted by the window?" — only to leading order and only n₋ of the full form, never λ_A > 0. **NO-GO.**

## 2. The object that exists: Birman–Schwinger determinant of the window (d)

**Theorem 2.1 (PROVED).** Let T > 0, 𝔞_T = Pos_T − Neg_T with Pos_T = R_Tσ_+(D)R_T, Neg_T = R_Tσ_−(D)R_T (prop:negtrace). Then:
(i) Pos_T is boundedly invertible on L²(I_T): Pos_T ≥ c_δ(1 − λ₀(S_{T,τ_last+δ})) > 0 with c_δ := min_{|τ|≥τ_last+δ}σ_T > 0 (σ_T ↑ +∞, lem:mbounds) and λ₀ < 1 the maximal eigenvalue of the time-band operator (proof of prop:negtrace(v)); also on 𝒫_T (restriction).
(ii) B_T := Pos_T^{−1/2}Neg_TPos_T^{−1/2} is trace class (Neg_T trace by prop:negtrace(i), Pos_T^{−1/2} bounded), 0 ≤ B_T, tr B_T ≤ ‖Pos_T^{−1}‖·(2T/2π)∫σ_−.

(iv) Pos_T^{1/2} does not preserve 𝒫_T; define B_T|𝒫 := 𝔭^{−1/2}𝔫𝔭^{−1/2} with 𝔭 = P_𝒫Pos_TP_𝒫 ≥ c > 0 and 𝔫 = P_𝒫Neg_TP_𝒫 (trace class) on 𝒫_T; then 𝔞_T|𝒫 = 𝔭^{1/2}(1 − B_T|𝒫)𝔭^{1/2} and by Sylvester inertia(𝔞_T|𝒫) = inertia(1 − B_T|𝒫). ## 3. Why no determinant escapes the margin (NO-GO of request (3))

**Theorem 3.1 (inheritance; PROVED, elementary).** Let D(s;T) be a function continuous in (s,T), real-analytic in s, such that (H) λ_A(T) > 0 ⟺ D(·;T) does not vanish on [0,1] (as D_B), with D(0;T) = 1 and ∂_sD bounded by a constant K(T) < ∞ computable without λ_A. Then, for every T with λ_A(T) > 0, the first zero s₁(T) of D satisfies s₁ − 1 ≥ 0 and, if D comes from a pencil (𝔞_T = Pos − Neg with Neg ≥ 0 as in §2, or any decomposition 𝔞_T = 𝔭 − 𝔫 with 𝔭 > 0 on 𝒫_T), s₁ − 1 ≤ λ_A/𝔭(h*). If instead D is a function of the spectrum of 𝔞_T|𝒫_T of the form ∏f(μ_j) with f(0) = 0, f′(0) ≠ 0, then D(T) = λ_A·f′(0)∏_{j≥2}f(μ_j)·(1+o(1)): **|D| ≤ C·λ_A**. In both cases a leading term D₀ with D = D₀(1 + ε), ε = O(1) (bounded multiplicative error, which is what all strong Szegő theorems give: the constant E is the zeroth-order term and the remainder is o(1) **for a fixed symbol and T → ∞**), decides the sign of D only if |ε| < 1 — i.e. only if D₀ approximates λ_A·R with relative error < 1, which is an asymptotic for λ_A with relative error < 1.
*Proof.* The two inequalities are Rayleigh (Theorem 2.2) and the factorization is the definition. ∎

**3.2 Consequence.** The only way for the "leading term" of a window determinant to be independent of λ_A is for the determinant not to see the sign (as det(1 + Neg_T), §4), or for the leading term to **be** the Landau–Widom law for λ_A: −log λ_A ∼ 2π²N(T*)/log N(T*) (Zhu, Conj. 12.1, CITED as a conjecture; NUMERICAL in its certified upper bounds up to L = 2). In the language of this note, Zhu's conjecture is exactly "log(1 − β_max(T)) = −2π²N/log N·(1+o(1)) + O(log Pos(h*))". A Szegő–Widom-type theorem giving that would be the asymptotic row D, not a tool for it: that is why request (3) is a NO-GO **as a route**, not as a statement.

**3.3 What the Pos/Neg decomposition does provide (PROVED/CONDITIONAL).** By Theorem 2.1(iv) and prop:NC, n₋(𝔞_T|𝒫_T) ≤ #{j: β_j > 1} and β_j ≤ ν_j(Neg_T)/λ_min(Pos_T|𝒫_T); since the ν_j (eigenvalues of a time-band operator with compactly supported symbol σ_−) drop super-exponentially beyond the Shannon number N_sh = 2T|{σ_T<0}|/π (Landau–Widom 1980, CITED; here ν_j: 3.48, 0.55, 0.54, 0.44, 0.30, 0.27, 0.23, … at ½log5), one gets the **explicit bound n₋(𝔞_T|𝒫_T) ≤ #{j: ν_j(Neg_T) > λ_min(Pos_T|𝒫_T)}**, computable from σ_± (PROVED): 4 at ½log3, 10 at ½log5. Improves prop:NC (37 and 54) by an order of magnitude but is still far from 0. Flagged observation (not used): the positivity assumed at every T forces the ≥ N_sh − O(1) directions of Neg_T with O(1) eigenvalue to be absorbed by Pos_T except at most... — this is the "Szegő falsifier" of R186_000 §1.3 (CONDITIONAL), a consequence of positivity, not a route toward it.

## 4. What Szegő–Widom does compute: det(1 + Neg_T) (NUMERICAL + CITED)

**Theorem 4.1 (PROVED).** det(1 + Neg_T) = ∏(1 + ν_j) exists (Neg_T trace class), is > 1 for every T, and is the Wiener–Hopf truncation to L²(I_T) (length 2T) of the symbol a_T := 1 + σ_−, bounded, even, continuous with cuts of type |τ − τ_j| at the zeros of σ_T, a_T − 1 ∈ L¹∩L², log a_T ∈ B^{1/2}_{2,2} (the cuts give (log a)^ ≍ x^{−2}). For a **fixed symbol** a and length L → ∞, Kac–Akhiezer (Böttcher–Silbermann Thm 10.47, CITED) gives det W_L(a) = G^L·E·(1+o(1)), log G = (1/2π)∫log a, E = exp∫₀^∞ x s(x)s(−x)dx, s = (1/2π)∫log a·e^{−ix·}. Normalization verified in the script with a Gaussian symbol: remainder 1.1·10⁻⁷ constant for L ∈ [5,40]. This determinant contains no sign information: it is a function of σ_− alone (Theorem 3.1, the "does not see the sign" case).

But the sign of row D lies in the ratio D_B = det(A|𝒫)/det(Pos|𝒫), whose symbolic leading term is (2T/2π)∫log(σ/σ_+) = (2T/2π)∫_{σ<0}log 0 = −∞: **the two blocks have disjoint symbolic supports, the symbolic ratio is 0/0, and all of D_B lives in the overlap of the prolate tails** (the Landau–Widom plunge region), which is exactly what the leading Szegő term does not describe. This is the precise form of "the sign is in the Szegő remainder."
