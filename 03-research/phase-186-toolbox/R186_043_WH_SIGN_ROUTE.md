## 7. Honesty and what would make a theorem

7.1 **Inheritance.** (a) The span condition is computable from σ alone (modes of S_{J_i}, Pos, Neg, Tate) and has its own margin: 2.61·10⁻⁶ (½log5), 2.45·10⁻⁴ (log 2), 1.9·10⁻² (½log3). It does not inherit λ_A. It is a **necessary condition**: β_max|𝒫_T ≥ β_max|span; useful for falsifying (which it does with M₁) and not for proving. (b) This memo's sound **sufficient** conditions (Lemma M0(iii) with a step minorant, M1's Gershgorin) are monotone in σ₊ and therefore legitimate for every T, but on σ_T they give β > 1 (per-component steps: R186_042 M3′/M23′, −0.08/−0.03; here Gershgorin already fails in the model with T·dist = 1.6). (c) Nothing in the memo bounds 1 − β_max(ζ) from below. The slope law κawT²/b₁ is a sub-Shannon asymptotics of a model, not a theorem about σ_T.
7.2 **What would need to be proved for all T.** A theorem "λ_A(T) > 0 ∀T" via this route needs a sufficient condition that captures the slope at ∂U_T and the coupling at T·dist ≈ 2 simultaneously: (i) a version of Lemma M0(iii) with a **linear** minorant σ₊ ≥ b₁·dist(τ, U_T) near ∂U_T (requires a computation of ⟨W_T(b₁·dist)⁻¹ψ,ψ⟩ for unions of intervals — the slope analogue of Cor. 2.4, OPEN; it does not exist in the Widom/Slepian literature, which treats symbols with jumps or zeros of fixed order on a single interval), and (ii) a bound on the 1/(T·dist)-scale interference that is not Gershgorin (OPEN). Properties of m and of the comb at ∂U_T that would need to be proved: σ_T′(∂U_T) ≥ c·T²·∫_{J}σ₋ at each component — by §5(d), for τ < 7 this is an inequality about Σv_n log n·sin(τ_0 log n) (the comb's derivative at the zero τ_0) against the comb's mass below m, i.e. a **property of phase alignment of the comb at its own zeros**; for large τ it is m′ = ½Re ψ′(¼+iτ/2) ≈ 1/τ against O(1/τ) masses of small components (N_J → 0): there M0 applies and gives β_i → 0 (confirmed: 0.002–0.02 at τ ≥ 62). Neither is proved; the first is false as an isolated sufficient condition (§5(a): the low components' local margins are negative) and makes sense only together with Tate + coupling.
7.3 **Falsifier**: passed in the requested sense (the span condition fails for M₁ and not for ζ nor for δ = 0.001); **not** passed in the local sense: no inequality at ∂U separates M₁ from ζ. So the "candidate insight" stands as: PROVED in M0, NO-GO as a local criterion on σ_T, and the part that does see the sign (coupled span) is the necessary condition of rank #comp already anticipated by R186_040 §2.2 and R186_042 §3.2(iii).
