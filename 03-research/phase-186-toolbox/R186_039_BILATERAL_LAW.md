## 1. Zhu's Conjecture 12.1 and the target statement

**1.1 Zhu's text (CITED, read in 2608.24827v2 lines 1388–1392).** With λ*(L) := inf_f Q(f)/‖f‖₂² over f real even, supp f ⊆ [−L, L] (Q = the full Weil form; Zhu's normalization matches ours: his L is our T, his Q is W_T, and Yoshida's "2L ≤ log 2" is our T ≤ ½log2; hence λ*(L) = λ_W(T) restricted to even tests):
**Conjecture 12.1.** As L → ∞, −ln λ*(L) = 2π²·N(T*)/ln N(T*)·(1+o(1)), T* = 2πe^{2L}, N(T*) = e^{2L}(2L−1) + O(L).
Remark 12.2: the data discriminate the *form* N/ln N; the constant 2π² is read off the plateau 20.02–20.27 (= 1.015–1.027 × 2π²) and "is not derived." What is known: (a) certified upper bounds (Table 3): λ*(0.5) ≤ 1.05·10⁻⁶, λ*(0.8) ≤ 2.27·10⁻¹⁷, …, λ*(2.0) ≤ 3.19·10⁻²⁸³, with the out-of-sample test 1.2 → 1.4–2.0 that rejects "one nat per zero"; (b) certified lower bound λ*(0.8) ≥ 8.9·10⁻¹⁸ (his Thm 1.2) — the **only** window where the law is boxed in from both sides (factor 2.6); (c) Thm 1.3 (conditional): λ*(L) ≤ exp(−Le^L) for L ≥ L₀ — exponent e^L, not e^{2L}, due to the window obstruction of his Rem. 13.6; (d) open problem (i): prove 12.1 "presumably via multi-interval Landau–Widom asymptotics"; (iii): lower bound on the cost of every certificate "via the sampling theory of low zeta zeros," since "the window floor [is] twice the squared smallest singular value of a low-zero sampling operator" (§14.2).
Our data: λ_A(½log3) = 9.49·10⁻⁴, λ_A(½log5) = 3.86·10⁻¹² (NUMERICAL upper bound; certified ≥ 2.5·10⁻¹²) are the **primitive** form (𝔞_T over 𝒫_T); λ_W(½log5) ∈ [10⁻¹⁸, 2.3·10⁻¹⁷] (thm:log5W + Zhu). The two differ by 5–6 orders of magnitude: λ_W ≤ λ_A and the ratio is the polar term (R186_028 Lemma 1.1); Zhu's law is formulated for λ_W. Neither primitive value is in the law's regime: 2π²N/ln N at ½log5 (N = 4) is 57 against −ln λ_A = 26.3 and −ln λ_W ≈ 38–41; Zhu himself only claims the plateau from L = 1.4 (N = 30) onward.

**1.2 Target statement (BL, OPEN).** There exists an explicit L: (0,∞) → (0,∞) such that λ_A(T) ≥ L(T)(1 − ε(T)) with ε → 0 (weak form), or λ_A(T) = L(T)(1+o(1)) (bilateral). By lem:monoT and Thm M, any admissible L satisfies L(T) ≤ exp(−e^{(2−ε)T}); by thm:margin/cor:margin1, L(T) ≤ 4·10⁹ T e^{−15.449T} for T ≥ 1 (weaker but explicit bound). BL is strictly stronger than D (D is "λ_A ≥ 0"; BL gives λ_A > 0 with a rate). The unique candidate compatible with 1.1: **L_Z(T) = exp(−2π²N(T*)/ln N(T*)·(1+o(1)))** (in λ_A: same exponent except for the polar term, which is O(e^{T}) multiplicative and fits inside the o(1) of the exponent — ARGUED by R186_028 §1: 𝔞_T = W_T − 2ℓ_ev² + 2ℓ_odd²). In Birman–Schwinger form (R186_034 Thm 2.2): BL ⟺ log(1 − β_max(T)) = −2π²N(T*)/ln N(T*)·(1+o(1)) (Pos(h*) ∈ [0.18, 0.23] in the computed windows, O(1) in the exponent).

**1.3 Why L_Sh = exp(−c N_Sh log N_Sh) is not bilateral (ARGUED + NUMERICAL).** N_Sh(T) := 2T|{σ_T<0}|/π. (a) Growth: {σ_T<0} = {Σ_{𝒩_T} v_n cos(τ log n) > m(τ)} with m(τ) ≈ log(τ/2π) (lem:mbounds). The comb has mean 0 and variance ½Σv_n² = 2Σ_{n<e^{2T}}Λ(n)²/n ≈ 4T²; with equidistribution of (τ log p)_p (Kronecker) and Gaussian approximation at moderate deviations, |{σ_T<0}| ≈ 2π∫₀^{A_T} e^{x}e^{−x²/8T²}dx ≍ T·e^{2T²} (maximum at x = 4T² < A_T ≈ 4e^T). Hence N_Sh ≍ T²e^{2T²} ≫ N(T*) ≍ Te^{2T}. This is ARGUED (the Gaussian approximation fails at large deviation; the true bound lies between e^{cT²} and τ^{lo} = 2πe^{A_T}); in the data, |{σ<0}| = 6.89, 11.64, 13.75 for contacts {2}, {2,3}, {2,3,4}. (b) Consequence: if BL holds with L_Z, then λ_A ≫ L_Sh for every c > 0 from moderate T onward: L_Sh would be at most a lower bound, not a law. (c) Fits (script, 7 windows, −log λ_A = 0.7 … 26.3): none of N_Sh, N_Sh log N_Sh, e^{2T}, 2T·N(T*), Te^{2T²} fits with relative residual < 0.5; **NUMERICAL: at T ≤ ½log5 the data do not discriminate between laws** (pre-asymptotic regime, N(T*) ≤ 4), in agreement with Zhu §12. What the data do discriminate is the **count** of directions (§2.4).

## 2. Proof structure: the bundle (Neg_T, Pos_T) over 𝒫_T and the two Landau–Widom regimes

**2.1 Framework (R186_034 Thm 2.1, PROVED).** 𝔞_T = Pos_T − Neg_T, B_T|𝒫 = 𝔭^{−1/2}𝔫𝔭^{−1/2} with eigenvalues β_1 ≥ β_2 ≥ … ≥ 0 (trace class); λ_A > 0 ⟺ β_1 < 1; 1 − β_1 ∈ [λ_A/Pos(h*), λ_A/λ_min(Pos|𝒫)]. Let S_T := R_T 1_{\{σ_T<0\}}(D) R_T be the time-band operator of the negative set (finite union of intervals, prop:band), eigenvalues 1 > λ_0 ≥ λ_1 ≥ …, Shannon number N_Sh = tr S_T. Let s_- := max σ_- and c_P := λ_min(Pos_T|𝒫_T) > 0 (R186_034 Thm 2.1(i)).

**Lemma 2.1 (prolate tail; PROVED modulo CITED).** (i) ν_j(Neg_T|𝒫) ≤ ν_j(Neg_T) ≤ s_-·λ_j(S_T) (Neg_T = R_Tσ_-(D)R_T ≤ s_-S_T as forms; compression to 𝒫 via min–max). (ii) β_j ≤ ν_j(Neg_T|𝒫)/c_P (Sylvester + min–max in the bundle). (iii) [Landau–Widom 1980, CITED, valid for finite unions of intervals in time and frequency] #{j : λ_j(S_T) > α} = N_Sh + (1/π²) log N_Sh · log((1−α)/α) + o(log N_Sh) for fixed α ∈ (0,1). Hence, with α := c_P/s_-,
 **β_j < 1 for every j > J(T) := N_Sh + (1/π²) log N_Sh · log(s_-/c_P) + o(log N_Sh).**
(iv) c_P ≥ c_δ(1 − λ_0(S_{T,τ_last+δ})) (R186_034) and, more finely, c_P ≳ (min_{off} σ_+)·(1 − λ_0(S_T)) with 1 − λ_0(S_T) ≍ exp(−π²N_Sh/log N_Sh) (LW plateau, ARGUED in the multi-interval case); hence log(s_-/c_P) ≲ π²N_Sh/log N_Sh and **J(T) ≤ 2N_Sh + O(log N_Sh)**. NUMERICAL: J = 4 at ½log3 and 10 at ½log5 with the crude bound of R186_034 §3.3 (ν_j > c_P).
*Proof.* (i)–(ii) as stated; (iii) is the cited theorem applied to the set {σ_T<0} = union of ≤ 8 intervals (prop:band/align, Rem. bandtable); (iv) Rayleigh. ∎
Consequence (PROVED mod CITED): **BL ⟺ a statement about the head block** H_T := span of the first J(T) eigenvectors of the bundle, dim ≤ 2N_Sh + O(log N_Sh), finite for each T, → ∞ with T. Lemma 2.1 is what Zhu lacks: it converts "multi-interval LW asymptotics" (his open problem (i)) into "tail PROVED + head OPEN" with the head's dimension made explicit.

**2.2 Where does the sign of 1 − β_j come from for j ≤ J(T)?** From the direction-by-direction comparison of Neg (≈ s_-·λ_j(S_T)·‖·‖², for eigenvectors of S_T) with Pos (= ∫_{off}σ_+|F̂|²/2π, the leakage of the prolate into {σ>0} weighted by σ_+ ~ log). The **LW main-term model** (pure prolate: leakage 1 − λ_j, σ_+ ≈ s_+ constant) gives β_j^{mod} ≈ (s_-/s_+)·λ_j/(1−λ_j), which is > 1 for j < N_Sh − (log N_Sh/π²)log(s_+/s_-): the model predicts **negativity** of size N_Sh − O(log N_Sh) directions. This is prop:negtrace(v) made quantitative. NUMERICAL (script, seven windows): the first prolate of S_T projected onto 𝒫_T has 𝔞_T(u₀) = +0.099 = Pos 0.375 − Neg 0.276 at ½log5 (positive in every window), because λ_0(S_T) ≤ 0.89 (N_Sh ≤ 7: still the transition zone, not the plateau) and the 11–33% leakage falls where σ_+ ≥ 2. The model does not even predict the sign in these windows; as N_Sh → ∞ it predicts the wrong sign. **The true sign is not a function of the LW main term: it is a function of the fine geometry σ_+ ↔ σ_- in the overlap of tails** (R186_034 §4: "the symbolic ratio is 0/0").

**2.3 Relative error of time-band asymptotics in what matters (CITED + elementary PROVED). NO-GO.**
(a) Landau–Widom 1980 (and Landau 1993 for unions of intervals) control N(α) = #{λ_j > α} with error o(log c), α fixed. Inverted: for j = c + (b/π²)log c with b bounded, λ_j = (1+e^{b})^{−1} + o(1) **absolute**; the relative error in 1 − λ_j is o(1) only if b (and hence 1 − λ_j) is bounded away from 0. Slepian 1965 (one interval) gives the same expansion with O(1/log c) correction in b; Bonami–Jaming–Karoui 2021 give non-asymptotic two-sided bounds for one interval with O(1) constants. (b) On the plateau (b → −∞, 1 − λ_j ≪ 1) the only asymptotics with relative error → 0 is the fixed-index-n, c → ∞ one (Fuchs 1964, Widom 1964: 1 − λ_n ∼ C_n c^{n+1/2}e^{−2c}), for **one** interval; for unions of intervals no such result exists in the literature (R183_031 §(ii) already noted this). (c) But 1 − β_1 = λ_A/Pos(h*) = 1.7·10⁻¹¹ at ½log5 is a **plateau** number of the **bundle**, not of S_T: neither (a) nor (b) reaches it. And even if a multi-interval plateau asymptotics for S_T with relative error → 0 existed, by 2.2 the bundle is not S_T: its sign depends on σ_+ in the leakage. (d) Impossibility statement in the declared class (PROVED, elementary): let 𝔏 be the class of claims "1 − β_1(T) = P(T)(1 + ε(T))" with P computable from invariants of S_T alone (λ_j(S_T), N_Sh, measures of the intervals) and |ε| = O(1). By prop:negtrace(v), there exist symbols σ′ with the same negative set and the same σ_- (hence the same S_T and the same P) with β_1(σ′) > 1; so no P ∈ 𝔏 has a sign correlated with 1 − β_1 and, if |ε| = O(1) is not bounded by 1, it decides nothing. This is exactly R186_034 Thm 3.1 (W-det) read in the bundle: **the main term decides if and only if it is the law**, and the law carries σ_+ (the leakage weighted by the archimedean term plus the primes), not only σ_-. **NO-GO** for the route "LW in the transition zone fixes the sign"; box BL remains OPEN, not refuted.

## 4. Honesty, first lemma, falsifier
