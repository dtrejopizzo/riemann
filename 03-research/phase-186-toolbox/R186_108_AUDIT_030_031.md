## 1. Attack 1 — Thm 2 ("the prime side is the zero side")

**Correct label: ARGUED, not PROVED.** The statement has no hypothesis class: "every closed form of the prime
part coming from the Euler product" is not defined; the "proof" *postulates* that such a form is
Σ Λ(n)n^{−1/2}ω(log n)cos(τ log n) with Σ Λ(n)n^{−1/2}|ω(log n)| < ∞ and then applies thm:Z(ii). Two problems:

(a) thm:Z(ii) is stated for ω ∈ 𝒲_cl^reg (C_c^∞, ω ≡ 1 on the window). The example the text uses
(ω = e^{−σ|u|}, Poisson) is not in 𝒲_cl^reg nor in 𝒲_cl; the explicit formula holds for it by another route
(decay e^{−(½+δ)|u|} with σ > ½ and bounded variation), which the text does not cite. MINOR if stated; MAJOR as it
stands, because the theorem cites a result outside its range of validity.

(b) A prime-side presentation that escapes the taxonomy: for ω(u) = e^{−σ|u|} with σ > 0, the function
P_ω(τ) := Σ_n v_n ω(log n) cos(τ log n) = 2·Re(−ζ′/ζ)(½+σ+iτ) is the Fourier transform of the **positive** measure
Σ v_n ω(log n)·½(δ_{log n}+δ_{−log n}) on the multiplicative group: it is **positive definite by Bochner without going
through any zero** (hence Mertens: Σ v_nω(log n)(1 ± cos(τ log n))² ≥ 0, i.e. 3P_ω(0) ± 4P_ω(τ) + P_ω(2τ) ≥ 0, a sum of
squares indexed by *primes*). The claim "the only Euler product with a sign is Poisson positivity (Li–Keiper)" conflates
"positive definite" with "nonnegative"; the prime-side mechanism exists, it is Bochner on ℝ^×, and
**it does not reduce to (Z).** What is true, and is all the front needs: in f_w the comb enters with sign
−, and for a positive definite function P the only term-by-term lower bound is −P(τ) ≥ −P(0) (Mertens gives
−P(τ) ≥ −¾P(0) − ¼P(2τ), which with |P(2τ)| ≤ P(0) improves nothing). That is: **(P) is NO-GO because −(positive
definite) has no sign, not because it is (Z).** Likewise the scattering phase of R186_008 (C′) is an exact
presentation of the comb without zeros. I recommend rewriting Thm 2 as: "every term-by-term mechanism on the comb with
positive weights gives at most −P(τ) ≥ −P_ω(0) = −𝖠_T − (tail), which is lem:mbounds(iii): nothing new." That is indeed PROVED
and is what the text meant to say.
