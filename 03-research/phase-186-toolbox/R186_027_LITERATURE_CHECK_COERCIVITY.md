## 3. Unvarnished verdict

1. **No paper contains Theorems A/B, the piecewise Tate basis, K₁, or the norm π.** But the leading term log(1/a) of the short-window law is **observed numerically** in Kim et al. 2607.24830 (for the Suzuki operator, with no primes), and the qualitative uniform coercivity on short windows at finite codimension is **Yoshida 1992 Lemma 3** (orthogonal to Fourier modes) and **CC 2006.13771 Thm 3.6 + Cor 3.8**. All three must be cited when integrating into Paper 46, stating that what is new is the log(1/τ) rate with a constant and the Tate condition.
2. The finite reduction (item 4) exists in several sufficient forms (Zhu, Groskin, Liu, the Arb repo); ours is the equivalence in explicit dimension 2k−2. It must be stated as "exact reduction in an explicit basis", never as "the first finite reduction".
3. The jose-paul-b repo documents that the semilocal form with P=2 loses positivity at L ≈ 1.1147 (bracket [0.0154, 0.0161]) and that the odd sector fails first: an external NUMERICAL data point relevant to the S={∞,2} chain of R186_002–017 (beyond ½log5), not to 024/025.
4. R186_020 should add 2511.23257 to the CC/CvS list.
