## 2. R186.011

**R.** Redone: PF_Sξ_n = λ_nξ_n ⇒ ξ_n^♯ := λ_n^{−1}F_Sξ_n coincides with ξ_n on (0,λ] and (1−P)F_Sξ_n = λ_n(1−P)ξ_n^♯, so that ζ_n = λ_n(1−P)ξ_n^♯/√(1−λ_n²) and λ_n(1−λ_n²)^{−1/2}·λ_n(1−λ_n²)^{−1/2} = λ_n²/(1−λ_n²). With ϑ(ρ⁻¹)ζ(x) = ρ^{1/2}ζ(ρx): integrating over x ≤ λ, ρx > λ. **PROVED.** S (no alternation): PROVED (trivial from R).

**Q.** K_S(x,y) = Σ_{k≥0}c_k − c_{−1}; K_S(2x,y) = Σ_{k≥1}c_k − c_0; difference 2c_0 − c_{−1}; iterating gives B_j = Σ_{i<j}(2c_i − c_{i−1}), an integral kernel. Point the memo does not state and that makes it rigorous: each c_k is 2^{−k/2}/2 times a unitary (dilation ∘ F_∞) on **all** of L²(ℝ₊), so Σc_k converges in norm in L²(ℝ₊) (not only compressed by P), and the identity (F_Sξ)(2^j·)·2^{?} = ((F_S − B_j)ξ)(·) is an identity of operators in L², not merely of formal kernels. **PROVED.** MINOR: the memo should fix the normalization of the dilation (no 2^{j/2} factor in ξ^♯(2^jx); the 2^{j/2} appears separately in T_n): the formula for T_n(2^j) is consistent with this.
"Coefficient = mass + absolutely convergent term": B_j has a C^∞ kernel on [0,λ]², hence is trace class on L²(0,λ), and Σ_n|⟨1_Iξ_n, B_jξ_n⟩| ≤ ‖PB_jP‖_{S_1} < ∞; λ_n/(1−λ_n²) bounded (λ_1 < 1). **Correct.** An absolutely convergent term cannot cancel a divergent series of nonnegative terms.

**Sp.** ‖Pc_kP‖_{S_p} ≤ ‖Pc_kP‖^{1−2/p}‖Pc_kP‖_{HS}^{2/p} ≤ (½2^{−k/2})^{1−2/p}λ^{2/p}, summable for p > 2. **PROVED without using |λ_n| ≍ n^{−1/2}.** The concern ("Sp CONDITIONAL on the ARGUED law") does not apply: Sp is a direct Schatten bound.

**NH.** For I×(0,λ] of positive measure, the restricted kernel is the lacunary series Σc_k − c_{−1}, with non-ℓ² coefficients; the proof of R186.093 §5 (test against χc_m: ⟨k,χc_m⟩ → ½∫χ on one side, → 0 by Riemann–Lebesgue if k ∈ L²) adapts verbatim. ## 3. Reconciliation (the most important part)

**Chain of definitions.**
1. Π_S := Q₁ ∧ Q₂; Son_S(h) := Tr(ϑ(g)Π_Sϑ(g)^*), f = g*g^* (R186.009).
2. D: Q₁Q₂Q₁ = Π_S + Σλ_n²|ζ_n⟩⟨ζ_n|; hence Son_S = Tr ϑ(f)Q₁Q₂Q₁ − d_S(f).
3. Tr ϑ(f)Q₁Q₂Q₁ = Tr ϑ(f)Q₁ − Tr ϑ(f)Q₁P̂^SQ₁, and Thm C′ (R186.008): Tr ϑ(h)(P̂^S − P̂_∞) = Σ_k log2·2^{−k/2}(h(2^k)+h(2^{−k})) **exact**. But the relevant part is compressed by Q₁: Tr ϑQ₁P̂Q₁ ≠ Tr ϑP̂; the difference (cross PP̂, P̂P terms) is what CC (85)–(90) reorganize.
4. (84)_S (R186.009 §5): Son_S = W_∞(f) − **comb₂(f)** + ∫f(ρ⁻¹)ε′_S d*ρ, with ε′_S = ε_∞ + κ_S, and for ρ ≥ 1, ε_S(ρ) = Σλ_n(1−λ_n²)^{−1/2}⟨ξ_n|ϑ(ρ⁻¹)ζ_n⟩ (the diagonal d_S cancels, Prop. C). κ_S is **defined by difference** (R186.095 M2).
5. E_S = Son_S − ∫c_hε′_S (semilocal version of CC (83)), so E_S = W_∞ − comb₂ (plus the local term), and R′_S = −C_{ε′_S}.

**(a) Is there a contradiction?** No. The comb (with its atoms at ρ = 2^{±k}, i.e. x = ±k log 2, weight log2·2^{−k/2}) appears **as an explicit term** in (84)_S. By construction, ε_S is what remains after it has been subtracted. If the identity (84)_S holds (CONDITIONAL on CV_S), then Son_S − Son_∞ = −comb₂ + ∫f(ρ⁻¹)(ε′_S − ε′_∞)d*ρ: the atom is in the first term and **should not** appear in ε_S − ε_∞. The numerical absence of an atom is a consistency check of (84)_S, not a refutation of R183_025/R183_029 (3.1) nor of the editor's note R183_032 ("Son_S − Son_∞ = full prime term + ∫c_h δε_p with δε_p ∈ L²_loc"): R186.011 is exactly the numerical confirmation of that reading, with δε_p = ε_S − ε_∞ having a logarithmic singularity (L²_loc but not L^∞) at the dyadics. The minus sign of ε in E_S does not move the atom: the atom was never in ε. "Coexistence" is the correct answer: linear atom (comb) + nonlinear peak (in ε_S), at the same point ρ = 2^j.
Caveat: the memo says "the prime comb is, as R186.009 Δ states, in the linear part." To be precise: the comb comes from the phase of F_S relative to F_∞ (C′) and (84)_S extracts it from Tr ϑQ₁Q₂Q₁; that the extraction leaves **exactly** the series ε_S as the remainder is the content of (84)_S/Δ, still CONDITIONAL. The only thing proved unconditionally is C′ on 1 − P̂ with no compressions.

**(b) Is the two-boundary model wrong?** No; it computes a different quantity in a different model. R183_025 Thm 2 computes Tr(M_c(Π_S − Π_{[0,L]})) in a model with F_S replaced by a pure translation (hard Fourier boundary, K′ = L²[0,L+a]) and c of support < L; its error is O(ε^{2⌊L/a⌋}), and R183_029 Prop 3.3 shows that as a **density** the model does have first-order corrections localized at t ∈ (L, L+2a] that cancel against c_h of support < L. The model does not have the lacunary structure of F_S = Σ2c_k − c_{k−1} that produces the quasi-covariance Q; hence it cannot see the Div2 peak. What R186.011 adds does not contradict Thm 2: it indicates that the "correction" of the real Sonin is not a uniform O(ε^{2L/a}) but has singularities (pointwise infinite) **at the same positions as the atoms**. Consequence for the record: any statement of the type "Son_S − Son_∞ = exact prime term + small/continuous error" (R183_025 Cor 2.1 transferred to the real Sonin) must be read as "+ remainder in L²_loc (CONDITIONAL on L2_S), with peaks at 2^j"; not "+ continuous remainder".

**(c) Does Div2 kill the decomposition E_S = Son_S − ∫c_hε_S as a tool?** No.
- The identity only needs ε_S ∈ L¹_loc(ℝ*₊) **as an L¹_loc limit of the partial sums** (or as an order-0 distribution); divergence at {2^j} (measure zero) is irrelevant. But note: what must be proved is convergence of the series in L¹_loc (or L²_loc), not pointwise convergence at a generic ρ; neither has been proved (Prof is NUMERICAL; §4 "Littlewood–Paley" OPEN).
- R′_S(h) = −∫c_h ε′_S as a form on L²(I_T): kernel (x,y) ↦ ε′_S(e^{|x−y|}) (plus symmetrization); ‖·‖²_HS ≤ 2T·‖ε′_S‖²_{L²(−2T,2T)} (in x = log ρ). Log⁺(1/|ρ−2^j|) ∈ L²_loc. Therefore:

**Precise statement.** If (L2_S) holds — i.e., the partial sums of (84)_S converge in L²([e^{−2T},e^{2T}], d*ρ) to ε_S, and the identity (84)_S holds with that limit —, then R′_S is Hilbert–Schmidt on L²(I_T) and E_S = (nonnegative form Son_S) − (HS form), which is the form (M) of phase 183. **(M) is thereby reduced to L2_S, CONDITIONAL.** "Logarithmic structure" is not enough: convergence in L² of the series is needed (near-orthogonality between levels k, uniform in ρ near 2^j), plus the trace/series interchange of CC (85)–(90) in that topology. The form β_j log⁺ + O_T(1) of L2_S(ii) is stronger than necessary; I suggest stating L2_S as "convergence in L²_loc" and leaving the logarithmic asymptotic as NUMERICAL.
- Note: ε_S ∈ L^p_loc ∀p < ∞ (asserted in the Verdict of 011) is NUMERICAL, not PROVED.
