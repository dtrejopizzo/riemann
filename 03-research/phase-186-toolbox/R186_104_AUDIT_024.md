## 0. One-line verdict

The numbers of 024 reproduce and the decomposition 𝒱_k⊕E_k is correct, but the proposed proof of (L1) is broken in two of its three pieces: the prime bound 𝒜_T/2 "PROVED" is false for T > log 2 (the exact norm of the 2-term is v₂/√2, not v₂/2) and ‖𝒦₁‖_{𝒱_k} is not ≈ 0.74 but **π** in the continuum (the Tate condition has finite codimension and does not touch the essential Hilbert–Carleman spectrum); moreover the "stability with k ≍ T" is an artifact: 𝒱_k is a trivially coercive high-pass subspace and (L2) is W ≥ 0 rewritten in a piecewise-exponential basis with all of thm:margin's margin.

## 4. FATAL / MAJOR / MINOR

- **FATAL (for the proof proposal of §3, not for the tables):** 5b — ‖𝒦₁‖_{𝒱_k} = π, not ≈ 0.74; piece (b) is false and the "Hilbert operator with Tate" does not reduce the norm (finite codimension). The (L1) route via operator norms needs k ≈ 160 at ½log5 and a bound relative to the form (𝒦₁ ≤ ε·diag + C), which is not available.
- **MAJOR:** 3b — 𝒜_T/2 is not a proved bound for T > log 2 (correct value: 1.674); "positive across the whole grid with k = 13, margin 0.056" FALSE (−0.147 with the correct primes; much worse with 5b). "Cauchy–Schwarz" gives 𝒜_T.
- **MAJOR:** 6 — the stability with k ≍ T is a high-pass artifact; (L2) is W ≥ 0 rewritten and inherits thm:margin with no new structure; 024 presents it as a "finite window goal".
- **MINOR:** 4b (constancy of ‖𝒦₁‖ measured at fixed N); notation λ_W → λ_A; "K₁ ≤ 2e^{−5u/2}/(1−e^{−2u})" is tautological; §1.5 "not written out" (no content); k exponential in T already acknowledged by 024 but with unrealistic constants (τ₀ ≈ e^{−𝒜_T/2} should be e^{−π−𝒜_T/2−…}); T = 0.85 with N = 12 is already at the double-precision floor (3·10⁻¹⁴ vs 2.8·10⁻¹² with N = 10): nothing beyond 0.8, as 024 states.
