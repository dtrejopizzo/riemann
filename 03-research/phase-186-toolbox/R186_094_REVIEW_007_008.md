## MAJOR

**M1 (008, row (Δ) and §4 "Answers (Δ)").** (Δ) in R183.027 §6 is a statement about ρ_S − ρ_∞ with ρ_S the diagonal density of the **Sonin projection** Π_S (R183.021 §, ρ_S(s) = sup_{F∈V_∞}…), i.e., about the intersection (1−P)∧(1−P̂^S). C′ only treats the linear part 1−P̂^S. R183.027 already said "(Δ) holds for part of a condition" (via R183.015); what was open was exactly the overlap. Therefore C′ does **not** prove (Δ); at most it confirms, with a complete proof, the part-of-one-condition case. The editor's note R183_032 places (Δ) under (M) (CONDITIONAL). Downgrade: row (Δ) PROVED → "linear part PROVED; (Δ) for Π_S CONDITIONAL on (M) / OPEN", consistent with the Π row of the table itself (which the memo does mark OPEN: there is an internal contradiction between rows (Δ) and Π).

**M2 (008, novelty of C/C′).** (i) C: F_S = F_∞E^*E⁻¹ is already in R186.007 §1; the only new part is deriving the argument of (1−qe^{ict})/(1−qe^{−ict}): one line. (ii) C′: the Lemma in §4 is the dual-variable version of R183.015 (Hardy twisted by a unimodular u: Tr = −(2π)^{−1}∫|φ|²θ′); here the hard cutoff P_a is the Hardy projection in the other variable and U is a function of D. It is also the classical trace formula for Toeplitz/phase commutators (Widom/Kreĭn type) and reproduces the local-at-p term of Connes's semilocal formula (Selecta 1999), where that term is exact; Connes's o(1) comes from P̂P, not from the local term. The contrast "unlike Connes, with no o(1)" compares different objects and must be withdrawn or reworded. Required: cite R183.015 and the classical source; do not present it as a new result.
