### 1.1 Re-derivation of Lemma 3 (deflated Cavalieri) — PROVED, with two refinements

For ‖F‖ = 1 and μ_F(A) = (2π)⁻¹∫_A|F̂|² (probability, eq:form): Pos(F) = ∫σ₊dμ_F = ∫₀^∞ μ_F({σ₊ > s})ds (layers, σ₊ ≥ 0) and {σ₊ > s} = ℝ∖L_s with L_s = {σ_T ≤ s} (s ≥ 0). For F ⊥ V: μ_F(L_s) = ⟨F,S_{L_s}F⟩ = ⟨F,S̃_{L_s}F⟩ ≤ λ_max(S̃_{L_s}) ≤ ‖S̃_{L_s}‖ =: μ̄(s), where S̃_{L_s} = S_{L_s} − Σ_iΣ_±λ₀^{(i),±}|ψ_i^±⟩⟨ψ_i^±|. **Monotonicity of the layers:** since L_0 = Ū ⊇ E_i for every i, the deflation is the **same** for every s ≥ 0, so S̃_{L_s} is non-decreasing in s and so is μ̄; moreover S̃_{L_s} ⪰ S_{L_s} − Σ_i S_{E_i} + Σ_i(S_{E_i} − Σ_±λ₀P_i^±) ⪰ 0 (the E_i are disjoint and ⊂ L_s; each S_{E_i} − Σ_±λ₀^{(i),±}P_i^± ⪰ 0 because ψ_i^± are eigenvectors of S_{E_i}). The right-hand Riemann sum lower-bounds the integral without needing μ̄ to be monotone: for s ≤ s_{k+1}, (1 − μ_F(L_s)) ≥ (1 − sup_W μ_F(L_{s_{k+1}}))⁺ ≥ (1 − μ̄(s_{k+1}))⁺. ∎ **Formula Pos_lb = ∫(1 − μ̄)⁺ds: valid lower bound of ⟨Pos F,F⟩ on W.**
