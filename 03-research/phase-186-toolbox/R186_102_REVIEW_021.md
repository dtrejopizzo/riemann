## MAJOR
**M1 (§1, §5: figures ΔC(s₀) ≈ 0.150 and E_∞(s₀) ≈ 0.154 mix vectors).** They are obtained as C_S(s₀^S) − C_∞(s₀^∞) and
min Son_∞ − C_∞(s₀^∞), i.e. evaluating the ∞ part at s₀^∞ rather than at s₀. Evaluated consistently at the same s₀ (=s₀^S):
ΔC(s₀) = 0.1355 / 0.1335, E_∞(s₀) = 0.1406 / 0.1385, ΔSon(s₀) = −0.0008 (M = 16/32). The margin at s₀ (≈ 3.5%) survives, but
the quoted figures are incorrect as defined ("ΔC(s₀) = C_S − C_∞ at s₀"). Correct this.
**M2 (§5: "the margin is 3%").** The margin relevant to the proposed inequality is the supremum, not the value at s₀:
sup_{𝒫_T}(E_∞ − E_S)/E_∞ = 0.9930 (M=16), 0.9936 (M=32), and it grows with M. Real margin ≈ 0.65% and decreasing with
resolution (min E_S: 1.05e-3 → 0.97e-3). State the margin at the maximizer, with its trend in M.
Also, make explicit (the memo only half-states it): the inequality of §5 is *identically* E_S ≻ 0, because
(ΔC − ΔSon)(h) = −(E_S − E_∞)(h) = −(prime-2 term)(h); the ratio is "−prime term / E_∞ < 1", with no new content.
The memo calls it a "restatement" (correct); but the phrase "useful only if ΔC and ΔSon are bounded separately" is
misleading: ΔSon(s₀) ≈ −0.0008 ≪ ΔC, so separating gains nothing in the critical direction; all the difficulty is in ΔC.

## MINOR
**m1 (§3.1, "PROVED" monotonicity).** Correct in the continuum problem: 𝒫_T ⊂ 𝒫_{T′} by extension by zero (cp is the same
functional, Son_S(h) depends only on h with λ = 1 and Π_S fixed), hence λ_min non-increasing — PROVED. But (i) the discrete
spaces (polynomial basis in u = y/T with factor (1−u²)²) are **not** nested, so the observed numerical monotonicity is not a
consequence of the theorem; (ii) "strictly decreasing" is NUMERICAL. The NO-GO of §3 only needs non-constancy, which is
NUMERICAL (the trivial theorem does not give it). Correct label for the item: PROVED (non-increasing) + NUMERICAL (non-constant).
**m2 (stability in M).** "≤ 4e-3" holds, but there is a monotonic drift: ρ*(½log3) = 0.9408, 0.9436, 0.9447 (M = 16/24/32), and
λ_min, min E_S decrease. The M → ∞ limit is not fixed to the 1e-3 level; closeness to 1 must be read with that drift in mind.
**m3 (T ≤ 0.35).** With the S grid, ρ* < 0 (C ≤ 0 throughout 𝒫_T); with RS, ρ* > 0. The sign of ρ* (presence or absence of a
direction with C > 0) is grid-dependent there, not just its value. State this.
**m4 (§4, "a 0.4% mixture suffices").** Neither verified nor necessary; the subspace test already shows the jump comes from
s₂ and s₄ (k=3: +0.03, k=5: +0.16). With ⟨u,s₀⟩ = 0.998 the angle is ≈ 6%, not 0.4%; the arithmetic is consistent (sin²α·Son(w) ~ Son(s₀)).
Reword.
**m5 (§2, "archimedean s₀").** Overlap ≥ 0.989 confirmed in `r021_24.txt` via the vector file; it is NUMERICAL and
depends on Son_∞ = E_∞ + C_∞ with ε_∞ from grid A (not converged: R186.101 MAJOR-1). Correct label.
**m6 (NO-GO λ_min = 1 − λ₂²).** Points 2 and 3 are correct; point 3 is "structural" with no proof — label it heuristic.
