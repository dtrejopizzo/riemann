## A. R186_003 — Falsifier F1

### A.1 Model (PROVED, confirmed against the source)

- **Cutoff P^S_λ.** CCM Prop groundstate (ii) defines P^S_λ as the subspace of L²(X_S)^{K_S} supported in {|x| ≤ λ}.
 The complement of C_S in X_S has measure zero [l. 731]. The fundamental domain is F = R_S^* × ℝ_+^*, and on (y, u) with
 y ∈ ℤ₂^* the modulus equals |y|₂·u = u. Hence P^S_λ = 1_{(0,λ]}(u) **exactly**: the 2-adic factor does not alter the cutoff.
 Independent check: a ℤ₂^*-invariant function on (ℝ×ℚ₂)/{±2^n} depends on (|x_∞|, |x_2|₂) modulo
 (|x|, |y|) ~ (2|x|, |y|/2), and the only invariant of the generic orbit is u = |x||y|₂. The K_S-invariant functions
 are, then, functions of the modulus (up to measure zero).
- **Measure.** w_S η_S(f)(u) = u^{1/2}Σ_{k≥0} f(2^k u) in L²(ℝ_+^*, d^*u) [eq. (essl)]. Thus ‖·‖² = ∫ h² du with
 h(u) = Σ_k f(2^k u). With f = e^{−πx²} this gives h_S = Σ_{n≥0} e^{−π4^n u²} (the factor 2^{1/4} is irrelevant). Confirmed.
- **Operators.** 𝔰 = −i(H+½) acts only on the archimedean variable, and 𝐖_{λ,S} = (H+½)² + λ²N_S [l. 843] = −𝔰² + λ²N_S.
 Also −𝔰² = (u∂+½)² = ∂(u²∂) + ¼ (verified). e₁ = L h_S is orthogonal to h_S because L is antisymmetric in L²(du); e₂ is
 orthogonal to e₁ by parity.

### A.2 Green's lemma (PROVED)

- The boundary u = 0 is fine. h_S ~ log(1/u)/log4 plus a bounded log-periodic oscillation, and u h_S′ = O(1), likewise
 u(Lh_S)′. Then u²(f g′ − f′g) = u·f·(u g′) − … = O(u log(1/u)) → 0.
- The 𝔰^j ξ_S are in L², because their Mellin transform is s^j L_2(½−is)γ_∞(s), of rapid decay.
- **Logic of the criterion.** If P commutes with W in any standard sense (P D(W) ⊂ D(W) and WP = PW there, or
 equivalently with resolvents), then ⟨Pe_i, We_j⟩ = ⟨We_i, Pe_j⟩. Hence E_{ij} ≠ 0 implies non-commutation.
 The criterion is a valid necessary condition.
- **MINOR.** This step, "commutation ⟹ E_{ij} = 0", should be written out explicitly in the document.

### A.3 Archimedean check (PROVED, recomputed)

`f1_exact.py` and `rv_chk.py` give B(f₀, Lf₀) = −½(α − 8π)(4πu² − 1)e^{−2πu²}. The solution is α = 8π, consistent with
2π(4N+1). `f1.py` gives α* = 8π to 30 digits at λ = ¼, …, 2.

### A.4 NO-GO by exponent separation (PROVED)

- **Coefficient of e^{−5πu²}.** I have recomputed it independently: with α = 8π it equals **−12π(30π²u⁴ − 19πu² + 1)**.
 Confirmed.
- **Uniqueness of the exponents.** 4^n + 4^m (with n ≤ m) is written in base 4 uniquely: digits 1,1 or a single digit 2.
 Each exponent comes from a unique unordered pair. The first values are 2, 5, 8, 17, 20, 32, …, and 5 only comes from
 (0,1).
- **Independence with an infinite sum.** For u ≥ 1, the (n,m) term is bounded by C·64^{max}·u⁴e^{−π(4^n+4^m)u²}, which is
 summable and dominated. If Σ_c e^{−cu²}P_c ≡ 0, multiplying by e^{2πu²} and letting u → ∞ gives P_{2π} ≡ 0, hence α = 8π.
 Repeating the step with e^{5πu²} gives P_{5π} ≡ 0, which is false. The argument is valid.
- **Shifts.** The part contributed by 𝔰 + t is purely imaginary against the real part, so t = 0. aW + b only
 rescales E. Confirmed.

**M3.1 (scope MAJOR, not validity).** "For EVERY affine normalization" should read as **(α, β) independent of λ**.
The table of α*(λ) shows that, for each fixed λ, there exists α(λ) that annihilates E₀₁. With α depending on λ, the NO-GO of §3 does not
apply, and only §4 remains, which is NUMERICAL. The proposed wording: "For constants (a, b, α, β, t), E₀₁(λ) is analytic
in λ > 0 and not identically zero; hence [𝐖_{λ,S}, P^S_λ] ≠ 0 for every λ outside a discrete set." This version
(discrete, not "for every λ") is the one that is proved. The isolated zeros of F(λ) are **not** excluded.

**m3.2 (MINOR).** §4 (arbitrary c(N)) is well-labeled NUMERICAL. I have reproduced the defects 0.18080 and 0.00600, and in
the archimedean case < 10^{−27}. The consistency logic d₀₂ = d₀₁ + d₁₂ is correct.

**m3.3 (MINOR).** "No affine renormalization fixes it" does not cover substituting −𝔰² by another function of 𝔰, nor the
metaplectic candidate. The document says so (§5, point 5). Correct.

**Verdict R186_003:** items 1–4 PROVED (item 4 with the M3.1 wording), item 5 NUMERICAL, item 6 OPEN.
**Ready as a tool.**
