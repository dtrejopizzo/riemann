## Explicit answers

1. **Is D3 PROVED?** Yes, **from the record, without using the Frullani step**. Independent derivation:
 (i) S(λ,λ) = ϑ(λ)S(1,λ²) via a unitary that commutes with ϑ(f) (R183_029 l.144–145: S(α,β) ≅ S(cα,β/c), weighted traces depend only on αβ; with c = 1/λ, αβ = λ² = 1·b). Hence Tr ϑ(f)Π_{S(λ,λ)} = Tr ϑ(f)Π_{S(1,λ²)}.
 (ii) CC Thm 4.7 (83): Tr ϑ(f)Π_{S(1,1)} = W_∞(f) + ∫f(ρ⁻¹)ε(ρ)d*ρ, ∀f ∈ C_c^∞ (CITED).
 (iii) (Λ) with b = λ² ∈ (0,1]: Tr ϑ(f)(Π_{S(1,b)} − Π_{S(1,1)}) = log(1/b)f(1) + ∫fε′_b (PROVED, R183_029 Thm A, confirmed in R183_032).
 Adding up: Tr ϑ(f)Π_{S(λ,λ)} = W_∞(f) + 2log(1/λ)f(1) + ∫f(ε + ε′_{λ²}). This is D3 with ε_λ := ε_1 + ε′_{λ²}. (The f(ρ) / f(ρ⁻¹) discrepancy between R183_029 and CC is harmless: ε′_b can be symmetrized, and for f = g*g^* the traces are real.) The requested "S(λ,λ) vs S(1,λ²)" consistency check: **verified**, log(1/b) = 2log(1/λ).
 Restrictions: (a) only λ ∈ (0,1] (b ≤ 1 in (Λ)); λ > 1 is not covered. (b) The *identification* of ε_λ = ε_1 + ε′_{λ²} with the Halmos series of the pair (P_1, P̂_b) (what `cvl.py` computes) is a separate statement: it follows from the Halmos decomposition at level b (R186_017 §1(b), R186.014 §5, valid for any pair) by subtracting the identity: PROVED to the extent that R186.014 §5 is. (c) Step (c) of R186_017 (a/c interchange of Frullani type with c_0 = max(1,e^{−a}) depending on a, "O(c^{−2})" not uniform) **is not justified** as written: it is ARGUED; it remains a redundant second derivation, corroborated by Ω_b = δ_b − δ_1 to 1e-16 (rerun: ω ∈ {0, ±1.1e-16, 2.2e-16} for a ∈ [0,1.09]).
 **Label for D3: PROVED (via (i)–(iii)) for 0 < λ ≤ 1.** In R186_017, replace the proof in §1(c) with (i)–(iii), or downgrade §1(c) to "alternative verification".

## MAJOR

**MAJOR-1 (R186_016 A(i), NO-GO "−C_{ε_∞} ≥ 0 in L²(h), n₋ = 0").** Three defects:
- It is a result **at λ = ½**, presented as a property of the "archimedean remainder". In 016's own §2 table, at λ = 1 (CC (83)'s level): n₋(−C_{ε_∞^{(1)}}) = **1** (eigenvalue −0.032) at ½log3. That is exactly what R183_026 predicts (ε̂ > 0 at low frequency ⇒ −C negative at low frequency): the objection is correct and the data confirming it is in the memo itself.
- After D3, the remainder at level ½ is R^{(½)} = 2log2‖h‖² + ∫c_hε^{(½)} (E = Son − R). Rerun: min(2log2 + C_{ε^{(½)}}) ≥ +1.31 at ½log3; hence −R^{(½)} is **negative definite** (n₋ = dim 𝒫_T), not "n₋ = 0". The NO-GO compares the wrong piece.
- That n₋ = 0 at λ = ½ with ε̂_trunc(0) = 1.64 > 0 does not contradict R183_026: the form on 𝒫_T only sees ε truncated to |a| ≤ 2T, Tate's restriction `cp` removes the ~0-frequency mode, and ε^{(½)} ≠ ε^{(1)}. But neither does it prove anything general.
- T = 0.8: **not verifiable** with the existing grids (truncated to |a| ≤ 1.098 < 1.6). Not checked.
Correct label: **A(i) WITHDRAWN** (not a NO-GO). The question "n₋ ≤ 1 with a low-frequency vector" reopens at λ = 1, where the archimedean part gives n₋ = 1; the semilocal case at λ = 1 is blocked by L1 (numerically OPEN). The editor's note of 016 must be extended to A(i) and A(ii).

**MAJOR-2 (R186_015 §5 and row (84)_S).** "For every finite S ∋ ∞ and T > 0: E_S = Son_S − ∫c_hε_S" and "(84)_S for CC's λ": correct only at λ = 1. In the model u with generic P = 1_{(0,λ]}, 2log(1/λ)‖h‖² must be added (D3). The constants of §5 (‖A_S‖, ‖κ_S‖) depend on λ and must be indexed by λ.

**MAJOR-3 (R186_015 row S(T), §5).** Labeled ARGUED, but it is heuristic: (a) Poisson summation over ℤ is only legitimate for X ≤ e^{2T}; beyond that Ψ(X,e^{2T}) drops (Dickman) and the "cutoff at scale e^{−2T}" is not derived; (b) for S(T) the range r ~ e^{2T}/T grows and the boundary lines (v_p = −1, coefficients −1/p, alternating sign) are no longer negligible compared with the interior part (the rank r−1 argument stops giving a logarithmic advantage as r → ∞); (c) rationals a/b with b ≤ e^{2T} are dense and their interaction with the integer singularities may cancel; (d) the bound "‖κ_{S(T)}‖ ≳ e^{3T}/T²" is presented as a lower bound but is an order of magnitude with no control of cancellations. The arithmetic is internally consistent (c_T⁴·Σ_{n≤e^{2T}}n·e^{2T} ~ e^{6T}/T⁴, w_n ≈ λc_T²n^{1/2}/4 consistent with Poisson Σ_ℤS(Nt) = 1/(2|t|)). **Label: CONJECTURAL (heuristic)**; in Paper 46 not as a "Remark" but as an open question.

## MINOR

- **015 NHS (r ≥ 2).** "The other frequencies N ≠ N_m give o(1)" is not enough: ⟨S⟩^± is denser and there are infinitely many frequencies; one needs |N − N_m| → ∞ uniformly for N ∈ ⟨S⟩^±∖{N_m} with a_N ≠ 0 (bounded denominators). It is true (gaps between S-units grow: Størmer/Tijdeman, CITED) and with smooth χ the dominated sum → 0. One-line patch; PROVED after the patch.
- **015 Lac_r.** Σ_{N≤1/|t|}(Nt)² ≤ C_S is false for r ≥ 2: the block count gives Σ_k e^{−2k}(log X)^{r−1} = O((log 1/|t|)^{r−1}). This is absorbed into the already-stated error O(1 + log^{r−1}): the lemma stands as PROVED with that correction.
- **015 boundary lines.** "Order ≤ log^r" is conservative: families with some v_p = −1 have rank ≤ r−1 (v_p fixed), contributing ≤ log^{r−1}. This **reinforces** β_q > 0 as the leading coefficient (it cannot be cancelled by the boundary) and hence d_S ∉ L^∞_loc. Write it out.
- **015 β_q.** Power of 2 unverified (the memo admits this); ARGUED for the constant, PROVED for the order log^r.
- **017 D3 §1(c).** See answer 1(c); also the case b > 1 ("symmetric with flipped signs") is not worked out.
- **017 C1′.** It is not a test of the constant: it only requires a coefficient ≥ 0.19; the constant is fixed by derivation (i)–(iii), not by the numbers.
- **017 D2 / R183_026 Prop 2.1.** With E = (1/2π)∫|ĥ|²ε̂ and W = (1/2π)∫m|ĥ|², the symbol of Son is m + ε̂, not m/2π + ε̂: Prop 2.1 should read ε̂ ≥ −m. The set {m < 0} = {|s| < 6.29} does not change, so "ε̂ > 0 for |s| < s_m" survives; the quantitative constant of the lower bound changes by a factor of 2π.

## Verdict
