## MAJOR-1 (dependence on CC Thm 4.7; memo's flag (4))
The claim that "the relative statement Δ and all corollaries on ε_S, κ_S, R′_S are independent of this point" is false for R′_S. R′_S is defined with respect to the true Weil distribution W_S = W_∞ − comb₂; by Δ, R′_S = R′_∞ − C_{κ_S}, and R′_∞ = −C_{ε_∞} is exactly CC Thm 4.7 at level λ (here λ = ½). What is indeed independent: Δ, ε_S = d_S as the density of E_S, κ_S ∈ L^p_loc.
