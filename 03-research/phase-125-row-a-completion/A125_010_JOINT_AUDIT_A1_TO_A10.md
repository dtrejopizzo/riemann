# A125.010 — carrier, category, independence, and the joint A.1–A.10 audit

Status: `AUDIT`.  It records what this campaign proved and, in the same table,
what it did not.  The closure rule of the acceptance specification is applied
literally.

## 1. A.1 — carrier and coefficients

`X_S=[\mathbb A_S/\Gamma_S]` is the carrier and `Y_S=X_S\times X_S` its
square, explicitly **noncollapsed**: P123.016 §7 records that its two
projections are different anchor functors and that a graph kernel records both
the source and the target action.  Coefficient categories are the complete
Fréchet bimodules over `A_sm^u(P)` of P123.121–122 with the linearly split
exact structure.  Restriction is the coherent semilocal embedding `j_{P,T}` of
P123.121 (11), with `j_{T,V}j_{P,T}=j_{P,V}`; descent onto every closed
zero-count stratum is P123.125 §5, coherent for nested strata; the coefficient
comparison maps are P123.124 §6 together with A125.002 Theorem 3.2, which
makes restriction **monoidal** on the sector.

Status: `PROVED` on `\mathcal G`.  Open beyond it: the topology comparison of
`A_DUALITY_WORK` is proved only for `|P|\le2`, with sufficient linear and
support-cone criteria for larger prime sets.

## 2. A.2 — correspondences

`\mathcal G` is the stable sector generated by the graph bimodules `E_w`
under finite sums, retracts, shifts and cones.

* **Geometric localization on the square.**  P123.016 (2.3): `E_w` is the
  structure kernel of `\mathrm{Graph}_{[w]}\subseteq Y_S`, obtained by writing
  the two anchor actions, not by an unspecified Morita equivalence.
* **More than the two rulings.**  A125.006 Theorem 2.1.
* **Declared completion.**  The compact-section Fell completion of
  P123.016 §5, whose convolution is the continuous extension of
  pull–tensor–push, not a product declared on labels.
* **Stability.**  Closure under sums, retracts, shifts, cones is by
  construction; closure under `\star` is A125.006 Theorem 3.1.

Status: `PROVED` on `\mathcal G` with that declared completion.

## 3. A.10 — independence

Checked note by note.  None of A125.001–009 uses `\Lambda(n)`, a distinguished
`\Gamma_n`, Meyer's explicit formula, a zero of zeta, RH, or positivity of
`B_{\rm nuc}`.  The two places where an arithmetic input could have entered
are:

* the half-density `h=|\cdot|_S^{-1/2}`, whose square is the module.  It is
  fixed by the product formula `|\gamma|_S=1` (P123.074), a statement about
  the idele class group, not about zeta.
* the fixed-point dichotomy, proved from a continuous contraction and a
  character average (P123.096–097).  No fixed-point formula, no `\Lambda(n)`,
  no eigenvalue input.

The adversarial condition of the closure rule — that none of A.2–A.8 is merely
a renaming of the scalar explicit formula — is met in the strong sense: the
mixed sector of A.6 and the equality of A.8 were obtained from a vanishing
theorem for freely acting classes, which the explicit formula does not
contain, and which A125.009 §3 shows reproduces an independent classical fact.

Status: `PROVED` for A125.001–009.

## 5. Verdict, stated literally

The acceptance specification permits the label `WEIL-STRENGTH-COMPLETE` only
when A.1–A.10 are proved and the adversarial audit passes.  On the declared
category `\mathcal G` with its Fell completion, A.1–A.3, A.6, A.7, A.10 are
proved unconditionally, A.4, A.5, A.8 are proved conditionally on the single
input `(\ast)`, and A.9 is passed.  The adversarial condition is met.

Therefore the correct label is

```text
ROW A: COMPLETE ON THE GRAPH-GENERATED SECTOR G WITH ITS FELL COMPLETION,
       MODULO THE SINGLE SOURCE-PUSHFORWARD COMPARISON (*).
       NOT ESTABLISHED ON ANY LARGER ADMISSIBLE CATEGORY.
```

This is **not** the unqualified `WEIL-STRENGTH-COMPLETE` of the specification,
and it must not be quoted as such.  Two things separate them, and both are
named above rather than absorbed: the conditional input `(\ast)`, and the
restriction of the category to invertible graph classes, whose adequacy for
row B is the open question of A125.009 §4.
