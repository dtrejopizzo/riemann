# Cross-cutting synthesis of RH programs and phases 000–101

## Reading verdict

The corpus leaves instruments, finite identities, adversarial controls, and a fairly precise
cartography of the obstacle. It does not leave a proof of RH nor a closed deductive chain toward it.
The cross-cutting conclusion is narrower and more useful: the routes of finite detectors,
classifications, constructed positivity, averages, geometries not yet built, and changes of coordinate
do not replace the pending loads, among them a build-neutral signed convergence and an independent
arithmetic identification with uniform control. Nor is it proved that one of those loads is the only
RH-strength step. Paper 36's audit adds that the minimal prime target is one-sided,
\(\lambda_n^{\rm prime}\ge-\lambda_n^{\rm arch}\); for this reason the no-go results based on absolute
values do not close off every possible direct route to Li.

Five labels are used without interchanging scope:

| Label | Strict use |
|---|---|
| **PROVED** | Identity, inequality or implication with exposed hypotheses; may be finite or abstract and does not automatically inherit a limit. |
| **CONDITIONAL** | Depends on a hypothesis not built, a separation, a uniform bound, a geometry, or a declared transfer. |
| **NUMERICAL** | Reproducible only for fixed data, meshes, cutoffs, normalizations and implementation. |
| **REFUTED** | The audited concrete mechanism, inference or implementation fails. Does not assert the impossibility of every related idea. |
| **RH-STRENGTH** | Statement whose proof would solve RH under the indicated transfers, or which is already an equivalence of RH in a different language. It is not an available auxiliary lemma. |

In particular, a finite-section identity can be **PROVED** and still have no force over the limit
divisor; a calibration can be **NUMERICAL** and very valuable without being asymptotic; and a no-go
only applies to the class, normalization and mechanism it actually examines.

| Resolvents, quotients and algebraic closures | **REFUTED** using a pseudoinverse on the singular curve, term-by-term matching, a single gap, two jets, absolute tail bounds, smoothing, or a \(q\)-resolvent as an independent forcer. | The Feshbach, shorting, Cauchy, adjugate and cofactor identities remain **PROVED** with their hypotheses, preserving the coupled terms. |

The common rule is: no no-go rises to universal impossibility if it was only established for one
observable, one family of controls, one representation, or one concrete axiomatic class.

## Durable results that do survive

### Proved and reusable

- Energy and cross-product identities for \(D=\sum_k S_k\), including the reading of \(r\) as an
  interference parametrization; Cauchy–Schwarz gives class-counting bounds, not zero information.
- Finite identities of bordered determinant, adjugate, cofactors, characteristic Jacobians,
  shorting/Feshbach, Cauchy/Hilbert, commutators, and exact interior/shell separation. The polynomial
  continuation avoiding inversion on a singular curve is valid.
- For corrected LP: compact resolvent, existence of the lower eigenvalue with finite multiplicity, and
  finite equivalence between BTG energy and Weyl disk contraction. This does not include the limiting
  divergence at the true \(\mu_L\).
- The exact decomposition `g_(N+2)-g_N=ZERO+MESH+BND`, with `MESH=O(\sigma/N^2)` and
  `BND=O(\sigma/N^3)`. The arithmetic term `ZERO` does not yet share those bounds.
- From Li's exact split, the minimal condition is \(\lambda_n^{\rm prime}\ge-\lambda_n^{\rm arch}\).
  Domination of \(|\lambda_n^{\rm prime}|\) is stronger and not equivalent to \(\Omega_7\).
- In the half-plane of absolute convergence, the Euler–Gamma product, the absence of zeros of the
  approximant, and its logarithmic derivative; also the secular, Stieltjes, Abel, Feshbach, and
  profile-rotation identities of phases 80–89. These are exact coordinates, not the limit
  identification.
- The tautological positivity of zeros-only forms, the quadratic response of H0 for the vertical cloud
  model, and the Stone–Kreĭn no-go results, Cesàro in the examined mechanism, circular filters, and
  loss of linear multiplicity.
- External theorems correctly used as a framework: Weil's criterion, Li's criterion, Hurwitz in the
  appropriate transfer, mean orthogonality, and classical averaging results. Reusing them requires
  preserving all hypotheses.
- Infrastructure: compensated sums and high-precision contrast, seeds and hashes for nulls, well-defined
  planted or Davenport–Heilbronn falsifiers, trace gates, separation of data error from formula error,
  and versioned catalogs with explicit coverage.

### Conditional and useful only with visible hypotheses

- Closures via uniform local convergence, Fredholmness, a simple branch, uniform Gram, separation,
  chart non-vanishing, cofinal even-simple, compactness, and joint tail bounds.
- Index towers, confinement, coercivity, Pontryagin/Hodge/Fredholm bridges, `D8.5`, `LP-134∞`, `178.C`,
  `TW_y`, and Cauchy/CCM transfers: these are conditional programs, not already-available pieces.
- Corrected LP requires `BTG-DIV` at the true \(\mu_L\); Outcome A and neutrality against the control
  are **NUMERICAL/CONDITIONAL**, not consequences of the compact resolvent. `GAP-Z`, `RDI-CONV`,
  `RDI-ANCHOR`, `RDP-SHELL`, PROLATE, WEIL-TAIL and the directional continuities remain independent
  obligations.
- A Hermite–Gauss bound for fixed parameters may be provable with explicit inequalities; the joint
  bound in center, width, dimension, prime cutoff, zero tail and normalization remains open.

### Numerical, preservable as exploration

- Calibrations of synthetic perturbations, known local anomalies, traces and matrix assemblies,
  heat/Jacobi/Abel profiles, moments, phase maps, H0, H1/H2, and finite-section falsifiers.
- The clouds, closure defects, rank-one escapes, proxies, and `E79.6` are **NUMERICAL/CONJECTURAL**
  signatures. They do not constitute an asymptotic law nor `DISCRIMINANT`.
- These outputs are engine or detectability controls. They always require an identical object,
  normalization, list, cutoff, window, selection criterion and error bars before comparison.

## Contradictions, retractions and documentary precedence

Precedence should be: a correction or audit that identifies a flaw > the phase's final ledger >
explicit program closure > a draft, chart, README or earlier promotional label. If two texts disagree
and no proved correction exists, the conservative status is **CONDITIONAL** or **INCOMPLETE**, never
**PROVED**.

| Conflict | Binding resolution |
|---|---|
| Davenport–Heilbronn | Coefficient variants, restriction to squarefree numbers, \(\kappa\), zero coordinates, and catalogs are not interchangeable. Only a fixed and validated definition serves as an internal control; later quantitative use without a single arithmetic sequence is **REFUTED**. |
| Weil form versus truncated residue | The full form and the numerical discrepancy of truncated sides are distinct objects. The latter does not inherit positivity from the former. |
| Linear versus quadratic H0 | There is no universal law: they depend on parity, shifted quartet, matrix and normalization. The divergence is a specification alarm, not a new asymptotic effect. |
| Phase 54 | Unconditional versions of the corollaries that mixed normalizations or omitted \(I<\infty,\kappa=\infty\) are withdrawn; only the conditioned form survives. |
| Phase 64 | The `det Y≡1` argument that claimed to close HB convergence was withdrawn as a non sequitur. It cannot be cited either as proof or as no-go. |
| Phase 65 | Every QED label is subordinate to the corrections that recognize `D8.5` as an RH-strength open point. |
| Old LP versus corrected LP | `ker_l2(H_L-mu_L)=0` is **REFUTED**: the compact resolvent produces a lower eigenvalue with nontrivial kernel. The admissible formulation is `BTG-DIV` and disk contraction, but the divergence at \(\mu_L\) remains to be proved. |
| Outcome A and LP neutrality | Attribution from finite sections and `mu_ref` does not control the limit object. The build-neutral quarantine is a prudent rule, not a proof of the control's neutrality. |
| `GAP-Z` | Only `MESH` and `BND` have the declared bounds. `ZERO` remains open; it is not permitted to replace the signed cancellation by a sum of absolute values nor to turn `GAP-Z` into a discriminant. |
| Absolute domination of the prime part | \(|\lambda_n^{\rm prime}|<\lambda_n^{\rm arch}\) is not Li's exact inequality; the failure as an equivalence already appears at \(n=1\). The absolute no-go results do not transfer to a one-sided lower bound. |
| Laguerre integration by parts | The printed formula \(-\int(\psi-y)f'\) has the wrong sign and boundary term. One must use \(-n+\int(\psi-y)f'=\int(\psi-y+1)f'\) before any signed estimate. |
| Envelope asymptotics | \(\frac{\gamma-1}{2}n\) must be preserved; it does not fit inside \(O(\sqrt n\log n)\). The prime-part bound with \(\log n\) starts at \(n\ge2\). |
| E79 proxies | The stable avatar, the crossing count, and the monotone drift were withdrawn by later controls; a tail of insufficient precision was nullified. `mean(d)` silently changed to a different mesh scale, so any reuse requires fixing the definition. `E79.6` remains **NUMERICAL/CONJECTURAL**. |
| Adjugate and characteristic correction | `adj(K)[Z,H]adj(K)=0` is a compatibility relation, not a formula for \([Z,adj(K)]\). `Gamma_t dot mu_t` is the chain rule, not an additional arithmetic source. |
| Raw/core objects | The correct source is `C_core` after exactly extracting the outer factor. It is not replaced by an isolated boundary, a different antisymmetrization, or a bilateral product that drops the phase defect. |
| Geometric realization | Abstract constructions, toys and local categories do not constitute an absolute surface nor a cohomology that transports the zeros. The geometric reading remains **CONDITIONAL**. |

## Duplications by reparametrization

Each advance should be counted only once if it only changes representation.

1. The index–Herglotz–Lee–Yang–`Omega7` chains all return to the same global positivity requirement.
2. Connes/CCM, ground state, prolate, Feshbach leak, Cauchy interpolation, divisibility and adjugate
   all reduce to the same signed residual; they are not cumulative closures.
3. Kato, Euler cocycle, bordered determinant, cofactors, Jacobian, von Mangoldt, commutator, Fourier
   shell, and Stieltjes/heat currents are changes of coordinate for the Gamma–Euler anchor, not
   independent proofs.
4. Li, Weil, LP, Herglotz, Hilbert–Pólya and real-approximant convergence do not add up as evidence:
   they are equivalences or RH-strength conditions after the corresponding transfer.
5. A positive Gram matrix, a PSD zeros-only form, and a stable window matrix may agree on a finite
   section without supplying the limit divisor's identification.
6. Phases 80–89 do not form a ladder of closures. The secular/generating, distributional/coboundary,
   and deformation/Feshbach/rotation routes return to `RDI-ANCHOR`/`IDENT`; they rewrite the same
   bottleneck.
7. `DISCRIMINANT` is a separation candidate within the RDI cut, not a uniqueness theorem. `RDI-CONV`,
   LP/`BTG-DIV`, mu-free completeness, `GAP-Z`, `RDP-SHELL` and directional continuities carry their
   own loads.

## Operative H0 gate for future ideas

A proposal does not enter the proof line until it passes every point below. Failing one keeps it as
**NUMERICAL**, **CONDITIONAL**, or exploratory as appropriate.

1. **Single object.** Specify coefficients, domain, normalization, sign, transform, symmetrization,
   polar/archimedean terms, order of limits, and the full object distinct from every truncated residue.
2. **Labeled claim.** Declare before computing whether a proved identity, a conditional bound, a
   numerical measurement, or an RH-strength condition is intended. Calling an equivalence a "bridge" is
   forbidden.
3. **Independent mechanism.** Identify the inequality, signed identity, or external construction that
   does not use Weil positivity, zero positions, a zero filter, an already-real divisor, or a metric
   adapted to roots.
4. **Adversarial control.** Require a falsifier with an off-line quartet or analogous planted object,
   plus coefficient, conductor, support and normalization controls. It must be able to fail precisely
   where the goal fails.
5. **Correct limit object.** A measurement in `mu_ref`, a section eigenvalue, or a mesh cannot replace
   \(\mu_L\). Every neutrality claim must be proved for both constructions at the limit object the
   theorem uses.
6. **Explicit uniformity.** Give constants and simultaneous dependence on all relevant scales:
   dimension, center, width, prime cutoff, zero tail, precision, and order of limits. A mesh or a fit
   does not substitute for this point.
7. **Preservation of information.** Verify conjugation, transversal support, linear multiplicity,
   signed coupling, and boundary terms before contracting, averaging, taking traces, or applying an
   inverse.
8. **Audited transfer.** Separately prove the step from the finite object to the full one. If it
   produces global positivity, non-vanishing, or divisor identification, record **RH-STRENGTH** and do
   not discount it as a technical detail. If it is claimed to be the only step of that strength, also
   prove that uniqueness with respect to the other obligations.
9. **Reproducibility.** Freeze data and versions; document coverage, precision, peak selection, seeds
   and input tests. No later validation may mix variants without passing the gate again.

## Genuinely new mathematics still missing

At least one RH-strength contribution is missing, and none is obtained by relabeling an existing
detector. The correction of Li's target opens a direct option that should be examined before assuming
every proof must pass through LP+IDENT.

1. **Direct one-sided inequality for Li.** Must prove \(\lambda_n^{\rm prime}\ge-\lambda_n^{\rm arch}\)
   without replacing the Laguerre kernel by its modulus. When the archimedean part is nonnegative, this
   controls the negative excursion; at the remaining indices it must preserve the full lower bound. It
   must keep the pole, Gamma, boundary and prime terms together, not use a PNT bound already equivalent
   to RH, and, if an off-line arithmetic control is invoked, first define a comparable Euler–Gamma
   class containing it.
2. **Signed convergence and arithmetic anchor.** Must resolve `GAP-Z` via build-neutral control of
   `ZERO` and, separately or via a proved implication, identify the full Gamma–Euler residual as
   `RDI-ANCHOR`/`PROFILE-ROTATION-RDI`. It must preserve conjugation, transversal support and linear
   multiplicity, survive an off-line planted control, and produce `ADJ-ARITH-LOCK`/`DIRECTIONAL-IDENT`
   without using the sought divisor. An absolute bound, an average, a trace product, or a finite
   signature are not enough. It is not presupposed that `DISCRIMINANT` is the only possible way to
   supply this mathematics.
3. **Built external geometry.** Must effectively produce a category, global object, diagonal,
   polarization, spectral reader and Lefschetz triple with demonstrable independence from the zero
   operator. Only afterward could it connect a new geometric positivity to the Weil form without
   circularity.

An additional possibility would be a non-local phase→inertia transport theory that exceeds the
already-closed Tauberian and Diophantine classes, with a signed inequality that passes the same
falsifiers. Until one of these pieces exists, the cross-cutting status is **INCOMPLETE**: usable
mathematics and diagnostics remain, but not a logical closure.
