### M1 = LF1 — the Bochner step (`𝔏` is not tempered)

**What was wrong.**  Round 1 wrote: "*`𝔏` is tempered because `ϖ`, `W_{fin}`,
`ω_∞` are (Paper 44 Definition–Theorem 3bis.8 for the last two) and `Π²`
preserves `S'(R)`*."  `ϖ(t) = 2\cosh(t/2)` grows exponentially; `W_{fin}`
carries mass `Λ(n)n^{−1/2}` at `t = ±\log n`, i.e. `≍ e^{|t|/2}|t|` per unit
length; and Paper 44 Def–Thm 3bis.8 constructs them as Radon measures against
`C_c^∞`, asserting no temperedness.  **The one hypothesis-check of the central
theorem of §3 was false.**

**Both halves of the repair are now in the paper.**

1. *`Πϖ = 0`, so the exponentially growing summand is not in `𝔏` at all.*
   `(−∂_t²+¼)e^{±t/2} = 0` and `ϖ = e^{t/2}+e^{−t/2}`, so

       𝔏 = Π²[ϖ − (W_fin + ω_∞)] = −Π²(W_fin + ω_∞) .            (3.2)

   This is LF1 verbatim, and it upgrades the round-1 repair note (b): the
   polar packet drops **identically**, as a property of `Π²`, not as a
   computation on the primitive sector.
2. *The answer to the review's question "after `Π²`, is the atom sum tempered?"
   is **no**, and I prove it.*  **Proposition 3.6a** (new) exhibits one
   Schwartz function that kills all three claims at once:
   `ψ(t) = χ(t)e^{−|t|^{1/2}}` with `χ` a smooth cutoff of `[−1,1]`.  `ψ ∈ S(R)`
   (every derivative of `e^{−|t|^{1/2}}` is a combination of
   `|t|^{−k/2}e^{−|t|^{1/2}}`) but decays **slower than every exponential**.
   Then `∫ϖψ = ∞`; `⟨W_{fin},ψ⟩ ≥ 2Σ_{p≥8}(\log p)p^{−1/2}e^{−(\log p)^{1/2}} =
   ∞` by Chebyshev plus partial summation; and, crucially, with
   `φ := Π^{−2}ψ` — which **is** Schwartz, because
   `\hatφ = \hatψ/(¼+τ²)²` and `(¼+τ²)^{−2}` is smooth with all derivatives
   bounded — `⟨Π²W_{fin},φ⟩ = ⟨W_{fin},Π²φ⟩ = ⟨W_{fin},ψ⟩ = ∞`.
   **So `Π²W_{fin} ∉ S'(R)`: `Π²` does not tame the sum, and the `S'` route is
   closed.**
3. *Therefore the version used is the `D'` one, and it is named as such.*
   `𝔏` is typed `𝔏 ∈ D'(R)` in (3.2); Theorem 3.7(c) reads "positive-definite
   distribution **on `D(R)`**" with the parenthetical "*no temperedness is
   assumed; by Proposition 3.6a none is available a priori*"; Theorem 3.7(d)
   reads "*`𝔏` **extends to** a tempered distribution and …*", with
   "*temperedness here is a **conclusion**, not a hypothesis*".
   **(X3)** in §8 now reads: *Bochner–Schwartz theorem, `D'(R)` form
   (L. Schwartz): a positive-definite distribution on `D(R)` is automatically
   tempered and is the Fourier transform of a unique positive tempered
   measure*, with the round-2 bracket explaining why the `S'` form was
   withdrawn.  `(d) ⟹ (c)` is now stated as the elementary direction,
   `\hatμ(g⋆g^†) = ∫|\hat g|²dμ ≥ 0`.
4. *Well-definedness of `𝔏 ∈ D'(R)` is sourced*: `W_{fin}+ω_∞` pairs with
   every `h ∈ C_c^∞` by Paper 45 **Lemma 5.1a** (no support hypothesis), and
