# Phase 35 — Five Fronts

This phase runs five more concentrated tests growing out of Phase 34's directions B and D: convergence/rigidity of the CCM spectral triples, a semilocal Maslov-index probe, Fourier-interpolation frame structure of the zero exponentials, an audit of the Bagchi universality criterion, and a sharpening of the Jacobi-trace criterion to a single scale $\lambda_0$.

## Results
- Convergence $H_x\to H_\infty$ and Carathéodory–Fejér rigidity in the CCM spectral triples: established. (PROVED)
- Semilocal Maslov index: formulated as the first falsifiable step of the Direction-B program $\mathbf{C}_D^B$. (OPEN, falsifiable test set up)
- A toy off-critical quadruple test (Direction B+D combined) on a finite-dimensional model: non-correspondence and correspondence results proved within the toy model, conditional on several technical hypotheses (T1)–(T4) and an acknowledged gap (G4). (PROVED within toy model / CONDITIONAL in general)
- Fourier interpolation and the frame structure of $\{e^{i\gamma_n s}\}$: structural analysis, no closure stated. (OPEN)
- Audit of the Bagchi criterion (strong recurrence, Voronin universality): reviewed for applicability to the program. (AUDIT, inconclusive for closure)
- Strict positivity of $W_\lambda(s)>0$ for every real $s$ (sharpening Doc 39/Phase 29): proved, and used to show RH is equivalent to the trace vanishing $T_{\lambda_0}=0$ at a *single* scale $\lambda_0$, rather than for all $\lambda$ — removing a spurious degree of freedom from the Phase 32–33 trace criterion. (PROVED)
