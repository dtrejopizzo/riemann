## B. Constraint set on any future `h⁰` / nonnegative invariant

1. Must not be defined using positivity, the kernel, or the spectral decomposition of `Δ_T` (P122_D_SPECIFICATION §1; restated as Definition 6.8 (R1) and never relaxed).
2. Must be `Z_{≥0}`-valued (or into a totally ordered monoid with 0 least element) and **finite** — rules out `h⁰_⊥` (`≡∞`, Thm 6.3) and `h⁰_spec` (`∈{0,∞}`, Thm 6.4) (Paper 47 Thm 6.7).
3. Cannot be the effective-cone indicator alone: `Eff_A^tr ∩ {primitive} = {0}` (Thm 6.1), and effectivity is not the operator-positive cone in either direction (Thm 6.5, counterexamples both ways; `I_A` has no sign on `Eff_A^tr`, Thm 10.3c(d)).
4. Cannot be built from the scalar analytic determinant `L_S` alone: its linear term is trivial on the rulings, so no ruling-side vanishing theorem converts it into an inequality (Thm 6.6).
5. Must carry a duality `F↦F^∨` that moves the primitive sector off itself (R5); the only duality rows A–C supply, the half-density dagger `†`, preserves primitivity and is excluded. The correct type is an **affine** involution (`σ_K(E)=K−E`), not an algebra involution — supplied in Paper 47 §12, closing this item, but any competing construction must still satisfy R5 in this affine sense (Thm 12.2, 12.2a; §9.1 item 3a).
6. Must satisfy, for the Hodge-index transfer route, exactly three properties on `D^W` (Thm 12.3): (a) `h⁰(E)≥0` for all `E`; (b′) `h⁰(E)>0 ⇒ ∃E'≥0, E'≢0, E−E'∈𝓝_∞`; (c) `h⁰(E)+h⁰(K−E) ≥ ½I_∞(E,E) − ½I_∞(E,K) + χ` for some constant `χ` (no sign condition on `χ` required).
7. The quadratic term of any RR-type identity must be `I_∞(F,F)` itself, not a comparison with it (R3, retained in spirit inside (c)).
8. No vanishing theorem may be postulated on the primitive sector by fiat; if used at all it must be **derived**, as `h⁰(K−nF)=0` for large `n` is derived from ampleness + (b′), not assumed (R4 deleted, Prop 12.5).
9. `χ ≥ 0` is **not** required (superseding Def 6.8's (6.2) side condition); the quadratic term in Thm 12.3 Step 3 dominates regardless of the sign of `χ`.
10. No **model-free** derivation of (a)+(b′)+(c) can exist: any valid proof must, at some line, read a datum of `ξ`/`ζ` that the falsifier `Ξ_a=ξ(s+a)ξ(s−a)` lacks (Restatement 12.4a, a schema about proofs, not about function definitions — round-1's stronger claim that no such function exists was withdrawn). The natural discriminating datum, heuristically, is unimodularity `Frob_n⋆Frob_n^† = Δ^{(n)}` (fails for `Ξ_a`, which gives `(n^a+n^{-a})Δ^{(n)}`) — Remark 12.4b, Theorem 7.1, a direction, not a theorem.
11. Any candidate law must state whether it extends to Dedekind zeta, and if so must declare that it would imply the corresponding GRH (P122 §5 falsifier list, item retained at Paper 47 §7.3 and §9.1 item 10).
12. Any candidate must fail, for a visible structural reason before spectral comparison, on Davenport–Heilbronn and on the shifted pair `Ξ_a` (P122 §5; realized concretely for `Ξ_a` at P124.014 and Paper 47 Thm 12.4/§7.1).
13. Per `P3_COMMON_FAILURE_ARCHITECTURE` §8: a successor rigidity law must act on mixed source lines (not placewise coefficients), retain exact prime phases and the coherent degree port, preserve both Tate conditions without projection by the target form, and must not be a tensor amplification, recurrence argument, commutator estimate, or positive-lift construction already covered by Theorem 2.1 of that note.
14. Per P122 §6 acceptance test: carrier + operations + orientations + restriction must be constructed *without* `B_nuc` positivity; an independent theorem must prove Hodge/RR/oriented reciprocity; the candidate must fail a non-Euler explicit-formula model *before* comparison; comparison with `B_nuc` must be proved last.
15. Whether `#H⁰_ℤ` itself (not its logarithm) could occupy the slot is excluded on type grounds — a raw count is not a dimension and cannot appear additively in an RR identity (Paper 47 §14.5 item 17).

## C. Partial successes on positivity, exact scope

1. **Phase-118 log-2 certificate.** Unconditional theorem: `A_T ≥ 0` for all `0 < T ≤ log 2`, an interval-arithmetic certificate with 5 SHA-256 pins, reproducing in 0.109 s, independently re-derived for two Arb steps. Scope: this is the *only* range with a certified enclosure; extension to `(1/2)log5` is blocked by genuine smallness of a Feshbach gap (`h=0.0012`), not by numerical precision, and requires analytic cross-coupling terms `κ, C_D` retracted from phase 114. (`PHASE_118_CLOSURE.md` item 4)
2. **Phase-124.013 shielding dichotomy.** `Σ(T)≥0` for all `T<T_q` is proved **equivalent** to Gate R (a genuine model reduction with no unknown minimizer), and the criterion `liminf_{T↑T_*} λ_min Σ(T) > −∞` is proved to be a congruence (basis-change) invariant, though the numeric value `λ_min Σ(T)` itself is not. Scope: reformulation only; `Σ(T)≥0` itself remains open, and is false for the `Ξ_a` model. (P124_013 §3, Cor 3.3 and its phase-134 note)
3. **Paper 44 Theorem 8bis.4a scope note.** Positivity fails unconditionally at every finite chart for the *pure* Frobenius form `Q_fin`; the object whose Hodge index actually matters is the Gram form of `Frob⋆Δ^rad` together with Theorem 8bis.1e — i.e. row B supplies the Gram form but explicitly not the sign. Scope: a negative result stated with sharp boundaries (which object is/isn't at issue). (`04-papers/44-row-b-arithmetic-family/main.md:340-354`)
4. **Paper 47 §13 lattice candidate `h⁰_ℤ`.** Satisfies (a) and (b′) *unconditionally* (Theorem 13.5), with linear independence of generators (13.5(i)) and a proved bound on every finite truncation `Q`: `h⁰_ℤ,Q(mK) = O_Q(log m) = o(m²)` (Lemma 13.5a, Thm 13.6(ii)) — so (c) fails on every finite truncation but the full-lattice case is explicitly left open (item 23), not closed.
5. **Paper 47 §12 transfer theorem itself.** A genuine unconditional reduction (Theorem 12.3, `PROVED` conditional on `h⁰`'s existence) of row D's target to one function with three properties — this is the sharpest positive structural result of the whole sector, though it asserts no sign and constructs no `h⁰`.
6. **Phase-122 finite-place subobject `D^fin_{T,sf}`** — constructs most of the required carrier data (all active prime powers, correct weights, two causal orientations, port-swap sign, cross-place phase, semifinite determinant class, local positive rank-one curvature) — genuine partial progress on the *carrier*, explicitly not on the sign (P122_D_SPECIFICATION §7 table).
