# P121 -- exact source-matrix audit of CMO

## Verdict

The proposed causal Mobius observability theorem does not have the asserted
identity diagonal.  The finite divisor zeta matrix is unitriangular, but its
logarithmic connection is strictly triangular, and every Gamma--translation
commutator is also strictly delayed.  The root diagonal of the lifted system
is therefore the original observed Gamma--Tate transport operator (445), not
the identity.

Mobius elimination can remove auxiliary divisor-history coordinates.  It
cannot force the root amplitude `h` to vanish without assuming the uniqueness
statement (446).  This is kill condition 6 of the candidate specification.

CMO is retired.  No claim about row (d) follows.

## 1. Minimal divisor orbit

Order the divisor-closed orbit

\[
 \mathcal D=(1,2,3,4,6,12)
\]

and put `a=2^{-1/2}`, `b=3^{-1/2}`.  Central conjugation gives the exact
weighted zeta-incidence matrix

\[
Z=\begin{pmatrix}
1&0&0&0&0&0\\
a&1&0&0&0&0\\
b&0&1&0&0&0\\
a^2&a&0&1&0&0\\
ab&b&a&0&1&0\\
a^2b&ab&a^2&b&a&1
\end{pmatrix}.                                      \tag{1.1}
\]

Its exact Mobius inverse is

\[
Z^{-1}=\begin{pmatrix}
1&0&0&0&0&0\\
-a&1&0&0&0&0\\
-b&0&1&0&0&0\\
0&-a&0&1&0&0\\
ab&-b&-a&0&1&0\\
0&ab&0&-b&-a&1
\end{pmatrix}.                                      \tag{1.2}
\]

Both matrices are unitriangular.  This proves the algebraic part of the
candidate and also shows that no numerical gate is needed.

## 2. The logarithmic connection has zero diagonal

Let `\partial U_n=-(\log n)U_n`.  Direct multiplication, equivalently
`\Lambda=\mu*\log`, gives

\[
L:=Z\partial Z^{-1}
=\begin{pmatrix}
0&0&0&0&0&0\\
a\log2&0&0&0&0&0\\
b\log3&0&0&0&0&0\\
a^2\log2&a\log2&0&0&0&0\\
0&b\log3&a\log2&0&0&0\\
0&0&a^2\log2&b\log3&a\log2&0
\end{pmatrix}.                                      \tag{2.1}
\]

Thus `L` has the exact physical entries

\[
 L_{m,d}=\frac{\Lambda(m/d)}{\sqrt{m/d}}\,1_{d\mid m},
\]

but it is strictly lower triangular.  The identity diagonal of `Z` is
annihilated by the derivation.  Consequently the fact which creates the
von Mangoldt coefficient is precisely the fact which prevents the desired
algebraic observability diagonal.

Mixed-prime entries such as `L_{6,1}` and `L_{12,1}` vanish, while the two
factorization paths survive in different columns.  This correctly retains
prime-power support; it does not supply an equation for the root column.

## 3. Gamma commutators cannot restore the root diagonal

For every quarter-shift cell `C_k^+` and every nontrivial delay `U_m^+`,
the proved ODE formula is

\[
 (U_m^+C_k^+-C_k^+U_m^+)F
 =\text{a free response generated by the boundary sample }b_{k,m}^+.
                                                               \tag{3.1}
\]

For the identity node,

\[
 [C_k^+,U_1^+]=[C_k^+,I]=0.                         \tag{3.2}
\]

The same holds for `D_k`, `G_\Gamma` and the reflected orientation.  Hence
every history column created by commuting Gamma through `Z^{-1}` is attached
to `m>1`.  No commutator contributes an identity coefficient to the root
amplitude.

Abstractly, for `D_\Gamma=G_\Gamma-m_0I`,

\[
D_\Gamma Z^{-1}
=Z^{-1}D_\Gamma+[D_\Gamma,Z^{-1}],                  \tag{3.3}
\]

and the second term in (3.3) is strictly delayed.  Multiplication by `Z`
therefore yields

\[
D_\Gamma h+Z[D_\Gamma,Z^{-1}]h,                    \tag{3.4}
\]

where the first summand is the unchanged root bulk and only the second is a
Mobius-resolvable history.

## 4. Both orientations retain the old root equation

For a parity-`\varepsilon` state the exact observed equation is

\[
\left[D_\Gamma Z^{-1}-Z^{-1}Q
-\varepsilon\mathcal RZ^{-1}Q\right]h=r_\varepsilon.
                                                               \tag{4.1}
\]

The positive orientation is causal-lower-triangular.  Reflection supplies
the opposite causal orientation and couples it back to the same root fiber.
It does not create an algebraic diagonal.  After every strictly delayed
history variable is eliminated, (4.1) remains.

But uniqueness of (4.1) on `Z\mathcal P_T` is exactly v94 (446), the local
annular gap-transport theorem.  Replacing its root block by the identity is
therefore not a consequence of divisor incidence; it is the desired
uniqueness inserted into the source matrix.

## 5. No-go theorem

### Root-diagonal persistence

Consider any lift obtained by:

1. applying the finite oriented shifts `U_m^\pm` to (4.1);
2. commuting the Gamma ODE cells through those shifts using their exact
   boundary-response formulas; and
3. changing divisor coordinates by `Z` or `Z^{-1}`.

Then every off-root Gamma history block is triangular in divisor depth, but
the root diagonal is similar to the operator in (4.1).  In particular it is
the identity only if that operator has first been inverted on the physical
primitive range.

Such an inverse exists with the required root uniqueness if and only if
(446) holds.  Therefore this entire class of divisor-orbit lifts cannot
prove (446) by unitriangular Mobius elimination.

#### Proof

Equations (2.1) and (3.2) show that all new arithmetic and boundary terms
have positive divisor depth.  Triangular coordinate changes preserve the
depth-zero diagonal up to similarity.  Equation (3.3) identifies that
diagonal as the uncommuted Gamma bulk, while (4.1) adds the already present
degree, reflection and Tate terms.  Hence the root block is precisely the
observed transport operator.  Making it the identity requires its inverse;
injectivity of that inverse is (446).  This proves the assertion.

## 6. Consequence for phase 121

The useful exact output is (2.1): the arithmetic lift is correct and
retains the physical von Mangoldt weights without mixed-prime contamination.
The proposed closure mechanism is nevertheless dead because differentiation
removes the unit diagonal before the physical contact appears.

The next candidate must generate an additional independent identity on a
first-loss radical.  Reindexing, translating or Mobius-inverting the same
zero-mode equation cannot do so.
