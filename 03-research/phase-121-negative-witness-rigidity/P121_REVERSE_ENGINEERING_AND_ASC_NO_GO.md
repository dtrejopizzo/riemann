# P121 -- reverse engineering row (d) and the arithmetic-singularity no-go

## 1. Purpose

This note starts from the two counterfactual worlds instead of proposing a
new positive realization of the Weil form.  It records exactly what must
happen if row (d) is true, exactly what must happen if it is false, and then
audits a new source-side proposal: arithmetic propagation of boundary
singularities by the incommensurable shifts `log 2` and `log 3`.

No statement below promotes row (d) to proved.

## 2. The counterfactual bifurcation

Write

\[
 q_T(F)=-B_{\rm nuc,T}(F,F),\qquad F\in\mathcal P_T,
\]

and let `H_T` be its Friedrichs operator on the two-Tate primitive source.
The previously proved initial interval and continuity/compact-resolvent
theorems give the following exhaustive alternative.

### Branch D+

If the global square is true, then

\[
 H_T\geq0\quad\hbox{for every }T>0.                 \tag{2.1}
\]

The zero-residual Green identity of phase 120 then has nonnegative physical
compression for every window.  Cofinal stabilization on compactly supported
tests gives row (d).  Equivalently, the odd nuclear quotient of row (c) has
no off-central spectral character.

### Branch D-

If the global square is false, there is a first parameter `T_* > log 2` and
a nonzero parity vector

\[
 0\ne F_*\in\mathcal P_{T_*},\qquad H_{T_*}F_*=0.   \tag{2.2}
\]

The phase-116/v94 first-loss analysis already proves that this vector must
simultaneously have all of the following properties:

1. its support saturates both endpoints of `(-T_*,T_*)`;
2. its zero extension is not a translation-smooth vector;
3. it has no equality-mode Meyer/Poisson lift;
4. its membership in the maximal global co-Poisson domain was open in v94;
   `P121_DYADIC_RADICAL_BOOTSTRAP.md` now proves that it belongs to that
   domain, so rough graph escape is no longer possible;
5. by parity, neither oriented arithmetic exterior tail is in `L2`;
6. nevertheless its local causal co-Poisson amplitude
   `h=Z_{T_*,+}F_*` is well defined and the unitriangular operator
   `Z_{T_*,+}` is invertible.

Thus failure of row (d) is not compatible with a translation-smooth
negative witness.  It may nevertheless be sub-half-Sobolev: the new dyadic
bootstrap places it in the nuclear co-Poisson graph completion but does not
give the annular gap-transport identity required to annihilate it.

This is the precise point at which rows (a)--(c) stop: they construct and
identify the trace on the common nuclear core, but they do not assert that
this core is equality-state complete for the physical row-(d) graph norm.

## 3. Proposed arithmetic-singularity cascade

The proposed mechanism was:

> An endpoint singularity of a first-loss vector is propagated by the
> translated Euler terms through the additive group generated by the
> `log p`; unique factorization prevents labelled path cancellation, and
> the two incommensurable shifts `log 2`, `log 3` make the propagated orbit
> recurrent.  A nonzero compactly supported equality state should therefore
> acquire an impossible dense singular carrier.

This is nonlinear in the support/singular carrier of the witness, does not
construct a positive norm, and has a genuine pre-row-(d) kill condition:
labelled singularities must survive the passage from row (b) to the physical
row-(c) equation.

## 4. What is true

On the decorated row-(b) source, different prime words remain distinct.
Unique factorization gives

\[
 \sum_p k_p\log p=\sum_p l_p\log p
 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad k_p=l_p\quad\hbox{for every }p. \tag{4.1}
\]

Also `log 2/log 3` is irrational, so the additive group

\[
 \{j\log2-k\log3:j,k\in\mathbb Z\}                 \tag{4.2}
\]

is dense in `R`.  These facts do give recurrence and no collision for
*decorated words*.

## 5. Exact kill: row (c) erases the labels before the equality equation

The physical zero-mode equation is scalar:

\[
 (G_{\Gamma,T}-m_0I)F
 -\sum_{n<e^{2T}}{\Lambda(n)\over\sqrt n}
       (S_{\log n}+S_{-\log n})F
 =r_{\rm Tate}.                                    \tag{5.1}
\]

Row (b) retains the decorated arrows `Gamma_n`, but row (c) applies the
continuous contact functional and forms the operator-valued *sum* (5.1).
There is no label-resolved vector equation behind (5.1).  In particular,

\[
 \sum_n u_n=0                                      \tag{5.2}
\]

in the scalar distribution space does not imply `u_n=0` for each decorated
source label.  This failure already occurs with two labels: for any nonzero
distribution `u`, the labelled family `(u,-u)` has zero scalar trace.

Microlocalization does not repair the loss.  Several translated singular
germs may reach the same physical point from different existing singular
points, and their scalar principal symbols can cancel.  Unique
factorization distinguishes the *paths*, not arbitrary complex amplitudes
attached to their starting germs.  The equality state supplies only their
sum.

Therefore the decisive no-cancellation lemma required by the cascade is
false at the type in which the zero-mode equation is known.  Lifting (5.1)
back to a labelwise equality would add information not present in row (c).

## 6. The order-theoretic repair is independently unavailable

One might try to forbid cancellation by choosing a one-signed ground state.
That repair is already impossible for the literal completed operator:

* the first Beurling--Deny criterion fails, so the heat semigroup is not
  positivity preserving on the natural cone;
* the exact Gamma density is strictly log-convex in the wrong direction,
  and the kernel is not `TP_2`;
* hence Perron--Frobenius, Harnack and variation-diminishing oscillation do
  not provide a canonical one-signed first-loss vector.

These are exact no-go results in paper 41, not failures of numerical tests.

## 7. Reverse-engineering conclusion

The two worlds now differ at one sharply typed statement:

\[
\boxed{\text{Can a physical radical vector in the row-(c) nuclear graph
 fail equality-state annular gap transport?}}       \tag{7.1}
\]

* In branch D+, no such vector exists.
* In branch D-, the first-loss vector is forced to be a graph vector whose
  local co-Poisson amplitude fails the annular vanishing (441).

Merely asserting that every equality vector is captured by the nuclear
topology is equivalent to excluding first loss and is circular.  A valid
successor must instead produce a source-defined operation, already
continuous in the row-(c) topology, whose value on an equality-state
approximating sequence controls the missing graph seminorm.  It must do so
only on the radical, without a global estimate of the form (434), because
that full estimate asks for an unavailable uniform local second moment of
`zeta`.

The arithmetic-singularity cascade does not supply that operation and is
retired.

This note proves a new architectural no-go and an exact counterfactual
reduction.  It does **not** close the global square.
