# P121 -- direct closure audit for F2

## Verdict

F2 is not proved.  Three direct attempts were tested and rejected:

1. conjugation by the exact Gamma step produces only commutator identities;
2. microlocal separation is unavailable because the two critical principal
   parts are approached from the same half-plane and cancel there;
3. rank one at each finite Euler place does not make the global two-port
   conormal pair equal.

The last point admits a universal local countermodel.  It proves that no
argument using only the already constructed conormal jets, their scalar
sum, Real covariance and a rank-one source line can close F2.  A valid proof
must extract an additional nonlinear equality from the *global first-contact
equation*.  No such equality is established here.

## 1. The universal conormal model

Let

\[
 \mathcal O=\mathbb C\{z\},\qquad E(z)=z,\qquad I=(E),
 \qquad K_c(z)=c
\]

for an arbitrary real scalar `c`.  Put `H_c=E K_c=cz`.  Then

\[
 [H_c]=c[E]\quad\hbox{in }I/I^2,                 \tag{1.1}
\]

and the two frozen-source dual jets have normal symbols

\[
 j_+(c)=-c,\qquad j_-(c)=c.                       \tag{1.2}
\]

Consequently

\[
 j_+(c)+j_-(c)=0                                   \tag{1.3}
\]

for every `c`, while the matching required by F2,

\[
 j_+(c)=j_-(c),                                    \tag{1.4}
\]

holds if and only if `c=0`.

The conormal source is the single line generated by `[E]`; hence it is rank
one.  The involution exchanging the two orientations sends `c` to `-c` and
preserves (1.3).  Thus rank one, orientation exchange, Real covariance and
the scalar conservation law all hold for every nonzero `c`.

### Proposition 1.1 -- first-order source data do not imply F2

There is no formal implication from

* a principal Euler ideal `I=(E)`;
* a rank-one conormal line `I/I^2`;
* the frozen-source jets with symbols `(-c,c)`;
* orientation exchange and Real covariance; and
* vanishing scalar supertrace `j_++j_-=0`

to the matching `j_+=j_-`.

#### Proof

The model above satisfies every hypothesis for every real `c`.  Taking
`c != 0` violates the conclusion.  QED.

This countermodel is local and deliberately does not satisfy a global
first-contact equation.  Its role is exact: it proves that the missing
information cannot be manufactured from the already closed local package.

## 2. Why the three direct attacks fail

### 2.1 Gamma-step conjugation

The recurrence

\[
 \psi(w+1)-\psi(w)={1\over w}
\]

does separate oriented Gamma symbols after conjugation by an exponential
weight.  Applied to a first-contact equation, however, the resulting
relations are commutators of that same equation.  Pairing them with the
radical vector gives

\[
 \langle F,[\Delta_T,X]F\rangle=0,
\]

which is identically `0=0`.  Higher relations require invariance of the
translation jet of `F` under `ker Delta_T`; that is the already rejected
commutator-moment gate, not a consequence of first contact.

### 2.2 Microlocal separation

Near a simple critical zero `z=i gamma`, the two physical meromorphic germs
have principal parts

\[
 {-c\over\sigma+i(\tau-\gamma)},\qquad
 {+c\over\sigma+i(\tau-\gamma)}.                  \tag{2.1}
\]

Both are boundary approaches from the same half-plane.  Their wave-front
directions therefore coincide; they are not disjoint causal cones.  The
regularity of their sum is exactly the cancellation visible in (2.1) and
does not imply `c=0`.

### 2.3 Rank-one residue matrices

Rank one is proved for the primitive contact line at each finite place.
The critical conormal coefficient is formed only after the global Euler
determinant has been continued and then sampled at its critical divisor.
Putting the two orientations into a diagonal two-by-two residue matrix is
not a functorial consequence of the local rank-one lines.  If that diagonal
matrix is imposed, the assertion that it still has rank at most one is
already `c=0`.  Hence the proposed rank argument inserts F2 in the passage
from local lines to the global critical fibre.

## 3. Exact remaining form of F2

The first-contact equation must supply a source identity which is absent
from the universal model.  Because every linear orientation-invariant law
annihilates the antisymmetric line automatically, the new identity must be
either

1. orientation-breaking, selecting one causal representative before
   supertrace; or
2. nonlinear and faithful on the antisymmetric conormal coefficient, for
   example an independently derived law whose leading term is a nonzero
   multiple of `c^2`.

Merely defining that nonlinear expression does not help.  Its vanishing
must be derived from

\[
 P_T\mathscr A_TF=0
\]

without using positivity, an inverse of `Delta_T`, or critical-zero data.
This is exactly the global-square core.  It remains open.
