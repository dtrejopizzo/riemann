## Why the survivor is outside the retired architecture

The CMO mechanism lay outside the retired positive-lift architecture: it
used Mobius inversion as the inverse of a unitriangular incidence operator,
not as a Hilbert adjoint.  It nevertheless fails its own independent source
gate.  Architectural novelty is necessary but not sufficient.

The mechanism is allowed to prove a uniqueness theorem.  It is not allowed
to define its history matrix from the desired Weil operator or to infer
unitriangularity from row (d).

## Work order

The corona row is no longer the live first-loss branch: the dyadic theorem
proves that the radical does not escape the graph.  The next step must
generate annular vanishing for that specific graph vector, independently of
the desired sign.  A translation, reindexing or triangular inversion of the
same equation is inadmissible by the root-diagonal persistence theorem.

The reverse-engineering audit adds a second constraint: information living
in the decorated row-(b) source cannot be used labelwise after row (c) has
taken the scalar contact trace, unless a source-defined separating
operation is constructed before that trace.

## New binding requirement

The next candidate must be nonlinear in the hypothetical witness and must
use a law specific to the Euler product.  Before construction it must pass
this test:

> after linearization at the witness, does the decisive identity reduce to
> `HF=0`, `P_TA_TF=0`, a first commutator of that equation, or an invertible
> change of its coordinates?

If yes, it is rejected immediately.  If its second variation is merely a
positive Gram, contraction, entropy Hessian or stability form, it is rejected
by the phase-120 common-failure theorem.  A survivor must use genuinely
nonlinear arithmetic information -- for example a source-level product law
whose equality case is not determined by the quadratic Weil form.
