## The idea to test (the blueprint's, to be built or refuted)

In Weil's proof the quadratic growth `h⁰(m(F_1+F_2)) ≍ m²` on the square `C×C` is **automatic from the
product structure**: `H⁰(C×C, O(mF_1+mF_2)) = H⁰(C,O(m)) ⊗ H⁰(C,O(m))`, so linear growth on the curve
gives quadratic growth on the square. Riemann–Roch on the square (ledger step W2, the ABSENT step) is
therefore *not* a new counting problem: it is the tensor square of the curve's Riemann–Roch plus the
intersection theory of the correspondences. The arithmetic side has the two ingredients that could play
these roles: (i) the inverse-semigroup law `Frob_m ⋆ Frob_n = Frob_{mn}` (coprime `m,n`) — the Euler
product as a **product structure** on the Frobenius family (this is exactly what Epstein lacks: `r_Q(n)`
is not multiplicative); (ii) a curve-level `h⁰` with linear growth in the degree — Connes–Consani's
Riemann–Roch for `Spec Z̄` (2205.01391: `dim_S H⁰(D) − dim_S H¹(D) = ⌈(deg D + log 2)/log 3⌉' − 1_L`) is
the one such object in the literature. The phase asks whether (i)+(ii) assemble into an `h⁰_□` on the
square meeting (a),(b′),(c) of (P139), and if not, **which clause absorbs the zero information** — the
answer is the deliverable either way.

## D.23 — The square as a tensor structure (the Euler-product step made geometric)
1. Define the divisor group of the square `Div_□ := Z·F_1 ⊕ Z·F_2 ⊕ Z·Δ ⊕ ⊕_n Z·Frob_n` (fibre classes
   `F_1, F_2` = the two rulings `e_∓` of Paper 43 §9 in the Weil completion; `Δ = Frob_1`), with the
   bidegree map `bideg: Div_□ → R²` (`F_1 ↦ (1,0)`, `F_2 ↦ (0,1)`, `Frob_n ↦ (n^{1/2}, n^{−1/2})`, `Δ ↦ (1,1)`)
   and the composition law `⋆` (Paper 44 §3ter). Prove: the subsemigroup generated by `{Frob_n}` is the
   **multiplicative** semigroup `(N, ·)` (for coprime `m,n`; state exactly what happens for `Frob_p ⋆ Frob_p`
   — Paper 44's `Δ^{(n)}` and the non-monoidality theorem), and bidegree is multiplicative on it. This is
   the Euler product as a structure on `Div_□`. Falsifier: for `Ξ_Q` define the analogous family
   `Frob^{(Q)}_n` (translates weighted by `r_Q(n)`-type atoms) and prove the law **fails** (name the line:
   non-multiplicativity of `r_Q(n)` for `h > 1`). For `Ξ_a`: the law holds (Λ_a is a sum of two
   multiplicative-type weights) but bidegrees become `(n^{½+a}, n^{−½+a})`-type — record the exact

## D.24 — Tensor-square `h⁰` candidates and the clause that absorbs the zeros
1. **Curve-level input.** Let `h_c: R → R_{≥0}` be a "curve Riemann–Roch" function: `h_c(x) ≥ 0`,
   `h_c(x) = 0` for `x < 0` (or `≤ −c`), `h_c(x) ≥ x − c'` (linear growth, slope 1). Candidates: (α) the
   trivial `h_c(x) = max(x,0)`; (β) CC's `dim_S H⁰` for `Spec Z̄` (2205.01391 — read `cc_2205.01391` if
   present in the corpus, else the arXiv text; state its exact growth: slope `1/log 3` in CC's real degree —
   convert units to ours and determine whether it reaches the slope (P139)'s growth needs).
2. **Tensor-square candidate.** For `E ∈ D^W` with rulings `(a,b) = (deg⁻E, deg⁺E)` define
   `h⁰_□(E) := h_c(a)·h_c(b)` (and variants: `h_c(a)h_c(b) + h_c(Frobenius part)`, using D.11's `h⁰_Z` on
   the Frobenius lattice). Work out (a), (b′), (c) of (P139) **exactly** for `h⁰_□` with each `h_c`:
   * (a) trivial;
   * (b′) `h⁰_□(E) > 0 ⟹ ∃E' ≥ 0, E' ≢ 0, E − E' ∈ 𝓝_∞`: this is a **moment problem at the zeros** —
     given `E` with positive rulings, find a nonnegative `E'` with `Φ_{E'} = Φ_E` on `Z(ξ) ∪ {0,1}`
     (order-one vanishing in the radical). Determine whether (b′) is exactly Weil positivity / Hodge index
     in disguise (expected for (α)), or strictly weaker, or unconditionally provable (the blueprint's guess: for
     `E ∈ mK + Λ_W` it is trivial since `Λ_W ⊂ 𝓝_∞` and `mK ≥ 0`; for general `E` it is a positivity
     statement — locate exactly where the zeros enter);
   * (c) `h⁰_□(E) + h⁰_□(K−E) ≥ ½I_∞(E,E) − ½I_∞(E,K) + χ`: with `I_∞(E,E) = 2ab − W(E)` (`W(E)` the zero
     sum) this is an inequality between `h_c(a)h_c(b) + h_c(½−a)h_c(½−b)`, `ab`, `(a+b)`, `χ` and `W(E)`;
     write it out and determine for which `(a,b)` it holds without `W(E)` and where `W(E) ≥ …` is forced
     (R140_000 computed for (α) with `χ = ¼`: (c) forces `W(E) ≥ 2·(a+b)/4·… − 2ab`, which fails at
     `E = tK`, `t < ½`, since `W(tK) = 0` — verify or correct this computation; then optimize `χ` and `h_c`).
   Deliverable: the **exact statement of what remains** after the tensor-square `h⁰` — either a clause of
   (P139) is reduced to a moment problem / an inequality of a stated shape, or the candidate is refuted with
   the witness.
3. **Tests.** (AT-1) `Ξ_Q`: `h⁰_□` cannot be defined because `Div_□^{(Q)}` has no product structure —
