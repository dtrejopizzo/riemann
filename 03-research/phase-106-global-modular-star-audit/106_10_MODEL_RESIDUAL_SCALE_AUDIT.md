# 106.10 — CCM model residual: the exact scale supplied by Meixner

## Purpose

The global curvature gate is

\[
 \partial_z^2\log\frac{\widehat\phi_L}{\widehat k_L}\longrightarrow0.
 \tag{1}
\]

Documents 106.07--106.09 reduce one variational route to (1) to a weighted
Rayleigh-excess/next-even-gap estimate.  The remaining question addressed
here is deliberately narrower:

> What does the fixed-order prolate-to-Hermite estimate quoted by CCM
> actually prove about the model Rayleigh value?

The answer is exact.  It proves an \(L^2\) source error of order
\(\lambda^{-1/2}=e^{-L/4}\), and the corresponding transform convergence on
closed substrips.  It does **not** prove convergence in the form norm of the
semilocal Weil operator, convergence of the model Rayleigh value, a model
residual estimate, or the relative estimate required by the collapsing
gap.  The obstruction is quantitative: the prime block has operator norm
\(O(\lambda)\), exactly cancelling the squared \(L^2\) gain
\(O(\lambda^{-1})\), while the Gamma multiplier is unbounded.

This note proves those assertions and records the precise additional
estimates that would be sufficient.

## 1. Normalization and the CCM input

Put

\[
 \lambda=e^{L/2},\qquad
 \mathcal E(f)(u)=u^{1/2}\sum_{n\ge1}f(nu).
 \tag{2}
\]

Let

\[
 h(u)=\frac\pi2u^2(2\pi u^2-3)e^{-\pi u^2},
 \qquad k=\mathcal E(h).
 \tag{3}
\]

The CCM prolate combination \(h_\lambda\) is defined on
\([-\lambda,\lambda]\), extended by zero when it is inserted in
\(\mathcal E\), and normalized so that

\[
 \int_{\mathbb R}h_\lambda(t)\,dt=0.
 \tag{4}
\]

Their Meixner--Sch\"afke input is the existence of a constant \(C_M\),
independent of \(\lambda\), such that

\[
 \delta_\lambda
 :=\sup_{|t|\le\lambda}|h_\lambda(t)-h(t)|
 \le C_M\lambda^{-2}.
 \tag{5}
\]

On the multiplicative interval

\[
 I_\lambda=[\lambda^{-1},\lambda]
 \tag{6}
\]

write

\[
 k_\lambda=\mathbf 1_{I_\lambda}\mathcal E(h_\lambda),
 \qquad e_\lambda=k_\lambda-k,
 \tag{7}
\]

where \(k_\lambda\) is extended by zero outside \(I_\lambda\).
It is useful to retain the exact split

\[
 e_\lambda=d_\lambda+r_\lambda,\qquad
 d_\lambda=\mathbf1_{I_\lambda}
   \bigl(\mathcal E(h_\lambda)-\mathcal E(h)\bigr),\qquad
 r_\lambda=-\mathbf1_{\mathbb R_+^*\setminus I_\lambda}k.
 \tag{7a}
\]

The in-band error \(d_\lambda\) is supported in \(I_\lambda\); the exterior
term \(r_\lambda\) has the fixed kernel's Gaussian theta decay.

The distinction between the finite sum generated by the compactly
supported \(h_\lambda\) and the Gaussian tail generated by \(h\) is retained
below.  Omitting that tail, as is harmless for qualitative convergence,
would not be legitimate in a quantitative identity.

## 2. Pointwise and \(L^2\) transfer

### Lemma 1 — Quantitative physical error

There are constants \(C_h,c_h>0\) and a fixed polynomial \(P_h\) such that,
for \(\lambda\ge2\) and \(u\in I_\lambda\),

\[
 \boxed{
 |\mathcal E(h_\lambda)(u)-\mathcal E(h)(u)|
 \le D_\lambda u^{-1/2},
 \qquad
 D_\lambda=C_M\lambda^{-1}
       +C_hP_h(\lambda)e^{-c_h\lambda^2}.}
 \tag{8}
\]

#### Proof

For the terms with \(nu\le\lambda\), (5) gives

\[
 u^{1/2}\sum_{n\le\lambda/u}
 |h_\lambda(nu)-h(nu)|
 \le u^{1/2}\delta_\lambda\frac\lambda u
 \le C_M\lambda^{-1}u^{-1/2}.
 \tag{9}
\]

The remaining terms contain only \(h\).  From (3), for \(t\ge2\),

\[
 |h(t)|\le C(1+t^4)e^{-\pi t^2}.
 \tag{10}
\]

Comparison of the decreasing Gaussian tail with an integral, with one
extra polynomial factor to cover the first lattice point, gives uniformly
for \(u\in I_\lambda\)

\[
 u^{1/2}\sum_{n>\lambda/u}|h(nu)|
 \le C_hP_h(\lambda)e^{-c_h\lambda^2}u^{-1/2}.
 \tag{11}
\]

More explicitly, if \(n_0=\lfloor\lambda/u\rfloor+1\) and
\(H(t)=C(1+t^4)e^{-\pi t^2}\), then for \(\lambda\ge2\)

\[
\begin{aligned}
 \sum_{n\ge n_0}H(nu)
 &\le H(n_0u)+u^{-1}\int_{n_0u}^{\infty}H(t)\,dt,\\
 uH(n_0u)+\int_{n_0u}^{\infty}H(t)\,dt
 &\le C(1+\lambda^5)e^{-\pi\lambda^2}.
\end{aligned}
\tag{11a}
\]

Thus one may take \(c_h=\pi\) after enlarging a degree-five polynomial.

Equations (9)--(11) prove (8). \(\square\)

### Theorem 2 — The strongest direct \(L^2\) consequence

With Haar measure \(d^*u=du/u\),

\[
 \boxed{
 \|e_\lambda\|_{L^2(\mathbb R_+^*,d^*u)}^2
 \le D_\lambda^2(\lambda-\lambda^{-1})
      +C_hP_h(\lambda)e^{-c_h\lambda^2}
 =\frac{C_M^2+o(1)}{\lambda}.}
 \tag{12}
\]

Consequently,

\[
 \boxed{\|e_\lambda\|_2=O(\lambda^{-1/2})=O(e^{-L/4}),}
 \tag{13}
\]

and

\[
 \|k_\lambda\|_2\longrightarrow\|k\|_2>0.
 \tag{14}
\]

#### Proof

On \(I_\lambda\), square (8) and integrate:

\[
 \int_{\lambda^{-1}}^\lambda
 D_\lambda^2u^{-1}\,\frac{du}{u}
 =D_\lambda^2(\lambda-\lambda^{-1}).
 \tag{15}
\]

The fixed kernel \(k\) has Gaussian theta tails at \(+\infty\).  Its modular
symmetry \(k(u)=k(u^{-1})\) gives the identical estimate at zero.  Hence

\[
 \int_{\mathbb R_+^*\setminus I_\lambda}|k(u)|^2d^*u
 \le C_hP_h(\lambda)e^{-c_h\lambda^2}.
 \tag{16}
\]

Combining (15) and (16) proves (12)--(13).  Since
\(\widehat k=\Xi\not\equiv0\), Plancherel gives \(\|k\|_2>0\); (14) follows
from (13). \(\square\)

### Corollary 3 — Closed-substrip transform rate

For every fixed \(0\le B<1/2\),

\[
 \boxed{
 \sup_{|\mathrm{Im}\,z|\le B}
 |\widehat k_\lambda(z)-\Xi(z)|
 =O_B(\lambda^{B-1/2})}
 \tag{17}
\]

up to the Gaussian tail already displayed in (12).

#### Proof

Either integrate (8) directly against \(u^{-iz}d^*u\), or use
Cauchy--Schwarz and

\[
 \int_{\lambda^{-1}}^\lambda u^{2\beta}d^*u
 =\frac{\lambda^{2\beta}-\lambda^{-2\beta}}{2\beta}
 \quad(\beta\ne0).
 \tag{18}
\]

Together with (13), the worst boundary of the strip gives
\(O_B(\lambda^{B-1/2})\). \(\square\)

This recovers the topology of the CCM model theorem.  It is transform
convergence, not semilocal-ground convergence.

## 3. The exact Weil-form obstruction

Let \(A_L\) be the semilocal Weil operator and

\[
 q_L=\frac{k_\lambda}{\|k_\lambda\|_2},
 \qquad
 R_L=\langle A_Lq_L,q_L\rangle.
 \tag{19}
\]

Riemann's full kernel is an unconditional polarized radical:

\[
 QW(k,g)=0
 \tag{20}
\]

for every admissible \(g\), because \(\widehat k=\Xi\) vanishes on the
complete nontrivial divisor.  Therefore

\[
 \boxed{
 QW(k_\lambda,k_\lambda)=QW(e_\lambda,e_\lambda),
 \qquad
 R_L=\frac{QW(e_\lambda,e_\lambda)}{\|k_\lambda\|_2^2}.}
 \tag{21}
\]

Identity (21) is exact, but (12) does not bound its right-hand side.  This
can be seen separately in the three terms of the explicit formula.

### 3.1 The Gamma multiplier is unbounded

The archimedean term is

\[
 \int_{\mathbb R}|\widehat f(t)|^2
 \frac{\theta'(t)}{\pi}\,dt,
 \tag{22}
\]

and Stirling's formula gives

\[
 \frac{\theta'(t)}{\pi}
 =\frac1{2\pi}\log\frac{|t|}{2\pi}+O(t^{-2})
 \qquad(|t|\to\infty).
 \tag{23}
\]

Thus the form topology contains a logarithmic Fourier derivative which is
not controlled by \(L^2\).  In particular, no constant \(C\), uniform in
frequency, can satisfy

\[
 \left|\int|\widehat f(t)|^2\frac{\theta'(t)}\pi dt\right|
 \le C\|f\|_2^2.
 \tag{24}
\]

The failure is quantitative, not merely functional analytic.  In the
additive coordinate \(x=\log u\), take a fixed
\(\chi\in C_c^\infty((-1,1))\) and

\[
 f_{\lambda,M}(x)=\lambda^{-1}\chi(x)e^{iMx}.
 \tag{25}
\]

It obeys the pointwise envelope in (8) on its support and
\(\|f_{\lambda,M}\|_2=O(\lambda^{-1})\), but

\[
 \int|\widehat f_{\lambda,M}(t)|^2
       \frac{\theta'(t)}\pi dt
 =\frac{\|\chi\|_2^2}{\lambda^2}\log M
   +O(\lambda^{-2}).
 \tag{26}
\]

Choosing \(M\) arbitrarily large shows that the pointwise and \(L^2\)
consequences of (5) do not imply any upper bound for the Gamma form.  This
does not assert that the actual prolate function contains such a modulation;
it proves that the quoted \(C^0\) Meixner estimate, by itself, excludes no
such form energy.

### 3.2 The prime norm consumes the full \(L^2\) gain

For the CCM translation operator \(T(n)\), the two convolution evaluations
in the defining identity

\[
 \langle f,T(n)g\rangle
 =n^{-1/2}\bigl((f^**g)(n)+(f^**g)(n^{-1})\bigr)
 \tag{26a}
\]

are matrix coefficients of unitary multiplicative translations.
Cauchy--Schwarz therefore gives

\[
 \|T(n)\|\le2n^{-1/2}.
 \tag{27}
\]

Let \(\psi(x)=\sum_{n\le x}\Lambda(n)\), and choose an unconditional
Chebyshev constant \(C_\psi\) such that \(\psi(x)\le C_\psi x\) for
\(x\ge2\).  Partial summation gives

\[
 \sum_{n\le x}\frac{\Lambda(n)}{\sqrt n}
 =\frac{\psi(x)}{\sqrt x}
 +\frac12\int_1^x\frac{\psi(t)}{t^{3/2}}dt
 \le2C_\psi\sqrt x.
 \tag{28}
\]

Consequently,

\[
 \boxed{
 \left\|\sum_{1<n\le\lambda^2}\Lambda(n)T(n)\right\|
 \le4C_\psi\lambda.}
 \tag{29}
\]

Using the PNT only to state the asymptotic size, the right side obtained by
this absolute estimate is \(4\lambda+o(\lambda)\).  Apply it to the
in-band error \(d_\lambda\) from (7a).  Equations (8) and (15) give

\[
 \left|
 \sum_{1<n\le\lambda^2}\Lambda(n)
 \langle d_\lambda,T(n)d_\lambda\rangle
 \right|
 \le4C_\psi C_M^2+o(1).
 \tag{30}
\]

Thus the prime term is only \(O(1)\), not \(o(1)\).  The factor
\(O(\lambda)\) in (29) exactly uses up the squared error
\(O(\lambda^{-1})\) in (12).

The replacement of \(e_\lambda\) by \(d_\lambda\) in (30) is essential:
\(e_\lambda\) is not compactly supported.  The fixed exterior vector
\(r_\lambda\) is double-exponentially small in physical weighted norms, but
the quoted Meixner estimate contains no mixed form estimate for
\(QW(d_\lambda,r_\lambda)\).  Thus the exterior split cannot be used to
upgrade (30) to \(o(1)\) without additional form control.  In particular,
it does not remove the \(O(1)\) in-band ceiling.

### 3.3 The polar term is not the obstruction

The same pointwise estimate gives

\[
 |\widehat e_\lambda(i/2)|
 =O(\lambda^{-1}\log\lambda),
 \qquad
 |\widehat e_\lambda(-i/2)|=O(1),
 \tag{31}
\]

with Gaussian corrections from the exterior tails.  Hence

\[
 2\left|\widehat e_\lambda(i/2)
          \overline{\widehat e_\lambda(-i/2)}\right|
 =O\left(\frac{\log\lambda}{\lambda}\right).
 \tag{32}
\]

The unresolved pieces are therefore the logarithmic Gamma form and the
signed prime--Gamma cancellation, not the two polar evaluations.

### Theorem 4 — Binding verdict on the model Rayleigh value

From the CCM estimate (5) and the exact Weil formula one may conclude

\[
 \|k_\lambda-k\|_2=O(\lambda^{-1/2}),
 \qquad
 \widehat k_\lambda\to\Xi
 \text{ on closed substrips},
 \tag{33}
\]

but one may **not** conclude any of

\[
 R_L\to0,
 \qquad
 \|A_Lq_L\|\to0,
 \qquad
 \frac{R_L-\mu_{0,L}^+}{g_L^+}\to0.
 \tag{34}
\]

#### Proof

The positive assertions are Theorem 2 and Corollary 3.  Formula (21)
reduces \(R_L\) to the form value of \(e_\lambda\).  Equations (23)--(26)
show that the available estimate supplies no Gamma-form control, while
(29)--(30) show that absolute prime control is only \(O(1)\).  Neither an
operator residual nor a spectral gap appears in (5).  Hence none of the
three limits in (34) follows. \(\square\)

The theorem is a non-implication from the stated hypotheses.  It is not a
claim that the actual \(R_L\) fails to converge; that convergence remains a
source theorem to be proved.

## 4. Exact estimates that would be sufficient

### 4.1 A form-norm package for the scalar Rayleigh value

One direct sufficient estimate is

\[
\begin{aligned}
 &\int_{\mathbb R}|\widehat e_\lambda(t)|^2
       \bigl(1+\log(2+|t|)\bigr)dt
   \longrightarrow0,\\
 &\sum_{n\ge2}\Lambda(n)
   |\langle e_\lambda,T(n)e_\lambda\rangle|
   \longrightarrow0,\\
 &|\widehat e_\lambda(i/2)
    \widehat e_\lambda(-i/2)|\longrightarrow0.
\end{aligned}
\tag{35}
\]

Equations (21)--(23) would then imply \(R_L\to0\).  A concrete sufficient
condition for the in-band diagonal part of the second line is

\[
 \lambda\|d_\lambda\|_2^2\longrightarrow0,
 \tag{35a}
\]

together with an \(o(1)\) estimate for the Euler terms containing
\(r_\lambda\).  Condition (35a) is strictly stronger than (12): CCM gives
only \(\lambda\|d_\lambda\|_2^2=O(1)\).

The previously named source packages

\[
 \texttt{PROLATE-W21},
 \qquad
 \texttt{PROLATE-BV-MOMENT}
 \tag{36}
\]

in E101.049 and E101.053 control Fourier projection and endpoint transfer.
They do not contain the logarithmic Gamma energy or the
\(o(\lambda^{-1})\) squared \(L^2\) estimate in (35a).  Thus even proving
those two packages would not, without another argument preserving signed
prime--Gamma cancellation, prove \(R_L\to0\).

### 4.2 Gate A requires a residual and anti-orthogonality

The smaller RH-sufficient gate of 106.08 is

\[
 \boxed{
 \frac{\|A_Lq_L\|}
      {|\langle\phi_L,q_L\rangle|}\longrightarrow0.}
 \tag{37}
\]

Neither the numerator nor the denominator is controlled by (5).  Even a
proof of (35) gives a scalar expectation, not the operator residual in
(37), and the positivity-improving countermodel of 106.08 shows that a
vanishing residual cannot replace the overlap denominator.

### 4.3 Gate B lives below the collapsing near-radical cluster

With

\[
 g_L^+=\mu_{1,L}^+-\mu_{0,L}^+,
 \tag{38}
\]

the exact weighted gate from E101.095 and 106.07 is

\[
 \boxed{
 \frac{\|k_\lambda\|_2W_{L,B}}
      {\inf_{K_r}|\widehat k_\lambda|}
 \left(\frac{R_L-\mu_{0,L}^+}{g_L^+}\right)^{1/2}
 \longrightarrow0,}
 \tag{39}
\]

plus the Fourier projection tail.  Away from the limiting zeros,
\(\|k_\lambda\|_2\) and the denominator are bounded above and below, while
\(W_{L,B}^2\asymp\lambda^{2B}\).  Therefore the force-bearing estimate is

\[
 \boxed{
 R_L-\mu_{0,L}^+
 =o\bigl(g_L^+\lambda^{-2B}\bigr)
 \quad\text{for every fixed }B<1/2.}
 \tag{40}
\]

Document 106.09 proves, under RH, the upper bound

\[
 g_L^+\le C\lambda^Me^{-c\lambda^2}.
 \tag{41}
\]

Hence the available squared source error \(O(\lambda^{-1})\) is not merely
missing a constant: its certified scale is exponentially larger than the
entire next-even cluster.  Combining (40)--(41), a proof on the RH branch
would have to resolve the model Rayleigh excess at least at a
double-exponential-in-\(L\) relative scale, with the additional
\(\lambda^{-2B}\) Paley--Wiener factor.

The comparison is one-sided: (41) is an upper bound for the gap, not an
asymptotic formula.  It is nevertheless enough to rule out every proposed
closure that uses only a polynomial model error and a fixed or polynomial
gap.

## 5. Conclusion

The exact source-scale ledger is

\[
\begin{array}{c|c}
\text{statement}&\text{status from the CCM/Meixner input}\\ \hline
\|k_\lambda-k\|_2=O(\lambda^{-1/2})&\text{proved}\\
\widehat k_\lambda\to\Xi\text{ on closed substrips}&\text{proved}\\
\text{polar defect}=O((\log\lambda)/\lambda)&\text{proved}\\
\text{absolute prime defect}&O(1)\text{ only}\\
\text{Gamma form defect}&\text{not controlled}\\
R_L\to0&\text{not proved}\\
\text{Gate A or Gate B}&\text{not implied}.
\end{array}
\tag{42}
\]

Thus the Meixner theorem does exactly what CCM use it for: it identifies
the explicit prolate transform with \(\Xi\).  It does not identify the
actual semilocal Weil ground state with that model.  The missing theorem is
not another \(C^0\) prolate asymptotic.  It is either the signed
prime--Gamma form estimate and branch selection encoded in (37), or the
relative Rayleigh-excess/gap hierarchy (39)--(40).  Both are ordinary-prime
arithmetic statements; neither is present in the two audited source papers.

No statement in this document proves RH.
