## 1. Why this is the right comparison

E79.64 replaced the raw shell value `edge0` by the invariant correction

```text
relDef = 1 - edge0 / intensity.                                          (65-1)
```

The next candid question was whether `relDef` already exists elsewhere in the
phase-79 bookkeeping. The closest candidates are the cumulative edge-prefix
fractions from E79.3f:

```text
edge0frac, edge1frac, edge2frac,
and the first prefix increments edge1frac-edge0frac, edge2frac-edge1frac. (65-2)
```

These are not new fitted moments; they are direct cumulative shell data.

## 2. Result

On the audited zeta ladder, `relDef` is almost perfectly aligned with those
shortest-prefix variables. The measured correlations are:

```text
corr(relDef, edge0frac)      ~ -0.977
corr(relDef, edge1frac)      ~ -0.976
corr(relDef, edge2frac)      ~ -0.977
corr(relDef, edge1frac-edge0frac) ~ -0.974
corr(relDef, edge2frac-edge1frac) ~ -0.979.                             (65-3)
```

So the boundary correction discovered through the predictor audits is already
present, almost verbatim, in the shortest prefix bookkeeping of the common edge.
