# 107.202 -- Gamma and pole blocks cannot cancel the det_5 prime counterterm

## 1. Counterterm continuation

Let

\[
 C_5(s)=P(s)+\frac12P(2s),
 \qquad
 P(s)=\sum_{n\ge1}{\mu(n)\over n}\log\zeta(ns),
 \tag{1.1}
\]

where the second identity gives the standard analytic continuation of
the prime-zeta function away from its branch set.

The coefficient of \(\log\zeta(ns)\) in \(C_5\) is

\[
 c_n={\mu(n)+\mathbf1_{2\mid n}\mu(n/2)\over n}.
 \tag{1.2}
\]

At \(n=2\), \(c_2=0\).  This proves the nontrivial cancellation of
the apparent branch at \(s=1/2\).

## 2. Fractional branch at one third

At \(n=3\),

\[
 c_3=-\frac13.
\]

Since

\[
 \log\zeta(3s)=-\log(3s-1)+O(1)
 \qquad(s\to1/3),
\]

one obtains

\[
 C_5(s)=\frac13\log(3s-1)+O(1).
 \tag{2.1}
\]

Consequently the correcting factor required to recover
\(\zeta^{-1}\) from \(\det_5\) behaves as

\[
 e^{-C_5(s)}=(3s-1)^{-1/3}\,h(s),
 \qquad h(1/3)\ne0.
 \tag{2.2}
\]

It has nontrivial cubic monodromy around \(s=1/3\).

## 3. Archimedean mismatch theorem

The standard archimedean and degree-zero/two determinant is generated by

\[
 s,\quad s-1,\quad \pi^{-s/2},\quad\Gamma(s/2)
 \tag{3.1}
\]

with the fixed integral/half-integral exponents of the completed zeta
function.  Every factor in (3.1) is holomorphic and nonzero in a
neighborhood of \(s=1/3\).  Products and quotients of them are therefore
single-valued and meromorphic there.

**Theorem.**  No standard Gamma/pole block can cancel the order-five
prime counterterm \(e^{C_5(s)}\) on the critical strip.

**Proof.**  A single-valued meromorphic function has integral local
order and trivial monodromy.  Equation (2.2) has local order \(-1/3\)
and nontrivial monodromy.  Multiplication by any function generated by
(3.1) preserves that fractional branch. \(\square\)

The same mismatch occurs at further reciprocal integers and at scaled
zeta zeros.  The point \(1/3\) alone already proves the no-go.

## 4. Consequence

The archimedean number operator of 107_186--187 cannot serve as the
missing higher-Schatten renormalization.  The counterterm must be
cancelled on the prime side by a relative determinant or be retained in
a branched/renormalized determinant line.

This does not disprove a larger global construction with additional
prime-indexed ghost sectors.  It excludes only cancellation by the
existing Gamma and pole sectors.

## 5. Falsifier

The verifier computes (1.2) exactly through \(n=30\), checks
\(c_2=0\), \(c_3=-1/3\), and the resulting nontrivial monodromy.
It independently evaluates a truncated Mobius continuation near
\(s=1/3\) and recovers the logarithmic slope \(1/3\).  It also checks
that the completed archimedean factor is finite and nonzero there.
