# 107.11 -- Paper C, Part II: Picard/Jacobian realization and exact radical control

## 1. Purpose

This note executes Work Package III-B of `107_00`.  Its task is to state
the divisor-to-line-bundle realization that would place the Phase 107
source package inside the domain of arithmetic Hodge theory.

The required output is not merely a set-theoretic assignment.  It must
be a faithful Abel--Jacobi map

\[
 D_f\longmapsto \overline M_f
 \in \widehat{\operatorname{Pic}}^0(\mathcal X_T)
 \tag{1.1}
\]

or, if necessary, into the precise Yuan--Zhang adelic category, with
exact control of the real kernel:

\[
 \ker(f\mapsto \overline M_f)=\mathfrak R_W.
 \tag{1.2}
\]

This exact-kernel requirement is the equality-case audit of Phase 107.

## 2. Why III-B is separate from III-A

`107_10` fixes the existence problem for proper global models
\(\mathcal X_T\).  Even if such a model exists, the Phase 107 program is
not yet in the Hodge domain until source divisors become actual degree-zero
metrized line bundles with the correct metric and degree-one behavior.

This separation is essential.  A model can fail III-B in several ways:

1. the source divisor classes may collapse in \(\operatorname{Pic}^0\);
2. the induced metric may secretly be defined from the Weil form rather
   than from the source Green geometry;
3. the scaling action may be lost after passage to line bundles;
4. the kernel may be larger than the explicit Weil radical.

III-B is therefore the faithfulness audit for the geometric realization.

## 4. The target categories

There are only two acceptable target categories.

### Target A: direct square target

For each \(T\), realize the divisor package on a regular proper model
\(\mathcal Y_T/\operatorname{Spec}\mathbb Z\) of relative dimension two
and define

\[
 \overline M_f\in\widehat{\operatorname{Pic}}_{\rm int}(\mathcal Y_T),
 \qquad M_f\cdot H_T=0.
 \tag{4.1}
\]

This is the direct route to Yuan--Zhang in generic dimension two, with
a fixed nef and big polarization \(\overline H_T\).

### Target B: precise adelic replacement

If the source metric is naturally adelic rather than modelwise smooth,
realize instead

\[
 \overline M_f\in\widehat{\operatorname{Pic}}_{\rm int}(\mathcal Y_T),
 \qquad M_f\cdot H_T=0
 \tag{4.2}
\]

in the exact integrable adelic category to which Yuan--Zhang applies.

No third option is admitted.  In particular, a purely Hilbert or
distributional target with no proved Hodge theorem is insufficient for
Part IV.

An unpolarized Faltings--Hriljac pairing is a separate curve route.  It
is available only after constructing an independent pairing-preserving
pushforward from the square to a curve or Jacobian; Phase 107 does not
currently contain such a map.

## 5. The desired Abel--Jacobi map

### Definition 5.1: finite-support realization map

Fix \(T\) and let \(D_{f,T}\) be the realized divisor on \(\mathcal Y_T\)
coming from the source divisor \(D_f\) with support inside \(S_T\).
The desired realization map is

\[
 \mathcal A_T:
 \operatorname{Div}_{\mathrm{EF}}(T)
 \longrightarrow
 \widehat{\operatorname{Pic}}^0(\mathcal X_T)
 \quad\text{or}\quad
 \widehat{\operatorname{Pic}}^0_{\rm int}(\mathcal X_T),
 \tag{5.1}
\]

defined by

\[
 \mathcal A_T(D_f)=\overline M_f.
 \tag{5.2}
\]

Here \(\operatorname{Div}_{\mathrm{EF}}(T)\) denotes the finite-support
subspace generated by prime-power returns with \(k\log p\le T\), together
with the polar, diagonal, and archimedean generators.

### Requirement 5.1: source-defined metric

The metric on \(\overline M_f\) must be induced from the Gamma--polar
Green package of `107_05`, not from the already completed Weil form
\(\mathcal Q_W\).

This is the stop-test content of III-B(2): positivity may be used only
after the map is constructed.

## 6. Compatibility requirements

The map \(\mathcal A_T\) must satisfy four compatibilities.

### 6.1. Additivity and finite support

\[
 \mathcal A_T(D_1+D_2)=\mathcal A_T(D_1)\otimes\mathcal A_T(D_2),
 \tag{6.1}
\]

and every compactly supported test must factor through one finite level
\(\mathcal A_T\).

### 6.2. Degree zero

For every admissible source divisor \(D_f\),

\[
 \deg(\overline M_f)=0
 \tag{6.2}
\]

relative to the fixed polarization on \(\mathcal Y_T\) or its adelic
analogue.

### 6.3. Scaling covariance

The flow action on source correspondences must descend to weight one on
the Picard/Jacobian side:

\[
 \mathcal A_T(\vartheta_t\cdot D_f)
 =
 e^{t/2}\,\mathcal A_T(D_f)
 \tag{6.3}
\]

in the precise sense of the chosen Picard/Jacobian target.

The exponent \(1/2\) records the critical normalization already built
into the prime coefficients \(p^{-k/2}\).  The statement here is not a
proof but a compatibility obligation.

### 6.4. Pairing transport

The source intersection pairing must match the height pairing:

\[
 \langle D_f,D_g\rangle_{\rm src}
 =
 -\,\overline M_f\cdot\overline M_g\cdot\overline H_T.
 \tag{6.4}
\]

Equation (6.4) is the real content behind the future terminal identity.
Without it, Part IV cannot begin.

## 7. Exact-kernel audit

This is the load-bearing theorem target of III-B.

### Conjecture 7.1: faithful realization modulo the Weil radical

After tensoring with \(\mathbb R\), the map \(\mathcal A_T\) satisfies

\[
 \boxed{
 \ker \mathcal A_T
 =
 \mathfrak R_W\cap \operatorname{Div}_{\mathrm{EF}}(T)_{\mathbb R}.
 }
 \tag{7.1}
\]

Passing to the filtered union over \(T\) yields

\[
 \boxed{
 \ker(f\mapsto \overline M_f)=\mathfrak R_W
 }
 \tag{7.2}
\]

on the precise real test space of `107_00`.

### Consequence 7.1: radical jets

For the known radical jets

\[
 r_j=\frac{K^{(2j)}}K-4^{-j},
 \tag{7.3}
\]

one must have

\[
 \mathcal A_T(r_j)
 \text{ torsion}
 \qquad\text{for all }j
 \tag{7.4}
\]

whenever \(r_j\) belongs to the admissible finite-support test class.
After realification, these classes vanish.

### Consequence 7.2: no larger kernel

If a non-radical test \(f\notin\mathfrak R_W\) maps to torsion or to zero
in the realified target, then the candidate realization fails.

This forbids replacing the exact radical by a merely finite-codimensional
quotient or by an uncontrolled completion kernel.

## 8. Why the Jacobian target is plausible but not yet sufficient

The Connes--Consani arithmetic Jacobian is the natural source-side
candidate because it already packages:

1. rank-one torsion-free groups with rigidification;
2. the generic orbit and the prime fibres;
3. the monoidal boundary phenomena required by the spectral picture.

However, Phase 39 and `106.164` also make clear what it does not yet
provide by itself:

1. no proved arithmetic intersection pairing of the Faltings--Hriljac
   type on the desired degree-one target;
2. no built-in theorem identifying its classes with metrized line bundles
   on a proper arithmetic surface;
3. no proof that its kernel agrees exactly with \(\mathfrak R_W\).

Therefore the Jacobian is a guiding universal target, not yet the final
theorem.

## 9. Construction program

III-B now breaks into five concrete tasks.

1. Define \(\mathcal A_T\) on the generators
   \(F_{\mathrm v},F_{\mathrm h},\Delta,Z_\infty,\Gamma_{p,k}\).
2. Prove additivity, degree zero, and compatibility with transpose and
   scaling.
3. Identify the induced metric with the source Gamma--polar Green metric.
4. Prove pairing transport (6.4).
5. Prove the exact kernel identity (7.1)--(7.2).

The fifth task is the equality-case audit and cannot be postponed to the
very end.
