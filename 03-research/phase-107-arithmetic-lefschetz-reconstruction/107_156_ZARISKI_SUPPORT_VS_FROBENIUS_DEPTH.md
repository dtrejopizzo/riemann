# 107.156 -- Zariski finite support does not bound Frobenius depth

## 1. Published global-section theorem

Connes--Consani's construction of

\[
 \Theta:\operatorname{Spec}\mathbb Z\longrightarrow\widehat{\mathbb N_0^\times}
\]

uses the sheaf \(\mathcal S\) whose stalk at \(p\) is
\(\mathbb Z[1/p]_+\) and whose generic stalk is \(0\).  Their definition
and Proposition on the pulled-back structure sheaf state that a section
on a Zariski open \(U\) is zero at all but finitely many primes.

Thus the published geometry already proves finite support in the prime
coordinate.  This is the correct global input for the finite-support
part of Phase 107.

## 2. Denominator-depth obstruction

Finite prime support is not finite generation.  Fix one prime \(p\).
The local additive group contains

\[
 1,\ p^{-1},\ p^{-2},\ldots .
\]

All these elements lie in the ordinary absolute-value ball
\(\{x\in\mathbb Z[1/p]:|x|\le1\}\).

> **Theorem.** This ball is not linearly generated by any finite family
> with coefficients in \(\{0,\pm1\}\), even if the mass condition is
> omitted.

**Proof.** Let \(F\subset\mathbb Z[1/p]\) be finite.  There is \(K\) such
that every element of \(F\) belongs to \(p^{-K}\mathbb Z\).  Every
integral linear combination of \(F\), and therefore every signed subset
sum, still belongs to \(p^{-K}\mathbb Z\).  But
\(p^{-(K+1)}\notin p^{-K}\mathbb Z\).  Hence \(F\) does not generate the
ball. \(\square\)

The same argument applies to any mass that assigns uniformly bounded
mass to the sequence \(p^{-k}\), including a fixed \(\log p\) place
weight.

## 3. Exact support requirement

The global pulled-back sheaf supplies

\[
 \#\{p:\xi_p\ne0\}<\infty,
\]

but finite CC dimension additionally requires, at every admitted prime,
a bound on the Frobenius/denominator coordinate \(j\) in
\(a=c p^j\).

This is precisely the \(R\)-coordinate of the finite levels in 107_154.
Combining the published Zariski support theorem with 107_155 gives the
remaining condition for a divisor \(D\):

\[
 \operatorname{supp}_{\rm prime}(D)\text{ finite}
 \quad\text{and}\quad
 \operatorname{depth}_p(D)<\infty
 \text{ for each admitted }p.
\]

The first condition is inherited from the geometry.  The second must be
derived from the divisor/correspondence support \(k\log p\le T\); it
cannot be inferred from Zariski support alone.

## 4. Consequence

This identifies the exact unresolved descent datum in the local
pro-system: prove that a divisor supported on finitely many returns
\((p,k)\) admits no monomial section deeper than a bound determined
before cohomology is computed.  If such a bound is proved, 107_155 gives
stabilization.  If arbitrarily deep \(p^{-j}\) remain admissible for one
fixed divisor, finite integer-dimensional Riemann--Roch is impossible.
