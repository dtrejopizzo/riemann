# 107.165 -- Universal support enrichment above the idempotent square

## 1. Construction

Let \(M\) be a commutative additive monoid.  The semiring

\[
 \mathbb N[M]
 =\left\{\sum_{u\in M}c_uX^u:c_u\in\mathbb N,
 \ c_u=0\text{ almost everywhere}\right\}
\]

is the free commutative semiring generated by \(M\).  Its additive
Grothendieck completion is the monoid ring

\[
 \mathbb Z[M]=K_0^+(\mathbb N[M]).
 \tag{1.1}
\]

For the square of the Arithmetic Site, take

\[
 M=\mathbb Z^2
\]

globally, with the \(\mathbb N^{\times2}\)-action

\[
 (r,s)(a,b)=(ra,sb).
\]

This defines semiring and ring objects in the presheaf topos
\(\widehat{\mathbb N^{\times2}}\).  At the point determined by ordered
groups \((H_1,H_2)\), the corresponding monomial exponents are
\(H_1^+\times H_2^+\); in particular, over the prime point \(p\) they
give the rings used in 107_163.

## 2. Tropical shadow

Let \(\mathcal U(M)\) be the semiring of finitely generated upper sets
of \(M\) for the product order, with union and Minkowski sum.  Define

\[
 \mathrm{Trop}_+(f)
 =\mathrm{Supp}(f)+M_+.
 \tag{2.1}
\]

For positive coefficients,

\[
 \mathrm{Trop}_+(f+g)
 =\mathrm{Trop}_+(f)\cup\mathrm{Trop}_+(g),
\]

and

\[
 \mathrm{Trop}_+(fg)
 =\mathrm{Trop}_+(f)+\mathrm{Trop}_+(g).
\]

Thus (2.1) is a surjective semiring morphism onto the unreduced Boolean
tensor square.  Composition with convex hull is the published Newton
reduction

\[
 \mathbb N[M]\twoheadrightarrow\mathcal O_{\rm unred}
 \twoheadrightarrow\mathcal O_{\rm red}.
 \tag{2.2}
\]

Both arrows are \(\mathbb N^{\times2}\)-equivariant.

There is no additive extension of (2.1) to \(\mathbb Z[M]\), by
107_164.  For a signed polynomial, its nonzero support still gives a
well-defined Newton shadow, but cancellation makes this operation
non-additive.  This is required behavior, not a defect to quotient out.

## 3. Universality and minimality

**Theorem.**  The diagram (2.2) is the initial coefficient-sensitive
semiring enhancement of the monomial square.  More precisely, for any
commutative semiring \(R\) and monoid homomorphism
\(\iota:M\to(R,\cdot)\), there is a unique semiring homomorphism

\[
 \widetilde\iota:\mathbb N[M]\longrightarrow R,
 \qquad
 \sum c_uX^u\longmapsto\sum c_u\iota(u).
\]

If the target is a ring, this extends uniquely through (1.1) to
\(\mathbb Z[M]\).

This is the universal property of monoid semirings.  It proves that no
smaller canonical additive enrichment can retain arbitrary monomial
coefficients.  Newton reduction is recovered by forgetting
multiplicities and then interior support points.

## 4. Frobenius correspondences and bounded modules

For coprime \((n,m)\), the map

\[
 \Lambda_{n,m}:\mathbb Z[H_p^+\times H_p^+]
 \longrightarrow\mathbb Z[H_p^+],
 \qquad X^aY^b\longmapsto T^{na+mb}
\]

is induced by a monoid homomorphism and hence by universality is a ring
map of the enrichment.  On the positive cone, its least-exponent shadow
is exactly the CC rational Frobenius correspondence.  Its kernels are
the directed root ideals computed in 107_163.

For \(N\ge0\), put

\[
 E_N(M)=\left\{f\in\mathbb Z[M]:\sum_u|c_u|\le N\right\}.
\]

This is a bounded \(\mathbb S[\pm1]\)-submodule of
\(H\mathbb Z[M]\).  Frobenius acts by basis maps and preserves mass;
\(\Lambda_{n,m}\) is mass-contracting.  At finite support/depth, these
are exactly the finite-dimensional modules of 107_150 and 107_154.

Consequently the enriched square has all three categorical features
needed before forming tolerant cohomology:

1. a global two-ruling object with Frobenius action;
2. finite-level abelian coefficient modules with intrinsic dimension;
3. exact diagonal/Frobenius kernels compatible with level transition.

## 5. Remaining sheaf theorem

The construction is an object of the Arithmetic Site square, not yet a
proved replacement for its structure sheaf in Riemann--Roch.  The next
step is to prove descent for the bounded submodules under the chosen
cover and to show that the resulting Cech terms stabilize for every
finite-support divisor.  Only after that can 107_151 define the global
middle tolerance cohomology.

No claim is made here about properness, Serre duality in dimension two,
intersection numbers, or the RR formula.

## 6. Falsifier

The verifier operates on actual prime actions \(2,3,5,7,11\).  It
checks semiring compatibility of (2.1), equivariance under both
Frobenius rulings, compatibility of \(\Lambda_{n,m}\), and mass
contraction.  It also verifies a signed cancellation example where an
additive tropical extension would give the wrong answer.
