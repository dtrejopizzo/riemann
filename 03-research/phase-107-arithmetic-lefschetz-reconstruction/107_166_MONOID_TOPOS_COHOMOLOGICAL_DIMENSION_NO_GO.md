# 107.166 -- The raw monoid-topos cohomology is not surface cohomology

## 1. The tempting route

The support enrichment of 107_165 is a global object of the square
topos \(\widehat{\mathbb N^{\times2}}\).  A tempting next step is to
define its cohomology as the derived functors of invariant global
sections in that presheaf topos.  This note proves that this route has
the wrong cohomological dimension before any divisor is introduced.

## 2. Finite-prime calculation

Fix a finite set \(P\) of \(r\) primes.  The submonoid generated by the
two Frobenius rulings is the free commutative monoid

\[
 M_P\cong\mathbb N^{2r}.
\]

After integral linearization its monoid ring is

\[
 R_P=\mathbb Z[x_p,y_p:p\in P].
\]

Let \(\mathbb Z\) be the constant module, on which every \(x_p,y_p\)
acts as the identity.  Equivalently,

\[
 \mathbb Z=R_P/(x_p-1,y_p-1:p\in P).
\]

**Theorem.**  For \(0\le k\le2r\),

\[
 \operatorname{Ext}_{R_P}^k(\mathbb Z,\mathbb Z)
 \cong\bigwedge^k\mathbb Z^{2r},
 \tag{2.1}
\]

and it vanishes only for \(k>2r\).

**Proof.**  The sequence

\[
 (x_p-1,y_p-1)_{p\in P}
\]

is a regular sequence of length \(2r\) in the polynomial ring \(R_P\).
Its Koszul complex is a free resolution of \(\mathbb Z\).  Applying
\(\operatorname{Hom}_{R_P}(-,\mathbb Z)\) kills every differential,
because all \(x_p-1,y_p-1\) act as zero on \(\mathbb Z\).  The degree
\(k\) term is therefore the \(k\)-th exterior power in (2.1). \(\square\)

For \(r\ge2\), degrees \(3\) and \(4\) are already nonzero.  As the
support bound \(T\) grows, \(r=\pi(e^T)\) is unbounded, so the amplitude
of this derived-invariants theory is unbounded.

## 3. Consequence

A Weil square requires cohomological amplitude \([0,2]\), with middle
\(H^1\) and top \(H^2\).  Formula (2.1) proves that the ordinary derived
global sections of the raw monoid-action topos cannot supply that
surface theory.  The extra degrees count independent prime-action
directions, not geometric dimensions of an arithmetic surface.

Thus 107_165 provides the correct coefficient object but not the
cohomology functor.  One must obtain the two-dimensional complex from
additional geometry: a two-dimensional Cech cover, the foliated complex
lift of the Scaling Site, or another construction with a proved
amplitude bound.  Truncating the Koszul complex by hand at degree two
is forbidden because it discards nonzero functorial classes without a
geometric vanishing theorem.

This no-go is compatible with 107_151.  Tolerance cohomology defines the
middle term once a genuine three-term geometric complex is supplied; it
does not turn an unbounded monoid resolution into a surface complex.

## 4. Scope

The theorem does not say that every sheaf on the Arithmetic Site has
nonzero high cohomology.  It says that the ambient derived-invariants
construction has unbounded amplitude and already fails the surface gate
on the constant module.  Any successful row-(a) theory must explain why
its specific geometric realization has amplitude two.

## 5. Falsifier

For the actual visible prime sets at \(T=2,3,4,5\), the verifier computes
all ranks

\[
 \operatorname{rank}\operatorname{Ext}^k
 =\binom{2\pi(e^T)}k
\]

and checks explicitly that higher cohomology appears as soon as two
primes are present.  It can return `NO` if the claimed Koszul amplitude
or rank formula fails.
