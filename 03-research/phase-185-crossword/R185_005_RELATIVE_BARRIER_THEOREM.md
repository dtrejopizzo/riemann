## 1. The class of methods 𝔐

### 1.1 Common framework (R166_802 §1; R185_001 §1; R185_003 §0)

- Witness F of type T_c, G = F̂ real, real-rooted, G ≢ 0; hypothetical quadruple at t_q; parameters π = (t_q, G).
- **Admissible tests 𝒦 of bounded type:** K continuous, K ≥ 0 on ℝ, of type ≤ Δ_max, with Weil admissibility of
  Ψ = G²K (R166_802 §1; R185_091 m4). 𝒦 is a convex cone.
- Exact identity (1.1): Z_K(Σ) = 𝔄_K − Tail_K(λ), linear in K (checked against eq:nuw of Paper 47: R185_091 m5).
  Z_K = Q_K + R_K + O_K, O_K ≥ 0 (real zeros).
- **Remainder models** R (other quadruples):
  - (M1) test-by-test bounds R_K ≥ −S_K; witness set O_U, U_K := 𝔄_K − Q^{hyp}_K + S_K.
  - (M2) joint restriction R ∈ 𝓡, 𝓡 closed convex; **relaxed** witness set W₂ = 𝔄 − Q^{hyp} − 𝓡 − C̄, with C̄
    the closed cone generated by (G(γ)²K(γ))_K, γ ∈ ℝ (R185_001 Lemma 4; R185_091 m2). W₂ forgets the integrality of
    multiplicities and the Riemann–von Mangoldt density (R185_091 M2).

### 1.2 Arithmetic statements: the atoms (R185_004 Def 5 + CROSSWORD §4.2)

Arithmetic data: the class 𝒟 of discrete Beurling systems (R185_004 §0), ζ ∈ 𝒟. Deletion d ↦ d∖S, S ⊂ ℙ∩(X,∞).
An **atom** is a statement true at ζ and provable without zeros, of one of these four types:

- **L (local):** depends only on Λ∣[1,Y] for some Y < ∞. Includes any nonlinear operation on Λ∣[1,Y] and its
  convolutions (R185_004 Lemma 1), the window data, and the finite window→tail identities (Λ∗1 = log on [1,Y]).
- **D (closed under deletion):** {d : P(d)} stable under d ↦ d∖S beyond some X_P. Includes Λ ≥ 0, support in log ℕ,
  integrality, B_int, multiplicativity, **internal** Selberg and Möbius (μ_d), upper Chebyshev, every upper bound with
  weights ≥ 0, in particular the upper sieves S1–S3 and measure bounds (R185_004 Lemma 2, Table §2).
- **C (completeness):** P(d) ∧ d ≡_{X_W} ζ ⟹ N_d = ⌊·⌋ on [X_W,∞), with **X_P ≤ X_W**, the window (R185_092 M3;
  genuine examples have X_P = 1). Type priority C > Q > L/D (R185_092 m7); 𝔐_Λ is a finitary closure (m8). Includes
  Λ∗1_ℕ = log over all ℕ, Euler = ζ exactly, μ_d = ℕ's μ, Selberg with ℕ's μ (R185_004 Lemma 3).
- **Q_θ (counting):** consequence of N_d(x) = A_d x + O(x^θ), or a lower counting bound ψ_d(x) ≥ cx on [X,∞).

**Definition (the class 𝔐).** A *proof scheme in 𝔐* of "there is no first-contact witness" (equivalently, of
W_T ≥ 0 on 𝒫_T for every T on the declared carrier) consists of:
1. an arithmetic sentence P ∈ 𝔐_Λ := closure of the atoms L, D, C, Q under ∧ and ∨ (nonlinear zero-free operations
   fall within each atom, because L and D are semantic classes of sets: R185_004 Def 5 and audit note 2);
2. an arbitrary family 𝔉 ⊂ 𝒦 of admissible tests of type ≤ Δ_max and their identities (1.1);
3. O_K ≥ 0 and the information about the remainder in model (M1) or (M2);
4. inertia, remainder norm bounds, and the other I1–I18 inputs (CROSSWORD §3.1) enter only through their shadow
   in (1)–(3); their own NO-GOs are cited, not reproved.

The logic is **proof transfer** (type W3; R185_091 m8, R185_003 §0): a scheme that only uses (1)–(3) holds for every
λ′ satisfying P, so it excludes the witness only if Sh_𝔉(P) ∩ W = ∅.

## 2. Theorem (Relative barrier)

### 2.1 Statement (Spanish original)

**Theorem BR.** Let a proof scheme in 𝔐 of "there is no first-contact witness" on the declared carrier have
sentence P ∈ 𝔐_Λ in normal form ∨_j K_j, and let J₀ ≠ ∅ be the set of disjuncts true at ζ. Then:

**(0) Factorization.** If the scheme excludes the witness of parameter π, there exists a single test K* ∈ 𝒦 (in (M1)
a K* ∈ 𝔉; in (M2) with 𝓡 compact in the product topology, a finite combination K* = Σ a_iK_i ≥ 0 on ℝ) such that
Tail_{K*}(Λ_ζ) > U_{K*}, equivalently, by true (1.1), Z_{K*}(Σ_ζ) < Q^{hyp}_{K*} − S(K*). This inequality is
false if the quadruple π is present (E1 at the witness). **Scope:** in (M1), O_U is excluded, which is R166_802's
witness set. In (M2), the **relaxed set W₂** is excluded, not the true witness (R185_091 M2, R185_092 M1).

Moreover, at least one of the following holds:

**(a) Deletion.** Some K_j, j ∈ J₀, has only L and D atoms. Then the sentence is deletion-closed beyond the window,
and exclusion of O_U (M1) is equivalent to ∃K: E_K > U_K (power contribution, invariant under deletion). In (M2),
the exclusion is of W₂. By (0), every
exclusion of this type is the lower bound of a single test Tail_{K*}(Λ_ζ) > U_{K*}, i.e. the zero-side statement
Z_{K*}(Σ_ζ) < Q^{hyp} − S.

**(b) Identification.** Some K_j, j ∈ J₀, contains a C atom. Then 𝒞_{K_j} ∩ {d ≡_X ζ} = {ζ} and the exclusion is RH
unchanged.

**(c) Counting.** All K_j, j ∈ J₀, are only of type Q (with L/D). Within ζ's (1.1) frame, the exclusion is covered
by **circularity**, with no falsifier: by (0) (R185_003 Thm 1/2) there exists K* with Z_{K*}(Σ_ζ) < Q^{hyp} − S, false
if the quadruple is present, and R166_802 Thm 1/3 requires at that scale the exponent strength δ_q = Θ − ½. (Class
remark, outside the barrier: for class Ex, θ_P > ⅓ is falsified with BFdisc glued to ℕ ≤ X (sketch of R185_092
M4(1)); the subsystems ℙ∖S with p_k ≥ 2^{2^k} give RH; and θ_P ≤ ⅓ is OPEN, a class problem: Paper 48 Problem 6.)

**Conclusion.** No scheme in 𝔐 produces a statement strictly weaker than RH that excludes every witness.
In (a), the conjunction over all π of the inequalities of (0) is the absence of every witness, which is D, and D is
equivalent to RH on the declared carrier (cluster E1, I1). In (b), the exclusion is RH. In (c), it is false, or it is
RH, or it is stronger than RH. This holds except at the residuals marked in §2.3.

**Final label by branch (Revision 2):**
- (0): PROVED in (M1) for O_U. In (M2), PROVED for W₂ with 𝓡 compact, which by Tychonoff includes every closed 𝓡
  with coordinate-wise bounds. Narrow residual: ρ1′ OPEN.
- (a): core PROVED. The zero-side reading is PROVED via (0) applied to Λ_ζ. "⟹ E1 via Λ_∅" is ARGUED, and is not used.
- (b): PROVED, with Def 5(C) corrected (X_P ≤ window).
- (c): PROVED by circularity, with the same scope as (0). (iii-a) and (iii-c) stop being leaves of the barrier.
- Conclusion: PROVED relative to 𝔐, modulo ρ1′, ρ2 (M2 only), and the exhaustiveness of 𝔐 (ARGUED).

### 2.2 Statement (English, for Papers 47/48)

**Theorem (relative barrier).** Let a proof scheme in the class 𝔐 establish "no first-contact witness" on the declared
carrier, through an arithmetic sentence P ∈ 𝔐_Λ in normal form ∨_j K_j, admissible tests 𝔉 ⊂ 𝒦 of type ≤ Δ_max, and
remainder information in model (M1), or in model (M2) with 𝓡 compact in the product topology. Then:
(0) every exclusion of the witness π factors through a single admissible test K* ∈ 𝒦:
Tail_{K*}(Λ_ζ) > 𝔄_{K*} − Q^{hyp}_{K*} + S(K*) ⟺ Z_{K*}(Σ_ζ) < Q^{hyp}_{K*} − S(K*), and this inequality fails whenever
π is present. In (M2) the conclusion concerns the relaxed witness set W₂.
Moreover, at least one of the following holds:
(a) some disjunct true at ζ is deletion-closed beyond the window, and the exclusion is equivalent to ∃K: E_K > U_K;
(b) some disjunct true at ζ contains a completeness atom, which identifies ζ, and the exclusion is RH;
(c) every disjunct true at ζ is of counting type. Then, inside the explicit-formula frame (1.1) of ζ, the exclusion is
covered by circularity alone (part (0); R166_802 Thms 1, 3): it requires exponent strength Θ − ½ at the witness's scale.
No falsifier is used, and the frontier θ ≤ 1/3 is a separate class problem, not a gap in this theorem.
In (M1) "exclusion" means of O_U. In (M2) it means of the relaxed set W₂, not of the true witness set.
Consequently, no scheme in 𝔐 excludes all witnesses through a statement strictly weaker than RH, except possibly through
the residues (ρ1′) (narrow: non-compact couplings, including ba(𝔉) functionals), (ρ2) and the exhaustiveness of 𝔐 (ARGUED).

   and in (a) it is Ex_W. They connect via R166_802 Thm 2, PROVED.

## 3. Consequences, honestly

**(i) It is a barrier RELATIVE to 𝔐, not an impossibility theorem.** The theorem classifies the form of every
exclusion obtainable with 𝔐's inputs. It does not say RH is unprovable, nor that D is unreachable. Mandatory
vocabulary: "relative to 𝔐 (Def §1.2)" and never "absolute barrier" (R185_004, For Paper 47/48).

**(ii) What lies outside 𝔐 is the positive program.**
- **Unbounded type / (TC).** Phase 164: (★_cell) ⟹ (TC). (TC) on the witness is RH (R164 README, Rem 4.8a); (TC)
  and (★_cell) remain **OPEN**. R185_003 (ρ1″) locates here the only escape from reduction to a test in 𝒦, and
  **does not** assert any implication with (TC).
- **Zero-side inputs with the Euler product** (zero density, (Dom_θ), (P_gap), integrality of the count: ρ2). They
  lie outside because they are not statements about Λ. Used uniformly they prove too much (I6 Thm 4: zero-free strip
  = RH) and used with Euler in the window they fall into I11 (M_m / f_t).
- **Joint statements (Λ, Γ) not already equivalent to RH.** W11 says the prime + archimedean coupling is RH, and
  W11′/I8 says there is no third definition of the remainder. The gap that remains is narrow: a coupling that is
  neither one-sided positivity nor a Sonin norm bound.
- **Operator-type inputs.** W10: there is no MW-5 carrier or h⁰. The object is missing, and no meta-theorem of the
  corpus excludes it.
- **Atoms outside L ∪ D ∪ C ∪ Q** (O5). Exhaustiveness is ARGUED. A new true, zero-free atom, not closed under
  deletion, without completeness and not of counting type, would be the counterexample to exhaustiveness and the
  only live arithmetic box (R185_002 Conj G, which is PROVED within 𝔐_Λ and ARGUED outside).
- **The frontier (iii-c)**, θ ≤ ⅓: no longer a leaf of the barrier, but a class problem. It is pure Beurling,
  publishable, and cannot close D (CROSSWORD §5, item 9).

(witness, W_T, certificates, tails Tail_K); Paper 48 = the map.
