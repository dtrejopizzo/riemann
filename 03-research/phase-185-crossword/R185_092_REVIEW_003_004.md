## MAJOR

**M1 (R185_003 Thm 2, scope).** Inherits R185_091 M2. W = w₀ − 𝓡 − C̄ is the relaxation: C̄ forgets the integrality of multiplicities and the Riemann–von Mangoldt density, and contains o = 0. The theorem classifies exclusions of the **relaxed set W₂**, not of the actual witness. The final statement ("excludes the witness") should read "excludes W (= O_U in M1, W₂ in M2)". In (M1) there is no problem, because O_U is the definition from R166_802.

**M2 (R185_003 Prop 3 and (ρ1′): the topology.)** Prop 3 only treats functionals ℓ_μ with μ a Radon measure on compact 𝔉, i.e. the dual of C(𝔉).
- If 𝓡 is defined by sup bounds over K, the natural space is ℓ^∞(𝔉), whose dual is ba(𝔉) (finitely additive measures, Banach limits along K_n). Prop 3 does not cover this.
- What survives for μ ∈ ba(𝔉):
  - (a) μ ≥ 0 in (M1), by the same argument with t = U − s·1_E.
  - (b) K_μ(γ) := μ(K ↦ K(γ)) ≥ 0 for all γ.
  - If the types are ≤ Δ_max and the family is bounded in PW, then K_μ lies in the PW ball of type Δ_max, which is convex and weak-* compact. This uses the bounded type.
  - **Tail_{K_μ} = μ(Tail_K)** holds, because the tail is a **finite** sum of evaluations at n ∈ [X, Xe^{Δ_max}]. This is the other use of the bounded type.
- What **fails** is Z_{K_μ} = μ(Z_K), and in general 𝔄_{K_μ} = μ(𝔄_K): an infinite sum over ρ does not commute with a merely finitely additive μ.
- This is precisely the loss of type (ρ1′), and the memo presents it as covered. **Correction:** state Prop 3 for "Radon μ (weighted ℓ¹ / C(𝔉))" and add to (ρ1′) the ba(𝔉) case: K_μ ∈ 𝒦 and the tail transfers, but the zero side does not.
- **Narrowing of (ρ1′) in the memo's favor.** If 𝓡 ⊂ ∏_K[−S_K, S_K] with all S_K < ∞ and 𝓡 closed, then it is compact in the product topology (Tychonoff), and Thm 2 applies.
  - For one-sided bounds 𝓡 = {r_K ≥ −S_K}: since K ≥ 0, C̄ lies in the positive orthant and 0 ∈ C̄, so −𝓡 − C̄ = S − ℝ^𝔉_{≥0}. This set is closed and is case (M1).
  - Hence (ρ1′) only lives on 𝓡 with coordinates unbounded in both directions or with non-compact couplings. The memo should say so: the residue is much smaller than "𝓡 non-compact".

**M3 (R185_004 Def 5(C): quantification of X).** Atom C says "P(d) ∧ d ≡_X ζ ⟹ N_d = ⌊·⌋ on [X,∞) **for some X**". In (ii) it is used with the given window X_W.
- If the X of the atom exceeds X_W, nothing is known on [X_W, X_P] and there is no identification. Example: "N(x) = ⌊x⌋ for x ≥ X_P" with X_P > X_W.
- **Correction:** require X_P ≤ X_W (equivalently, window ≥ X_P), or define C with "on [1,∞) given d ≡_{X_W} ζ". The genuine examples (Λ∗1_ℕ = log on all of ℕ, Euler = ζ, μ_d = μ) have X = 1 and are unaffected.

**M4 (R185_004 U3(iii-a): exactly what it falsifies).**
- **(1) The window CONDITIONAL can be removed for Ex** (sketch, to be verified). Replace the primes of BFdisc ≤ X with those of ℕ ≤ X:
  ζ_spliced = ζ_𝒫 · ∏_{p≤X}(1−p^{−s})^{−1} · ∏_{q∈𝒫, q≤X}(1−q^{−s})^{m_q}.
  - The quotient is a finite product, holomorphic and zero-free in σ > 0. Hence the zeros at (1+θ)/2 of R163_803 Thm C(iii) survive.
  - The multiplicities remain integer ≥ 0.
  - N_spliced = N_𝒫 ∗ ν, with ν of variation ≪ (log x)^{O_X(1)}, gives ρ′x + O(x^{θ+2ε}).
  - On [1,X] the system coincides with ℕ, because primes > X do not generate integers ≤ X.
  This turns (iii-a) into PROVED for Ex(P) with window, provided the remaining D/L atoms of K_j are satisfied by the spliced system. It must be checked atom by atom: upper Chebyshev yes, B_int yes; support on log ℕ no, and it is excluded by hypothesis.
- **(2) But Ex(P) is not Ex_W.** A class falsifier refutes "P ⇒ no zero off the line in the class". It does not refute "P plus the explicit formula (1.1) **of ζ** excludes the witness".
  - The splice has a different residue (ρ′ ≠ 1), a different polar term and a different explicit formula, so it does not realize the framework (1.1) with 𝔄_K of ζ.
  - The √p systems of R184_005 do have the window and the zeros of ζ, but have no zeros off the line, so they falsify nothing.
  - M_m has a window and zeros off the line, but is not discrete, and the Q sentences on Λ are not defined for it.
  - **No system is known with the window of ζ, an off-line zero, (1.1) with 𝔄 of ζ, and Q.** The gap is real.
- **(3) The gap does not affect the barrier.** A Q-type exclusion within the framework (1.1) is covered, independently of any falsifier, by circularity: R185_003 Thm 1 in (M1) gives a K* with a zero-side inequality, false if the quartet holds, and R166_802 Thm 1/Thm 3 gives the strength of exponent δ_q at one scale.
  **Recommendation:** rewrite (iii) as follows: "(iii) for Ex_W: circular via R185_003 Thm 1/Thm 2 (no falsifier needed); for class Ex: (iii-a) falsifier [PROVED after splicing], (iii-c) OPEN". This way (iii-c) stops being a hole in the row-D barrier and remains only a class problem (Paper 48 Problem 6).

**M5 (R185_004 U3(iii-b): wrong citation and a false bound).**
- R184_005 Prop 4.1 is the **√p substitution** system (primes of ℕ outside S, plus √p for p ∈ S). It is not a subsystem of ℕ: its support is not on log ℕ and it violates the "support on log ℕ" atom that defines (iii-b).
- The correct family is 𝒫′ = ℙ∖S with p_k ≥ 2^{2^k}. Then N(x) = Σ_J (−1)^{|J|}⌊x/d_J⌋, with d_J = ∏_{p∈J} p, and the same count from R184_005 gives N = ρx + O(log x). It must be written out and proved in the memo, not merely cited.
- "Σ_{p∉𝒫′} p^{−1/2} < ∞ ⟹ ∏(1−p^{−s}) holomorphic and zero-free in σ > 0" is false in general: it only gives σ > ½ (plus continuity at σ = ½). The conclusion of (iii-b) only needs σ > ½, so it survives. For S doubly exponential it holds in σ > 0.

## MINOR

- **m1 (R185_003 Thm 1).** Correct. K* = K*(λ) depends on λ ∈ Sh(B), which is legitimate, and the conclusion (ii)–(iii) is stated only for Λ_ζ, as it should be. Make explicit in the statement that U_K uses 𝔄_K of ζ (fixed window) for every λ′. It is the R166_802 framework, not an identity for λ′.
- **m2 (R185_003 Lemma 7).** The convergence of Σ_γ G(γ)²K(γ) for every K ∈ 𝔉 must be a hypothesis on the hypothetical configuration, not only on that of ζ. The RvM density suffices for the admissible class.
- **m3 (R185_003 (ρ1″)).** The citation is correct: the phase-164 README says "(TC) on the witness is RH (Rem 4.8a)", and the direction proved in that phase is (★_cell) ⟹ (TC). The memo claims no implication; it is fine. It is worth adding that (TC) is OPEN and that phase 164 does not prove it (already stated).
- **m4 (R185_004 Lemma 1).** In the recursion, "u ∣ x" should be "u·v = x with u, v in the support of N_d". In a Beurling system divisibility is that of the generated monoid.
- **m5 (R185_004 Lemma 3).** "Λ_d ∗ 1_ℕ" is well defined as a multiplicative convolution of measures on [1,∞). No monoid is needed, because it is a convolution on the group ℝ_{>0}. The internal Beurling identity is log·dN_d = Λ_d ∗ dN_d, always true. The content of (a) is mixing Λ_d with dN_ζ, which is equivalent to N_d = ⌊·⌋. The statement is correct, but it is worth writing out both versions explicitly (as §6 of the audit does for Selberg/Möbius).
- **m6 (R185_004 Obs 4).**
  - Hypotheses (a) and (b) of Thm R hold for every d ∈ 𝒟: N_d = δ₁ = N_ζ on [1, min(q₁,2)) and N_d ≥ 0.
  - A line is missing. Thm R requires absolute convergence in σ > 1. If σ_d > 1, by Landau (coefficients ≥ 0) there is a real singularity at σ_d > 1, contradicting that the polar data are only 0 and 1. So EF₁ forces σ_d ≤ 1.
  - "Conductor 1" is not a hypothesis of Thm R. What is essential is the residues ±1 and finite order (R164_401 stresses that the residue is essential). EF₁ includes them. With that, Obs 4 is correct.
  - The right citation is Thm R. Thm O / Hamburger is the case of support on ℕ and is not enough for general 𝒟.
- **m7 (R185_004 Def 5, overlapping types).** L atoms are also D (stable beyond Y). An atom can be both C and Q. The "type of K" is defined by priority (C > Q > L/D), and this should be stated so that "exactly one" is correct.
- **m8 (R185_004 normal form).** 𝔐_Λ is a **finitary** closure under ∧/∨. The infinite disjunctions (like those of R185_003 Thm 1) and uniform quantification in X remain within the atoms, being semantic. The normal form exists, by finite distributivity. It must be stated that the infinite families of R185_003 are not in 𝔐_Λ except as atoms.
- **m9 (R185_004 (iii-a)).** BFdisc satisfies Q_{θ+ε}, not Q_θ. Choose θ′ ∈ (⅓, θ_P) with θ′ + ε ≤ θ_P. It is trivial, but must be written down.
