# Phase 52 — Homotopic inertia: is m a metric-free secondary invariant?

Since every canonical metric found so far for reading the integer m (the count of off-line zero
quadruplets; RH ⟺ m = 0) turns out to carry ζ via modular structure (phase-51's circularity), this
phase asks whether m can instead be read as a secondary homotopical/categorical invariant — a Toda
bracket, k-invariant, or Adams e-invariant — built with no bilinear form or metric at all, exploiting
the fact that the primary (Witt-class) invariant is known to vanish on the off-line part.

## Results
- Construction of a candidate reading of m as a secondary homotopic invariant (Toda
  bracket/k-invariant/e-invariant style), motivated by the vanishing of the primary Witt-class
  invariant: CONSTRUCTION (Doc 162).
- Adversarial destruction: all three standard architectures of a secondary invariant are shown to
  reduce to a single obstruction already present in the program (the Fredholmness/orientation
  circularity of earlier phases), so the construction does not deliver an RH-free 2m: NO-GO (Doc 163).
