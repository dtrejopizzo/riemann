# Phase 47 — Live Fronts

This phase pushes forward several fronts left open by Phase 46's audits: a direct attack on the minimal-finiteness repulsion statement LP-141 (weaker cousin of LP-134), deciding the spectrality trilemma GAP-142.B for the destructive semilocal object, determining whether the Davenport–Heilbronn function's off-line zeros are "fat" (macroscopically off the line) or merely asymptotically close to it, an attempted repair of Problem 139.A's ¬RH branch, and the open Beurling–Landau frame question GAP-135.A.

## Results
- Attack on LP-141 (a weaker, $\limsup$-type repulsion statement, strictly implied by LP-134): formalized and shown strictly weaker than LP-134; refuted in the purely analytic category by an explicit Hadamard counterexample (an entire function of order 1 with the right functional equation and infinitely many off-line zeros, all simultaneously sub-resolution for any calendar) — so LP-141, like LP-134, needs genuinely arithmetic input; a conditional partial lower bound is proved in a restricted Euler-product category, with a named reduced gap. (NO-GO for the analytic category; PROVED-PARTIAL in the arithmetic category, with a gap)
- Deciding GAP-142.B (spectrality trilemma for the destructive semilocal object $X_S^{\mathrm{ar}}$): proved as a theorem (not merely argued) that the object is never "positive-spectral" with an on-line divisor — the archimedean core's symbol changes sign, so by a Bochner-type criterion it cannot be a positive spectral measure. Consequence: Conjecture H⁺ is not weaker than RH for $\zeta$-type archimedean terms; H⁺ restricted to any finite prime set $S$ is vacuous (empty class); GAP-135.B is diagnosed as ill-posed. The short-window positivity and transmission-identity results of Phase 46 are unaffected and restated. (PROVED — decisive negative result)
- GAP-141.DH (are the Davenport–Heilbronn off-line zeros "fat" or asymptotically sub-resolution?): proved fat in the strongest sense — a positive density of D–H zeros lies at macroscopic (bounded-below) distance from the critical line, via a classical literature fact about zero density in a fixed off-line vertical strip, corroborated by an explicit verified zero. The mechanism is traced to D–H's lack of a genuine Euler product and the resulting density (Bohr–Landau/Voronin) of its values in $\sigma>1$. (PROVED)
- Repair attempt of Problem 139.A's ¬RH branch (via exponential dominance of the classical Weil criterion instead of the divergent normalization constant): a witness construction is produced whose negativity is exponentially dominant with no diverging constants, eliminating the parameter $M$ from the earlier failed proof; the construction is non-effective (the relevant scale is finite but not computed). (PROVED, with an explicit non-effectivity caveat; audited further in Phase 48)
- GAP-135.A (is a union of two arithmetic-progression exponential systems a Riesz/frame sequence in the window $\log q<2T<\log(pq)$?): identified as a genuine open problem in frame theory that the $\zeta$-context illuminates but does not resolve in general; for exact (unperturbed) progressions the answer is believed accessible via model-space (Avdonin/Pavlov–Khrushchev–Nikolskii) machinery and affirmative generically; the genuinely open and new content is the quantitative dependence of the frame bound on the irrationality measure of $\log p/\log q$. (OPEN, with a partial positive expectation)
