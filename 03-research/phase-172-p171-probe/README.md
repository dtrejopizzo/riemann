# Phase 172 — Using the phase-171 counterexample (P₁₇₁) as a probe

Phase 171 built a discrete Beurling system (P₁₇₁) with integer multiplicities, class-level
regularity, and off-line zeros. This phase tests the working hypothesis that there is a single
category [F] of hypotheses (the functional equation) responsible for every place a general
construction fails to transport to ζ, by running P₁₇₁ through the concrete chain of phases
166–170 built for row D and classifying, piece by piece, where transport succeeds or fails.

## Results

- The working hypothesis ("the only relevant category [F] is the functional equation") is **REFUTED** in its literal reading.
- Dependency classification theorem — **PROVED**: every transport failure of P₁₇₁ through the row-D chain falls into exactly one of two families — analytic self-duality centered at 1/2 (functional equation, Gamma factor, poles, quartets, explicit formula), or exact additive geometry of ℕ (integer comb, n↦n+1, Poisson–Euler–Maclaurin, the hyperbola, PC–Tate identities).
- The coupling of ordinary Euler structure over ℕ together with exact Poisson/functional-equation structure on that same lattice is identified as a candidate minimal sufficient condition — **ARGUED**, sufficiency and minimality not proved.
- A totalization of the sign along a counting measure is shown not to be canonical in general (two extensions can agree on the totalized quantity yet take different signs in the residue) — **NO-GO** (round 3) for the separate-sign route within the class examined; not a universal impossibility result.
- Neither RH nor a new positivity window for ζ is proved anywhere in the phase.
