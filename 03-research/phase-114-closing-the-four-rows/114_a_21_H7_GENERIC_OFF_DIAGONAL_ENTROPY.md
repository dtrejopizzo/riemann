## 1. The basic off-diagonal defect

Work in Haran's commutative prop formulation and put

\[
 A=\mathbb Z\boxtimes_{\mathbb F}\mathbb Z.               \tag{1.1}
\]

Let `i_1,i_2:Z->A` be the two structure maps and let

\[
 v_1=i_1(v)=v\boxtimes1,
 \qquad v_2=i_2(v)=1\boxtimes v,                           \tag{1.2}
\]

where `v=(1,1)` is the binary addition generator of `Z`.  The diagonal fold

\[
 \nabla:A\longrightarrow\mathbb Z                         \tag{1.3}
\]

satisfies `nabla(v_1)=nabla(v_2)=v`.

### Proposition 1.1

\[
 v_1\ne v_2\quad\text{in }A.                              \tag{1.4}
\]

### Proof

Haran states that the commutative arithmetic surface `Spec(A)` does not
reduce to its diagonal.  Algebraically, `A` is generated over `F` by `v_1`,
`v_2` and `-1`, while `Z` is generated by `v` and `-1`.  If `v_1=v_2`, the two
structure maps would coincide. Their common map `i:Z->A` satisfies
`nabla i=id_Z`; conversely `i nabla` fixes both generators of `A` and `-1`,
so `i nabla=id_A`. This would make (1.3) an isomorphism and collapse the
surface to its diagonal, contrary to the source statement. QED.

This identifies a literal, not numerical, off-diagonal bit.

## 2. Exact block amplification

For a binary word `epsilon=(epsilon_1,...,epsilon_N) in {1,2}^N`, define the
block operation

\[
 V_\epsilon=\bigoplus_{k=1}^N v_{\epsilon_k}: [2N]\longrightarrow[N].      \tag{2.1}
\]

The direct sum is part of the prop structure.  Let `j_k:[2]->[2N]` and
`r_k:[N]->[1]` be the canonical input inclusion and output projection for the
`k`-th block.

### Theorem 2.1

The family `{V_epsilon:epsilon in {1,2}^N}` has exactly `2^N` distinct
members, and

\[
 \nabla(V_\epsilon)=\bigoplus_{k=1}^Nv                         \tag{2.2}
\]

is independent of `epsilon`.

### Proof

Block extraction gives

\[
 r_k\,V_\epsilon\,j_k=v_{\epsilon_k}.                         \tag{2.3}
\]

If `V_epsilon=V_eta`, (2.3) and Proposition 1.1 imply
`epsilon_k=eta_k` for every `k`; hence the words are equal.  This proves the
cardinality.  Equation (2.2) follows from functoriality of direct sum and
`nabla(v_1)=nabla(v_2)=v`. QED.

### Corollary 2.2

The fiber of the generic diagonal fold in arity `[2N],[N]` contains at least
`2^N` elements.  Taking `N=mn` gives a rigorously constructed generic family
with logarithmic cardinality `mn log 2`.

## 3. Why this is not yet H7-K

Theorem 2.1 proves that the required entropy is present in the non-totally
commutative generic algebra.  It does **not** prove quadratic bounded-section
growth, because its elements have type

\[
 [2N]\longrightarrow[N],                                  \tag{3.1}
\]

whereas the desired rank-one bundle sections must lie in Haran's appropriate
`O(B_{m,n})_{d_1}` component and satisfy every finite and real local bound.

The remaining mixed-section problem is now split into three exact lemmas:

1. **one-output typing: CLOSED by `a_22`.** Compose with one outer addition
   and recover each bit by zeroing the other input blocks;
2. **H7-B (boundedness):** prove the encoded family satisfies both
   archimedean contraction conditions and the finite denominator bounds;
3. **H7-U (upper bound):** prove that all bounded mixed normal forms have at
   most `exp(O(mn))` equivalence classes.

`114_a_20` shows why noncollision cannot be certified by diagonal restriction;
Theorem 2.1 shows that the ambient algebra has enough information, and
`114_a_22` supplies one-output typing. `114_a_23` proves that an intrinsic
rank/arity budget is still required; H7-R, H7-B and H7-U remain.
