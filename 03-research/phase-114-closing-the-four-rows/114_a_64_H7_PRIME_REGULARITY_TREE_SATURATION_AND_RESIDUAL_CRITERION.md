## 1. The full tree presentation

Haran, *New foundations for geometry*, arXiv:1508.04636, Section 13.2,
constructs the positive arithmetic plane

\[
 \Upsilon=\mathcal G(\mathbb N)\otimes_{\mathbb F}\mathcal G(\mathbb N)
\]

from reduced oriented trees.  The integer plane is a quotient

\[
 \pi:\Upsilon\otimes_{\mathbb F}\mathbb F\{\pm1\}
 \twoheadrightarrow
 \mathcal G(\mathbb Z)\otimes_{\mathbb F\{\pm1\}}
 \mathcal G(\mathbb Z),                                                   \tag{1.1}
\]

whose kernel is the equivalence ideal generated by the two left/right
additive-cancellation elements `x_0,x_1`.  The shorter 2017 source gives the
same tree operations and graphical cancellation relation in
(10.6)--(10.22).

Let `~_Z` denote this quotient congruence, including its localizations on the
finite-stage charts of the compactified square.

## 2. Exact combinatorial form of H7-PRIME-REG

Fix a prime `p`, represented through the first integer ruling.  For a tree
class `F`, write `pF` for first-additive multiplication by `p`.

### Theorem 2.1 (saturation equivalence)

The following are equivalent on any fixed chart and arity.

1. Multiplication by `p` on the integer-plane structure object is injective.
2. The cancellation congruence is `p`-root-closed:
   \[
      pF\sim_{\mathbb Z}pG\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
      F\sim_{\mathbb Z}G.                                                \tag{2.1}
   \]
3. The zero equivalence ideal in the quotient is saturated with respect to
   `S_p={1,p,p^2,...}`.

### Proof

Equality of the quotient classes of `pF,pG` is exactly the hypothesis in
(2.1).  Injectivity cancels `p` and gives equality of the classes of `F,G`.
Conversely, (2.1) applied to representatives proves injectivity.  Iteration
is equivalent to `S_p`-saturation.  QED.

Consequently H7-PRIME-REG is precisely Theorem 2.1 simultaneously for all
arities, all relevant opens and all later pro-levels, matching the quantifiers
in Haran (11.1).  The source defines saturated ideals but does not prove that
the cancellation ideal in (1.1) has this property.

## 3. A residual-characteristic sufficient criterion

Let `A` be one of the local integer-plane bios and suppose there is a family
of bio maps

\[
 \varepsilon_\lambda:A\longrightarrow K_\lambda                       \tag{3.1}
\]

such that:

1. every target has characteristic `q_lambda` coprime to `p`;
2. multiplication by `p` is injective in every target arity;
3. the product map `A -> product K_lambda` is injective in every arity.

### Theorem 3.1 (residual criterion)

Under these three conditions, multiplication by `p` on `A` is injective.

### Proof

If `pF=pG`, then `p epsilon_lambda(F)=p epsilon_lambda(G)`.  Cancel `p` in
each target and use joint faithfulness to conclude `F=G`.  QED.

The finite twisted-field bios of `a_51` satisfy conditions 1--2.  Their odd
Vandermonde product is jointly faithful on the balanced code, so
H7-PRIME-REG is already proved on that sector.  Neither `a_49` nor `a_51`
proves condition 3 for every scalar tree, much less every arity and local
chart.  The remaining exact target is therefore:

> **H7-RF-ALL.** Prove that finite twisted-field evaluations of auxiliary
> characteristics are jointly faithful on the full localized tree
> presentation, uniformly across the Section-11 pro-quantifiers.

Either a direct proof of tree saturation (2.1) or H7-RF-ALL closes
H7-PRIME-REG.  Finite tests on bounded-depth trees are evidence only and
cannot replace one of these theorems.

## 4. Verification scope

`114_a_64_h7_prime_regularity_saturation_verify.py` checks the local source
anchors, saturation/injectivity equivalence in exhaustive finite congruence
models, and the residual-characteristic cancellation implication.  It does
not assert full tree saturation or H7-RF-ALL.

**Later resolution (`a108`).**  The criterion is decided negatively at
`p=2`: the nonzero scalar `kappa=(1,-1)_1 o (1,1)_2^t` has `2kappa=0`.
Therefore full H7-PRIME-REG and 2-root-closure are false.  The residual/tree
program above remains meaningful only for odd primes or for a modified
torsion-free replacement, neither of which restores the original all-prime
Section-11 route.
