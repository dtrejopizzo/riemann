# Categorical realization of the nuclear correspondence envelope

## 1. Input category on the carrier

Let `Y` be the supportwise regularized Haran pro-square.  For every open
pro-chart `U` of `Y`, let `Corr_dec(U)` be the symmetric monoidal category
whose objects are the restrictions `Gamma_n|U` of the already constructed
decorated Frobenius-contact pro-spans.  A decoration consists of the Witt
map, the metrized rank-one torsor and the reduced contact projector.
Restriction of pro-spans and of each decoration defines functors

`Corr_dec(U) -> Corr_dec(V)`

for `V subset U`.  Composition satisfies

`Gamma_m|U o Gamma_n|U ~= Gamma_(mn)|U`

and commutes with restriction.

The metric makes the global labels faithful.  An isomorphism between
`Gamma_m` and `Gamma_n` identifies their rank-one torsor characters and
therefore has zero logarithmic norm defect `log(m/n)`.  Hence `m=n`.

## 2. Additive and split-Grothendieck envelope

Form the free complex-linear additive envelope

`A(U)=Add_C(Corr_dec(U))`.

This step does not assert that the non-additive structure sheaf itself has
direct sums.  It is the standard additive envelope of a correspondence
category: objects are finite formal sums and morphisms are finite matrices
of complex linear combinations of decorated correspondence maps.

Composition extends biadditively.  Its split Grothendieck group, complexified,

`K_fin(U)=K_0^split(A(U)) tensor_Z C`,

is a commutative algebra under correspondence composition.  Globally the
faithful labels give an injective algebra map

`C[N^x] -> K_fin(Y),  delta_n |-> [Gamma_n]`.

On the free subcategory generated by the `Gamma_n`, this is an isomorphism
by the definition of the free additive envelope.

## 3. Label-controlled nuclear completion

For a finite sum `x=sum_n a_n[Gamma_n|U]`, define quotient seminorms

`q_(r,U)(x)=inf {sum_n |b_n| n^r : x=sum_n b_n[Gamma_n|U]}`.

The infimum is needed because restrictions can identify or annihilate
classes locally.  It makes every restriction map contractive.  Complete the
Hausdorff quotient for all `r>=0` and denote it by `K_nuc(U)`.

The estimate

`q_(r,U)(x o y) <= q_(r,U)(x) q_(r,U)(y)`

follows by choosing presentations, distributing composition and using
`(mn)^r=m^r n^r`; passage to the infimum proves it for quotient seminorms.
Thus every `K_nuc(U)` is a complete locally convex algebra and restriction
is continuous.

On the global free labelled sector no quotient occurs.  Therefore

`K_nuc(Y) = {a=(a_n): sum |a_n|n^r<infinity for every r}`

and it is nuclear by the `n^-2` bonding-map argument of the preceding note.

## 4. Algebraic sheafification and the topology that is actually proved

The assignment `U |-> K_nuc(U)` is a presheaf of complete locally convex
algebras and continuous homomorphisms.  Forget topology temporarily and let

`K_nuc_sheaf`

be its ordinary sheafification in complex vector spaces.  Since
multiplication is a morphism of presheaves, it sheafifies and makes
`K_nuc_sheaf` a sheaf of commutative complex algebras.  The canonical map
from the presheaf sends every actual restricted decorated correspondence to
a local section and respects composition.

On the four standard charts, the underlying decorated pro-spans already
satisfy descent.  Hence a finite labelled section has effective descent.
For a globally rapidly decreasing labelled section, truncate it at label
`N`; the truncations descend and are Cauchy in every `q_r`.  Completeness and
contractivity make their limits agree on overlaps.  This proves topological
descent for rapidly decreasing labelled families that arise as limits of
global truncations.  Ordinary sheafification supplies algebraic descent for
arbitrary local sections.

Thus `K_nuc_sheaf` is an internal sheaf of numerical correspondence
algebras on the carrier, not merely a vector space attached to its set of
prime labels.  What is proved topologically is that its distinguished global
labelled subalgebra is nuclear and that globally generated restrictions are
continuous.  We do **not** claim that algebraic sheafification in vector
spaces automatically produces a sheaf in the category of complete nuclear
spaces; completion need not commute with arbitrary sheaf equalizers.  A
strict topological descent theorem is a further gate.

## 5. Contact as a continuous sheaf functional

On a chart meeting the diagonal, reduced contact of a finite section is

`ell_U(sum a_n[Gamma_n|U])=sum a_n Lambda(n)`

with the summands whose contact support misses `U` omitted.  These local
functionals agree under restriction because the reduced contact complexes
do.  The bound `Lambda(n)<=n` gives

`|ell_U(x)| <= q_(1,U)(x)`

on every presentation and hence on the quotient seminorm.  The functionals
extend continuously to the completions and sheafify.  Globally

`ell([Gamma_n])=Lambda(n)`.

The bilinear form `K(x,y)=ell(x o y)` is therefore an internal continuous
pairing on the nuclear numerical correspondence sheaf and retains
`K(Gamma_m,Gamma_n)=Lambda(mn)` exactly.

## 6. Proven advance and remaining gate

The construction proves:

1. actual decorated correspondence objects on the Haran carrier generate
   the completion;
2. its algebraic sheafification is a sheaf on that carrier;
3. composition, restriction and contact are continuous before
   sheafification, and topological descent holds for globally generated
   rapidly decreasing families;
4. global exact arithmetic and nuclearity are retained.

It does not yet prove strict topological descent for arbitrary completed
local sections, or identify `K_nuc_sheaf` with a sheaf of Cartier divisors
or line bundles.  Nor does it define `R Gamma` of such line bundles,
effectivity, or Riemann--Roch.  The next gate is to construct a Picard action
on a sheaf of nuclear section objects whose decategorification is
`K_nuc_sheaf`.  Without that lift, the present result is an internal
algebraic numerical correspondence sheaf with a nuclear global sector, not
the full strong row A.
