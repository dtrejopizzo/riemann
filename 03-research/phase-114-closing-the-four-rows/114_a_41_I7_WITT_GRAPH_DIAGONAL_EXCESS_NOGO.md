# 114.a.41 — I7 no-go: Witt graph/diagonal intersection has horizontal excess

```
+--------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| STAGE       A_a=W_{p^a}=sum_{j=0}^a Z phi_{p^j}.                         |
| FIXED RING  A_a/(F_p(x)-x : x in A_a) is exactly Z.                     |
| INTERSECT   Graph(F_p) cap Delta = Spec Z, a horizontal one-dimensional  |
|             component, not a finite zero-cycle.                          |
| NO-GO       Ordinary graph intersection length cannot equal log p.       |
| SURVIVOR    H7-WLEF must remove the F_0 horizontal component and use a   |
|             derived/excess-normal determinant or another reduced theory. |
+--------------------------------------------------------------------------+
```

## 1. The prime-power Witt stage

Fix a prime `p` and `a>=1`. Write

\[
 A_a=\mathcal W_{p^a}
 =\bigoplus_{j=0}^{a}\mathbb Z e_j,
 \qquad e_j:=\phi_{p^j},\quad e_0=\phi_1.              \tag{1.1}
\]

Haran 2022, (12.20), gives

\[
 F_p(e_0)=e_0,qquad
 F_p(e_1)=(p-1)e_0,qquad
 F_p(e_j)=p e_{j-1}\quad(j\ge2).                       \tag{1.2}
\]

Let

\[
 I_{p,a}=(F_p(x)-x:x\in A_a)                           \tag{1.3}
\]

be the ring ideal defining the scheme-theoretic fixed locus.

## 2. Exact fixed-ring theorem

### Theorem 2.1

For every prime `p` and `a>=1`,

\[
 \boxed{\quad A_a/I_{p,a}\simeq\mathbb Z.\quad}        \tag{2.1}
\]

### Proof

Relations (1.2)--(1.3) give

\[
 e_1=(p-1)e_0,qquad e_j=p e_{j-1}\quad(2\le j\le a). \tag{2.2}
\]

Thus the additive quotient is generated by `e_0`. The relation matrix has,
in rows `e_1,...,e_a`, a triangular `a by a` minor with diagonal `-1`, so
the additive quotient has no torsion and is a quotient of `Z e_0`.

On the other hand Haran's map

\[
 F_0:A_a\longrightarrow\mathbb Z,qquad
 F_0(e_j)=\varphi(p^j),                                 \tag{2.3}
\]

satisfies `F_0 F_p=F_0` and `F_0(e_0)=1`. It therefore factors through
`A_a/I_{p,a}` and maps the class of `e_0` to `1`. The possible further
quotient of `Z e_0` is consequently trivial, proving (2.1) as a unital ring.
QED.

## 3. Geometric intersection consequence

For an affine endomorphism `f=Spec(F_p)` of `Z_a=Spec A_a`, its graph and
the diagonal have fiber product

\[
 \Gamma(f)\times_{Z_a\times_{\mathbb Z}Z_a}\Delta
 \simeq\mathrm{Spec}(A_a/I_{p,a}).                \tag{3.1}
\]

Theorem 2.1 therefore gives

\[
 \boxed{\quad\Gamma(f)\cap\Delta\simeq\mathrm{Spec}\,\mathbb Z.\quad} \tag{3.2}
\]

This fixed locus is horizontal and one-dimensional. It has no ordinary
finite length whose logarithm could be

\[
 \Lambda(p)=\log p.                                    \tag{3.3}
\]

### Corollary 3.1 (ordinary H7-WLEF no-go)

Neither scheme-theoretic graph intersection nor its ordinary finite length
can realize the Witt lambda-trace mass of `a_36`. The failure already occurs
at every finite prime-power stage.

## 4. Exact surviving trace/intersection gate

The horizontal component is not accidental: it is selected by the common
Frobenius-invariant character `F_0`, since `F_0F_m=F_0` for every `m`.
Accordingly H7-WLEF must be replaced by the sharper statement:

> **H7-WLEF-red.** Define a derived or excess intersection of the Witt graph
> and diagonal relative to their common `F_0` horizontal component, transport
> its normal/determinant class through a non-total H7-WBASE kernel, and prove
> that its Arakelov degree is
> \[
> \log|\mathrm{tr}(\lambda_1(V_n\phi_1))|=\Lambda(n).               \tag{4.1}
> \]

Any proposal must state which complex removes the free `Z` direction and
why its remaining determinant is finite and functorial under
`G_m compose G_n=G_mn`. Simply declaring the fixed locus to have mass
`Lambda(n)` contradicts Theorem 2.1.
