## 1. The span category test

Let

\[
 S=\operatorname{Spec}\mathbb F\{\pm1\},\qquad
 X=\overline{\operatorname{Spec}\mathbb Z}.
\]

Every category with finite fiber products has a category of spans. The left
prime ruling of `a_17` defines the endocorrespondence

\[
 R_p=\left(X\xleftarrow{\ i_p\operatorname{pr}_1\ }
 x_p\times_SX\xrightarrow{\ \operatorname{pr}_2\ }X\right).             \tag{1.1}
\]

Composition by fiber product gives

\[
 R_p\circ R_q=left(
 X\longleftarrow x_p\times_Sx_q\times_SX\longrightarrow X
 \right),                                                               \tag{1.2}
\]

where the left leg factors through `i_p:x_p->X`. Reversing the order gives

\[
 R_q\circ R_p=left(
 X\longleftarrow x_q\times_Sx_p\times_SX\longrightarrow X
 \right),                                                               \tag{1.3}
\]

whose left leg factors through `i_q`.

## 2. The commutativity/faithfulness dichotomy

### Theorem 2.1

For distinct rational primes `p!=q`, the family `p -> R_p` cannot extend to a
faithful representation of the commutative monoid `N^x` in spans.

### Proof

Put `A_pq=x_p times_S x_q times_S X`.

If `A_pq` is initial, (1.2) is the zero correspondence. Hence the proposed
image of the nonzero label `pq` vanishes and the representation is not
faithful.

Suppose `A_pq` is not initial. An isomorphism of spans between (1.2) and
(1.3) would commute with their left legs. The first left leg factors through
the closed point `x_p`, while the second factors through the distinct closed
point `x_q`. On the ordinary dense chart these closed images are disjoint, so
a noninitial span cannot have both factorizations. Thus

\[
 R_p\circ R_q\not\simeq R_q\circ R_p.                                  \tag{2.1}
\]

But `pq=qp` in `N^x`. Therefore commutativity fails in the second case. The
two cases exhaust the possibilities. QED.

The same argument, with endpoints reversed, applies to the right rulings.

### Corollary 2.2

The geometric seed `Delta cap V_p=Spec F_p` remains valid, but one cannot set
`Gamma_p:=V_p` and obtain H7-CYCLE-LIFT by ordinary span composition. A
successful `Gamma_p` must add a dynamic or transverse component whose
composition retains the Witt label while its diagonal contact reduces to the
prime carrier.

## 3. Comparison with the arithmetic-site model

Connes--Consani's reduced correspondences `Psi(lambda)` on the square of the
arithmetic site do satisfy

\[
 \Psi(m)\circ\Psi(n)=\Psi(mn)\qquad(m,n\in\mathbb N_{>0})                \tag{3.1}
\]

by arXiv:1502.05580v1, Theorem `thmcomp`. They live in a different semiringed
topos and the source supplies neither a functor to Haran's pro-square nor a
diagonal intersection theory producing `M_n`. Thus (3.1) is an exact model
of the missing dynamic datum, not a construction of H7-CYCLE-LIFT.

The remaining gate can now be stated more tightly:

> **H7-DYNAMIC-LIFT.** Construct a faithful commutative family of
> correspondences on Haran's square, not generated by either ruling alone,
> together with a diagonal-contact functor sending it to `M_n`.

`a_61`/`a_66` close faithfulness and the commutative law in the abstract
Picard tensor category, not in spans. H7-PRIME-REG supplies the completed
lattice refinement. Promoting that family to the dynamics required here is
the sharper gate H7-PIC-TO-DYNAMIC.
