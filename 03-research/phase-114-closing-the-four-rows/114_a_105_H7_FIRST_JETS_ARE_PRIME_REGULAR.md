# 114.a.105 — H7: first infinitesimal jets are prime-regular in every arity

```
+------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| MAPS        J_12,J_21 send one ruling to (x,0), the other to (x,dx).    |
| TARGET      H=F(Z) Pi N, with H_(Y,X)=Mat_(Y,X)(Z) x N_(Y,X).           |
| FREEDOM     N_(Y,X) is free abelian on primitive direction pairs.       |
| RESULT      Every prime acts injectively on every target arity.          |
| REDUCTION   Any source p-collision lies in ker(J_12) intersect ker(J_21).|
| REMAINS     N-jet-invisible kernel; universal first jets may be larger.  |
+------------------------------------------------------------------------+
```

## 1. The two signed jet maps are correctly typed

Let

\[
 P=\mathbb Z_1\otimes_{\mathbb F\{\pm1\}}\mathbb Z_2
\]

be Haran's commutative involutive `F`-ring plane.  As in `a104`, put
`R=F(Z)` and let `N` be Haran's commutative `R`-module generated by
`[a|b]` modulo scalar transfer.  The relative derivation

\[
 d:R\longrightarrow N,
 \qquad d|_{F\{\pm1\}}=0                                      \tag{1.1}
\]

gives two ruling pairs into the commutative involutive infinitesimal
extension `H=R Pi N`:

\[
 J_{12}:\quad x_1\mapsto(x,0),\quad x_2\mapsto(x,dx),           \tag{1.2}
\]

\[
 J_{21}:\quad x_1\mapsto(x,dx),\quad x_2\mapsto(x,0).           \tag{1.3}
\]

The maps in each pair agree on the signed base by (1.1), so the pushout
property supplies `J_12,J_21:P->H`.

The `F`-ring/generalized-ring presentations agree at precisely the required
level.  Haran 2017 constructs the adjunction

\[
 \mathcal F:C\mathcal GR\rightleftarrows C\mathbb FR^t:\mathcal U,
 \qquad \mathcal U\mathcal F\simeq id.                         \tag{1.4}
\]

Thus `F` is fully faithful and preserves pushouts.  Applying it to the
signed generalized-ring plane gives the prop plane above.  In particular,
(1.2)--(1.3) act on the same full bilateral operation sets on which
H7-PRIME-REG is asked.

## 2. The target has no prime torsion

For nonzero integer vectors write

\[
 a=s(a)g(a)p(a),\qquad b=s(b)g(b)p(b),                       \tag{2.1}
\]

with positive content `g`, sign `s`, and primitive direction `p` normalized
by a positive first nonzero coordinate.  The normal form proved in `a104`
works in every arity:

\[
 [a|b]\longmapsto s(a)s(b)g(a)g(b)[p(a),p(b)].                \tag{2.2}
\]

### Proposition 2.1

For all finite `Y,X`, `N_(Y,X)` is a free abelian group.  Consequently

\[
 H_{Y,X}=\mathbb Z^{Y\times X}\times N_{Y,X}                 \tag{2.3}
\]

is torsion-free.

### Proof

Scalar transfer reduces every nonzero generator uniquely to (2.2), and the
map back from a primitive-direction basis respects all transfer relations,
including negative and zero scalars.  Hence the maps are inverse.  The first
factor in (2.3) is the matrix `F`-ring `F(Z)`.  Both factors are free
abelian.  QED.

The image of an integer prime from either ruling is `(p,0)`, because
`d_(1,1)=0`.  Its action is therefore

\[
 (a,m)\longmapsto(pa,pm).                                      \tag{2.4}
\]

### Corollary 2.2

Multiplication by every prime is injective on `H_(Y,X)` for every arity.

## 3. Exact localization of every possible collision

Let `J=(J_12,J_21):P->H x H`.

### Theorem 3.1 (first-jet collision theorem)

For every prime `p` and every arity,

\[
 pF=pG\ \text{in }P\quad\Longrightarrow\quad J(F)=J(G).       \tag{3.1}
\]

### Proof

Apply either jet map to the equality.  It gives
`p J_ij(F)=p J_ij(G)` in `H`.  Corollary 2.2 cancels `p`.  Do this for both
orders.  QED.

Equivalently, any failure of H7-PRIME-REG is confined to the equivalence
kernel

\[
 \mathcal K_{\rm jet}^{(1)}=ker(J_{12})\cap\ker(J_{21}),       \tag{3.2}
\]

where `ker` means equality kernel of operation pairs, not an untyped
additive ideal.

The centre/grid difference of `a104` is outside (3.2): `J_12` sends it to
the nine-coordinate nonzero element of `N_(2,2)`.  Hence that canonical
nontameness class is not killed by any prime or prime power.  Nontameness
therefore does not itself create the sought obstruction to regularity.

## 4. What remains for A

This theorem closes the sector detected by Haran's explicit module `N`, but
not the entire universal first-order sector and not H7-PRIME-REG.  The source
only gives a surjection

\[
 C\Omega(F(Z)/F\{\pm1\})\twoheadrightarrow N,                 \tag{4.1}
\]

and comments out the claim that this map is injective.  Thus the common
kernel (3.2) can still contain first-order classes lost by `N`; it must not
be called purely higher-order.  The remaining direct gate is nevertheless
sharper than undifferentiated p-CONVEX/p-DIVPATH:

> **H7-JET1-KERNEL-PURE.** Prove prime cancellation on the common kernel
> (3.2), first by deciding the universal differential kernel in (4.1), or
> construct a further prime-regular target separating every pair in (3.2).

In macro-graph language, a two-gate nondivisible component witnessing
failure of p-CONVEX must have both boundary classes equal under `J_12` and
`J_21`.  Searches and invariants may therefore discard every candidate with
a nonzero `N`-derivative in either ruling.

This is real progress toward the direct route, but completed lattices,
degree/gauge transport and row A remain open.

Primary sources: Haran, [*New foundations for geometry*](https://arxiv.org/abs/1508.04636),
Sections 7.5--7.8; Haran, [*Geometry over F1*](https://arxiv.org/abs/1709.05831),
the adjunction (3) and the description following (3.17).

**Later resolution (`a106`).**  The omitted injectivity is false:
`[1,1|1]` is an infinite-order entropy-cocycle class killed by
`C Omega -> N`.  Replacing `N` by the rationalized universal module
`C Omega tensor Q` gives a strictly stronger prime-regular first-jet target.

**Final collision (`a108`).**  The rational jet necessarily loses the
order-two class `tau`.  That class integrates to the actual scalar
`kappa=(1,-1)_1 o (1,1)_2^t`, with `kappa!=0` and `2kappa=0`.
Consequently H7-PRIME-REG is false.
