## 1. Character chain on the prime-power Witt order

Fix `a>=1` and use

\[
 \mathcal W_{p^a}=\bigoplus_{j=0}^{a}\mathbb Z e_j,
 \qquad e_j=\phi_{p^j}.                                 \tag{1.1}
\]

For `0<=r<a`, let

\[
 \theta_r=t_{p^r}=\mathrm{tr}\,\circ F_{p^r},
 \qquad \theta_a=F_0.                                  \tag{1.2}
\]

Haran's character formulas following (12.30) give

\[
 \theta_r(e_j)=
 \begin{cases}
  \varphi(p^j),&0\le j\le r,\\
  -p^r,&j=r+1,\\
  0,&j>r+1,
 \end{cases}                                           \tag{1.3}
\]

while `theta_a(e_j)=varphi(p^j)` for all `j<=a`.

## 2. Exact adjacent intersection thickness

The two characters `theta_{k-1},theta_k`, `1<=k<=a`, define two closed
`Spec Z` branches in `Spec W_{p^a}`.

### Theorem 2.1

Their scheme-theoretic intersection is

\[
 \boxed{\quad
 \mathrm{Spec}\,\mathbb Z
 \times_{\mathrm{Spec}\,\mathcal W_{p^a}}
 \mathrm{Spec}\,\mathbb Z
 \simeq\mathrm{Spec}(\mathbb Z/p^k\mathbb Z).
 \quad}                                                \tag{2.1}
\]

### Proof

The balanced tensor product is `Z` modulo the ideal generated by all
differences

\[
 \theta_k(e_j)-\theta_{k-1}(e_j).                      \tag{2.2}
\]

For `j<=k-1` the difference is zero. At `j=k`, it is

\[
 \varphi(p^k)-(-p^{k-1})
 =p^{k-1}(p-1)+p^{k-1}=p^k.                            \tag{2.3}
\]

If `k<a`, the difference at `j=k+1` is `-p^k`, and all later differences
are zero. For `k=a`, (2.3) is the last basis difference. Thus the generated
ideal is exactly `(p^k)`, proving (2.1). QED.

For `k=1`, this recovers the reduced node `Spec F_p` of `a_43`.

## 3. Primitive contact layer

Let

\[
 C_{p,k}:=\mathbb Z/p^k\mathbb Z.                      \tag{3.1}
\]

The natural reduction maps give the contact filtration

\[
 C_{p,k}\twoheadrightarrow C_{p,k-1}\twoheadrightarrow\cdots
 \twoheadrightarrow C_{p,1}.                           \tag{3.2}
\]

Define the primitive `k`-th layer by

\[
 P_{p,k}:=\ker(C_{p,k}\to C_{p,k-1})
 =p^{k-1}\mathbb Z/p^k\mathbb Z                       \tag{3.3}
\]

with `C_{p,0}=0`. Then

\[
 P_{p,k}\simeq\mathbb F_p                              \tag{3.4}
\]

for every `k>=1`.

### Theorem 3.1 (prime-power Lambda layer)

\[
 \boxed{\quad
 \deg P_{p,k}=\log\#P_{p,k}=\log p=\Lambda(p^k).
 \quad}                                                \tag{3.5}
\]

The full intersection `C_{p,k}` has degree `k log p`; taking it as the mass
would overcount all earlier contacts. The associated-graded primitive layer
selects exactly the new cyclotomic factor at level `p^k`.

## 4. Order-theoretic interpretation

The simultaneous character embedding

\[
 \mathcal W_{p^a}\hookrightarrow\mathbb Z^{a+1},
 \qquad x\longmapsto(\theta_0(x),\ldots,\theta_a(x))    \tag{4.1}

has determinant/index

\[
 p^{1+2+\cdots+a}=p^{a(a+1)/2}.                        \tag{4.2}
\]

Indeed consecutive row differences contribute the diagonal factors
`p,p^2,...,p^a`. Thus the entire non-normality of the prime-power Witt order
is stratified by the contacts (2.1), and every associated-graded step
contains one residue-field mass `log p`.
