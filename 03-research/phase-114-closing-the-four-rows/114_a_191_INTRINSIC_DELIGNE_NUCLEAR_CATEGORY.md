# The intrinsic periodic--Deligne--nuclear category

## 1. Why `Perf_DN` alone is not enough

The integral additive base change of the spherical square cannot see an
off-diagonal pair `(p,q)` in the same way as the periodic coefficient
geometry.  In particular, forcing a periodic prime-pair section object to
be an ordinary `O_Z`-module supported at `(p,q)` would reintroduce the
finite--finite blindness that the spherical and tropical structures were
introduced to avoid.

The correct unification is therefore not to forget the periodic
coefficient category and declare a globally free module.  It is to glue the
periodic cotangent category to `Perf_DN` along the nuclear linearization
which both constructions actually share.

## 2. The periodic perfect category

At finite prime support and finite Frobenius depth, let `RegPer` be the
category generated by the regular periodic section moduli and their
cotangent frames.  An elementary object is

`(S^reg,Omega^per,V_Ext)`,

where `S^reg` is the regular locus of actual periodic sections,
`Omega^per` is its cotangent bundle and

`V_Ext=R[Ext(E)xExt(F)]^vee`

is the globally ordered invariant cotangent frame.  Morphisms are generated
by principal translations, periodic coordinate isomorphisms, Kunneth maps
and the arithmetic scalar endomorphisms.  Use split exact sequences on the
finite frame spaces and take the stable idempotent completion.  Denote the
result by `Perf_per`.

Tensor product is induced by external product of section moduli and
Cartesian product of extremal sets.  It is exact in each variable and the
Kunneth theorem gives

`V_Ext(P tensor Q)=V_Ext(P) tensor_R V_Ext(Q)`.

Thus `Perf_per` is a small stable symmetric monoidal category after choosing
a skeleton of the finite supports and depths.

Extend scalars by the tensor product of small stable categories

`Perf_(per,C):=Perf_per tensor_(Perf(R)) Perf(O_C)`.

It contains coefficient-only objects `1_per tensor Q` for every
`Q in Perf(O_C)`, as well as the nuclear realization of every geometric
periodic object.  Extend the frame functor and define

`T_C:Perf_(per,C) ->Perf(O_C)`,

`T_C(P tensor Q)=(O_C tensor_R V_Ext(P)) tensor_(O_C) Q`.

On generators this is finite free.  Cartesian products of extremal bases
show that `T_C` is exact and strong symmetric monoidal, so it extends
uniquely to the stable idempotent completion.

## 3. The homotopy-pullback category

Let `ev_C:Perf_DN(Y_S)->Perf(O_C)` select the nuclear component.  Define

`Perf_IDN(Y_S)
  :=Perf_DN(Y_S) x^h_(Perf(O_C)) Perf_(per,C)`,

using `ev_C` and `T_C`.

Concretely an object is

`(M_Z,M_C,alpha_R; P,beta_C)`,

where `(M_Z,M_C,alpha_R)` is Deligne--nuclear perfect, `P` is periodic
perfect, and

`beta_C:T_C(P) ~=M_C`

is an equivalence.  Morphisms are pairs commuting with `beta_C`.

Limits of stable infinity-categories along exact functors are stable.
Because both comparison functors are strong symmetric monoidal, the
homotopy pullback inherits a symmetric monoidal structure computed
componentwise.  Duals and finite cofibres are componentwise.  Hence
`Perf_IDN` is a well-defined stable symmetric monoidal perfect category.

The scalar completion is necessary: every old Deligne--nuclear object `M`
has the coefficient-only lift

`M |->(M,1_per tensor M_C,id_(M_C))`,

while an intrinsic cohomology object uses its geometric periodic factor.
The comparison equivalence prevents the periodic cotangent and its nuclear
realization from varying independently.

## 4. The intrinsic cohomology object

For a periodic coefficient divisor `x` at depth `(r,s)`, let `P_(r,s)(x)`
be its regular-section cotangent object.  Put

`M_C=O_C tensor_R V_Ext(P_(r,s)(x))`,

`M_Z=O_Z tensor_Z Z[Ext_(r,s)(x)]^vee`,

and let `alpha_R` identify the two ordered real bases.  With `beta_C` the
identity equivalence, these data define

`H_(r,s)^int(x) in Perf_IDN(Y_S)`.

Its periodic component is the cotangent of actual intrinsic coefficient
sections; its integral and nuclear components are not separately chosen
rank counts but the two canonical based realizations of that cotangent
frame.  Principal descent and Kunneth hold in every component and commute
with both comparison equivalences.

## 5. Contact objects in the same category

Pair the reduced prime contact `C_p` with the zero object of `Perf_per` and
the zero comparison arrow.  Thus

`C_p^int=(C_p,0) in Perf_IDN(Y_S)`.

The dynamic Witt contact `L_n` of row B enters in the same way, and its
proved equivalence with `C_p` for `n=p^k` is an equivalence in `Perf_IDN`,
not merely after forgetting periodic cohomology.

The continuous cohomology determinant and the torsion contact determinant
therefore land in the same determinant Picard groupoid.  Their quotient
defines the Green line exactly as before, now with the Riemann--Roch factor
coming from intrinsic regular section moduli.

## 6. Arithmetic action

Enrich the periodic frame by the nuclear scalar algebra.  Left convolution
by `delta_n` defines the coefficient endomorphism
`id_P tensor L_(delta_n)` in `Perf_(per,C)` and its image under `T_C` is
the same convolution map on `M_C`.  On the `Perf_DN` component use the same
`rho_n`.  The square with `beta_C` then commutes tautologically,
so `rho_n` is a well-typed endomorphism in `Perf_IDN` and

`rho_m rho_n=rho_(mn)`.

The contact functional and distributional realization remain

`ell(delta_n)=Lambda(n)=-log||det_tor L_n||`,

`j(delta_n)=delta_(n^-1)`,

so the row-B local contact and row-C nuclear multiplier are compatible with
the intrinsic cohomology category.

## 7. Relation with the negabinary package

The negabinary cotangent has a canonical fixed-divisor isomorphism with
`H^int` only after passage to the quadratic asymptotic quotient.  Its
continuous determinant is canonically isometric to the determinant of
`H^int`.  Therefore the row-A tuple should use `H^int` as its cohomology
entry and retain the negabinary construction as an auxiliary spherical
determinant certificate.

No false finite-stage or transition-wise equivalence is required.  The
intrinsic object, its determinant, contact, arithmetic action and Kunneth
maps now live in the single category `Perf_IDN`.

## 8. Remaining publication audit

Before this construction is promoted to the paper, the following source
checks remain mandatory:

1. cite the exact theorem constructing the arithmetic pullback and its
   periodic fibers;
2. cite the extremal-generation and one-dimensional continuous-dimension
   theorems for the periodic section modules;
3. spell out the chosen small enriched models and the passage to stable
   idempotent completions;
4. verify that every row-B and row-C use of `Perf_DN` extends along the
   coefficient-only lift into `Perf_IDN`.

These are formalization and source-integration obligations, not undefined
mathematical objects.
