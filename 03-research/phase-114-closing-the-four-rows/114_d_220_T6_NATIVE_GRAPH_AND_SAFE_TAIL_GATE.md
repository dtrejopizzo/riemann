## 3. Why this is not yet the full endpoint

After the finite graph congruence, the full primitive operator has the
three-block form

\[
 \mathcal A=
 \begin{pmatrix}
 K&0&C_G\\
 0&B_{SS}&C_S\\
 C_G^*&C_S^*&A_{QQ}
 \end{pmatrix}.                                    \tag{3.1}
\]

The zero in (3.1) is exact, but (C_S) is not zero.  The gap
(A_{QQ}\geq\delta Q) controls the restriction (G\dotplus Q), not the
shorted complement (S\dotplus Q).  In particular, neither

\[
 K-\delta^{-1}C_GC_G^*>0
\]

nor the stronger certified inequality (2.1) permits (C_S) to be omitted.
Doing so would repeat exactly the invalid two-block inference isolated in
D.200.

## 4. Exact remaining local theorem

\[
 \Sigma_Q:=A_{QQ}-C_S^*B_{SS}^{-1}C_S.             \tag{4.1}
\]

Because the graph-safe cross is zero, the graph-tail cross remains (C_G).
The full endpoint is positive if and only if the supported-range conditions
hold and

\[
 \boxed{

 \qquad
 K-C_G\Sigma_Q^\dagger C_G^*\geq0 .}              \tag{4.2}
\]

Equivalently, using the Galerkin residual identity of D.210, choose a
source-defined finite trial space inside (Q), retain its Green operator

only unpaid term is the change from (A_{QQ}^{-1}) to the safe-shorted
