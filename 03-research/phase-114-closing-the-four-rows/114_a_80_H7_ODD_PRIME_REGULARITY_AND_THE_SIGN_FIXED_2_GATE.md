## 1. The valid conditional algebra

Suppose that, in a fixed arity/chart, the first-additive relation group of
all contextual cancellation images admits a basis in which every connected
relation component is a signed incidence graph: relations are generated by
`e_C-e_D`, `e_C+e_D`, and `e_C`.  Then each connected component contributes
`Z`, `Z/2Z`, or zero.  Consequently every odd prime acts injectively, and
the only remaining prime obstruction is a retained sign-inconsistent
component at `2`.

More generally, total unimodularity of the full integral context-relation
matrix would make its cokernel torsion-free and prove every-prime
regularity.

These are correct sufficient criteria.  The finite signed-orbit computation
in the accompanying verifier checks the first one exactly.

## 2. Why the criterion is not yet applicable

The previous version claimed that deletion and tree contraction cannot fuse
fixed-incidence components, and from this inferred that all cancellation
relations were componentwise.  The local no-fusion statement itself is
true, but it does not cover arbitrary generalized-ring contexts.

The `K2,2` datum of `a_81` is produced by applying a binary context of the
other ruling to `x_0`.  It is locally connected and has no visible parallel
pair, yet the full congruence kills it.  Hence the fixed-incidence component
matrix omits genuine macro relations.

Nor does confluence alone control torsion: the terminating confluent
presentation `3e -> 0` has cokernel `Z/3Z`.  Therefore the attempted
unconditional statement

> "every odd prime is regular on the base arithmetic plane"

is retracted.  It was not established.

## 3. Exact remaining linear-algebra gate

Let `R_X` be the integral relation matrix whose rows are the first-additive
component vectors of **all** multiplication/contraction context images of
`x_0,x_1`, after consistent commutativity.  The exact questions are:

1. **H7-MACRO-PRESENT:** prove that a specified enumerable family of context
   rows presents the full relation subgroup;
2. **H7-MACRO-SMITH:** prove that every finite minor system of `R_X` has no
   forbidden prime Smith factor, uniformly in arity and chart;
3. equivalently for the desired conclusion, prove
   `(E_cancel:p)=E_cancel` directly as in `a_76`.

The K2,2 row is the first nonlocal macro row and must be included in any such
matrix.  No odd-prime or `2`-prime conclusion is claimed here.

## 4. Verification scope

`114_a_80_h7_odd_prime_and_sign_orbit_verify.py` verifies the conditional
signed-incidence theorem, the local no-fusion graph fact and the `Z/3`
warning.  It does not assert that Haran's macro relation matrix is signed
incidence or totally unimodular, and it does not assert base odd-prime
regularity.

H7-PRIME-REG, boundary transport, the completed Section-11 lattice and row A
remain open.
