# Cross-program synthesis of RH programs and phases 000–101

## Reading verdict

The corpus leaves instruments, finite identities, adversarial controls and a fairly precise cartography of the obstacle. It does not leave a proof of RH nor a closed deductive chain toward it. The transversal conclusion is narrower and more useful: the routes of finite detectors, classifications, constructed positivity, averages, geometries not yet constructed and changes of coordinates do not substitute for the pending burdens, among them a signed build-neutral convergence and an independent arithmetic identification with uniform control. It is also not proved that one of these burdens is the only RH-strength step. The audit of paper 36 adds that the minimal prime target is one-sided, \(\lambda_n^{\rm prime}\ge-\lambda_n^{\rm arch}\); for this reason the no-gos based on absolute values do not close every possible direct route to Li.

Five labels are used without interchange of scope:

| Label | Strict use |
|---|---|
| **PROVED** | Identity, inequality or implication with stated hypotheses; may be finite or abstract and does not automatically inherit a limit. |
| **CONDITIONAL** | Depends on an unconstructed hypothesis, a separation, a uniform bound, a geometry or a stated transfer. |
| **NUMERICAL** | Reproducible only for fixed data, grids, cuts, normalizations and implementation. |
| **REFUTED** | The mechanism, inference or concrete audited implementation fails. Does not claim impossibility of every related idea. |
| **RH-STRENGTH** | Statement whose proof would resolve RH under the indicated transfers, or that is already an equivalence of RH in different language. It is not an available auxiliary lemma. |

In particular, a finite-section identity can be **PROVED** and still carry no force over the limiting divisor; a calibration can be **NUMERICAL** and very valuable without being asymptotic; and a no-go applies only to the class, normalization and mechanism it actually examines.

The common rule is: no no-go rises to universal impossibility if it was only established for one observable, one family of controls, one representation or one concrete axiomatic class.

## Durable results that do survive

### Proved and reusable

- Energy and cross-product identities for \(D=\sum_k S_k\), including the reading of \(r\) as an interference parametrization; Cauchy–Schwarz gives class-counting bounds, not zero information.
- Finite identities of bordered determinant, adjugate, cofactors, characteristic Jacobians, shorting/Feshbach, Cauchy/Hilbert, commutators and exact interior/shell separation. The polynomial continuation that avoids inverting on a singular curve is valid.
- For corrected LP: compact resolvent, existence of the lower eigenvalue of finite multiplicity, and finite equivalence between BTG energy and Weyl-disk contraction. This does not include the limiting divergence in the true \(\mu_L\).
- The exact decomposition `g_(N+2)-g_N=ZERO+MESH+BND`, with `MESH=O(\sigma/N^2)` and `BND=O(\sigma/N^3)`. The arithmetic term `ZERO` does not yet share these bounds.
- From the exact Li split, the minimal condition is \(\lambda_n^{\rm prime}\ge-\lambda_n^{\rm arch}\). Domination of \(|\lambda_n^{\rm prime}|\) is stronger and not equivalent to \(\Omega_7\).
- In the half-plane of absolute convergence, the Euler–Gamma product, the absence of zeros of the approximant and its logarithmic derivative; also the secular, Stieltjes, Abel, Feshbach and profile-rotation identities of phases 80–89. These are exact coordinates, not the identification of the limit.
- The tautological positivity of zero-only forms, the quadratic H0 response for the vertical cloud model, and the no-gos of Stone–Kreĭn, Cesàro in the examined mechanism, circular filters and loss of linear multiplicity.
- External theorems correctly used as a framework: Weil criterion, Li criterion, Hurwitz in the appropriate transfer, mean orthogonality and classical averaging results. Their reuse requires keeping all hypotheses.
- Infrastructure: compensated summation and high-precision contrast, seeds and hashes for nulls, well-defined planted or Davenport–Heilbronn falsifiers, trace gates, separation of data error from formula error, and versioned catalogs with explicit coverage.

### Conditional and useful only with visible hypotheses

- Closures via local uniform convergence, Fredholmness, simple branch, uniform Gram, separation, chart non-vanishing, cofinal even-simple, compactness and joint tail bounds.
- Index towers, confinement, coercivity, Pontryagin/Hodge/Fredholm bridges, `D8.5`, `LP-134∞`, `178.C`, `TW_y` and Cauchy/CCM transfers: these are conditional programs, not pieces already available.
- Corrected LP requires `BTG-DIV` in the true \(\mu_L\); Outcome A and neutrality against the control are **NUMERICAL/CONDITIONAL**, not consequences of the compact resolvent. `GAP-Z`, `RDI-CONV`, `RDI-ANCHOR`, `RDP-SHELL`, PROLATE and WEIL-TAIL remain independent obligations.
- A Hermite–Gauss bound for fixed parameters may be provable with explicit inequalities; the joint bound in center, width, dimension, prime cut, zero tail and normalization remains open.

### Numerical, retained as exploration

- Calibrations of synthetic perturbations, known local anomalies, traces and matrix assemblies, heat/Jacobi/Abel profiles, moments, phase maps, H0, H1/H2 and finite-section falsifiers.
- Clouds, closure defects, rank-one escapes, proxies and `E79.6` are **NUMERICAL/CONJECTURAL** signatures. They do not constitute an asymptotic law or `DISCRIMINANT`.
- These outputs are engine or detectability controls. They always require object, normalization, list, cut, window, selection criterion and identical error bars before comparing.

## Contradictions, retractions and documentary precedence

Precedence should be: correction or audit that identifies a failure > final phase ledger > explicit program closure > earlier draft, plot, README or promotional label. If two texts disagree and no proved correction exists, the conservative status is **CONDITIONAL** or **INCOMPLETE**, never **PROVED**.

| Conflict | Binding resolution |
|---|---|
| Davenport–Heilbronn | Coefficient variants, square-free restriction, \(\kappa\), zero coordinates and catalogs are not interchangeable. Only one fixed and validated definition serves as internal control; subsequent quantitative use without a single arithmetic sequence is **REFUTED**. |
| Weil form versus truncated residue | The complete form and the numerical discrepancy of truncated sides are distinct objects. The latter does not inherit positivity from the former. |
| Linear versus quadratic H0 | There is no universal law: they depend on parity, shifted quartet, matrix and normalization. The divergence is a specification alarm, not a new asymptotic effect. |
| Phase 54 | The unconditional versions of the corollaries that mixed normalizations or omitted \(I<\infty,\kappa=\infty\) are retracted; only the conditioned form survives. |
| Phase 64 | The `det Y≡1` argument that aimed to close HB convergence was retracted as a non sequitur. It cannot be cited either as proof or as a no-go. |
| Phase 65 | Every QED label is subordinate to the corrections that recognize `D8.5` as an open RH-strength point. |
| Old LP versus corrected LP | `ker_l2(H_L-mu_L)=0` is **REFUTED**: the compact resolvent produces a lower eigenvalue with nonzero kernel. The admissible formulation is `BTG-DIV` and disk contraction, but the divergence in \(\mu_L\) remains to be proved. |
| Outcome A and LP neutrality | The attribution from finite sections and `mu_ref` does not control the limiting object. The build-neutral quarantine is a prudent rule, not a proof of control neutrality. |
| `GAP-Z` | Only `MESH` and `BND` have the stated bounds. `ZERO` remains open; the signed cancellation may not be replaced by a sum of absolute values, nor may `GAP-Z` be turned into a discriminant. |
| Absolute domination of the prime part | \(|\lambda_n^{\rm prime}|<\lambda_n^{\rm arch}\) is not the exact Li inequality; the failure as an equivalence already appears at \(n=1\). Absolute no-gos do not transfer to a one-sided lower bound. |
| Laguerre integration by parts | The printed formula \(-\int(\psi-y)f'\) has the wrong sign and boundary term. One must use \(-n+\int(\psi-y)f'=\int(\psi-y+1)f'\) before any signed estimate. |
| Envelope asymptotics | \(\frac{\gamma-1}{2}n\) must be kept; it does not fit inside \(O(\sqrt n\log n)\). The prime-part bound with \(\log n\) starts at \(n\ge2\). |
| E79 proxies | The stable avatar, crossing count and monotone drift were retracted by later controls; an insufficiently precise tail was nullified. `mean(d)` silently changed to a different grid scale, so any reuse requires fixing the definition. `E79.6` remains **NUMERICAL/CONJECTURAL**. |
| Adjugate and characteristic correction | `adj(K)[Z,H]adj(K)=0` is compatibility, not a formula for \([Z,adj(K)]\). `Gamma_t dot mu_t` is the chain rule, not an additional arithmetic source. |
| Raw/core objects | The correct source is `C_core` after extracting exactly the outer factor. It may not be replaced by an isolated boundary, a different antisymmetrization, nor a two-sided product without carrying the phase defect. |
| Geometric realization | Abstract constructions, toy models and local categories do not constitute an absolute surface nor a cohomology that transports the zeros. The geometric reading remains **CONDITIONAL**. |

## Duplications by reparametrization

Each advance should be counted only once if it merely changes representation.

1. The index–Herglotz–Lee–Yang–`Omega7` chains return to the same global-positivity requirement.
2. Connes/CCM, ground state, prolate, Feshbach leakage, Cauchy interpolation, divisibility and adjugate reduce to the same signed residual; they are not cumulative closures.
3. Kato, Euler cocycle, bordered determinant, cofactors, Jacobian, von Mangoldt, commutator, Fourier shell and Stieltjes/heat currents are coordinate changes of the Gamma–Euler anchor, not independent proofs.
4. Li, Weil, LP, Herglotz, Hilbert–Pólya and real-approximant convergence do not add up as evidence: they are equivalences or RH-strength conditions after the corresponding transfer.
5. A positive Gram, a PSD zero-only form and a stable window matrix may coincide on a finite section without supplying the identification of the limiting divisor.
6. Phases 80–89 do not form a ladder of closures. The secular/generating, distributional/coboundary and deformation/Feshbach/rotation routes return to `RDI-ANCHOR`/`IDENT`; they rewrite the same bottleneck.
7. `DISCRIMINANT` is a separation candidate within the RDI cut, not a uniqueness theorem. `RDI-CONV`, LP/`BTG-DIV`, mu-free completeness, `GAP-Z`, `RDP-SHELL` and directional continuities carry their own burdens.

## Operational H0 gate for future ideas

A proposal does not enter the proof line until it clears every point below. Failing one keeps it as **NUMERICAL**, **CONDITIONAL** or exploratory as appropriate.

1. **Single object.** Specify coefficients, domain, normalization, sign, transform, symmetrization, polar/archimedean terms, order of limits, and the complete object distinct from any truncated residue.
2. **Labeled claim.** State before computing whether a proved identity, a conditional bound, a numerical measurement or an RH-strength condition is intended. It is forbidden to call an equivalence a bridge.
3. **Independent mechanism.** Identify the inequality, signed identity or external construction that does not use Weil positivity, zero positions, zero filter, an already-real divisor, nor a root-adapted metric.
4. **Adversarial control.** Require a falsifier with an off-line quartet or planted analogue, plus controls for coefficients, conductor, support and normalization. It must be able to fail precisely where the target fails.
5. **Correct limiting object.** A measurement in `mu_ref`, a sectional eigenvalue or a grid cannot substitute for \(\mu_L\). Every neutrality claim must be proved for both constructions on the limiting object the theorem uses.
6. **Explicit uniformity.** Give constants and simultaneous dependence on all relevant scales: dimension, center, width, prime cut, zero tail, precision and order of limits. A grid or a fit does not substitute for this point.
7. **Information preservation.** Verify conjugation, transversal support, linear multiplicity, signed coupling and boundary terms before contracting, averaging, taking traces or applying an inverse.
8. **Audited transfer.** Separately prove the step from the finite object to the full one. If this produces global positivity, non-vanishing or divisor identification, record **RH-STRENGTH** and do not discount it as a technical detail. If it is claimed to be the only step of that strength, also prove that uniqueness with respect to the other obligations.
9. **Reproducibility.** Freeze data and versions; document coverage, precision, peak selection, seeds and input tests. No later validation may mix variants without passing the gate again.

## Genuinely new mathematics still missing

At least one RH-strength contribution is missing, and none is obtained by relabeling an existing detector. The correction of the Li target opens a direct option that must be examined before assuming that every proof goes through LP+IDENT.

1. **Direct one-sided inequality for Li.** It must prove \(\lambda_n^{\rm prime}\ge-\lambda_n^{\rm arch}\) without replacing the Laguerre kernel by its modulus. When the archimedean part is nonnegative, this controls the negative excursion; in the remaining indices it must keep the full lower bound. It must keep the pole, Gamma, boundary and prime terms together, not use a PNT bound already equivalent to RH, and, if an off-line arithmetic control is invoked, first define a comparable Euler–Gamma class that contains it.
2. **Signed convergence and arithmetic anchor.** It must resolve `GAP-Z` via build-neutral control of `ZERO` and, separately or through a proved implication, identify the full Gamma–Euler residual as `RDI-ANCHOR`/`PROFILE-ROTATION-RDI`. It must preserve conjugation, transversal support and linear multiplicity, survive an off-line planted control, and produce `ADJ-ARITH-LOCK`/`DIRECTIONAL-IDENT` without using the sought divisor. An absolute bound, an average, a trace product or a finite signature do not suffice. It is not presupposed that `DISCRIMINANT` is the only possible way to supply this mathematics.
3. **Constructed external geometry.** It must effectively produce category, global object, diagonal, polarization, spectral reader and Lefschetz triple with demonstrable independence from the zero operator. Only afterward could a new geometric positivity connect to the Weil form without circularity.

An additional possibility would be a non-local phase→inertia transport theory that exceeds the already-closed Tauberian and Diophantine classes, with a signed inequality that passes the same falsifiers. Until one of these pieces exists, the transversal status is **INCOMPLETE**: there remain usable mathematics and diagnostics, but no logical closure.
