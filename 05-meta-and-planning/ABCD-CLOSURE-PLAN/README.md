# A–B–C–D closure plan

This folder holds the planning and synthesis documents for closing rows A, B, C and D of the
program over Spec Z. Rows A, B and C are recorded as complete on the declared carrier; most
documents here concern row D, Weil positivity on the declared carrier, treated as a target in its
own right. The files map what has been built, which reductions are proved, which steps remain
open, and how the record was kept consistent. None of them proves a new theorem or changes a label.

## Contents

- `PLAN.md` — the general A–B–C–D closure plan.
- `PRIORITY_PLAN.md` — earlier plan for closing row A and choosing a route.
- `FIRST_CAMPAIGN.md` — first campaign on viability of the carrier and of the contact.
- `CORPUS_COVERAGE.md` — inventory of how the corpus covers each row.
- `CORPUS_CHECK_ROW_D_PLAN.md` — what the corpus already did on eight candidate row D inputs.
- `ROW-D-COMPLETION-PLAN.md` — completion plan for phases 139–143.
- `ROW-D-CLOSURE-PLAN.md` — closure plan built from four independent reviews of the corpus.
- `ROW-D-SYNTHESIS-PHASES-135-144.md` — synthesis after phases 135–144.
- `ROW-D-MAP-b.md` — ASCII map of row D after phase 142.
- `HANDOFF-PHASE-149.md` — state of the record and next steps at phase 149.
- `PATH-TO-D.md` — synthesis of the first 155 phases and a ranked path to row D.
- `ROW-D-REDUCED-STATEMENT.md` — the reduced statement of row D after phases 134–156.
- `INVENTORY-PHASES-168-184.md` — record survey of phases 168–184.
- `CROSSWORD-TO-D.md` — complete dependency graph ("crossword") toward row D.
- `SIGN-REAUDIT.md` — re-audit of the sign question of row D.
- `SUPPORT-DESCENT-TEST.md` — test of a support-descent implication at first contact.
- `RECORDS-CLEANUP.md` — cleanup of the research records.
