# Meta and planning

This folder holds the working guides and work plans of the program: the plans written before each
push, the syntheses written after it, the instruments used to triage candidate approaches, the
consolidated context of the whole record, and the single index of routes attempted toward the
sign. **None of these documents is a paper or a phase result.** They are the scaffolding that
guided the research, kept so that the reason for every choice of direction can be reconstructed.

For the papers see [`../04-papers/`](../04-papers/); for the phase-by-phase record see
[`../03-research/`](../03-research/).

## How to read this folder

1. To know **what has been tried toward the sign and why it failed**, start with
   [`ROUTES-MASTER-INDEX.md`](ROUTES-MASTER-INDEX.md). It is the only place where new attempts are
   recorded; every other document here is closed or historical.
2. To follow **how the planning evolved**, read the numbered plans `01`–`24` in order (they are
   numbered in the order in which they were written). Each one opens with a status line saying what
   became of it.
3. For **rows A–D over Spec Z** (rows A, B, C complete on the declared carrier; row D, Weil
   positivity, open), read [`ABCD-CLOSURE-PLAN/`](ABCD-CLOSURE-PLAN/), starting with its `PLAN.md`.
4. For the **consolidated review of the record up to phase 101** (labels, no-gos, retractions and
   the Ω₇ restart plan), read [`RH-MASTER-CONTEXT/`](RH-MASTER-CONTEXT/).
5. [`archive-top-level/`](archive-top-level/) keeps earlier top-level documents for reference only.

Paper numbering: Paper 46 is the paper on the sign (row D) and Paper 47 is the intersection frame.

## `ABCD-CLOSURE-PLAN/`

Plans and syntheses for closing rows A, B, C and D over Spec Z. Rows A, B and C are recorded as
complete on the declared carrier; most documents concern row D, Weil positivity, treated as a
target in its own right. Start with `PLAN.md` (the general plan and its running record), then
`ROW-D-REDUCED-STATEMENT.md` and `CROSSWORD-TO-D.md` (the dependency graph toward row D, now a
closed record). See [`ABCD-CLOSURE-PLAN/README.md`](ABCD-CLOSURE-PLAN/README.md) for the full list.

## `RH-MASTER-CONTEXT/`

A frozen, self-contained review of the record up to phase 101, written before the Ω₇ work was
resumed: scope and labels, the master synthesis, the check of Paper 36's logic, the assessment of
the nine exploratory programs RH2017–RH2025, the walk through phases 0–101, no-gos and
retractions, and the restart plan. `fragments/` keeps the block-by-block checks behind it and
`tools/` one interval verifier. See [`RH-MASTER-CONTEXT/README.md`](RH-MASTER-CONTEXT/README.md).

## `ROUTES-MASTER-INDEX.md`

The single index of every route attempted toward the sign of the Paper 46 form (row D): what was
attempted, where, what it gave, why it failed, what works, the constraints the missing object must
satisfy, maps by family, and the register of new attempts (Part V). It absorbs the former route
indices, the master grid and the crossword, which receive no further entries. Before any new
construction the idea is looked up there. Paper 48 is its published summary.
