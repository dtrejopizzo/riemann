  about 1,400 routes from programs RH1–RH9, phases 0–209, the papers and the literature, each with
  its tool family, location, outcome (DEAD, NO-GO in a declared class, FALSIFIED, PARTIAL,
  NUMERICAL-ONLY, CIRCULAR, or ending at a reformulation of row D) and the reason it failed.
- [Paper 48](04-papers/48-row-d-route-map/) — the same routes classified on an 8×8 grid, with the
  walls W1–W12 stated as theorems or meta-theorems and the specification S1–S12 a closing argument
  must meet.

The barrier theorems themselves are proved in [Paper 47](04-papers/47-row-d-dual-certificates/).
