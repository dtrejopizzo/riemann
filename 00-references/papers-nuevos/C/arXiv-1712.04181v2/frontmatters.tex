\begin{abstract}
In this paper, we give concrete descriptions of leafwise cohomology groups and show the regularized determinant expression of the dynamical zeta function for fiber bundles over $S^{1}$.
As applications, we show a functional equation and some formulas for special values of the dynamical zeta function.
\end{abstract}
