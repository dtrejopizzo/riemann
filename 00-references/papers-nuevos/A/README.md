# Connes–Consani: arithmetic site and F₁

arXiv source packages of the Connes–Consani papers on the arithmetic site, the scaling site, the Riemann–Roch strategy, Riemann–Roch and the Jacobian of Spec Z̄, ζ-cycles, knots and primes, and the Fargues–Fontaine curve; Haran's *Geometry over F₁*; and the PDF of *On the metaphysics of F₁* (`2307.06748v1.pdf`). The folder keeps its one-letter name because verification scripts in phases 107, 114 and 116 read files by this path.
