# papers-nuevos (newer references)

This folder (Spanish name, "new papers") collects third-party arXiv papers gathered as background for the search for new method families on row D. The material is grouped in subfolders by theme; most entries are arXiv source folders named by their identifier. These are works by other authors, kept for reading and citation.

## Contents

- `A/` — Connes–Consani program: arithmetic site (1405.4527), geometry of the arithmetic site (1502.05580), scaling site (1507.05818), Riemann–Roch strategy (1805.10501), Riemann–Roch for Spec Z̄ (2205.01391), Hochschild homology, trace map and ζ-cycles (2207.10419), knots, primes and the adele class space (2401.08401), on the Jacobian of Spec Z̄ (2602.15941), absolute geometry of Spec Z and the Fargues–Fontaine curve (2606.06604); A. Connes, an essay on the Riemann Hypothesis (1509.05576); S. Haran, geometry over F₁ (1709.05831).
- `B/` — C. Deninger: number theory and dynamical systems on foliated spaces (math/0204110), arithmetic geometry and analysis on foliated spaces (math/0505354), dynamical systems for arithmetic schemes (1807.06400), primes, knots and periodic orbits (2301.11643), a Birch and Swinnerton-Dyer conjecture for Dedekind zeta functions (2504.15767), rational Witt vectors and associated sheaves (2508.05329).
- `C/` — foliations and dynamical zeta functions: E. Leichtnam (1307.3851); J. Kim (1712.04181, 1912.02159); J. A. Álvarez López, Y. Kordyukov (1905.12912); J. A. Álvarez López, J. Kim, M. Morishita, regularized determinant formulas for 3-dimensional Riemannian foliated dynamical systems (2410.20758); R. Meyer, a spectral interpretation for the zeros of the Riemann zeta function (math/0412277).
- `D/` — arithmetic intersection theory: X. Yuan, S.-W. Zhang, arithmetic Hodge index theorem I and II (1304.3538, 1304.3539); A. Carney (1810.06342); F. Déglise, J. Fasel, quadratic Riemann–Roch formulas (2403.09266); Z. Zhu, Riemann–Hurwitz formula for arithmetic surfaces (2510.08033); W. He, numerical cohomology for arithmetic surfaces (2512.01811). Also one administrative PDF that is not a reference.
- `mas-papers/` — tropical and characteristic-one geometry: D. Cartwright (1308.3813, 1506.02023, 1511.00650); A. Connes, C. Consani, homological algebra in characteristic one (1703.02325); P. Jell, J. Rau, K. Shaw (1711.07900); O. Amini, M. Piquerez (2007.07826); K. Sumi (2105.12216); S. Haran, non-additive geometry and Frobenius correspondences (2209.08536); G. Mikhalkin, I. Zharkov, tropical curves and their Jacobians (math/0612267).
- `Puente/` — M. Morishita, on a relation between Deninger's foliated dynamical systems and Connes–Consani's adelic spaces (2508.15971).
- `extension-clase-test/` — PDFs on the Weil form and test classes: Connes–Consani, the scaling Hamiltonian (1910.14368) and Weil positivity and trace formula, the archimedean place (2006.13771); Connes–Consani, zeta zeros and prolate wave operators (2310.18423); Connes–Consani–Moscovici, zeta spectral triples (2511.22755); M. Suzuki, Weil's quadratic form via the screw function (2606.09096); J. C. Lagarias, Li coefficients for automorphic L-functions (math/0404394); J.-F. Burnol, the explicit formula in simple terms (math/9810169) and the explicit formula and the conductor operator (math/9902080).
