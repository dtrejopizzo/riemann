## (b) Deep-read notes

1. **CC 2609.00299 (absolute twistor line).** Builds Spec Z‾ absolutely: affine (Spec Z)_{F₁} glued to an
   archimedean F_{1²} component; odd-integer arithmetic topos; Frobenius on complex points generates Adams
   operations and complex conjugation. No square, no intersection pairing, no positivity statement. Properties
   of the missing object: none of (1)–(4). KNOWN (L42 extension).
2. **CC 2606.06604.** (Spec Z)_{F₁} as common origin of FF curve, complex analytic, scaling site. Curve-level
   only; same verdict.
3. **Connes 2602.04022.** PROVED: zeros of finite-Euler-product extremized quadratic forms lie on the line;
   strategy (convergence as primes→∞) HEURISTIC. Already the semilocal/prolate family (phase 183 Sonin chain;
   positivity there is per-finite-S, comb-generic in our sense). KNOWN.
4. **Yuan–Zhang + 2503.14099 (local).** Hodge index for adelic line bundles holds in arbitrary finite
   dimension over finitely generated fields; positivity mechanism = arithmetic ampleness + local
   Hodge index at each place (place by place, violates (2)). The obstruction recorded in L26/L83 stands:
   no infinite-dimensional "Spec Z × Spec Z" carrier. KNOWN.
5. **Hajli 2608.25450.** New (Aug 2026), not in index. Equilibrium measure as "detector of arithmetic positivity"
   for Hermitian line bundles on arithmetic varieties. Finite-dimensional; does not read Λ. IRRELEVANT.
6. **Bost theta invariants (1512.08946).** The only existing framework where h⁰ is defined for
   *infinite-dimensional* Hermitian bundles over Spec Z‾, with subadditivity/summability (a built-in
   inequality, property (4)-like). But it does not read the comb and the h⁰-route clauses (a),(b′),(c) were
   shown unsatisfiable (R137 D.11, R140, R141, W10). KNOWN in substance.
7. **Kurokawa ⊗ / Akatsuka / Tanaka.** ζ⊗ζ has an Euler product over pairs of primes — the only literature
   object here that is non-linear in Λ and reads pairs (m,n) (semiring-principle flavor). Already examined
   and closed in phase 101 (E101_083, prime-event tensor transport NO-GO). KNOWN.
8. **Durov.** Spec Z ⊗_{F₁} Spec Z ≅ Spec Z: proved degeneracy of the naive absolute square. Haran's
   F-ring square is non-degenerate but carries no intersection theory with proved positivity.

## A.1 Targeted questions

**Q1. Any positivity/sign result for Kurokawa's ζ⊗ζ, its zeros, or its explicit formula after phase 101?**
None found. Items in the chain: Kurokawa "Values of absolute tensor products" (PJA 2005); Akatsuka
"Euler product of triple zeta" (IJM 2005), "The double Riemann zeta function" (CNTP 2009), RIMS 2012;
Kurokawa–Koyama "Dualities for absolute zeta…" (PJA 2013); Tanaka (APM 2024 / 2008.07752);
Koyama "First eigenvalue problem and tensor products of zeta functions" (PJA 2004 — tensor products used
for Laplacian eigenvalue bounds on Bianchi manifolds via Luo–Rudnick–Sarnak; positivity is of the
Rankin–Selberg type, not of ζ⊗ζ; IRRELEVANT); Koyama "Absolute zeta, absolute RH and Casimir energies"
(2010) and S.-J. Kim "RH and functional equations for zeta over F₁" (PJA 2009) — both concern absolute
zeta of F₁-schemes (finitely many zeros, trivially checkable), not ζ⊗ζ; Tomita thesis (canonical
constructions of absolute zeta). The zeros of ζ⊗ζ are by definition the sums ρ+ρ′ (no new sign
information); no explicit-formula positivity statement exists in the literature found. Verdict: KNOWN
(E101_083 stands), no new reason to reopen.
