## (b) Deep-read notes

1. **Suzuki 1606.05726 (JFA).** For L in a subclass of Selberg class, builds E = ξ_L-type Hermite–Biehler
   function on a shifted line; GRH(L) ⟺ the associated de Branges chain is generated by a canonical system
   whose Hamiltonian is positive semidefinite, and the Hamiltonian is computed from the solution of an
   integral equation whose kernel is built from the explicit-formula data (Λ + Γ). Positivity of H is
   not built in: it is the target re-expressed. Non-linear in Λ (Kreĭn-type integral equation) — property 3
   holds — but property 4 fails. KNOWN (phase 155 Kreĭn indeterminacy, L20/L38).
2. **Suzuki 2606.09096.** Self-adjoint operator realizing Weil's form on (−a,a) via screw function g;
   localized positivity for small a is PROVED (L12, used). KNOWN.
3. **Burnol (Sonine/co-Poisson).** Global FE enters via co-Poisson intertwining; zeros give complete/minimal
   systems in extended Sonine spaces. Purely structural; no sign. KNOWN (phase 183 Sonin chain).
4. **Conrey–Li 2000.** De Branges' positivity condition fails for ζ and Dirichlet L. The de Branges-type
   "built-in" positivity therefore cannot be the carrier. KNOWN (L16).
5. **Kaltenbäck–Woracek Pontryagin series.** Hermitian-indefinite continuous functions on an interval with κ
   negative squares ↔ de Branges–Pontryagin spaces / canonical systems with κ singularities. Applied to the
   window form 𝔞_T this counts λ_A(T)<0 directions as a Pontryagin index; the index is an integer-valued,
   T-monotone invariant. Already covered as Kreĭn–Langer / integer-invariant routes (README: "invariante
   entero / flujo espectral" dead; Theorem P n₋ ≤ 5/7/8 in phase 155). KNOWN.
6. **Makarov–Poltoratski Etudes I/II, 2603.13586, 2505.00669.** General ISP machinery (Toeplitz kernels,
   gap theory, PW-canonical systems). No arithmetic input, so property 1 fails by construction. Tool only.
7. **Bombieri–Garrett 2002.07929.** Restrict Δ on Γ\H to functions whose constant term vanishes beyond
   height a and is orthogonal to a chosen distribution (Eisenstein constant term at a point); Friedrichs
   extension is self-adjoint ⇒ its discrete spectrum λ = s(s−1) is real, and those s are zeros of ζ_k
   (k imaginary quadratic). Positivity is genuinely built in (Friedrichs ⇒ semibounded, self-adjoint).
   The FE enters globally through E(z,s) constant term y^s + φ(s) y^{1−s}. BUT: only a sparse subset of
   zeros can appear (≤94% under pair correlation; possibly none); the prime comb is never read individually
   (property 1 fails — any Beurling-type Eisenstein data with the same FE shape would work); conclusion is
   on zeros, not on a window form. Not in index.
8. **Faddeev–Pavlov / Lax–Phillips.** Wave equation on Γ\H, incoming/outgoing subspaces; Z(t) contraction
   semigroup whose generator's spectrum is the set of zeros of the scattering matrix φ(s)=ξ(2s−1)/ξ(2s);
   statement about ζ zeros ⟺ a decay/completeness property of Z(t). Positivity built in = unitarity of the
   wave group (energy form). Reads primes only through ζ(2s) (the Eisenstein constant term), so the window
   form's comb Σ v_n c_F(log n) is not visible at the right scale; property 1 at best partial. Not in index.
9. **CCM 2403.01247.** Jacobi matrix for S={∞,p}: coefficients are q-series (q=1/p), in ℤ[1/√2]. Reads p
   exactly (1), is non-linear (3), but is semilocal (fails 2: place by place) and its positivity is that of
   a moment measure |∏ local factors|² (automatic, comb-generic). Family covered (L07, integrality A3). KNOWN.
10. **Transfer operators (Mayer, Lewis–Zagier).** Perron–Frobenius positivity of L_s for real s gives the
    leading eigenvalue; Selberg Z(s) = det(1−L_s^+)det(1−L_s^−). Gives information about Selberg zeros
    (Laplace eigenvalues, where "RH" is Selberg's theorem), not about ζ's comb; ζ enters only via the
    continuous spectrum. Index: 06_ residue, NO-GO ARGUED. KNOWN.
11. **Hedenmalm 2606.17494.** Pair (LD, L) of first-order operators on the half-line from θ; notion of
    self-adjointness for a pencil. Framework only, no positivity theorem. Watch item.
12. **2408.15135 (W ≥ 0 intertwining).** W ≥ 0 is stated to be an operator form of Weil positivity; it is a
    reformulation (REF-D). KNOWN (L46).
13. **Nyman–Beurling recent (Colombeau 2606.22562, Zenodo d_N papers, probabilistic NB).** All are
    distance/closure reformulations; Gram matrices linear in μ-data; no built-in sign. KNOWN (L18/L61/L79).
14. **Groskin 2607.02828.** Finite Guinand–Weil dictionary; archimedean tail is a totally positive
    Cauchy–Stieltjes increment (built-in positivity of the archimedean tail only). Certificate-type
    (thm:Z class). KNOWN (L09 family). Note: "totally positive tail" is archimedean, comb-blind.

3. **Kaltenbäck–Woracek Pontryagin index of 𝔞_T (KNOWN family).** Non-linear integer invariant; already
   killed as "invariante entero". Listed only because the precise κ-continuity theorems (index stable under
   finite-rank perturbation of the Hermitian-indefinite function) are not cited in our corpus — tool, not route.
4. **CCM q-series Jacobi matrices (KNOWN family).** Reads p exactly and non-linearly; semilocal. Covered.
5. **Suzuki canonical-system Hamiltonian (KNOWN).** Reads Λ and FE non-linearly; positivity = target.

## Quick check (i): Bombieri–Garrett is comb-generic — NO-GO (PROVED from the paper's own structure)
Full text read (pdftotext). The constraint is a distribution θ = Σ ν_d θ_d, θ_d = Σ_{z∈H_d} δ_z (Heegner
points), and θE_s = const·|d|^{s/2} ζ_k(s)/ζ(2s) (Hecke). The self-adjointness (Friedrichs extension of Δ
restricted by θ and the constant-term cutoff, Thm 23/27, Cor 28) uses only: θ ∈ E^{−1−ε}, and the
functional equation c_w θE_{1−w} = θE_w. Neither the Euler product nor class-group summation is used in the
operator construction. Replacing θ_d by a single point δ_{z0} (z0 a CM point with h(d)>1) gives θE_s =
Epstein zeta of one form class; same FE, same Friedrichs argument (single point is in E^{−1−ε}). Epstein
zetas with h>1 have zeros off the line (Davenport–Heilbronn type), so the conclusion "eigen-parameters ⊂
zeros" survives with zeros off-line ⇒ the positivity (self-adjointness) only constrains which zeros appear as
eigenvalues, never forces zeros onto the line. The construction is FE-generic, not Λ-reading: property 1
fails. The paper itself (Cor. 69, 76–77) only gets spacing constraints. **Verdict: NO-GO for row D** (route
class: "self-adjoint extension with Eisenstein constraint"). To be added to ROUTES-TRIED-INDEX by the coordinator.

## Quick check (ii): Faddeev–Pavlov primes sit at the wrong weight/line — NO-GO (ARGUED)
For SL₂(ℤ), φ(s) = ξ(2s−1)/ξ(2s). On the unitary axis s = ½+ir: ζ(2s) = ζ(1+2ir), ζ(2s−1) = ζ(2ir) ≅
ζ(1−2ir) by FE. So −φ′/φ contains Σ Λ(n) n^{−1}·(n^{−2ir} + n^{2ir}): primes enter at frequency 2·log n
(i.e. log n², in the wave-time variable t ↔ r) with weight Λ(n)/n, the edge of absolute convergence,
NOT Λ(n)/√n at log n as in v_n c_F(log n). ζ-zeros appear as resonances at s = ρ/2 (Re = ¼ under the target),
inside the region where the Lax–Phillips semigroup is a contraction for any data; unitarity of the wave group
gives no constraint on Re ρ. Rescaling t→2t fixes the frequency but not the weight (1/n vs 1/√n): the
critical-line comb is only reachable by analytic continuation, where the energy positivity is lost.
**Verdict: NO-GO (ARGUED)**; property 1 fails at the correct weight.
