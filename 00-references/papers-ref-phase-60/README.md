# papers-ref-phase-60

This folder holds third-party references collected around phase 60, when the
Connes–Consani–Moscovici spectral triple was implemented and validated as the project's numerical
engine. It contains arXiv source packages, single LaTeX files and two figure files from these works.

## Contents

- `arXiv-2501.06560v1/` — A. Connes, C. Consani, "Knots, primes and class field theory".
- `arXiv-2511.22755v1/` — A. Connes, C. Consani, H. Moscovici, "Zeta Spectral Triples"; basis of
  the engine in `02-foundations/engine/`.
- `arXiv-2511.23257v1/` — A. Connes, W. D. van Suijlekom, "Quadratic forms, real zeros and echoes
  of the spectral action".
- `arXiv-2602.04022v1.tex` — A. Connes, "The Riemann Hypothesis: past, present and a letter
  through time".
- `compareplots3.pdf`, `weilcomp.png` — figures included by `arXiv-2602.04022v1.tex`.

The sources of "On the absolute geometry of Spec Z and the Fargues–Fontaine curve" (2606.06604) and
"On the Jacobian of Spec Z̄" (2602.15941) are in [../papers-nuevos/A/](../papers-nuevos/A/).
