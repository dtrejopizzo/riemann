                                             Let N be a positive integer and let A ⊆ [N ]. For each prime p, write
                                                                                  Ap = {a      (mod p) : a ∈ A}
                                           for the set of residue classes occupied by A modulo p. The large sieve [14] and Gallagher’s larger
                                           sieve [7] turn upper bounds for the sets Ap into global upper bounds for |A|. For background on
                                           sieve methods, we refer to [6]. A broad inverse principle in sieve theory asserts that if a set is close
                                           to extremal for such a sieve bound, then the local restrictions should not be arbitrary: either the set
                                           is substantially smaller than the sieve bound, or it possesses algebraic structure. This is the general
                                           inverse sieve philosophy studied, for example, in Croot–Lev [1], Helfgott–Venkatesh [11], Walsh
                                           [18, 19], Green–Harper [8], Shao [16, 17], Hanson [10], and Croot–Mao–Yip [2].
                                              The present paper concerns the quadratic threshold, one of the central cases of this philosophy.√ If
                                           |Ap | ≤ p/2+O(1) for every prime p, then both the large sieve and the larger sieve give |A| ≪ N .
                                           This is sharp up to the implied constant: the squares, and more generally the integer values of
                                           a rational quadratic, occupy only about half the residue classes modulo every odd prime. The
                                           quadratic inverse sieve conjecture predicts that these examples account for all near-equality cases.
                                           A concrete form of the conjecture is the following; see for example Problem 47 on Green’s list of
                                           100 open problems [9] and [1, Section 7.4] in Croot–Lev’s list of open problems.

                                           Conjecture 1.1. If A ⊆ [N ] satisfies |Ap | ≤ (p + 1)/2 for every prime and |A| ≫ N , then a
                                           positive proportion of A should lie in the image of a rational quadratic.

   Our proof uses a weighted entropy argument together with sieve estimates. Recently, Croot,
Mao, Pohoata, Sheffer and Yip [3] developed a weighted entropy large-sieve method for sets with
bounded algebraic multiplicities, in which local savings are combined through product weights.
The present argument is similar in spirit and is inspried by [3], although the exact proof is quite
different: our local weights come from a weighted random choice of the shift in a quadratic-
character root-counting function, biased toward shifts with favorable quadratic-character correla-
tion and smoothed by the uniform distribution.
