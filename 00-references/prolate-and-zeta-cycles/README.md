# prolate-and-zeta-cycles — Connes–Consani–Moscovici

Sources on the spectral approach to the zeros via ζ-cycles, the prolate spheroidal operator and
semilocal adelic operators (formerly `papers-ref-2`).

- `arXiv-2106.01715v1/` — A. Connes, C. Consani, *Spectral triples and ζ-cycles*.
- `arXiv-2112.05500v1/` — A. Connes, H. Moscovici, *Prolate spheroidal operator and zeta*; `draft4.pdf` is a PDF draft of the same paper.
- `connes-moscovici-2022-the-uv-prolate-spectrum-matches-the-zeros-of-zeta.pdf` — A. Connes, H. Moscovici, PNAS 119 (2022).
- `arXiv-2310.18423v2/`, `2310.18423v2.pdf` — A. Connes, C. Consani, H. Moscovici, *Zeta zeros and prolate wave operators: semilocal adelic operators*; `Zeta-zeros-and-prolateproofs-final-2024.pdf` is the journal proof (Ann. Funct. Anal., 2024).
- `arXiv-2402.13082v1/` — A. Connes, *Heat expansion and zeta*.
