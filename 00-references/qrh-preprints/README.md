# Quasi-Riemann hypothesis preprints

Third-party preprints on fixed zero-free half-planes for Dirichlet and Hecke L-functions,
kept here as the sources for phase 210. None of them is refereed. Text extractions are
included for searching; the PDFs are the documents of record.

- `openai-qrh-seven-eighths.pdf` — OpenAI, *The Quasi-Riemann Hypothesis: A Zero-Free
  Half-Plane Re(s) > 7/8*, 199 pp. Part I proves 11/12 by a probe/Mellin-continuation
  argument; Part II reaches 7/8 with a compensated probe, a zero detector and two
  recursive moment estimates (Lemmas 17.1 and 18.1), combined in Section 20.
- `openai-qrh-eleven-twelfths.pdf` — OpenAI, *The Quasi-Riemann Hypothesis*, 49 pp.
  A self-contained proof of 11/12 via a mean square of sextic-twisted Möbius sums
  (Proposition 3.1), Poisson summation, Kubota's cubic theta function and the quadratic
  large sieve.
- `kintali-qrh-short-proof.pdf` — S. Kintali, *A Short Proof of the Quasi-Riemann
  Hypothesis*, 27 pp. Adapts the probe framework to a weaker half-plane Re s > 47/48.
